ME Group International plc26
Laundry rollout & Wash.ME App
Evolving the user experience through innovation – the 
new laundry App
The laundry App creates a more seamless experience for 
consumers who are either new to or regularly using our laundry 
services. Through the App, users can:
• locate and manage machines with a single click. 
• Search from over 3,000 Wash.ME locations across the UK and 
Europe
• Build loyalty points and access unique codes and discounts for 
our laundry services
• Receive real-time notifications and updates on the status of 
their laundry 
• Make payments for laundry services directly via the app
An example of ME Group’s dedicated approach to innovation and 
digitalization, which is driving a more seamless experience for 
consumers and meeting their needs in an increasingly digital-first 
world.
From January 2026 the Group has started launching App in all 
countries where laundry is available
