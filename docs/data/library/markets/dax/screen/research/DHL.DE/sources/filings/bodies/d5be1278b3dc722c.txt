Ad hoc: DHL AG raises full-year 2026 earnings guidance 16/10/2026 Disclosure of inside information pursuant to Article 17 MAR Today the management of DHL Group assessed the business outlook of its divisions for the remainder of the current year. In light of the ongoing earnings momentum, in particular within the Express division and indications of a robust seasonal peak in Q4, management has decided to raise its outlook for the full year 2026 as follows: Reported Group EBIT is now expected to ‘exceed EUR 7.0 billion’ (previously: ‘> EUR 6.5 billion‘). Expected EBIT for the DHL divisions has been increased to more than EUR 6.4 billion (previously: > EUR 5.9 billion). The outlook remains unchanged for Post & Parcel Germany (EBIT > EUR 900 million) and for Group Functions (around EUR -400 million). Also, the outlook for FY 2026 FCF excl. Net M&A has been revised upwards to ‘more than EUR 3.2 billion’ (previously: ‘around EUR 3.0 billion‘). The strong OCF performance despite some growth-driven working capital build-up allows for investment into further growth. In order to take advantage of selected opportunities amid the ongoing business dynamic, capex outlook for FY 2026 is increased to now ‘around EUR 3.6 billion’ (previously: ‘EUR 3.0-3.3 billion‘). Furthermore, the current mid-term guidance (EBIT of more than EUR 7 billion in a normalized macro environment) will be reviewed in March next year. For better context these are the preliminary financials for Q3 2026: Business development in the third quarter showed a broad-based growth in demand and, overall, positive earnings momentum for the Group, particularly in the DHL Express division. Group revenue increased by more than 12% in Q3 2026 year-over-year to around EUR 22.6 billion. Group EBIT reached around EUR 1,710 million (prior year: EUR 1,477 million), corresponding to a year-over-year increase of around 16%. DHL Express reported EBIT of around EUR 1,035 million in Q3, significantly above the prior-year figure (EUR 692 million, which included EUR -54 million negative non-recurring effects). This is driven by continued benefits from yield and cost measures as well as operating leverage from weight growth. This result had a limited support of around EUR 100 million from capacity constraints in the air freight market. DHL Global Forwarding generated EBIT of around EUR 260 million compared to prior year’s result of EUR 195 million, which included EUR -14 million Cost of Change in Q3 2025. DHL Supply Chain recorded earnings of around EUR 300 million. The prior-year result of EUR 278 million included negative non-recurring effects of EUR -7 million. DHL eCommerce reported EBIT of around EUR 50 million. The prior year result of EUR 176 million included positive non-recurring effects of EUR 123 million. Post & Parcel Germany achieved EBIT of around EUR 150 million (prior year: EUR 218 million). FCF excl. Net M&A for Q3 was around EUR 900 million. This number includes net IEEPA outflows of around EUR 255 million in the quarter, without which the FCF would have been accordingly higher in the quarter. Capex (owned assets) booked in the third quarter was around EUR 900 million, leading to an EUR 0.5 billion increase year-over-year in the first nine months to around EUR 2.2 billion. The full Q3 2026 report will be published as scheduled on November 5, 2026.
