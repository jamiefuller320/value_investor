# AMRIZE N (AMRZ.SW) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-26T17:52:41.473973+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Amrize Ltd (AMRZ.SW) is the June 2025 spin-off of Holcim’s North American building materials and building envelope franchise, dual-listed in Zurich and New York. The quantitative screen flags a buy on cheapness, dividend/capital return, and balance-sheet risk metrics (composite 75%, P/E 23.7, P/B 2.1, debt/equity 54%), but the signal is new (one week) and the headline 21.0% yield likely overstates recurring income given the approved special distribution. The investment case rests on a cash-generative, North America–focused platform with post-separation deleveraging, a $1.0bn buyback, and shareholder distributions, set against cyclical construction exposure, Q1 2026 losses, and Building Envelope softness. Key debate: whether spin-off transition costs and seasonal/Q1 noise mask an improving core, or whether roofing margin pressure and flat cement pricing cap earnings recovery.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Amrize offers a recently separated, investment-grade-aspiring building solutions compounder at a modest absolute multiple rather than a deep cyclical trough. The screen passes six of 22 models—Graham Enterprising, FCF Yield, Dividend Growth, Composite Value, Earnings Quality, and Financial Health—clustering around acceptable valuation (P/E below 25, P/B below 3), manageable leverage, and cash conversion rather than classic deep-value or quality-moat screens (Buffett, Magic Formula, Piotroski all fail; F-Score 5/9).

Business quality is reasonable for the sector: two segments (Building Materials: cement, aggregates, concrete; Building Envelope: commercial/residential roofing and wall systems), >19,000 employees, >1,000 sites, and a stated strategy of organic growth, bolt-on M&A, and capital returns. Post-spin, management lowered external borrowings and established standalone governance; April 2026 shareholder approval of a $0.44 special dividend, up to $0.44 ordinary dividend, and a $1.0bn repurchase programme align with the screen’s dividend and FCF-yield families.

The buy case is not pristine earnings momentum—reported FY2025 net income fell 7% year-on-year (Yahoo fallback; see Financial Review)—but the franchise generates substantial free cash flow and trades at a discount to many building-products peers on headline multiples. For value investors willing to underwrite spin-off noise, seasonality, and construction-cycle risk, the screen’s buy signal is directionally supported by capital structure improvement and shareholder-return policy, though not by recent quarterly profitability.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Spin-off and governance.** Amrize has limited standalone operating history (separation completed 23 June 2025). Filings cite risks around Holcim indemnification, potential tax liabilities if Holcim fails to pay separation-related taxes, transitional service agreements (largely ended February 2026), and executive conflicts of interest. Pre-spin financials may not be fully representative of future standalone results.

**Cyclical and operational.** Construction demand, weather/seasonality (Q1/Q4 typically weakest), raw-material and energy inflation, and infrastructure spending uncertainty are emphasised throughout 10-K risk factors. Q1 2026 losses and negative operating cash flow are seasonally normal but magnified by envelope weakness and higher standalone SG&A.

**Building Envelope.** Revenue and segment EBITDA declined year-on-year in Q1 2026; distributor concentration in this segment is disclosed. Warranty charges tied to a manufacturing issue appear in Q1 reconciling items. Truist’s July 2026 downgrade (news manifest) cited commercial roofing margin pressure and limited near-term cement pricing upside—consistent with filing segment data.

**Balance sheet and pensions.** Total debt ~$5.3bn post-spin; covenant headroom exists but leverage remains material for a cyclical issuer. Defined benefit pension and OPEB obligations are disclosed (~$229m non-current employee benefits per Yahoo balance sheet; pension cash flows in 10-Q footnotes). No going-concern language identified.

**Legal, environmental, and labour.** Ordinary-course litigation, environmental remediation, and mining reclamation provisions are referenced. ~6,200 hourly employees under collective bargaining agreements; labour disruption risk is explicit. Product liability and uninsured warranty exposure in Building Envelope noted in 10-K.

**Screen-specific.** Piotroski F-Score 5/9 (ROA, current ratio, gross margin, asset turnover not improving). The 21.0% screen yield is inconsistent with a sustainable ~$0.44 ordinary dividend on a ~$50 equivalent NYSE price and likely reflects special-distribution treatment—do not rely on it for income modelling.
