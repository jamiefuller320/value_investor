Corpus Resources Plc (" Corpus " or the " Company ") Restoration of Listing and Trading 24 June 2026 Further to the Company's announcement of 1 May 2026 regarding the temporary suspension of the listing and trading of its ordinary shares, the Board of Corpus Resources Plc is pleased to announce that the Financial Conduct Authority has restored the listing of the Company's ordinary shares to the Official List and that trading in the Company's ordinary shares on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange resumed with effect from 7:30 a.m. today, 24 June 2026.
