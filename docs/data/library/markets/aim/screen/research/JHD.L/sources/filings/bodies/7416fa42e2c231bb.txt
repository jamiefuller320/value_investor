Results of AGM James Halstead PLC 06 December 2024 6 December 2024 James Halstead PLC (The "Company") Results of Annual General Meeting James Halsted plc (AIM: JHD), a leading manufacturer and international distributor of commercial floor coverings, is pleased to announce that all resolutions proposed at its 109 th Annual General Meeting held earlier today, as set out in the Notice convening the Annual General Meeting, were duly passed. In addition, and further to the Company's announcement on 1 October 2024, the Board confirms that Mr Anthony Wild stood down as the Chairman and as a director following the conclusion of the AGM.
