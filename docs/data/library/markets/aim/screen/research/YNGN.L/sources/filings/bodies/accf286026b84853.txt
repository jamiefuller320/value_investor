Disclaimer* 11 September 2026 YOUNG & CO'S BREWERY PLC ("Young’s", or the "Company") Transaction in Own Shares Young & Co.’s Brewery, P.L.C. (the “Company”) announces that between 28 August and 03 September, in accordance with the terms of its share buyback programme announced by the Company on 08 July 2026 (the “Share Buyback Programme”), it has purchased the following Non-voting ordinary shares of 12.5 pence each (“Non-voting Shares”) and A ordinary shares of 12.5 pence each (“A Shares”) in the Company through Peel Hunt LLP (“Peel Hunt”) on the London Stock Exchange. The purchased Non-voting Shares will be cancelled and the purchased A Shares will be held in Treasury, following which the Company will have 22,487,389 Non-voting Shares and 38,048,005 A Shares in issue (including 178,735 A Shares in Treasury).
