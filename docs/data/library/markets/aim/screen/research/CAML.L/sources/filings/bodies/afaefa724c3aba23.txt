Disclaimer* Central Asia Metals PLC 26 October 2026 26 October 2026 Central Asia Metals PLC (the ‘Group’, the ‘Company’ or ‘CAML’) Application for Admission Central Asia Metals PLC (AIM, TSX: CAML) confirms that application has been made to the London Stock Exchange for 78,474,252 New Ordinary Shares to be admitted to trading on AIM (‘Admission’). The New Ordinary Shares will rank pari passu with the existing issued ordinary shares of the Company. It is expected that Admission will become effective and that trading in the New Ordinary Shares will commence at or around 08:00 (GMT) on 27 October 2026. Total Voting Rights Following Admission, the Company's issued share capital will comprise 256,378,533 ordinary shares of US$0.01 each. The above number includes 193,325 ordinary shares held by the Company in treasury. Accordingly, the total number of voting rights in the Company following Admission is 256,185,208. This figure may be used by shareholders as the denominator for the calculations by which they determine whether they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, the Company under the Financial Conduct Authority's Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules.
