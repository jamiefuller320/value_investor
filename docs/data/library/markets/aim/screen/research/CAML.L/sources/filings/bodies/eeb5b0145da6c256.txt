Disclaimer* Central Asia Metals PLC 13 October 2026 13 October 2026 Central Asia Metals PLC (the ‘Company’ or ‘CAML’) Receipt of Kazakh regulatory approval for the proposed acquisition of Cygnus Metals Central Asia Metals PLC (AIM: CAML) issues a further update regarding the proposed acquisition of Cygnus Metals Limited (ASX:CY5, TSXV: CYG, OTCQB: CYGGF) (‘Cygnus’) by means of an Australian Scheme of Arrangement (the ‘Scheme’) as announced on 2 June 2026 (the ‘Cygnus Transaction’). Under the Scheme, CAML will acquire 100% of Cygnus in an all-share transaction. CAML is pleased to announce receipt of approval from the relevant authorities in Kazakhstan for the issue of the New CAML shares pursuant to the Scheme, which was a condition precedent to the Scheme. Cygnus has advised CAML that it anticipates that the remaining conditions precedent to the Scheme will be satisfied or waived (as applicable) at or immediately prior to the Second Court Hearing. The Second Court Hearing remains scheduled to take place at 14:15 (AWST [1] ) on 14 October 2026. The indicative timetable for completion of the Cygnus Transaction is unchanged from that detailed in CAML’s announcement of 23 September 2026: an Effective Date of 15 October 2026, with the Implementation Date following on 27 October 2026 [2] .
