Director/PDMR Shareholding AI Summary Close X Polar Capital Holdings PLC announced the vesting of its 2023 Long Term Incentive Plan awards for key personnel, including Iain Evans (CEO), Samir Ayub (CFO), Alexander Black (CIO), and Nicholas Farren (COO). Following the vesting on July 16, 2026, a portion of the shares were sold to cover tax liabilities, with the remaining net shares subject to a holding period until July 16, 2028. Specifically, Iain Evans and Alexander Black received 117,773 vested shares each, Samir Ayub received 107,066, and Nicholas Farren received 102,784. These vested shares were acquired at nil cost, and a portion were subsequently sold at £8.82 per share.
