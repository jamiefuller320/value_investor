Director/PDMR Shareholding AI Summary Close X Polar Capital Holdings PLC has announced the grant of conditional awards under its 2026 Executive Incentive Plan to key Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs). The Chief Executive Officer received 117,005 shares, while the Chief Financial Officer, Chief Investment Officer, and Chief Operating Officer each received 69,077, 69,077, and 58,343 shares respectively.
