Disclaimer* Premier Miton Group PLC 15 October 2026 Premier Miton Group plc (‘Premier Miton’, ‘Group’ or the ‘Company’) Q4 AuM update and Notice of results Improving investment performance, lower redemptions and resilient gross sales; further cost savings identified Premier Miton Group plc (AIM: PMI) today provides an update on its unaudited Assets under Management (‘AuM’) as at 30 September 2026, covering the fourth quarter of its financial year (the ‘Quarter’ or ‘Period’). £8.1 billion AuM as at 30 September 2026 (30 September 2025: £10.3 billion) £428 million of net outflows for the Quarter £1.5 million in annualised cost savings identified £26.0 million cash balances at 30 September 2026 Improving investment performance and lower redemption levels provide a more encouraging backdrop for flows Mike O'Shea, Chief Executive Officer, commented: “Assets under Management ended the quarter at £8.1 billion, with net outflows of £428 million. This has undoubtedly been a difficult year for the Group. A period of weaker investment performance across several of our larger strategies has resulted in substantial outflows and put pressure on both AuM and profitability. “We have responded by taking significant steps to simplify the business, reduce our cost base and increase our focus on those investment capabilities where we believe we can deliver attractive long-term investment outcomes for our clients. Following a review over the summer, we have identified a further £1.5 million of annualised cost savings which are expected to be delivered over the next 12 months with an estimated one-off cost of £0.3 million. This will ensure that our operating model is appropriate for the current size of the business while retaining the ability to scale efficiently over time. “Despite the difficult backdrop, there are encouraging signs of greater stability across several of our investment strategies. Investment performance has improved with 74% of our AuM performing above median 4 year to date, 71% over one year and 68% over three years. Following the elevated outflows in July and August, net outflows reduced significantly in September as redemption levels materially reduced across several strategies, while fixed income and macro thematic multi-asset funds continued to see good demand. “While it remains too early to conclude that these trends will be sustained, improving investment performance, lower redemption levels and continued inflows into several of our strategies provide some evidence that the significant headwinds experienced over the past year are beginning to ease. “Our UK equity strategies have performed strongly in the short term, improving their medium-term performance record. We have a strong team of experienced fund managers and believe we are well positioned both to retain existing assets and to benefit when investor demand for UK equities improves. “There are, however, areas where further progress is required. We continue to focus on improving shorter-term performance across our diversified multi-asset strategies. Absolute return has also experienced a challenging period for performance, reflecting a difficult backdrop for long-short strategies. More recent investment performance has been stable, and we retain confidence in the team and its ability to deliver the investment outcomes required to support greater stability in assets. “The changes made to our US strategy have contributed to greater stability in AuM and improved shorter-term performance, while the significant improvement in relative performance from our European equity team is encouraging. Redemption rates from these strategies reduced by 80% and 40% respectively in the final quarter compared with earlier quarters in the financial year. “Taken together these developments provide a more encouraging backdrop as we enter the new financial year. Our distribution team continues to perform well with gross sales of over £2.7 billion for the seventh consecutive year. This demonstrates continued demand across our product range, although elevated redemptions have more than offset these sales during the year. If the recent improvement in investment performance is sustained and redemption levels continue to moderate, we believe there is a clear opportunity to stabilise net flows and, over time, return the business to growth. “There is clearly more work to do, and sustained investment performance will be required to achieve a more stable flow environment. However, we enter the new financial year with a leaner and more focussed business, improving investment performance in key capabilities and identifiable areas for organic growth, both in our home market and internationally. “At the same time, we continue to evaluate strategic opportunities that have the potential to enhance shareholder value. We have a strong investment culture and a well-respected distribution capability that can make us an attractive home for talented investment teams. Our strong balance sheet and position as a listed business provide us with strategic and financial flexibility to pursue inorganic opportunities that can add scale, enhance our investment capabilities and broaden our access to attractive client segments. “Our priorities remain clear: delivering strong investment outcomes for our clients, stabilising AuM and maintaining an efficient and effective operating platform. We believe that continued progress in these areas will improve profitability and deliver long-term value for shareholders.” Assets under Management: On 30 September 2026, our AuM stood at £8.1 billion.
