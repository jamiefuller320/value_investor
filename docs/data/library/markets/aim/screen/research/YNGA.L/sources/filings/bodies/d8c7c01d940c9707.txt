(the “Company”) announces that between 07 August and 13 August, in accordance with the terms of its share buyback programme announced by the Company on 08 July 2026 (the “Share Buyback Programme”), it has purchased the following Non-voting ordinary shares of 12.5 pence each (“Non-voting Shares”) and A ordinary shares of 12.5 pence each (“A Shares”) in the Company through Peel Hunt LLP (“Peel Hunt”) on the London Stock Exchange.
