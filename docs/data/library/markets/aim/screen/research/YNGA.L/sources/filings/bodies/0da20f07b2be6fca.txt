(the " Company ") Block Admission Application The Company confirms that a new block admission application (the " New Block Admission Application ") has been made to the London Stock Exchange (“LSE”) for 1,000,000 A ordinary shares of £0.125 each in the capital of the Company (the " New A Ordinary Shares ") to be admitted to trading on the LSE’s main market for listed securities. The New A Ordinary Shares will, upon issue, rank equally with all existing A ordinary shares of £0.125 each in the capital of the Company (" A Ordinary Shares ") that are then in issue. The New A Ordinary Shares will be issued by the Company from time to time under the terms of the following plans: the Young & Co.’s Brewery, P.L.C. Deferred Annual Bonus Scheme 2022; the Young & Co.’s Brewery, P.L.C. Long Term Incentive Plan 2022; and the Young & Co.’s Brewery, P.L.C. Sharesave Plan 2022 It is expected that admission of the New A Ordinary Shares will become effective on 28 May 2026.
