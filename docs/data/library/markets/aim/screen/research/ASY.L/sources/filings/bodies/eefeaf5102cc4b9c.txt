Result of AGM Andrews Sykes Group PLC 14 June 2023 14 June 2023 ANDREWS SYKES GROUP PLC ("Andrews Sykes" or the "Company") Result of AGM Andrews Sykes is pleased to announce that at its Annual General Meeting (AGM), held today, all the resolutions as set out in the Company's Notice of AGM were duly passed. The final dividend of 14.0 pence per ordinary share of 1 pence each ("Ordinary Share") will be paid, as proposed, on 16 June 2023. The table below sets out the proxy voting results.
