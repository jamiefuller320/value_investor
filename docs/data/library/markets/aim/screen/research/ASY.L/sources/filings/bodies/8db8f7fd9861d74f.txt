Result of AGM Andrews Sykes Group PLC 17 June 2025 17 June 2025 ANDREWS SYKES GROUP PLC ("Andrews Sykes" or the "Company") Result of AGM Andrews Sykes is pleased to announce that at its Annual General Meeting (AGM), held today, all the resolutions as set out in the Company's Notice of AGM were duly passed.
