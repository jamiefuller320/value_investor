Posting of Annual Report and Notice of AGM Andrews Sykes Group PLC 13 May 2024 13 May 2024 Andrews Sykes Group PLC ("Andrews Sykes" or the "Company") Posting of Annual Report and Notice of AGM Andrews Sykes announces that its Report and Accounts for the financial year ended 31 December 2023 is being posted to applicable shareholders today along with notice (the "Notice") of the Company's Annual General Meeting (the "AGM"). Both documents are also being made available on the Company's website at www.andrews-sykes.com . The AGM will be held at 3.00 p.m. on Tuesday, 18 June 2024 at Unit 5, Peninsular Park Road, London, SE7 7TZ. Shareholders are invited to attend in person and should see the Notice for more information.
