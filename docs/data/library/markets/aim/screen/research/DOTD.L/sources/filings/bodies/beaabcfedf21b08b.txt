Disclaimer* dotDigital Group plc 05 November 2026 5 November 2026 Dotdigital Group plc ("Dotdigital" or the "Group") Final Dividend Timetable Dotdigital (AIM: DOTD), the provider of a leading AI-powered marketing platform for customer acquisition, engagement, retention and advocacy, announced on 3 November 2026 the Board's proposal to pay a final dividend totalling 1.33p per share in respect of the year ended 30 June 2026. This will be presented to shareholders for approval at the Annual General Meeting of the Company due to be held in December 2026. If approved, the dividend will be paid on 29 January 2027 to shareholders on the register of members on 8 January 2027, the ex-dividend date will be 7 January 2027.
