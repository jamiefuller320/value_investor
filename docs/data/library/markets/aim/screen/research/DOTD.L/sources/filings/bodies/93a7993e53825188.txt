John Conoley Non-Executive Chairman 3 November 2026 Chief Executive Officer's Report and Financial Review Overview Dotdigital delivered FY26 in line with market expectations, ending the year a stronger, more diversified business. Group Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) rose 18% to £85.4m, with organic ARR growth improving to 8% from 6% at the half year, driven by strong growth in Alia and Social Snowball during their periods of ownership. Recurring subscription revenue increased across both acquisitions and the Dotdigital Marketing platform, strengthening the Group's revenue mix and providing a larger recurring-revenue base entering FY27. Over the past three years, Dotdigital has evolved from a single-product, email-led offering into a diversified, multi-product group serving the full customer lifecycle - acquisition, engagement, retention, loyalty and advocacy. Recent acquisitions, product investment and integration have brought these capabilities together in a more connected proposition, underpinned by shared data and AI. The priority now is to accelerate growth by bringing the expanded product portfolio to the Group's base of around 9,800 brands, extending its reach through new integrations and scaling internationally. To capture this opportunity, we have moved to an integrated global go-to-market model, supported by stronger leadership, systems and processes. Gross retention improved in FY26 through more active management of at-risk customers and better CRM data. Since the year end, we have broadened this work through clearer accountability, deeper customer insight and earlier intervention, while maintaining discipline over costs and capital allocation. We have also begun to establish the expanded cross-sell model. Alia and Social Snowball are now integrated with the Dotdigital Marketing platform at product and data level, while retaining their own brands and go-to-market models. Alia has been made available beyond Shopify to all customers across our wider base. From September 2026, our revenue teams have been able to sell Alia, supported by new incentives and dedicated senior commercial leadership. We expect the contribution from cross-sell to build through FY27. Product development continued at pace, strengthening the Group's ability to turn customer data into more timely, personalized and measurable marketing. We advanced our AI capabilities through the launch of Dotdigital's MCP server and the development of our Agents programme, which attracted strong customer engagement. Continued investment in the platform's shared data foundation, the post-year-end launch of Dotdigital Loyalty and growing WhatsApp adoption further broadened the Dotdigital Marketing platform. Together, these developments have strengthened the Group's ability to deepen customer relationships, extend adoption across the product set and support more scalable growth through FY27. Results Dotdigital's financial model combines high-margin recurring software revenue with recurring usage-based fees and a smaller contribution from one-off revenues such as professional services. Growth comes from winning new customers, increasing product adoption and usage, improving retention and cross-selling the Group's broader customer-lifecycle proposition. Group revenue increased 8% to £90.9m (FY25: £83.9m), or 9% on a constant-currency basis, with contracted recurring revenue up 14% to £76.8m (FY25: £67.3m) and representing 84% of the total. Growth was led by strong contributions from Alia and Social Snowball, alongside more modest growth in the Dotdigital Marketing platform, where contracted recurring revenue increased 3% to £69.4m. This was partly offset by the expected reduction in revenue from the low-margin Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) business, following the planned exit from a non-core contract. Softer new business and longer customer decision cycles also reduced one-off project revenue. The resulting shift towards higher-quality subscription revenue, including the acquisitions, supported an improvement in Group gross margin to 80% (FY25: 79%). Forward-looking contracted ARR increased 18% to £85.4m (FY25: £72.6m), or 8% organically, an improvement on the 6% reported at the half year. Alia's ARR increased 45% in less than four months following its acquisition, while Social Snowball's rose 33% in its first full year of ownership. Together, Alia and Social Snowball now account for around 18% of Group ARR (FY25: 6%), with their faster growth increasing their contribution to the Group's overall growth. Gross retention in the Dotdigital Marketing platform improved by one percentage point to 85% in FY26, supported by more active management of at-risk customers, better CRM data and more consistent retention processes. Average monthly recurring revenue per customer (ARPC) increased across all three brands. ARPC in the Dotdigital Marketing platform rose 6% to £1,943 (FY25: £1,829), while Social Snowball's increased 35% to US$327 (FY25: US$243). Alia's ARPC increased at an annualised rate of 16% to US$261 from US$248 at acquisition in March. The Group maintained its adjusted EBITDA margin despite the pace of acquisition activity over the past two years. Adjusted EBITDA was £29.4m (FY25: £26.8m), representing a margin of 32%, while adjusted profit before tax was £19.4m (FY25: £19.0m) and adjusted diluted EPS was 4.94p (FY25: 4.80p). Adjusted EBITDA grew faster than adjusted profit before tax, principally reflecting lower interest income as interest rates reduced and cash was deployed into acquisitions to support the Group's longer-term growth and earnings. As planned, margins improved in the second half as the investment made in Social Snowball during H1 began to deliver improved returns. Cash at 30 June 2026 was £17.1m (30 June 2025: £36.2m), after funding the initial US$30m consideration for Alia. The Group continued to generate cash from its underlying operations, including the acquired businesses. This cash will be used to fund anticipated earn-out payments relating to the Alia and Social Snowball acquisitions over the next couple of years. As these commitments reduce, the Group expects cash balances to rebuild. The Board is proposing a final dividend of 1.33p per share (FY25: 1.21p), subject to shareholder approval, in line with the Group's progressive dividend policy. Our market Dotdigital's broader proposition positions it well to serve brands seeking to manage more of the customer lifecycle with one provider. Several structural trends continue to support demand for this proposition. Brands are consolidating their marketing technology and favouring platforms that can replace multiple point solutions, while marketers increasingly expect embedded AI that uses first-party data to identify opportunities, accelerate execution and demonstrate measurable returns. As paid acquisition becomes more expensive, investment is shifting towards owned channels, supported by unified customer data. Buyers also place greater emphasis on rapid time to value, integration with existing systems, and trusted platforms that give them control over their data and the use of AI. Despite these long-term drivers, mixed macroeconomic conditions in FY26 lengthened buying decision cycles among our traditional customer cohort and increased the number of stakeholders involved, while many mid-sized businesses paused to assess how AI fits within their technology strategies. This weighed on new customer wins and one-off professional services activity. These conditions reinforced the importance of our work during the year to sharpen our sales and marketing efforts on customer segments whose needs are best served by Dotdigital's broad platform functionality, supporting a stronger position from which to capture a wider growth opportunity. Business review Our offering Dotdigital helps around 9,800 brands in 150 countries turn customer data into measurable growth. Alia and Social Snowball support customer acquisition; email, SMS and WhatsApp enable engagement; and Dotdigital Personalization (formerly Fresh Relevance) and Dotdigital Loyalty support conversion, retention and loyalty. These products connect through the Dotdigital Marketing platform, giving customers a broader set of capabilities as their needs develop. Underpinning this offering is Dotdigital's established AI capability, formerly known as WinstonAI. This is evolving from assisting marketers with content, optimisation and insights towards agents that can identify opportunities and execute campaigns, while keeping the marketer in control. This combination of a broader product set, shared data foundation and AI capability enables the Group to address two complementary segments of the market through propositions and go-to-market models tailored to their needs: · The integrated Dotdigital Marketing platform, which serves around 4,000 established mid-market and enterprise brands with more complex requirements, bringing together marketing automation, personalization, loyalty and communications, alongside the capabilities of Alia and Social Snowball, on a common data foundation, supported by deep integrations and long-standing customer relationships. The Group has sharpened its ideal customer profile to focus on relationship-led brands: established mid-market businesses that grow through ongoing, identifiable customer relationships and need to orchestrate complex data across multiple channels. · Product-led solutions, currently Alia and Social Snowball, which serve smaller and lower mid-market ecommerce brands through focused, product-led propositions. They retain their own brands and partner-driven go-to-market models, with particular strength in the Shopify ecosystem. Together, these two propositions broaden the Group's addressable market while preserving the specialist products and go-to-market approaches that work for each customer segment. Alia and Social Snowball provide entry points and additional capabilities for relationship-led customers, while product and data integration creates opportunities to offer complementary products together and cross-sell more of the Group's offering over time. M&A Strategy: disciplined execution On 3 March 2026, we acquired Alia, an AI-native list-growth and on-site capture platform built for Shopify merchants, for an initial cash consideration of US$30m, with total consideration of up to US$60m dependent on future performance. Alia has been a standout performer, with ARR increasing 45% organically in less than four months, from £6.9m at acquisition to £10.0m at 30 June 2026. It contributed £2.7m of recurring revenue between acquisition and the year end. Social Snowball, our influencer, affiliate and referral marketing platform acquired in June 2025, grew ARR 33% from £4.1m to £5.4m in its first full year of ownership. Growth accelerated through the year, with an annualized growth rate of 30% in H1 and 36% in H2, helped by the go-to-market investment we made in the first half and the launch of CreatorSearch. Meeting the ARR growth hurdle of approximately 25%, together with the required profitability targets, resulted in first-year deferred consideration of US$0.7m being paid in early FY27. Cross-sell is now moving from integration to commercial execution. A referral scheme between Social Snowball and Alia was established in early FY27 and is showing early success. Alia is now also available to the full Dotdigital customer base following its expansion beyond Shopify. Acquisitions remain part of our strategy as we continue to assess the landscape for viable, commercially-ready new technologies. With our current product portfolio, we continue to be selective, and any further acquisitions will be judged on strategic fit, integration potential and value. Financial Review Business Model We sell access to the Dotdigital marketing platform and messaging services (email, SMS, MMS, WhatsApp and push notifications) on contracts of one to three years, priced according to the modules selected and contact, data, page-view and message volumes. Revenue is recognised evenly over the contract life under IFRS 15, with customers able to upgrade during term as usage grows. Revenue from Social Snowball, our influencer, affiliate and referral marketing platform, and Alia, our AI-native list-growth and on-site capture platform, is recognized in the same way as our other software revenue, although the vast majority of their contracts are currently month to month. These products broaden our addressable market and increase cross-sell potential, with a margin profile consistent with the Group. Revenue from professional services accounts for well under 5% of Group revenue and is recognized as the services are delivered. Revenue and gross margin Total revenue increased 8% in reported currency, or 9% at constant currency, to £90.9m (FY25: £83.9m). This included a full-year contribution from Social Snowball (FY25: five days) and just under four months from Alia (FY25: nil). Excluding acquisitions, this total Group revenue, which includes the non-core, low margin transactional messaging business, was flat, principally reflecting two previously announced factors: the exit of a contract in the low margin transactional messaging CPaaS business; and the unwinding of a strong H1 FY25 comparator for non-recurring core marketing platform revenue, both of which are now fully reflected in the FY26 base for comparison in FY27. Recognised recurring revenue from the marketing platform, including Alia and Social Snowball, increased 14% in reported currency, or 15% at constant currency, to £76.8m (FY25: £67.3m). Repeating revenue from the low-margin transactional messaging business, CPaaS, was £10.3m (FY25: £12.0m). Together, recurring and repeating revenue represented 96% of Group revenue (FY25: 94%). By region (local currency): EMEA £63.4m (+3%); North America US$25.0m (+37%); APAC A$17.4m (+5%). International revenue (from outside the UK) represented 37% of Group revenue (FY25: 33%). Gross profit was £72.4m (FY25: £66.6m), with a gross margin of 80%, up one percentage point on the prior year. The improvement reflects the continued shift towards the higher-margin marketing platform business, including the acquisitions. This business operates at gross margins of around 90%, compared with around 15% for the transactional CPaaS business. R&D (RDEC) credit & tax Since FY25, the UK R&D incentive has been presented in Other income under the Research and Development Expenditure Credit (RDEC) regime rather than as a tax credit. EARNINGS PER SHARE Earnings per share data is based on the consolidated profit and the weighted average number of shares in issue of the parent Company. Basic earnings per share are calculated by dividing the earnings attributable to ordinary shareholders by the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the year, excluding ordinary shares held in treasury. Diluted earnings per share is calculated using the weighted average number of shares adjusted to assume the conversion of all dilutive potential ordinary shares. Adjusted earnings per share is based on the consolidated profit deducting exceptional costs, share based payment, gain on contingent consideration, foreign exchange on intercompany loans and amortisation of acquired intangibles. A number of non-IFRS adjusted profit measures are used in the annual report and financial statements and in these financial statements. Adjusting items are excluded from our headline performance measures by virtue of their size and nature, in order to reflect management's view of the performance of the Group.
