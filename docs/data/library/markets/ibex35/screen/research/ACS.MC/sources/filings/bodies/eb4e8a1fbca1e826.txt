The English version of the annual accounts for those entities who are obliged to submit them in Spanish has been translated by the actual entity and should therefore not be considered, under any circumstances, as official. Although issuers should make every effort to maintain consistency with the Spanish version and fully translate all the documentation included in the original version, should the entity not sent all the information in English, we kindly advise you that the only official information and the one that thus prevails shall be the one in Spanish.
