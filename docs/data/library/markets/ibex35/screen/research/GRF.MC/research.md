# Grifols, S.A. (GRF.MC) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-26T17:40:18.894996+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Grifols is a global plasma-derived therapies and diagnostics group whose shares screen as a value candidate on cheapness, free cash flow, and dividend metrics, but with weak quality and financial-health scores. The investment case rests on earnings recovery (Yahoo: net income €402m in FY2025 vs €157m in FY2024), solid operating cash generation, and a reasonable headline P/E of 15.8, against a backdrop of elevated leverage (~€7.7bn net debt), negative tangible book value, and persistent governance/regulatory overhang. The central debate is whether operational improvement and strategic actions (US Biopharma IPO evaluation, debt refinancing, Biotest consolidation) will unlock value, or whether accounting scrutiny, dilution from a proposed capital increase, and heavy interest costs will keep the stock depressed. Primary annual and interim filing bodies are absent from the research corpus; financial trends below rely on Yahoo fallback data and CNMV notice headlines only.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The quantitative screen rates Grifols a **buy**, passing four models across three families: **cheapness** (FCF Yield, Magic Formula), **dividend** (Dividend Growth), and **risk** (Earnings Quality). Composite score is 62% (sector-relative 60%), with full data quality (20/20 metrics) but low timing conviction (23%, signal new for one week). Key screen metrics include P/E 15.8, P/B 1.2, FCF yield 13.5%, and reported dividend yield 85.0% — the latter is almost certainly a data artefact (Yahoo shows €128m cash dividends paid in FY2025, not an 85% recurring yield). FCF of ~€887m per the screen aligns with Yahoo FY2025 free cash flow of €624m after capex.

For a value investor, the hook is a cyclically depressed plasma/biopharma franchise trading below historical multiples after a multi-year de-rating driven by Gotham City Research allegations, CNMV investigation, and balance-sheet concerns. Operating fundamentals appear to be stabilising: Yahoo shows revenue rising from €6.6bn (2023) to €7.5bn (2025), operating income above €1.2bn, and FCF turning consistently positive (€624m in 2025 vs −€93m in 2023). The screen’s Earnings Quality pass and positive operating cash flow vs net income (Piotroski component) suggest reported earnings are cash-backed, albeit with a middling Piotroski F-Score of 6/9 (leverage not declining, current ratio and gross margin not improving).

Business quality is harder to endorse: the screen fails Financial Health, Graham Defensive/Enterprising, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, and Composite Value. ROE is only 6.1%. Plasma collection is capital-intensive and regulated; Grifols competes with CSL, Takeda, and Octapharma. The dual-listing (BME:GRF, NASDAQ:GRFS) and family-controlled structure add governance complexity. Net-net, the buy case is **valuation-led recovery in a leveraged, event-driven name**, not a high-quality compounder.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Leverage and interest burden:** Net debt ~€7.7bn and interest expense ~€576m (FY2025 Yahoo) represent the primary financial risk. The screen fails Financial Health and Piotroski’s “leverage declining” test. Refinancing 2027 maturities reduces near-term rollover risk but leaves the capital structure elevated.

**Governance and regulatory:** CNMV initiated an administrative disciplinary proceeding (inside-information notice, 25 September 2024). Trading was suspended in July 2024 and subsequently lifted. These events followed Gotham City Research short-report allegations on accounting and related-party transactions; litigation between Grifols and Gotham continued through 2025 (news: “La batalla judicial entre Grifols y Gotham vive un nuevo episodio”, 5 March 2025). This is a material red flag not captured by quantitative screens.

**Negative tangible equity:** Goodwill/intangibles of ~€9.6bn drive tangible book to −€4.3bn. Any impairment would directly hit equity and covenant headroom.

**Dilution risk:** News (15 May 2025: “Grifols propone ampliar capital social en un 50% en junta de accionistas”) flags a proposed 50% capital increase, which would dilute existing holders if executed.

**Short interest:** CNMV short-position register (body extract, 4 June 2025) shows Kintbury Capital LLP with a 1.99% net short position (position date 20 July 2026 in the extract). Elevated short interest aligns with scepticism on debt reduction and accounting quality (news: “Acciones de Grifols siguen bajo presión mientras el mercado duda de su capacidad para reducir deuda”, 26 May 2025).

**Operational/legal:** News (9 April 2025: “Dos muertes y una demanda: el escándalo que sacude a las acciones de Grifols”) references patient-death-related litigation, adding reputational and contingent-liability risk. Plasma supply constraints and albumin/alpha-1 volume softness were cited in analyst downgrades (Deutsche Bank target cut, May 2025).

**Screen failures:** No pass on Piotroski (6/9), Graham, Quality Value, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, or High Dividend Yield — confirming the name is cheap on cash-flow metrics but not on holistic quality.
