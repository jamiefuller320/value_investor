WYNNSTAY PROPERTIES PLC ("Wynnstay" or "the Company") Trading Update 28 April 2023 Wynnstay Properties Plc (AIM:WSP) is pleased to provide a trading update ahead of the announcement of its audited results for the financial year ended 25 March 2023 ("Financial Year"): o Wynnstay's portfolio, which comprises exclusively freehold properties, was valued as at 25 March 2023 by BNP Paribas Real Estate, Chartered Surveyors, acting in the capacity of external valuers. The valuation was undertaken on the basis of Fair Value in accordance with the requirements of IFRS 13 and the RICS Valuation - Global Standards 2021, effective 31 January 2022. o The valuers report that the total aggregate Fair Value of Wynnstay's portfolio was £39,320,000 (FY 2022: £38,975,000), which represents an increase of £345,000, or 0.9 per cent . o Rental income in the Financial Year, on a like-for-like basis, increased by 10.4 per cent. to £2,304,000 (2022 adjusted: £2,087,000 * ). o Collection of rental income exceeded 99.7 per cent. for the Financial Year. o The portfolio was fully let, with no voids, at the end of the Financial Year. Net debt at the end of the Financial Year was £6,683,000 (2022: £6,447,000). *2022 rental income of £2,252,000 adjusted to exclude income from the Surbiton property sold in the prior year. Wynnstay expects to announce its results for the Financial Year and to publish its Annual Report and Financial Statements, as usual, in mid-June 2023. As previously announced the Company will hold its Annual General Meeting on 18 July 2023.
