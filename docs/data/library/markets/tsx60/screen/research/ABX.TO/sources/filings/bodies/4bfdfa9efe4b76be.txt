(NYSE:IAUX) (TSX: IAU ) ("i-80 Gold" or the "Company") reports its financial and operating results, as well as development highlights, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. "During the third quarter, we continued to advance i-80 Gold's hub-and-spoke mining and processing platform which is expected to improve our cost structure and cash generation, and increase annual production to a target of 150,000 to 200,000 ounces of gold in 2028 (1) ," said Richard Young, President & Chief Executive Officer. "Granite Creek underground announced maiden mineral reserves, Archimedes underground continued to advance on schedule from a September 2025 construction decision toward planned production mining before year-end 2026, and key milestones and cost to complete the Lone Tree Plant refurbishment continued to track the original engineering schedule and cost estimate. With Mineral Point open pit now prioritized as our next major development project, drilling and technical work are advancing toward a pre-feasibility study to support targeted annual gold equivalent production of 400,000 to 500,000 ounces in the early 2030s. (1) Together, these milestones build the foundation for i-80 Gold to deliver substantial long-term value for our shareholders." THIRD QUARTER HIGHLIGHTS Three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to three months ended September 30, 2025. Unless otherwise stated, all amounts referred to herein are in U.S. dollars . Development Published a feasibility study for Granite Creek underground declaring initial Mineral Reserves of 2.20 million tonnes grading 7.87 grams per tonne gold containing 556,500 ounces of gold. Measured and Indicated underground Mineral Resources increased by 229% to 3.73 million tonnes grading 7.17 grams per tonne gold containing 859,500 ounces of gold, compared to the 2025 preliminary economic assessment; (2) Inferred Mineral Resources decreased by 38% to 0.89 million tonnes grading 7.06 grams per tonne gold containing 202,800 ounces of gold as the infill program converted a portion of Inferred Mineral Resources into the higher classification categories. (3) Granite Creek underground development continued to expand access to high-grade headings , supporting the ongoing production ramp up. The project is expected to be within its full-year production guidance range. Archimedes underground advanced on schedule and largely on budget , with main decline development on track and construction of the second ventilation and escape raise underway. Surface and underground infrastructure required to support the start of production mining remains scheduled for completion in early November, ahead of the planned start of production mining by year-end. Lone Tree Plant refurbishment advanced on schedule and largely on budget with bulk demolition activities complete and mobilization underway for initial construction activities to commence in the fourth quarter of 2026, as planned. Project capital remains largely on budget with approximately 44% of project capital having been committed and approximately 20% of the contingency having been drawn. Completed approximately 36,000 meters of drilling for the quarter bringing the total to approximately 62,000 meters for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Activities primarily focused on technical drilling at Mineral Point open pit and infill drilling at Archimedes underground to enhance resource definition in support of the planned 2027 technical studies, as well as resource definition drilling at Granite Creek underground beyond the area included in the September 2026 feasibility study. (2) Permitting remained largely on track across the development portfolio as associated actions continued to advance. Financial and Operating Revenues were $32.7 million, representing 7,744 ounces in gold sold (4) at an average realized gold price (5) of $4,211 per ounce, compared to $32.0 million represented by 9,368 ounces at an average realized gold price (5) of $3,412 per ounce in the prior year period. Gold production was marginally lower at 11,685 ounces compared with 12,428 ounces in the prior year period. Consolidated production is expected to be within the lower half of the guidance range. Gross profit increased to $5.0 million from $3.1 million in the prior year period due to a higher realized gold price. Net loss increased to $48.4 million compared to $41.9 million in the prior year period, due primarily to higher pre-development, evaluation and exploration costs incurred as the Company advances multiple projects within its development plan. The higher costs were related to drilling programs at the Ruby Hill property partially offset by lower revaluation expenses recorded within other expenses. In September 2026, the company issued the Granite Creek Feasibility Study (2) which declared initial mineral reserves. Accordingly, the Company will capitalize qualifying development expenditures at Granite Creek, which were previously expensed as pre-development, evaluation and exploration costs. Net loss per share of $0.06 is comparable to the prior year period of $0.05. Cash and cash equivalents were $411.4 million as of September 30, 2026, a decrease of $53.2 million compared to June 30, 2026, primarily due to cash used in operations of $32.5 million and capital expenditures of $15.0 million primarily driven by the start of the Lone Tree Plant refurbishment project. Sustainability Published the Company's 2025 and fourth annual sustainability report and data book which highlights key achievements in community partnerships, environmental stewardship, and governance in 2025, and reports on continued progress in expanding its sustainability strategy. Coordinated a roundtable with U.S. Senator Jacky Rosen, Eureka County leaders, and state and regional partners in Eureka , the neighboring community to the Ruby Hill property, which hosts the Archimedes and Mineral Point projects. UPDATED DEVELOPMENT STRATEGY i-80 Gold is executing a multi-phase development plan aimed at creating a mid-tier gold producer in Nevada. The near-term focus is on developing two high-grade underground refractory gold projects (Granite Creek and Archimedes), and the refurbishment and commissioning of the Lone Tree Plant, which is expected to serve as a central processing hub for material from all underground projects. Development of the Company's two large, open pit oxide projects (Mineral Point and Granite Creek), as well as its third underground mine (Cove), are expected to follow to support a long-term target of 500,000 to 600,000 (1) ounces of annual gold output. Permitting, technical studies, development work and ramp up are actively being advanced across the portfolio of five gold projects. On October 14, 2026, the Company updated its development plan to better align plant feed and processing capacity, and optimize capital allocation. While the Company's operating and near-term development projects remain on their initial schedule, the sequence of medium-to-long term projects has been updated as follows: Establish hub-and-spoke mining and processing platform : Ramp up production at Granite Creek underground, bring Archimedes underground into production, and refurbish and commission the Lone Tree Plant as a centralized processing facility -- which remains consistent with previously forecasted plans. Prioritize Mineral Point open pit as the next major development project : Add a large-scale open pit heap leach project. Advance remaining growth project s: Develop and construct the Granite Creek open pit once the Mineral Point open pit project is in operation with the Cove underground project sequenced later as an important source of future refractory feed to the Lone Tree Plant when future mining rates decline at the first two underground mines. This revised sequence of development projects provides an opportunity for the Company to prioritize the Mineral Point open pit project, which is the Company's largest and most valuable asset. Currently, the anticipated timing of Mineral Point development has not changed from its March 2025 PEA. The exploration program, early-stage pre-permitting activities, technical studies, metallurgical test work, and engineering are ongoing and continue to improve the Company's understanding of the deposit to support future project planning. The development plan targets annual gold production of 150,000 to 200,000 ounces beginning in 2028, supported by the Lone Tree Plant processing material from Granite Creek underground and Archimedes underground. (1) Cove underground is expected to sustain this production level by supplying refractory feed to the Lone Tree Plant as production from Granite Creek underground and Archimedes underground declines. In addition to the quarter end cash balance of $411 million, additional liquidity is available through: (i) the remaining $25 million from the committed net smelter return royalty financing arrangement (the "NSR Royalty"); (ii) the $100 million accordion feature under the 2026 Gold Prepay – each of which are subject to certain specified project and drawdown conditions; (iii) future anticipated proceeds from warrant exercises which are due to mature in 2027 and 2028; (iv) and a proposed ore stockpile financing facility which has been approved as an additional permitting financing facility under the 2026 Gold Prepay. Together, these sources could provide up to approximately $350 million of additional liquidity over the next 18 months. Other than the remaining $25 million under the NSR Royalty, each of these sources is subject to lender approval, the negotiation of definitive agreements or the exercise of outstanding warrants by their holders, as applicable. To support Mineral Point's development scope, the Company is in dialogue with its lead lenders to establish a corporate revolving credit facility for additional liquidity, if needed. Parallel to the updated development plan, the Company has optimized its processing strategy where a large portion of refractory material from the Granite Creek and Archimedes underground projects is expected to be stockpiled in 2027 for processing at the Company's wholly owned Lone Tree Plant once commissioned, rather than processed through a third-party facility. Commissioning is expected in the fourth quarter of 2027. The Company's expectations for 2026 have changed from that guidance as follows: Gold production is expected to be in the lower half of the guidance range. This is in part due to the decision to leach Archimedes oxide material on our existing leach pad rather than selling it under the ore purchase agreement, which is expected to delay recording production from the fourth quarter of 2026 to the first quarter of 2027. Operating costs are expected to be at the low end of the range due to gold production being in the lower half of the range and the mining of more oxide material than planned, at lower processing costs, from Granite Creek. Sustaining capital, growth capital and pre-development expense are all anticipated to be at the low end of the guidance range. The Lone Tree Plant refurbishment is on schedule, however, capital expenditures for 2026 are expected to be lower than guidance. Management was conservative in estimating these expenditures during the recapitalization process earlier in year to ensure the Company raised sufficient capital. The original guidance assumed a gap between commitment and invoicing/payment of 30 days, in practice, the Company is experiencing between four to five months for this timing difference. The Company continues to expect the refurbishment capital to be nearly fully committed by year-end. Exploration expenses are expected to be lower than planned for 2026, reflecting fewer meters drilled due to previously reported contractor personnel shortages and limited drill rig availability at Archimedes and Mineral Point. During the third quarter, drill rigs have since arrived on site and staffing levels have increased, addressing these constraints. Granite Creek Underground Project Mining & Processing Ore mined at Granite Creek underground is currently processed as follows: (i) sulfide ore is processed at a third-party processing facility and subject to a toll milling agreement entered into in March 2025; (ii) high-grade and low-grade oxide ore is subject to an ore purchase agreement. Low-grade oxide ore was sold under the ore purchase agreement beginning in the first quarter of 2026; and (iii) residual leaching of low-grade oxide material previously placed on a segregated section of the Company's Lone Tree heap leach facility continues with no additional ore having been placed after the first quarter of 2026. The Company continued mining in the Ogee, upper South Pacific, and Otto zones at Granite Creek underground. During the three months ended September 30, 2026, mining activities were impacted by employee turnover at its mining contractor as a result of increased competition for skilled underground labor in the region, which reduced ore and waste mining rates and delayed access to several high-grade headings. In response, to strengthen workforce retention and support planned mining rates, the Company implemented a competitive retention incentive program for its mining contractor personnel. The mining contractor has since increased staffing to align with the mining rates required through the balance of 2026. Horizontal development remains on track and, as part of the mine plan optimization, vertical development of the ventilation raise has been postponed to late 2026 or early 2027. The Company expects full-year production for the project to be within its guidance range. On September 21, 2026, the Company announced the results of a feasibility study for Granite Creek underground supported by two technical reports: (1) a technical report prepared under National Instrument 43-101 - Standards of Disclosure for Mineral projects which, in the opinion of the qualified persons, constitutes a feasibility study under NI 43-101, and (2) a technical report summary prepared under Subpart 1300 of Regulation S-K ("S-K 1300"), which, in the opinion of the qualified persons, constitutes a pre-feasibility study under S-K 1300. (the "Granite Creek Underground Feasibility Study"). The Granite Creek Underground Feasibility Study declared maiden mineral reserves at the project. Accordingly, qualifying development expenditures at Granite Creek incurred after the declaration of reserves, which were previously expensed as pre-development, evaluation and exploration costs, will be capitalized in future periods. Water inflow volumes to the mine remained largely unchanged and continue to be managed well using the current underground pumping system and expanded sump. Work on an enhanced pumping system, including expanded sumps at lower levels and higher-capacity pumps, advanced during the quarter. Power and discharge lines were completed for the two completed dewatering wells, with transformers and assemblies expected to be installed during the fourth quarter of 2026. The second water treatment plant is fully operational and expected to increase water flows towards its capacity of approximately 3,500 gallons per minute with the commissioning of the two new dewatering wells in the fourth quarter. The water is being treated to potable drinking standards and discharged to the rapid infiltration basins. At September 30, 2026, the Company had in-process material containing over 6,200 recoverable gold ounces at its third-party processing facility. The Company expects over half of the material at the third-party facility to be processed by the end of 2026. Approximately 4,100 ounces of gold was also held in inventory at September 30, 2026. Gold ounces sold were lower than the prior year periods due to timing of settlements. Mining cost per tonne has increased due to higher contractor costs and increased labor to support the mine, as well as lower tonnes produced. Processing cost per unit has increased compared to the prior year periods due to a higher proportion of sulfide material being processed at the third-party processing facility. During the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, growth capital expenditures were primarily related to the water treatment plant project. Sustaining capital expenditures were related primarily to the electrical substation replacement. Pre-development, evaluation, and exploration expenses were $5.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, which were related to underground mine development and infill drilling to upgrade mineral resources and step-out drilling. Drill Program During the quarter, the Company continued infill and step-out drilling at Granite Creek. The underground drill program remained focused on infill drilling to support resource conversion and mine planning. Drilling progressed slower than anticipated during the quarter due to water management and insufficient contractor personnel to maintain continuous operations. Ongoing 2026 infill and step-out drilling supports continued reserve development and resource growth in the South Pacific, Rangefront, Otto and Ogee zones, with the high-grade portion of the CX Zone targeted for further evaluation as part of an anticipated extensive multi-year drill program planned to commence in 2027. The Granite Creek Underground Feasibility Study outlined several improvements relative to the Granite Creek 2025 PEA, including an optimized mine design, improved geological definition, increased mining flexibility and additional working faces, particularly within the Ogee Zone. Successful infill and definition drilling within the South Pacific Zone closed gaps in the mineralization, improved continuity and upgraded existing mineral resources to higher-confidence categories. These results supported a larger mineable inventory and an 8.5-year mine life after more than a year of mining. Improved geological definition also increased the number of available ore faces and total planned ore mined, supporting higher planned production and reduced waste development requirements. Ongoing infrastructure upgrades, including increased water treatment and dewatering capacity and ventilation optimization, further support the mine plan. The Granite Creek Underground Feasibility study also incorporated an optimized processing strategy which includes stockpiling Granite Creek's sulfide and oxide ore ahead of the planned commissioning of the Lone Tree Plant. This strategy is expected to increase the amount of Granite Creek ore processed at the Lone Tree Plant at more favorable processing costs, while reducing reliance on third-party processing. Pre-development, evaluation and exploration expenses were higher than the prior year periods primarily due to underground development at the Archimedes underground project which began in the third quarter of 2025. Development during the quarter focused on advancement of the main ramp and lateral access for drilling platforms. An infill drilling program largely within the lower portion of the 426 Zone and Ruby Deeps Zone commenced in the second quarter of 2026, targeting approximately 55,000 meters planned across 140 drill holes in support of the planned feasibility study for Archimedes ("Archimedes Feasibility Study"). (2) Drilling activities during the quarter continued with 7,990 meters of infill drilling of the lower 426 Zone and Ruby Deeps Zone. The Archimedes Feasibility Study is anticipated to be completed in the second half of 2027, subject to timing of completion of the drilling program. Consistent with previously reported results, ongoing drilling continues to intersect significant intervals of oxide mineralization outside the mineral resource estimate supporting the Archimedes 2025 PEA. Ongoing metallurgical test work also demonstrates the potential to process more oxide material on the Ruby Hill property's existing active heap leach pad than contemplated in the project's 2025 PEA mine plan, potentially improving cash margins for a portion of near-term production at Archimedes. Mineral Point Open Pit Project The Company's updated development plan prioritizes the Mineral Point open pit as the Company's next major development project to drive the next phase of production growth following the anticipated establishment of its underground hub-and-spoke mining and processing platform. At Mineral Point open pit, the Company advanced its surface drill program during the quarter as part of the largest drill program in the Company's history, completing approximately 25,865 meters of core drilling and reverse circulation drilling with a fleet of up to seven drill rigs, primarily for infill drilling of the currently classified Inferred Resources. The program achieved steady state during the quarter, with drilling footage rates improving significantly from earlier in the year following the mobilization of additional drill rigs. Initial infill and step-out drill results released during the third quarter were encouraging, highlighting the potential to expand the mineralized footprint outside of the project's 2025 PEA pit shell. The geotechnical phase of the surface core program in support of the pre-feasibility study was completed during the quarter (see the Company's September 22, 2026 press release). The results from this program will support a pre-feasibility study for the project, which is expected to be completed in the second half of 2027, pending timing of completion of the drilling program. Early-stage pre-permitting activities continued to progress during the quarter. Cove Underground Project Cove is an advanced stage exploration project and is expected to be the Company's third underground mine. During the quarter, the Company continued to advance technical and economic studies for the Cove Underground project. The permitting action is anticipated to require a Notice of Intent to complete an Environmental Impact Statement through the Bureau of Land Management. Federal and State of Nevada permit approvals remain on track for targeted receipt by year-end 2027. A feasibility study for Cove is expected to be completed in the fourth quarter of 2026. (2) Demonstrated resource expansion potential at Granite Creek and Archimedes, the Company's first two underground projects, has provided the flexibility to sequence Cove underground later in the development pipeline as an important future source of refractory feed to the Lone Tree Plant, when future mining rates decline at the first two underground mines. Lone Tree Plant Refurbishment The Lone Tree Plant is currently undergoing a refurbishment following a positive construction decision made by the Board of Directors during the first quarter of 2026. i-80 Gold is one of two gold companies in Nevada with autoclave processing facilities (the other being owned by Nevada Gold Mines LLC, a joint venture between Barrick Mining Corporation and Newmont Corporation). The Lone Tree Plant is envisioned to process material from the Company's three underground mines, Granite Creek, Archimedes, and in the future Cove, to establish a regional hub-and-spoke mining and processing model. Upon refurbishment and commissioning, the Lone Tree Plant is intended to allow the Company to transition from toll milling to owner-operated processing. This shift is expected to materially increase operating margins and enhance free cash flow generation. Bulk demolition was completed in the three months ended September 30, 2026 as planned, and mobilization has begun in preparation for the start of initial construction, with a focus on civil preparation and concrete work. Detailed engineering and procurement continue to advance in line with the construction and commissioning schedule with approximately 74% of procurement packages, by value, awarded as of the end of September 2026. Refurbishment timing and costs incurred to date remain in line with the Company's December 2025 engineering study, which included a total capital cost estimate of $430 million, inclusive of approximately 12% contingency, owner's cost, and capital spares. The Lone Tree Plant refurbishment capital expenditures for 2026 are expected to be lower than original guidance. Refer to the "Outlook" section above for 2026 capital expenditure guidance. The Company continues to expect the refurbishment capital to be nearly fully committed by year-end, and the project remains on schedule. Overall labor costs to date are running approximately 5% to 10% above initial estimates, reflecting higher wages and incidentals due to the competitive labor market in Northern Nevada; however, these remain within the allotted project contingency. Total project capital remains largely as expected with approximately 20% of the total project contingency drawn down and approximately 44% of capital committed at the end of the third quarter of 2026. The Company expects all major procurement and contracts to be in place by the end of 2026 providing greater certainty around the projected cost to complete the project. State level permits have been received on schedule as of the end of September to allow for the mobilization of site contractors to begin construction. The Lone Tree Plant is permitted for the existing operational components in use, and the receipt of normal course design and environmental state permits remains on track. Capital expenditures for the three months and nine months ended September 30, 2026 were primarily related to the refurbishment of the Lone Tree Plant. See Outlook section above for anticipated 2026 project spend. The leaching of the historic leach pad at Lone Tree continues to produce gold at profitable quantities. Processing costs per produced ounce is higher than the prior year periods due to a higher volume of reagents used to improve gold production.
