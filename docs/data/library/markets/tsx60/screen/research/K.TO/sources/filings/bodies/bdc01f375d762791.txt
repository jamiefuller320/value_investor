Energy Density 21700 Cylindrical Cell & Awarded Grand Prize at K-Battery Show News provided by NEO Battery Materials Ltd. Sep 10, 2026, 11:39 ET Share this article Share to X Share this article Share to X Launched high energy density 6.3Ah 21700 cylindrical cell achieving 307 Wh/kg to respond to growing demand from drone and robotics manufacturers Awarded Grand Prize in Innovation Category at K-Battery Show in recognition of new product's performance enhancement Installing pre-commercial assembly equipment for tabless-architecture 21700 cell with targeted annual capacity of 360,000 cells and commissioning by end of year Opening 21700 manufacturing capacity for foundry services and custom cell development to provide alternative, secure supply for non-Chinese, NDAA-compliant cells TORONTO , Sept. 10, 2026 /CNW/ -- NEO Battery Materials Ltd. (" NEO " or the " Company ") (TSXV: NBM ) (OTC: NBMFF), a low-cost, silicon-enhanced battery manufacturer enabling high-performance capabilities for drones, robotics, and physical AI, is pleased to announce the launch of a high energy density 6.3Ah 21700-format cylindrical cell, responding to the growing demand from drone/UAV and robotics manufacturers for standardized cell format offerings. Alongside the product launch, the Company's 21700 cell was awarded the Grand Prize in the Innovation category at the K-Battery Show, one of South Korea's largest battery exhibitions. Continue Reading NEO High Energy Density 6.3Ah 21700 Cylindrical Cell K-Battery Show Grand Prize Ceremony. Source: The Korea Industry Daily NEO's 6.3Ah 21700 cylindrical cell achieves a high energy density of 307 Wh/kg with a maximum continuous discharge rate of 2C and a target cycle life of 1,000 cycles based on standard charging/discharging protocols . As the 21700 format is a standardized form factor across various industries, the Company's cells can be adopted as a direct replacement in systems while extending operational runtime or endurance through higher energy content and lighter construction . NEO aims to distribute and supply the cells directly into drone, robotics, defense, and e-mobility applications, starting with local and Asia-Pacific markets. In recognition of the product's capacity and performance advancement, NEO's 21700 cylindrical cell was awarded the Grand Prize in the Innovation Award category at the K-Battery Show, one of South Korea's largest lithium-ion battery exhibitions . The award was determined through a competitive selection process judged by a committee of independent battery engineers and industry experts. At the Cell Assembly Expansion Factory in South Korea, the Company has procured pre-commercial manufacturing equipment for tabless-architecture 21700 cylindrical cells and expects to commission production by the end of this year . The targeted pre-commercial capacity is approximately 360,000 cells annually on a continuous production basis, translating into 6 to 8 MWh of annual output depending on energy- or power-focused battery designs. In parallel with direct sales, the Company will also open 21700 manufacturing capacity for foundry services (contract manufacturing) and custom cell development and commercialization, serving U.S. and NATO defense and battery companies that need a manufacturing partner to advance in-house design to qualified production . This offering positions NEO to provide customers an alternative, secure supply of U.S. Department of Defense NDAA-compliant, non-Chinese cylindrical cells , as 21700 cell procurement has been materially constrained by substantial consumption from drone warfare. " NEO's product innovation is driven by a team carrying more than 100 years of combined experience from the world's largest lithium-ion battery manufacturers ," stated Mr. Spencer Huh, CEO & President of NEO. " Prior to this launch, our engineers have commercialized various renditions of the 18650 and 21700 cylindricals at the gigafactory scale, in which many products are being mass-produced and utilized in active systems today. As we possess the necessary technical and manufacturing capabilities internally, NEO will actively move the cylindrical offering from pilot to commercial-scale production along with its pouch-format portfolio . " About NEO Battery Materials Ltd . NEO Battery Materials is a Canadian-South Korean battery technology company focused on developing and producing silicon-enhanced lithium-ion batteries in drones, robotics, physical AI, electric vehicles, and energy storage systems. With a patent-protected, low-cost silicon manufacturing process, NEO Battery enables longer-running and ultra-fast charging properties and provides end-to-end battery solutions from materials selection, cell architecture, and process optimization. The Company aims to be a globally-leading producer of high-performance lithium-ion batteries and materials, building a secure, robust battery supply chain for Western manufacturers. For more information, please visit the Company's website at: https://www.neobatterymaterials.com/ .
