DISTRICT-SCALE EXPLORATION STRATEGY WITH WEST HAMMOND FIELD PROGRAM USA - English USA - English News provided by Falcon Gold Corp. Sep 10, 2026, 04:23 ET Share this article Share to X Share this article Share to X VANCOUVER, BC , Sept. 10, 2026 /CNW/ -- Falcon Gold Corp . (TSXV: FG ) (FSE: 3FA) (OTC-Pink: FGLDF) (" Falcon " or the " Company ") is pleased to announce that it has commenced field exploration at its West Hammond Contact Property in northwestern Ontario, advancing the Company's strategy of assembling and evaluating prospective gold exploration opportunities within the Atikokan–Hammond Reef Gold District. Continue Reading Image 1. West Hammond Contact Property in relation to Agnico Eagles’s Hammond Reef The West Hammond program represents the next step in Falcon's effort to build a district-scale exploration portfolio around its Central Canada Gold Project. The Company is focused on identifying prospective satellite targets and additional gold-bearing systems within the broader regional geological setting. "Falcon is strategically positioned in an emerging gold district anchored by the Hammond Reef deposit, one of Canada's significant undeveloped gold deposits," said Karim Rayani, Chief Executive Officer of Falcon Gold . "Our strategy is to acquire prospective ground in established gold-bearing districts, evaluate historical occurrences and structural targets, and systematically advance opportunities that may have been overlooked. West Hammond is an important part of that strategy, and we are pleased to begin evaluating the property on the ground." A District with a Long Mining History The Atikokan–Hammond Reef district has a history of gold exploration and development extending back more than a century. Gold was discovered at Hammond Reef in 1895, followed by early underground development and subsequent exploration by several major and junior mining companies. The project was later advanced through extensive drilling and technical studies by Brett Resources and Osisko before becoming part of Agnico Eagle's portfolio. According to Agnico Eagle's public disclosure, Hammond Reef hosts approximately 3.3 million ounces of probable gold reserves and an additional 2.3 million ounces of measured and indicated gold resources as of December 31, 2025. 1 The project has received federal and provincial environmental approvals, although additional permits and a development decision would be required before construction. Falcon believes the scale and history of Hammond Reef demonstrate the significance of the broader district and support continued exploration for additional gold-bearing systems. Mineralization at Hammond Reef is not necessarily indicative of mineralization on Falcon's properties. West Hammond Exploration The West Hammond Contact Property comprises 156 mining claims covering approximately 3,068 hectares. The property is situated within the prospective Finlayson Greenstone Belt and associated regional structural corridors, including interpreted extensions of the Marmion Shear Zone. Historical exploration has identified gold occurrences, geochemical anomalies and favourable geological contacts across the property. Falcon's initial field program will focus on prospecting and geological evaluation of priority areas, with the objective of refining targets for follow-up exploration. "Rather than relying on a single project, we are building a portfolio of prospective exploration opportunities within a district that has already demonstrated its ability to host significant gold mineralization," added Rayani . "Our approach is to acquire strategically located ground, apply disciplined exploration and advance the most compelling targets as the geological picture develops." The Company looks forward to providing an update in the coming weeks following completion of the sampling program and the definition of priority targets for follow-up exploration. About Falcon Gold Corp. Falcon Gold Corp. is a Canadian mineral exploration company focused on the acquisition, exploration, and advancement of precious and battery metals opportunities across the Americas, with a portfolio spanning established mining camps and emerging exploration districts. Its flagship asset, the Central Canada Gold Project, is located approximately 20 kilometres southeast of Agnico Eagle's Hammond Reef Gold Deposit in northwestern Ontario. The project lies within the highly prospective Quetico Fault Zone, a major regional structural corridor interpreted as a key control on gold mineralization in the district. The Hammond Reef deposit is associated with a northeast-trending structural system linked to this broader regional framework, highlighting the significance of the geological setting. The Central Canada property has a documented exploration and development history spanning more than a century. Early work between 1901 and 1907 included shallow shaft development and small-scale production from high-grade material processed through stamp milling. Between 1930 and 1935, Central Canada Mines Ltd. further advanced the project with deeper underground development, crosscutting, and the installation of a small-scale gold mill. Subsequent exploration programs have included diamond drilling campaigns that returned multiple high-grade gold intercepts, supporting the presence of significant mineralization within the system. Beyond its flagship project, Falcon Gold maintains a diversified portfolio of Canadian exploration assets. This includes a 49% interest in the Burton Gold Property in partnership with IAMGOLD near Sudbury, Ontario, exploration-stage gold targets in British Columbia through the Spitfire and Sunny Boy claims, and the Great Burnt Copper-Gold Project in central Newfoundland.
