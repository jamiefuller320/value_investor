REPORT OF VOTING RESULTS 
This report on the voting results of the Annual Meeting of Shareholders of Canadian Tire Corporation, Limited 
(the “Corporation”) held on May 8, 2025 (the “Meeting”) is made in accordance with Section 11.3 of National 
Instrument 51-102 - Continuous Disclosure Obligations .  Each of the matters set out below is described in 
greater detail in the Corporation’s Management Information Circular dated March 6, 2025, which is available 
on the System for Electronic Document Analysis and Retrieval  + (SEDAR+) at sedarplus.ca and under the 
Financial Reporting section of the Corporation’s website at investors.canadiantire.ca.
