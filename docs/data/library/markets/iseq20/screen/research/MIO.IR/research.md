Until Irish Stock Exchange / company annual and interim releases are matched to MIO.IR, conviction should stay provisional.

The Strong Buy is therefore a **balance-sheet, dividend, and modest-multiple** call rather than a high-return compounder call — credible on Yahoo data, but unconfirmed against Mincon’s own filings.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
1. **Filing misattribution (governance / process):** The research pack’s primary filings describe a UK SPAC with material going-concern uncertainty, majority-shareholder loan dependence (**£1m** facility), related-party concentration (BPM Trading **76.6%**), no D&O insurance, and auditor emphasis-of-matter on going concern — none of which applies to Mincon’s industrial operations but creates **severe verify-before-trade risk** if any downstream process treats these as Mincon filings.

2. **Cyclical end-markets:** Revenue and earnings remain below FY2022 peaks; screen flags cyclical exposure overlay as false, but five-year Yahoo trend and industrial inventory build suggest latent cyclicality not fully captured.

3. **Returns and quality:** ROE **7.2%**, thin margins versus moat screens, and FCF yield **3.6%** below the 5% hurdle indicate limited economic surplus for reinvestment or re-rating.

4. **Working capital / inventory:** **$71.5m** inventory (FY2025) and negative WC movement in FY2025 OCF raise obsolescence and demand-risk questions — unaddressable without Mincon management commentary.

5. **Discontinued operations:** FY2024–FY2025 earnings include discontinued lines; underlying continuing profit trend is flatter than headline net income suggests.

6. **Leverage and interest:** Net debt manageable, but interest **$2.0m** consumes ~36% of FY2025 net income; covenant detail absent without primary filings.

7. **Dividend sustainability:** Screen passes Dividend Growth, but FCF coverage is only ~1.2× gross; news flow (May 2026) questions dividend safety ahead of ex-dates.

8. **No primary filing language available** for Mincon on going concern, contingencies, pensions, or customer concentration — all unverified gaps.
