Activity 
Eiffage generated consolidated revenue of €6.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 3.3% compared with 
the third quarter of 2025 (+4.0% in Contracting and -0.1% in Concessions). Although motorway traffic remains 
adversely affected by fuel prices, quarterly organic growth in Contracting picked up to +2.5% (compared to 
+1.2% during the first half), buoyed by strong performance at Eiffage Énergie Systèmes (+5.0% organic 
growth for the quarter versus +2.0% in the first half). 
The Group’s consolidated revenue to 30 September 2026 therefore comes to a total of €18.8 billion, an 
increase of 2.6% or 1.5% lfl.  
 
In Contracting, revenue rose by 3.2% (up 1.6% lfl) relative to 2025, reaching close to €15.8 billion, 43.5% of 
which was generated outside France (compared with 42.5% to September 2025). The activity remains robust 
in Europe outisde France (+9.3% actual; +6.2% lfl) and continues to show resilience in France (+1.4% actual; 
+0.9% lfl) thanks to the Group’s diversified business activities. Outside Europe, the 36.0% contraction in 
activity stems from the winding down of several major export contracts, in line with expectations. As a 
reminder, Europe represents the Group’s core strategic focus. 
As Goyer, previously part of the Infrastructure branch, has joined the Off-Site business unit of the 
Construction division, figures for 20252 have been amended accordingly. 
 
Construction: €3,118 million (+3.0% actual or +2.2% lfl) 
- Revenue increased by 2.7% in France to €2,369 million and rose by 8.2% in Europe outside France 
(+4.9% lfl) to €750 million. Activity remained dynamic across Belgium, Luxembourg and Poland, while the 
integration of the Baatz Group’s construction entities in Luxembourg made a 3.5% contribution in Europe 
outside France. 
- In property development, revenue was up 9.2% to €393 million. Residential property marketing stood at 
1,891 reservations at the end of September 2026, compared with 1,286 at the end of September 2025. In 
a French market that remains depressed, these figures include an increasing number of block sales, 
including more than 300 units in Dieppe in connection with the Penly EPR2 construction project. 
- The order book stood at €6.0 billion at 30 September 2026, an increase of 2% year-on-year.  
 
Infrastructure: €6,623 million (+1.1% actual or +0.2% lfl)  
- In France revenue increased by 0.9% to €3,385 million, with disparities depending on the business line 
(Eiffage Génie Civil +4.4%, Eiffage Métal +39.1%, Eiffage Route -6.9%). Eiffage Route activity was affected 
by the cycle following the March 2026 municipal elections, while Eiffage Métal benefits fully from the solid 
momentum of major offshore wind farm projects and Civil engineering metal structures in Germany. 
- In Europe outside France, revenue rose 7.3% to €3,057 million, 2.3% of which came from the integration of 
HSM Offshore Energy, consolidated as of the beginning of June 2025. In Germany, the activity is impacted 
by the completion of works on the A3 highway, but is otherwise sensibly progressing. Spain is boosted by 
a number of flagship projects. 
- Outside Europe, revenue totalled €181 million compared with €349 million during the period to 
30 September 2025. This reduction relates primarily to the expected decline in operations in Senegal as 
well as the Puerto Antioquia project in Colombia coming to an end. 
- The order book declined by 1% year-on-year to €15.9 billion.

Energy Systems: €6,023 million (+5.8% actual or +3.0% lfl) 
- In France, revenue totalled €3,155 million, an increase of 1.1% (down 0.5% lfl). 
- In Europe outside France, activity grew by 12.1% to €2,702 million (+7.7% lfl). Organic growth proved 
particularly dynamic in Germany, Spain and the Netherlands. Furthermore, acquisitions carried out in Spain, 
Germany and Italy contributed 4.4% to growth. 
- Outside Europe, revenue stood at €166 million, compared with €163 million at 30 September 2025, mainly 
generated from electrification projects (substations and power lines). 
- The order book grew by 7% year-on-year to €9.5 billion.  
 
In the Concessions division, revenue grew at constant scope (+0.6%) but declined by 0.3% on an actual 
basis to €3,031 million. This drop is due to the consolidation of Nové under the equity method since 
31 December 2025 (€27 million in revenue at 30 September 2025). 
Revenue from the APRR and AREA non-construction networks came to €2,442 million3 during the period to 
30 September 2026, an increase of 0.2% relative to 2025. Traffic decreased by 2.9% (LV -3.7%, HGV +1.9%) 
and the toll revenue by 0.8%. 
Revenue from other Group concessions came to €593 million, broken down into three areas as follows:  
- Revenue from the other motorway concessions increasing by 2.2% to €278 million. In France: 
− Aliaé (A79), with revenue of over €45 million, an increase of 4.4% (traffic +1.4%) 
− Aliénor (A65) with €64 million, up 3.7% (traffic -2.2%) 
− The Millau viaduct with €54 million, down 4.8% (traffic -6.8%) 
− Adelac (A41) with €60 million, an increase of 1.4% (traffic -0.6%). 
Internationally, the Autoroute de l'Avenir motorway in Senegal saw growth of 6.9% to €55 million (traffic 
+7.0%). 
- The two airport concessions (Lille and Toulouse airports) saw revenue growth of 3.5% to around 
€151 million, with passenger traffic up 2.8% (+2.2% for Toulouse and +5.4% for Lille). 
- PPPs and equivalents generated revenue of €164 million (€188 million during the period to 30 September 
2025), down as a result of the consolidation under the equity method of Nové as of 31 December 2025. 
 
Financial position 
The Group benefits from a solid financial position. Eiffage SA and its Contracting subsidiaries have a short-
term rating of F2 from Fitch, confirmed on 23 September 2026. APRR is rated A/Stable by Fitch, also 
confirmed on 14 September 2026, and A-/Stable by S&P.  
The liquidity of Eiffage SA and its Contracting subsidiaries was €4.4 billion as at 30 September 2026, 
comprising €2.4 billion of cash and cash equivalents and an undrawn bank credit facility of €2 billion. This 
facility with no financial covenants matures in 2031, with the possibility of being extended by one year. 
APRR’s liquidity was €2.8 billion as at 30 September 2026, comprising €1.3 billion of cash and cash 
equivalents and an undrawn bank credit facility of €1.5 billion. This facility with no financial covenants matures 
in 2031, with the possibility of being extended by one year.

Subsequent events 
On 21 October 2026, Eiffage signed a memorandum of understanding with yhe LOSC concerning the 
proposed sale of the companies in charge of operating the Decathlon Arena - Stade Pierre-Mauroy Stadium 
and associated spaces. 
 
2026 outlook 
In Contracting, Eiffage benefits from excellent visibility thanks to an order book of €31.4 billion as at 
30 September 2026, very close to its 30th June 2026 level (€31.5 billion). It is up 2% year-on-year and 5% 
over nine months, representing 17.3 months’ of acitvity.  
Considering this order backlog and third-quarter revenue, the Group confirms its guidance of revenue growth 
and an increase in both operating profit on ordinary activities and net profit Group share, as previously 
announced, with an unchanged French tax framework for 2026. 
However, should the 2027 Finance Bill, currently before Parliament, raise the Operation of Long-Distance 
Transport Infrastructure Tax (TEITLD) as outlined in its Article 22, the potential impact on 2026 would be 
approximately €210 million on operating profit on ordinary activities and €110 million on net profit Group 
share, based on 2025 figures. This increase would exclusively affect the APRR and AREA networks, with 
their applicable TEITLD rate going from 4.6% to 12.2% of toll revenues.  
 
The Group will report its revenue and results for the 2026 financial year on 24 February 2027 after market 
close.

Appendix 3: Alternative performance metrics: definitions and calculation methods and reconciliation 
with financial statement aggregates 
 
Definitions  
 
Concessions’ 
“Construction” revenue 
(Ifric 12) 
Concessions’ “Construction” revenue corresponds to costs relating to the provision of 
construction services or infrastructure improvements by the company awarded the 
concession contract in accordance with Ifric 12 “Service Concession Arrangements”, after 
elimination of intragroup transactions 
Contracting order book Proportion of signed contracts not executed 
Net debt  Net debt excluding debt under IFRS 16 applied since 1 January 2019 and fair value of derivatives 
Free cash flow 
Free cash flow is calculated as follows: 
Net cash flow from operating activities  
- net operating investment 
- repayment of lease liabilities 
- repayment of receivables in relation to PPP agreements 
Operating margin Operating profit on ordinary activities as a percentage of revenue 
EBITDA Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation (net of reversals) 
Like-for-like (lfl) 
At constant scope, adjusted for: 
- the 2026 contribution of companies added to the scope of consolidation in 2026 
- the 2026 contribution of companies added to the scope of consolidation in 2025 for the 
equivalent period to that of 2025 prior to the date they were added 
- the 2025 contribution of companies removed from the scope of consolidation in 2026 for 
the equivalent period to that of 2026 prior to the date they were removed 
- the 2025 contribution of companies removed from the scope of consolidation in 2025 
At constant exchange rates: 
- 2025 exchange rates applied to foreign currency revenue for 2026 
Group liquidity 
The Group’s liquidity is calculated as follows:  
cash and cash equivalents managed by Eiffage SA and its Contracting subsidiaries + 
Eiffage SA undrawn bank credit facilities 
APRR liquidity 
APRR’s liquidity is calculated as follows:  
cash and cash equivalents managed by APRR and its subsidiaries + APRR undrawn bank 
credit facilities
