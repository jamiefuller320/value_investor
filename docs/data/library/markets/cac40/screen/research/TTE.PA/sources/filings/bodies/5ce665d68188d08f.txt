Disclaimer* In an energy context marked by rising petroleum and gas products prices, TotalEnergies (Paris:TTE) (LSE:TTE) (NYSE:TTE) has made it a clear priority to share the Company’s success and performance with its employees by strengthening value-sharing mechanisms and specific measures to support their energy purchasing power. An Exceptional Value-Sharing Bonus in 2026 TotalEnergies has confirmed that it will distribute an exceptional value-sharing bonus to eligible employees 1 in France and around the world in 2026. With this decision, TotalEnergies wishes to recognize the daily commitment of TotalEnergies’ teams, whose professionalism and dedication contribute in particular to the reliability of France’s fuel supply. In France, pending the mandatory annual negotiations scheduled for December 2026, TotalEnergies is paying an advance on this exceptional bonus of €1,000 gross . The final amount of the bonus and terms of payment will be discussed with employee representative organizations within the framework of its social dialogue. The value-sharing bonus comes on top of other benefits available to the nearly 15,000 French employees in the Common Social Framework: profit-sharing and incentive plans, with an average incentive payment of €10,136 paid in 2026, and employer contributions to employee savings plans made by the TotalEnergies’ companies in addition to employees’ voluntary contributions to these plans. A new capital increase reserved for employees with a 20% discount Following the 2026 campaign, the Board of Directors authorized a new capital increase reserved for employees, who will enjoy a 20% share-purchasing discount and one bonus share for each share subscribed , up to a maximum of 10 bonus shares . More than 70% of TotalEnergies’ employees worldwide (and more than 80% in France) are TotalEnergies shareholders. In 2025, TotalEnergies paid out approximately €700 million in dividends to employee and former employee shareholders, including approximately €450 million in France . The dividend growth policy of at least 5% per year for the next five years up to 2030, announced by the Board of Directors in September, will also directly benefit employees. Finally, 13,500 employees worldwide, including 6,500 in France, were granted performance shares in 2026, with an average allocation of 545 shares per employee. Increased support for fuel purchases for employees in France and around the world In response to rising fuel prices in many countries, TotalEnergies paid a fuel allowance of €200 2 to all its employees worldwide in June 2026. TotalEnergies has also decided to automatically provide all 35,400 employees with a Fleet card, entitling them to a discount of 8 cents per liter on gasoline and diesel purchased at TotalEnergies service stations, in addition to the price caps in effect for all customers in France. This discount, currently available to nearly 15,000 employees in France, will now be extended to all employees in the country.
