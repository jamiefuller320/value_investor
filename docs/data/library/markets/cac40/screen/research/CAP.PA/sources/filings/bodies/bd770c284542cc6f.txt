Q3 2026 momentum drives upgrade in Capgemini's 2026 outlook 
 
 
• Q3 2026 revenues of €6,073 million, up +11.7% at constant exchange rates* 
• 9M 2026 revenues of €18,155 million, up +11.4% at constant exchange rates 
• Q3 2026 bookings of €5,401 million, up +3.4% at constant exchange rates 
• Upgraded 2026 financial targets 
o Constant currency revenue growth* of +9.0% to +9.5% (was around +8.5% to +9.0%) 
o Operating margin* of 13.6% to 13.8% (unchanged) 
o Organic free cash flow* of around €1.8 billion to €1.9 billion (unchanged). 
 
 
 
Paris, October 30, 2026 – The Capgemini Group reported Q3 2026 revenues of €6,073 million, up +12.6% at current 
exchange rates and +11.7% at constant exchange rates*. 
Aiman Ezzat, Chief Executive Officer of the Capgemini Group, said: “Capgemini delivered another solid quarter, with growth 
of +11.7% at constant exchange rates and underlying growth close to Q2, once again outperforming the industry. In a stable 
demand environment, we continue to see strong momentum in North America and the United Kingdom , and a clear  
acceleration in France. 
Our strategy, focused on the new value pools created by AI, is translating into tangible business traction. Clients are now 
focused on deploying AI at  enterprise scale, while addressing challenges related to governance, cost and security. This is 
driving demand for the modernization of their technology and data foundations, the critical prerequisite for the agentic 
enterprise. At the same time, clients are transforming their business operations with agentic AI, as reflected in another quarter 
of double-digit like-for-like growth in Intelligent Business Operations, strengthened by WNS’s scale and capabilities. We are 
also capturing opportunities in the defense sector and around sovereignty, as clients place greater emphasis on control and 
strategic resilience. 
Capgemini is uniquely positioned to make AI real for its clients, combining technology, deep industry expertise, business 
transformation and operational execution, and increasingly leveraging an expanded partner ecosystem. We expect to 
maintain this momentum and target, on a higher comparison basis, a constant currency growth of 3% to 4% in Q4 . On the 
strength of this quarter, we are raising our 2026 revenue growth target to +9.0% to +9.5% at constant exchange rates, while 
confirming our operating margin and organic free cash flow objectives.”

The Group reported revenues of €6,073 million in Q3 2026, up +12.6% year-on-year. Excluding the +0.9% tailwind from 
currency fluctuations, constant currency growth* was +11.7%, reflecting solid underlying growth complemented by the 
contribution of WNS and Cloud4C. 
The Group continued to execute strongly against the strategy presented at its Capital Markets Day. Clients are 
increasingly focused on AI transformation and scaling deployments enterprise-wide. This is driving growing demand for 
the modernization of their technology foundations and the deployment of the new agentic tech stack, while opening 
new opportunities to redesign and transform business operations with agentic AI. 
With bookings of €5,401 million in Q3, up +3.4% year-on-year at constant exchange rates, the Group demonstrated good 
commercial momentum.

The Group’s underlying growth (i.e. excluding the impact of acquisitions) in Q3 2026 was solid, supported by the positive 
momentum in North America and the United Kingdom, as well as continued improvement s in France and the Rest of 
Europe. Acquisitions (notably WNS and Cloud4C  closed in Q4 2025 ) had a material contribution to growth in the North 
America, United Kingdom & Ireland and Asia-Pacific regions. 
In Q3 2026 and at constant exchange rates, revenues in North America (29% of 2025 Group revenues) grew +18.3% year-
on-year, led by continued momentum in Financial Services, alongside strong performances in Manufacturing and 
Consumer Goods & Retail. 
The United Kingdom and Ireland  region (13% of 2025 Group revenues) posted revenue growth of +25. 2% with strong 
traction in the Public Sector and a very dynamic Consumer Goods & Retail sector while TMT declined. 
France (19% of 2025 Group revenues)  continued its recovery, with revenues up by + 4.9% year-on-year. Growth was 
primarily driven by Manufacturing and Financial Services, even as the Public Sector remained weak. 
Revenues in the Rest of Europe region (30% of 2025 Group revenues)  rose by +3.4%. The Public, Energy & Utilities and 
Services sectors all grew strongly, and Manufacturing turned slightly positive.

At constant exchange rates, Strategy & Transformation (8% of 2025 Group revenues) reported +18.6% growth in total 
revenues in Q3 2026. This strong performance reflects the growing demand for  business transformation in the era of 
agentic AI , as clients redesign their enterprise processes around AI,  and the relevance of Capgemini’s deep industry 
expertise and understanding of clients’ business challenges. 
Applications & Technology (63% of 2025 Group revenues and Capgemini’s core business) reported total revenue growth 
of +3.5%, supported by continued demand for the modernization of legacy technologies, a critical prerequisite for 
building the new agentic technology stack. 
Finally, total revenues in Operations & Engineering (29% of 2025 Group revenues) increased +26.9%, with double-digit 
growth on a like -for-like basis in Intelligent Business Operations, with strong performance across  Capgemini and WNS  
activities. 
Headcount 
As of September 30, 2026, the Group’s total headcount stood at 417,500, up 62, 800 or +18% year -on-year, primarily 
reflecting the integration of WNS team members, and down 5,900 compared to the end of 2025.  
The onshore workforce was 141,900 employees. The offshore workforce was 275,600 employees, stable at 66% of the 
total headcount compared to the end of 2025. 
Outlook 
The Group’s financial targets for 2026 are updated as follows: 
• Revenue growth of + 9.0% to +9.5% at constant exchange rates  (was around + 8.5% to +9. 0%). The inorganic 
contribution is estimated at around 5 points (unchanged); 
• Operating margin of 13.6% to 13.8% (unchanged); 
• Organic free cash flow of around €1.8 billion to €1.9 billion (unchanged). 
The organic free cash flow target takes into account an increase in restructuring cash outflow of around €200 million 
compared to 2025 related to the Fit-for-Growth initiatives.

When determining activity trends by business and in accordance with internal operating performance measures, growth 
at constant exchange rates is calculated based on total revenues, i.e., before elimination of inter -business billing. The 
Group considers this to be more representative of activity levels by business. As its businesses change, an increasing 
number of contracts require a range of business expertise for delivery, leading to a rise in inter-business flows.  
Operating margin is one of the Group’s key performance indicators. It is defined as the difference between revenues 
and operating costs. It is calculated before “Other operating income and expenses” which include amortization of 
intangible assets recognized in business combinations, expenses relative to share-based compensation (including social 
security contributions and employer contributions) and employee share ownership plan, and non-recurring revenues and 
expenses, notably impairment of goodwill, negative goodwill, ca pital gains or losses on disposals of consolidated 
companies or businesses, restructuring costs incurred under a detailed formal plan approved by the Group’s 
management, the cost of acquiring and integrating companies acquired by the Group, including earn -outs comprising 
conditions of presence, and the effects of curtailments, settlements and transfers of defined benefit pension plans. 
Normalized net profit is equal to profit for the year (Group share) adjusted for the impact of items recognized in “Other 
operating income and expense”, net of tax calculated using the effective tax rate. Normalized earnings per share is 
computed like basic earnings per share, i.e., excluding dilution. 
Organic free cash flow is equal to cash flow from operations  less acquisitions of property, plant, equipment and 
intangible assets (net of disposals) and repayments of lease liabilities, adjusted for cash out relating to the net interest 
cost. 
Net debt (or net cash) comprises (i) cash and cash equivalents, as presented in the Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows 
(consisting of short -term investments and cash at bank) less bank overdrafts, and also including (ii) cash management 
assets (assets presented separately in the Consolidated Statement of Financial Position due to their characteristics), less 
(iii) short- and long-term borrowings. Account is also taken of (iv) the impact of hedging instruments when these relate 
to borrowings, intercompany loans, and own shares.
