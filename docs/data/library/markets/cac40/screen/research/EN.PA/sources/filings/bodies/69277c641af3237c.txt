Bouygues records a non-cash impairment on its stake in TF1. The Group 
reafﬁrms its conﬁdence in TF1’s transformation strategy. 
 
Within the framework of closing off its consolidated ﬁnancial statements for the ﬁrst nine months of 2026, 
which are due to be published on 5 November 2026, the Bouygues group will record an impairment on 
its stake in TF1 amounting to €569 million, corre sponding to the full amount of goodwill relating to this 
stake. The carrying amount of TF1 in the Group’s consolidated balance sheet will therefore be reduced to 
€2.0 billion as of 30 September 2026, representing €9.67 per share (compared with €15.36 before the 
impairment). This charge, which is not tax-deductible, will be recognised in non-current proﬁt or loss and 
will reduce Bouygues’ net proﬁt attributable to the Group. It will have no impact on current operating 
proﬁt from activities (COPA). Given that it will have no impact on the Group’s net debt, it will not impact 
its liquidity or its investment capacity. 
The impairment will not affect the TF1 group’s consolidated ﬁnancial statements. It will not impact the 
Bouygues SA parent company ﬁnancial statements or distributable earnings either. 
The impairment follows TF1’s announcement of a sharp decline in linear advertising revenue for the fourth 
consecutive quarter, conﬁrming that the linear TV advertising market is shrinking faster than anticipated. 
This decline is the result of structural trends observed globally, characterised by rapid changes in the way 
televisual content is consumed and the increasing shift of advertising spend towards on- line platforms. 
This situation is exacerbated by the absence of any prospect of improvement in the short term in the 
macroeconomic and political environment, or in creating a more  level regulatory playing ﬁeld with the 
platforms. 
This adjustment to the carrying amount does not call into question the strategic importance of TF1 to the 
Bouygues group. Bouygues reafﬁrms its conﬁdence in the ability of TF1’s senior management to 
successfully continue the digital transformation of its business model and to adapt its offering to changing 
viewer and advertiser behaviour.  
Bouygues will continue to support the implementation of TF1’s ambitious strategic roadmap, which is 
beginning to yield results and will enable it to consolidate its competitive position in the long term.
