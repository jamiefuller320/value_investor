constant
 
exchange
 
rates.
 
 
Gross  margin   
Gross  margin  corresponds  to  the  sum  of  net  sales  and  other  income,  reduced  by  loyalty  program  costs  and  cost  of  
goods
 
sold.

of

Recurring  Operating  Income  corresponds  to  the  gross  margin  lowered  by  sales,  general  and  administrative  expenses,  
depreciation
 
and
 
amortization.
 
 
Recurring  Operating  Income  Before  Depreciation  and  Amortization  (EBITDA)
