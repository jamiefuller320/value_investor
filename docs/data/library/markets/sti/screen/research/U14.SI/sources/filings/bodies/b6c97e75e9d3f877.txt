The event brought together Camogie teams from across the country, delivering a feast of competitive camogie and showcasing the depth of young talent in the game. Mary Moran Cup – Kilcock GAA Kilcock GAA hosted the Mary Moran Cup , where the competition was fierce from the outset. Making their debut at this level, Derry impressed with a strong run to the Cup Final, where they faced Tipperary in a highly entertaining contest. The Premier County girls held firm to take the honours on the day. Elsewhere in the division: 2nd Place Final – Limerick emerged winners. 3rd Place Final – Galway came out on top. 4th Place Final – Clare claimed victory. Lily Spence Division – St Anne’s GAA, South Dublin Warm but breezy conditions greeted the Lily Spence Division , which went right down to the wire in both groups. Waterford and Wexford progressed to the Cup Final, with the Déise taking a dramatic one-point win. Other results included: 2nd Place Final – Cork took the win. 3rd Place Final – Offaly came through on top. 4th Place Final – Antrim secured the honours. Rosina McManus Division – Meath Centre of Excellence At the Rosina McManus Division , the standard of play matched the fine conditions. After a series of thrilling matches, Kilkenny and Kildare advanced to the Cup Final, with Kilkenny producing a composed and clinical performance to secure the title. Also in this division: 2nd Place Final – Cork claimed victory. 3rd Place Final – Clare edged Wexford after extra time in a nail-biting clash. 4th Place Final – Laois overcame Dublin. Mairead Meehan Division – Naomh Mearnóg GAA Naomh Mearnóg provided a lively setting for the Mairead Meehan Division , with tight group-stage games setting up the final pairings. Derry maintained their winning momentum to lift the Cup after defeating Meath in the decider. Other placings: 2nd Place Final – Tipperary triumphed. 3rd Place Final – Westmeath beat Carlow. Sophie Brack Division – Kiltale GAA The Sophie Brack Division delivered end-to-end excitement in Kiltale. Down and Armagh contested a thrilling Cup Final, with Down pulling away late to secure the title. Further results: 2nd Place Final – Waterford overcame Roscommon. 3rd Place Final – Clare defeated Kerry. Five-Team Division – Inisfails GAA, Dublin The day’s only five-team round-robin division took place in Inisfails GAA, with Laois 2, Kildare 2, Cavan 1, Offaly 2, and Dublin 3 all facing each other. Every game was competitive, providing valuable game time for all involved. Final results: Cup Final – Cavan beat Offaly. 2nd Place Final – Dublin edged Kildare. Mary Lynch Division – Trim GAA The Mary Lynch Division in Trim was full of high-energy clashes. Tyrone proved the most consistent, lifting the Cup after a strong campaign. Other placings: 2nd Place Final – Meath came out on top. 3rd Place Final – Down secured victory. 4th Place Final – Mayo battled hard throughout the day. U14 Development Division – Naomh Mearnóg GAA The U14 Development Division also took place at Naomh Mearnóg, where Monaghan impressed from start to finish, clinching the Cup with style. Additional results: 2nd Place Final – Westmeath took the win. 3rd Place Final – Armagh proved too strong. 4th Place Final – Carlow gave a committed performance. A Celebration of Young Talent This year’s U14 National Blitz Programme was a celebration of skill, commitment, and sportsmanship. Across all divisions, the matches were played in the right spirit, with players, coaches, referees, and volunteers contributing to a memorable weekend for everyone involved. The future of camogie is bright – and it was on full display last weekend.
