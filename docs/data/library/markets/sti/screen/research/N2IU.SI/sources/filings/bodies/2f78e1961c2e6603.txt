Singapore already contributes more than 60% of revenue and NPI, while aggregate leverage of 38.1% and an all-in debt cost of 2.95% align with the lower financing cost angle. Distribution per unit remains steady at S$0.039 for H1 despite softer top line numbers and overseas drag. This suggests early progress on the resilience goal rather than conclusive evidence. Bear Case Checkpoint: Overseas Drags Vs Singapore Strength Bearish investors worry that overseas weakness will keep undermining Mapletree Pan Asia Commercial Trust even as Singapore holds up. "While VivoCity and other Singapore properties have benefited from asset enhancement and higher NPI, the sharp fall in Japan occupancy from 71.5% to a pro forma 57.1% after the expiry of a key master lease, together with tenant retention in Japan in only the mid 30% range, signals that ongoing churn and weak leasing demand could offset domestic gains and limit future group revenue growth." Latest half year figures show revenue and NPI both lower, with management explicitly pointing to Japan non renewals and overseas rental reversions as key drivers. Singapore heavyweights like VivoCity and Mapletree Business City are helping distributions stay flat, yet the earnings drag from Japan, China and Hong Kong is clearly visible and this concern is not disproved by Q2.
