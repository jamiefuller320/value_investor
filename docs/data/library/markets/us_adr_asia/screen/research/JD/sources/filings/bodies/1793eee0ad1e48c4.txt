(“JD.com” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: JD and HKEX: 9618 (HKD counter) and 89618 (RMB counter)), a leading supply technology and service provider, today announced the completion of its previously announced voluntary public takeover offer for CECONOMY AG (“CECONOMY”) (XETRA: CEC), acquiring 59.8% of the share capital and voting rights of CECONOMY with total consideration of EUR 1,335 million. As a result, CECONOMY becomes a subsidiary of JD.com, and its financial results are consolidated into JD.com’s consolidated financial statements from November 12, 2026. Together with the 25.3% stake retained by JD.com’s partner Convergenta, this acquisition brings our combined shareholding in CECONOMY to 85.2%. The offer was made through JINGDONG HOLDING GERMANY GMBH, an indirect subsidiary of JD.com (the “Bidder”), at EUR 4.60 in cash per share. This follows the fulfillment of all offer conditions for completion of the transaction, including merger control clearances, foreign investment clearances and EU foreign subsidies clearance. “JD.com and CECONOMY share a common vision to build Europe’s leading consumer electronics platform. We can now start to implement our vision and bring it to life,” said Sandy Xu, CEO of JD.com. “CECONOMY has a clear and compelling strategy for the European retail market and for Europe’s consumers. Supported by its talented employees, strong brand recognition, and extensive online and offline capabilities, it has the foundations from which to grow and thrive. We are also confident our partnership supports Europe’s broader objectives for innovation and competitiveness, and will contribute to a more dynamic European consumer retail market.” “Today marks the beginning of a new chapter for CECONOMY. Our partnership with JD.com has become reality and we are ready to move into implementation with full momentum,” said Remko Rijnders, CEO of CECONOMY. “CECONOMY has the right foundation to accelerate its ambition of becoming Europe’s most trusted tech platform. What stays the same is everything that makes us strong, our mission, our iconic brands, our employees and our commitment to our customers. What changes is that we can now move from vision to action, combining our local expertise and customer relationships with JD.com’s technology and logistics capabilities to deliver an even better experience for European consumers.” CECONOMY operates more than 1,000 consumer electronics stores in 11 markets under the MediaMarkt, Saturn and MediaWorld brands, alongside its online retail operations. The business employs more than 50,000 people and, in 2025, had more than 53 million loyalty members and 2.2 billion annual customer contacts. Its established customer relationships and combination of digital and physical retail channels provide a strong foundation for the partnership’s next phase of growth. As part of the strategic roadmap, CECONOMY will remain a business in Europe with a local independent technology stack, and no changes are planned to the workforce, employee agreements and sites. JD.com is committed to investing in CECONOMY’s strong consumer brands and staying true to their heritage. CECONOMY will continue to focus on developing its customer relationships and building new ones across its platform. About JD.com, Inc. JD.com is a leading supply technology and service provider. The Company’s retail infrastructure seeks to enable consumers to buy whatever they want, whenever and wherever they want it. The Company has opened its technology and infrastructure to partners, brands and other sectors, as part of its Retail as a Service offering to help drive productivity and innovation across a range of industries.
