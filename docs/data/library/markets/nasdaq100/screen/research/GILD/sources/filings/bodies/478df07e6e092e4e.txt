On August 10, 2025, the Board of Directors of Gilead Sciences, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), appointed Erin Burkhart as Senior Vice President, Controllership and principal accounting officer of the Company, effective as of September 22, 2025 (the “Transition Date”). Ms. Burkhart, age 46, has served as Group Vice President and Chief Accounting Officer of BioMarin Pharmaceutical Inc., a biotechnology company, since May 2022. Ms. Burkhart previously worked at Eli Lilly and Company, a pharmaceutical company, from August 2014 to April 2022, where she held various accounting and finance roles of increasing responsibility during her tenure. Ms. Burkhart started her career in public accounting in 2001 with Arthur Andersen and Deloitte before serving in accounting and finance roles with various other companies. Ms. Burkhart is a licensed certified public accountant and holds a B.S. in Accounting from Butler University. Ms. Burkhart succeeds Diane E. Wilfong, who will step down as the Company’s interim Controller and principal accounting officer, effective as of the Transition Date.
