# Linde plc (LIN) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T10:51:25.261320+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Linde plc is the global leader in industrial gases, operating a contract-heavy on-site and merchant model across Americas, EMEA, APAC and Engineering. The quantitative screen rates LIN a **buy**, supported by Neff PEGY, Dividend Growth, Magic Formula and Earnings Quality passes (composite 58%, four value families cleared). Filings show a durable earnings compounder: FY2025 net income attributable to Linde plc rose to **$6.9bn** and diluted EPS to **$14.61** (+7% YoY), with Q1 2026 accelerating to **$8.8bn** quarterly sales (+8%) and EPS **$3.98** (+13%). The central debate is whether pricing power, electronics/helium exposure and project backlog justify a **33.5x P/E** and record share-price levels, or whether volume softness, rising leverage and a premium multiple cap upside.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value-oriented investor, Linde offers **quality-at-a-reasonable-growth-price** rather than deep cyclical cheapness. The screen’s buy signal rests on four families — cheapness, dividend, GARP and risk — with standout metrics: **PEGY 0.24**, **13.4% earnings growth**, **ROE 18.2%**, and dividend-growth model pass. That aligns with the business: long-term take-or-pay on-site contracts, merchant pricing attainment (+2–4% recent quarters), and productivity offsetting cost inflation.

Business quality is high. Filings describe a vertically integrated model — Linde designs, builds and operates plants while customers take molecules under multi-year agreements. Americas operating margins run **~31%**; EMEA **~36%**. Adjusted operating margins hold near **30%** across recent quarters. Management returns substantial cash: FY2025 buybacks **~$4.6bn** and dividends **~$2.8bn** (Yahoo fallback for cash-flow detail; see Financial Review). Earnings Quality and Magic Formula passes reflect stable cash conversion and high returns on capital versus Basic Materials peers (sector composite 57%).

The buy case is **confirmed on franchise quality and earnings trajectory**, tempered by absolute valuation: headline **P/E 33.5** and **P/B 6.1** are not statistically cheap, and news flow flags the stock trading near record levels with third-party DCF work suggesting downside to intrinsic value. The screen’s cheapness pass is growth-adjusted (PEGY), not multiple-based — appropriate for Linde, but not a classic net-of-risk bargain.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Leverage:** Total debt increased **~25%** in FY2025 to **$27.0bn**. While covenant-light and supported by investment-grade profile, rising rates (10-year UST **4.68%** per macro context) and commercial-paper reliance warrant monitoring. No going-concern language identified in available filing bodies.

**Volume cyclicality:** Base volumes declined through much of 2025, with growth dependent on new project start-ups. A manufacturing downturn could pressure merchant volumes before project backlog converts.

**Valuation:** At **P/E 33.5** and **P/B 6.1**, LIN prices in sustained quality. News cites third-party DCF work implying material overvaluation versus spot (~$506 vs ~$327). Screen cheapness is PEGY-driven, not multiple-driven.

**Energy and pass-through:** Filings flag exposure to natural gas and electricity costs, FX, and commodity inputs. Linde typically recovers via surcharges, but timing lags can compress margins in inflationary periods.

**Geopolitical and regulatory:** Forward-looking statements cite trade conflicts, tariffs, environmental and healthcare regulation, and catastrophic events. Helium export curbs (China, Russia, Qatar) are a **double-edged sword** — supportive of pricing for incumbents but politically volatile.

**Pension:** Defined-benefit obligations across multiple jurisdictions; sensitivity to discount rates and asset returns. Not flagged as acute, but a long-tail liability.

**Governance / management:** 8-K (7 Aug 2025) — EVP HR David Strauss retiring Mar 2026; Desiree Bacher appointed SVP HR effective Sep 2025. Routine succession, not a red flag.

**Legal:** Item 1 Legal Proceedings cross-referenced without material quantified exposure in available extracts. Linde states it is subject to ordinary-course litigation and regulatory investigations.

**Filing gaps:** 10-K body extracts are truncated before full Item 1A Risk Factors and income/cash-flow statements. Interim 10-Q bodies are complete for quarterly P&L. Seven of nine 8-K filings in the index lack downloadable bodies.
