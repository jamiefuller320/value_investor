BETA Close X Coca-Cola Europacific Partners plc announced on April 22, 2026, that several persons discharging managerial responsibilities (PDMRs) acquired ordinary shares. Edward Walker, Chief Financial Officer, acquired 3.725670 ordinary shares through the UK Share Plan, with a weighted average price of USD $53.65. Svetlana Walker, General Counsel and Company Secretary, also acquired 3.725670 ordinary shares under the same plan. Stephen Lusk, Chief Commercial Officer, and Leendert den Hollander, General Manager of France and Northern Europe, each acquired 3.725670 ordinary shares via the UK Share Plan. Additionally, Ana Callol, General Manager of the Iberian Business Unit, acquired 3.909854 ordinary shares from the Employee Benefit Trust following vesting of matching share awards, with a transaction price of USD $0. Jose Antonio Echeverria, Chief Customer Service and Supply Chain Officer, acquired 5.865136 ordinary shares from the Employee Benefit Trust under similar conditions, also with a transaction price of USD $0. All these transactions occurred on April 20 or 21, 2026, primarily on the Nasdaq Stock Market LLC.
