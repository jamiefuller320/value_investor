BETA Close X Coca-Cola Europacific Partners plc announced on March 27, 2026, that several Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) and their closely associated persons received grants of Performance Share Units (PSUs) under the Company's Long Term Incentive Plan. These grants, which are subject to continued service and performance conditions, will vest on March 26, 2029.
