# Baker Hughes Company (BKR) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T10:23:43.535943+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Baker Hughes is a diversified energy-technology group spanning oilfield services (OFSE) and industrial/energy technology (IET), with a record $35.9bn remaining performance obligation (RPO) and growing exposure to LNG, gas infrastructure, and data-centre power. The quantitative screen flags the name as a strong buy on cheapness (P/E 17.9, P/B 2.9), quality (ROE 17.2%), dividend, GARP, and risk metrics, with 12 of 22 models passing at full data quality. The valuation hook is a cash-generative franchise trading below 25x earnings despite a multi-year backlog build and optionality from AI/data-centre and new-energy orders. The central debate is whether IET-led structural growth and portfolio reshaping (Chart acquisition, PSI divestiture, Cactus JV) can offset OFSE cyclicality, integration risk from the pending ~$13bn Chart deal, and lumpy reported earnings.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, BKR offers a rare combination in energy services: visible backlog, improving capital returns, and a screen profile that passes both classic value (Graham Enterprising, Dreman Contrarian, Magic Formula) and quality/compounders (Buffett Quality, Quality Value, Piotroski F-Score, Earnings Quality). Debt/equity at 83% sits within the screen’s risk threshold; FCF yield and dividend-growth screens both pass, supported by FY2025 free cash flow of $2.5bn (Yahoo fallback — see Financial Review).

Business quality is anchored in IET, which holds $32.4bn of the $35.9bn RPO (FY2025 10-K) and is booking data-centre-related orders ($1bn in 2025; management targets ~$3bn cumulatively over 2025–27). Orders rose to $29.6bn in 2025 from $28.2bn in 2024, with IET orders up to $14.9bn while OFSE softened to $14.7bn — a mix shift that supports margin stability and reduces pure-play drilling cyclicality. R&D of $600m and 1,400+ patents (FY2025 10-K) underpin technology differentiation versus SLB and Halliburton in OFSE and Siemens Energy in IET.

The screen’s strong-buy signal aligns with a business transitioning from GE Oil & Gas heritage toward a higher-multiple industrial-energy platform. At 17.9x P/E versus mid-teens historical norms for diversified oilfield services, the market may still be pricing BKR as cyclical OFSE rather than a backlog-backed technology company. Conviction is building (68%, three weeks at signal, stable trend), though timing data are insufficient for a tactical overlay.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical and macro:** OFSE remains exposed to global rig counts, E&P capital discipline, and oil-price volatility. News flow ahead of Q2 2026 results cites potential Middle East conflict impacts on oilfield-services earnings (“Oil-Service Companies Set to Reveal War’s Impact on Earnings,” Bloomberg via Yahoo, 20 Jul 2026). OFSE orders declined to $14.7bn in 2025 from $15.2bn in 2024 (FY2025 10-K).

**M&A and balance-sheet:** The pending Chart Industries acquisition at $210/share in cash (announced 29 Jul 2025; shareholder approval 6 Oct 2025; HSR waiting period expired 6 Nov 2025 per 8-K) will substantially increase leverage. The FY2025 10-K explicitly flags financing risk, integration risk, and the possibility that expected synergies may not materialise. Closing is expected in Q2 2026, subject to remaining regulatory approvals.

**Supplier concentration:** Extensive dependence on GE Vernova and GE Aerospace for gas turbines, aeroderivative technology, and IP licensing. Loss or disruption of these relationships could impair IET’s core offering (FY2025 10-K Risk Factors).

**Energy-transition positioning:** FY2025 10-K language acknowledges a potential slowdown in transition spending and sustained conventional oil/gas demand — creating portfolio allocation risk if new-energy investments under-earn while traditional demand rebounds faster than planned.

**Operational and legal:** Fixed-price contract exposure with liquidated-damage risk; knock-for-knock indemnity may not protect in all jurisdictions. Environmental remediation accrual of $53m; filing states no current matters expected to be materially adverse but offers no guarantee. Russia/Ukraine sanctions exposure largely wound down but residual entity-closure complexity remains.

**Governance and earnings quality:** CFO transition to Ahmed Moghal (FY2025 10-K) from Nancy Buese (FY2024 10-K). Q1 2026 reported earnings inflated by ~$588m other income — a red flag for screen-based earnings-quality models until normalised. Pension obligation ~$1.07bn (Yahoo) is manageable relative to equity but adds long-tail risk.

**Competitive:** Intense rivalry from SLB, Halliburton, Siemens Energy, and Flowserve across overlapping product lines.
