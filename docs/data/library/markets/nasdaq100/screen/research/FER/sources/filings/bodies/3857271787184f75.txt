This dynamic system is designed to prevent congestion, keep traffic flowing in the express lanes at minimum speeds of 45 mph. When demand increases, tolls adjust upward, but as traffic lessens, tolls on the I-77 Express Lanes are lowered. This allows drivers to decide when to take advantage of the Express Lanes, making it the best option for those seeking a faster and more efficient route.
