Other Events. On November 12, 2026, Keurig Dr Pepper Inc. (the “Company”) entered into an underwriting agreement (the “Underwriting Agreement”) with Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC (the “Underwriters”), pursuant to which the Company agreed to sell 57,971,015 shares of the Company’s common stock, par value $0.01 per share (“Common Stock”), at a public offering price of $34.50 per share (the “Offering”). In connection with the Offering, the Company granted the Underwriters an option to purchase up to an additional 8,695,652 shares of Common Stock. The Underwriting Agreement includes customary representations, warranties and covenants by the Company. It also provides that the Company will indemnify the Underwriters against certain liabilities, including liabilities under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”). The foregoing description of the Underwriting Agreement is not complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the complete text of the Underwriting Agreement, a copy of which is attached as Exhibit 1.1 and incorporated herein by reference. On November 16, 2026, the Company completed the Offering, including 8,695,652 shares of Common Stock sold pursuant to the Underwriters’ exercise in full of their option to purchase additional shares. The Company received approximately $2.25 billion of net proceeds from the Offering, after deducting the underwriting discount (without deducting other offering fees and expenses). The Company intends to use the net proceeds to repay or repurchase its outstanding debt, which may include borrowings under its Delayed Draw Term Loan Agreement, commercial paper notes and senior unsecured notes. The Offering was made pursuant to the Company’s registration statement on Form S-3ASR (File No. 333-289652) (the “Registration Statement”), as supplemented by a preliminary prospectus supplement, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on November 12, 2026, and a final prospectus supplement, dated November 12, 2026, filed with the SEC on November 13, 2026 pursuant to Rule 424(b) under the Securities Act. In connection with the Offering, the legal opinion as to the legality of the Common Stock sold in the Offering is being filed as Exhibit 5.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein and into the Registration Statement by reference. On November 12, 2026, the Company issued press releases announcing the launch and the pricing of the Offering.
