# Roper Technologies, Inc. (ROP) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T10:15:24.619080+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Roper Technologies is a decentralised acquirer of vertical software and technology-enabled niche businesses, compounding revenue and cash flow through M&A and high-margin recurring models. The quantitative screen rates ROP a **strong buy** (10/22 models, 87% composite), anchored by a sub-15x P/E, modest P/B, positive FCF yield, and quality/dividend/GARP factor passes — unusual for a software-heavy compounder. The central debate is whether the current valuation adequately compensates for rising leverage, goodwill-heavy accounting, and earnings volatility from fair-value marks on the Indicor minority stake, versus durable mid-single-digit organic growth and visible backlog. Filings confirm operational momentum into 2026, but annual income-statement detail in local 10-K body extracts is truncated; treat headline EPS/FCF as Yahoo-sourced until full statements are parsed.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, ROP offers a rare intersection: **business quality typically associated with growth software** (69%+ gross margins, SaaS/deferred-revenue model, niche market leadership) at **multiples more typical of industrial value names** (screen P/E 14.8, P/B 1.9, D/E 61%, yield 1.0%, ROE 13.1%). The screen passes five factor families — cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk — including Graham Enterprising, FCF Yield, Lynch/Neff PEG variants, Magic Formula, Quality Value, Dividend Growth, Dreman Contrarian, Composite Value, and Earnings Quality.

The business model is defensible: three segments (Application Software ~57%, Network Software ~20%, Technology Enabled Products ~23%) serving sticky vertical end-markets (healthcare, legal, insurance, education, freight, campus payments). Management deployed ~$8,960m on acquisitions over the last three years (FY2025 10-K), including CentralReach (~$1,850m) and Subsplash (~$800m) in 2025, while maintaining dividend growth (quarterly raised to $0.91, +10%, November 2025). Backlog rose to $3,424.6m at 31 December 2025 (+10.3% YoY), with remaining performance obligations of $5,204.2m, supporting revenue visibility.

The screen’s strong-buy signal aligns with a name that has de-rated materially from prior highs (news coverage references ~40% drawdown from 52-week peak) whilst fundamentals remain intact. Conviction is **building** (74% screen conviction, three weeks at signal, stable trend). This is not a deep cyclical value trap; it is a compounder trading through a sentiment/valuation dislocation that quantitative cheapness metrics have captured.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Leverage and covenants:** Total debt ~$9.4–10.5bn against equity ~$19.9bn. The FY2025 10-K explicitly warns that indebtedness may limit acquisitions, dividends, and capex, and that **credit-facility covenant breaches could accelerate repayment**. Net leverage is trending up (34.9% net debt/capital, Q1 2026) alongside aggressive buybacks.

**Goodwill and intangibles:** Goodwill of $21.3bn exceeds stockholders’ equity; tangible book is deeply negative (Yahoo: -$11.2bn). Any impairment would hit reported earnings materially. Filings flag impairment risk from underperformance, competing technologies (including AI), or rising discount rates.

**Earnings quality / non-operating volatility:** Q1 2026 net earnings were flattered by a **$167.3m Indicor equity fair-value gain** (vs a $44.4m loss in Q1 2025). Indicor marks depend on comparable public-company multiples and are inherently volatile. This supports screen concerns about earnings quality (news: *“Roper Technologies' (NASDAQ:ROP) Profits Appear To Have Quality Issues”*, 9 May 2026).

**Interest-rate and refinancing:** Interest expense is rising sharply (Q1 2026: $99.3m vs $62.9m prior year). $700m of 1.0% senior notes matured September 2025; further maturities and revolver dependence add rollover risk in a 4.7% 10-year yield environment (macro context only).

**M&A and integration:** ~$9bn deployed over three years creates integration, antitrust (HSR clearance), and unforeseen-liability risk. 2025 acquisitions (CentralReach, Subsplash) are increasing amortisation and, in some cases, diluting segment margins (Network Software op. margin fell to 40.6% in Q1 2026 from 44.3%).

**Cybersecurity, privacy, AI regulation:** Extensive 10-K risk disclosure on GDPR/CCPA, healthcare (FDA/HIPAA), cloud concentration (AWS/Azure/GCP), and AI liability. Vertafore prior data-exposure litigation was dismissed, but sector risk persists.

**No going-concern flag:** Filings contain no going-concern language. Liquidity appears adequate: management states existing cash, operating cash flows, and revolver capacity are sufficient for foreseeable requirements.

**Pension:** Current filing extracts do not surface material defined-benefit pension obligations; not identified as a primary risk in available bodies.
