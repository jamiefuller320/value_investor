Our businesses rely on centrally managed commercial and technology operations, including sales capabilities, digital customer experience, product and content development and global facilities. Global Print Transaction On October 1, 2026, Thomson Reuters sold a 51% stake in its Global Print business to capital accounts advised by KKR, with the two parties forming a joint venture to operate the Global Print business, now operating as Westbridge Print. We received approximately $500 million in gross proceeds at closing and retained a 49% equity interest in the joint venture. Westbridge Print, an independent company, serves legal and tax professionals across 13 countries and provides commercial printing services to book publishers across a wide range of markets. Thomson Reuters maintains intellectual property rights and full editorial control over its content portfolio. As part of the transaction, Thomson Reuters has agreed to provide certain financial support designed to give KKR a minimum return on its equity investment in the joint venture under certain circumstances. Beginning in the fourth quarter of 2026, Westbridge Print will pay a royalty to Thomson Reuters equal to 20% of its Professional revenues (approximately 85% – 90% of its total revenues) for the exclusive license to distribute the company’s intellectual property in print and on ProView, its eBook platform. Additionally, Westbridge Print entered a agreement with Thomson Reuters whereby it will reimburse our company for certain transition services as the joint venture migrates to a entity. The royalty and transition services reimbursement are largely expected to offset the stranded costs from the separation. As a result, we expect the impact from the Global Print transaction to be approximately neutral to our total company adjusted EBITDA margin following the transaction close, as well as 60 – 70 basis points accretive to our annual organic revenue growth rate. The Global Print business has been classified as discontinued operations in the third quarter of 2026 and will no longer be presented as a reportable segment. We expect to record a gain on the transaction in the fourth quarter of 2026. Results of Operations Our revenues and operating profit on a consolidated basis do not tend to be significantly impacted by seasonality as we record a large portion of our revenues ratably over the contract term and our costs are generally incurred evenly throughout the year. However, at the segment level, revenues on a consecutive quarter basis can be impacted by seasonality, most notably in our Tax, Audit & Accounting Professionals business, where revenues tend to be concentrated in the first and fourth quarters.
