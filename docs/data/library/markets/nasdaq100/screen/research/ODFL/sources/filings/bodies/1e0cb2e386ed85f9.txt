On November 17, 2026, Old Dominion Freight Line, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into a , $500.0 million senior unsecured revolving credit facility pursuant to the terms of a fourth amended and restated credit agreement (the “Credit Agreement”), dated as of November 17, 2026, with Wells Fargo Bank, National Association, as administrative agent, and the Lenders named therein (collectively, the “Lenders”). The Credit Agreement amends and restates the terms of the Company’s existing , $400.0 million senior unsecured revolving credit facility. The following description is a summary of the material terms and conditions of the Credit Agreement. The definitions of capitalized terms, if not so defined herein, may be found in the Credit Agreement. Of the $500.0 million in line of credit commitments provided by the Lenders at closing, $100.0 million may be used for letters of credit and $75.0 million may be used for Swingline Loans. In addition, the Company may request an increase in the aggregate Commitments of up to $250.0 million, which may include a Revolving Commitment Increase or one or more Term Loan Commitments, for a total facility size of up to $750.0 million. Any such increase is subject to obtaining additional commitments from existing or new lenders and the satisfaction of certain other conditions. At the Company’s option, borrowings under the Credit Agreement shall generally bear interest at a rate based on either (i) Term SOFR or Daily Simple SOFR (each based on the Secured Overnight Financing Rate (SOFR)) plus an applicable margin that shall range from 1.000% to 1.375%; or (ii) a Base Rate plus an applicable margin that shall range from 0.000% to 0.375%. The applicable margin for each of the above options is dependent upon the Company’s Consolidated Debt to Consolidated Total Capitalization ratio. The Credit Agreement contains terms and provisions (including representations, covenants and conditions) customary for transactions of this type. The sole financial covenant is a maximum Consolidated Debt to Consolidated Total Capitalization ratio (not to be more than 0.60 to 1.00). Other covenants include, but are not limited to, mergers and consolidations, additional indebtedness, limitations on liens, disposition of assets, and restricted payments. The Credit Agreement also contains customary events of default. The foregoing description of the Credit Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Credit Agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit 4.22 and incorporated herein by reference. The Company intends to use any proceeds of the borrowings under the Credit Agreement for working capital, the issuance of Letters of Credit and general corporate purposes in accordance with the terms and provisions of the Credit Agreement. The Company has normal banking relationships with the Lenders. Item 9.01 Financial Statements and Exhibits.
