# Autodesk, Inc. (ADSK) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T11:34:42.680337+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Autodesk is a subscription-led design-and-make software platform with durable recurring revenue, accelerating top-line growth, and cash generation that screens attractively on FCF yield and growth-adjusted metrics. The quantitative model flags a Strong Buy across cheapness, quality, GARP, and risk families (7/22 models passed; FCF yield ~6.9%, Lynch PEG ~0.13, ROE ~50%). Filings show FY2027 has started strongly—Q1 net revenue rose 18% year on year to $1.93bn—with RPO of $7.81bn underpinning visibility. The central debate is whether recent share weakness and a pending ~$3.6bn MaintainX acquisition create a value entry, or whether restructuring charges, litigation overhang (appeal pending), and rising capital deployment offset the screen signal. Annual income-statement detail in local 10-K body extracts is truncated before Item 8; annual figures below rely on Yahoo where noted.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value-oriented investor, ADSK combines **quality at a relative discount**: high recurring revenue (97% of net revenue in Q1 FY2027, per 10-Q), net revenue retention slightly above management’s 100–110% target range, and FCF that the screen prices at a ~6.9% yield despite mid-teens revenue growth. The screen’s Strong Buy is not a low-quality deep-value catch—P/E ~29.9 and P/B ~13.9 are not statistically “cheap” on headline multiples—but the name passes FCF Yield, Lynch PEG, Economic Moat, Magic Formula, Piotroski F-Score, Composite Value, and Earnings Quality, indicating **cash-backed growth at a reasonable price**.

Business quality supports the signal: Autodesk holds entrenched positions in AECO, manufacturing, and media/entertainment CAD/BIM workflows; subscription transition is largely complete; and the “Make” portfolio (construction cloud, Fusion) is compounding faster than the corporate average (third-party commentary cites ~$600m construction revenue growing >20%). Direct and online transaction models are reducing distributor concentration—TD Synnex fell to 9% of Q1 FY2027 revenue from 20% a year earlier—improving economics and customer data ownership. Aggressive buybacks ($448m in Q1 FY2027 alone) align capital return with FCF. The screen’s “building” conviction (50%) and stable 3-week signal suggest the quantitative case is intact but not yet fully validated by price action—consistent with news noting shares down materially from prior highs.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Restructuring and margin:** The January 2026 Plan (sales/marketing optimisation and resource reallocation) drove $30m charges in Q1 FY2027; FY2026 annual restructuring/M&A costs were material per Yahoo. Execution risk if AutoCAD seat sales do not migrate to Industry Collections (explicit 10-K risk language).

**M&A and leverage:** Definitive agreement to acquire MaintainX for **~$3.6bn cash** (expected close FY2027, subject to regulatory approval); funding from debt and cash. Raises integration risk and pro forma leverage; goodwill already ~$4.3bn.

**Governance / litigation:** Internal Investigation (commenced March 2024) into free cash flow and operating margin practices led to a federal securities class action. District court **dismissed with prejudice** (26 January 2026); **plaintiffs appealed** (opening brief May 2026). Company states loss not reasonably estimable—overhang remains.

**Concentration and macro:** International revenue ~64% (10-K); tariff/trade-policy volatility extensively flagged in risk factors (including 2025–2026 U.S. tariff actions). TD Synnex concentration declining but still meaningful.

**Competition / AI:** Intense competition from Adobe, Dassault, PTC, Bentley, Trimble, Procore, and AI-native entrants; risk that AI disrupts pricing or displaces seat growth.

**Accounting / quality:** Heavy stock-based compensation ($788m FY2026 per Yahoo; $155m in Q1 FY2027 per 10-Q); negative tangible equity; deferred revenue and RPO are positive for visibility but tie reported revenue to billing timing. Pension/other post-retirement liabilities ~$659m current (Yahoo FY2026 balance sheet).

**Pension:** Defined-benefit pension components in accumulated other comprehensive loss; not flagged as material going-concern issue.
