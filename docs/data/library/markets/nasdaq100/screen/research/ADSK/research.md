# Autodesk, Inc. (ADSK) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T10:41:38.060315+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Autodesk is a subscription-led design-and-make software franchise (AutoCAD, Revit, Fusion, construction cloud) with durable recurring revenue, high gross margins, and accelerating free cash flow generation. The quantitative screen flags a **Strong Buy** on cheapness (FCF yield ~6.9%), quality (ROE ~50%), GARP (PEG ~0.13), and risk metrics, against a share price that news flow describes as materially below prior highs. Filings confirm mid-teens organic revenue growth and strong Q1 FY2027 profitability, but also highlight billings-model transition noise (seasonal declines in deferred revenue and RPO), heavy intangibles, and cyclical AEC exposure. **Key debate:** whether the market is over-discounting a high-quality compounder on timing effects, or correctly pricing slower billings growth and AI/competitive disruption risk.

---

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen’s Strong Buy rests on four families passing simultaneously—**cheapness, quality, GARP, and risk**—which is unusual for a Nasdaq-100 software name trading at ~30× earnings. That alignment is plausible here: subscription revenue is **97% of net revenue** (Q1 FY2027 10-Q), NR3 sits **slightly above the 100–110% target range**, and recurring revenue grew **18% YoY to $1.88bn** in the quarter. Operating leverage is visible: Q1 FY2027 income from operations was **$541m vs $233m** prior year, with gross profit **$1.76bn on $1.93bn revenue** (~91% gross margin per filing statements).

For a value investor, the hook is **cash, not headline P/E alone**. Yahoo fallback for FY2026 (year ended 31 January 2026) shows **FCF of $2.38bn** on **$7.21bn revenue**—roughly 33% FCF margin—and net debt of only **$234m** against **$2.25bn cash**. The screen’s FCF figure (~$3.1bn) is higher than Yahoo’s annual FCF; we treat **filings/Yahoo as primary** and note the screen may reflect a different timing or definition. ROE of ~50% reflects both earnings power and a **thin equity base** (accumulated deficit from sustained buybacks, negative tangible book), so quality is real but not “asset-heavy.”

Business quality supports the screen: mission-critical workflows in AEC and manufacturing, switching costs, and a pivot to direct/digital sales (TD Synnex fell to **9% of Q1 revenue vs 20%** a year ago). Construction/AEC remains a structural tailwind—news cites ~$600m annual revenue growing >20% from earlier acquisitions. At ~30× P/E with mid-teens growth and ~7% FCF yield, the name fits **GARP/value hybrid** rather than deep cyclical value.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**From FY2026 10-K risk factors (filing body):**
- **Concentration:** substantial revenue from a small number of solutions (AutoCAD ecosystem and collections); failure to upsell collections remains an explicit risk.
- **Competition & AI:** rapid tech change; AI/ML features may fail to gain adoption or operate as expected; governance challenges around AI in offerings.
- **Macro & geopolitical:** global economic/political conditions, trade tensions, emerging-market FX; ~37% of revenue via indirect channel.
- **Subscription model execution:** uncertainty in renewal rates, new transaction/billing models, and restructuring plan outcomes; explicit warning of potential **revenue, billings, earnings, and cash-flow shortfalls**.
- **Cyber & IP:** security incidents; **IP litigation** from third-party patent claims—“costly litigation” acknowledged historically.
- **Debt:** “debt service obligations” listed as a risk (notes payable **~$2.5bn** on Q1 balance sheet); no going-concern language found.
- **International:** significant non-US revenue and regulatory exposure.

**Financial-structure flags (filings + Yahoo):**
- **Negative tangible equity** and large goodwill/intangibles (~$4.8bn)—equity cushion is thin if growth slows.
- **Negative retained earnings / accumulated deficit** (~$1.4bn)— driven by buybacks, not operating losses, but limits balance-sheet flexibility optics.
- **Billings transition:** Q1 shows **RPO and deferred revenue down seasonally** vs Q4; misread as deceleration if not adjusted for annual-billing shift.
- **Customer concentration shift:** TD Synnex revenue mix falling fast—positive for margins/direct control, but channel disruption risk if poorly managed.
- **Restructuring:** Q1 FY2027 still incurred **$30m** restructuring; FY2026 Yahoo shows **$216m** related charges—execution risk on cost programmes.
- **Pension:** defined-benefit pension items immaterial in Q1 comprehensive income; not a primary red flag in extracts.
- **No acquisitions in FY2026** (10-K)—organic story, but limits inorganic diversification.

**Cyclical:** AEC/construction exposure makes revenue sensitive to capital spending cycles; macro markers (US 10y ~4.7%) are background only—not used to override the screen.
