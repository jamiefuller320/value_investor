Statement from the Chairwoman and the Chief 
Executive Officer
Shareholders,
The year 2025 was a year of milestones, confirmations and achievements unprecedented in the history of Poste Italiane. We 
celebrated ten years since listing on the stock exchange, a milestone that is not just an anniversary, but the concrete sign of a 
path built with a clear vision: we have transformed a historic Italian company into a modern, dynamic and sustainable platform, 
deeply rooted in its service mission and projected towards the future.
We have shown that a company with solid roots can be a driver of innovation and growth, generating and distributing value for 
shareholders and strengthening its role as a strategic infrastructure for Italy. Since 2016, Poste Italiane has distributed almost 
€9 billion in dividends, guaranteeing attractive yet sustainable yields.
In recent years, Poste Italiane has built a true Platform Company, capable of combining the solidity of traditional services with 
the power of digital innovation. Poste Italiane represents the connective tissue of the country: a national, reliable and inclusive 
infrastructure capable of combining territorial proximity, advanced technologies and industrial capacity, and of contributing to 
Italy’s economic and social growth in the long term.
The 2025 financial statements confirm the validity of our path: we have recorded the best results in our history despite an 
international scenario marked by geopolitical and economic uncertainties and accentuated market volatility. All business areas 
contributed to the achievement of the targets, thanks to solid business dynamics, strict cost discipline and consistent returns 
on the investment portfolio. Revenue reached an all-time high of €13.1 billion, the adjusted operating profit (EBIT) reached a 
record level of €3.24 billion and net profit stood at €2.22 billion. 
The impact on the national economy was equally significant with a €14 billion contribution to GDP , 182 thousand jobs support-
ed and €2.6 billion in tax revenue generated.
In 2025, postal savings turned 150 years old, the culmination of a history in which we, together with Cassa Depositi e Prestiti, 
have played a leading role. With around 27 million subscribers and a stock of €320 billion, postal savings continue to represent 
a pillar of trust and stability, supporting Italy’s economic and social development and accompanying millions of citizens in the 
realisation of their life plans.

Presentation of the Annual Report
For the eighth consecutive year, the Annual Report of the Poste Italiane Group, presents both financial and sustainability infor-
mation in an integrated manner. It aims to provide a clear, accurate and transparent representation of the Group’s activities 
performance, achieved results and the services offered.
This document, which had already been significantly revised in the previous reporting year in order to comply with the require-
ments of the EU 2022/2464 Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), implemented in Italy by Legislative Decree 
125/2024, has been brought into line with the most recent developments in the European regulatory framework on sustaina -
bility reporting, with particular reference to the extension of the phasing-in provisions set forth in the Commission’s Delegated 
Regulation (EU) 2025/1416 of 11 July 2025 (the so-called “Quick Fix”) and the simplification of the classification system of 
economic activities and sustainable investments within the EU Taxonomy provided for in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/73 
of 4 July 2025, achieving alignment with the most recent EU requirements. In addition, the Poste Italiane Group has taken into 
account the ESMA priorities in terms of sustainability disclosure, including key aspects such as double materiality, the scope 
of consolidation and the structure of Sustainability Report, as outlined in the ESRS. Therefore, in application of the provisions 
of Legislative Decree no. 125/2024, sustainability information was prepared in accordance with the European Sustainability 
Reporting Standards (ESRS) developed by the European Financial Reporting Advisory Group (EFRAG) and included within a 
specific section of the Report on Operations - Chapter 8 “The Sustainability Report”.
In line with Appendix F of ESRS 1, the Sustainability Report is structured into the following four sections: 1) “General informa-
tion”, containing the information referred to in ESRS 2 “General information”; 2) “Environmental information”, containing the 
information referred to in ESRS E1 “Climate change”, E2 “Pollution” and E5 “Resource use and circular economy”, as well 
as the information related to the EU Taxonomy; 3) “Social information”, containing the information set out in ESRS S1 “Own 
workforce”, S2 “Workers in the value chain”, S3 “Affected communities” and S4 “Consumers and end-users”; 4) “Governance 
information”, containing the information set out in ESRS G1 “Business conduct” and Entity-Specific “Sustainable finance”. 
With particular reference to the information on the European Taxonomy, the Poste Italiane Group has decided to maintain an 
approach of methodological continuity for the 2025 financial year, preparing the disclosure on the EU Taxonomy in line with 
what was adopted in the previous year. Therefore, Regulation (EU) 2021/2178, Regulation (EU) 2021/2139 and Regulation (EU) 
2023/2486 were adopted in the version applicable on 31 December 2025. 
In addition, the “General information” section of the Sustainability Report describes the methodology adopted by the Group 
for the double materiality analysis, the process of which complies with the ESRS standards and the Materiality Assessment 
Implementation Guidance (MAIG Guidance Line) developed by EFRAG. This analysis enabled the identification of the most 
relevant sustainability issues for the Poste Italiane Group, adopting an integrated approach that took into account both the 
positive and negative impacts on people and the environment that can be generated or could be generated through the 
performance of business activities, and the sustainability risks and opportunities that could influence the Group’s economic 
and financial performance. In addition, within the “General information” section, under the heading “Index of ESRS content 
and content from other EU legislation”, there is a list of the disclosure requirements reported within the Sustainability Report, 
associated with the issues found to be relevant for the Group as a result of the double materiality analysis conducted, as well 
as their positioning within the document.
In line with previous years, the document highlights how sustainability is now deeply integrated into the Group’s business 
model, offering a comprehensive representation of financial, environmental, social and governance performance. Poste Italiane 
Group’s sustainability strategy is embodied in a set of annual and multi-year objectives, designed to ensure maximum trans -
parency for stakeholders regarding the Group’s commitment and the progress achieved. 
Through this approach, the Group aims to promote a more inclusive society and a more sustainable future, operating accord-
ing to a sustainability-driven business model that fosters the creation of shared value and generates significant impacts in line 
with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), as better illustrated in the table below. 
The evolution of the Group’s business model has enabled it to actively involve players along the value chain, encouraging them 
to adopt increasingly responsible conduct, and fostering a strong interrelationship between the resources used, the outputs 
generated and the outcomes/benefits achieved and delivered to stakeholders, both financially, socially and environmentally.

3. Outlook
In insurance, the Group in 2026 will be committed to improving net flows in the Investment and Pension business supported 
by improved domestic life market conditions, more competitive insurance portfolios in terms of return and a strong focus of 
the sales network. In Protection, the Group confirms its role in supporting the reduction of the country’s under-insurance in a 
growing market by exploiting cross-selling opportunities with investment products and third-party networks. 
In the course of 2026, Poste Italiane with its PostePay service offering will continue its commitment to accompany the chang-
ing habits of consumers, businesses and Public Administration and to create an ecosystem of integrated services through 
omnichannel, sustainable and transparent solutions. In particular, in the payments business, the aim is to enrich and optimise 
the services offered, with a particular focus on digital channels to improve the customer experience and transactional perfor -
mance, capitalising on the leadership in e-commerce and maintaining a solid grip on regulatory developments. In the area of 
telecommunications, the migration to the TIM mobile network infrastructure is scheduled to be completed during the year, 
which will bring improved customer service as well as higher profitability. 
Several sites will be dedicated to the development of the partnership with TIM to launch new initiatives and generate synergies 
between the two groups. Sales of the PosteEnergia offer are already active in the about 700 TIM shops while, in the last quarter 
of 2025, TIM enhanced its insurance protection offer for SMEs with cyber, catastrophe and legal protection cover provided 
by the Poste Vita Group and, from the first quarter of 2026, extended the perimeter to retail customers with home and cyber 
cover.
In 2026, the Group will continue with the implementation of “Polis”, a strategic project to support the country’s social cohesion, 
which involves approximately 7,000 municipalities with less than 15 thousand inhabitants, in which the Post Office will be trans-
formed into a hub of digital services for rapid and easy access to the Public Administration’s services. Some 250 co-working 
spaces nationwide are also planned, as well as the implementation of numerous initiatives to support the country’s energy 
transition. Since the start of the project, about 4,800 Post Offices and 160 Spaces for Italy (co-working) have been completed.

4.5.1.3 The logistics network 
The Group’s mail and parcel services are provided through two integrated and synergistic logistics networks: the postal logis-
tics network for the management of mail, now evolved to help manage small parcels and the parcel logistics network. The 
delivery of these small parcels in Italy can be carried out synergistically by the postal logistics network and the courier logistics 
network according to a dynamic approach, aimed at maximising efficiency for each individual area. 
As part of the transformation plan for the Mail and Parcels segment and with the aim of making the Poste Italiane Group an 
integrated logistics operator, an important initiative was launched in 2023 to develop the Integrated Logistics market seg -

acquisition of the company Plurima, which has been operating in the hospital sector for several years, offering logistics solu -
tions and services such as outsourced pharmaceutical warehouse management.
As of February 2024, the Poste Italiane Group is also active in fresh food logistics, through the refrigerated transport service

During 2025, the market in the Investment and Pension business recorded positive net inflows of €9.9 billion, after a 2023 
and 2024 strongly affected by the macroeconomic context, an improvement of €13.4 billion compared to 2024, when it was 
negative. This result is mainly attributable to the growth in gross inflows (+7.4% compared to 2024), mainly related to class III 
products, and the decrease in outflows (-4.6% compared to 2024) mainly related to lower lapses of class I and III products. 
The average lapse rate  recorded in the market at 31 December 2025 was 9.40%106, down from the value in 2024 (10.38%).
In the first nine months of 2025, the Protection insurance market continued on a path of robust growth in terms of premium 
inflows, recording, at 30 September 2025, €21.2 billion in premiums for non-motor P&C business (+7.6% compared to the 
same period in 2024), and €15 billion in premiums for motor business (+5.9% y/y), due not only to the positive trend in demand 
for Motor business, but also to an increase in rates as a result of the high inflation of recent years. In addition, premiums from 
the Protection segment of the Life business recorded gross inflows during the period of €2.5 billion (+€0.2 billion), compared 
to €2.3 billion in the same period of 2024.
