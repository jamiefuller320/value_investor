AGM Statement SIMEC Atlantis Energy Limited 18 July 2024 SIMEC Atlantis Energy Limited ("SAE Renewables", "SAE" or the "Company") 18 July 2024 Result of AGM SAE announces that at the Annual General Meeting of the Company held today, all resolutions put to shareholders were duly passed, with the exception of special resolution 8 which was not passed. All resolutions were decided on a poll. The Company will engage with the largest of those shareholders that voted against Resolution 8 to understand their reasoning.
