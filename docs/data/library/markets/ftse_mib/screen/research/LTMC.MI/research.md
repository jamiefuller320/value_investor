## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Lottomatica Group S.p.A. is Italy’s consolidated gaming and betting operator, rated **buy** by the quantitative screen on growth-adjusted cheapness (PEG 0.78, FCF yield 6.4%) and quality metrics (ROE 39%, Piotroski 7/9), despite headline multiples that look expensive (P/E 31.2, P/B 16.8). Yahoo-sourced financials show a clear earnings and cash-flow inflection: FY2025 revenue of €2.25bn (+12% YoY), EBITDA €724m (+15%), net income €174m (+77%), and FCF €406m (+18%), with management raising 2026 guidance after a strong Q1. The central debate is whether online volume growth and cost synergies can sustain deleveraging against ~€1.84bn net debt, ~€187m annual interest, and goodwill-heavy balance sheet, in a heavily regulated sector. Primary regulatory filings were not retrieved; this memo relies on Yahoo financials and news headlines, limiting covenant, pension, and contingency verification.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen flags LTMC as a **buy** (7/22 models, composite 59%, five factor families: cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, risk) with improving but still low conviction (37%, one week at signal). The case is not classic deep value—the stock fails Graham, Schloss, Deep Value, Low P/E, and Financial Health screens—but rather **GARP plus cash-generation quality**: Lynch PEG (0.78), Neff PEGY, FCF Yield (6.4%), Magic Formula, Dividend Growth, Piotroski F-Score (7/9), and Earnings Quality all pass.

Business quality, per Yahoo data, supports the screen’s growth narrative. Revenue has compounded from €1.39bn (2022) to €2.25bn (2025); EBITDA from €407m to €724m; net income from €72m to €174m. Operating cash flow exceeded net income in FY2025 (€635m vs €174m), and FCF of €406m (~€366m per screen) covers dividends (€78m) and buybacks (€127m) while funding capex and intangible investment. ROE of 39% reflects post-acquisition equity base and buyback shrinkage rather than asset-light economics alone.

For a value investor, the hook is **earnings power growing faster than the price implies** (screen growth estimate 40%), with management returning capital via dividends (1.8% yield) and an active buyback programme. Q1 2026 Yahoo quarterly data (revenue €600m, EBITDA €204m, net income €66m) suggests momentum continues into FY2026, corroborated by news of raised guidance. The screen’s buy signal is directionally supported by fundamentals, but headline valuation and leverage cap upside until primary filings confirm debt terms and regulatory outlook.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Evidence limitation:** Without filing bodies, going-concern statements, debt covenants, litigation contingencies, and lease/pension footnotes cannot be quoted. The following risks are inferred from Yahoo data, screen failures, and sector context.

**Leverage and interest burden:** Net debt ~€1.84bn and interest expense ~€187m consume a large share of EBIT (~€463m). Coverage near 2.5× leaves limited buffer if EBITDA normalises or rates re-price on refinancing. The screen’s **Financial Health** model failure aligns with this. Q1 2026 earnings-call coverage (Yahoo Finance headline, 7 May 2026) mentions “strategic refinancing,” but terms are unverified without filings.

**Intangible-heavy balance sheet:** Goodwill of €2.08bn and other intangibles €745m against equity of €325m imply impairment risk if Italian gaming volumes disappoint or regulatory changes compress margins. Tangible book is deeply negative (−€2.5bn); the stock fails low P/B and asset-based value screens.

**Liquidity and working capital:** Negative working capital (−€417m) and low cash (€144m vs €2.06bn gross debt) suggest reliance on operating cash generation and revolving facilities—details unavailable without interim reports.

**Regulatory and competitive:** Italian gaming is licence-concentrated and politically sensitive. No primary filing language on licence renewals, tax changes, or advertising restrictions is in the source pack. Competitive pressure from online operators and potential market liberalisation/tightening are unquantified here.

**Governance and capital allocation:** Aggressive buybacks (€127m FY2025 plus ongoing 2026 purchases) while net debt rose (€1.77bn to €1.84bn) and equity fell may benefit EPS but increase financial risk if the cycle turns.

**Tax and earnings quality:** Effective tax rate jumped to ~35% in 2025; large non-cash adjustments in cash-flow statement require audit confirmation. Piotroski passes earnings quality, but screen conviction remains low (37%).

**Cyclicality:** Classified Consumer Cyclical; gaming spend can be discretionary. Macro context (Euro Stoxx 50, EUR/USD) is neutral backdrop only and does not alter this assessment.
