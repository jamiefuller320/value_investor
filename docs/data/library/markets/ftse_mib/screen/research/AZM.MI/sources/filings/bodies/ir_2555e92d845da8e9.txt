The Board of Directors of Azimut Holding  S.p.A. (AZM.IM) has approved today the consolidated financial results 
as of 30 June 2025. 
 
Pietro Giuliani, Chairman of the Group, states: “Our strong net profit and growth trajectory in this half-year are 
the result of investments made in people and the business for more than 35 years . Our full-year 2025 guidance1 
of over € 1 billion in net profit and more than € 30 billion in net inflows  would position us, for the second year in a 
row, as  Italy’s number one player.  Cohesion, discipline and courage, which have always been our Group's 
guiding values, will continue to light the way in the interests of our clients and shareholders. Since our listing about 
21 years ago, we have delivered an excepti onal 20x to shareholders  (capital appreciation plus dividends ), 
turning a € 100,000 investment at listing into  more than € 2 million today. This performance places Azimut now in 
second place within the FTSE MIB index, just behind Recordati and ahead of Interpump – companies with whom 
we are honoured to share the podium.” 
 
Giorgio Medda, CEO of  the Group, comments: “This has been a defining semester for Azimut . We exceeded 
expectations across the board  and navigated volatile market conditions with resilience and discipline. At the 
same time, we laid the groundwork for the Group’s next phase of growth. We are accelerating our global 
expansion, streamlining our structure, and enhancing transparency . The acquisition of NSI –  which consolidates 
the US as our second-largest market with $50 billion in assets –  underscores the strategic importance of the US in 
our long -term vision . Meanwhile, our strategic alliances with Italian champions like Ferrari and Eni Next 
demonstrate our unique positioning as both a global investor and a promoter of national excellence. We look 
ahead to the second half with confidence, a strong product pipeline, and AI -powered financial planning tools 
to empower our financial advisors and deliver personalization at scale for our clients.” 
 
Alessandro Zambotti, CEO and CFO of the Group, adds: “ We are extremely proud of our robust first -half 2025 as

1H 2025 Financials  
Azimut Holding total revenues amounted to € 646 million in 1 H 2025, driven by a 7% increase in recurring fees 
across all core markets, with particularly strong contributions from Italy, USA, Brazil, Singapore and Turkey. 
Recurring insurance revenues grew by 11% year-on-year, supported by underlying asset growth and product mix 
evolution. While performance fees were lower in the first half year, reflecting market dynamics, Azimut’s diversified 
and recurring business model continues to deliver stable, high-quality earnings.  
 
Total operating costs  amounted to € 355 million in 1 H 2025 and increased in line with the Group’s continued 
international expansion and commercial momentum. 
 
Operating profit (EBIT) amounted to € 291 million in 1H 2025, with recurring EBIT3 growing 9% year-on-year to € 281 
million. 
 
Finance income amounted to € 43 million in 1H 2025, and included, among others, € 21m of assets and portfolio 
performance, € 12m fair -value options & equity participations, € 6m dividends from the Group’s GP staking 
activities and net results of strategic affiliates, as well as € 6 million of net interest earned.  
 
As a result, net profit equalled to € 240 million in 1H 2025, with recurring net profit4 growing by 18% to € 234 million 
and reflecting the resilience and scalability of Azimut’s business model. 
 
 
FY 2025 Guidance 
Based on the strong commercial momentum to date and the M&A transactions executed, the Group upgrades 
its targets for 2025, estimating, subject to the closing of the NSI transaction by year-end 2025, total net inflows of 
€ 28-31 billion (from previous € 10bn) . Net profit is projected to exceed € 1 billion (from previous ~€ 1bn) , 
dependent on TNB receiving authorization to operate in 2025 and subject to final accounting treatment of the 
transaction upon closing.

Dati finanziari del 1° semestre 2025  
I ricavi totali del Gruppo Azimut si attestano a € 646 milioni nel primo semestre 2025, sostenuti da un aumento del 
7% dei ricavi ricorrenti in tutti i mercati chiave, con contributi particolarmente solidi da Italia, Stati Uniti, Brasile, 
Singapore e Turchia. I ricavi ricorrenti da prodotti assicurativi sono cresciuti dell’11% su base annua, trainati dalla 
crescita delle masse sottostanti e da un’evoluzione favorevole del mix di prodotto. Nonostante un minor apporto 
delle commissioni di performance  nel semestre, in linea con l’evoluzione dei mercati, il modello di business di 
Azimut, fondato su flussi ricorrenti, continua a generare risultati stabili e di elevata qualità.  
 
I costi operativi totali ammontano a € 355 milioni nel primo semestre 2025, registrando un aumento coerente con 
il percorso di espansione internazionale del Gruppo e con la costante dinamicità commerciale. 
 
L'utile operativo (EBIT) si attesta a € 291 milioni nel primo semestre 2025, mentre l’EBIT ricorrente3 cresce del 9% su 
base annua, raggiungendo € 281 milioni. 
 
I proventi finanziari, pari a € 43 milioni nel primo semestre 2025, includono, inter alia, € 21 milioni di performance 
di asset e portafoglio, € 12 milioni di opzioni e partecipazioni azionarie al fair value, € 6 milioni di dividendi derivanti 
dalle attività di GP staking del Gruppo e dai risultati netti delle partecipate  strategiche, nonché € 6 milioni di 
interessi netti attivi. 
 
Azimut chiude il primo semestre 2025 con un utile netto di € 240 milioni e un utile netto ricorrente4 in crescita del 
18% a € 234 milioni, a ulteriore conferma della stabilità e dell’affidabilità dei risultati generati dal modello di 
business del Gruppo.

gli obiettivi fissati per il 2025 stimando, condizionatamente al closing dell’operazione di NSI entro fine 2025 , una
