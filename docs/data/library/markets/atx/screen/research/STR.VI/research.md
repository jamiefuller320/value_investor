Business quality, as inferred from Yahoo trends, is improving rather than stagnant. ROE ~17% and dividend growth (cash dividends paid rose from €173m in 2022 to €243m in 2025) support the dividend and GARP passes, even though yield is not top-quartile (failed High Dividend Yield). Failures are instructive rather than disqualifying: Graham Defensive (current ratio 1.23 vs 2.0 threshold), Graham Net-Net (NCAV unavailable), Schloss/Deep Value (P/B above 1.0), Economic Moat (ROE below 18%, ROA below 8%, margins below 12%), and High Dividend Yield—not a broken balance sheet or earnings collapse story.

The signal is new (one week at Strong Buy; conviction score 0%; timing data insufficient), so the quantitative case is fresh and untested by price persistence. Contract-win news flow (Germany, Czech Republic, Slovenia) and UK bolt-on M&A (Crofton Engineering, April 2026) are consistent with an active order book, but do not substitute for verified backlog, margin guidance, or segment disclosure from primary filings.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical exposure.** Construction and civil-engineering revenue is inherently cyclical; the screen’s cyclical overlay is not flagged, but thin operating margins (~5%) leave little buffer if European infrastructure or building spend slows. FCF volatility (2022 €182m vs 2023 €1.28bn) illustrates sensitivity to working-capital swings.

**Liquidity and working capital.** Current ratio 1.23 fails conservative liquidity screens. Large receivables, inventory, and payables (accounts payable €2.98bn FY2025) create execution and collection risk on long-duration projects—unquantified without filing narrative.

**Provisions and unusual items.** Yahoo shows substantial provisions (~€2.8bn combined current and long-term) and recurring special charges/write-offs. Without filing bodies, contingency quantification, litigation reserves, and warranty exposure cannot be assessed from primary language.

**Competitive / moat.** Failed Economic Moat model (ROE <18%, ROA <8%, margins <12%) signals commodity-like economics despite scale. Strabag wins large public contracts (news flow), but pricing power and backlog visibility are unverified.

**Governance / ownership.** Strabag has a concentrated ownership structure (Haselsteiner family influence is widely known in market commentary, but not documented in this source pack). No governance or related-party disclosures were available from filings.

**M&A integration.** April 2026 acquisition of Crofton Engineering (UK) adds integration execution risk; size appears modest relative to group revenue but is unquantified in available sources.

**Data and accounting verification.** Zero primary filings is the largest red flag for a verify-before-trade workflow: no independent check on revenue recognition (percentage-of-completion), pension obligations, off-balance-sheet commitments, or covenant compliance. Yahoo normalised vs reported earnings divergence (~€69m FY2025) warrants scrutiny.
