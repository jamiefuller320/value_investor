Disclaimer* Wolfram Resources PLC 16 November 2026 16 November 2026 WOLFRAM RESOURCES PLC Response to press speculation and Suspension of Listing and Trading Wolfram Resources Plc (LSE: WFR, “ Wolfram ”, the “ Company ”), notes recent press speculation. The Company confirms that it is in an active discussion regarding the potential acquisition of Ellipses Pharma Ltd (the “Potential Acquisition” ). Suspension of Ordinary Shares As the Potential Acquisition would be classified as an “initial transaction” under the FCA’s Listing Rules (UKLR 13.4.2R), the Company has requested, in accordance with UKLR 21.1.4G and UKLR 21.3, that the FCA suspend the listing of its Ordinary Shares on the Equity Shares (Shell Companies) category of the FCA Official List with effect from 7.30 a.m. today. Trading in the Ordinary Shares on the LSE's Main Market will also be suspended. The suspension is expected to remain in place until either: (i) completion of the Potential Acquisition, at which point the Company expects the listing of its Ordinary Shares to be cancelled and that it will apply for the re ‑ admission of its enlarged Ordinary Share capital to the Equity Shares (Commercial Companies) category of the FCA Official List; or (ii) an announcement that the Potential Acquisition will not proceed, following which it is expected that the suspension of the Company’s listing and trading will be lifted, subject to FCA approval, and that trading in the Ordinary Shares will recommence. The Company will make further announcements in relation to the Potential Acquisition as appropriate in accordance with its obligations under the UK Market Abuse Regulation and the Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules. This announcement contains information which, prior to its disclosure, was inside information for the purpose of the Market Abuse Regulation (as in force in the United Kingdom). Wolfram intends to appoint a Sponsor in relation to this transaction.
