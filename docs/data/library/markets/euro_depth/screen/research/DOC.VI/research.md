## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
DO & CO is an Austrian premium catering and hospitality group (airline catering, event catering, restaurants) that the quantitative screen rates as a **buy**, driven by quality, GARP, and cash-generation factors rather than deep value. Yahoo-sourced financials show a multi-year compounding story: revenue rose from €1.42bn (FY2023) to €2.46bn (FY2026), with net income more than tripling to €106m and free cash flow nearly doubling to €192m in the latest year. The valuation hook is growth at a reasonable price—P/E ~20.5x, FCF yield ~8.1%, PEGY ~0.15, ROE ~25.8%—supported by a perfect Piotroski F-Score (9/9). The key debate is whether record margins and cash conversion are sustainable against airline customer concentration, travel/event cyclicality, and a recent quarter showing softer revenue and net income year-on-year; absent primary filings, covenant, pension, and contingency language cannot be verified from source.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value-oriented investor, DOC.VI qualifies as a **quality compounder at a fair price**, not a net-net or Graham defensive name. The screen passes five factor families—cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk—and ten individual models including FCF Yield, Neff PEGY, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, Dividend Growth, Magic Formula, Acquirer's Multiple, Piotroski F-Score, Earnings Quality, and Financial Health. Failed models (Graham Defensive/Enterprising, Deep Value, Schloss Low P/B, High Dividend Yield) confirm the stock is **not statistically cheap** on headline multiples (P/B 4.4x, P/E 20.5x, dividend yield 1.2%), but the pass set aligns with a business delivering superior returns on capital and accelerating cash generation.

Business quality indicators from Yahoo are strong: ROE ~25.8%, operating cash flow €244m vs net income €106m (cash conversion well above reported earnings), and gross margin expanding from ~24% (FY2023) to ~27% (FY2026). FCF of €192m (FY2026) against a market cap implied by an 8.1% FCF yield suggests the market still prices some execution risk. Statutory earnings growth of ~16% and a PEGY of 0.15 indicate the growth trajectory has not been fully capitalised. The investment case rests on DO & CO's positioning in premium airline and event catering—segments with high switching costs and brand sensitivity—translating revenue momentum into durable free cash flow, while accepting that headline valuation screens will always flag the name as "expensive."

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Customer concentration:** Simplywall.st (13 August 2026) explicitly frames "record margins" against "concentration risk." Airline catering revenue is inherently tied to a small number of carrier contracts; contract loss or renegotiation could compress margins rapidly. No primary filing quantifies top-customer exposure.

**Cyclicality:** Revenue is linked to global air travel, premium events (Formula 1, concerts), and discretionary restaurant spending. The screen's cyclical overlay did not trigger, but the business remains macro-sensitive. Latest quarter revenue decline (−2.8% YoY, Yahoo) warrants monitoring.

**Lease and leverage structure:** Capital lease obligations of €241m (FY2026, Yahoo) represent a fixed charge burden alongside rent/landing fees of €123m in the income statement. Without filing maturity schedules, refinancing and covenant risk is unverified.

**Recurring special charges:** €20–35m of unusual items annually for four consecutive years raises questions about underlying earnings quality and whether impairments reflect operational issues or acquisition integration.

**Minority interest complexity:** Rising minority stakes and charges suggest significant JV/partnership structures where group shareholders capture less than 100% of economic profit; attributable net income growth understates group-level performance but also obscures capital allocation to partners.

**Liquidity / working capital:** Current ratio 1.44 fails Graham Defensive threshold (<2.0). Inventory rose to €54m (FY2026) from €45m (FY2023); receivables €265m on €2.46bn revenue (~39 days implied). Not alarming but unverified against filing ageing.

**Governance / key person:** DO & CO is founder-led (Doğudan family); high insider ownership is noted in Yahoo screen context ("European Growth Companies With High Insider Ownership," June 2026) but governance disclosures are absent from the pack.

**Valuation risk:** P/B 4.4x and P/E 20.5x offer limited margin of safety if growth decelerates; Lynch PEG of 1.26 failed the screen threshold.

**Filing gap (meta-risk):** Unable to confirm going concern, litigation contingencies, pension deficits, or debt covenants from primary sources—this is the most material red flag for a verify-before-trade workflow.
