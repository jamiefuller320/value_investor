Corpus Resources Plc (" Corpus " or the " Company ") Appointment of Non-Executive Director Fundraising PDMR Dealings 23 April 2026 Corpus Resources plc (LSE: COR) is pleased to announce the appointment of James Stenhouse as a Non-Executive Director of the Company with immediate effect. The Company is also pleased to announce the successful completion of a fundraising, in aggregate, £311,000 (before expenses) at a price of £0.0001 per ordinary share, processed by AlbR Capital Limited. The Board is delighted to welcome James to the Board. He brings significant technical, operational and commercial experience in the international oil and gas sector, which the Board believes will be of considerable value as the Company continues to assess and pursue oil and gas project opportunities. The Board is also pleased with the successful outcome of the private placement, which provides the Company with additional working capital and strengthens its ability to continue progressing its strategic review of suitable oil and gas opportunities. Appointment of James Stenhouse Mr. James Stenhouse is a highly accomplished well operations and oil and gas executive with over 19 years of international industry experience, with expertise in well testing, completions, production operations, field development and well operations. He is the Founder and Managing Director of H2Oil Group, a business he has led since 2009, driving its expansion across multiple international markets, including Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific and the Americas. Mr. Stenhouse has also played a leading role in technical and commercial due diligence, including support for H2Oil's acquisition of a 50% interest in the EIV-1 gas-producing licence area in Suceava, Romania. Mr. Stenhouse is a Member of the Petroleum Club of Romania and an active member of, and mentor within, the Society of Petroleum Engineers. The Board believes that James's strong technical and operational background, together with his commercial and transaction experience, will materially enhance the Company's capabilities as it evaluates prospective opportunities in the upstream oil and gas sector. Directorships and further disclosures in relation to James Stenhouse are as follows: Current directorships / partnerships: · H2Oil Limited (UK) · H2Oil Well Services SRL (Romania) · H2Oil LLC (Ukraine) · Poseidon Energy Services Limited (UK) Previous directorships / partnerships held within the last five years: · Valmont Energy SRL (Romania) Save as set out above, there is no further information to be disclosed in respect of James Stenhouse pursuant to UKLR 6.4.8R. Result of Private Placement The Company has successfully raised £311,000 through the private placement by the issue of 3,110,000,000 new ordinary shares of £0.0001 each in the Company at a price of £0.0001 per ordinary share. The private placement includes subscriptions of: · £55,000 from Richard Glass, the Non-Executive Chairman of the Company; and · £106,000 from James Stenhouse, the newly appointed Non-Executive Director of the Company. The balance of the private placement, being £150,000, has been subscribed for by external investors.
