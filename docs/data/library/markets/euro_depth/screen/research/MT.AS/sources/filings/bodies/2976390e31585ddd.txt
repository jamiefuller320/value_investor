Recent development • On September 25, 2026, ArcelorMittal announced that it is unable to safely and sustainably operate ArcelorMittal Kryvyi Rih (Ukraine) following multiple missile strikes. ArcelorMittal Kryvyi Rih has continued to operate in an increasingly challenging environment since the onset of the conflict. Following a fundamental change in the safety risk profile, together with severe pressure on the logistics system, the Company took the difficult decision to idle operations, with the safety of employees remaining the overriding priority. Since the onset of the war, ArcelorMittal has provided over $700 million of financial support to sustain the business and maintain continuity of operations. Going forward the future cash spend is expected to be limited (including 4Q 2026) and focused on asset preservation, security and environmental compliance while maintaining restart optionality. Adjusted net income: refers to reported net income less impairment items and exceptional items and related tax impacts and tax charges. Apparent steel consumption (ASC): calculated as the sum of production plus imports minus exports. Average steel selling prices: calculated as steel sales divided by steel shipments. Cash and cash equivalents : represent cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash and investments. Capex: represents the purchase of property, plant and equipment and intangibles. EPS: refers to basic or diluted earnings/(loss) per share. EBITDA: defined as operating income plus depreciation, impairment items and exceptional items and income (loss) from associates, joint ventures and other investments (excluding impairments and exceptional items if any).
