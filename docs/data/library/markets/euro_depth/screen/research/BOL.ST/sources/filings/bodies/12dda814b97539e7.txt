This report brings together the company’s strategy, financial results, and developments in the areas of compliance and sustainability. All of this serves one clear objective: the realization of the Green Steel Project. The report shows how the company is evolving during a period of change. Many employees work every day on steel that is of great importance to the Netherlands and Europe, while at the same time efforts are being made to transition to a different way of producing steel—with lower emissions and reduced impact on the surrounding environment. We acknowledge that steel production has an impact and that continuous improvement is required. The Green Steel Project plays a central role in this. This project marks a fundamental transformation of steel production in the Netherlands, with the aim of maintaining steel production while making it cleaner, greener, and more circular. This transformation requires adjustments in processes, organization, and ways of working. At the same time, much is at stake: together with suppliers and partners, Tata Steel Nederland supports an ecosystem on which tens of thousands of families depend. With this annual report, Tata Steel Nederland accounts for its results and progress in the financial year from April 1, 2025, through March 31, 2026. At the same time, it illustrates how the company is working toward the realization of the Green Steel Project and the further sustainability of steel production in the Netherlands. The report also represents a step toward stricter European reporting requirements (CSRD), in which demands for transparency and substantiation are further tightened.
