Adds Taylor Bol Bowen 05/05/26 | Men's Basketball Share: EUGENE, Ore. – Oregon men's basketball has announced the signing of transfer forward Taylor Bol Bowen. The 6-foot-10 forward has played in 90 career games (39 starts) over three seasons for Florida State and Alabama, with career averages of 5.8 points, 4.0 rebounds and 1.0 blocks in 18.1 minutes per game. As a junior in 2025-26, Bol Bowen appeared in 30 games with 11 starts for Alabama, averaging 6.3 points, 4.2 rebounds and 1.0 blocks in 19.0 minutes per game while shooting 41.2 percent from the field. He scored in double figures nine times, highlighted by a season-high 17 points on 6-of-7 shooting with nine rebounds at St. John's. Bol Bowen also scored 15 points in an NCAA Tournament first-round win against Hofstra, added 13 points versus Purdue and 12 against Illinois, and contributed 11 points, five rebounds, three assists and two blocks in a win over Clemson. He recorded his lone double-double of the season with 11 points and 10 rebounds against Texas and led Alabama in blocks six times, including a career-high four blocks against Arizona. Prior to his time at Alabama, Bol Bowen played the 2024-25 season at Florida State, where he averaged 8.0 points, 5.2 rebounds, 1.5 blocks and 1.0 steals in 25.0 minutes per game over 29 appearances with 28 starts. He shot 47.7 percent from the field and 41.4 percent from three-point range. He posted a career-high 20 points in a win over Georgia Tech and recorded 17 points, six rebounds, three steals and two blocks against Northern Kentucky. Bowen also added 16 points, six rebounds and three assists in a road win at Miami. He finished the season with two double-doubles, including a 13-point, 13-rebound performance against Winthrop and a 14-point, 10-rebound outing versus Syracuse. He totaled 13 multi-block games and ranked third in the ACC in blocks per game. As a freshman in 2023-24, Bowen played in 31 games, averaging 3.1 points and 2.6 rebounds in 10.9 minutes per game at Florida State. Bol Bowen was born in Egypt and raised in Jericho, Vermont. A four-star recruit, he was a member of the 2023 Nike Hoop Summit roster and spent his final two high school seasons at Brewster Academy in New Hampshire, helping lead the program to two Elite Eight appearances in the National Prep Championship.
