BETA Close X Ameriprise Financial, Inc. has notified Cairn Homes Plc of a change in its major holdings, crossing a 4% threshold on September 9, 2026. The firm's voting rights in Cairn Homes Plc have decreased from 4.873% to 3.535%, with no voting rights held through financial instruments. This change is attributed to the combined holdings of various subsidiaries within the Ameriprise Financial group, including Threadneedle Asset Management Limited holding 2.722%, Columbia Threadneedle Management Limited holding 0.791%, and Columbia Management Investment Advisers, LLC holding 0.023% of the voting rights. The total number of voting rights in the issuer is 628,365,176.
