BETA Close X Cairn Homes Plc announced transactions by its Chief Executive Officer, Michael Stanley, and Chief Financial Officer, Richard Ball, on April 1, 2026. Michael Stanley purchased 97,849 ordinary shares at €2.15 each, utilizing 33% of his 2025 bonus award, and also received a conditional grant of 303,571 shares under the Long Term Incentive Plan with nil consideration, subject to performance conditions. Richard Ball purchased 66,191 ordinary shares at €2.15 each, also using 33% of his 2025 bonus award, and was conditionally granted 267,857 shares under the same plan for nil consideration. Additionally, Company Secretary Tara Grimley received a conditional grant of 80,952 shares under the Long Term Incentive Plan for nil consideration. These transactions were conducted on Euronext Dublin or outside a trading venue.
