BETA Close X Cairn Homes Plc has announced transactions by persons discharging managerial responsibilities. On April 7, 2026, Chief Executive Officer Michael Stanley disposed of 216,750 ordinary shares at €2.138 per share, totaling €463,411.5, to settle a tax liability from the vesting of 709,428 shares awarded under the Long Term Incentive Plan. Additionally, on April 9, 2026, Michael Stanley disposed of 937,610 ordinary shares at €2.18869 per share, totaling €2,052,137.63, to settle a tax liability from the vesting of 3,068,818 shares under the Stretch CEO Long Term Incentive Plan. All transactions occurred on Euronext Dublin.
