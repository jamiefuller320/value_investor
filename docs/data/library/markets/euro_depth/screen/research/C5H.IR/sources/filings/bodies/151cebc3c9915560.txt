BETA Close X Cairn Homes Plc announced that all resolutions proposed at its Annual General Meeting held today were passed by poll. The financial statements received 100% approval with 432,555,732 votes for and 0.05% withheld. The Directors' Remuneration Reports passed with 97.82% for and 2.18% against. The final dividend of 5.9 cents was approved unanimously, and re-appointments of directors and auditors also saw strong support, with most resolutions achieving over 97% approval and representing approximately 68.80% of the issued capital.
