BETA Close X TotalEnergies SE has announced various transactions by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) involving the acquisition and sale of TotalEnergies Actionnariat France "A" Part C shares on September 4, 2026. These transactions, which occurred outside a trading venue, involved individuals such as Romain Garcia Ivaldi, Stéphane Michel, Anne Therese Michel, Bernard Pinatel, Angel Pobo, Patrick Pouyanne, Jean Pierre Sbraire, Namita Shah, Nicolas Terraz, Aurelien Hamelle, Vincent Stoquart, Valerie Della Puppa Tibi, and Emmanuelle Guegan. The prices per share ranged from EUR 77.2429 to EUR 77.61, with aggregated transaction values reaching over EUR 4.8 million for Jean Pierre Sbraire and over EUR 4.3 million for Bernard Pinatel. Disclaimer* Notification and Public Disclosure of transactions by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities Paris, September 7, 2026 – TotalEnergies SE has received various notifications of dealing from Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (“PDMR”). The notification of Dealing Form for each PDMR can be found below. This notification is made in accordance with the UK Market Abuse Regulation.
