BETA Close X TotalEnergies SE has announced various dealings by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) on July 3, 2026, involving the acquisition of TotalEnergies shares through the reinvestment of the Final 2025 dividend into the TotalEnergies Actionnariat France fund. Notably, Stéphane Michel, a Member of the Executive Committee, acquired shares totaling €26,217.90 at a price of €67.7083 per share, and an additional €3,778.21 worth of shares at €110.3787 per share. Other PDMRs, including Romain Garcia Ivaldi, Anne Therese Michel, Bernard Pinatel, Angel Pobo, Patrick Pouyanne, Jean Pierre Sbraire, Namita Shah, Nicolas Terraz, Aurelien Hamelle, Vincent Stoquart, Valerie Della Puppa Tibi, and Catherine Remy, also made similar dividend reinvestment acquisitions, with individual transaction values ranging from €2.57 to €49,264.95. Disclaimer* Notification and Public Disclosure of transactions by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities Paris, July 6, 2026 – TotalEnergies SE has received various notifications of dealing from Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (“PDMR”). The notification of Dealing Form for each PDMR can be found below. This notification is made in accordance with the UK Market Abuse Regulation. About TotalEnergies TotalEnergies is a global integrated energy company that produces and markets energies: oil and biofuels, natural gas, biogas and low-carbon hydrogen, renewables and electricity. Our more than 100,000 employees are committed to provide as many people as possible with energy that is more reliable, more affordable and more sustainable.
