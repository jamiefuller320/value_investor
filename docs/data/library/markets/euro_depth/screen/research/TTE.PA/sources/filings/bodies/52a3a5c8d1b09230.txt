BETA Close X TotalEnergies SE has announced dealings by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) in accordance with UK Market Abuse Regulation. Vincent Stoquart, a Member of the Executive Committee, reinvested dividends into TotalEnergies shares, acquiring 54.5689 units for €3,649.57 and 5.1661 units for €345.51 on July 6, 2026. Bernard Pinatel and Namita Shah, also Members of the Executive Committee, reinvested dividends from option exercises into employee shareholder plans on July 7, 2026, with Pinatel acquiring 32.5331 units for €2,167.50 and Shah acquiring 17.2234 units for €1,147.50, all transactions occurring outside a trading venue.
