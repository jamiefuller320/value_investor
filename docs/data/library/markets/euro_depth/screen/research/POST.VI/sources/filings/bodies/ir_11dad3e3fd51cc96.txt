Statement by the Management Board 
Dear Shareholders, 
The market environment of the postal and logistics sector continues to be shaped by geopolitical and economic un-
certainties, ongoing digitisation as well as new regulatory frameworks. Letter mail and direct mail volumes are subject to a 
structural decline whereas e-commerce continues to grow. However, national and international duties and fees are leading 
to subdued and volatile trends in e-commerce parcel volumes – a negative trend that is expected to persist over the coming 
quarters. Austrian Post is responding to these changes by offering a broad range of services, leveraging its strong position in 
the Austrian parcel market, and strategically expanding its international e-commerce fulfilment business.  
Austrian Post generated revenue of EUR 2,306.8m in the first three quarters of 2026. This represents a 4.3 % in-
crease from the prior-year period. The E-Commerce & Logistics division was once again the growth driver, generating an 
11.1 % revenue increase to EUR 1,372.3m. Revenue of the Mail, Branch & Services division fell by 6.2 % to EUR 834.2m. The 
Bank division reported revenue of EUR 108.5m in the first three quarters, comprising a slight increase of 0.9 % from the pre-
vious year. Austrian Post have stayed the course in a demanding market environment. The positive development of the Aus-
trian parcel business, the expansion of our integrated logistics business for cross-border e-commerce and the considerable 
earnings improvement of bank99 are particularly encouraging. At the same time, the company is consistently driving for-
ward the transformation of the business and addressing the structural decline in letter mail through efficiency, innovation 
and new services. 
In terms of earnings, EBITDA climbed by 11.1 % to EUR 328.0m in the first three quarters of 2026. Earnings before 
interest and taxes (EBIT) rose by 1.8 % to EUR 137.7m. This was supported by net positive one-off effects in the third quar-
ter totalling about EUR 25m, particularly from the reversal of a provision relating to compensation payments for social con-
tributions for civil servants. An impairment of non-current assets in Southeast and Eastern Europe, among other factors, 
had a negative impact. The profit for the period amounted to EUR 81.9m compared to EUR 93.7m in the prior-year period. 
Earnings per share changed from EUR 1.35 to EUR 1.18 (–12.9 %).  
Developments over the past few months reinforce the existing outlook for a slight increase in revenue for the full 
year 2026. For the Mail, Branch & Services division, a mid single-digit revenue decline is still expected. In the E-commerce & 
Logistics division, revenue growth in the upper single-digit range is forecast, supported by the integration of the newly ac-
quired subsidiaries. An improvement in revenue and earnings is also expected in the Bank division. 
Due to the extraordinary earnings effects in the third quarter, EBIT in 2026 is expected to be at the upper end of the 
range of recent years, thus surpassing the level of EUR 200m. The key uncertainties continue to include an accelerated de-
cline in mail volumes as well as the unpredictable short-term trend in parcel volumes, which is being negatively affected in 
particular by new national and international duties and fees. Investments in property, plant and equipment (CAPEX) are ex-
pected to be in the range of EUR 120m to EUR 130m. This creates the necessary infrastructure for further growth of the 
Austrian Post Group while strengthening efficiency and sustainability at the same time.

1.1 Segment information and adjustment 
of prior-year comparative figures 
The segment structure of Austrian Post Group 
changed effective 1 January 2026. Reporting is now 
based on the divisions “Mail, Branch & Services”, “E-Com-
merce & Logistics”, “Bank” as well as “Corporate,” which 
also represent the reporting segments according to 
IFRS 8. The new “Mail, Branch & Services” division now in-
cludes the branch network in addition to the previously 
reported product and service portfolio of the former Mail 
Division. The new “Bank” division primarily represents the 
financial services business in cooperation with bank99. 
This new structure enables Austrian Post to enhance the 
transparency of its reporting and takes into account the 
evolution of its internal business structure. Prior-year fig-
ures have been adjusted to ensure comparability. 
The accounting treatment of liabilities from obliga-
tion to acquire non-controlling interests was changed in 
the third quarter of 2026. Accordingly, the initial recogni-
tion, subsequent measurement and valuation of these lia-
bilities are recognised directly in equity (retained earn-
ings). Given the current circumstances, the change in ac-
counting method is intended to provide more reliable and 
relevant information by ensuring that temporary fluctua-
tions in value arising from exchange rate movements or 
inflation, which are not directly related to operating activi-
ties, no longer affect profit for the period. The compara-
tive information for the prior-year period presented was 
adjusted accordingly. Valuation effects previously recog-
nised in profit or loss were retrospectively reclassified to 
retained earnings. This had no material impact on EBITDA 
or EBIT in the first three quarters of 2025. The financial 
result decreased by EUR 3.8m. In the current 2026 finan-
cial year, the negative valuation effects of EUR 22.2m rec-
ognised in the first half of the year were reclassified ac-
cordingly. Liabilities from obligation to acquire non-con-
trolling interests continue to be recognised as liabilities at 
the present value of the exercise amount. Non-controlling 
interests were reclassified to equity attributable to share-
holders of the parent company (retained earnings). The 
adjustments resulted solely in reclassifications within eq-
uity and had no impact on the reported amount of equity 
as at the respective reporting date. 
1.2 Changes in the scope of consolidation 
The full consolidation of the Greek IT service pro-
vider Agile Actors Hellas Single Member S.A. took effect 
on 1 January 2026. The company, which had been ac-
counted for using the equity method until 31 Decem-
ber 2025, is now subject to full consolidation following the 
implementation of agreed corporate governance arrange-
ments. The ownership interest of 80 % remained un-
changed. The acquisition of a 70 % stake in the Bulgarian 
e-commerce service provider euShipments.com AD was 
completed on 6 March 2026. With the acquisition of the 
shares, Austrian Post Group obtained control of the com-
pany and its subsidiaries InOut Trade EOOD, Helpship SRL, 
Swiss Point Data a.s. and Pick&Pack d.o.o. On 10 Au-
gust 2026, euShipments.com AD acquired 100 % of the 
shares in the Slovakian fulfilment provider FHB-Group 
s.r.o. Based on Austrian Post Group's 70 % interest in eu-
Shipments.com AD, this represents an effective 70 % in-
terest for Austrian Post Group. 
From that point forward, these companies and 
their subsidiaries have been included in the consolidated 
financial statements through full consolidation.

Revenue of the E-Commerce & Logistics division 
including the newly consolidated subsidiaries increased by 
11.1 % in the first three quarters of 2026 to 
EUR 1,372.3m, with growth recorded across all regions.  
In Austria, divisional revenue grew by 8.9 % to 
EUR 752.2m, accompanied by a 7 % increase in parcel vol-
umes. Since the introduction of an EU customs duty of 
EUR 3 per item on e-commerce shipments from non-EU 
countries with a value of up to EUR 150 – effective 
1 July 2026 – there has been noticeable uncertainty and 
restraint in consumer purchasing behaviour.  
Revenue in the Türkiye+ region climbed by 5.1 % to 
EUR 382.2m in the first three quarters of 2026, with the 
overall development characterised by intense competition 
and persistent high inflation. The tightening of Turkish im-
port regulations for cross-border e-commerce shipments 
which took effect at the beginning of 2026 led to an ap-
proximately 5 % decline in parcel volumes. In particular, 
the termination of simplified customs clearance proce-
dures for low-value B2C parcels from abroad with a value 
of up to EUR 30 had a dampening effect on volume devel-
opment. Moreover, business performance continued to be 
impacted by high inflation and exchange rate fluctuations 
of the Turkish lira.  
Parcel revenue in Southeast and Eastern Europe 
(CEE/SEE) rose by 4.5 % in the first three quarters of 2026 
to EUR 158.8m. In particular, the 3 % volume increase was 
driven by higher e-commerce shipment volumes from Asia 
during the first two quarters. In the third quarter, however, 
these shipment volumes showed a downward trend. The 
competitive and margin environment in these markets 
continues to be challenging.  
Revenue of the Group Logistics Solutions business 
increased from EUR 40.5m to EUR 89.8m in the current 
reporting period. This is also related to the initial consoli-
dation of euShipments.com and FHB Group, which con-
tributed about EUR 45m to revenue.

1.3.2 EARNINGS DEVELOPMENT 
The largest expense items in relation to Austrian 
Post’s total Group revenue are staff costs (45.8 %), raw 
materials, consumables and services used (31.6 %) and 
other operating expenses (14.2 %). In addition, deprecia-
tion, amortisation and impairment losses accounted for 
8.2 % and financial services expenses for 1.1 %.  
Staff costs totalled EUR 1,056.4m in the first three 
quarters of 2026, comprising an increase of 2.7 % or 
EUR 28.2m. This change is entirely contributable to the 
consolidation of the new subsidiaries as well as inflation-
related salary adjustments in Türkiye. Austrian Post Group 
employed an average of 27,681 employees (full-time 
equivalents) in the first nine months of 2026, compared to 
the average of 28,202 employees in the prior-year period 
(–1.8 %). 
Non-operating staff costs relate to severance pay-
ments and changes in provisions, which are primarily at-
tributable to the specific employment situation of civil 
servant employees at Austrian Post. No significant 
charges of this nature were incurred during the first nine 
months of 2026. 
Raw materials, consumables and services used in-
creased by 12.2 % to EUR 728.0m. This increase is largely 
attributable to higher transport volumes and the consoli-
dation of the new subsidiaries. 
Other operating income increased from EUR 87.3m 
to EUR 148.7m (EUR +61.4m) in the first three quarters 
of 2026. The increase includes a positive one-off effect 
from the reversal of provisions in connection with an 
agreement between the Republic of Austria and Austrian 
Post concerning compensation payments for social contri-
butions for civil servants from previous years. As a result 
of this agreement, provisions totalling EUR 47.8m were re-
versed in the third quarter 2026 and recognised in profit 
or loss. Other operating expenses increased by 7.5 % to 
EUR 326.9m.  
Accounting standard IAS 29 (Financial Reporting 
in Hyperinflationary Economies) needs to be applied for 
the Turkish subsidiaries. Accordingly, all items in the in-
come statement as well as the non-monetary items were 
adjusted using a general price index (see 2025 Annual Re-
port, Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 2.2 Hyper-
inflation). The resulting gain or loss on the net monetary 
position is presented separately in the income statement.

1.9 Outlook for 2026/2027 
The underlying trends in the international mail and 
parcel markets remain unchanged. Ongoing digitisation 
leads to declining mail volumes, whereas e-commerce 
continues to grow. However, current regional and EU-wide 
regulations are resulting in a subdued and volatile trend 
across many markets.  
REVENUE IN 2026  
Developments over the past few months reinforce 
the existing outlook for a slight increase in revenue for the 
full year 2026.  
The Mail, Branch & Services division continues to 
expect a mid single-digit decline in revenue. Decreasing 
letter mail and direct mail volumes can only be partially 
offset by product and pricing adjustments. Revenue gen-
erated by Austrian Post’s own mobile telephony brand 
YELLLOW since the second quarter of 2026 has had a 
positive impact. In turn, revenue from the terminated tele-
communications sales partnership from previous years 
will no longer contribute to the total.  
The E-Commerce & Logistics division expects 
growth in the upper single-digit range, supported by the 
integration of the newly acquired companies. Due to the 
current environment relating to duties and fees, parcel 
volumes are not expected to increase across regions in 
the fourth quarter. In addition, the competitive environ-
ment is expected to remain challenging, particularly in 
Southeast and Eastern Europe as well as in Türkiye. By 
contrast, e-commerce fulfilment continues to develop 
positively and record further growth. 
The Bank division anticipates a slight year-on-year 
revenue increase. Earnings are expected to improve 
in 2026 following the completion of the core banking sys-
tem migration and the additional business generated from 
the securities deposit offering. 
EARNINGS IN 2026 
EBIT is expected to be at the upper end of the 
range generated in previous years – thus above the level 
of EUR 200m - due to the extraordinary earnings effect in 
the third quarter. Positive developments were observed, 
particularly in the integration of new companies within the 
e-commerce fulfilment sector, as well as in the financial 
services business in Austria.   
Key uncertainties include a more pronounced de-
cline in letter mail volumes, which could potentially stabi-
lise at a rate of about ten per cent per annum, as well as 
the unpredictable short-term trend in parcel volumes, 
which is being negatively impacted by new national and in-
ternational customs duties and fees. Forecasting accuracy 
continues to be limited with respect to the earnings devel-
opment in Türkiye in light of the high volatility of inflation 
and exchange rates.  
Forecast for 2027 
For 2027, it is expected that current trends in the 
traditional letter mail business and the uncertainty and 
volatility in the parcel segment resulting from duties and 
fees will persist in 2027. A continued positive trend is ex-
pected to continue in financial services and e-commerce 
fulfilment. Additional revenue effects will result from the 
integration of the newly acquired companies. 
Revenue in 2027 is expected to be slightly above 
the level of 2026. Assuming a slightly improved market 
environment with declining mail volumes and increasing 
parcel volumes, Austrian Post aims to generate earnings 
(EBIT) in the region of EUR 200m for the financial year. 
INVESTMENTS IN 2026/2027  
Investments in property, plant and equipment 
(CAPEX) are expected to remain at a level comparable to 
the previous year in both 2026 and 2027, amounting to 
approximately EUR 120m to EUR 130m in each year. In-
vestment activities will focus on the expansion and mod-
ernisation of the logistics centres in Salzburg, Budapest 
(Hungary) and Žilina (Slovakia), the further development of 
the parcel locker network – particularly in Southeast and 
Eastern Europe – and the continued electrification of the 
delivery fleet. The goal remains to make last-mile delivery 
in Austria completely CO₂-free by 2030.
