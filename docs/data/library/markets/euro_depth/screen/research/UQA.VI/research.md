The key debate is whether sustained CEE premium growth and investment income outweigh reserving discipline, catastrophe exposure, and the absence of verified Solvency II / IFRS primary disclosures in this pack.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, UNIQA offers a classic “quality at a reasonable price” insurance profile rather than deep net-net territory. The screen passes Graham Defensive/Enterprising, Earnings Yield, FCF Yield, Low P/E + High Yield, Quality Value, Dividend Growth, Acquirer's Multiple, Dreman Contrarian, Piotroski F-Score, and Earnings Quality—eleven models across cheapness, quality, dividend, and risk families. Failed screens (Graham Net-Net, Schloss, Deep Value, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat) largely reflect insurer balance-sheet structure: P/B above 1.0, leverage metrics that penalise large policy liabilities, and ROE (~13.7%) below “moat” thresholds—not necessarily weak underwriting.

Business quality, per Yahoo fallback data, is improving: net income compounded strongly off a 2022 trough; 2025 operating cash flow was €1.14bn versus €581m in 2024; free cash flow reached €842m (2025) versus €420m (2024). Dividends paid rose modestly (€185m in 2025 vs €178m in 2024) while earnings accelerated, suggesting headroom. Share count is stable (~307m diluted shares). The screen’s new signal (one week, conviction 29%) warrants patience, but the fundamental trajectory supports the buy classification pending filing verification.

- **Accounting / data:** Heavy reliance on Yahoo; no IFRS note review; 2022 OCF/FCF volatility flags insurance working-capital complexity.
