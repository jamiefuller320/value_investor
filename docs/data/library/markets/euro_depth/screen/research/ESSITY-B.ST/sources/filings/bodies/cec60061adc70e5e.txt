Search Sign in --> Sign in Breaking News --> Financial Essity Releases Third Quarter Results Net sales grew 4.1% October 23, 2026 By: Tara Olivo Associate Editor at Nonwovens Industry , Rodman Media Save Image caption --> Net sales increased 4.1% in the third quarter of 2026 compared with the corresponding period a year ago and amounted to SEK 36.06 billion ($3.6 billion). The acquisition in Feminine Care in North America increased the group’s consolidated net sales by 1.9%. Organic sales decreased 0.3%, primarily as a result of price reductions in previous quarters. Sales prices were higher in the quarter compared with the second quarter across all business areas. The product mix developed positively and volumes were stable. Growth was positive in North America, with a high growth in both Health & Medical and Personal Care. In Europe and Latin America, organic sales were slightly lower, primarily due to reduced sales in Consumer Tissue. In the Health & Medical segment, net sales increased organically by 1.2% to SEK 7.06 billion ($711 million), driven by higher sales prices. Growth was high in North America, while sales were stable in Europe and declined in Latin America. In Incontinence Products, both higher volumes and sales prices supported organic growth. Higher sales prices were the main driver for Medical Solutions, with good growth noted in wound care. In the Personal Care segment, net sales increased organically by 3.2% to SEK 9.2 billion ($926 million), primarily driven by good volume growth. The product mix was also favorable, while sales prices were lower. Market shares increased for branded sales. Growth was high in North America and Latin America, while sales were stable in Europe. Incontinence Products and Feminine Care reported high organic growth, primarily due to strong volume growth, and strengthened their market shares during the quarter. In Baby Care, volumes were negatively impacted by a shrinking market, while the market positions of the leading brand, Libero, were further strengthened. In the Consumer Tissue segment, net sales decreased organically by 3% to SEK 10.5 billion ($1.06 billion) due to lower volumes, mainly driven by optimization of the production footprint in Latin America, as well as lower sales prices related to price adjustments in previous quarters. Sales prices were higher compared with the second quarter. Sales declined in both Europe and Latin America. In the Professional Hygiene segment, net sales decreased organically by 1.1% to SEK 9.3 billion ($936 million) due to lower volumes, mainly in the base assortment, and lower sales prices. The product mix remained favorable with an increased share of premium products. Sales declined in North America and Europe, while remaining stable in Latin America.
