Item 8.01 Other Events. On October 29, 2026, Sandisk Corporation (the “Company”) announced that its Board of Directors approved an forward stock split (the “Stock Split”) of its common stock, par value $0.01 per share ("Common Stock"). The Stock Split will be effected through an amendment (the “Charter Amendment”) to the Company’s Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation. The Charter Amendment, when effected, will proportionately increase the number of authorized shares of the Company’s Common Stock from 450 million to 3.6 billion. In connection with the Stock Split, each of the Company’s stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 9, 2026 will receive seven additional shares for each share held, which will be issued after market close on Thursday, November 19, 2026. The Company’s Common Stock is expected to begin trading on a basis at the market open on Friday, November 20, 2026 under the Company’s existing trading symbol “SNDK”.
