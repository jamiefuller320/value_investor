# Illinois Tool Works Inc. (ITW) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-09-13T06:46:38.088469+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Illinois Tool Works is a high-quality industrial compounder organised around seven differentiated segments and the ITW Business Model (80/20, enterprise initiatives), with interim filings showing re-accelerating revenue and record-margin narrative into 2026. The quantitative screen flags **buy** on quality, dividend, GARP, and risk-adjusted cheapness (Neff PEGY, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat), not on classic deep value: P/E ~24×, P/B ~26×, and FCF yield below many value thresholds. The central debate is whether sustained mid-single-digit organic growth, margin discipline, and capital return (Dividend King track record plus enlarged buybacks) justify a premium multiple, or whether elevated leverage, cyclical end-markets (notably automotive OEM and food equipment), and rich absolute valuation cap upside for strict value accounts.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value-oriented process that blends **quality and growth-at-a-reasonable-yield**, ITW fits the screen’s intent more than a Graham or Schloss profile. The run passes **6 of 22** models with **composite score ~54%** (sector-relative ~56%), all **five factor families** (cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, risk), **20/20 data quality**, and improving but **new** conviction (one week at signal). Standouts include **Neff PEGY** (PEGY ~0.09 on screen inputs with ~10% earnings growth and ~2.6% yield), **Buffett Quality**, **Economic Moat**, **Dividend Growth**, **Magic Formula**, and **Earnings Quality**—consistent with a wide-moat, cash-generative franchise rather than a distressed asset.

Filings reinforce that story: seven focused segments (Automotive OEM, Food Equipment, Test & Measurement & Electronics, Welding, Polymers & Fluids, Construction Products, Specialty Products), emphasis on differentiated niches and 80/20-driven margin improvement, and H1 2026 revenue and operating income ahead of the prior year. Capital allocation remains shareholder-friendly—ongoing repurchases (e.g. **$1.125bn** repurchased in H1 2026 per the Q2 2026 10-Q) and dividend growth (public news of a **7% dividend increase** and **$6bn** repurchase authorisation in August 2026). The buy case is **quality + compounding + shareholder yield**, not net-net or low P/B; screens that fail on leverage, FCF yield, and Piotroski (4/9) correctly flag that this is not a balance-sheet bargain.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical and macro:** The FY2025 10-K risk section stresses exposure to global industrial cycles, inflation, interest rates, supply chain disruption, and geopolitical conflict—material for Automotive OEM and Construction Products.

**Leverage and liquidity:** Interim debt rose to **~$9.7bn** with heavy **commercial paper** reliance; equity is thin relative to assets after cumulative buybacks. Screen failures on **Financial Health**, **Graham Enterprising** (leverage), and **FCF yield** align with this. Rising rates or tighter CP markets would bite even with investment-grade history (not fully re-underwritten here).

**Pension and post-retirement:** Filings cite **defined benefit pension** market and interest-rate risk; balance-sheet employee benefit lines remain material (Yahoo: non-current pension/post-retirement **~$205m** liability bucket at YE2025—full funded status requires complete 10-K note).

**Goodwill / intangibles:** ~**$5.6bn+** goodwill and intangibles; 10-K warns of **impairment** risk if segments underperform—relevant if automotive or food service weaken further (consistent with recent press on those end-markets).

**Competitive and execution:** Niche differentiation is the moat; failure to sustain **80/20** and innovation (Customer-Back Innovation) would compress margins. **Cybersecurity** risk is explicitly flagged.

**Governance / legal:** No going-concern language surfaced in available extracts. **Legal proceedings** section in Q2 2026 10-Q body is not substantive in the extract (table of contents only)—**contingency detail gap**. May 2025 8-K confirms routine annual meeting outcomes (directors elected, Deloitte ratified, say-on-pay approved).

**Accounting:** Yahoo flags **2024 LIFO method change** at certain U.S. businesses (mentioned in truncated 10-K body)—low risk but worth noting for inventory/cost comparability.
