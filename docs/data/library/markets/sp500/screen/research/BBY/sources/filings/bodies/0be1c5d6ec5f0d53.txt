Disclaimer* Balfour Beatty PLC 05 May 2026 TRANSACTIONS IN OWN SHARES Tuesday 5 May 2026 Balfour Beatty plc (the "Group" or "Balfour Beatty"), the international infrastructure group, announces that, in accordance with the terms of its share buyback programme announced on 5 January 2026, and increased on 11 March 2026 (the "Share Buyback"), the Group purchased 904,393 of its ordinary shares of 50 pence each through Deutsche Bank AG, London Branch (trading for these purposes as Deutsche Numis) ("Deutsche Numis") between 27 April 2026 and 1 May 2026 . The Group will hold the repurchased shares in treasury. Following the purchase of these shares, the remaining number of ordinary shares in issue will be 486,254,282 (excluding treasury shares). The Group has, under the Share Buyback, repurchased 6,594,302 ordinary shares at a volume weighted average price of 767.7582 pence, which are to be held in treasury with no voting rights attached. The shares repurchased under the Share Buyback are the only shares held in Treasury by the Group. Accordingly, the total number of voting rights in the Company is 486,254,282 . This figure may be used by shareholders as the denominator for the calculation by which they will determine whether they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, Balfour Beatty plc under the FCA's Disclosure and Transparency Rules.
