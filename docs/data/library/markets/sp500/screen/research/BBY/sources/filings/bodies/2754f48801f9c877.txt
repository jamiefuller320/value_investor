BETA Close X Balfour Beatty plc announced that all resolutions presented at its Annual General Meeting on May 7, 2026, were passed by poll, with the exception of resolution 9 which was withdrawn. Key resolutions approved included the audited accounts for the year ended December 31, 2025, the Directors' Remuneration Report and Policy, and the declaration of a final dividend of 9.8p per share. Directors were re-elected with strong support, and KPMG LLP was reappointed as auditor. Authorities were also granted for share allotment, including non-pre-emptive issues, and for the company to purchase its own shares.
