# Accenture plc (ACN) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-09-08T16:38:36.586973+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Accenture is a scale global professional-services franchise—roughly **$69.7bn** in fiscal 2025 revenues, **~779,000** employees, and deep Fortune Global 100 penetration—trading at a depressed multiple after a multi-year share-price drawdown. The quantitative screen flags **strong buy**, though the local screening snapshot carries **no populated model metrics** (data quality score 0.0), so the label should be treated as directional rather than fully evidenced in this pack.

The investment case rests on durable **free cash flow** (filing-backed interim trend plus annual Yahoo fallback), aggressive **capital return** (dividends and buybacks), and a credible **generative-AI reinvention** narrative backed by ecosystem deals and a reorganised “Reinvention Services” model. The central debate is whether AI is a **demand tailwind** (implementation, managed services, agentic deployment) or a **margin and growth headwind** (client budget deferral, commoditisation, and large-enterprise consulting softness)—a tension visible in both filings and news flow since the June 2025 guidance reset.

---

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value-oriented investor, ACN offers a rare combination: **asset-light economics**, **net cash liquidity**, double-digit **FCF conversion**, and a shareholder-return programme that has continued through the downturn. Fiscal 2025 revenue rose to **$69.7bn** (10-K highlights) from **$64.9bn** in fiscal 2024—a **~7%** step-up—while interim fiscal 2026 filings show revenue momentum persisting (**$55.5bn** for the nine months to May 2026, **+6.6%** year-on-year per Q3 10-Q).

Business quality markers from filings include long-dated client relationships (195 of the top 200 clients for 10+ years), diversified geographic and industry exposure, and sustained investment (**$1.5bn** across 23 acquisitions, **$0.8bn** R&D, **$1.0bn** in learning in FY2025). The company frames generative AI as a **$3bn multi-year investment** already positioning it for incremental spend—a narrative reinforced by the September 2026 **Google Cloud Gemini Enterprise Business Group** launch (1,000 forward-deployed engineers).

The **strong buy** screen signal aligns with a business generating substantial cash despite cyclical pressure: nine-month operating cash flow of **$9.27bn** (Q3 FY2026 10-Q) versus **$7.56bn** prior year, with diluted EPS of **$10.27** versus **$9.90**. What screens cannot capture— and what partially offsets the value case—is **goodwill-heavy M&A** (~$24.9bn goodwill and intangibles per Yahoo fallback at FY2025 year-end), **cyclical consulting exposure**, and the market’s scepticism after FY2025 guidance cuts. The stock appears priced for stagnation while fundamentals show mid-single-digit growth and improving cash generation; that gap supports a value accumulation thesis, not a high-growth compounder call.

**Caveat:** The screening snapshot records **zero models passed**, **zero key metrics**, and **insufficient timing data**—deep research cannot fully validate the quantitative signal from this file alone.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical / macro (filing language):** The FY2025 10-K risk-factor text warns that volatile macroeconomic and geopolitical conditions—including inflation, downturns, trade restrictions, and protectionism—have **“in the past had a negative impact and could in the future have a significant negative impact”** on results, causing clients to defer or reduce spending and making revenue forecasting difficult.

**AI / competitive:** News and sell-side commentary (Financial Times, 18 Jun 2025: *“Accenture shares fall to lowest since 2017 as AI threat mounts”*) frame the stock debate around whether AI automates away consulting work or expands the addressable market. Accenture is investing heavily to be on the right side of that shift, but **competitive intensity** from global SI peers, cloud hyperscalers’ direct services arms, and boutique AI specialists remains elevated.

**M&A / accounting:** Goodwill and intangibles of **~$25bn** (Yahoo, FY2025) against tangible book of **~$6.2bn** create **impairment sensitivity** if growth slows. FY2025 acquisition spend moderated to **$1.5bn / 23 deals** (10-K) from **$6.6bn / 46 deals** in FY2024—integration and earn-out risks persist.

**Governance / key person:** The 20 Jun 2025 8-K announces a major **Reinvention Services** reorganisation effective 1 Sep 2025, with Manish Sharma as Chief Services Officer and several senior departures (Angela Beatty, Jack Azagury, Karthik Narain). Leadership transitions add execution risk during a strategic pivot.

**Pension:** Non-current employee benefit obligations of **~$1.86bn** (Yahoo, FY2025 balance sheet; XBRL pension tags in 10-K). Not material relative to equity, but a long-tail liability.

**Leverage / liquidity:** Total debt rose to **$8.2bn** in FY2025 (Yahoo) while cash also rose to **$11.5bn**—**net cash** position; no going-concern language identified. Filing bodies do not surface covenant breach disclosures.

**Litigation:** Item 3 Legal Proceedings is referenced in the 10-K table of contents, but **readable legal-proceedings body text is not present** in the truncated extract—litigation exposure cannot be assessed from this pack.

**Client concentration:** Not a red flag—**9,000+ clients**, no single-customer concentration disclosed in available excerpts.
