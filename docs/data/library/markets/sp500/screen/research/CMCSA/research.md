# Comcast Corporation (CMCSA) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-26T17:23:42.836272+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Comcast screens as a strong buy on deep value metrics—P/E 7.1, P/B 0.9, ~5.9% dividend yield, EV/EBITDA 4.8—with 13 of 22 quantitative models passing and a Piotroski F-Score of 8/9. The investment case rests on substantial operating cash generation, a pending NBCUniversal/Sky separation that could unlock sum-of-the-parts value, and offsetting growth in wireless, Peacock, and theme parks. The central debate is whether headline cheapness adequately compensates for accelerating domestic broadband subscriber losses, a structurally declining cable/video franchise, and spin-off execution risk. Filings confirm liquidity and covenant compliance, but GAAP earnings are volatile and connectivity margins are compressing.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The quantitative screen flags CMCSA across five factor families—cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk—with a composite score of 91% (sector-relative 86%). That aligns with a business still producing large cash flows: FY2025 operating cash flow was $33.6bn (10-K), dividend payments were $4.9bn, and the board maintained a $0.33 quarterly dividend ($1.32 annualised). Passes on Deep Value, FCF Yield, Earnings Yield, Magic Formula, and Acquirer's Multiple point to a market pricing the stock as a melting ice cube rather than a hybrid connectivity/media/parks compounder.

Business quality is mixed rather than pristine—the screen fails Graham Defensive, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, and Financial Health, which is consistent with filings describing persistent broadband and video customer losses in an "increasingly competitive environment." Offsets are real: domestic wireless lines rose to 10.2m (+883k H1 2026, Q2 10-Q), Peacock paid subscribers reached 48m (41m prior year), and Universal Orlando benefited from Epic Universe (opened May 2025). For a value investor, CMCSA offers a rare combination of sub-book equity, high cash yield, and identifiable corporate actions (Versant separation completed 2 Jan 2026; NBCUniversal/Sky spin announced Jun 2026) that could force re-rating—provided connectivity erosion does not outrun cash returns.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Structural/connectivity:** Filings repeatedly warn that customer additions are "negatively impacted by an increasingly competitive environment." Domestic broadband ARPU pressure is explicit from simplified pricing and a free wireless line promotion. Q2 2026 residential connectivity Adjusted EBITDA fell 8% YoY with margin down 160 bps to 37.7%.

**Spin-off execution:** Q2 2026 10-Q Item 1A flags that the NBCUniversal/Sky separation may be delayed or fail conditions (board approval, tax opinions, regulatory approvals, financing). It may not qualify as tax-free; completion creates two smaller entities with duplicated costs. Share repurchases were **suspended from Q3 2026** in connection with the proposed NBCUniversal transaction—removing a key capital-return lever near 52-week lows.

**Earnings quality:** FY2025 and Q2 2025 GAAP net income were heavily influenced by investment gains/losses ($9.5bn gain in FY2025; $9.8bn prior-year Q2 gain). Screen cheapness partly reflects non-recurring items, not recurring cable earnings.

**Balance sheet and obligations:** ~$99bn gross debt (Dec 2025), negative tangible book (Yahoo: -$58.7bn), and $93.8bn programming/production commitments—with 36% due beyond five years, mostly sports rights (NFL, NBA, Olympics, Premier League). Versant separation transferred ~$3.0bn of debt off Comcast's balance sheet but added transaction complexity.

**Liquidity/covenants:** No covenant breach flagged; revolving facility leverage covenant tested quarterly and met. However, H1 2026 financing cash outflows rose to $11.3bn (debt repayment, Versant cash transfer), and Orlando theme-park attendance softened in June 2026 (news, consistent with filing commentary on post-Epic normalisation).

**Governance/pension:** No material pension or governance issues surfaced in available filing extracts; Roberts family control is not documented in this source pack.
