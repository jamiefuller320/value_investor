Disclaimer* CRH plc (“the Company”) Voting Rights and Capital ----------------------- In accordance with DTR 5.6.1 of the UK's Financial Conduct Authority’s Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules (DTR), CRH plc announces that: The total number of Ordinary Shares of EUR 0.32 each in issue as at 31 st March 2026 was 704,027,647.
