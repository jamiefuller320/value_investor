Capitalized terms used herein and not otherwise defined have the meanings ascribed to them in the 2025 Credit Facility. All other terms and conditions of the 2025 Credit Facility are unchanged and remain in full force and effect. The foregoing description of the 2026 Extension does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by the full terms of the 2025 Credit Facility, which is incorporated herein by reference.
