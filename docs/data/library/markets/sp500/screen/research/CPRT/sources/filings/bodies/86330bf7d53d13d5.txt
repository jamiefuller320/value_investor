Mr. Berger is a Senior Partner of the law firm Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati, P.C. (“Wilson Sonsini”), which serves as outside corporate counsel to the Company. Although there are no related party transactions between the Company and Mr. Berger (or any of his immediate family members) requiring disclosure under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K for the fiscal year ended July 31, 2026, the Company anticipates that it will, on a basis, engage Wilson Sonsini from time to time to perform routine legal services for the Company and its subsidiaries. The Company expects that any fees payable to Wilson Sonsini for the fiscal year ending July 31, 2027 as a result of this engagement will not be material in amount. Mr. Berger does not have any family relationships with any of the Company’s directors or executive officers. Mr. Berger will participate in the Company’s outside director compensation program, as described in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on October 24, 2025. In addition, the Company has entered into its standard form of indemnification agreement with Mr. Berger.
