NEWMONT THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS | NEWS RELEASE 1 Delivering on Newmont's Consistent Capital Allocation Framework Newmont's consistent capital allocation framework is designed to be sustainable through the commodity and investment cycles while maximizing total return of capital to shareholders, maintaining a flexible and resilient balance sheet, and focusing on capital investments for value creation. The capital allocation uses below are presented in order of priority. 1 Newmont is consistently delivering on these priorities, supported by the robust free cash flow generated to date in 2026. Ongoing Sustaining Capital Investment in Portfolio Newmont expects to spend $1.95 billion in 2026 in sustaining capital through targeted investments in critical infrastructure, including tailings solutions, as detailed in the '2026 Guidance Expectations' section below. As of the end of the third quarter, Newmont has invested $1.33 billion of sustaining capital in 2026, with 2026 guidance remaining unchanged. 2 Sustainable Through the Cycle Cash Dividend Newmont is committed to returning capital to shareholders through a sustainable cash dividend of $1.1 billion per year. Central to this framework is a dividend structured to grow on a basis without increasing Newmont's financial commitment, as share repurchases executed through the cycle permanently lower the outstanding share count. The annual total per share dividend target will be calculated annually in February based on the current number of shares issued and outstanding. The dividend payment will be divided into four equal payments rounded up to the nearest $0.01, to be paid out on a quarterly basis, subject to quarterly approval by Newmont's Board of Directors 1 . In line with this commitment, a dividend of $0.26 per share for the third quarter of 2026 has been declared payable on December 21, 2026, to holders of record of such common stock at the close of business on November 25, 2026. This equates to an indicated total annualized dividend of $1.04 per share, with continued dividend increases expected as share repurchases continue. Disciplined Approach to Development Capital Reinvestment Newmont expects to invest $1.4 billion of development capital in 2026 as it advances the free cash flow generative projects, while continuing to study, evaluate and define the future growth profile of its portfolio. As of the end of the third quarter, Newmont has invested $878 million in its current development projects in 2026, with 2026 guidance remaining unchanged. 2 Maintaining an Optimized Capital Structure Through the Cycle Newmont is focused on maintaining a resilient balance sheet, anchored by a $1 billion net cash target 3 , with flexibility of plus or minus $2 billion depending on market conditions. During strong commodity price environments, Newmont intends to further optimize its balance sheet by actively managing gross debt, while maintaining a minimum cash balance of $5 billion through the cycle. RED CHRIS BLOCK CAVE PROJECT 1,2 Initial Development Establishes Foundation for Potential 70-Year Operation The Red Chris Block Cave project is a rare, opportunity located in the Golden Triangle of British Columbia, Canada. The brownfield extension project is designed to mine the porphyry system beneath the existing open pit and includes an underground block cave mine, expansion of existing processing facilities to 15 million tonnes per year, and upgrades to site infrastructure unlocking mineralization at depth. Newmont has a 70 percent ownership interest in Red Chris and is advancing the project with the partnership and support of Imperial Metals, its joint venture partner. The feasibility study is nearing completion, subject to review and governance oversight, with a investment decision now expected in early 2027. As the feasibility study nears completion, the project has already achieved several key milestones, including the receipt of amended regulatory approvals from the Province of British Columbia in June 2026 that enable the transition from open pit mining to block caving. The amended Environmental Assessment Certificate was achieved through a process with the Tahltan Nation, reflecting a shared commitment to responsible resource development, meaningful Indigenous participation and benefits for local communities. The Golden Triangle has been prioritized by both the Provincial Government of British Columbia and the Federal Government of Canada as a region of national importance. Critical minerals, Indigenous partnerships, clean energy infrastructure, and Northwest B.C. economic development have been identified as strategic priorities, resulting in enhanced policy support, regulatory attention, and infrastructure investment in the region. In July 2026, the Government of Canada announced a CAD$500 million contribution to support the project, reinforcing Red Chris as a nationally significant critical minerals project.
