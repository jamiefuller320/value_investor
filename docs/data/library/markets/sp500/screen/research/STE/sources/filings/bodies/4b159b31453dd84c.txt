Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. At the 2026 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (“Meeting”) of STERIS plc (the “Company”) held on July 31, 2026, shareholders voted on the matters specified below, with the final voting results as specified. According to the final report of the inspector of election, there were 97,457,680 ordinary shares of the Company (the “Ordinary Shares”) outstanding and entitled to vote at the Meeting. There were present at the Meeting, in person or by proxy, the holders of 89,909,204 Ordinary Shares or 92.25% of the outstanding Ordinary Shares of the Company on the record date, constituting a quorum. Effective as of the completion of the Meeting, the size of the board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) was reduced to nine members. 1.
