Accordingly, the Company has raised gross proceeds of £661,122 from the Placing and the Retail Offer. Pursuant to the Retail Offer, the Company will issue 67,913,333 new Ordinary Shares (the " New Ordinary Shares "). The Retail Offer is conditional on the New Ordinary Shares being admitted to trading on the AIM market operated by London Stock Exchange plc (" Admission "). Admission is expected to take place on 8 May 2024. Following Admission, the Company will have 4,469,672,441 New Ordinary Shares in issue.
