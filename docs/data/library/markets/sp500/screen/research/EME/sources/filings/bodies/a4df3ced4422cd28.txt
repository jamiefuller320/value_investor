(NYSE: EME) today reported results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Third Quarter 2026 Results of Operations For the third quarter of 2026, revenues totaled a quarterly record of $5.54 billion, an increase of 28.8%, compared to revenues of $4.30 billion for the third quarter of 2025. On an organic basis, when adjusting for incremental acquisition contribution and the impact of the sale of the Company's United Kingdom operations in 2025, third quarter revenues increased by 25.5%. Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $399.9 million, or $9.04 per diluted share, compared to net income of $295.4 million, or $6.57 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. Net income for the third quarter of 2026 included a impairment loss on identifiable intangible assets of $34.0 million, or $24.7 million after taxes. Excluding such impairment, net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $424.7 million, or $9.60 per diluted share. Operating income for the third quarter of 2026 was $545.8 million, or 9.8% of revenues, compared to operating income of $405.7 million, or 9.4% of revenues, for the third quarter of 2025. Excluding the previously referenced impairment loss, operating income for the third quarter of 2026 was $579.8 million, or 10.5% of revenues. Operating income included depreciation and amortization expense (inclusive of amortization of identifiable intangible assets) of $57.3 million and $47.8 million for the third quarter of 2026 and 2025, respectively. – MORE – EMCOR Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results Page 2 Refer to the attached tables for a reconciliation of organic revenue growth, operating income, operating margin, net income, and diluted earnings per share to the comparable GAAP measures. Selling, general and administrative expenses for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $528.4 million, or 9.5% of revenues, compared to $429.6 million, or 10.0% of revenues, for the third quarter of 2025. The Company's income tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 27.2%, compared to 27.5% for the third quarter of 2025. Remaining performance obligations (“RPOs”) as of September 30, 2026 were a record $18.57 billion, compared to $12.61 billion as of September 30, 2025, an increase of $5.96 billion , due to new contract awards across the majority of the sectors in which the Company operates. The most significant RPO growth occurred within the Network and Communications market sector as mission critical facilities continue to require substantial electrical infrastructure and increasingly sophisticated cooling systems. The Company also benefited from a broad range of customer investments across other large end markets, including the Healthcare, Water and Wastewater, Institutional, and Manufacturing and Industrial sectors. Tony Guzzi, Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer of EMCOR, commented, “EMCOR delivered another quarter of exceptional growth, margin performance, operating cash flow, and capital allocation. Our strong revenue growth, coupled with excellent field execution and improved SG&A leverage, resulted in meaningful expansion of our operating margin. We continued to outgrow the broader construction market, reinforcing the depth of demand for our services." First Nine Months 2026 Results of Operations Revenues for the first nine months of 2026 totaled $15.32 billion, an increase of 22.9%, compared to revenues of $12.47 billion for the first nine months of 2025. On an organic basis, when adjusting for incremental acquisition contribution and the impact of the sale of the Company's United Kingdom operations in 2025, revenues for the first nine months of 2026 increased by 20.8%. Net income for the first nine months of 2026 was $1.11 billion, or $24.93 per diluted share, compared to net income of $838.2 million, or $18.53 per diluted share, for the first nine months of 2025. Net income for the first nine months of 2026 included a impairment loss on identifiable intangible assets of $34.0 million, or $24.7 million after taxes. Net income for the first nine months of 2025 included $9.4 million, or $6.9 million after taxes, of transaction related costs associated with the acquisition of Miller Electric Company. Excluding these items, net income for the first nine months of 2026 was $1.13 billion, or $25.48 per diluted share, and net income for the first nine months of 2025 was $845.2 million, or $18.68 per diluted share. Operating income for the first nine months of 2026 was $1.50 billion, or 9.8% of revenues, compared to operating income of $1.14 billion, or 9.1% of revenues, for the first nine months of 2025. Excluding the previously referenced impairment loss in 2026 and transaction costs in 2025, operating income for the first nine months of 2026 was $1.53 billion, or 10.0% of revenues, and operating income for the first nine months of 2025 was $1.15 billion, or 9.2% of revenues. Operating income included depreciation and amortization expense (inclusive of amortization of identifiable intangible assets) of $164.1 million and $136.4 million for the first nine months of 2026 and 2025, respectively. – MORE – EMCOR Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results Page 3 Refer to the attached tables for a reconciliation of organic revenue growth, operating income, operating margin, net income, and diluted earnings per share to the comparable GAAP measures. Selling, general and administrative expenses totaled $1.46 billion, or 9.6% of revenues, for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $1.25 billion, or 10.0% of revenues, for the first nine months of 2025. The Company's income tax rate was 26.6% for the first nine months of 2026, compared to 26.7% for the first nine months of 2025. Increases Share Repurchase Program The Company also announced that its Board of Directors has authorized an additional $500 million for the purchase of shares of its outstanding common stock under its existing share repurchase program. Tony Guzzi, Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer of EMCOR, remarked, “The increase in our share repurchase authorization reflects our strong financial position, confidence in the prospects of our business, and commitment to balanced and disciplined capital allocation. Our capital allocation strategy supports value creation and has provided the foundation for our compounding record of success." Repurchases under the program will be funded by the Company’s operations. Shares will be repurchased from time to time on the open market or through privately negotiated transactions at the Company’s discretion, subject to market conditions, and in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.
