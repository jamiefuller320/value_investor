Financial Results Overview First Nine Months 2026 Financial Results 1 For the first nine months of 2026, net earnings attributable to common stockholders were $1.78 billion, or $11.60 per diluted share, EBITDA was $3.00 billion, and adjusted EBITDA was $3.01 billion. These results compare to first nine months of 2025 net earnings attributable to common stockholders of $1.05 billion, or $6.39 per diluted share, EBITDA of $2.05 billion, and adjusted EBITDA of $2.07 billion. First nine months 2026 financial results include a gain of approximately $170 million from a litigation settlement. Net sales in the first nine months of 2026 were $6.07 billion compared to $5.21 billion in the first nine months of 2025. Average selling prices for the first nine months of 2026 were higher than in the first nine months of 2025. Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results For the third quarter of 2026, net earnings attributable to common stockholders were $436 million, or $2.87 per diluted share, EBITDA was $822 million, and adjusted EBITDA was $833 million. Sales volumes were 7% higher in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 due to higher UAN, urea and other sales, partially offset by AN and ammonia sales. Excluding the impact of lost product availability from the Company’s Yazoo City Complex, sales volumes in the third quarter of 2026 were approximately 13% higher than the third quarter of 2025. Cost of sales for the third quarter of 2026 was higher compared to the third quarter of 2025 due primarily to the impact of higher costs for purchased products, freight and distribution and maintenance, including the extended outage at the Company’s Yazoo City Complex, partially offset by lower realized natural gas costs. The average cost of natural gas, including the impact of realized derivatives, reflected in the Company’s cost of sales was $2.88 per MMBtu in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the average cost of natural gas in cost of sales of $2.96 per MMBtu in the third quarter of 2025. Nitrogen Market Outlook Global nitrogen purchasing slowed late in the second quarter of 2026, but rebounded in the third quarter in North America, India, South America and Europe. Buying activity was driven by higher corn and wheat prices, which, in addition to lower global nitrogen prices than earlier in 2026, enabled some purchases following demand deferral. Global nitrogen prices increased through the quarter compared to the end of the second quarter of 2026 as a result of the higher demand against continued constrained global nitrogen supply. 3 Based on continued global nitrogen supply constraints, management expects the global nitrogen balance to remain constructive for producers operating in and North America in the near- and . Ongoing geopolitical risks for major nitrogen exporters: CF Industries estimates that only about half of average urea exports from the Middle East have exited the Persian Gulf region since the conflict with Iran began. Management expects supply from the region to remain below levels due to continued hostilities, difficulty transiting the Strait of Hormuz, and the time required to restore production facilities. The Company also expects higher shipping costs from the region to persist over the longer term. Production from nitrogen facilities in Russia also remains at risk due to the ongoing war and shipments are constrained due to hostilities in the Black Sea. , production remains : Production economics in Europe have become more challenged due to sharply higher natural gas prices and stable global nitrogen prices while natural gas availability continues to affect production in Trinidad. An estimated 37% of Europe’s nameplate ammonia capacity was reportedly offline or operating at reduced rates in October 2026. In addition, approximately 35% of ammonia capacity in Trinidad is being permanently shut down. The Company believes these dynamics will further pressure marginal producers in Europe and other regions. Global demand expected to remain robust: Management believes nitrogen channel inventory is low throughout the global system for all nitrogen products, including in North America. India is expected to import over 10 million metric tons of urea in 2026 while nitrogen imports into South America and Southeast Asian countries are below average levels . As a result, management expects robust demand in the short- to to meet global nitrogen requirements. Chinese urea exports helping meet global demand: Management projects that Chinese urea exports in 2026 will be 2-4 million metric tons higher than in 2025, filling part of the gap in traded supply due to geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East and in Russia. Management expects the Chinese government to continue to manage nitrogen export levels in line with its domestic food security policies. The Company continues to expect the global nitrogen balance to tighten over the long term, as capacity additions under construction are not projected to keep pace with demand growth over the next four years. Additionally, the time required for building future nitrogen production has increased in part due to geopolitical disruptions affecting the procurement of equipment, extending the cycle duration for new capacity additions to the global market. Strategic Initiatives Update Blue Point One Joint Venture with JERA and Mitsui The Blue Point One joint venture is constructing at CF Industries’ Blue Point Complex in Modeste, Louisiana, an autothermal reforming ammonia production facility with a carbon dioxide (CO 2 ) dehydration and compression unit to prepare captured CO 2 for transportation and sequestration, and CF Industries will build scalable infrastructure to supply the ammonia production facility with services, including production storage and vessel loading. Construction on Blue Point One and the common facilities commenced in August. Courtright Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) FEED Study CF Industries commenced a engineering and design (FEED) study led by thyssenkrupp Uhde for the construction of a new urea liquor upgrade plant at its Courtright, Ontario, Complex. The project, if a positive final investment decision (FID) is reached, is expected to add incremental DEF capacity of more than 400,000 tons per year. FID is expected in 2027. Yazoo City Restoration Project Management expects the Yazoo City Complex to resume operations during the first half of 2027. The Company is finalizing cost estimates for restoration of operations at the facility and expects a substantial portion of the cost will be covered by insurance.
