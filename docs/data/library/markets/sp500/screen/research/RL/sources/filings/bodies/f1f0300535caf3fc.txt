" Recent Developments Next Generation Transformation Project We began a global project in Fiscal 2024 that is expected to significantly transform the way in which we operate our business and further enable our strategic pivot towards a global model (the "Next Generation Transformation project" or "NGT project"). The NGT project is expected to continue over the next several years, with implementation expected to occur in phases by region and/or capability, and involves the redesigning of certain processes and the implementation of a suite of technology systems on a global scale.
