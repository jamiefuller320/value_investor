(NYSE: GL) reported today that for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, net income was $4.62 per diluted common share, compared with $4.73 per diluted common share for the quarter. Net operating income was $4.84 per diluted common share, compared with $4.81 per diluted common share for the quarter. HIGHLIGHTS: l Net income as an ROE (1) was 19.7% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Book value per share was $85.35, an increase of 23% over the quarter. l Net operating income as an ROE (1) excluding accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI) was 15.9% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Book value per share excluding AOCI was $103.62, an increase of 11% over the quarter. l Total premium revenue grew 7% over the quarter. l At the American Income Life Division, life premium increased 4% and life underwriting margin (1) increased 3% over the quarter. l At the Liberty National Division, life net sales increased 7% and life premium increased 4% over the quarter. Additionally, the average producing agent count increased 9% over the quarter. l At the Family Heritage Division, health underwriting margin (1) increased 12%, health premium increased 8%, and health net sales increased 5% over the quarter. l 1.1 million shares of Globe Life Inc. common stock were repurchased during the quarter at a total cost of $185 million. (1) The Company updates reserve assumptions in the third quarter each year. The results include the impact of this assumption update as a before tax remeasurement gain or loss. The current period includes a remeasurement gain due to assumption updates of $120.5 million ($96.4 million and $24.1 million gain related to Life and Health respectively) as compared assumption related remeasurement gains of $134.3 million ($130.9 and $3.4 million gains related to Life and Health respectively) in the year ago quarter. These amounts, along with experience related adjustments, are included within the remeasurement gain or loss in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Operations. Insurance underwriting income is the sum of the insurance underwriting margins of the life and health segments, plus annuity and other income, less administrative expenses. It excludes the investment segment, interest on debt, Parent Company expense, stock compensation expense and income taxes. Management believes this information helps provide a better understanding of the business and a more meaningful analysis of underwriting results by distribution channel. Insurance underwriting income, a measure, is a component of net operating income, which is reconciled to net income in the Results of Operations section above. LIQUIDITY/CAPITAL: Globe Life's operations consist primarily of writing basic protection life and supplemental health insurance policies which generate strong and stable cash flows. These cash flows are not impacted by volatile equity markets. Liquidity at the Parent Company is sufficient to meet additional capital needs of the insurance companies. EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2026 and 2027: The Company adjusted 2026 earnings guidance to a range of $15.35 to $15.65 due to two items impacting the results at our United American General Agency division, rather than a fundamental change in our underlying core business: claims at Evry, a developing group health business still growing and scaling to a size that will enable stable results, and a claims processing delay at the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services which resulted in excess Medicare Supplement claims during the third quarter. For the year ending December 31, 2027, we estimate that net operating income per share will be in the range of $16.10 to $17.10, an increase of 7.1% at the midpoint.
