Item 8.01 Other Events. On October 29, 2026, Valero , L.P., a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire all the outstanding equity interests of LYB Houston Hub Holdings LLC (“LYB Houston”) from a subsidiary of LyondellBasell for a purchase price of $320.5 million, subject to closing adjustments. Closing of the transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including the expiration or termination of the waiting period under the Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended, and is expected to occur by the end of 2026. LYB Houston indirectly owns an approximately 700-acre facility adjacent to the Company’s Houston refinery on the Houston Ship Channel in Texas that operated as a petroleum refinery.
