(d) On September 18, 2025, the board of directors (the “Board”) of Valero Energy Corporation (“Valero”) increased its size to 10 members and elected Robert L. Reymond as a member of the Board, with an initial term beginning immediately and expiring at Valero’s 2026 annual meeting of stockholders (the “2026 Annual Meeting”). Additionally, the Board appointed Mr. Reymond to serve on the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee of the Board, effective immediately. Mr. Reymond is expected to stand for at the 2026 Annual Meeting. Mr. Reymond is entitled to participate in Valero’s director compensation program, as described under “Director Compensation” in Valero’s proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 18, 2025. Mr. Reymond is entitled to receive compensation for the period of his service on the Board from the date of his election on September 18, 2025, to the anticipated date of the 2026 Annual Meeting. Accordingly, Valero granted Mr. Reymond a equity grant of 924 stock units upon his election to the Board, which are scheduled to vest in full on the first anniversary of the date of grant, and Mr. Reymond is also entitled to receive a annual cash retainer in the amount of $97,500.
