BETA Close X Beximco Pharmaceuticals PLC reported strong financial results for the year ended 30 June 2025, with net revenue increasing by 10.7% to BDT 49,120.4 million (£295.6 million), driven by a 37.7% surge in export sales to BDT 4,073.5 million (£24.5 million).
