(NYSE: BXP) , the largest publicly traded developer, owner, and manager of premier workplaces in the United States, reported results today for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights • Revenue increased 4.6% to $911.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $871.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. • Net income (loss) attributable to BXP, Inc. of $114.5 million, or $0.71 per diluted share (EPS), for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $(121.7) million, or $(0.77) per diluted share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. ◦ EPS for the third quarter 2026 exceeded the midpoint of BXP’s guidance by $0.20 per diluted share primarily due to gains on sales related to disposition activity and portfolio performance. • Funds from Operations (FFO) of $302.2 million, or $1.89 per diluted share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to FFO of $276.7 million, or $1.74 per diluted share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. ◦ FFO for the third quarter 2026 exceeded the midpoint of BXP’s guidance by $0.08 per diluted share primarily due to portfolio performance. Guidance BXP provided guidance for 2026 EPS of $2.35 - $2.37 and FFO of $7.06 - $7.08 per diluted share. The midpoint of 2026 guidance for EPS increased by $0.17 per diluted share, primarily due to the aforementioned gains on sales and portfolio performance. The midpoint of 2026 guidance for FFO increased by $0.05 per diluted share primarily due to portfolio performance as described above. See “EPS and FFO per Share Guidance” below. Leasing & Occupancy • Executed 74 leases in the third quarter totaling approximately 1.2 million square feet with a lease term of 10.3 years. Notables leases include: ◦ an approximately 206,000 square foot lease with a technology company at 500 North Capitol in Washington, DC. ◦ an approximately 112,000 square foot lease renewal with a law firm at Madison Centre in Seattle, Washington. –more– • For the third quarter, BXP’s CBD portfolio of premier workplaces was 91.8% occupied and 94.1% leased (including vacant space for which we have signed leases that have not yet commenced revenue recognition in accordance with GAAP). Approximately 90.4% of BXP’s Share of annualized rental obligations is derived from clients located in our CBD portfolio, underscoring the strength of BXP’s strategy to invest in the highest quality buildings in dynamic urban gateway markets. • BXP’s total portfolio occupancy for the third quarter was 89.4%, an increase of 100 basis points from Q2 2026, marking the fourth consecutive quarter of occupancy gains. • Total portfolio leased percentage was 91.9% (including vacant space for which we have signed leases that have not yet commenced revenue recognition in accordance with GAAP), an increase of 60 basis points from Q2 2026. As of September 30, 2026, the spread between leased and occupied square footage was 250 basis points, representing approximately 1.1 million square feet of future lease commencements, with approximately half expected to commence before 2026. Transactions • Completed the sale of a 10.6% equity interest in the 343 Madison Avenue development to AVRS Partners, generating additional capital, reducing BXP's required equity commitment, and preserving BXP's full control of the project, including all development, leasing, and property management responsibilities. • During the third quarter, we completed the sales of Sumner Square and 1330 Connecticut Avenue in Washington, DC. The aggregate gross sales price of these two office sales totaled approximately $155.4 million, resulting in aggregate net proceeds of approximately $150.3 million and gains on sales of real estate of approximately $10.5 million. Balance Sheet & Liquidity • BXP entered into a $1.2 billion construction loan for the development of 343 Madison Avenue in New York City, New York. The loan has a initial term, plus a extension option subject to customary conditions, and was executed on competitive terms, including an initial interest rate of Term SOFR plus 2.50%, which will be reduced to Term SOFR plus 2.25% upon the achievement of certain leasing and construction milestones. The financing represents a significant milestone in the capitalization of the project and supports the ongoing construction of one of New York City's most anticipated workplace developments. • Boston Properties Limited Partnership (“BPLP”) completed a public offering of $700.0 million in aggregate principal amount of its 6.050% unsecured senior notes due 2036. The notes were priced at 99.837% of the principal amount to yield an effective rate (including financing fees) of approximately 6.162% per annum to maturity. The notes will mature on October 15, 2036, unless earlier redeemed. The aggregate net proceeds from the offering were approximately $692.4 million after deducting underwriting discounts and transaction expenses. • A joint venture in which BXP owns a 30% interest modified the financing secured by its 500 North Capitol property in Washington, D.C., consolidating two mortgage loans totaling $105.0 million into a single, $105.0 million facility maturing in September 2031. The facility consists of a $70.0 million component bearing interest at a fixed rate of 6.778% and a $35.0 million component bearing interest at Term SOFR plus 2.00%, replacing debt that previously bore interest at a fixed rate of 6.88%. 500 North Capitol Street is an office property with –more– approximately 231,000 net rentable square feet located in Washington, DC. and is 95% leased. Management generally considers FFO and FFO per share to be useful measures for understanding and comparing our operating results because, by excluding gains and losses related to sales or a change in control of previously depreciated operating real estate assets, impairment losses and real estate asset depreciation and amortization (which can differ across owners of similar assets in similar condition based on historical cost accounting and useful life estimates), FFO and FFO per share can help investors compare the operating performance of a company ’ s real estate across reporting periods and to the operating performance of other companies.
