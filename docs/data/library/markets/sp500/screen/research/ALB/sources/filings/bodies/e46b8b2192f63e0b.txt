If applicable performance exceeds threshold but is less than target for an applicable year, the AIP payment for such year will be less than the target bonus. If applicable performance exceeds target for an applicable year, the AIP payment for such year will be greater than the target bonus. It is understood that the Company determines eligibility for awards and the terms of awards on an annual basis, and that information about awards shall be communicated to Executive in accordance with Company practice and this Section 4(b).
