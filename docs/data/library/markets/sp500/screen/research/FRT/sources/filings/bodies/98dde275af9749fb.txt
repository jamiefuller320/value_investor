Overview Federal Realty Investment Trust is a leader in the ownership, operation, and redevelopment of properties located primarily in major coastal markets and select underserved regions that we believe have strong economic and demographic fundamentals. Founded in 1962, our mission is to deliver , sustainable growth through investing in communities where we believe retail demand exceeds supply.
