On May 7, 2025, Federal Realty Investment Trust (the “Company”) and Mr. Daniel Guglielmone, the Company’s Executive Vice Financial Officer and Treasurer, entered into an amendment to Mr. Guglielmone’s Severance Agreement dated August 15, 2016.
