United States U.S. Patent Litigation On August 2, 2022, the Company filed a patent infringement lawsuit against Mylan Pharmaceuticals Inc. ("Mylan"), a subsidiary of Viatris Inc., in the United States District Court for the Northern District of West Virginia alleging that Mylan's filing for U.S. Food and Drug Administration ("FDA") approval of an aflibercept 2 mg biosimilar infringes certain Company patents.
