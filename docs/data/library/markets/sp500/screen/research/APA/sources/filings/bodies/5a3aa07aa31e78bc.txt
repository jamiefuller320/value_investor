HOUSTON, Nov. 4, 2026 – APA Corporation (Nasdaq: APA) today announced its financial and operational results for the third quarter of 2026. APA reported net income attributable to common stock of $698 million, or $2.00 per diluted share. When adjusted for certain items that impact the comparability of results, APA’s earnings totaled $676 million, or $1.94 per diluted share. 1 APA CORPORATION ANNOUNCES 2026 FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL RESULTS — PAGE 2 of 6 summary reported production was 450,000 BOE per day, and adjusted production was 381,000 BOE per day, both exceeding guidance. U.S. oil production averaged 128,000 barrels per day, 4,000 barrels per day above guidance, reflecting high uptime, earlier and robust new well performance in the Permian Basin. In Egypt, gross production averaged 206,000 BOE per day, relatively flat with the second quarter. Gross gas production increased to 542 million cubic feet (MMCF) per day, exceeding guidance through strong well performance and more resilient base production. Average gas realizations increased to $4.34 per Mcf from $4.23 in the second quarter. Adjusted production averaged 64,000 BOE per day, slightly lower than guidance primarily due to production sharing contract impacts associated with higher Brent prices. Net cash provided by operating activities was $1.38 billion, and adjusted EBITDAX was $1.86 billion. Upstream capital investment was $466 million, approximately $30 million below guidance, primarily reflecting timing impacts of GranMorgu capital spending that shifted into the fourth quarter; lease operating expense was $418 million. Free cash flow totaled $699 million, bringing free cash flow to $1.91 billion. Balance sheet and shareholder returns APA has repaid $1.2 billion of debt maturities , including $451 million in the third quarter. The company has reduced total debt by $2.8 billion since 2024, lowering annualized interest expense by more than $180 million; these savings are incremental to the company’s controllable spend reduction program. Net debt was $3.2 billion at the end of the third quarter. APA returned $537 million to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases during the third quarter. Through October, the company repurchased an additional 4.5 million shares at an average price of $47.32 per share, bringing total repurchases to 17.8 million shares. Consistent with its established capital returns framework, APA expects to return at least 60% of 2026 free cash flow to shareholders while continuing to strengthen its balance sheet. At current strip prices APA expects to reach its $3 billion net debt target by . Building on this progress, the company has updated its net debt target to $2 billion. Continued debt reduction will lower financing costs and enhance financial flexibility. Christmann IV, APA’s CEO. “Our teams are raising the standard for performance, operating more efficiently, and translating those gains into substantial cash flow.
