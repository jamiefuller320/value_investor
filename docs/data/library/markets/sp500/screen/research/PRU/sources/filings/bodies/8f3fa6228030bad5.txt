On October 20, 2026, Prudential Financial, Inc. (“Prudential” or the “Company”), announced that it has entered into definitive agreements to sell 100% of the Company’s equity interests in each of Prudential do Brasil Seguros S.A. (“Prudential of Brazil”) and Prudential Seguros Mexico, S.A. de C.V. (“Prudential of Mexico”) to the CNP Assurances Group (respectively to CNP Seguradora, CNP Assurances’ Brazilian subsidiary, and to CNP Assurances Holding) for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $4.025 billion in cash, subject to certain closing adjustments. The Company expects the transaction to close in the second half of 2027, subject to the receipt of regulatory approvals and the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure. The Company is furnishing herewith as Exhibit 99.1 a news release announcing the transaction.
