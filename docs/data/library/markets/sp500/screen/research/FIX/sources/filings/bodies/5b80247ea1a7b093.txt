(NYSE: FIX) (the "Company"), a leading provider of commercial, industrial and institutional heating, ventilation, air conditioning and electrical contracting services, today announced that Bill George, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, will retire following the filing of the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ending December 31, 2026, completing a remarkable 30-year career that helped build the Company from its founding into one of the nation’s leading building services companies. In accordance with the Company’s succession plan, Julie Shaeff, currently the Company's Senior Vice President and Chief Accounting Officer, is being promoted to Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, effective upon Mr. George vacating that role. Mr. George helped found the Company in 1997 and has served as Chief Financial Officer since May 2005. Over the course of his career, he helped guide the Company through periods of significant growth and change, including its formation and initial public offering, the development of its acquisition program and the evolution of the Company’s strategy that produced growth. He played a key role in strengthening the Company’s balance sheet, establishing its disciplined capital allocation approach and supporting its long record of financial performance. Ms. Shaeff has served as the Company's Chief Accounting Officer since May 2005 and was formerly Vice President and Corporate Controller from March 2002 to May 2005 and Assistant Corporate Controller from September 1999 to February 2002. From 1996 to August 1999, Ms. Shaeff was Financial Accounting Manager—Corporate Controllers Group for Industries, Inc., a waste services company. From 1987 to 1995, she held various positions with Arthur Andersen LLP. Ms. Shaeff is a Certified Public Accountant and holds a Bachelor of Business Administration in Accounting from Texas A&M University. Brian E. Lane, Chief Executive Officer, commented, "Our thoughtful succession planning has positioned Comfort Systems USA for this transition. Julie has been an integral part of our finance organization for over 25 years, and her deep knowledge of our operations, judgment and leadership make her the ideal person to lead our finance function going forward." Mr. Lane continued, "Bill has been part of Comfort Systems USA since its formation in 1997. Over three decades, Bill has led with integrity, building a financial foundation, acquisition environment and overall culture that have carried this company to where we are today. On behalf of our Board, our leadership team and our employees across the country, I want to thank Bill for his extraordinary service, his friendship and his steady leadership. We are deeply grateful, and we wish him a long and retirement." Mr. George said, "It has been the honor of my career to help build and serve Comfort Systems USA, and I treasure my relationships with the team of professionals in Houston and in our organizations across this country. Comfort Systems USA is great because of the amazing individuals who call this company their professional home, and everything good that has happened at Comfort has been accomplished through teamwork. Julie is an amazing financial leader, and I am deeply indebted for her keen judgement, unerring instincts and technical excellence for our finance team and in many other corners of our organization. I am retiring confident that Brian, Trent, Julie and the team that remains at Comfort Systems USA will continue to prove that they are the very best in our business."
