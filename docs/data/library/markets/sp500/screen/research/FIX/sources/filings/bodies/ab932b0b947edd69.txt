(NYSE: FIX) (“Comfort Systems USA”), a leading provider of commercial, industrial and institutional heating, ventilation, air conditioning and electrical contracting services, today announced that its Board of Directors has approved an amendment to Comfort Systems USA’s stock repurchase program to increase the shares authorized and remaining as available for repurchase to 1,000,000 shares by authorizing Comfort Systems USA to acquire up to 679,520 additional shares of its outstanding common stock. Comfort Systems USA’s existing stock repurchase program had previously authorized the repurchase of up to 12,752,570 shares of Comfort Systems USA’s outstanding common stock. From January 1, 2026 through November 16, 2026, Comfort Systems USA repurchased 1,552,236 shares of Comfort Systems USA’s common stock at an aggregate price of $1,891,702,158 (including commissions and exclusive of excise tax). The repurchases were made from ongoing cash flows and a portion of existing cash balances. This increase of the stock repurchase program will “top off” the plan and permit Comfort Systems USA to repurchase up to an additional 1,000,000 shares of its currently outstanding common stock beyond what had already been purchased through November 16, 2026. The share repurchases will be made from time to time at Comfort Systems USA’s discretion in the open market or privately negotiated transactions as permitted by securities laws and other legal requirements, and subject to market conditions and other factors. Comfort Systems USA expects that the share repurchases will be financed with available cash. The program does not obligate Comfort Systems USA to acquire any particular number of shares. Comfort Systems USA’s Board of Directors may modify, suspend, extend, or terminate the program at any time. Comfort Systems USA® is a leading provider of commercial, industrial and institutional heating, ventilation, air conditioning and electrical contracting services, with 211 locations in 155 cities across the nation. For more information, visit Comfort Systems USA’s website at www.comfortsystemsusa.com.
