BETA Close X Empresaria Group plc anticipates its adjusted profit before tax for the year ended 31 December 2025 will be slightly ahead of market expectations, despite challenging trading conditions. The company reported unaudited net fee income of £47.3 million, a 6% decrease on a reported basis, but unchanged on a constant currency like-for-like basis. Significant growth was seen in Offshore Services (16% CC LFL) and the US (23% CC LFL), while other regions and temporary/contract placements saw reductions. Net debt increased to £17.1 million, and no final dividend will be recommended for 2025.
