Business GENERAL Fair Isaac Corporation (NYSE: FICO) (together with its consolidated subsidiaries, the “Company,” which may also be referred to in this report as “we,” “us,” “our,” and “FICO”) is a global analytics software leader. We were founded in 1956 on the premise that data, used intelligently, can improve business decisions. Today, FICO’s software and the widely used FICO ® Score operationalize analytics, enabling thousands of businesses in more than 70 countries to uncover new opportunities, make timely decisions that matter, and execute them at scale. Most leading banks and credit card issuers rely on our solutions, as do insurers, retailers, telecommunications providers, automotive lenders, consumer reporting agencies, public agencies, and organizations in other industries. We also serve consumers through online services that enable people to access and understand their FICO Scores — the standard measure of consumer credit risk in the United States (“U.S.”) — empowering them to increase financial literacy and manage their financial health. More information about us can be found on our website at www.fico.com. We make our Annual Reports on Forms 10-K, Quarterly Reports on Forms 10-Q, and Current Reports on Forms 8-K, as well as amendments to those reports, available free of charge on the “Investors” page of our website at www.fico.com as soon as reasonably practicable after we electronically file them with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”). References to our website address in this report do not constitute an incorporation by reference. Information on our website is not part of this report. PRODUCTS AND SERVICES Our business consists of two operating segments: Scores and Software. Our Scores segment includes our (“B2B”) scoring solutions and services which give our clients access to predictive credit and other scores that can be easily integrated into their transaction streams and processes. This segment also includes our (“B2C”) scoring solutions, including our myFICO.com subscription offerings. Our Software segment includes decisioning capabilities, models and solutions designed to operationalize intelligence across specific types of business needs or processes — such as account origination, customer management, customer engagement, fraud detection, and marketing — as well as associated professional services. This segment is anchored by FICO ® Platform, a modern and modular set of intelligent decisioning capabilities that are designed to support artificial intelligence (“AI”), and advanced analytic and decisioning use cases. Our offerings are available to our customers as (“SaaS”) or as software. Scores Our scoring solutions include the FICO ® Score, which is the standard measure of consumer credit risk in the U.S., providing lenders, investors, and consumers with a consistent and transparent framework for evaluating and communicating about credit risk throughout the lending lifecycle. It is used in most U.S. credit decisions, by nearly all major banks, credit card issuers, mortgage lenders, and auto loan originators. Our B2B scoring solutions are primarily distributed through major consumer reporting agencies in many countries around the world. Our B2C scores are sold directly to consumers through our myFICO.com website and other channels. The FICO ® Score is a number ranging from 300-850. Our proprietary analytic algorithms are applied to credit data collected and maintained by the three U.S. nationwide consumer reporting agencies — Experian, TransUnion and Equifax — to produce standard scores that are used across the credit lifecycle, including in origination, account management and consumer marketing. Users of our scores generally pay the consumer reporting agencies a fee for each individual score generated by our algorithms, and the consumer reporting agencies pay an associated fee to us. Except for product development using data, FICO does not collect or store the consumer credit data used in the calculation of our scores, and in most cases, we do not sell our scores directly to lenders or other . 3 Table of Contents Since the introduction of the FICO ® Score in the U.S. in 1989, we have regularly updated the score to take advantage of newly available data and enhanced analytics. Updated versions of our FICO Scores are generally designed to provide greater predictive accuracy than the scores they replace, and to be compatible with prior versions of the FICO Score. FICO ® Score 9 introduced the utilization of reported rental payment history, while also medical debt and disregarding paid collections. Our latest and most predictive score, FICO ® Score 10T, was introduced in 2020. To increase its predictive power, FICO Score 10T builds on FICO Score 9 but also incorporates trended credit data. Trended data considers a longer historical view, giving lenders even more insight into how individuals are managing their credit. In October 2025, we announced the FICO ® Mortgage Direct License Program. Under this program, resellers, platform providers, and other distributors will have the option to calculate and distribute FICO ® Scores directly to their customers (e.g., mortgage originators), without relying on the three nationwide consumer reporting agencies as intermediaries. Customers that prefer to continue accessing FICO Scores through the consumer reporting agencies may do so. The program is designed to increase price transparency and reduce costs for mortgage lenders, mortgage brokers, and other industry participants. In April 2026, we announced that FICO ® Score 10T would also be made available to mortgage lenders through the FICO Mortgage Direct License Program, consistent with the Federal Housing Finance Agency’s initiative to expand access to modernized mortgage credit scoring data. In addition to the FICO ® Score, we offer several other scores, including specific FICO ® Industry Scores such as Bankcard and Auto Industry versions of FICO Score. We also develop various custom scores for our financial services clients. The FICO ® Resilience Index offering is designed to complement FICO ® Score models by identifying those consumers who are more resilient to economic stress relative to other consumers within the same FICO Score bands. The FICO Resilience Index is designed to enable lenders to continue to lend and better manage risk by providing a more precise assessment of loan default risk during periods of economic stress. FICO has invested significant resources in the development of scores that can help expand credit access and lower borrowing costs for consumers that have limited credit history or who have sparse or inactive credit files. These scores use alternative data sources to enhance conventional consumer reporting agency data and generate scores for otherwise consumers and in many cases improve the credit scores of scorable consumers. • FICO ® Score XD uses public records and property data, and a consumer’s history with mobile phone, landline phone and cable/internet payments, to generate scores on the same 300-850 scale as standard FICO ® Scores. FICO Score XD is available to lenders through our distribution partners, LexisNexis Risk Solutions and Equifax. • The UltraFICO ® Score uses data such as checking, savings, or money market account data, to generate scores on the same 300-850 scale as standard FICO ® Scores. Incorporating data helps empower consumers to establish or improve their creditworthiness by using data that reflects sound financial activity, but that is not part of a conventional credit report. The UltraFICO Score is distributed to lenders through Plaid Check, a product of our distribution partner Plaid, and its consumer reporting agency. Both scores maintain the same score to risk relationship as standard FICO ® Scores, enhancing their compatibility with existing credit underwriting systems and models. Outside the U.S., we offer FICO ® Scores for consumer loans, and in some cases for small and medium business loans. These scores are typically sold to through consumer reporting agencies in those countries. FICO Scores have been made available in over 40 countries and we have also developed versions of the FICO Score in over ten countries. We also provide FICO ® Scores to consumers in the U.S. through our B2C scoring solutions. These Scores are distributed directly by us through our myFICO.com subscription offering and indirectly through our licensed distribution partners, including Experian and certain lenders through the FICO ® Score Open Access Program. Through myFICO.com and other channels, consumers can purchase their FICO Scores, including credit reports associated with the scores, explanations of the factors affecting their scores, and customized educational information on how to manage their scores. Consumers can use products to simulate how taking specific actions could affect their FICO Score. Consumers can also subscribe to credit monitoring, which delivers alerts via email and text when changes to a user’s FICO Scores or other credit report content are detected. In addition, consumers can purchase identity theft monitoring products that alert them to potential risks of identity fraud. 4 Table of Contents Our FICO ® Score Mortgage Simulator is the only mortgage credit score simulator built using our proprietary FICO ® Score algorithms, the same algorithms used by lenders in mortgage decisioning, and is made available to mortgage professionals through resellers and mortgage platform providers. Principal Areas of Expertise We specialize in capabilities, models and solutions that empower businesses to operationalize analytics and decisioning technologies to uncover new opportunities, make timely decisions that matter, and execute them at scale. We have found that bringing human and digital intelligence together empowers our customers to target and acquire customers more efficiently, increase customer value, reduce fraud and credit losses, optimize their operations, lower operating expenses, and enter new markets more profitably. These learnings from our customer deployments over the years have been applied to a new enterprise AI , known as intelligent decisioning platforms. We have been recognized as a leader in Decision Intelligence Platforms by the research and advisory firms Gartner and IDC, and as a leader in AI Decisioning Platforms by Forrester Research. Our principal areas of research and development expertise are focused on the following four domains. • Artificial Intelligence We have a long history of AI innovation, including one of the earliest and most broadly adopted production applications of neural networks for fraud prediction with FICO ® Falcon ® Fraud Manager in the 1990s. Our innovations in and responsible AI continue, with recent advancements in generative AI, agentic approaches, and governance. This includes patented and approaches for interpretable model architectures designed to assist users in meeting stringent ethical and transparency requirements, and a collection of blockchain applications spanning model development, model monitoring, agent definitions, and control and audit trail of tasks performed by complex and interconnected agents. We also recently launched the FICO ® Focused Foundation Model, a new class of models built for the responsible and governed use of generative approaches. To date, we hold over 130 U.S. and international patents covering scientific innovations in AI and responsible AI. • Predictive Modeling Predictive modeling identifies and mathematically represents underlying relationships in historical data to make predictions or classifications about future events. Predictive models typically analyze current and historical data about individuals to produce easily understood metrics such as scores. These scores individuals or specific transactions against a particular variable such as the likelihood of making credit payments on time, the likelihood of a transaction being fraudulent, or the probability of responding to a particular offer for services. These include, but are not limited to, proprietary applications of segmented scorecards and unique methods for reject inference (estimating how rejected loan applicants would have performed if they had been approved). We also apply various statistical techniques for analysis and pattern detection within high velocity datasets and can derive insights and predictive features from various forms of data, including unstructured data. • Decision Analysis and Augmentation We are focused on advancing collaboration for complex problem solving across a variety of scenarios and with multiple tools. This includes research and development of a highly advanced suite of mathematical optimization solvers addressing both linear and problem types. This innovation is purpose built for transaction and sequence data and incorporates a consumer’s complete transaction history and context directly in a decisioning system, providing more robust predictive information for the business to use.
