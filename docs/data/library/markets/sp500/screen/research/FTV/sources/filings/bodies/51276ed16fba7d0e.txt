The Shares were issued at offer prices based on an unaudited net asset value of 71.10 pence per share ranging from 71.10 pence to 76.30 pence. The total gross proceeds receivable by the Company for this allotment is approximately £9.4 million. Application has been made for the admission of the Shares to trading on London Stock Exchange plc’s market for listed securities on or around 23 February 2026. In total the Company has allotted 51,894,559 Ordinary Shares to date under the Offer. Following this allotment there are now 350,923,047 Ordinary Shares of 1p each in issue.
