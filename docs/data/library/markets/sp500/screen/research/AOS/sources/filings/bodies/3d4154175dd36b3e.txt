O. Smith Announces Majority Interest of China Business Acquired by CPE; A. O. Smith to Retain 19% Stake and Brand License Milwaukee, Wis . — Global water technology company A. O. Smith Corporation (“A. O. Smith” or “the Company”) (NYSE: AOS) today announced definitive agreements with CPE, a leading alternative asset manager, under which CPE will acquire an 81% interest in A. O. Smith’s China business. A. O. Smith will retain a 19% interest and enter into a brand licensing agreement, both of which allow it to participate in the business's future growth. The transaction values the China business at approximately $400 million on a , basis, before taking into account the brand licensing royalty stream. The total value, including the net present value of the ongoing licensing economics payable to A. O. Smith over the period, is expected to exceed $500 million. In addition, the transaction structure will enable A. O. Smith to repatriate approximately $80 million in cash that is currently being held in China. Consistent with its commitment to returning capital to shareholders, the Company intends to prioritize share repurchases as a use of the proceeds and repatriated cash following the closing of the transaction. The agreements follow a comprehensive review of strategic alternatives for the China business, announced in July 2025. After evaluating a broad range of options, A. O. Smith’s Board of Directors concluded that a strategic transaction with CPE is the best path forward for the China business, the Company and its shareholders. The transaction will enable A. O. Smith to sharpen its portfolio focus while retaining the opportunity to participate in future value creation in China. “For more than three decades, A. O. Smith has built a leading premium brand in China, supported by an extensive distribution network, differentiated innovation and a highly talented local team,” said Steve Shafer, A. O. Smith chairman and chief executive officer. “We believe this transaction positions the China business for continued growth under experienced local ownership, while enabling A. O. Smith to focus resources on our opportunities and drive sustainable value creation.” A. O. Smith and CPE are committed to the business's customers and distributors throughout the transition and beyond. As brand owner and a minority shareholder, A. O. Smith will continue to cooperate with CPE on branding, product innovation and service quality. “We are deeply grateful to our employees in China for their leadership and continued dedication, particularly in a challenging market,” Shafer added. “We are confident CPE is the right partner to lead the business going forward.” CPE takes a , approach to investing across the full AI value chain, advanced manufacturing, consumer, healthcare and infrastructure, and has significant experience partnering with multinational companies in China, including its recent strategic partnership with Restaurant Brands International to support the Burger King China business. “A. O. Smith has built a trusted, premium brand and a strong business in China over more than three decades,” said Liuwei Qin, Managing Director of CPE. “We are excited and proud to invest in A. O. Smith’s China business and look forward to supporting its next phase of growth through continued investment in its product innovation, service capabilities and customer relationships.” Transaction Details CPE will acquire an 81% equity interest in A. O. Smith (China) Investment Co., Ltd. (“CIC”), the holding company for A. O. Smith’s China business, and A. O. Smith will retain a 19% interest. A. O. Smith will also receive ongoing royalties under a license for the A. O. Smith brand in China. Additional information about the transaction is available on our website. The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions. Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC served as financial advisor and Latham & Watkins LLP served as legal counsel to A. O. Smith. A. O. Smith will release third quarter financial results on Oct. 29, 2026 and host a webcasted conference call at 10:00 a.m. (Eastern Daylight Time) to discuss those results and its guidance for the remainder of the year. The call can be heard live on the Company’s website. An audio replay of the call, along with the related presentation, will be available on the Company’s website after the live event.
