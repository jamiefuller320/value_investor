(“Travelers”) increased the number of directors on the Board from nine to ten and elected Lesley Goldwasser as a director to serve until the next annual election of directors by shareholders and until her successor is elected and has qualified. Ms. Goldwasser was also appointed as a member of the Compensation, Investment and Capital Markets and Nominating and Governance Committees of the Board. Ms. Goldwasser is Vice Chairman of GreensLedge Capital Markets LLC (“GreensLedge”), a Raymond James company. Ms. Goldwasser had been a Managing Partner of GreensLedge from 2013 until its acquisition by Raymond James in March 2026. Prior to joining GreensLedge, Ms. Goldwasser was a Managing Director with Credit Suisse Group with global responsibility for the hedge fund strategic services unit.
