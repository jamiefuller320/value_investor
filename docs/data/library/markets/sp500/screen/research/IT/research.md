# Gartner, Inc. (IT) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-26T17:58:00.610386+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Gartner, Inc. (NYSE: IT) is a subscription-led IT and business insights franchise trading at a depressed multiple after a sharp 2026 drawdown, which the quantitative screen flags as a strong buy on cash-flow yield, quality, and GARP metrics (P/E ~13.9, FCF yield ~11.3%, Lynch PEG ~0.80). The core Insights business generates recurring revenue from ~13,000 enterprise clients with 85% client retention and contract value of ~$5.3bn, supported by durable deferred revenue (~$3.0bn). The central debate is whether AI commoditises proprietary research or reinforces demand for trusted, independent guidance — management is betting on the latter via AskGartner and continued product investment, while the market has punished the stock on Q4 2025 revenue disappointment, federal contract attrition, and AI-disruption fears. At current levels, aggressive buybacks (~$535m in Q1 2026 alone at ~$158/share) and strong operating cash conversion provide a tangible valuation floor, but leverage, negative tangible equity, and earnings volatility warrant selective accumulation rather than indiscriminate sizing.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen rates IT a strong buy (8/22 models passed; composite 71%; families: cheapness, quality, GARP, risk; data quality 19/20). That signal aligns with a business that screens cheaply relative to its cash economics: subscription Insights revenues (~78% of total per FY2025 10-K) throw off high-margin, deferred-revenue-backed cash flows, and the company returned substantial capital via buybacks while contract value continued to grow.

For a value investor, the hook is mispricing of normalized earnings power. Reported FY2025 net income was depressed by a $150m Digital Markets goodwill impairment (Q3 2025 10-Q); normalized earnings (Yahoo fallback: ~$842m) sit well above reported ~$729m. FCF of ~$1.18bn (Yahoo fallback for FY2025) against an enterprise value implied by an ~11% FCF yield supports the screen’s FCF Yield, Quality Value, Buffett Quality, Magic Formula, and Acquirer’s Multiple passes. Growth of ~17.3% (screen metric) and PEG ~0.80 (Lynch PEG pass) suggest the market is pricing stagnation despite Q1 2026 contract value up 1% FX-neutral to $5.3bn (Q1 2026 10-Q).

Business quality indicators from filings reinforce the screen’s quality/GARP passes: Insights gross contribution margin ~78% (Q1 2026 10-Q); wallet retention 97–98% in GTS/GBS; Conferences attendance resilient (53 events, 83,000+ attendees in FY2025 per 10-K). The Piotroski F-Score of 6/9 (failed: ROA trend, leverage trend, current ratio trend) and failures on Graham Defensive, Deep Value, and Financial Health models flag balance-sheet fragility from years of buybacks — not a broken franchise, but a levered capital-return story.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**AI disruption (strategic):** FY2025 10-K devotes extensive risk disclosure to AI/LLM competition, content scraping, AskGartner execution risk, and the possibility that free AI tools reduce willingness to pay for subscription research. This is the primary long-term threat and is not fully captured by backward-looking screens.

**Federal/government exposure (cyclical/policy):** FY2025 10-K states U.S. federal revenues fell ~$58m in 2025; Q1 2025 10-Q showed federal research contract value falling from ~$275m to ~$225m with weak renewal retention. Government contracts (~$1.0bn outstanding) are subject to appropriation risk and termination-for-convenience. Q1 2026 10-Q notes high single-digit public-sector CV decline.

**Goodwill/intangibles and impairment:** $150m Digital Markets impairment in Q3 2025; goodwill ~38% of total assets at 31 December 2025 (10-K). Intangible-heavy balance sheet limits tangible asset support.

**Leverage and buyback intensity:** Senior notes total ~$3.0bn (2028–2035 tranches per 10-K); FY2025 repurchases ~$2.0bn (Yahoo) against equity of ~$320m. Covenant breach could accelerate debt (10-K: “failure to comply… could result in acceleration”). Piotroski leverage and current-ratio components failed.

**Shareholder litigation:** News manifest flags Bernstein Liebhard investigation (9 July 2025) following the February 2026 sell-off; Q1 2025/Q1 2026 10-Qs note ordinary-course legal proceedings with no material accruals disclosed, but litigation risk is elevated post earnings volatility.

**Consulting softness:** Consulting revenue −15% YoY in Q1 2026, consistent with broader IT services budget pressure (IBM revenue warning cited in news as peer read-through, 14 July 2025).

**Pension:** Non-current employee benefit obligations ~$215m (Yahoo FY2025 balance sheet); defined benefit plans referenced in 10-Q OCI — not a primary concern but present.

No going-concern qualification identified in available extracts.
