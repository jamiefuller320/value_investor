On November 18, 2026, Occidental Petroleum Corporation (“ Occidental ”) issued a press release announcing that it had commenced cash tender offers (the “ Tender Offers ”) to purchase, in two separate pools, (i) up to $500.0 million aggregate principal amount (which amount may be increased by Occidental) of its outstanding 7.200% Debentures due 2029 (the “ 7.200% 2029 Debentures ”), 6.625% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “ 6.625% 2030 Notes ”), 7.950% Debentures due 2029 (the “ 7.950% 2029 Debentures ”), 8.450% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “ 8.450% 2029 Notes ”) and 8.875% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “ 8.875% 2030 Notes ”) and (ii) up to $500.0 million aggregate principal amount (which amount may be increased by Occidental) of its outstanding 6.050% Senior Notes due 2054 (the “ 6.050% 2054 Notes ”), 5.550% Senior Notes due 2034 (the “ 5.550% 2034 Notes ”), 4.10% Senior Notes due 2047 (the “ 4.10% 2047 Notes ”), 4.20% Senior Notes due 2048 (the “ 4.20% 2048 Notes ”), 4.400% Senior Notes due 2049 (the “ 4.400% 2049 Notes ”), 4.400% Senior Notes due 2046 (the “ 4.400% 2046 Notes ”), 4.500% Senior Notes due 2044 (the “ 4.500% 2044 Notes ”), 4.625% Senior Notes due 2045 (the “ 4.625% 2045 Notes ”), 4.300% Senior Notes due 2039 (the “ 4.300% 2039 Notes ”), 6.200% Senior Notes due 2040 (the “ 6.200% 2040 Notes ”) and 6.600% Senior Notes due 2046 (the “ 6.600% 2046 Notes ” and, together with the 7.200% 2029 Debentures, the 6.625% 2030 Notes, the 7.950% 2029 Debentures, the 8.450% 2029 Notes, the 8.875% 2030 Notes, the 6.050% 2054 Notes, the 5.550% 2034 Notes, the 4.10% 2047 Notes, the 4.20% 2048 Notes, the 4.400% 2049 Notes, the 4.400% 2046 Notes, the 4.500% 2044 Notes, the 4.625% 2045 Notes, the 4.300% 2039 Notes and the 6.200% 2040 Notes, the “ Subject Notes ”). In connection with the Tender Offers, Occidental is also soliciting consents from the holders of each series of Subject Notes (the “ Consent Solicitations ”) for proposed amendments described in the Offer to Purchase (as defined below) that would, among other things, eliminate certain covenants contained in the respective indenture governing such series of Subject Notes (the “ Proposed Amendments ”). Adoption of the Proposed Amendments with respect to each such series of Subject Notes requires the requisite consent applicable to each such series of Subject Notes as described in the Offer to Purchase. The Tender Offers and Consent Solicitations are made upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase and Consent Solicitation Statement, dated November 18, 2026 (the “ Offer to Purchase ”). The Tender Offers and Consent Solicitations will each expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 17, 2026, unless extended or earlier terminated by Occidental. Holders of Subject Notes that are validly tendered at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on December 2, 2026 and accepted for purchase pursuant to the applicable Tender Offer will receive, in addition to the applicable tender offer consideration, the applicable early tender premium for such series of Subject Notes, in each case subject to the terms and conditions described in the Offer to Purchase. Occidental intends to fund the Tender Offers with cash on hand. The Tender Offers and Consent Solicitations are conditioned upon the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase. Occidental reserves the right to amend, extend, withdraw or terminate any of the Tender Offers and/or Consent Solicitations in its sole discretion, subject to applicable law. A copy of the press release relating to the commencement of the Tender Offers and Consent Solicitations is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference in its entirety.
