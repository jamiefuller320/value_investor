# McDonald's Corporation (MCD) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-26T18:13:29.113598+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
McDonald's is a global quick-service restaurant operator built around a heavily franchised, real-estate-backed model: 45,356 systemwide restaurants at year-end 2025, with franchise rent and royalty streams driving the majority of consolidated revenue. The quantitative screen flags a buy on dividend growth, GARP (PEGY 0.08), earnings quality, and contrarian factors, with P/E 21.8 and a 2.8% yield — a quality-compounder profile rather than a classic deep-value dislocation. The valuation hook is steady mid-single-digit earnings growth and cash generation funding rising dividends and buybacks, set against elevated leverage (~$40bn long-term debt) and negative reported shareholders' equity from decades of repurchases. The central debate is whether recent share weakness and screen cheapness adequately compensate for traffic sensitivity, geographic headwinds (notably Middle East), and a capital structure that screens poorly on book value and several quality models.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, McDonald's offers a franchise cash engine with visible, filing-supported earnings momentum rather than a net-net or asset bargain. The screen passes four metric families — cheapness, dividend, GARP, and risk — with standout metrics including PEGY 0.08, ~6.9% growth, and a dividend that the FY2025 10-K shows rising to $7.17 declared per share (from $6.78 in 2024). Business quality is high by operational standards: franchised-restaurant revenues reached $16.5bn in 2025 (+5.3% YoY per 10-K), operating margin expanded (operating income $12.4bn on $26.9bn revenue), and systemwide unit growth continued (+1,879 net restaurants in 2025).

However, the screen's breadth is narrow — only 5 of 22 models pass. Failures include Graham Defensive/Enterprising, Schloss Low P/B, FCF Yield, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, and Composite Value. Piotroski F-Score is 6/9 (ROA and asset turnover not improving). The negative P/B (-146.3) reflects accounting equity deficit ($1.8bn at 31 Dec 2025), not absence of economic value; the real estate and franchise royalty stream are the assets. The buy case rests on a GARP-plus-dividend overlay: durable franchise economics, comp-sales recovery, and a contrarian entry after recent market weakness — not on balance-sheet liquidation value.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Leverage and capital returns.** Long-term debt of $39.97bn and total debt obligations of ~$40.1bn (FY2025 10-K) against $774m cash. Interest coverage remains comfortable (operating income/interest ≈ 7.8×), but the balance sheet is debt-heavy by value-investor standards. Shareholders' equity remains negative; buybacks ($2.1bn in 2025) and dividends ($5.1bn) together exceed free cash flow.

**Restructuring.** "Accelerating the Organization" incurred $226m charges in 2025; the 10-K expects ~$250m in 2026, with total programme charges of $697m since 2023. Completion anticipated 2027.

**Tax and regulatory.** Gross unrecognized tax benefits $414m (FY2025 10-K). IRS audits open for 2011–2012 and 2016–2021. Filing states liabilities recorded are "appropriate and adequate" but resolution timing is uncertain.

**Geopolitical / cyclical.** Q3 2025 10-Q explicitly cites Middle East war impact on IDL segment sales. U.S. comp growth in recent quarters is driven by check growth; guest-count recovery is less visible in filing extracts.

**Legal contingencies.** FY2025 10-K: "The Company does not believe that any such matter currently being reviewed will have a material adverse effect on its financial condition or results of operations." Ordinary-course litigation across franchise, employment, IP, and regulatory domains.

**Debt covenants.** FY2025 10-K states no provisions accelerate repayment solely on credit-rating change or material adverse business change; certain covenants restrict subsidiary mortgages and debt. $4.0bn revolving credit facility undrawn at 31 Dec 2025.

**Screen-specific flags.** Failed FCF Yield, Economic Moat, and Financial Health models; P/B unusable. These are not immaterial for a verify-before-trade pack.
