(b) Director Resignation On August 24, 2025, Shailesh Jejurikar, a member of the Board of Directors (the “ Board ”) of Otis Worldwide Corporation (the “ Company ”) and Chair of the Compensation Committee, notified the Company that he will resign from the Board effective September 9, 2025. Mr. Jejurikar’s decision to resign from the Board was not the result of any disagreement with the Company on any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies or practices. On August 26, 2025, the Board decreased the size of the Board from eleven (11) directors to ten (10) directors, effective September 9, 2025. New Committee Assignments As a result of Mr. Jejurikar’s resignation, the Board approved changes to the composition of certain of the Board’s committees. Effective immediately, Kathy Hopinkah Hannan rotated off of the Audit Committee and Nominations and Governance Committee, and replaced Mr. Jejurikar as Chair of the Compensation Committee.
