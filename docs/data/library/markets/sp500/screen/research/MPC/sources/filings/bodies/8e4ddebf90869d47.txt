On October 28, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Marathon Petroleum Corporation (the “Company”) increased the size of the Board to eleven members and elected David E. Constable as a director of the Company, with such increase in the size of the Board and election effective as of March 1, 2027. Mr. Constable was appointed to serve on the Board’s Compensation and Organization Development Committee and Sustainability and Public Policy Committee, also effective March 1, 2027. As a director, Mr. Constable will receive the same compensation as the Company’s other directors, as described in the Company’s definitive proxy statement on Schedule 14A, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 16, 2026.
