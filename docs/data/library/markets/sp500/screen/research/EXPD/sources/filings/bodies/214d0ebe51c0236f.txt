( ) today announced third quarter 2026 financial results including the following comparisons to the same quarter of 2025: • Diluted Net Earnings Attributable to Shareholders per share (EPS) increased 62% to $2.65 • Net Earnings Attributable to Shareholders increased 55% to $345 million • Operating Income increased 55% to $445 million • Revenues increased 42% to $4.1 billion • Airfreight tonnage increased 13% and ocean container volume increased 7% • Customs, Transcon, Distribution, and Order Management each achieved revenue growth for a third consecutive quarter • Cash returned to shareholders in the form of share repurchases and dividends was $197 million and $946 million for the third quarter and period of 2026, respectively Daniel R. Wall, President and Chief Executive Officer, commented: “We continue to gain share, particularly in markets that we have made a strategic focus. By continuing to streamline our processes and prioritize companywide growth across every region, product, and district, we once again generated growth across most of our businesses. While we have thrived during periods of significant disruption throughout our history, the strength of demand for our products and services is what really drove Q3 performance. Demand from certain customers, such as the hyperscalers, has been outpacing the market overall, but we have been very deliberate to expand our customer base across our global network and further diversify our revenues. I sincerely thank our global operations, transition & implementation, sales, and account management teams for their continued focus and dedication to our customers. Through those efforts, we are now charting a course for durable performance no matter what the operating environment.” Q3 2026 Operational Highlights Airfreight services : “Airfreight demand remained strong during the quarter, with record global air tonnage increasing approximately 13% , driven primarily by continued growth in South Asia, where export tonnage increased most notably. Hyperscaler demand, which also includes shipments from Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers supporting AI infrastructure, was a significant driver of volume growth, while air capacity remains constrained by limited fleet expansion and ongoing new aircraft delivery delays. Capacity was also impacted by aircraft redeployments that affected e-commerce, stemming from European Union regulations meant to curb the flow of de minimis imports. At the same time, .S. e-commerce tonnage has nearly recovered to what it was prior to the U.S. termination of de minimis shipments in May 2025. Adding geopolitical events to this mix, airfreight rates remained elevated during the quarter, with demand outpacing available capacity.” NOTE: See Disclaimer on Statements in this release. Ocean freight and ocean services : “After the flattening we saw in Q2’26, ocean volumes increased 7% in Q3, with particularly strong comparative growth in September and across Asia export markets. Significant congestion at major Asian ports and transshipment hubs impacted the ocean markets, as capacity was idled and port operations were halted as a result of three typhoons. Nevertheless, demand was consistently healthy and we were able to lean into our longstanding carrier relationships to secure the capacity we needed to support both existing and new customers amidst these service disruptions. The strength of our global network and world class operating teams enabled us to maintain service continuity and respond effectively as conditions deteriorated. We expect congestion and supply chain challenges to persist through the remainder of 2026.” Customs brokerage and other services : “All of the products in our customs brokerage and other services continued to generate growth during the quarter, supported by strong customer demand across transportation management, distribution, and supply chain solutions. Much of the strength in our customs brokerage business was from fees and activity that began in Q3 2025. However, growth during the most recent quarter was driven by significant new business wins, higher declaration volumes, and customs services across all regions, from both new and existing customers. Demand from AI hyperscalers and other technology customers continued to drive growth across a diverse range of geographies and business sectors. Growth during the quarter was particularly strong in our Transcon road freight and warehousing and distribution businesses, with robust demand from large technology customers.” David A. Hackett, Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, added: “We had another exceptional quarter and our pipeline of new business remains strong, showing the durability of our earnings growth. Our revenue grew 42%, while headcount increased only 1% from prior year, demonstrating the scalability of our operating model and driving significant operating leverage. Our operating efficiency increased to 36.7%, well above our historical target of 30%. The growth in operating income of 55% combined with a lower tax rate and a lower share count drove the 62% increase in diluted EPS. “We have made and are continuing to make investments in temperature-controlled facilities, critical logistics services, and productivity initiatives, including investments in artificial intelligence and other technology solutions. These investments, coupled with our strong operational performance, are driving revenue growth and improvements in productivity as we seek to decouple headcount growth from revenue growth over the long term. We will continue making investments, including additional investments in artificial intelligence and other technology solutions to further increase our net operating margins over the long term.” Mr. Hackett noted that the Company returned $197 million to shareholders through share repurchases during the quarter and $946 million in dividends and share repurchases for the first nine months of 2026. 2026 Investor Day As previously announced, Expeditors plans to hold an Investor Day for its shareholders and analysts on the morning of Wednesday, November 18, 2026, in New York City.
