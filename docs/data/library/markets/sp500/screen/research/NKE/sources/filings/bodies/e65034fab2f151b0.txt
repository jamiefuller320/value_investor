On July 17, 2025, the Board of Directors of NIKE, Inc. (the “Company”) adopted an amendment and restatement of the NIKE, Inc. Stock Incentive Plan (as amended and restated, the “Plan”), subject to shareholder approval at the Company’s annual meeting of shareholders (the “Annual Meeting”). At the Annual Meeting, held on September 9, 2025, the Company’s shareholders approved the Plan to, among other changes, increase the number of shares of the Company’s Class B Common Stock authorized for issuance under the Plan by 45,000,000 shares. A description of the Plan was set forth in the Company’s definitive proxy statement on Form 14A filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on July 17, 2025 (the “Proxy Statement”) in the section titled “Summary of the Stock Incentive Plan.” The descriptions of the Plan contained herein and in the Proxy Statement are qualified in their entirety by reference to the Plan, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
