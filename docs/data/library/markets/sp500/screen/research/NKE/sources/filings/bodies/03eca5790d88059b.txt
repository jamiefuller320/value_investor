Announces Full Stride Ahead Strategy and 2031 Financial Framework at Investor Day • Introduces Full Stride Ahead, NIKE's strategy to get closer to athletes* and deliver sustainable, profitable growth • Reaffirms fiscal 2027 financial guidance • Establishes 2031 financial framework targeting approximately $5 billion in incremental revenue growth, with fiscal 2027 as the base, and operating margins** of 12-14% and adjusted EPS (1) of $3.00 to $3.50 by the end of fiscal 2031 • Expects approximately $12 billion of cumulative capital returns through dividends to shareholders from fiscal 2027 through fiscal 2031 • Unveils next generation of innovation across Performance Sports and Sportswear BEAVERTON, Ore., November 17, 2026 — NIKE, Inc. (NYSE: NKE) today will host its 2026 Investor Day at the Philip H. Knight Campus in Beaverton, Oregon, at 8:30 a.m. PT. The Company will introduce Full Stride Ahead, its strategy to get closer to athletes and focus resources on the greatest opportunities for growth. NIKE will also outline its 2031 financial framework. "NIKE is at its best when we start with the athlete and bring the full power of the Company together around sport," said Elliott Hill, President and Chief Executive Officer of NIKE, Inc. Over the last two years, our Win Now actions have strengthened our foundation. Full Stride Ahead builds on that progress — bringing greater focus, speed and discipline to how we serve athletes and compete around the world." Mr. Hill continued, "We're concentrating our resources where we have the greatest potential to win. We're also changing how we operate — delivering breakthrough products, building deeper local connections and moving faster to capture demand. Together, these actions are designed to return NIKE to sustainable, profitable growth." Full Stride Ahead: Getting Closer to Athletes and Driving Sustainable, Profitable Growth NIKE, Inc. has a portfolio of three distinct brands — Nike, Jordan and Converse. Each has a unique role to play, amplified by the shared capabilities and global scale of NIKE, Inc. Within this portfolio, NIKE, Inc. will focus on the Performance Sports and Sportswear opportunities with the greatest potential for growth. This includes five priority Performance Sports — Running, Training, Global Football, Basketball and Outdoor — and Sportswear, which encompasses Nike Sportswear, Jordan and Converse. Together with resources focused on 23 priority countries, these dimensions are expected to drive more than 90% of NIKE, Inc.'s revenue growth through fiscal 2031. To capture these growth opportunities and create shareholder value, NIKE, Inc. is advancing three connected strategic pillars: EPIC – Create Athlete Demand EPIC is designed to create demand through breakthrough innovation, distinctive products and compelling storytelling and experiences that inspire athletes. The Company will: • Accelerate the Sport Offense across priority Performance Sports • Scale breakthrough innovations into platforms that reach more athletes across more sports • Bring greater newness, distinction and cultural relevance to Nike Sportswear • Restore Jordan's premium positioning and marketplace health • Sharpen Converse's lifestyle focus and strengthen its connection to creative culture CONNECTED – Deepen Local Relevance CONNECTED is designed to deepen relationships with local athletes and communities, translating insights into more relevant products, stories and experiences, online and in stores. The Company will: • Move authority and resources closer to priority countries and cities • Build deeper relationships with athletes and communities on the ground • Bring our brands to life through locally relevant storytelling and experiences • Work with our marketplace partners to tailor product assortments and experiences to local athletes • Continue the reset in Greater China and accelerate growth across other priority markets FAST – Capture Demand Faster and Operate More Efficiently FAST is designed to capture more of the demand it creates by responding faster and operating more efficiently. The Company will: • Connect demand, inventory, supply and financial decisions through integrated planning • Build a more flexible and responsive supply network • Simplify and standardize processes to improve execution and productivity • Accelerate product creation to bring new innovations to athletes faster • Use data, technology and AI to improve , speed and efficiency Fiscal 2027 Outlook NIKE, Inc. is reaffirming its guidance for fiscal 2027. The Company continues to expect fiscal 2027 revenue to decline in the digit range and operating income** to decline faster than revenue, resulting in an adjusted operating margin (2) of approximately 6% and adjusted EPS of $1.15 to $1.35, which excludes approximately $0.15 per share of restructuring expenses related to Pace*** for fiscal 2027. The Company expects to return approximately $2.4 billion to shareholders through dividends in fiscal 2027. 2031 Financial Framework NIKE's 2031 financial framework establishes a path to sustainable, profitable growth. Using fiscal 2027 as the base, by fiscal 2031, the Company is targeting approximately $5 billion in incremental revenue growth. Performance Sports are expected to be the primary engine of growth, contributing approximately $4 billion of the incremental $5 billion of revenue growth, with Sportswear contributing approximately $1 billion. Fiscal 2027 and fiscal 2028 represent a period of investment toward growth, focused on strengthening the underlying quality of the business. Revenue declines are expected to moderate in fiscal 2028, with NIKE targeting a return to revenue growth in fiscal 2029. In fiscal 2030 and fiscal 2031, the Company expects revenue growth to accelerate and exceed market growth as its strategic investments gain traction, revenue quality improves and previously announced productivity initiatives contribute more fully to financial performance. Pace is expected to drive approximately $2.5 billion of annualized savings by fiscal 2031 with benefits beginning in fiscal 2027 and ramping up in fiscal 2029 and fiscal 2030. NIKE expects to incur approximately $1.3 billion in charges associated with implementing Pace, which includes $0.3 billion recognized in fiscal 2026 and $0.3 billion expected in fiscal 2027 for costs. revenue, gross margin expansion and productivity improvements are expected to drive operating margins of 12-14% by the end of fiscal 2031. Together, these drivers are expected to deliver adjusted EPS of $3.00 to $3.50 by the end of fiscal 2031. Including fiscal 2027, the Company plans to return approximately $12 billion of cumulative capital to shareholders through dividends through the end of fiscal 2031, reinforcing its commitment to delivering shareholder returns while investing to restore sustainable growth. "NIKE's extraordinary assets – the strength of its brands, deep athlete relationships, innovation capabilities, and global reach, are unrivaled," said Dave Denton, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of NIKE, Inc. "Our current financial performance does not yet reflect the full potential of those assets. We have clear opportunities to improve the quality of our revenue, rebuild gross margin, and instill greater discipline across our cost base, while continuing to invest for growth." Mr. Denton continued, "As we move forward, we are holding ourselves to a higher standard of discipline and execution across the business, with clear financial milestones to measure our progress. Our objective is to restore NIKE's earnings power, invest with purpose and translate those investments into sustainable, profitable growth and shareholder value."
