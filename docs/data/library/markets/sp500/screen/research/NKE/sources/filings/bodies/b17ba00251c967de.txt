Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers On November 9, 2026, NIKE, Inc. (the “Company”) announced the appointment of Dan McKenzie as Vice President, Chief Accounting Officer, Finance Operations & Transformation, and the Company’s principal accounting officer, effective as of November 9, 2026 (the “Effective Date”). There are no arrangements or understandings between Mr. McKenzie and any person pursuant to which Mr. McKenzie was selected as an officer, and no family relationships exist between Mr. McKenzie and any director or executive officer of the Company. Mr. McKenzie is not a party to any transaction to which the Company is or was a participant and in which Mr. McKenzie has a direct or indirect material interest subject to disclosure under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K. On November 9, 2026, the Company also announced that, as of the Effective Date, David Denton, the Company’s Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, will cease serving as Interim Corporate Controller and principal accounting officer. Biographical Information Mr. McKenzie, 55, retired from the Company in July 2026 and has not held another position since his retirement. Prior to his retirement, Mr. McKenzie served more than 19 years at the Company holding various leadership positions, most recently serving as the Company’s VP, Chief Financial Officer, Global Operations and Technology from August 2025 to July 2026, and previously as VP, Chief Financial Officer, North America from July 2023 to July 2025, and VP, Chief Financial Officer, EMEA from November 2019 to June 2023. Mr. McKenzie has more than 32 years of finance and operational expertise and holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Accounting from the University of Oregon. Offer Letter with Mr. McKenzie On November 4, 2026, the Company entered into an offer letter with Mr. McKenzie (the “Offer Letter”) establishing his compensation as Vice President, Chief Accounting Officer, Finance Operations & Transformation. Under the Offer Letter, Mr. McKenzie’s compensation as Vice President, Chief Accounting Officer, Finance Operations & Transformation, will comprise an annual base salary of $500,000; a target annual bonus of 50% of eligible fiscal year earnings; and an annual target incentive award of $525,000 (the “Annual LTI Target Amount”). Mr. McKenzie will receive certain equity awards in connection with his appointment, subject to the terms of the form of restricted stock unit (“RSU”) agreement approved by the Compensation Committee of the Company’s Board of Directors and the NIKE, Inc. Stock Incentive Plan, as it may be amended from time to time (the “Incentive Plan”), in each case, to be granted as soon as administratively feasible after commencement of his employment, as follows: • A RSU with a target grant value equal to the Annual LTI Target Amount, which will vest in four equal annual installments, with the first tranche vesting on September 1, 2027, consistent with the Company’s September 1, 2026 annual equity grants to executive officers. • A RSU award with a target grant value equal to $600,000, which will vest in three equal annual installments on the anniversary of the date of grant. Mr. McKenzie will generally be eligible to participate in the Company’s corporate benefit plans and programs, as may be in effect from time to time. As part of standard compensation and benefit arrangements for similarly situated Vice Presidents, the Company and Mr. McKenzie have entered into an agreement containing a covenant not to compete (the “McKenzie RCA”) that will extend for twelve months following the termination of his employment with the Company. The McKenzie RCA provides that, if Mr. McKenzie’s employment is terminated by the Company without cause (as defined in the McKenzie RCA) at any time, the Company will make monthly payments to him during the noncompetition period in an amount equal to of his current annual base salary. The McKenzie RCA provides further that, if Mr. McKenzie voluntarily resigns at any time, the Company will make monthly payments to him during the noncompetition period in an amount equal to fourth of his current annual base salary. The Company may unilaterally waive the covenant. If the covenant is waived, the Company will not be required to make the payments described above for the months as to which the waiver applies.
