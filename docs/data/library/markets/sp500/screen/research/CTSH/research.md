# Cognizant Technology Solutions Corporation (CTSH) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-09-10T07:37:21.084728+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Cognizant is a global IT services and consulting franchise repositioning around AI-led modernisation across four industry segments (Health Sciences, Financial Services, Products & Resources, Communications, Media & Technology). The quantitative screen rates the name a strong buy; filing evidence supports a value case built on re-accelerating revenue, modest margin expansion, and substantial free cash flow returned via dividends and an enlarged buyback programme. The stock trades at a depressed multiple relative to earnings and cash generation (news commentary cites roughly 10× earnings), reflecting investor scepticism that legacy outsourcing can defend share against AI-native competitors and hyperscaler-led disintermediation. The central debate is whether Cognizant’s AI pivot, Belcan/3Cloud-style bolt-ons, and Project Leap productivity programme can sustain mid-single-digit organic growth and stable margins, or whether cyclical IT spend cuts and commoditisation cap returns despite attractive headline valuation.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, CTSH offers a cash-rich, moderately levered compounder at a cyclical trough in sentiment rather than in fundamentals. Revenue has inflected: first-half 2026 revenues were $10.9bn versus $10.4bn in H1 2025 (+5.1% per Q2 2026 10-Q), with Q2 2026 revenue of $5.48bn (+4.5% reported, +4.1% constant currency) and adjusted operating margin up 40bps year on year. FY2025 revenue reached $21.1bn and diluted EPS $4.56 (Yahoo fallback — 10-K body lacks readable consolidated income statement); net income has been broadly flat at ~$2.2–2.3bn since 2022 while the share count has fallen through repurchases.

Cash conversion is the screen’s natural anchor: FY2025 operating cash flow was $2.88bn and free cash flow $2.60bn (Yahoo fallback), with TTM FCF of ~$2.60bn per cached quarterly data. Management raised the 2026 repurchase target to $2.0bn (8-K/news, 18 May 2026) and completed a $500m accelerated share repurchase in Q2 2026 (9.7m shares at $51.54 per Q2 2026 10-Q). Dividends (~$610m in FY2025) remain well covered by FCF.

Business quality is adequate rather than exceptional: Cognizant competes with Accenture, Indian peers, and client GCCs in “highly competitive” markets (FY2025 10-K), but retains sticky enterprise relationships, diversified segment exposure, and a deepening AI partner ecosystem (Google, OpenAI, hyperscalers — news flow). ## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Regulatory / tax (India):** Q2 2026 10-Q and FY2025 10-K disclose ongoing disputes with the Indian Income Tax Department regarding CTS India share repurchase transactions in 2013 ($523m) and 2016 ($2.8bn). Q3 2025’s $613m tax provision illustrates earnings volatility from tax audits and interpretations. Labour Code reforms effective November 2025 add India employment-cost uncertainty.

**Competitive / AI:** FY2025 10-K forward-looking statements flag intense competition, the need to keep pace with technological advances, and risk that AI may reduce demand or pricing power. Competitors include Accenture, Indian majors, GCCs, and boutique AI firms.

**Customer concentration:** FY2025 10-K states that loss of a significant client or few clients in a segment could materially reduce segment revenues; work for large clients is critical to their operations but can still vary year to year.

**Cyclical exposure:** Macro/geopolitical conditions and client spending reductions are explicit 10-K risk factors; IT services revenue remains tied to enterprise discretionary budgets.

**M&A / goodwill:** Goodwill rose to $8.1bn (39% of total assets at mid-2026); 3Cloud ($728m) and other H1 2026 purchases (~$604m in Q2 per Yahoo quarterly cash flow) increase integration and impairment risk.

**Restructuring:** Project Leap (initiated Q2 2026) targets operating-model transformation; $84m charges booked with $30–50m of additional charges anticipated — margin benefit unproven.

**Litigation:** 10-K references Syntel litigation, Title VII claims, and general legal proceedings; no going-concern language identified. Controls: Item 4 referenced in Q2 10-Q table of contents; detailed attestation text not present in truncated extract — cannot confirm beyond 10-K cover-page SOX 404(b) attestation flag.

**Liquidity:** Cash has fallen sharply while revolver is fully drawn ($1.0bn); covenant compliance affirmed but leverage and acquisition pace warrant monitoring.

No material weakness identified in available extracts.
