Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities. On November 13, 2026, Diamondback Energy, Inc. (the “Company”) and Diamondback E&P LLC (the “Purchaser”), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, entered into a definitive securities purchase agreement (the “Purchase Agreement”) with Double Eagle IV Midco, LLC (the “Spanish Peak Seller”) and Double Eagle V Midco, LLC (the “DEV Seller” and, together with the Spanish Peak Seller, the “Sellers”), pursuant to which the Purchaser will acquire (i) from the Spanish Peak Seller, all of the issued and outstanding interests of Spanish Peak Energy, LLC, a Texas limited liability company, and (ii) from the DEV Seller, all of the issued and outstanding interests of Lone Peak Energy, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, and DE West Texas, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “Pending Acquisition”). Consideration for the Pending Acquisition consists of approximately $2.15 billion in cash and approximately 15.56 million shares of the Company’s common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “Shares”), subject to customary adjustments. The cash portion of this transaction is expected to be funded through a combination of cash on hand, borrowings under the Company’s credit facility and/or proceeds from term loans. The Shares will be issued in reliance upon the exemption from the registration requirements of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), provided by Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act as sales by an issuer not involving any public offering. The Pending Acquisition is expected to close on December 21, 2026, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions, including the expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended.
