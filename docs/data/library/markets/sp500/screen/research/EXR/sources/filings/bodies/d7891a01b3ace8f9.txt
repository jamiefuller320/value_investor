Result of AGM Engage XR Holdings PLC 26 June 2025 26 June 2025 Engage XR Holdings plc ("ENGAGE XR", the "Company", or the "Group") Result of Annual General Meeting The Annual General Meeting of Engage XR Holdings plc was held at Unit 9, Cleaboy Business Park, Old Kilmeaden Road, Waterford, X91 AX83 on 26 June 2025 at 11:00 a.m. All 13 resolutions put to members were passed on a poll. Resolutions 1 to 12 were passed as ordinary resolutions and resolutions 13 were passed as special resolutions.
