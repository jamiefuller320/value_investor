Result of AGM Engage XR Holdings PLC 27 June 2024 27 June 2024 ENGAGE XR Holdings Plc ("ENGAGE XR", the "Company", or the "Group") Result of Annual General Meeting The Annual General Meeting of Engage XR Holdings plc was held at Unit 9, Cleaboy Business Park, Old Kilmeaden Road, Waterford, X91 AX83, Ireland on 27 June 2024 at 11:00 a.m. All 11 resolutions put to members were passed on a poll.
