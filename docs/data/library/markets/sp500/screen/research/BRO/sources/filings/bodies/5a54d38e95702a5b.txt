( ) (the "Company") announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter of 2026. For the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company achieved: • Total revenues of $1.7 billion, increasing $119 million, or 7.4%, compared to the third quarter of the prior year, with growth of Organic Revenue of 1.2% and growth of Organic Revenue with Contingents of 1.5%. • Income before income taxes of $320 million, increasing 2.9%, with Income Before Income Taxes Margin of 18.6%, compared to 19.4% in the third quarter of the prior year. • EBITDAC - Adjusted of $597 million, increasing 1.7%, with EBITDAC Margin - Adjusted of 34.6%, compared to 36.6% in the third quarter of the prior year. • Net income attributable to the Company of $240 million, increasing $13 million, or 5.7%, compared to the third quarter of the prior year. • Diluted net income per share of $0.67, a decrease of 1.5%, with Diluted Net Income Per Share - Adjusted increasing to $1.07, or 1.9%, each compared to the third quarter of the prior year. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company achieved: • Total revenues of $5.3 billion, increasing $1.0 billion, or 23.4%, compared to the same period in 2025, with growth of Organic Revenue of 0.3% and growth of Organic Revenue with Contingents of 1.5%. • Income before income taxes of $1.2 billion, increasing 17.6%, with Income Before Income Taxes Margin of 23.3%, compared to 24.4% in the same period in 2025. • EBITDAC - Adjusted of $1.9 billion, increasing 20.9%, with EBITDAC Margin - Adjusted of 36.3%, compared to 37.1% in the same period in 2025. • Net income attributable to the Company of $954 million, increasing $164 million, or 20.8%, compared to the same period in 2025. • Diluted net income per share of $2.57, flat compared to the same period in 2025, with Diluted Net Income Per Share - Adjusted increasing to $3.52, or 5.7%, compared to the same period in 2025. We present these measures because we believe such information is of interest to the investment community and because we believe they provide additional meaningful methods to evaluate the Company’s operating performance from period to period on a basis that may not be otherwise apparent on a GAAP basis due to the impact of certain items that have a high degree of variability, that we believe are not indicative of ongoing performance and that are not easily comparable from period to period. This financial information should be considered in addition to, not in lieu of, GAAP information as of the relevant date. Consistent with Regulation G, a description of such information is provided below, and tabular reconciliations of such items to our most directly comparable GAAP information can be found within this press release as well as in our periodic filings with the SEC. We view Organic Revenue and Organic Revenue growth (including Organic Revenue with Contingents and its growth) as important indicators when assessing and evaluating our performance on a consolidated basis and for each of our two segments, because they allow us to determine a comparable, but , measurement of revenue growth that is associated with the revenue sources that were a part of our business in both the current and prior year and that are expected to continue in the future. In addition, we believe Diluted Net Income Per Share - Adjusted provides a meaningful representation of our operating performance and improves the comparability of our results between periods by excluding the impact of the change in estimated acquisition payables, the impact of amortization of intangible assets and certain other or infrequently occurring items. We also view EBITDAC, EBITDAC - Adjusted, EBITDAC Margin and EBITDAC Margin - Adjusted as important indicators when assessing and evaluating our performance, as they present more comparable measurements of our operating margins in a meaningful and consistent manner. As disclosed in our most recent proxy statement, we use Organic Revenue growth, Diluted Net Income Per Share - Adjusted and EBITDAC Margin - Adjusted as key performance metrics for our and incentive compensation plans for executive officers and other key employees. Revenue Measures • Organic Revenue is our core commissions and fees less: (i) the core commissions and fees earned for the first twelve months by newly acquired operations; (ii) divested business (core commissions and fees generated from offices, books of business or niches sold or terminated during the comparable period); (iii) Foreign Currency Translation (as defined below) and (iv) the Impact. The term “core commissions and fees” excludes contingent commissions (“Contingents”); and therefore, represents the revenues earned directly from specific insurance policies sold and specific services rendered. Growth of Organic Revenue can be expressed as a dollar amount or a percentage rate. • Organic Revenue with Contingents is Organic Revenue plus Organic Contingents (as defined below). Growth of Organic Revenue with Contingents can be expressed as a dollar amount or a percentage rate. Earnings Measures 8 • EBITDAC is defined as income before interest, income taxes, depreciation, amortization and the change in estimated acquisition payables. • EBITDAC Margin is defined as EBITDAC divided by total revenues. • EBITDAC - Adjusted is defined as EBITDAC, excluding (i) (gain)/loss on disposal (as defined below), (ii) Acquisition/Integration Costs (as defined below) and (iii) of escrow liability (as defined below). • EBITDAC Margin - Adjusted is defined as EBITDAC - Adjusted divided by total revenues. • Diluted Net Income Per Share - Adjusted is defined as diluted net income per share, excluding the impact of (i) the change in estimated acquisition payables, (ii) (gain)/loss on disposal, (as defined below), (iii) Acquisition/Integration Costs (as defined below), (iv) of escrow liability (as defined below) in periods wherein the effect of of escrow liability is not dilutive to the Company's earnings and, therefore, not already excluded from the calculation of diluted net income per share in accordance with ASC 260, and (v) amortization. Definitions Related to Certain Components of Measures • “Acquisition/Integration Costs” means the acquisition and integration costs (e.g., costs associated with regulatory filings; costs for professional services, including legal, accounting, consulting, financial advisory and due diligence; costs and fees associated with entry into the bridge financing commitment; costs of integrating or streamlining processes and information technology systems, including data migration and system integration; costs associated with optimizing vendor agreements and leased office space, including exit costs related to location combinations; and costs, including severance payments, costs associated with the transition of certain legacy compensation programs, compensation expenses, and incentive payments) arising out of our acquisition of Accession and acquisitions previously completed by Accession, which are not considered to be normal, recurring or part of ongoing operations. • “Foreign Currency Translation” means the impact of foreign currency translation, which is calculated by applying foreign exchange rates to the various functional currencies in our business to our reporting currency of US dollars for the same period in the prior year. • “(Gain)/loss on disposal” is a caption on our consolidated statements of income which reflects net proceeds received as compared to the net book value related to sales of books of business and other divestiture transactions. • “ of escrow liability ” is a caption on our consolidated statements of income which reflects the change in the fair value associated with certain shares of the Company’s common stock held in escrow. The change is driven by fluctuations in our stock price between the beginning of the period and the end of the period. These escrowed shares represent a portion of the merger consideration payable in connection with our acquisition of Accession. The escrowed shares secure certain indemnification obligations of the Accession equity holders related to businesses that are in or discontinued. • “ Impact” means the core commissions and fees attributable to (i) the loss of specifically identified customer accounts and (ii) new business generated in the prior year by certain former employees, in each case in connection with the conduct of a competitor that is the subject of pending litigation in multiple jurisdictions. • “Organic Contingents” are Contingents, less (i) Contingents earned for the first twelve months by newly acquired operations and (ii) Contingents earned from divested operations (Contingents generated from operations sold or terminated during the comparable period).
