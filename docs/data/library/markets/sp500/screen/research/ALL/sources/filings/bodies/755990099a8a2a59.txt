September Quarter Activities 
During the period, the Comp any continued to advance its ﬂ agship project, the Ewoyaa Lithium Project, towards 
production to become Ghana’s ﬁrst operating lithium mine, while progressing the conditions precedent, as deﬁned in 
the Scheme Implementation Deed (“SID”), in support of the proposed acquisition of the Company.  
Ewoyaa, located in the pro-mining jurisdiction of Ghana, West Africa, approximately 100km southwest of the capital 
of Accra, comprises eight main deposits, including Ewoyaa, Okwesikrom, Anokyi, Grasscutter, Abonko, Kaampakrom, 
Sill and Bypass. The Project is well located to operational infrastructure, including being within 1km of the Takoradi – 
Accra N1 highway, 110km from the Takoradi deep-sea port and adjacent to grid power (refer Figure 1). 
 
Figure 1: Location of the Ewoyaa Lithium Project, Ghana  
 
Concurrent to its activities at Ewoyaa, the Company continues to undertake low-cost exploration across the contiguous 
Agboville and Rubino exploration licences, which are 100% owned through its wholly-owned Ivorian subsidiary Khaleesi 
Resources SARL, in the mining-friendly jurisdiction of Côte d'Ivoire in West Africa. 
The Agboville and Rubino licences, which cover 396.89 km² and 374.18 km² respectively, provide the Company with 
exclusive rights to conduct lithium exploration over highly prospective tenure for lithium discovery. 
Leveraging synergies with its existing operations in Ghana, the Company is applying its proven track record of lithium 
exploration, discovery and evaluation in tropical weathering environments, as demonstrated at Ewoyaa, to its 
exploration portfolio in Côte d’Ivoire.

Scheme Implementation Deed  
Scheme Overview 
On 7 May 2026, the Company announced that it had entered into a SID with major new energy materials company 
Zhejiang Huayou Cobalt Co., Limited (“Huayou”), under which it is proposed that Huayou will acquire all of the issued 
shares in Atlantic Lithium by way of an Australian scheme of arrangement for cash consideration of US$0.25486 per 
share (A$0.354 per share / £0.188 per share)1 (“Scheme Consideration”) (the “Scheme” or “Transaction”). 
The Scheme Consideration values the Company at approximately US$210 million (approximately A$292 million and 
£155 million) and represents a 26.6% premium to Atlantic Lithium’s closing price of A$0.280 per share on 6 May 2026 
and a 21.8% premium to Atlantic Lithium’s 30-day VWAP of A$0.291 (£0.154) per share up to and including 6 May 2026. 
The Company’s Board entered into the Transaction, in consultation with its advisers, after carefully assessing the 
valuation, funding, timing and execution certainty of the Transaction against other strategic options available to the 
Company. After considering the development risks, risk of raising project ﬁnance, joint venture arrangements, future 
shareholder dilution, and timeline associated with the development of the Project, the Board concluded that the 
Transaction represents the most attractive, certain, and accelerated realisation of value on a risk-adjusted basis versus 
other strategic alternatives and, therefore, the best outcome for Atlantic Lithium shareholders. 
FIRB Approval 
During the period, the Company announced that the Australian Treasury had provided written notiﬁcation to Huayou 
under section 75 of the Foreign Acquisitions and Takeovers Act 1975  (Cth) that the Commonwealth Government of 
Australia has no objection to the Scheme. 
Accordingly, the condition precedent under clause 3.1(a) of the SID relating to the approval by the Foreign Investment 
Review Board (“FIRB Approval”) has been satisﬁed. 
Scheme Conditions 
The Scheme remains subject to the satisfaction or waiver of a number of other conditions precedent, including other 
regulatory approvals, approval by the requisite majority of Atlantic Lithium shareholders at the Scheme Meeting and 
approval by the Supreme Court of Western Australia.  
The Company continues to work with Huayou to advance the various conditions precedent. 
Further detail in relation to the conditions precedent will be set out in the Scheme Booklet. 
Recommendation of the Atlantic Lithium Board 
The Atlantic Lithium Board continues to unanimously recommend that shareholders vote in favour of the Scheme, in 
the absence of a superior proposal and subject to an independent expert concluding (and continuing to conclude) that 
the Scheme is in the best interests of Atlantic Lithium shareholders. Subject to those same qualiﬁcations, each member 
of the Atlantic Lithium Board intends to vote all Atlantic Lithium shares held or controlled by them in favour of the 
Scheme.

Novation Agreement 
Subsequent to the announcement of the SID, the Company announced that it had provided its consent under an 
agreement for Elevra Lithium Limited (“Elevra”), formerly Piedmont Lithium Inc, to sell all of its rights and interests in 
respect of the agreements related to Ewoyaa (“Project Agreement”) to Huayou (“Novation Agreement”). 
Post-period end, the Company announced that it had been advised by Huayou that it had received confirmation from 
Ghana’s Ministry of Lands and Natural Resources that it had no objection to Huayou becoming a controller of the 
relevant Singaporean and Ghanaian entities connected with the Company’s portfolio of lithium projects in Ghana 
(“Ghana Portfolio”). 
Following the completion of the Novation Agreement, Huayou now holds the rights to a 22.5% interest in the Ghana 
Portfolio, which includes the Project, and assumes Elevra’s prior spodumene concentrate offtake rights  and other 
rights and obligations under the Project Agreement. 
As a result of the completion, Huayou has agreed that the development costs conditions precedent under the Project 
Agreement are deemed to be satisfied or otherwise waived. Accordingly, subject to and pursuant to  the Project 
Agreement and relevant supplementary agreements, Huayou will begin sole funding the Project’s development costs 
up to the remainder of the US$70 million sole-funding obligations under Stage 3 of  the Project Agreement (being 
US$66 million), through which Huayou will earn a further 27.5% in the Ghana Portfolio  (to take its total interest to 
50%), with all costs shared equally between the Company and Huayou thereafter. 
The Company warmly welcomes Huayou as its new joint venture partner and looks forward to working collaboratively 
to advance the Project towards development. With Huayou’s extensive technical expertise, financial capabilities and 
established track record in  developing and operating mining projects across Africa, the Company believes that the 
partnership represents a significant step forward in unlocking the Project’s considerable potential.

Sustainability 
Women's Health and Well-being Programme 
In collaboration with the Mfantseman Municipal Health Directorate and the Commission on Human Rights and 
Administrative Justice, the Company delivered an engagement programme focused on women’s health and well-being 
across communities in the catchment area of the Project. 
The sessions reached over 800 participants, predominantly comprising women and girls aged 11 and above, alongside 
a number of men who participated to strengthen their awareness and understanding of women’s health and related 
issues. The programme covered reproductive health, menstrual and physical hygiene, gender -based viol ence and 
access to support, with the aim of addressing misconceptions, raising awareness of rights and encouraging positive 
behavioural change.  Approximately 1,600 sanitary packs were distributed to women and girls as part of the 
programme.
