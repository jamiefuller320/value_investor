Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. On November 13, 2026, Western Digital Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“Western Digital”), entered into a credit agreement (“Credit Agreement”) among Western Digital, Bank of America, N.A., as administrative agent (the “Agent”), and the lenders party thereto. The Credit Agreement provides for a new senior unsecured 5-year $1.5 billion revolving credit facility maturing in 2031 (the “Revolving Facility”), including a $200 million sublimit for letters of credit. In connection with the effectiveness of the Credit Agreement, Western Digital’s existing Amended and Restated Loan Agreement, dated as of January 7, 2022, as amended, was terminated, and all loans and other amounts outstanding thereunder were paid. The obligations under the Credit Agreement will be senior unsecured obligations and will not benefit from any collateral or subsidiary guarantees. Subject to the terms and conditions of the Credit Agreement, subsidiaries of Western Digital may become additional borrowers. Loans under the Revolving Facility bear interest at a per annum rate, at the applicable borrower’s option, equal to either (x) the Term SOFR Rate (as defined in the Credit Agreement) plus an applicable margin varying from 0.875% to 1.500% or (y) a base rate plus an applicable margin varying from 0.000% to 0.500%, in each case based on the corporate family ratings of Western Digital from at least two of Standard & Poor’s Financial Services LLC (“S&P”), Moody’s Investors Service, Inc. (“Moody’s”) and Fitch, Inc. (“Fitch”), with an initial interest rate equal to the Term SOFR Rate plus 1.250% or the base rate plus 0.250%, as applicable. Western Digital will also pay an unused commitment fee on the Revolving Facility ranging from 0.080% to 0.250% per annum based on Western Digital’s corporate family ratings, with an initial unused commitment fee of 0.150% per annum. The Revolving Facility will be subject to customary covenants, including a maximum leverage ratio of 3.25 to 1.00 (the “Leverage Ratio”). The Leverage Ratio is calculated net of certain unrestricted cash and may be increased to 3.75 to 1.00 for four consecutive fiscal quarters following the consummation of a material acquisition. The foregoing description is only a summary of certain provisions of the Credit Agreement and is qualified in its entirety by the terms of the Credit Agreement, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 hereto and is hereby incorporated by reference into this Item 1.01.
