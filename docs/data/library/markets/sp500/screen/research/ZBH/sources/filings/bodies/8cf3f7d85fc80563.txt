On September 8, 2026, Zimmer Biomet Holdings, Inc. (the “Company”) notified Kevin Thornal, Group President, Global Businesses and the Americas, that his position is being restructured. Mr. Thornal will be departing from the Company, effective on September 30, 2026. Mr. Thornal will be eligible for severance benefits under the Company’s Restated Executive Severance Plan, as amended (the “Severance Plan”). Pursuant to the Severance Plan, Mr. Thornal will receive benefits, including a lump sum amount equal to the sum of twelve months of his annual base salary plus target annual bonus, plus twelve months of his current monthly COBRA premiums. His right to receive such severance payments and benefits is conditioned upon his executing and not revoking a general release in favor of the Company. A summary of the Severance Plan is included in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed on April 1, 2026 under “Executive Compensation – Potential Payments upon Termination of Employment – Executive Severance Plan.” The Severance Plan was filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q filed on August 6, 2018, and the Amendment to the Severance Plan was filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q filed on May 5, 2022.
