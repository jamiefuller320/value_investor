("Textron") (NYSE: TXT) announced today that it has entered into a definitive agreement to sell its Industrial segment ("Industrial"), comprising the Kautex and Textron Specialized Vehicles businesses, to an affiliate of Kingswood Capital Management for $1.2 billion. Net cash proceeds to Textron are expected to be approximately $1.1 billion after tax and transaction-related expenses. Upon closing, New Textron will be a aerospace and defense company aligned to its core businesses of Textron Aviation, Bell and Textron Systems. "Today’s agreement is a defining milestone which will establish Textron as a premier, aerospace and defense company," said Lisa M. Atherton, Chief Executive Officer of Textron. "The sale of Industrial allows us to sharpen our strategic focus on growing our core A&D businesses while also returning capital to our shareholders. I would like to thank all Industrial employees for their dedication and contributions over the years. Both Kautex and Textron Specialized Vehicles are leaders in their respective markets with iconic brands and talented teams.” New Textron: A Premier Aerospace & Defense Company New Textron, with over $12 billion in expected 2026 revenues and $19 billion in backlog, will be a premier aerospace and defense company with engineering capabilities, a long track record of innovation and proven manufacturing and support operations. New Textron will continue to be a leader in general aviation under the Cessna and Beechcraft brands, military and commercial rotorcraft under the Bell brand and Textron Systems' differentiated suite of aerospace and defense products and services. The company remains committed to maintaining a strong balance sheet and consistent capital allocation priorities, including investments in research and development and capital expenditures, as well as funding additional share repurchases in 2027 from the proceeds of the sale. Transaction Details Included in the sale are all the Industrial businesses and brands – Kautex, Pentatonic, Textron Specialized Vehicles, E-Z-GO, PACE Technologies, TUG Technologies, Douglas Equipment, Premier, Safeaero, Ransomes, Jacobsen and Cushman. The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to the receipt of required regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions. As a result of our Board of Directors’ approval of the sale and entry into the definitive agreement with an affiliate of Kingswood Capital Management, the Industrial segment will meet the criteria for classification as held for sale and will be presented as discontinued operations beginning in the fourth quarter of 2026. Accordingly, the segment’s historical results will be presented as discontinued operations beginning with Textron’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ending January 2, 2027. November 18, 2026 Share Repurchase Plan Textron’s Board of Directors has also authorized the repurchase of up to 35 million shares of the company’s common stock which is sufficient for share repurchases related to the Industrial divestiture as well as to continue the company’s practice of repurchasing shares to offset the impact of dilution from compensation and benefit plans, and for opportunistic capital management purposes. The new authorization replaces the previous one approved in February 2026. Advisors Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC is serving as exclusive financial advisor, and Latham & Watkins LLP is serving as legal advisor, to Textron. About Textron Industrial Textron Industrial is comprised of Kautex, a leader in plastic fuel systems, battery enclosures and systems for the automotive industry, and Textron Specialized Vehicles, a manufacturer of specialized vehicles and equipment with globally recognized brands such as E-Z-GO, PACE Technologies, Jacobsen and TUG Technologies. About New Textron New Textron is an aerospace and defense company that leverages its global network of aircraft, defense and finance businesses to provide customers with innovative solutions and services. The company is known around the world for its powerful brands such as Bell, Cessna, Beechcraft and Textron Systems. For more information, visit www.textron.com .
