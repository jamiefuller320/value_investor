## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Fox Corporation is a US news, sports and entertainment group built around Fox News Media, Fox Sports, the Fox broadcast network, Tubi AVOD and 29 owned-and-operated TV stations. The quantitative screen rates FOX as a **buy**, but the snapshot carries no passed models, composite score or key metrics — so the signal is a label only, not a substantiated factor stack.

The investment case rests on premium live/appointment content (NFL, World Cup, cable news leadership), a strong balance sheet (~$4.2bn cash per FY2026 10-K narrative), and active capital return. The central debate is whether streaming pivot (FOX One) and the proposed **~$22bn Roku acquisition** create durable value, or whether cord-cutting, sports-rights inflation and regulatory delay erode the franchise. FY2026 showed revenue growth but weaker reported earnings and a sharp free-cash-flow step-down versus FY2025.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, FOX offers a cash-generative media franchise trading with a dual-class structure (Class B FOX / Class A FOXA) and a history of buybacks and dividends. FY2025 (Yahoo fallback — annual filing bodies lack extractable statement tables) showed revenue of **$16.3bn**, operating income of **$3.23bn**, net income of **$2.26bn** and free cash flow of **$2.99bn**, with **$5.35bn** cash and net debt of **~$1.25bn**. The FY2025 10-K narrative confirms **~$5.4bn** cash, **~$1.25bn** returned via repurchases and dividends, and **$600m** debt retirement.

FY2026 (Yahoo fallback, partially corroborated by FY2026 10-K narrative) extended the revenue trend to **$17.1bn** (+5%) but net income fell to **$1.69bn** (diluted EPS **$3.84**) amid large non-operating/unusual items; free cash flow dropped to **$1.47bn** from **$2.99bn**, with operating cash flow of **$1.97bn** versus **$3.32bn** prior year. The FY2026 10-K cites **~$4.2bn** cash and **~$2.3bn** capital return including a **$1.5bn** accelerated share repurchase.

Business quality remains high: Fox News has led cable news for 20+ years; Tubi averaged **~2.2%** of US TV viewing (Nielsen Gauge, per FY2026 10-K); sports rights include long-dated NFL and FIFA World Cup exposure. Tubi and FOX One extend reach to cord-nevers.

The screen’s **buy** label aligns directionally with a capital-return, asset-backed media compounder, but **cannot be tied to specific passed factors** — `models_passed: 0`, `data_quality_score: 0.0`, and no key metrics are populated. Deep research supports franchise quality but flags **FCF deterioration** and **transformation risk** (Roku, FOX One) as reasons to size conservatively rather than treat the screen as fully validated.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Regulatory / M&A:** The **Roku acquisition** (~$22bn, announced 14 Jun 2026 per 8-K) is subject to HSR clearance. On **8 Sep 2026**, Fox and Roku received a **DOJ second request**, extending the review; closing is expected **H1 2027** if conditions are met. A **$1.0bn** contingent term loan facility was arranged (8-K, 30 Jun 2026). FY2026 10-K risk factors lead with Roku completion, regulatory conditioning and integration uncertainty.

**Governance / key person:** Dual-class structure with Murdoch family voting control (Stockholders Agreement referenced in XBRL). September 2025 news coverage reported Lachlan Murdoch prevailing in Murdoch family succession arrangements.

**Competitive / cyclical:** Q1 FY2026 10-Q notes **declining cable subscribers** despite rate increases; linear TV fragmentation persists. Sports programming rights are long-dated but costly; FY2025 added INDYCAR/LIV Golf while exiting WWE. Political-advertising cyclicality created **~$100m** quarterly comparability headwinds post-2024 elections.

**Litigation:** Filing metadata references **Smartmatic**, **Dominion**, a **Tubi lawsuit** (settled 20 May 2025 per XBRL tags), and **Fubo** litigation tied to the sports streaming JV (Jan 2026). Defamation/reputational tail risk in news operations remains structurally relevant. Item 3 legal-proceedings narrative is not fully present in truncated 10-K extracts.

**Pension:** Non-current pension/post-retirement obligations of **~$263–276m** (Yahoo balance sheet, FY2025–26).

**Leverage / liquidity:** Gross debt **~$7.6bn** against **~$4.2bn** cash (FY2026); manageable today but Roku financing adds pro-forma complexity. No going-concern or covenant-breach language found in available extracts.

**Accounting / earnings quality:** FY2026 net income depressed by **$912m** unusual items and **$761m** loss on sale of securities (Yahoo income statement), creating divergence between reported and normalised earnings. FanDuel/Flutter equity stake introduces mark-to-market volatility.
