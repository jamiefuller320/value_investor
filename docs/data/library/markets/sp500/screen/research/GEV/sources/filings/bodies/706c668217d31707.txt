The Company’s CODM is its Chief Executive Officer (CEO). Our operating activities are managed through three segments: Power, Electrification, and Wind. These segments have been identified based on the nature of the products and services sold and how the Company manages its operations. The performance of these segments is principally measured based on revenues and segment EBITDA. Segment EBITDA is determined based on the performance measures used by our CEO to assess the performance of each business in a given period. In connection with that assessment, the CEO may exclude matters, such as charges for impairments, significant restructuring programs, manufacturing footprint rationalization and other similar expenses, acquisition costs and other related charges, certain gains and losses from acquisitions or dispositions, and certain other items.
