OTHER EVENTS On November 25, 2025, Veralto Corporation (the “Company”) announced that its Board of Directors approved a share repurchase program authorizing the repurchase of up to $750 million of the Company’s common stock from time to time on the open market (including through the use of trading plans intended to qualify under Rule 10b5-1 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended), in privately negotiated transactions or by other methods, at the Company’s discretion. The program does not obligate the Company to acquire any particular amount of its common stock, has no expiration date, and will continue until otherwise suspended or terminated at any time for any reason. The timing and amount of any shares repurchased under the program will be determined by members of the Company’s management based on their evaluation of market, business conditions, and other factors.
