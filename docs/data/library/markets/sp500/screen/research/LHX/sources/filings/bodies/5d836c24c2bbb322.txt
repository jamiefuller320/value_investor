NOTE B: STRATEGIC INVESTMENT IN SUBSIDIARY Overview On April 17, 2026, with respect to our wholly owned subsidiary, Aerojet Rocketdyne Holdings, Inc. (“AR”), which operates as our Missile Solutions segment, we entered into a definitive agreement (the “DoW Investment Agreement”) with the United States Department of War (“DoW” or the “Investor”). The DoW Investment Agreement provided for the issuance and sale of 10,000 shares of Redeemable AR Series A Convertible Preferred Stock (the “Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock”) par value of $ 0.0001 per share and stated value of $ 100,000 per share, and public offering (“IPO”) and, upon a Qualified IPO (as defined in the DoW Investment Agreement), warrants (collectively the “Subsidiary Warrants”), for an aggregate purchase price of $ 1 billion, which is the initial face value of the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock. The Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock payout options include one of the following: (i) a liquidation preference equal to face value of the preferred shares plus 7 % cumulative annual dividends, compounded quarterly (the “Liquidation Preference”); (ii) an automatic conversion into common stock of our wholly owned subsidiary AXYV Inc. (“AXYV”), at a 20 % discount to the IPO stock price upon a Qualified IPO on or before December 31, 2027; or (iii) either Investor conversion on demand or redemption on or after April 17, 2031, at fair value, if a Qualified IPO does not occur by December 31, 2027. Under the DoW Investment Agreement, we are expected to use commercially reasonable efforts to consummate a Qualified IPO. The options are mutually exclusive and are dependent on achieving specified milestones. The purpose of the investment is to strengthen the U.S. defense industrial base by providing funding for the Missile Solutions segment to expand and modernize its facilities, accelerate research and development, and increase production capacity for critical technologies. The fair value of the investment exceeds the proceeds as it simultaneously provides financing, the opportunity to substantially expand an existing commercial relationship, and the benefit of an anchor investor whose participation tangibly demonstrates the Missile Solutions segment’s strong business prospects. The DoW Investment Agreement contains customary representations, warranties, and covenants by us and the Investor. Under the terms of the DoW Investment Agreement, AR may redeem, or the Investor may require the redemption of, the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock and Subsidiary Warrants held by the Investor under certain conditions. Use of Proceeds Under terms of the DoW Investment Agreement, net proceeds of $ 973 million are to be used to fund development and construction activities for expanding our missile manufacturing capacity and capabilities within our Missile Solutions segment. We intend to use the remaining net proceeds, if any, for general corporate purposes. The net proceeds are included in the “Proceeds from issuance of subsidiary Series A preferred stock, net” line item in our Condensed Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows as Net cash used in financing activities. Preferred Stock Because redemption of the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock is not solely within our control due to the redemption features described above, the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock is classified as mezzanine equity and _____________________________________________________________________ 9 NOTES TO CONDENSED CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (Unaudited) included in the “Redeemable subsidiary Series A convertible preferred stock” line item in our Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet. Conversion Upon Qualified IPO On or Before December 31, 2027. Upon completion of a Qualified IPO, the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock will convert into common stock of AXYV (“AXYV Common Stock”) at a 20 % discount to the public offering price in the Qualified IPO. The conversion terms are designed to provide the holder with a fixed return settled in a variable number of shares of AXYV Common Stock. The Investor’s ownership percentage would be determined based on the valuation of AXYV at the time of a Qualified IPO. Fair Value Conversion or Redemption After December 31, 2027. If a Qualified IPO does not occur on or before December 31, 2027, the redemption price calculation methodology varies depending on whether specified milestones have been achieved by such date. If such milestones are not achieved by December 31, 2027, the redemption price would be calculated by applying a contractual premium of 110 % to the Liquidation Preference. If the milestones are achieved by December 31, 2027, the Investor can convert the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock at any time (including in the case of an IPO after December 31, 2027), or a redemption on or after April 17, 2031 could occur. The Investor’s conversion rate will be determined based on the face value of the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock ($ 1 billion) divided by 15 times 2026 AXYV earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“EBITDA”). The redemption price would be calculated on or after April 17, 2031, and would equal the greater of (i) the Liquidation Preference or (ii) the fair market value, calculated in the same manner as noted above. Redemption Upon Sale of AXYV. If there is a sale of AXYV before a Qualified IPO, or after December 31, 2027 but prior to a Qualified IPO and specified milestones have not been achieved, the redemption price would be calculated by applying a contractual premium of 110 % to the Liquidation Preference. If specified milestones have been achieved, the Investor is entitled to receive cash equal to the greater of the Liquidation Preference or a pro forma ownership percentage which is calculated as the face value of the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock divided by 15 times 2026 AXYV EBITDA. Conversion and Redemption Feature Embedded Derivatives . Certain of the conversion and redemption features represent embedded derivatives. These features of the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock were bifurcated from the host instrument and recorded as a derivative liability, which is included in the “Subsidiary Series A preferred stock conversion feature” line item in the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet at its fair value. The derivative liability is measured at fair value each reporting period, with changes recognized in earnings as a component of the “Other (expense) income, net” line item in our Condensed Consolidated Statement of Operations. Warrants The Subsidiary Warrants entitle the Investor to purchase shares representing 3 % of the AXYV Common Stock, on a fully diluted basis, and consist of warrants exercisable at 100 %, 110 %, and 120 % of the common stock price, and warrants exercisable at 100 %, 110 %, and 120 % of the Qualified IPO price, which take effect upon a Qualified IPO. The warrants become exercisable only upon (i) the occurrence of certain events outlined in the DoW Investment Agreement and (ii) the passage of time (i.e., on or after December 31, 2027), but only if certain operational conditions are met that are outside the control of the Company and the Investor. The warrants are accounted for as derivative liabilities, included in the “Subsidiary warrants” line item in the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet, initially measured at fair value with subsequent changes recognized in earnings as a component of the “Other (expense) income, net” line item in our Condensed Consolidated Statement of Operations. Immediately prior to the Qualified IPO, the warrants will automatically convert into warrants of AXYV. The number of shares underlying the warrants will be calculated as a specified percentage of the fully diluted AXYV Common Stock immediately prior to the Qualified IPO, after giving effect to the conversion of the Subsidiary Series A Preferred Stock. _____________________________________________________________________ 10 NOTES TO CONDENSED CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (Unaudited) Fair Value Measurements The conversion and redemption feature embedded derivatives and warrants are measured at fair value with probabilities associated with expected Qualified IPO timing and probability, projected valuation of AXYV, equity volatility, and probability-weighted scenario outcomes. NOTE H: FAIR VALUE MEASUREMENTS We measure certain assets and liabilities at fair value on a recurring basis utilizing a fair value hierarchy that prioritizes inputs based on market observability: • Level 1 — Quoted prices in active markets for identical assets or liabilities. • Level 2 — Observable inputs other than quoted prices included within Level 1, including: quoted prices for similar assets or liabilities in active or inactive markets; quoted prices for identical assets or liabilities in inactive markets; and inputs derived from or corroborated by observable market data. • Level 3 — Unobservable inputs with little or no market activity that are significant to the fair value of the assets or liabilities and reflect our assumptions about market participants’ pricing, using the best available information. We utilize observable inputs whenever available. In certain instances, fair value is estimated using quoted market prices from external pricing services. We assess the methodologies of these services to ensure valuations reflect fair value, including net asset value (“NAV”). The NAV reported by an asset manager may be adjusted when sufficient evidence indicates NAV is not representative of fair value. A single estimated profit margin is used to recognize profit for each performance obligation over its period of performance. At the outset of each contract, we gauge its complexity and perceived risks and establish an estimated total cost at completion in line with those expectations. Due to the nature of many of these contracts, developing the estimated total cost at completion and total transaction price often requires judgment. After establishing the estimated total cost at completion, we follow a standard estimate at completion (“EAC”) process in which we review the progress and performance on our ongoing contracts. If we successfully retire risks associated with the technical, schedule and cost aspects of a contract, we may lower our estimated total cost at completion commensurate with the retirement of these risks. Conversely, there are many reasons estimated contract costs can increase, including: (i) supply chain disruptions, inflation and labor issues; (ii) design or other development challenges; and (iii) program execution challenges (including technical schedule or quality issues and other performance concerns). Additionally, as the contract progresses, our estimates of total transaction price may increase or decrease if, for example, we receive incentive or award fees that are higher or lower than expected.
