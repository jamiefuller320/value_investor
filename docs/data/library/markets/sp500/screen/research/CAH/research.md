# Cardinal Health, Inc. (CAH) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T13:36:30.458468+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Cardinal Health is a transformed US healthcare distribution and services platform: low-margin pharmaceutical distribution remains the revenue engine, but MSO acquisitions (The Specialty Alliance, Navista/ION), home-care (ADS), and generics/BioPharma Solutions are driving a step-change in profitability and cash generation. The quantitative screen flags a buy on FCF yield (~9%), earnings quality, Magic Formula, and Neff PEGY metrics, though headline P/E (~34.5×) reflects a much-improved earnings base rather than deep cyclical cheapness. FY2025 GAAP net income reached $1.56bn and non-GAAP diluted EPS $8.24; nine-month FY2026 non-GAAP diluted EPS is already $8.35 with operating cash flow of $3.5bn. The central debate is whether acquisition-led growth, tariff/regulatory headwinds, and a negative book-value balance sheet are adequately priced after a strong multi-year rerating.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value-oriented investor, CAH offers a hybrid case: not a classic deep-value trough name, but a cash-compounding distributor pivoting into higher-return adjacencies.

**Screen alignment.** The buy signal (4/22 models; composite 57%) is driven by cheapness/GARP/risk families. Passed models — FCF Yield, Neff PEGY (0.48), Magic Formula, Earnings Quality — fit a business generating substantial free cash despite thin distribution margins. The screen’s “Yield 91.0%” label maps to ~9.1% FCF yield on ~$4.85bn FCF (`screening_snapshot.json`), not dividend yield (FY2025 dividend ~$2.02/share per 10-K).

**Business quality.** Filings show a durable turnaround from the GMPD goodwill write-down cycle (FY2022–FY2024 cumulative impairments >$3bn). FY2025 Pharma segment profit rose 12% to $2.26bn; FY2026 YTD Pharma profit is up 24% to $2.14bn (9M). MSO platforms and ADS are accretive to margin mix. Generics programme and BioPharma Solutions provide recurring profit levers beyond volume-led branded sales (which grow revenue but “did not meaningfully contribute to segment profit,” per filings).

**Capital allocation.** Management is actively deploying cash: ~$5.3bn acquisitions in FY2025, ~$1.0bn share repurchases (9M FY2026), and dividends (~$2.04/share annualised). Leverage remains within covenant (net leverage ≤3.75×; compliant at each filing date reviewed).

**Why not a slam-dunk value pick.** Negative shareholders’ equity (~$2.8bn deficit at June 2025) and elevated P/E limit traditional book-value screens (P/B −18.9 is an accounting artefact). Acquisition integration, physician-compensation costs, and amortisation are material ongoing adjustments.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Opioid litigation.** $4.3–4.9bn accrued; payments through 2038. FY2025 paid $798m; 9M FY2026 paid $417m. Residual tail risk beyond accruals remains possible; filings emphasise estimation uncertainty.

**Regulatory / reimbursement.** Filings flag US prescription drug pricing executive orders, 340B programme litigation, and potential Medicare/Medicaid utilisation effects from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Branded volume growth may not translate to profit if pricing compresses.

**Tariffs / trade.** Q3 FY2026 MD&A: ~$200m IEEPA tariffs paid, primarily GMPD; mitigation incomplete. Potential refunds uncertain; customer pass-back expected if refunds received. Section 122/301/232 tariffs remain dynamic.

**Goodwill / acquisitions.** FY2025 deployed $5.3bn on deals; FY2026 adds Solaris ($1.9bn). Q3 FY2026 Navista & ION impairment $184m signals MSO valuation sensitivity (10.5% discount rate; further 0.5pp increase could reduce fair value ~$70m per filing sensitivity). GMPD goodwill fully impaired in FY2024; Solutions goodwill sat within ~1% of fair value at FY2024 year-end.

**Leverage and integration.** Debt rose from $5.1bn (FY2024) to $8.9bn (Q3 FY2026) to fund MSO strategy. Acquisition-related cash/compensation costs ($243m 9M FY2026) and amortisation ($348m 9M) are recurring drags on GAAP earnings. Interest expense 9M FY2026 $269m (+91% YoY).

**Governance / accounting.** FY2024 10-K corrected Solutions revenue recognition error (immaterial individually). Elliott cooperation agreement expired Q2 FY2025. No pension red flags surfaced in extracts reviewed.

**Competitive.** Red Oak Sourcing dependency on CVS Health; large-customer concentration risk evidenced by OptumRx exit impact.
