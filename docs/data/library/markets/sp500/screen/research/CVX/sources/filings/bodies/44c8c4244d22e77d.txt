On October 6, 2026, Chevron Corporation issued a press release announcing that Chevron will divest its ownership interests in Hess Midstream LP ("Hess Midstream") and its DJ Basin crude oil midstream assets. A copy of the news release is attached hereto and furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K. The information included in this Item 7.01 and in Exhibit 99.1 shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended. As used in this report, terms such as “company,” “Chevron,” “we,” “its” and “our” may refer to Chevron Corporation, one or more of its consolidated subsidiaries, or to all of them taken as a whole. All of these terms are used for convenience only and are not intended as a precise description of any of the separate companies, each of which manages its own affairs. Item 8.01 Other Events. Chevron's preliminary accounting assessment indicates that upon closing of the referenced transaction, Chevron will deconsolidate Hess Midstream and recognize a loss estimated at approximately $3 to $4 billion, which it expects to treat as a special item. The transaction is subject to specified terms, including the satisfaction of customary closing conditions and required regulatory approvals, and any closing adjustments, and is expected to close by 2026.
