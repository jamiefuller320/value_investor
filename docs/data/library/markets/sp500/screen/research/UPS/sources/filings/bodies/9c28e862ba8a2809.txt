On November 4, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of United Parcel Service, Inc. (the “Company”), upon recommendation of the Board’s Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee, set the size of the Board to eleven members and appointed Shannon Eisenhardt to the Board, including as a member of its Audit Committee, effective immediately. Ms. Eisenhardt, 52, is Group Chief Financial Officer and an Executive Director of Reckitt Benckiser Group plc. Before joining Reckitt in 2023 as Global Chief Financial Officer Designate and assuming her current role in March 2024, she spent eight years at NIKE, Inc., most recently from 2020 to 2023 as Chief Financial Officer of Consumer, Marketplace and Brand, leading finance for Nike’s global geographies, Nike Direct and Global Marketing, as well as Global Business Planning. Ms. Eisenhardt will be entitled to participate in the Company’s compensation program for directors, as described under the heading “Director Compensation” in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 19, 2026. In connection with her appointment, Ms. Eisenhardt will receive an annual cash retainer payment of $125,000 and an annual equity award of $210,000, each prorated for the portion of the current Board service year remaining after the effective date of her appointment. There are no arrangements or understandings between Ms. Eisenhardt and any other person pursuant to which she was selected as a director. Ms. Eisenhardt does not have any family relationship with any director or executive officer of the Company, and there are no transactions involving Ms. Eisenhardt that would require disclosure under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K.
