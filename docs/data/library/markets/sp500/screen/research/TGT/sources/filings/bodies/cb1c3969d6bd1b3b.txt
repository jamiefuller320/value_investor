Target Corporation Reports Third Quarter Earnings — Page 3 of 15 Operating Results Net Sales of $27.1 billion in the third quarter were 7.1 percent higher than last year, reflecting a 6.9 percent increase in merchandise sales and a 19.3 percent increase in sales. Comparable sales grew 5.6 percent in the third quarter, reflecting a comparable store sales increase of 4.6 percent and comparable digital sales increase of 9.8 percent. Third quarter operating income, which included a $301 million benefit from tariff refunds, was $1.7 billion, compared with GAAP operating income of $0.9 billion and Adjusted operating income of $1.1 billion. Operating income margin rate of 6.2 percent, which included 1.1 percentage points of benefit from the tariff refunds, increased meaningfully from the GAAP operating income margin rate of 3.8 percent and Adjusted operating income rate of 4.4 percent. Third quarter gross margin rate was 30.5 percent, expanding 2.3 percentage points and reflecting 1.1 percentage points of benefit from tariff refunds. Excluding tariff refunds, third quarter gross margin rate would have expanded approximately 1.2 percentage points over the prior year rate of 28.2 percent, reflecting the comparison against last year’s elevated markdowns and tariff rates, as well as growth in sales and the benefit of productivity improvements at supply chain facilities. These benefits were partially offset by elevated freight and costs, continued investments in value, and modest pressure from category sales mix. Third quarter GAAP and Adjusted SG&A expense rate was 21.9 percent, compared with GAAP SG&A expense rate of 21.9 percent and Adjusted SG&A expense rate of 21.3 percent, which excluded costs related to our business transformation initiative. This increase in Adjusted SG&A rate reflects the impact of higher incentive compensation, additional store labor hours, as well as planned investments in new and existing stores, partially offset by the leverage benefit of strong topline growth. Interest Expense and Taxes The Company’s third quarter 2026 net interest expense was $95 million, compared with $115 million last year, reflecting higher interest income in the current year. Third quarter 2026 effective income tax rate increased to 23.9 percent, compared with the prior year rate of 19.8 percent, primarily driven by higher tax benefits recognized in the prior year as well as higher pretax earnings in the current year. – more – Target Corporation Reports Third Quarter Earnings — Page 4 of 15 Capital Deployment and Return on Invested Capital Third quarter capital expenditures of $1.4 billion were $0.4 billion higher than last year, driven primarily by increased investments in new and existing stores. The Company paid dividends of $527 million in the third quarter, compared with $518 million last year, reflecting a 1.8 percent increase in the dividend per share. The Company repurchased $251 million of its shares in the third quarter, retiring 1.6 million shares of common stock at an average price of $155.49. As of the end of the quarter, the Company had approximately $8.0 billion of remaining capacity under the repurchase program approved by Target’s Board of Directors in August 2021. For the trailing twelve months through third quarter 2026, return on invested capital (ROIC) was 16.5 percent, compared with 13.4 percent for the trailing twelve months through third quarter 2025.
