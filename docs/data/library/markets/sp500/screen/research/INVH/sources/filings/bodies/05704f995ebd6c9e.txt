(d) On November 9, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Invitation Homes Inc. (the “Company”) increased the size of the Board from 9 to 10 members and appointed M. Colin Connolly as a director of the Board, effective as of November 13, 2026, with a term expiring at the Company’s 2027 annual meeting of stockholders. The Board also appointed Mr. Connolly to serve on the Compensation and Management Development Committee and the Investment and Finance Committee. There is no agreement, arrangement or understanding with any person pursuant to which Mr. Connolly was selected as a director, and he is not a party to any transaction required to be disclosed pursuant to Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K. In connection with his appointment, and pursuant to the Company’s compensation policy for directors, Mr. Connolly will be granted a award of restricted stock units (“RSUs”) with a value of $96,000, which will generally vest in full on the date of the Company’s 2027 annual meeting of stockholders. Mr. Connolly will also receive the Company’s standard director compensation arrangement, as described in the Company’s definitive proxy statement relating to its 2026 annual meeting of stockholders. The Company will enter into an Indemnification Agreement with Mr. Connolly in substantially the same form that the Company has entered into with its other directors and executive officers. Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure. On November 11, 2026, the Company issued a press release announcing Mr. Connolly’s appointment to the Board. A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated by reference herein.
