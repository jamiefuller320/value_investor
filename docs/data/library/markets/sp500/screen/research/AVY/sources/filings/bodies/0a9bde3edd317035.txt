(c) On October 9, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Avery Dennison Corporation, a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), elected Helen J. Sheirbon as President, Solutions Group, and an “executive officer” of the Company under the rules of the Securities and Exchange Commission, effective November 3, 2026. Ms. Sheirbon, age 51, has served as Senior Vice President and Chief Operating Officer of Global HP Solutions, HP Inc., a global technology company, since July 2025 and will leave that company by the end of October 2026. Prior to her current position, she held roles of increasing responsibility within that company, including Senior Vice President and Division President, Collaboration and Accessories Solutions, from January 2024 to July 2025; Senior Vice President, Global Customer Support and Services Delivery, from March 2022 to December 2023; and Vice President, Print (Worldwide and North America), from March 2017 to February 2022. A copy of the press release, dated October 12, 2026, announcing Ms. Sheirbon’s role is attached as Exhibit 99.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference. In connection with her election, the Board’s Talent and Compensation Committee (the “Committee”) approved the following compensation package for Ms. Sheirbon: (i) an annual base salary of $625,000; (ii) a target Annual Incentive Plan opportunity of 60% of base salary; and (iii) a target incentive (LTI) opportunity of 200% of base salary. The Committee also approved the following compensation for Ms. Sheirbon: (i) a special equity award of restricted stock units with a grant date fair value of $2,000,000 to be granted on December 1, 2026, which will vest 50% on the first and second anniversaries of the grant date; (ii) a special equity award of performance units at the end of a performance period with a grant date fair value of $1,500,000 to be granted on the same date as that of the Company’s annual LTI awards in the first quarter of 2027, with performance objectives consistent with the awards then to be granted to executives; (iii) a cash signing bonus of $250,000 to be paid as soon as practicable following her commencement of employment; and (iv) to offset equity and cash incentives forfeited at her prior employer, a cash payment of $800,000 to be paid in February 2027 provided she is still employed by the Company and in good standing at that time. The equity awards are subject to her continued employment through the applicable vesting dates and the cash bonus and payment are required to be repaid by Ms. Sheirbon in the event she voluntarily terminates her employment within 24 months of her start date. As a Level 2 executive of the Company, Ms. Sheirbon will be eligible to receive an annual executive benefit allowance of $65,000; reimbursement of up to $15,000 per year for financial counseling and tax preparation services; a paid annual executive physical; and taxable relocation assistance benefits in accordance with the Company’s executive relocation policy. She will also be an eligible participant in the Company’s savings, deferred compensation, executive severance and key executive change of control severance plans, in each case as described in the Company’s 2026 Proxy Statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 12, 2026.
