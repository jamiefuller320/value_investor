LAB INVESTMENTS Healthpeak has acquired, or is under contract to acquire, four life science buildings totaling approximately 510,000 square feet across three campuses for approximately $233 million. Each acquisition is part of, or an extension of, an existing Healthpeak campus where the Company's local scale and relationships position it to drive leasing. On September 30, 2026, Healthpeak acquired the senior construction loan secured by HQ Point and acquired the remaining equity interest for a combined $105 million. HQ Point is a , 236,000 square foot lab campus in San Diego’s Sorrento Mesa submarket that is currently 29% occupied. Healthpeak is also under contract to acquire two life science properties that are expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026 or early 2027, subject to customary closing conditions. • South San Francisco: Healthpeak is under contract to acquire a 145,000 square foot life science building for approximately $92 million. The property is currently 49% occupied and sits adjacent to an existing 146,000 square foot Healthpeak life science building and between The Cove and The Shore, two Healthpeak campuses totaling more than 2 million square feet. • Cambridge: Healthpeak is under contract to acquire the remaining 51% ownership interest in 100 Acorn Park Drive, a 129,000 square foot life science building on the Cambridge Discovery Park campus for approximately $36 million. The property is currently 53% occupied. Upon closing, Healthpeak will own 100% of the 605,000 square foot Cambridge Discovery Park campus. These three opportunistic acquisitions have a blended occupancy of 41%, a current cash yield of approximately 5%, and an expected stabilized cash yield of approximately 9-10%. A presentation highlighting these investments is available at https://ir.healthpeak.com. OUTPATIENT MEDICAL ACQUISITION In October 2026, Healthpeak acquired Westway One, a 146,000 square foot Class A medical training facility in Houston, Texas, for $38 million. The property is 100% leased to affiliates of HCA Healthcare, with 11 years of remaining lease term. The acquisition represents an initial capitalization rate of approximately 7.4%, adds to Healthpeak's approximately 3.4 million square foot outpatient medical portfolio in Houston, and expands its portfolio of HCA nursing and professional education facilities.
