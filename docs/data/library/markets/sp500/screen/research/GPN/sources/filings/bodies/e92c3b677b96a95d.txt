ATLANTA -- Global Payments Inc. (NYSE: GPN) today announced results for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026. “We delivered third quarter results consistent with our expectations, reflecting the ongoing disciplined execution of our team members worldwide,” said Cameron Bready, chief executive officer. “We continue to make substantial progress integrating Worldpay, giving us confidence to increase our expense synergy target to $650 million. With the foundation of the combined company firmly established, we are sharply focused on executing our strategy and positioning Global Payments as the worldwide partner of choice for commerce solutions.” Bready continued, “Our strategy is anchored in delivering differentiated products and solutions, providing a distinctive client and partner experience, and harnessing our unmatched scale and distribution to deepen relationships, expand our reach and drive sustainable growth." Bready concluded, “We are also pleased to have returned more than $1.4 billion in capital to shareholders , underscoring our continued commitment to our capital allocation priorities.” Third Quarter 2026 Summary • GAAP revenues were $3.31 billion and diluted earnings per share were $1.06. • Adjusted net revenues increased approximately 31% to $3.18 billion. On a normalized basis 1 , consistent with our outlook, adjusted net revenue increased over 4%. Adjusted operating margin expanded 220 basis points on a normalized basis 1 to 42.5%. • Adjusted EPS increased 10% to $3.58. 1 Normalized comparisons include the results of Worldpay and exclude the results of Issuer Solutions and other divested businesses, as presented in Exhibit 99.2 to the Company's Current Report on Form 8-K filed on August 5, 2026. 1 2026 Outlook “Our third quarter financial performance demonstrates the durability of our business model and our ability to generate sustainable growth, margin expansion and strong free cash flow,” said Josh Whipple, chief financial officer. “We remain focused on driving commercial excellence, realizing the benefits of our increased scale and rigorous integration execution as we build on the financial strength of the combined company.” Whipple continued, “Our full year outlook is unchanged. We continue to expect normalized 1 , constant currency adjusted net revenue growth of approximately 4% – 5% and adjusted earnings per share of $13.60 – $13.80 for the full year. We continue to expect normalized 1 adjusted operating margin expansion of approximately 150 basis points for the full year and to return more than $2 billion of capital to shareholders in 2026.” Capital Allocation Global Payments’ Board of Directors approved a dividend of $0.25 per share payable on December 24, 2026 to shareholders of record as of December 10, 2026. FINANCIAL MEASURES Global Payments supplements revenues, operating income, operating margin, net income attributable to Global Payments, and earnings per share (EPS) determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP by providing these measures with certain adjustments (such measures being financial measures) in this document to assist with evaluating our performance. In addition to GAAP measures, management uses these financial measures to focus on the factors the company believes are pertinent to the daily management of our operations. The constant currency growth measures adjust for the impact of exchange rates and are calculated using average exchange rates during the comparable period in the prior year. Management uses these financial measures, together with other metrics, to set goals for and measure the performance of the business and to determine incentive compensation. Adjusted net revenue, adjusted operating income, adjusted operating margin, adjusted net income attributable to Global Payments, and adjusted EPS should be considered in addition to, and not as substitutes for, revenues, operating income, and EPS determined in accordance with GAAP. The financial measures reflect management's judgment of particular items, and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies. Adjusted net revenue excludes related payments associated with certain lines of business to reflect economic benefits to the company. On a GAAP basis, these payments are presented gross in both revenues and operating expenses. Management believes adjusted net revenue more closely reflects the economic benefits to the company's core business and allows for better comparisons with industry peers. Adjusted operating income, adjusted operating margin, adjusted net income attributable to Global Payments and adjusted EPS exclude acquisition-related amortization expense, acquisition, integration, separation and transformation expense, gains or losses on business dispositions, and certain other items specific to each reporting period as more fully described in the accompanying reconciliations in Schedules 6 and 7. In addition depreciation expense of certain acquired technology assets is also excluded, as it is a noncash expense and, based on its nature, is impacted by future integration initiatives. Excluding such depreciation expense supplements GAAP information with a measure that can be used to assess the comparability of operating performance across periods, as such assets were recognized as part of acquisition accounting. The tax rate used in determining the income tax impact of earnings adjustments is either the jurisdictional statutory rate in effect at the time of the adjustment or the jurisdictional expected annual effective tax rate for the period, depending on the nature and timing of the adjustment. In addition, income taxes on adjustments include the removal of tax charges related to business dispositions. Adjusted operating margin is derived by dividing adjusted operating income by adjusted net revenue. 14
