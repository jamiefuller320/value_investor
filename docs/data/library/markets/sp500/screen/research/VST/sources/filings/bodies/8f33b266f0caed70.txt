Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer acquired from Seller one hundred percent (100%) of the limited liability company interests in Q-Generation, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “Acquired Company”) (the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, the “Purchase Transaction”). Concurrently with the completion of the Purchase Transaction, Buyer and TSVME LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer (“Merger Sub”), completed the previously announced transaction pursuant to that certain Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”, and, together with the Purchase Agreement, the “Transaction Agreements”, and each a “Transaction Agreement”) with Hamilton Holdings II, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and, prior to the completion of the Purchase Transaction, an indirect subsidiary of the Acquired Company (the “Target Company”), and Seller solely for purposes of Article II of the Merger Agreement and in its capacity as Members’ Representative (as defined in the Merger Agreement). Under the Merger Agreement, Merger Sub merged with and into the Target Company (the “Merger,” and the Merger together with the Purchase Transaction, the “Transactions”), with the Target Company surviving the Merger (the “Surviving Company”) on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Merger Agreement and in accordance with the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act. Immediately following the consummation of the Purchase Transaction and prior to the Merger, Buyer contributed Merger Sub to a direct or indirect wholly owned subsidiary of the Acquired Company. As a result of the Merger, the Surviving Company became a wholly owned subsidiary of the Acquired Company, and Merger Sub ceased to exist. Pursuant to the Transaction Agreements, and in consideration for the limited liability company interests of the Acquired Company, consideration for the Merger, and other consideration received thereunder, Buyer and Vistra paid consideration consisting of (i) approximately $2.2 billion in cash (the “Cash Consideration”) (net of adjustments for working capital, acquired cash, the assumption of approximately $1.7 billion of outstanding indebtedness of the Acquired Company and its subsidiaries as of closing), and certain other adjustments and (ii) 5,000,000 shares of Vistra common stock, par value $0.01 per share, issued to Seller on the Closing Date at a mutually value of $185 per share (the “Stock Consideration”). Pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, Vistra is required to file with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”), a shelf registration statement (or supplement thereto) registering the resale on a continuous basis of the shares of Vistra common stock comprising the Stock Consideration. In addition, Seller has agreed not to transfer the Stock Consideration for a period beginning on the Closing Date and continuing through January 30, 2027, subject to certain exceptions. The foregoing description of the Transactions and the Transaction Agreements does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Purchase Agreement and Merger Agreement, copies of which are filed as Exhibits 2.1 and 2.2, respectively, herein . Item 2.03 Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation or an Obligation under an Sheet Arrangement of a Registrant. In connection with the Transactions, approximately $1.7 billion of indebtedness of the Acquired Company and its subsidiaries was assumed. The indebtedness consisted of (1) approximately $800 million under a credit agreement among Cogentrix Finance HoldCo I, LLC, an indirect subsidiary of the Acquired Company, and the lenders thereunder and (2) $900 million under a credit agreement among Hamilton Projects Acquiror, LLC, an indirect subsidiary of the Acquired Company, and the lenders thereunder. On November 2, 2026, Vistra delivered to the lenders notice of its intent to repay the loans under the two credit agreements in full. Vistra expects to fully repay all term loans and revolving loans under the credit agreements on or about November 5, 2026. 2 Item 3.02. Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities. On the Closing Date, Vistra issued 5,000,000 shares of Vistra common stock to Seller as the Stock Consideration. The shares of Vistra common stock comprising the Stock Consideration were issued in reliance on the exemption from registration requirements provided by Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), to a person who is an “accredited investor” as that term is defined in Rule 501 of Regulation D promulgated by the SEC, without the use of any general solicitation or advertising to market or otherwise offer the securities for sale. Vistra relied upon representations, warranties, certifications and agreements of Seller in support of the satisfaction of the conditions contained in Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act. The shares of Vistra common stock comprising the Stock Consideration have not been registered under the Securities Act or applicable state securities laws and may not be offered or sold in the United States absent registration under the Securities Act or an applicable exemption from such registration requirements.
