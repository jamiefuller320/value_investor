On November 12, 2026, Ronald L. Phillips, Executive Vice President, Chief Human Resources Officer of Sysco Corporation (the “Company”), age 61, notified the Company of his intention to transition to a strategic advisor role with the Company, effective December 14, 2026. Mr. Phillips has served as the Company’s Executive Vice President, Chief Human Resources Officer since May 3, 2021. In connection with Mr. Phillips’ transition, the Company will appoint a new Chief Human Resources Officer, who is an external hire and will commence employment with the Company on December 14, 2026. The Company will announce additional details regarding Mr. Phillips’ successor at a later date. Mr. Phillips intends to serve as a strategic advisor to support an orderly transition until his retirement, which is expected in the first quarter of calendar year 2027. Mr. Phillips will continue to be employed under his current employment terms, including compensation, in his new role.
