On June 4, 2025, General Dynamics Corporation (the “company”) announced that Danny Deep, currently executive vice president, Combat Systems, has been promoted to executive vice president, Global Operations. The company also determined the compensation arrangement for Mr. Deep’s new position. His base salary will be $1,200,000. His target annual incentive opportunity will be at 130% of his base salary. His incentive, which is comprised of equity compensation, will be set pursuant to the approach described in the company’s proxy statement for the 2025 annual meeting of shareholders, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 28, 2025, beginning on page 52.
