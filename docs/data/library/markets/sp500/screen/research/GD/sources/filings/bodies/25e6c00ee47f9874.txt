Davis Tel: 703 876 3483 press@generaldynamics.com General Dynamics Reports 2026 Financial Results October 30, 2026 • Revenue $14.6 billion, up 12.9% versus prior year • Diluted EPS $4.49, up 15.7% versus prior year • $1.7 billion cash from operating activities, 139% of net earnings • 6.8- , $220 billion backlog RESTON, Va . – General Dynamics (NYSE: GD) today reported 2026 operating earnings of $1.5 billion, and $4.49 per diluted share (EPS), on revenue of $14.6 billion. Compared with the quarter, revenue increased 12.9%, operating earnings increased 14.7%, and diluted EPS increased 15.7%. Operating margin of 10.5% was a 20- expansion from the quarter. “Our business had exceptional third quarter results, with revenue growth across all four segments, robust bookings and solid operational performance,” said Phebe Novakovic, chairman and chief executive officer. “With the consistent growth in our backlog and our significant investment in our businesses over the last decade, we are poised to drive continued performance in the years to come.” Cash and Capital Deployment Cash provided by operating activities in the quarter totaled $1.7 billion, or 139% of net earnings. During the quarter, the company paid $429 million in dividends, invested $481 million in capital expenditures, and reduced total debt by $500 million. The company ended the quarter with $7 billion in total debt and $4.4 billion in cash and equivalents on hand. Orders and Backlog Orders received in the quarter totaled $93.8 billion in the defense segments, driven by large orders in the Marine Systems segment for Virginia- and submarines, and $5 billion in the Aerospace segment, for a total of $98.8 billion. ratio, defined as orders divided by revenue, was 8.5- for the quarter for the defense segments, 1.4- for the Aerospace segment, and 6.8- on a basis. Backlog at the end of the quarter was $220.3 billion. Estimated potential contract value, representing management’s estimate of additional value in unfunded indefinite delivery, indefinite quantity (IDIQ) contracts and unexercised options, was $50.4 billion. Total estimated contract value, the sum of backlog plus estimated potential contract value, was $270.7 billion. – more – About General Dynamics Headquartered in Reston, Virginia, General Dynamics is a global aerospace and defense company that offers a broad portfolio of products and services in business aviation; ship construction and repair; land combat vehicles, weapon systems and munitions; and technology products and services.
