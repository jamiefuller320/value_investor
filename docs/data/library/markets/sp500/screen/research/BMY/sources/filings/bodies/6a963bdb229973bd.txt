BETA Close X Bloomsbury Publishing Plc has granted conditional share awards under its 2023 Executive Share Plan to three Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) and senior management. These awards, priced at £6.56 per share, are contingent on performance metrics including EPS, Academic & Professional Adjusted Profit, Consumer Adjusted Profit, and Total Revenue, measured over the three financial years ending February 2029. Executive Directors will also face a two-year post-vesting holding period. The Chief Executive received 130,319 shares, the Chief Financial & Operating Officer received 91,463 shares, and the Global Managing Director of Academic & Professional Division received 68,597 shares.
