Director/PDMR Shareholding Bloomsbury Publishing PLC 11 August 2025 11 August 2025 Bloomsbury Publishing Plc (the "Company") Director/PDMR Shareholding Vesting and Exercise of awards under the Bloomsbury Publishing Plc 2014 Performance Share Plan ("2014 PSP") As noted in the Company's annual report and accounts, on Sunday 10 August 2025, the conditional share awards granted in August 2022 under the 2014 PSP vested at 87% of the maximum based on the level of the Company's achievement against relevant performance conditions.
