BETA Close X On August 28, 2025, Bloomsbury Publishing PLC announced the grant of conditional share awards under the 2023 Executive Share Plan to two PDMRs and senior management. The ESP Awards were granted at £4.885 per share. Chief Executive Nigel Newton received awards for 136,589 shares, while Group Finance Director Penny Scott-Bayfield received awards for 85,317 shares. Vesting is contingent on performance measured over three financial years ending February 2028, based on EPS targets ranging from 41.8p to 57.0p, Academic & Professional Operating Profit targets from £14.5m to £19.0m, Consumer Operating Profit targets from £30.5m to £42.0m, and International Revenue targets from £217.7m to £273.9m.
