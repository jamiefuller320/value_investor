Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement . On November 13, 2026, Hormel Foods Corporation (the “Company”) entered into a Credit Agreement (the “Credit Agreement”) with the lenders party thereto and Wells Fargo Bank, National Association, as administrative agent. The Credit Agreement provides for a $1.0 billion delayed draw term loan facility, under which the Company may make up to two borrowings: one borrowing in connection with the Company’s acquisition of Brakebush Brothers, LLC (“Brakebush”); and one borrowing in connection with the refinancing of certain existing indebtedness of the Company (including, without limitation, the Company’s 4.800% Senior Notes due March 30, 2027). Borrowings under the Credit Agreement will bear interest, at the Company’s option, at either (i) the Base Rate (as defined in the Credit Agreement) plus an applicable margin or (ii) Term SOFR (as defined in the Credit Agreement) plus an applicable margin. The applicable margin varies based on the Company’s debt ratings. The term loans mature on November 13, 2029. The term loans are subject to mandatory prepayment requirements upon the occurrence of certain specified events and may be prepaid by the Company at its option, in whole or in part, subject to the terms of the Credit Agreement. Amounts repaid under the term loan facility may not be reborrowed. The Credit Agreement contains customary representations and warranties and affirmative and negative covenants, including compliance by the Company with a minimum consolidated interest coverage ratio. The Credit Agreement also contains customary events of default for term loan facilities of this type. Upon the occurrence and continuation of an event of default, the obligations under the Credit Agreement may be accelerated, in addition to other remedies that may be available to the lenders. The foregoing description of the Credit Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Credit Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference. Item 2.03 Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation or an Obligation under an Sheet Arrangement of a Registrant . On November 13, 2026, the Company borrowed $700 million under the term loan facility described in Item 1.01 above in connection with the acquisition of Brakebush. The proceeds of the borrowing were used to pay a portion of the purchase price for the acquisition and related fees and expenses. The remaining $300 million under the term loan facility remains available for borrowing in connection with the refinancing of certain existing indebtedness.
