This Form 8-K/A amends the Form 8-K filed by Hormel Foods Corporation (the “Company”) on October 29, 2025 (the “Prior 8-K”). As disclosed in the Prior 8-K, Mr. Paul Kuehneman was appointed as Interim Chief Financial Officer and Controller, effective October 27, 2025. In connection with this transition, Mr. Kuehneman will receive the following compensation and benefits: (1) during his service in the interim role, an annual base salary at the rate of $500,000 per year; (2) participation in the Company’s annual incentive compensation program, with a target award opportunity equal to $300,000 for the fiscal year 2026 performance period; (3) participation in The Hormel Foods Corporation 2018 Operators’ Share Incentive Compensation Subplan, with an aggregate grant of 70,000 operators’ shares for the fiscal year 2026 performance period; (4) participation in the Company’s incentive compensation plan, with a incentive target of $550,000 for fiscal year 2026, $125,000 of which will be delivered in the form of performance-based cash incentive awards, $125,000 of which will be delivered in the form of stock option awards, and $300,000 of which will be delivered in the form of restricted stock units; and (5) standard executive benefit and health and welfare plan participation. Mr. Kuehneman has previously entered into the Company’s standard indemnification agreement applicable to senior executives and the Company’s standard restrictive covenant agreement applicable to senior executives.
