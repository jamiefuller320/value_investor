Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets. The information set forth in the Introductory Note of this Current Report is incorporated by reference into this Item 2.01. At the First Effective Time, pursuant to the terms and subject to the conditions of the Merger Agreement, each share of Kenvue common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “ Kenvue Common Stock ”), issued and outstanding immediately prior to the First Effective Time (other than shares of Kenvue Common Stock that were owned by the Company or K-C or any wholly owned subsidiary of the Company or K-C (or were held in treasury by the Company)) was converted into the right to receive (A) 0.14625 shares (the “ Exchange Ratio ”) of K-C common stock, par value $1.25 per share (the “ K-C Common Stock ” and the shares of K-C Common Stock issued in connection with the First Merger, the “ Stock Consideration ”), subject to cash in lieu of fractional shares, plus (B) $3.50 in cash (the “ Cash Consideration ” and, together with the Stock Consideration, the “ Merger Consideration ”), in each case, without interest. At the Second Effective Time, by virtue of the Second Merger and without any action on the part of any of the parties, the Initial Surviving Company or the equityholders thereof, (i) each share of common stock of the Initial Surviving Company issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Second Effective Time was automatically canceled and ceased to exist without any conversion or payment therefor and (ii) each unit of Second Merger Sub issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Second Effective Time remained issued and outstanding and represented validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable units of the Final Surviving Company, which constituted the only outstanding units of the Final Surviving Company immediately following the Second Effective Time. No fractional shares of K-C Common Stock were issued in the Mergers, and holders of Kenvue Common Stock otherwise entitled to receive a fractional share of K-C Common Stock will receive a cash payment, without interest, in lieu thereof, based on the average of the average price per share of K-C Common Stock for each of the five consecutive trading days ending on (and including) the first trading day immediately prior to the Closing Date, as specified in the Merger Agreement. In accordance with the terms of the Merger Agreement, each equity award of the Company, consisting of stock options, restricted stock units, performance stock units and deferred stock units, that was outstanding immediately prior to the First Effective Time was converted into an equivalent equity award of K-C, including with respect to vesting conditions (including in connection with a qualifying termination of employment), except that performance stock units were converted into solely restricted stock units. The number of shares of K-C Common Stock subject to each converted equity award was equal to the number of shares of Kenvue Common Stock subject to the award, with the number of shares of Kenvue Common Stock subject to any performance stock unit determined based on the greater of target and actual performance measured through the First Effective Time, multiplied by the Equity Award Exchange Ratio (as defined below), and the exercise price of each converted stock option is equal to the exercise price of the stock option of the Company divided by the Equity Award Exchange Ratio. The “ Equity Award Exchange Ratio ” is a fraction, (i) the numerator of which is the sum of the Cash Consideration and an amount equal to the product of (A) the Exchange Ratio and (B) the closing price of K-C Common Stock on the trading day immediately prior to the date of the First Effective Time (the “ K-C Closing Price ”) and (ii) the denominator of which is the K-C Closing Price. On November 1, 2026, the Company’s direct wholly owned subsidiary, Kenvue Brands LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, entered into an agreement with Matthew Orlando, the Company’s General Counsel, that seeks to place Mr. Orlando in a position regarding the impact of Section 280G of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. The agreement provides that if Mr. Orlando incurs an excise tax in respect of any payment or benefit made or provided to him in connection with the Mergers, he will be entitled to an additional payment so that on a net basis he is in the same position as if no such excise tax had applied. The amount of the payments is not known since they depend on various factors that are unknown at this time. In no event will the value of the payments exceed $8 million, although they are expected to be substantially less than this. The Company determined that providing Mr. Orlando with this agreement was appropriate given his extraordinary efforts over the prior year to negotiate and consummate the Mergers and that he was the only executive who was expected to remain subject to Section 280G after the implementation of mitigation strategies.
