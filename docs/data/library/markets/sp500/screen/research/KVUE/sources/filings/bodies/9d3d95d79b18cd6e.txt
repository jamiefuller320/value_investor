Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement On October 9, 2026, Kenvue Inc. (the “Company”) entered into a Third Supplemental Indenture dated as of October 9, 2026 (the “Third Supplemental Indenture”), to the indenture dated as of March 22, 2023, between the Company and Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas, as trustee (the “Trustee”) (the “Base Indenture”), as supplemented by (i) the first supplemental indenture dated as of March 22, 2023 (the “First Supplemental Indenture”), providing for the issuance of the Company’s 5.050% Senior Notes due 2028 (the “2028 Notes”), 5.000% Senior Notes due 2030 (the “2030 Notes”), 4.900% Senior Notes due 2033 (the “2033 Notes”), 5.100% Senior Notes due 2043 (the “2043 Notes”), 5.050% Senior Notes due 2053 (the “2053 Notes”) and 5.200% Senior Notes due 2063 (the “2063 Notes”) and (ii) the second supplemental indenture dated as of May 22, 2025 (the “Second Supplemental Indenture” and, together with the Base Indenture and the First Supplemental Indenture, as further supplemented by the Third Supplemental Indenture, the “Indenture”), providing for the issuance of the Company’s 4.850% Senior Notes due 2032 (the “2032 Notes” and, together with the 2028 Notes, the 2030 Notes, the 2033 Notes, the 2043 Notes, the 2053 Notes and the 2063 Notes, the “Existing Kenvue Notes”). The Third Supplemental Indenture was entered into to effect certain amendments to the Indenture (described below) for which consents were solicited by Corporation (“ ”) on behalf of the Company (each a “Consent Solicitation” and, collectively, the “Consent Solicitations”), in connection with ’s previously announced offers to exchange any and all Existing Kenvue Notes for up to $7,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of new notes issued by (the “ Notes”) and cash (each, an “Exchange Offer” and, collectively, the “Exchange Offers”). The amendments contained in the Third Supplemental Indenture (1) eliminate substantially all of the restrictive covenants in the Indenture with respect to each series of Existing Kenvue Notes, (2) eliminate certain of the events which may lead to an “Event of Default” in the Indenture with respect to each series of Existing Kenvue Notes (other than the failure to pay principal, premium or interest), (3) eliminate the Securities and Exchange Commission reporting covenant in the Indenture with respect to each series of Existing Kenvue Notes and (4) eliminate certain restrictions on the Company in the Indenture from consolidating with or merging into any other person or conveying, transferring or leasing all or any of its properties and assets to any person with respect to each series of Existing Kenvue Notes (collectively, the “Amendments”). The Third Supplemental Indenture was effective upon execution; provided that the Amendments with respect to a series of the Existing Kenvue Notes will become operative only upon the settlement of the Exchange Offer and Consent Solicitation relating to such series of the Existing Kenvue Notes (the time of such settlement, the “Settlement Date”) in accordance with the terms of the confidential offering memorandum and consent solicitation statement, dated as of September 28, 2026 (as amended or supplemented from time to time). The Settlement Date is expected to occur promptly following the expiration date of the Exchange Offers (the time of such expiration, the “Expiration Date”). The Expiration Date is expected to occur on or about the closing date of the acquisition of the Company by . On the Settlement Date, with respect to a series of Existing Kenvue Notes, the Amendments effected by the Third Supplemental Indenture with respect to such series of Existing Kenvue Notes will become automatically operative without any further action from the Company, the Trustee, or the holders of the Existing Kenvue Notes. The foregoing description of the Third Supplemental Indenture does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Third Supplemental Indenture, a copy of which is attached as Exhibit 4.1 hereto and is incorporated herein by reference.
