Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. On October 25, 2026, EQT Corporation, a Pennsylvania corporation (“EQT”), EQT Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of EQT, Rhino Merger Sub Inc., a Delaware corporation and an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of EQT (“Merger Sub”), and Rhino Merger Sub LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of EQT (“LLC Sub”), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”) with Range Resources Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“Range”). Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Merger Agreement, which has been unanimously approved by the board of directors of EQT (the “EQT Board”) and by the board of directors of Range, Merger Sub will merge with and into Range (the “First Merger”), with Range surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of EQT (the “First Step Surviving Corporation”), and as the second step in a single integrated transaction with the First Merger, the First Step Surviving Corporation will merge with and into LLC Sub (the “Second Merger” and, together with the First Merger, the “Merger”), with LLC Sub surviving the Second Merger as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of EQT. At the effective time of the First Merger (the “Effective Time”), among other things, each share of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of Range (“Range Common Stock”) that is issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time (including Range restricted stock awards but excluding Excluded Shares (as defined in the Merger Agreement)) will be converted into the right to receive, without interest, 0.987 (the “Exchange Ratio”) shares of common stock, no par value, of EQT (“EQT Common Stock”), with cash in lieu of fractional shares. Following the closing of the First Merger (the “Closing”), EQT’s existing shareholders and Range’s existing stockholders will own approximately 73% and 27%, respectively, of the combined company on a fully diluted basis. Governance In the Merger Agreement, EQT has agreed to increase the size of the EQT Board to 12 members and appoint Dennis L. Degner, who is currently Range’s Chief Executive Officer and President, and one other individual selected by Range (who shall satisfy the independence requirements of the New York Stock Exchange (the “NYSE”) with respect to service on the EQT Board), after reasonable consultation with EQT and with the consent of EQT (not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed), (the “Range Designees”) to the EQT Board at the Effective Time. EQT has also agreed that, following the Closing, EQT will take all necessary action to nominate the Range Designees for election to the EQT Board at the first annual meeting of shareholders of EQT thereafter. Termination Rights The Merger Agreement contains certain termination rights for both EQT and Range, including the right of either party, subject to certain limitations specified therein, to terminate the Merger Agreement if the First Merger is not consummated on or prior to October 25, 2027 (the “End Date”), which date will be automatically extended until April 25, 2028 if all of the conditions to Closing, other than the condition relating to the absence of law, injunction, order or decree prohibiting consummation of the Transactions or the HSR Act Condition, have been satisfied or waived. The Merger Agreement may also be terminated by the mutual written consent of EQT and Range. In addition, the Merger Agreement may be terminated by either EQT or Range if (i) any injunction or other law has been issued, entered, enacted, promulgated or become effective, permanently restraining, enjoining or otherwise prohibiting or making illegal the consummation of the Merger and such injunction or other law has become final and nonappealable, (ii) the Range Stockholders’ Meeting has concluded and the Range Shareholder Approval has not been obtained or (iii) the EQT Shareholders’ Meeting has concluded and the EQT Shareholder Approval has not been obtained. The Merger Agreement also contains provisions granting EQT the right to terminate the Merger Agreement for certain reasons, including (i) if Range has breached or failed to perform its representations, warranties, covenants or other agreements set forth in the Merger Agreement, which breach or failure (a) would give rise to a failure of certain of the conditions to EQT’s obligations to consummate the Merger and (b) is incapable of being cured prior to the End Date or Range is not diligently attempting to cure such breach or failure, and (ii) prior to the receipt of the Range Shareholder Approval, if Range’s board of directors changes its recommendation that Range stockholders adopt the Merger Agreement or fails to make or reaffirm such recommendation in certain circumstances, or if Range willfully breaches its obligations under the covenant in a manner that materially impedes, interferes with or hinders the consummation of the Transactions prior to the End Date. Likewise, Range has the right to terminate the Merger Agreement for certain reasons, including (1) if EQT has breached or failed to perform its representations, warranties, covenants or agreements set forth in the Merger Agreement, which breach or failure (A) would give rise to a failure of certain of the conditions to Range’s obligations to consummate the Merger and (B) is incapable of being cured prior to the End Date or EQT is not diligently attempting to cure such breach or failure, and (2) prior to the receipt of the EQT Shareholder Approval, if the EQT Board changes its recommendation that EQT shareholders approve the Share Issuance or fails to make or reaffirm such recommendation in certain circumstances, or if EQT willfully breaches its obligations under the covenant in a manner that materially impedes, interferes with or hinders the consummation of the Transactions prior to the End Date. Upon termination of the Merger Agreement under certain circumstances specified therein, (i) Range will be required to pay EQT a termination fee equal to $423 million, and (ii) EQT will be required to pay Range a termination fee equal to $1,148 million. 2 Treatment of Range Equity Awards At the Effective Time, (i) each outstanding Range restricted stock unit award subject solely to vesting (other than any Range restricted stock unit award or performance share unit award that vests solely as a result of the Closing pursuant to its terms (each, a “ Award”)) will be assumed by EQT and converted into a restricted stock unit award with respect to a number of shares of EQT Common Stock determined based upon the Exchange Ratio, (ii) each outstanding Range performance share unit award (other than any Award) will be deemed earned at the greater of (x) actual performance determined as of the date of Closing (or a date reasonably proximate thereto for administrative practicability) and (y) the target level of performance (the “Earned PSUs”) and be assumed by EQT and converted into a restricted stock unit award with respect to a number of shares of EQT Common Stock determined based upon the Exchange Ratio, which will vest based on continued employment through the end of the originally applicable performance period, (iii) each outstanding Award will vest and be cancelled and converted into the right to receive a number of shares of EQT Common Stock determined based upon the Exchange Ratio (with any performance share unit award deemed earned on the same basis as the Earned PSUs) and all accumulated dividend equivalents thereon, subject to the holder’s execution and delivery of a release of claims, and (iv) each outstanding deferred unit in respect of a share of Range Common Stock will be assumed by EQT and converted into a deferred unit in respect of a number of shares of EQT Common Stock determined based upon the Exchange Ratio. Assumed awards will otherwise generally remain subject to the same terms and conditions that applied before the Effective Time.
