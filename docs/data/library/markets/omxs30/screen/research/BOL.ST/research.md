## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Boliden AB is a Nordic integrated miner–smelter (zinc, copper, nickel, precious metals) that the quantitative screen rates as a **buy** (12/22 models; composite 66%; P/E 11.7, P/B 1.9, yield 2.1%, ROE 16.8%, D/E 28%). The value case rests on mid-cycle earnings power at a modest multiple, a balance sheet that screens as conservative, and a dividend supported by recovering cash generation after the 2023 trough. The central debate is whether current earnings reflect sustainable mid-cycle conditions or a cyclical peak, and whether capital allocation—heavy capex, a large 2025 acquisition, and possible Nexa M&A—will deliver adequate free-cash-flow yield (screen FCF yield ~1.1%, failing the FCF model). **Primary regulatory filings are absent from the research pack** (empty `filings_index.json`; no body extracts), so this memo relies on Yahoo financials and news for all figures.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Boliden offers a classic cyclical-quality compounder at a discount to many global peers: cheapness screens pass (Graham Enterprising, Earnings Yield, Low P/E + High Yield, Lynch/Neff PEG), quality screens pass (Quality Value, Buffett Quality, Magic Formula), and risk screens pass (Financial Health; D/E ~28%, current ratio ~1.7). The business model—owning mines and smelters across the Nordics and Europe—provides vertical integration and exposure to electrification metals (copper, nickel) without pure-exploration risk.

Yahoo data (fallback; no filing verification) show earnings recovering from the 2023 low: revenue rose from SEK 78.6bn (2023) to SEK 93.5bn (2025), operating income from SEK 7.8bn to SEK 12.8bn, and free cash flow from SEK −3.3bn (2023) to SEK 5.2bn (2025). Reported net income of SEK 9.4bn (2025) is below the 2022 peak (SEK 12.4bn) but well above 2023 (SEK 6.1bn). Normalised earnings (Yahoo) were SEK 9.34bn in 2025 versus SEK 7.35bn in 2024, suggesting underlying profit improved even as reported 2024 was inflated by SEK 3.34bn unusual items.

The screen’s dividend and GARP families align with shareholder returns: AGM news cites a SEK 11.00 per share dividend (May 2026), and statutory earnings growth ~2.9% supports a modest growth overlay at ~12× earnings. Failures on FCF Yield, Economic Moat (ROE 16.8% vs 18% hurdle), and Piotroski F-Score (5/9) flag that cash conversion and momentum are not pristine—appropriate caveats for a capital-intensive cyclical, not automatic disqualifiers for a value entry.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical exposure:** Revenue and margins swung sharply between 2022 and 2023 (Yahoo: operating income −49%). Base-metal and treatment-charge cycles can reverse quickly; screen EV/EBIT ~13.1 narrowly fails the Acquirer’s Multiple hurdle.

**Operational disruption:** *“Garpenberg Seismic Halt Tests Boliden Production Reliability And Earnings Story”* (21 Mar 2026) reported full evacuation and production halt at Garpenberg pending safety inspections, with no restart timeline—direct hit to a key zinc asset and a recurring theme in Q1/Q2 2026 call summaries.

**Capital allocation and M&A:** 2025 Yahoo cash flow shows SEK 14.4bn acquisition spend plus equity issuance. *“Nexa Resources (NEXA) Confirms Boliden Takeover Talks With Votorantim Involved”* (3 Jul 2026) flags ongoing, non-binding discussions—integration, price, and leverage risk if a deal completes.

**Leverage trend:** Net debt rose SEK 5.2bn in 2025 to SEK 14.2bn; total debt SEK 23.8bn. Not alarming at current equity, but rising alongside capex and M&A.

**Environmental and closure liabilities:** SEK 13.4bn long-term provisions and SEK 1.17bn pension obligations (Yahoo 2025 balance sheet) are material; without annual-report text, contingent liabilities, covenants, and going-concern language cannot be assessed.

**Governance / disclosure gap:** Zero indexed Swedish/Euro regulatory filings despite OMXS30 listing limits audit of risk disclosures, related-party transactions, and segment commentary.

**Screen-specific flags:** Piotroski F-Score 5/9 (ROA not improving, leverage not declining, share dilution in 2025); FCF yield below threshold; ROE below moat hurdle.
