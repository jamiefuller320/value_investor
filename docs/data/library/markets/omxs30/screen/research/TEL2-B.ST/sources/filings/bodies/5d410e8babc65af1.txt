Tele2 reports third 
quarter 2026 results – 
record-high cash flow 
Stockholm – Tele2 AB (“Tele2”) (Nasdaq Stockholm: TEL2 A and TEL2 B) today 
announces its consolidated results for the third quarter 2026, including record-high 
equity free cash flow in the quarter and over the last twelve months. Full year 
guidance has been updated. 
Highlights 
• End-user service revenue of SEK 5.7 billion increased by 2% organically 
compared to Q3 2025 driven by growth across all operations. Total revenue of 
SEK 7.6 billion increased by 2% organically compared to Q3 2025. 
• Underlying EBITDAaL of SEK 3.1 billion increased by 3% organically compared 
to Q3 2025 driven by end-user service revenue growth and continued sharp 
cost control across all operations. Reported growth was flat due to the impact 
of the Baltic tower transaction. 
• Q3 2026 profit after financial items (EBT) of SEK 1.8 (1.6) billion. 
• Net profit from total operations of SEK 1.4 (1.3) billion and earnings per share of 
SEK 2.03 (1.86) in Q3 2026. 
• Equity free cash flow of SEK 2.3 (1.8) billion in Q3 2026. Over the last twelve 
months, SEK 6.7 billion has been generated, equivalent to SEK 9.69 (8.99) per 
share. 
• Full year 2026 guidance updated: Underlying EBITDAaL narrowed to 4-5% 
organic growth (previously low to mid-single-digit); capex to sales reduced to 
below 10% (previously 10-11%). 
• Tele2 has the most satisfied mobile consumer customers among the main 
brands, according to the Swedish Quality Index (SKI). 
• Tele2’s network selected as part of Sweden’s new emergency communications 
network (SWEN), with the highest quality score. 
• Vahdat Zehtab appointed EVP Chief Commercial Officer. 
Comment from Nicholas Högberg, President and Group CEO of Tele2: 
“We are delivering in line with our guidance for end-user service revenue, and we are 
comfortable with narrowing the full-year underlying EBITDAaL guidance. We remain 
disciplined with investments, prioritise what genuinely improves the customer
experience and scrutinise every contract and every single cost. This has allowed us to 
keep growing while offsetting the cost impact of geopolitical developments.  
“Every day I see very strong momentum across the company, in engagement, 
innovation and performance. That gives me confidence that we are on the right track, 
doing the right things and turning strategy and ideas into action at an accelerating 
pace.”

About Tele2  
Tele2 is a Nordic and Baltic telecom operator built on a strong challenger culture. 
Since 1993, Tele2 has challenged established norms in the telecom industry by 
making connectivity simpler, smarter and more cost efficient for customers. Tele2 
provides mobile, fixed connectivity, TV and streaming services, as well as IoT 
solutions, to consumers and businesses across the Nordic and Baltic region. Tele2 is 
listed on Nasdaq Stockholm. In 2025, Tele2 generated revenue of SEK 29.9 billion. For 
the latest news and definitions of measures, visit tele2.com.
