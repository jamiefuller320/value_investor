For a value investor, the thesis is **yield plus FCF conversion at a reasonable P/E**, not asset backing or growth at a reasonable price. Conviction on the screen is building (59%, stable, two weeks at signal) with full data quality (20/20 metrics).

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
1. **Leverage and liquidity:** D/E 135%; current ratio 0.57; net debt SEK 24.2bn. Screen failures on Graham Enterprising, Financial Health, and Economic Moat cite excessive leverage and weak liquidity. Telco capital structures often tolerate this, but rising rates or tighter credit markets would pressure dividends and buybacks. No covenant or going-concern language available from filing bodies.

2. **Competitive/regulatory:** Nordic mobile markets are concentrated but price-sensitive; news (Q2 2026 call, 16 Jul 2026) flags competitive pressures. Spectrum, wholesale, and consumer-protection regulation can compress ARPU and capex returns — typical telco risk, not quantified in the screen.

3. **Low growth / Lynch PEG failure:** Statutory earnings growth ~1.2%; PEG ~9.5. Revenue growth ~1% in 2025. The Strong Buy case depends on **yield and FCF**, not re-rating on growth.

4. **Intangible-heavy balance sheet:** P/B 4.0 with negative tangible equity; goodwill SEK 29.2bn. Impairment or accelerated amortisation would hit reported equity and ratios.

5. **Restructuring charges:** Recurring restructuring/M&A items (SEK 254m–476m annually per Yahoo) suggest ongoing portfolio and cost programmes — monitor for escalation.

6. **Research coverage gap:** Zero primary filings in the pack limits visibility into pensions, contingencies, segment profitability, and Swedish-specific disclosures.
