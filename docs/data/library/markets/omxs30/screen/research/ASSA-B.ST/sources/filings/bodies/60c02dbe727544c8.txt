Comments by the President and CEO 
 
I am happy to report another record strong quarter for ASSA ABLOY in which our sales grew 
by 7%, driven by 4% organic sales growth, 2% net acquired growth and 1% from currency 
movements. This is the seventh consecutive quarter with organic sales growth despite the 
geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainties we have seen in the last couple of years. The 
good organic sales growth contributed to a record-high operating margin and for the first  
time our operating profit exceeds SEK 7bn in a quarter! 
 
Organic sales growth was strong in the Americas and EMEIA divisions – 6% and 5% 
respectively. Americas growth was driven by continued strong growth in the non-residential 
segment and Latin America. The North America Residential segment improved and had good 
sales growth. In EMEIA, the growth was driven by very strong sales growth in Central Europe, 
while the other regions had good or stable sales growth developments. Global Technologies 
grew by 4%, with strong sales growth in HID, while Global Solutions’ growth was low single-
digit due to temporarily lower deliveries in the retail business unit. Entrance Systems reported 
good organic growth of 3%, driven by Pedestrian and Perimeter Security. Organic sales in Asia 
Pacific declined 1% due to weak sales in Greater China.  
 
The operating profit (EBIT) totaled SEK 7,102 M, up 11% vs. last year. The operating margin 
improved by 60 bps year-on-year to 17.4% (16.8) driven by excellent operating leverage of 
47% due to price realization and cost efficiencies. The operating cash flow improved by 6% 
year-on-year to SEK 7,405 M, resulting in an excellent cash conversion of 117%.  
 
Our people make the difference  
Our decentralized organization and commitment to innovation remain two of our key 
competitive advantages. However, the foundation of everything we do is our 65,000 people. 
Their dedication, expertise and curiosity drive our success. As more of our products and 
solutions become electromechanical, our technology platforms create new opportunities to 
realize synergies. To continue to lead this transition, we continue to invest in our people. Over 
the last eight years, the number of internal applications for each open position has almost 
tripled, demonstrating the strength of our culture and the career opportunities available 
within ASSA ABLOY. Developing talent remains a strategic priority. Through leadership 
development programs, continuous learning opportunities and increased cross-divisional 
collaboration, we are building the capabilities to support future growth. Our "Together We" 
culture is founded on empowerment, innovation and integrity. This supports our ambition to 
be a world-leading organization, knowing that when our people succeed, our business 
succeeds too.  
 
Our acquisition strategy also brings talented people into the Group, adding fresh perspectives, 
new ideas and entrepreneurship. We are proud that many founders of the companies we 
acquire choose to remain with ASSA ABLOY.  During the quarter, we acquired three 
businesses. We also announced the signing of Gunnebo Entrance Control, which will 
strengthen and complement our existing offering in pedestrian access solutions.  
 
We are proud of what we have accomplished in the first nine months and are confident in our 
ability to deliver another strong year. At the same time, we remain focused on the 
opportunities ahead. To ensure that ASSA ABLOY remains long-term competitive, we will also 
launch our 11th manufacturing footprint program during the fourth quarter.  
 
Thank you for your continued trust in ASSA ABLOY. 
 
Nico Delvaux 
President and CEO

Third quarter 
 
The Group’s sales totaled SEK 40,771 M (38,146), an increase of 7% over last year. Organic growth amounted to 4% (3). Net 
growth from acquisitions and divestments was 2% (5), of which 3% (6) were acquisitions. Exchange rates affected sales by  
1% (–6). 
 
The Group’s operating income before amortizations from acquisitions2 (EBITA) amounted to SEK 7,612 M (6,815). The 
corresponding EBITA margin was 18.7% (17.9). The Group’s operating income2 (EBIT) totaled SEK 7,102 M (6,416), an increase  
of 11%. The corresponding operating margin was 17.4% (16.8). Exchange rates had an impact of SEK 2 M (–476) on EBIT.   
 
Net financial items amounted to SEK –768 M (–853). The Group’s income before tax2 totaled SEK 6,333 M (5,563). Exchange 
rates had an impact of SEK 40 M (–444) on income before tax2. The corresponding profit margin was 15.5% (14.6). The estimated 
effective income tax rate, on an annualized basis and excluding items affecting comparability, is 25.5% (25.3 for full-year 2025). 
Earnings per share before and after dilution2 amounted to SEK 4.24 (3.73), an increase of 14% over last year.  
 
Operating cash flow was record strong for a third quarter and totaled SEK 7,405 M (6,969), which corresponds to a cash 
conversion2 of 1.17 (1.25). The net debt/equity ratio totaled 0.54 (0.63) at the end of the quarter. Financial net debt totaled SEK 
64,212 M (64,277). New bonds were issued during the quarter for a total value of SEK 849 M, while SEK 6,984 M in long-term 
loans were repaid. The Group’s long-term borrowing totaled SEK 48,560 M (46,553), while short-term borrowing totaled SEK 
10,330 M (12,185). 
 
First nine months of the year 
 
The Group’s sales for the first nine months of 2026 totaled SEK 115,781 M (114,102), an increase of 1% over last year. Organic 
growth amounted to 3% (3). Net growth from acquisitions and divestments was 2% (5), of which 2% (6) were acquisitions. 
Exchange rates affected sales by –4% (–5). 
 
The Group’s operating income before amortizations from acquisitions2 (EBITA) amounted to SEK 20,561 M (19,421). The 
corresponding EBITA margin was 17.8% (17.0). The Group’s operating income2 (EBIT) totaled SEK 19,242 M (18,216), an increase  
of 6% over last year. The corresponding operating margin was 16.6% (16.0).   
 
Net financial items amounted to SEK –2,225 M (–2,568). The Group’s income before tax2 totaled SEK 17,018 M (15,648). The 
corresponding profit margin was 14.7% (13.7). Earnings per share before and after dilution2 amounted to SEK 11.41 (10.49), an 
increase of 9%. Operating cash flow totaled SEK 16,847 M (14,845), which corresponds to a cash conversion2 of 0.99 (0.95). New 
bonds were issued during the interim period for a total value of SEK 6,794 M, while SEK 10,107 M in long-term loans were repaid. 
 
Restructuring measures 
 
Restructuring payments totaled SEK 168 M (158) for the quarter. The programs proceeded according to plan. At the end of the 
quarter, provisions of SEK 248 M remained in the balance sheet for carrying out these programs. 
 
A new Manufacturing Footprint Program will be launched during the fourth quarter of 2026. Similar to previous programs, 
closure of factories, warehouses and offices in the restructuring program will take place over a period of around two years.

Quarterly comments by division 
 
Opening Solutions EMEIA 
 
Sales for the quarter in EMEIA totaled SEK 6,727 M (6,325), with an organic sales growth of 5% (4). Organic sales growth was very 
strong in Central Europe, and good in both the Nordics and Middle East/India/Africa. In South Europe, organic sales growth was 
small, while it was stable in UK/Ireland. Sales growth from acquisitions was 1%. Operating income excluding items affecting 
comparability totaled SEK 1,062 M (951), which represents an operating margin (EBIT) of 15.8% (15.0). Return on capital 
employed, on an annualized basis and excluding items affecting comparability, totaled 16.7% (15.3). Operating cash flow before 
non-cash items and interest paid totaled SEK 1,153 M (1,179). 
 
Opening Solutions Americas 
 
Sales for the quarter in the Americas totaled SEK 11,831 M (10,840), with an organic sales growth of 6% (3). Organic sales growth 
was strong in the North America Non-Residential segment and good in both Latin America and the North America Residential 
segment. Net sales growth from acquisitions and divestments was 2%. Operating income excluding items affecting 
comparability totaled SEK 2,314 M (2,003), which represents an operating margin (EBIT) of 19.6% (18.5). Return on capital 
employed, on an annualized basis and excluding items affecting comparability, totaled 13.7% (12.6). Operating cash flow before 
non-cash items and interest paid totaled SEK 3,831 M (2,679). 
 
Opening Solutions Asia Pacific 
 
Sales for the quarter in Asia Pacific totaled SEK 2,214 M (2,119), with an organic sales growth of –1% (–4). The organic sales 
growth was small in Pacific & Northeast Asia, but it declined in Greater China & Southeast Asia. Sales growth from acquisitions 
was 2%. Operating income excluding items affecting comparability totaled SEK 217 M (217), which represents an operating 
margin (EBIT) of 9.8% (10.2). Return on capital employed, on an annualized basis and excluding items affecting comparability, 
totaled 6.8% (6.2). Operating cash flow before non-cash items and interest paid totaled SEK 272 M (318). 
 
Global Technologies 
 
Sales for the quarter in Global Technologies totaled SEK 7,104 M (6,729), with an organic sales growth of 4% (3). Organic sales 
growth was strong in HID and good in Global Solutions. Net sales growth from acquisitions and divestments was 1%. Operating 
income excluding items affecting comparability totaled SEK 1,421 M (1,330), which represents an operating margin (EBIT) of 
20.0% (19.8). Return on capital employed, on an annualized basis and excluding items affecting comparability, totaled 15.3% 
(14.7). Operating cash flow before non-cash items and interest paid totaled SEK 1,360 M (1,659). 
 
Entrance Systems 
 
Sales for the quarter in Entrance Systems totaled SEK 13,502 M (12,595), with an organic sales growth of 3% (4). Organic sales 
growth was strong in Pedestrian and Perimeter Security, small in Doors & Automation and declined in the Industrial segment. 
Sales growth from acquisitions was 4%. Operating income excluding items affecting comparability totaled SEK 2,302 M (2,187), 
which represents an operating margin (EBIT) of 17.1% (17.4). Return on capital employed, on an annualized basis and excluding 
items affecting comparability, totaled 18.6% (19.4). Operating cash flow before non-cash items and interest paid totaled SEK 
1,844 M (2,336).

Acquisitions and divestments 
 
Acquisitions 
 
Three acquisitions were completed during the third quarter of 2026. The combined acquisition price for all businesses acquired 
during the first nine months of 2026, including adjustments from prior-year acquisitions, totaled SEK 5,143 M. The 
corresponding acquisition price on a cash- and debt-free basis totaled SEK 5,876 M. Preliminary acquisition analyses indicate 
that goodwill and other intangible assets with indefinite useful life totaled SEK 4,256 M. Estimated deferred considerations for 
current year acquisitions were SEK 638 M. 
 
On 16 September, 2026, it was announced that ASSA ABLOY had acquired Pacific Lock Company, a US padlock and security 
hardware manufacturer. Sales for 2025 amounted to about SEK 155 M. 
 
On 3 August, 2026, it was announced that ASSA ABLOY had signed an agreement to acquire Gunnebo Entrance Control, an 
international provider of entrance control solutions, including speed gates, mass transit gates and security revolving doors.  
The business is headquartered in Maresfield, UK, with manufacturing sites across Europe, the US and Asia. Sales for 2025 
amounted to about SEK 1,670 M. The acquisition is subject to regulatory approval and customary closing conditions and is 
expected to close during the fourth quarter of 2026. 
 
On 24 July, 2026, it was announced that ASSA ABLOY had acquired Classic Brass, a US manufacturer of premium cabinet and 
door hardware. Sales for 2025 amounted to about SEK 155 M. 
 
Divestments 
 
ASSA ABLOY divested Flatley Dock Systems, within the Entrance Systems division, during the third quarter. Annual sales in 
2025 totaled about SEK 20 M. The divestment result was immaterial. 
 
Sustainable development 
 
ASSA ABLOY continues to make good progress toward its science-based climate targets and its long-term commitment to 
reducing carbon emissions across the value chain by 2030. Scope 3 remains a key priority, as it accounts for approximately  
70% of the Group’s total carbon footprint. To accelerate progress in this area, many of our colleagues participated in dedicated 
training programs during the quarter, focusing on identifying and implementing practical opportunities to reduce emissions 
throughout our supply chain. Building awareness, capability, and engagement across the organization is an important step in 
delivering our climate ambitions and supporting our customers on their own sustainability journeys. 
 
Innovation also remains at the heart of our approach to sustainability. In 2016, ASSA ABLOY introduced the Sustainability 
Compass, our in-house developed sustainable innovation framework that has helped guide the development of more 
sustainable products and solutions over the past decade. Building on this successful foundation, we are now in the final stages 
of developing our Sustainability Compass 2.0. The enhanced framework places a stronger emphasis on circular economy 
principles throughout the product development process, while ensuring a clear and tangible sustainability value proposition  
for our customers. Sustainability Compass 2.0 will be launched in early 2027 and will play an important role in supporting our 
Sustainability Program 2030 and science-based target climate commitments.

Risks and uncertainty factors 
 
ASSA ABLOY is an international Group with a wide geographical spread, involving exposure to various forms of strategic, 
operational and financial risks. Strategic risks refer to changes in the business environment with potentially significant effects on 
ASSA ABLOY’s operations and business objectives. Operational risks comprise risks directly attributable to business operations,  
entailing a potential impact on the Group’s financial position and performance, such as risks related to acquisitions of 
companies, as well as legal, environmental, climate, cyber and information security. Financial risks mainly comprise financing, 
currency, interest rate, credit, and risks associated with the Group’s pension obligations.  
 
Risk-taking in itself provides opportunities for continued economic growth, but the risks may also have a negative impact on 
business operations and company goals. It is therefore essential to have a systematic and efficient risk assessment process and 
an effective risk management program in general. The purpose of risk management at ASSA ABLOY is not to avoid risks, but to 
take a controlled approach to identifying, managing and minimizing the effects of these risks. This work is based on an 
assessment of the probability of the risks and their potential impact on the Group.

Definitions of financial performance measures 
Organic growth 
Change in sales for comparable units after adjustments for 
acquisitions, divestments and exchange rate effects. 
 
Operating margin (EBITDA) 
Operating income before depreciation, amortization and 
impairment as a percentage of sales. 
 
Operating margin (EBITA) 
Operating income before amortization and impairment of 
intangible assets recognized in business combinations, as a 
percentage of sales. 
 
Operating margin (EBIT) 
Operating income as a percentage of sales. 
 
Profit margin (EBT) 
Income before tax as a percentage of sales. 
 
Items affecting comparability 
Restructuring costs and significant non-recurring operating 
expenses such as revaluation of previously owned shares in 
associates, revaluation of inventory in business combinations 
and goodwill impairment. 
 
Operating cash flow 
Cash Flow from operating activities excluding restructuring 
payments and tax paid on income minus net capital 
expenditure and repayment of lease liabilities. 
 
Cash conversion 
Operating cash flow in relation to income before tax excluding 
items affecting comparability. 
 
Net capital expenditure 
Investments in, less disposals of, intangible assets and 
property, plant and equipment. 
 
Depreciation and amortization 
Depreciation, amortization and impairment of intangible 
assets, property, plant and equipment and right-of-use assets. 
 
Capital employed 
Total assets less interest-bearing assets and non-interest-
bearing liabilities including deferred tax liability. 
 
 
Average adjusted capital employed 
Average capital employed excluding restructuring reserves for 
the last twelve months. 
 
Net debt 
Interest-bearing liabilities less interest-bearing assets. See the 
table on net debt for detailed information. 
 
Net debt/EBITDA 
Net debt at the end of the period in relation to EBITDA for the 
last twelve months. 
 
Net debt/Equity ratio 
Net debt in relation to equity. 
 
Equity ratio 
Shareholders' equity as a percentage of total assets. 
 
Shareholders' equity per share 
Equity excluding non-controlling interests in relation to 
number of outstanding shares after any potential dilution. 
 
Return on capital employed 
Operating Income (EBIT), excluding Items Affecting 
Comparability, for the last twelve months as a percentage of 
average adjusted capital employed. 
 
Return on equity 
Net income attributable to parent company's shareholders for 
the last twelve months as a percentage of average equity 
attributable to parent company's shareholders for the same 
period. 
 
Earnings per share before and after dilution 
Net income attributable to parent company's shareholders 
divided by weighted average number of outstanding shares. 
None of the Group's outstanding long-term incentive 
programs are expected to result in significant dilution in the 
future. 
 
Earnings per share before and after dilution and excluding 
items affecting comparability 
Net income attributable to parent company's shareholders 
excluding items affecting comparability, net of tax, divided by 
weighted average number of outstanding shares. None of the 
Group's outstanding long-term incentive programs are 
expected to result in significant dilution in the future. 
 
 
To check how the financial measurements have been calculated for current and earlier periods, refer to the tabulated figures in this
