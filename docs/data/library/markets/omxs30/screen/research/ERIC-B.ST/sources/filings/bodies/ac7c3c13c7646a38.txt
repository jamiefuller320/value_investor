Third quarter report 2026
Strategic highlights – executing well in a challenging environment
– Solid customer demand in strategic markets, combined with near-term commercial actions, supported organic growth of 7%*.
– Customer wins and deployments across network modernization, differentiated connectivity and mission-critical networks.
– Driving portfolio optimization, pricing and efficiency measures to help offset higher component costs and to strengthen competitiveness.
 
Financial highlights – sales growth and cost discipline
– Reported sales were SEK 59.5 (56.2) b. Organic* sales increased by 7%* YoY primarily driven by higher sales in Networks. Organic* sales 
grew in three out of four market areas.
– Adjusted1 gross income increased to SEK 28.4 (27.0) b., driven by Mobile Networks. Reported gross income was SEK 28.0 (26.8) b.
– Adjusted1 gross margin was 47.7% (48.1%). Networks margin increased, supported by retroactive IPR revenues. Enterprise margin was 
lower, due to the divestment of iconectiv. Cloud Software and Services margin was stable. Reported gross margin was 47.1% (47.6%).
– Adjusted1 EBITA was SEK 10.1 (15.8) b. with a 17.0% (28.1%) margin. Excluding the prior-year period capital gain of SEK 7.6 b., adjusted 
EBITA increased. Operating expenses decreased YoY. Reported EBITA was SEK 9.2 (15.5) b., with a 15.5% (27.6%) margin.
– Net income was SEK 5.8 (11.3) b. and diluted EPS was SEK 1.77 (3.33). Prior-year EPS included a SEK 1.72 per share divestment gain.
– Free cash flow before M&A was SEK 4.9 (6.6) b. 
– Capital returns to shareholders were SEK 4.8 b. of share repurchases.

Net sales
Reported sales increased by 6% YoY to SEK 59.5 (56.2) b. Networks 
sales increased by 9% to SEK 38.7 b. Cloud Software and Services 
sales increased by 3% to SEK 15.8 b. Enterprise sales declined by 
-10% to SEK 4.6 b., reflecting the divestment of iconectiv in Q3 2025. 
Sales in segment Other were stable at SEK 0.4 b.
Organic sales increased by 7%* YoY primarily driven by increased 
Networks sales. Sales grew in market areas North East Asia, South 
East Asia, Oceania and India and in market area Europe, Middle East 
and Africa. Growth was partly offset by a -1%* decline in market 
area Americas, reflecting lower sales in Latin America. Networks 
sales increased by 9%* with growth in three of the four market areas. 
Cloud Software and Services sales grew by 3%*, with growth in three 
of the four market areas. Sales in segment Enterprise increased by 
1%*.
IPR licensing revenues increased to SEK 3.9 (3.1) b. The increase 
was partly driven by retroactive revenue related to previously 
unlicensed periods. 82% of IPR licensing revenues are reported in 
segment Networks, with the remainder in Cloud Software and 
Services. 
Gross income and margin
Gross margin declined slightly to 47.1% (47.6%), reflecting higher 
restructuring charges and the impact of the divestment of iconectiv. 
Gross income increased to SEK 28.0 (26.8) b. Higher gross income 
in Networks and in Cloud Software and Services was partly offset by 
lower gross income in Enterprise following the divestment of 
iconectiv. Gross margin and gross income benefited from higher IPR 
licensing revenues, in part reflecting retroactive revenue related to 
previously unlicensed periods.
Adjusted gross income increased to SEK 28.4 (27.0) b., with a 
margin of 47.7% (48.1%). 
Research and development (R&D) expenses
R&D expenses increased to SEK -11.8 (-11.5) b., reflecting higher 
restructuring charges of SEK -0.3 (0.0) b. Cost-reduction actions 
offset salary increases. 
Selling and administrative (SG&A) expenses
SG&A expenses were SEK -7.4 (-7.9) b., including restructuring 
charges of SEK -0.3 (-0.1) b. SG&A expenses declined as a result of 
cost-reduction actions, with the largest reduction in Enterprise.
Other operating income and expenses
Other operating income and expenses were SEK 0.1 (7.7) b. The 
prior-year period included a SEK 7.6 b. capital gain from the 
divestment of iconectiv.
Restructuring charges
Restructuring charges were SEK -0.9 (-0.3) b. Gross income included 
SEK -0.4 (-0.3) b. of restructuring charges, while operating expenses 
included SEK -0.5 (0.0) b. of restructuring charges.

EBITA
EBITA decreased to SEK 9.2 (15.5) b. reflecting a SEK 7.6 b. capital 
gain in the prior-year period as well as higher restructuring charges. 
Profitability improved excluding the capital gain. Gross income 
improved by SEK 1.2 b. while operating expenses decreased by SEK 
0.2 b. YoY. The EBITA margin was 15.5% (27.6%).
Adjusted EBITA decreased to SEK 10.1 (15.8) b. reflecting the 
capital gain in the prior-year period. The adjusted EBITA margin was 
17.0% (28.1%).
EBIT 
EBIT decreased to SEK 8.9 (15.2) b., reflecting the capital gain in the 
prior-year period. The EBIT margin was 14.9% (26.9%). 
Amortization was stable at SEK -0.4 (-0.4) b. 
Adjusted EBIT decreased to SEK 9.8 (15.5) b. reflecting the 
capital gain in the prior-year period. The adjusted EBIT margin was 
16.4% (27.5%). 
Financial income and expenses, net
Financial income and expenses were SEK -0.9 (-0.2) b. Financial net 
was negatively affected by provisions related to interest expenses 
associated with the resolution of tax matters and a currency hedge 
effect of SEK -0.2 (-0.1) b.
Income tax
Taxes were SEK -2.2 (-3.6) b. A tax rate of 28% is expected for the 
full year, mainly reflecting the impact of elevated restructuring costs 
and provisions for resolutions of tax matters. The effective tax rate in 
2025 was 25%.
Net income
Net income decreased to SEK 5.8 (11.3) b. Diluted EPS was 
SEK 1.77 (3.33).
Employees 
The number of employees on September 30, 2026, was 85,417 
compared with 86,536 on June 30, 2026. On September 30, 2025, 
the number of employees was 89,898.

North America in the prior-year period. IPR licensing revenues

from higher revenues related to previously unlicensed periods.

divestment of iconectiv.

10.7% (17.3%), impacted by lower net sales, increased restructuring 
charges, the divestment of iconectiv, and a negative currency impact 
of SEK -2.9 b. The prior-year period included a SEK 7.6 b. capital 
gain from the divestment of iconectiv. Adjusted EBITA decreased to 
SEK 22.6 (30.2) b., with a margin of 14.0% (18.0%).  
EBIT decreased to SEK 16.2 (27.5) b., with a margin of 
10.0% (16.4%). Adjusted EBIT was SEK 21.5 (28.7) b., with a margin 
of 13.3% (17.2%). Amortization of intangible assets was 
SEK -1.1 (-1.5) b. 
Net income decreased to SEK 10.8 (20.1) b. including 
restructuring charges of SEK -5.3 (-1.2) b. Diluted EPS decreased to 
SEK 3.24 (5.94).

Accounting policies for the Group
This condensed consolidated interim financial report for the 
reporting period ended September 30, 2026, has been prepared in 
accordance with International Accounting Standard IAS 34 “Interim 
Financial Reporting”. The term “IFRS Accounting Standards” used in 
this document refers to IFRS® Accounting standards as issued by the 
International Accounting Standards Board (IASB) as well as 
interpretations of these standards as issued by IASB’s Standards 
Interpretation Committee (SIC) and IFRS Interpretations Committee 
(IFRIC). The accounting policies adopted are consistent with those 
of the annual report for the year ended December 31, 2025, and 
should be read in conjunction with that annual report. Amendments 
to IFRS Accounting Standards that became effective during 2026 do 
not have a material impact on the result and financial position of the 
Company.
New accounting standards and interpretations
IASB has issued the following new standard with effective date of 
January 1, 2027:
– In April 2024, IASB issued a new standard, IFRS 18 that will 
replace IAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements. The standard 
sets out the requirements for the presentation and disclosure of 
information in the financial statements to ensure better 
comparability, consistency and faithful representation of an entity’s 
assets, liabilities, equity, income, and expenses. The new standard’s 
biggest impact is on the statement of profit or loss (income 
statement), where it includes more specific guidance on how the 
statement of profit or loss shall be presented mandating certain 
income and expense classification and subtotals to be presented.
– Impact at transition: The standard is effective for the annual 
periods beginning on or after January 1, 2027. The Company will 
apply the new standard as from January 1, 2027. At transition, the 
Company will apply the new presentation and disclosure 
requirements retrospectively for all periods presented. As the 
standard only impacts the presentation and disclosure requirements, 
and not the measurement of any items presented in the financial 
statements, there will be no effect on retained earnings at transition 
date. The largest effect is on the presentation of the income 
statement where certain items such as foreign exchange differences 
will be classified differently within the income statement. In addition, 
there will be new subtotals introduced such as Operating profit. 
Accordingly, the information for prior years will be restated. The 
transition note will include a bridge between the IAS 1 presentation 
and the new IFRS 18 presentation when the standard is applied.
– The Company is still assessing the detailed impact that the 
transition to IFRS 18 will have on the financial statements.
