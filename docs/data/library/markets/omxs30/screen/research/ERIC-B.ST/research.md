## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical exposure:** Four-year revenue decline and screen-flagged −10.9% earnings growth point to sustained softness in mobile-network capex; carrier spending cycles can extend beyond current margin recovery.

**Competitive and supply-chain pressure:** News coverage (29 Jul 2026) highlights memory-chip allocation competition from AI hyperscalers as a supply-chain risk for telecom equipment vendors—potentially affecting costs and delivery timelines, distinct from quarter-specific fundamentals.

**Customer concentration:** Network equipment remains concentrated among a handful of global mobile operators and a few large enterprise/government buyers; pricing pressure and project delays at key accounts are structurally under-represented in generic quality screens.

**Earnings quality / accounting:** FY2023’s SEK 32.1bn impairment and FY2025’s SEK 8.3bn securities gain create extreme volatility in reported ROE (26.1% on screen) and net income; value screens passing on cash flow may overstate recurring earnings power. Without filing bodies, impairment triggers, litigation reserves, and revenue-recognition policies cannot be assessed.

**Pension and employee benefits:** Non-current pension obligations of SEK 18.6bn (Yahoo, 2025) remain a meaningful off-balance-sheet-style liability; discount-rate sensitivity is unverified without annual-report note disclosure.

**Governance / regulatory:** Historical industry exposure to corruption investigations in certain markets (not documented in this source pack) and geopolitical restrictions on 5G participation remain sector-level overhangs; no RNS/SEC language on contingencies was available here.

**Liquidity / leverage:** Debt reduction is positive, but current ratio 1.29 (screen) sits below Graham Defensive thresholds; not acute, but less conservative than pure deep-value peers.
