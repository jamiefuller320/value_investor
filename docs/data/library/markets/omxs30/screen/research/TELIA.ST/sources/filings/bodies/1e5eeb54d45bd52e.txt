Third quarter summary 
– Revenue amounted to SEK 21.3 billion (19.9). 
– Service revenue increased 3.3%, like for like , driven mainly by 
strong development in Sweden, Norway and the Baltics.  
– Adjusted EBITDA increased 2.9%, like for like, driven by service rev-
enue growth across most markets coupled with lower operational 
expenses.  
– Operating income increased to SEK 4.4 billion (4.0). 
– Total net income increased to SEK 2.9 billion (2.5) and total EPS 
increased to SEK 0.69 (0.60).  
– CAPEX excluding spectrum, leases and non -organic assets in-
creased to SEK 2.9 billion (2.8). 
– Free cash flow increased to SEK 3.7 billion (2.9) and cash flow from 
operating activities increased to SEK 7.6 billion (7.1). 
– Leverage decreased to 2.00x compared with 2.06x in the previous 
quarter.  
– Dividend of SEK 0.51 per share was paid to shareholders. 
– Outlook 2026 (upgraded): Adjusted EBITDA growth, like for like, of 
3-4% (previously around 3%), Free cash flow of at least SEK 9 bil-
lion (previously around SEK 9 billion), see page 4.  
– Proposed further localization of operations and reduction of ~900 
positions across Telia announced on October 12, targeting SEK 1 
billion in annual gross savings with full effect from 2028. 
Nine months summary 
– Revenue amounted to SEK 62.0 billion (59.7). 
– Service revenue increased 2.8%, like for like, driven by growth in all 
markets, although mainly in Sweden.  
– Adjusted EBITDA increased 3.5%, like for like, driven by service rev-
enue growth across all markets coupled with lower operational ex-
penses.  
– Operating income increased to SEK 11.2 billion (11.0). 
– Free cash flow increased to SEK 7.8 billion (6.9) and cash flow from 
operating activities increased to SEK 20.3 billion (20.1). 
– The acquisition of Bredband2 i Skandinavien AB was completed, 
and the company was consolidated from February.

CEO comment 
“Our business momentum continues, with further improvements in customer satisfaction 
across our footprint. Financial results were somewhat better than anticipated, due to 
solid service revenue growth and lower operational expenses. As a result, we are raising 
our full-year EBITDA growth outlook from around 3% to 3-4%, and our full-year free 
cash flow outlook from around SEK 9 billion to at least SEK 9 billion. We are determined 
to become an even simpler, faster and more efficient company, and after the end of the 
quarter, we announced proposals to localize our operations further. This would 
strengthen the role of our country units in meeting fast-evolving customer needs and 
delivering profitable growth, while supporting annual gross savings of SEK 1 billion. 
 
Commercial progress 
In Sweden, the strong performance of our consumer business contin-
ued, with service revenue growth driven by TV and mobile. The inte-
gration of Bredband2 is progressing well, broadening our position in 
the value-driven fixed broadband sector and creating opportunities for 
cross-selling. Our Fello brand has Sweden’s most satisfied B2C cus-
tomers according to SKI’s latest customer satisfaction survey, with Telia 
topping the B2B rankings for the 20 th time in 22 years. As expected, 
EBITDA growth was lower, partly due to delivery of B2B customer pro-
jects with lower margins, with a rebound expected in Q4. Mission-crit-
ical revenue grew well, and after the end of the quarter, Telia was cho-
sen as the lead p artner and radio access network (RAN) supplier for 
Sweden’s new nationwide emergency communication network.  
In Finland, the consumer mobile sector remains competitive, nega-
tively impacting customer intake. However, consumer fixed broadband 
is growing, supported by our co-owned fiber company Valokuitunen. 
Our healthy momentum in the enterprise sector continues,  both with 
SMEs and large enterprise customers, with Telia rated number one for 
B2B customers in EPSI’s recent  survey. We completed the transfer of 
our Finnish enterprise cloud and capacity services to CGI, along with 
approximately 250 employees, in line with our strengthened focus on 
the core connectivity business. 
In Norway, we continue our turnaround with positive EBITDA growth 
returning in the quarter, primarily driven by price adjustments in fixed 
services. Mobile customer intake strengthened, and EPSI’s survey rec-
ognized Telia’s OneCall brand as having Norway’s most sati sfied B2C 
mobile customers. We have finalized all significant parts of our RAN 
partnership agreement with ice, which we expect to deliver clear cus-
tomer and financial benefits, and we are now focused on concluding 
regulatory discussions ahead of the planned start of operations in early 
2027.  
In Lithuania, mobile and fixed services again performed well, sup-
ported by strong customer demand and price adjustments. Telia has 
been selected to provide critical communications, connectivity and cy-
bersecurity infrastructure for Lithuania’s Presidency of the Council of 
the European Union in 2027, which represents a high -profile proof-
point of our trusted capabilities to secure digital environments. 
Estonia continued to grow both service revenue and EBITDA, mainly 
due to solid performance in mobile. During the quarter, we launched 
an AI-powered customer service assistant that has been well received 
by customers and is already shortening query response times.  
Technology is an important enabler of secure and resilient services for 
our customers and our teams. During the quarter, we strengthened our 
customers’ digital security by launching Enhanced Caller ID and mobile 
DNS firewall services, and we are expanding commercial 5G 
standalone capabilities across our footprint. More than 70% of our 
workforce now use AI in their daily work, helping us identify new sales 
opportunities, deliver more relevant customer offerings, accelerate 
software development and network analysis, and pr ovide faster cus-
tomer support. Satellite -based solutions are also becoming a part of 
our industry ecosystem, which has always been very competitive. Our 
priority is to provide the highest-quality and most reliable services. To 
this end, we are already complementing our terrestrial networks with 
satellite solutions to cater for emerging deman d from enterprise cus-
tomers with specific use cases, while exploring opportunities to create 
additional value as demand develops. 
Sustainability progress 
Telia became the first telecom operator to score 10/10 in Global Child 
Forum’s annual benchmark of how well companies protect and support 
children’s rights. Suppliers representing 64% of spend in Telia’s supply 
chain have now set science-based targets for greenhouse gas emission 
reductions, up 1% in the quarter, and we are actively engaging to in-
crease this proportion further. We also launched the latest Telia Trend 
Report, which shows how our solutions can enable sustainable busi-
ness value for companies and organizations. 
Financial progress 
Service revenue accelerated further in Q3, albeit with some deals ex-
pected in Q4 being brought forward by customers. With operational 
expenses down -2.6%, EBITDA growth increased to 2.9%, compared 
with the ~2% flagged last quarter. This reflects good progress on cost 
and capital discipline, as well as more temporary items including en-
ergy and bad debt. Free cash flow was strong, and driven by growth in 
EBITDA, lower CAPEX and positive working capital contribution. Our 
balance sheet is healthy, with leverage at 2.00x, which is at the lowest 
end of our target range.  
Looking ahead 
We are confident in delivering on our mid-term plan and financial am-
bitions. The progress Telia is making, combined with our upgraded full-
year EBITDA growth and free cash flow outlook, and the proposals for 
further localization and savings presented this month, all reflect our 
commitment to constant improvement. Our strategy continues to de-
liver results, and we are fully focused on execution and on meeting the 
evolving needs of consumers, businesses and societies.” 
 
Patrik Hofbauer

Sweden 
In the quarter, Telia signed an agreement to divest its ACE contact 
center software platform to LeadDesk . A s part of the transaction , a 
long-term strategic partnership was established, under which Telia will 
continue to offer ACE as part of its Enterprise portfolio. After the end 
of the quarter, it was announced that Telia was awarded  the contract 
by the Swedish Civil Defence and Resilience Agency to deliver the ra-
dio access network for the Swedish Emergency Network (SWEN). Fur-
thermore, the latest SKI survey showed that Telia’s Enterprise business 
and Fello have the most satisfied mobile customers in Sweden. 
Mobile postpaid subscriptions, excluding M2M services,  in-
creased by ~6,000, driven by the Consumer segment. Broadband sub-
scribers remained unchanged and demand for Telia’s TV service con-
tinued to be strong, which resulted in the addition of ~20,000 subscrip-
tions. 
Revenue increased 5.8%, like for like, driven mainly by strong ser-
vice revenue growth and to some extent increased sales of equipment. 
Service revenue increased 4.7%, like for like, driven partly by in-
creased mobile revenue, although mainly by increased fixed service 
revenue that benefited from continued strong revenue growth for TV 
and business solutions. In addition , Other service revenue  increased, 
due to continued high  demand for secure and robust mission - and 
business-critical services.
Adjusted EBITDA  margin decreased to 40.7% (43.2), related to 
increased revenues from lower-margin customer projects , and ad-
justed EBITDA increased 2.1%, like for like, driven by service revenue 
growth. 
CAPEX excluding spectrum , leases and non -organic assets  de-
creased to SEK 967 million (1,031).

from business solutions and broadband.

and adjusted EBITDA increased 0.9%, like for like , driven by a lower

Norway 
In the quarter, an agreement was signed for the acquisition of 
around 6,000 fiber customers from GlobalConnect. The transaction is 
expected to be completed in the fourth quarter of 2026. Furthermore, 
Telia Trygg Familie (Safe Family), a security service for consumers that

term agreement was signed with the real estate company Thon Grup-
pen, under which Telia will deliver amongst other around 1,500 mobile

data center was completed. Furthermore, Telia was once again ranked

personal identification numbers, of private customers who have TV or 
broadband services from Telia Norway. The stolen data also includes 
information about corporate customers to whom Telia Norway delivers 
data communication solutions. T his data includes information about 
contact forms with personal information such as name, address, email 
and telephone number of the contact person in the businesses. In addi-
tion, certain information related to current and former employees has 
also been stolen. Telia Company is working closely with the authorities,

authority (Datatilsynet) have initiated reviews of Telia Norway related to 
the data breach. Nkom conducted its review during August 2026, but

potential fines related to the data breach  was recognized in March 
2026. As the authorities’ reviews are still ongoing information remains 
limited and the estimated amount for potential fines is highly uncertain.

ited number of other data breach cases in Norway.

Risks 
Telia Company operates across six geographical markets, offering a 
broad range of products and services spanning telecomm unications 
and IT. These markets are competitive and highly regulated. Telia de-
fines risk as anything that could have a material adverse effect on the 
achievement of the company's goals. Risks can be threats, uncertainties 
or lost opportunities relating to the company's current or future opera-
tions or activities. 
Telia Company has an established Enterprise Risk Management Frame-
work that it uses to regularly identify, analyze, assess and report strate-
gic, operational, financial and compliance risks, and to manage such 
risks as appropriate. The Telia Company Risk Uni verse consists of a 
Principal Risk taxonomy, based on the Principal Risk areas and sub-risk 
areas identified and prioritized with Telia Company’s Group Executive 
Management as the most material risks related to the company's objec-
tives and operations. The Principal Risks are assessed and aggregated 
across the whole company using the Enterprise Risk Management 
Framework. Risk management is an integrated part of Telia Company’s 
business planning process and monitoring of business performance.  
For further information regarding details on risk exposure and risk man-
agement, see the Annual report 2025, Directors Report, section Risks.
