ASX Announcement: PXA 
11 November 2026 
PEXA 1Q27 Trading Update 
 
PEXA Group Limited (ASX: PXA) (“PEXA” or “Group”) has today provided an update on the quarter ended 
30 September 2026 (1Q27) performance of the Group1. 
 
Commenting on the update, Mr Russell Cohen, PEXA CEO and Group Managing Director said: 
 
“Against the backdrop of a significant decline in Australian property transaction market activity, PEXA’s 
Australian Exchange customer satisfaction has continued to remain broadly stable at approximately 90%, 
in line with historical levels, demonstrating our focus on providing outstanding service to our customers 
nationally. In Australia, consecutive interest rate rises, and falling property prices are weighing on buyer 
confidence and transfer volumes.  
 
PEXA’s platform remains resilient and we continue to invest in the strength and security of our network. 
National market penetration remained at 90%, and our coverage continued to expand, with Tasmanian 
volumes increasing during the quarter following the removal of the paper titles from Land Titles Office 
processes from 1 September.  
 
In the UK, NatWest remortgages on PEXA are delivering faster transactions and improved customer 
satisfaction, with Sale and Purchase implementation under way. We expect to process our first Sale and 
Purchase transaction with NatWest in March 2027. 
 
Through this challenging period of lower cyclical volumes, we are maintaining disciplined cost control while 
protecting the integrity of our platform and customer operations, and the strategic direction of the 
business.”

Key Australian business updates include: 
• 
IPART review of ELNO service fees: 
o IPART’s review of ELNO service fees continued during the quarter, with IPART submitting 
its final report to the NSW Premier on 30 October 2026. PEXA awaits the NSW 
Government’s release of the final report.  
o The final report will be made public by 18 November 2026. ARNECC will then review the 
report and commence its process to determine whether any changes to PEXA’s prices are 
required, and if so, to what extent.  
o PEXA will continue to work closely with ARNECC and other relevant stakeholders to 
advocate for an appropriate outcome for customers, shareholders and employees. 
• 
Total national market penetration was 90%, consistent with 2H26. 
• 
PEXA’s national coverage continued to expand during the quarter, with Tasmanian volumes 
increasing incrementally alongside the removal of paper titles from Land Titles Oﬃce processes on 
1 September. PEXA launched remortgage capabilities in Northern Territory in 2025, with transfer 
capabilities scheduled to launch in the week of 9th November 2026. 
• 
Our Exchange customer satisfaction score remained broadly stable, at 89.1% in 1Q27 compared 
with 89.8% in 4Q26. 
• 
Following the commencement of new Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing 
requirements on 1 July 2026, PEXA Clear continued to onboard customers and commenced 
monetisation in Q1. PEXA took a paced onboarding approach to support a reliable customer 
experience and position the platform to scale as active usage grows. 
 
 
Optima Legal remortgage completions grew 13% on the PCP to 15.4k as customers sought to lock in new 
rates ahead of expected interest rate rises, while Smoove remortgage completion volumes fell (18%) to 
7.4k due to a slowdown in volumes received from a panel lender.

About PEXA 
PEXA (Property Exchange Australia) is a world-leading, digital property exchange business, listed on the 
Australian Stock Exchange. Since 2013, PEXA has facilitated more than 28 million property settlements, 
and today, 90% of all property transfer settlements in Australia are processed on the PEXA platform. In 
2022, PEXA commenced its international expansion through entry into the UK digital refinancing market, 
followed by the UK launch of PEXA’s Sale & Purchase capability in 2025.
