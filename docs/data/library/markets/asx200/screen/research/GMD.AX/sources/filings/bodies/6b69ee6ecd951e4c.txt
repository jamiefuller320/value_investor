INDEX ANNOUNCEMENT 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Vault Minerals Limited to be removed from the 
S&P/ASX 200 Index 
 
SYDNEY, NOVEMBER 11, 2026: S&P Dow Jones Indices announced today that it will remove Vault 
Minerals Limited (XASX: VAU) from the S&P/ASX 200 Index, subject to final court approval whereby 
the company will be acquired by Genesis Minerals Limited (XASX: GMD). 
S&P Dow Jones Indices will remove Vault Minerals Limited from the S&P/ASX 200 effective prior to the 
open of trading on Wednesday, November 18, 2026. Vault Minerals Limited will be replaced by Dicker 
Data Limited (XASX: DDR), in the S&P/ASX 200 effective prior to the open of trading on Wednesday, 
November 18, 2026. 
For more information about S&P Dow Jones Indices, please visit www.spglobal.com/spdji.
