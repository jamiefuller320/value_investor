ASX ANNOUNCEMENT 
27 August 2026 
ACCC waiver received for merger with Genesis 
Vault Minerals Limited (ASX: VAU) (Vault) is pleased to provide an update in relation to its proposed merger 
with Genesis Minerals Limited (Genesis) by way of a scheme of arrangement  (Scheme), as announced to ASX 
on 14 July 2026. 
Genesis has confirmed that the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission  (ACCC) has determined 
that the Scheme is not required to be notified to the ACCC, and accordingly that the ACCC approval condition 
to the Scheme has been satisfied. 
The Scheme remains subject to certain other conditions as set out in the Scheme Implementation Deed entered 
into between Vault and Genesis ,1 including an independent expert concluding (and continuing to conclude)  
that the Scheme is in the best interests of Vault shareholders, Vault shareholder approval of the Scheme by the 
requisite majorities, approval by the Court and other customary conditions. 
Vault currently expects to dispatch a scheme booklet containing detailed information in relation to the Scheme 
to Vault shareholders in early October 2026, with a meeting of Vault shareholders expected to be held in late 
October 2026 to consider the Scheme.  
The Vault board continues to unanimously recommend that Vault shareholders vote in favour of the Scheme 
in the absence of a superior proposal 2 and subject to an independent expert concluding (and continuing to 
conclude) that the Scheme is in the best interests of Vault shareholders . Subject to the same qualifications, 
each Vault director intends to vote, or cause to be voted, all Vault shares in which they have a relevant interest 
in favour of the Scheme.  
This announcement was authorised for release to ASX by Luke Tonkin, Managing Director. For more
