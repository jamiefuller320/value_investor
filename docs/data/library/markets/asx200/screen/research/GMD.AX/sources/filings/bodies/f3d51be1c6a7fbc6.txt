PLACEMENT OVERVIEW 
Brightstar Resources Limited (Brightstar or the Company) (ASX: BTR) has received binding commitments 
from institutional and strategic investors to raise A$82 million of new equity (before costs) via a single -
tranche placement ( Placement) of 164 million new fully paid ordinary shares in the Company ( New 
Shares) at an issue price of A$0.50 per New Share ( Offer Price). The Placement was cornerstoned with a 
strategic investment by Genesis Minerals Limited (Genesis) for a total of A$30 million.  
Brightstar’s Managing Director, Alex Rovira, commented: 
“Recent drilling results at Two Mile Hill and progress on the Pre -Feasibility have materially changed the scale of 
Sandstone. In the space of three months , we have doubled the Two Mile Hill -Shillington Mineral Resource to 
1.45Moz, and the deeper Shirvington Zone drilling results demonstrate potentially material upside to the current 
Mineral Resource estimate.  
The true size of Sandstone is quickly emerging , which now warrants a faster and larger work program 
than we scoped at the start of the year. 
This capital raising  allows us to  fast-track the exploration workstreams  to define and enhance what an 
enlarged Sandstone development will look like . The Placement funds increased infill and extensional drilling at 
Two Mile Hill as well as additional drill testing of the multiple Two Mile Hill analogue targets.  
Importantly, the capital raising provides the funding to accelerate mine establishment at Two Mile Hill, with 
Brightstar seeking to execute an ‘early mining’ strategy with the development of the necessary infrastructure and 
mine camp needed to develop a cutback to the existing pit, followed by an underground exploration decline. 
We are delighted to welcome Genesis Minerals as a strategic shareholder. Genesis is a highly regarded ASX100 
Western Australian gold producer with a strategic focus on the Leonora-Laverton District, and its investment is a 
strong endorsement of the quality of our portfolio and our team. 
Importantly, development of the Goldfields Project continues apace. Construction remains on schedule for first 
gold in the June quarter of 2027 and on budget , and the project remains fully funded through the February 
2026 equity raising and the Bond. This Placement is dedicated to accelerating Sandstone and brings forward the 
next leg of growth towards our TARGET200 ambition.” 
STRATEGIC RATIONALE: ACCELERATE SANDSTONE EXPLORATION & DEVELOPMENT 
The Placement is a strategic capital raising to accelerate the Sandstone Gold Project in response to the 
scale of the results delivered at Two Mile Hill in recent months. 
When the Company completed its A$193 million capital raising in February an d March 2026 2, the 
Sandstone work program s were scoped to deliver a Pre -Feasibility Study and Definitive Feasibility Study 
primarily focused on the open pit deposits, with Two Mile Hill -Shillington hosting a previous  Mineral 
Resource of 731koz Au (now doubled to 1.45Moz Au).  
The results delivered by the recent exploration program since then have fundamentally changed the 
scale of the opportunity , with Two Mile Hill rapidly emerging as the cornerstone asset in Sandstone 
with meaningful potential to be a lon g-life underground mine to complement the proposed open pit 
developments.
3 
These recent achievements and developments include: 
• Deep drilling at Two Mile Hill -Shillington returned 305.4m @ 1.82g/t Au , with follow-up 
diamond drilling identif ying the Shirvington Zone  - a depth extension of the mineralised 
tonalite more than 300m wide at approximately 700m below surface 3 4; 
• Infill drilling above the Shirvington Zone returned 225.7m @ 3.11g/t Au from 284m5; 
• The Two Mile Hill-Shillington Mineral Resource Estimate doubled to 1.45Moz @ 1.8g/t Au, with 
Indicated Mineral Resources up 177% to 526koz, lifting the Sandstone Project Mineral Resource 
to 3.6Moz. The updated estimate does not yet incorporate the deeper Shirvington Zone 
drilling1; and 
• The first assays from the deep diamond program returned 305m @ 1.33g/t Au from 584m  in 
TMHDD26003, materially extending Two Mile Hill below the current Mineral Resource, with a 
multi-rig, ~6,500m follow-up diamond drilling program at Two Mile Hill underway6. 
The Placement proceeds allow Brightstar to pursue this opportunity at pace with the accelerated program 
comprising: 
• Infill drilling at the enlarged Two Mile Hill  to support the underground mining study and 
further conversion of the Mineral Resource Estimate ahead of the Definitive Feasibility Study; 
• Extensional drilling at the Shirvington Zone, expanding the ~6,500m follow-up program that 
commenced this week, to define the extent of the tonalite at depth and deliver further Mineral 
Resource Estimate updates; 
• Drill testing of the  multiple, nearby Two Mile Hill analogue targets  identified within the 
Sandstone tenure; 
• Enlarged and accelerated Definitive Feasibility Study drilling across the Sandstone tenure 
package; 
• Immediate progression to a Definitive Feasibility Study  to encapsulate a more 
comprehensive underground mining study of Two Mile Hill being advanced with Entech;  
• Infrastructure and mining establishment, together with the development of a Sandstone 
camp, to s upport the underground development of an exploration decline to de -risk the 
expanded drilling  programs at Two Mile Hill and fast -track future mining operations  at 
Sandstone; and  
• General working capital.

the US$120 million 12.5% senior secured bonds completed in March 2026 ( Bond).
