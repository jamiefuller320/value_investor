Charter Hall Long WALE REIT (ASX: CLW) (CLW or the REIT) provides an update on recently 
completed lease extensions across its portfolio. The extensions with long-term government tenants 
increase CLW’s weighted average lease expiry (WALE) from 9.2 years to 9.4 years1 with stabilised 
portfolio occupancy remaining at 99.9%. 
Lease extension – Department of Defence, Campbell Park, Canberra, ACT 
Charter Hall has agreed a five-year lease extension with the Department of Defence at Campbell Park, 
ACT. The property is fully leased to the Commonwealth Government and forms part of Canberra’s 
Defence Precinct, providing specialised administrative offices for the Department of Defence. CLW 
acquired a 49.9% interest in the property in July 2025, when two years remained on the lease. The 
extension moves the lease expiry from 14 June 2027 to 14 June 2032. Current rent is unchanged until 
the renewal term begins, when it will increase by 3%, followed by annual increases of 3% during the 
extended term. 
Lease extension – Australian Taxation Office, 520 Smollett Street, Albury, NSW 
CLW holds a 50% interest in the A-grade office building at 520 Smollett Street, Albury, NSW. Charter 
Hall has agreed a 10-year lease extension with the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) over levels 1–5 
and part of the ground floor, totalling 8,473 sqm (79% of the building by area). The extension 
commences on expiry of the current lease on 30 September 2027. The ATO has also agreed a 12-
month extension over level 6 (1,690 sqm or 16% by area), extending that lease expiry to 30 September 
2028. Market-related rent and an incentive have been agreed for the renewal. Agents are being 
appointed to lease level 6 following the ATO’s planned vacation of the floor in September 2028. 
Lease extension – Australian Taxation Office, 12–26 Franklin Street, Adelaide, SA 
CLW holds a 50% interest in the A-grade office building at 12–26 Franklin Street, Adelaide, SA. The 
ATO occupies 30,979 sqm of net lettable area, representing 84% of the building by area, under a lease 
expiring on 31 October 2027. The balance of the building is occupied by BHP, Australia Post and other 
government tenants. 
The ATO has advised that it has committed to a new building and will vacate the premises. An 18-
month lease extension has been agreed at the current face rent increased by 3.75%, with no incentive 
payable, extending the tenancy to 30 April 2029. CLW and its co-owner are implementing a re-leasing 
strategy, with agents appointed to lease the property. 
Avi Anger, CLW Fund Manager noted “The lease extensions announced today represent all upcoming 
material lease expiries for CLW in FY27 and FY28.  These lease extensions push out the expiry dates
