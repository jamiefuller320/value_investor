Charter Hall Long WALE REIT (ASX: CLW) notes recent media reports that David Jones has entered 
voluntary administration. David Jones has notified Charter Hall this morning that McGrathNicol has 
been appointed as voluntary administrators and FTI Consulting have been appointed as receivers and 
managers.  
CLW owns a 50% interest in the David Jones Elizabeth Street store in Sydney, which contributes 
approximately 4% of CLW's net property income. 
At this stage, CLW is assessing the implications of the voluntary administration and receivership and 
will engage with the administrators and receivers. CLW holds a bank guarantee in respect of the lease 
equivalent to approximately 12 months' rent. 
There is no change to the FY27 operating earnings guidance or distribution guidance provided in 
CLW's announcement dated 13 August 2026. 
Charter Hall is continuing to assess plans for the property and will provide a further update to the 
market as appropriate.
