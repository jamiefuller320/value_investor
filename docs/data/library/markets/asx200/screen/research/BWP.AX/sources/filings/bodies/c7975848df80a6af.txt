BWP REIT plc ("BWP REIT" or the "Company") Update on valuation BWP REIT Plc (IPSX ticker: BWP) is pleased to announce a quarterly property valuation as at 30 June 2023 by Avison Young which shows an increase in valuation to £60.3 million, compared to their 31 March 2023 valuation of £59.1 million which was published in the Company's interim report for the 6 months to 31 March 2023. BWP REIT Plc is a single asset company which owns Bridgewater Place, an office-led mixed-use property situated in a prime location in central Leeds. NOTES BWP REIT plc is a single asset REIT offering shareholders exposure to the performance of the Bridgewater Place which is an office-led mixed-use property situated in a prime location in central Leeds . The Company's asset manager is M7 Real Estate Limited ("M7"). M7 is a leading specialist in the pan-European, regional, multi-tenanted real estate market. It has over 225 employees in 14 countries and territories.
