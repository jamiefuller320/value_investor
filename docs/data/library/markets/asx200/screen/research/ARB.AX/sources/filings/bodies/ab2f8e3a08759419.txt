First Half
1H FY26 underlying profit before tax of $58.0m declined 16.3% compared with 1H FY25 due to the weaker Australian dollar against the THB and an over recovery of factory
fixed costs in the prior period reflected in higher inventory levels.
Net operating expenses of $143.0m were broadly in line with the prior comparative period despite a high inflationary environment.
Second Half
2H FY26 underlying profit before tax of $60.7m declined 4.3% compared with 2H FY25 as the Australian dollar returned to levels consistent with the prior period.
Net operating expenses of $142.7m increased 2.8% reflecting wage growth and the flow on impact of the conflict in the Middle East.

commenced operations in 2026, with local and

OE sales were $43.4M in FY26, down 27% on FY25.

ARB’s Aftermarket business in Q1 FY27 performed ahead
of new vehicle sales in a challenged economic
environment. Fleet and the FLA business continued to
outperform, the ARB Corporate retail stores showed
growth while the ARB independent stores, dealership
sales and wholesale business declined.
ARB will pursue a more aggressive, product-first
marketing drive for the balance of FY27.
Mixed ARB Export results. Markets such as Africa, Europe
and South-East Asia are achieving growth. However, new
vehicle supply issues continue to impact the UK, and NZ
demand is soft.
The US aftermarket business continued to demonstrate
strong growth in Q1 FY27. However, this growth has been
offset by a decline in OE sales due to the maturity of
current contracts.
Early sales to OEMs indicate strong signs of recovery
which is expected to continue based on OEM projections
for key vehicle platforms.
ARB has been successful in winning new OEM contracts,
including in the USA, which will commence from FY2028.
The Thai baht is hedged through to February 2027 at
rates that compare favourably with the prior year.
