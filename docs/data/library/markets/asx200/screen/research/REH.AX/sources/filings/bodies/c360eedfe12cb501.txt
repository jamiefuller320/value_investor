Trading Update – Q1 FY27   
 
Reece Limited (“Reece” or “the Group”) today announces its first quarter trading update1 for FY27. 
Reece delivered sales revenue of A$2,510 million for the first quarter, up 4.3% on the same period last 
year, and up 8.7% on a constant currency basis2.  
ANZ sales were up 12.8%, driven by continued momentum in volumes and a mid single digit contribution 
from inflation. US sales were up 5.6%, driven by network expansion and low single digit inflation, with 
like for like sales increasing 2.4%.   
Peter Wilson, Chairman & CEO, said: “We have seen a strong Q1 in ANZ as the existing pipeline of 
activity supported ongoing momentum in the region. The US has remained soft, with the subdued 
residential new construction market continuing to impact our business.  
“Looking ahead, we’re expecting a strong first half for ANZ and a continuation of the challenging 
environment in the US. We take a long term view, with a clear plan to deliver our 2030 vision and build a 
successful business in the US.” 
“Given the complexity of the current macro economic environment we remain cautious on the second 
half of FY27.”

About the Reece Group 
Reece Group is a leading distributor of plumbing, waterworks and HVAC-R products to commercial and residential 
customers through ~1000 branches in Australia, New Zealand and the United States. 
Established in 1920 and listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX: REH), Reece Group has approximately 
9,000 employees who are focused on building a better world for our customers by being our best.  
For further information on Reece Group and its portfolio of businesses please visit group.reece.com/au.
