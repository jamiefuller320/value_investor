About the Reece Group 
Reece Group is a leading distributor of plumbing, waterworks and HVAC-R products to commercial and residential 
customers through over 900 branches in Australia, New Zealand and the United States. 
Established in 1920 and listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX: REH), Reece Group has approximately 
9,000 employees who are focused on building a better world for our customers by being our best. 
For further information on Reece Group and its portfolio of businesses please visit group.reece.com/au.

Chairman & CEO Address – Peter Wilson  
Many of you will be familiar with Reece’s history, from humble beginnings in 1920 as a hardware 
business. In 1969, my family became the majority shareholder in Reece. At the time, Reece had just two 
stores in Caulfield and Clayton. We saw rapid growth through the 80s and 90s. I joined Reece during this 
period in 1993 as we expanded nationally. In the 2000s we kept growing. I became CEO at the end of 
2007 as we transformed Reece into a world class business and brand. Fast forward to today, we are an 
international business generating $9.4 billion of revenue, with operations across Australia, New Zealand 
and the United States, with over 9000 team members. 
We’ve achieved this firstly by focusing on our Blueprint, from purpose to promise. Our purpose and 
values shape the decisions we make every day, big and small – this is what we call The Reece Way. 
Our 2030 vision reflects a clear ambition to be our trade’s most valuable partner by being easy to do 
business with, in every branch, on every screen, every day. We bring this to life through our three 
strategic priorities: operational excellence, breakthrough innovation, and investing for profitable growth. 
The Blueprint culminates in the delivery of our Customer Promise, which we call customised service.  
Throughout our history we have always taken a long term view to growing the business. This approach 
supported by our ownership structure and Blueprint has enabled us to build to the position we are in 
today. Our ANZ business demonstrates how we have benefited from this strategy. And in the US we are 
eight years in and we will continue to take this approach to build the foundations to be successful over 
the long term. 
Turning to our performance this financial year, in summary, we delivered sales revenue of $9.4 billion, up 
4.5% on the prior year. We saw higher volumes and a small contribution from inflation in ANZ as a solid 
pipeline of activity continued. In the US, conditions were more challenging as weak new residential 
construction continued to impact demand. EBIT was down 2.6% to $534 million. Earnings per share 
increased to 49.5 cents and return on capital was 11.9%, up 5bps compared to the prior year. Total 
dividends increased 2.6% to 18.84 cents per share, fully franked, supported by the share buyback.  
We have also continued to reshape our Board this year, bringing in skills and expertise that will help 
deliver on our future ambitions while maintaining the benefits that our ownership structure has 
delivered. In practice, this makes us look quite different to many other ASX listed companies. We 
embrace this because it is one of the reasons for our success.  
During the year, we saw Jacqueline Chow take up her role as Remuneration Committee Chair, the 
appointment of Tim Ford as Lead Independent Director and Angela Mentis retire from the Board. I’d like 
to take this opportunity to thank Angela for her contribution and wish her all the best for the future. Tim’s 
appointment was a clear milestone. With his strong background in leadership including in the US, he will 
bring valuable operational expertise to the Board table while providing an independent voice for minority 
shareholders. As ever, our focus is on finding Directors who are the right long term fit for Reece.  
Group President & MD Address – Sasha Nikolic 
We delivered sales revenue of $9.4 billion in FY26, which was up 4.5% on the prior year, EBITDA was 
flat at $901 million and EBIT was down 2.6% to $534 million. In ANZ, sales were up 8.3%, driven by 
higher volumes, with a 2% inflation contribution. In the US, sales were up 6.5%, driven by continuing 
network expansion and a 2% inflation contribution. Like for like sales in the US were down 1.7% as weak 
new residential construction continued to impact demand. Our net leverage ratio was down to 1.0 times 
EBITDA, driven by stronger operational cash flow. While demand in the US remains challenging, FY26 
was a year of stabilisation, and we will continue to build the foundations in order to strengthen the 
business and its resilience over the long term.

Chairman & CEO – Peter Wilson: Q1 Sales Update 
In the first quarter of FY27, we have delivered sales of $2.5bn, up 4.3% on a reported basis, and up 
8.7% on a constant currency basis. In ANZ, sales were up 12.8%, driven by continued momentum in 
volumes and a mid single digit contribution from inflation. This strong performance has been supported 
by the pipeline of existing activity.  
In the US sales were up 5.6%, driven by network expansion and low single digit inflation. Like for like 
sales excluding network expansion was up 2.4%. The US has remained soft, with the subdued 
residential new construction market continuing to impact our business. 
Looking ahead, we’re expecting a strong first half for ANZ and a continuation of the challenging 
environment in the US. Given the complexity of the current environment we remain cautious on the 
second half of FY27.

Appendix
Group definitions and non-IFRS measures
Adjusted EBITDA / Adjusted EBIT
Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBIT are non-IFRS financial measures used by Reece for internal management reporting purposes to 
assess underlying performance. 
Return on capital (ROC)
Adjusted EBIT (12-month rolling basis) divided by shareholders equity plus net debt.
Free cash flow
Adjusted EBITDA less net movements in working capital, income tax paid and lease payments.
Net leverage ratio
Net debt over EBITDA (12-month rolling basis) calculated on a pre-AASB16 Leases basis.
Available liquidity
Cash plus headroom on the Group’s available facilities at period end.
Constant currency 
basis
Constant currency basis applies the average 1Q26 AUD/USD foreign exchange rate to current period sales to eliminate the foreign 
exchange impact when comparing sales against the previous corresponding period.
Like for like (LFL)
Like for like sales refers to branches that have reported a full 12-month of sales in both the current and prior year.
Net financing activities
Net financing cash flows excluding dividends and share buyback.
Average debt maturity
Average debt maturity of drawn funding facilities.
