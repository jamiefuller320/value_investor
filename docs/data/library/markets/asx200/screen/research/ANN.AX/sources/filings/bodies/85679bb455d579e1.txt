Chief Financial Officer Resignation 
16 April 2026 – Ansell Limited (ASX:ANN) announces that its Chief Financial Officer (CFO), Brian Montgomery, 
has resigned and is leaving to join Hologic, a US-based MedTech company recently acquired for ~US$18bn, as 
its new CFO. 
Ansell has engaged a search firm to support the recruitment of a new CFO. In the interim, Fred Marx, currently 
Vice President, Corporate FP&A, Strategy and Transformation, will take on the role as Acting CFO. Fred brings 
more than 20 years of senior leadership experience across finance, operations and strategy. His previous Ansell 
roles include Vice President of Global FP&A, Vice President of Finance for Global Operations and Supply Chain, 
and Finance Director for the Americas and the Medical Division. He also served as Acting CFO during the 
previous CFO transition period. 
Ansell’s Managing Director and CEO, Nathalie Ahlström, thanked Brian Montgomery for his strong leadership 
and contributions during his tenure, stating: “Brian has brought deep financial, strategic, and operational 
leadership to Ansell, strengthening our discipline and supporting the company through an important phase of 
transformation, and we wish him well. I look forward to working closely with Fred while we complete the process 
to appoint Brian’s successor”. 
This announcement was authorised for release by the Board of Directors of Ansell Limited.

About Ansell 
Ansell (ASX:ANN) is a global leader in safety solutions and an integrated manufacturer of personal protection equipment for 
healthcare and industrial workplaces. Each day, over 10 million workers in more than 100 countries trust their safety to Ansell 
brands such as HyFlex, Ringers, MICROFLEX, TouchNTuff, GAMMEX, Kimtech, KleenGuard, and AlphaTec. Driven by a vision to 
lead the world to a safer future, the company continuously pursues new product and service innovations that predict, prevent, and 
protect against workplace risk while promoting sustainable sourcing and manufacturing.
