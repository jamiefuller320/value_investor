BlueScope’s November 2025 New Zealand Site Visit and 2026 Financial Calendar
BlueScope advises that it plans to hold a site visit and investor briefing on 27 November 2025, to be held on-site 
at BlueScope’s Glenbrook Steelworks, south of Auckland, New Zealand. This will include a management presentation 
focussed on the New Zealand business, as well as an update on key sustainability matters for the Group. The event will 
be webcast live (at www.bluescope.com/investors), and a recording of the webcast will be posted on the BlueScope 
website following the event.
