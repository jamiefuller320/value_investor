It wasn’t that long ago the lack of supply-side motivation and engagement — a direct product of the typical summer malaise that grips US markets around this time of the year — looked like it may precipitate a look at the 26789-27191 all-time high. Granted, there’s been a bit of bad news generated by You Know Who regarding You Know What, but it seems it’s the lack of demand side motivation and engagement that threatens to flip the current equilibrium / sideways short term trend into a downtrend that may even challenge the long term uptrend ribbon. Friday’s candle is definitely in the MOTN group of candle scenarios that one would expect to play out after Thursday’s supply side showing. It wasn’t a total write off, though, the Comp closed well off its lows — but it also closed well off its session high, and well down from Thursday’s close . It is the latter that’s more important to me as a technical analyst. = Supply side control ⚠️🚨 So, is it panic stations!? No, I don’t think so. Even after Thurs-Fri’s little one-two supply side punch, the broader short term trend remains sideways … Arguably we’re still dealing with a market in some form of equilibrium. It will, however, be fascinating to see how the price action responds around the 24980-25015 demand zone. The “ It’s okay, there’s nothing to worry about here ” moment will come if / when we don’t even get there , logging a strong demand side candle to form a higher trough to 25015 (i.e., long white-bodied candle and or long downward pointing shadow, preferably on elevated volume to demonstrate the BTD crowd has removed a significant amount of the pesky latent supply that’s lurking in the system). The opposite, i.e., strong supply side candle closing within 24980-25015 — is truly going to put the proverbial cat among the proverbial pigeons! 😼 (i.e., long black-bodied candle and or long upward pointing shadow). If one is appropriately placed with respect to one’s portfolio risk, then neither prospect should scare one much . Me, I am a natural bull… It’s far easier to be a trend follower in a low volatility bull market than high volatility bear markets (as they tend to be). So, let’s all hope the next candle is long and white , and the next short term trend out of the present equilibrium is up! 📈 Hope for the best 🙏 Prepare for the worst 🛡️ That’s always the plan! 🧐 View I am at 1/2RP on my US portfolio ( RP = R isk P osition — it reflects my personal allowable capital allocation limit for my investments in US stocks.
