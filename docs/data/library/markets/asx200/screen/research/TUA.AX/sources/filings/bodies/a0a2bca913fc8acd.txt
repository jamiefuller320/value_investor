Update on M1 acquisition
• 100% acquisition of M1 Limited (excluding its ICT businesses) announced on 11 August 2025 
for an enterprise value of S$1,430m on a debt-free and cash-free basis
• Implied multiple of 7.3x M1 ex-ICT EBITDA for the last 12 months to 30 April 2025 of 
S$195.4m (excluding any pro forma synergies)
• Funded via existing cash, the completed equity raise of A$385m and S$1,100m of fully 
underwritten acquisition bank debt financing
• Aiming to raise up to A$50m additionally by way of a share purchase plan (SPP), which 
currently has a closing date of 25 Sept 2025. 
• The necessary Long Form Consolidation Joint Application has been submitted to IMDA and 
Tuas hopes to receive regulatory approval for the acquisition over the next few months
