5.1 Please provide any further information applicable to this dividend/distribution
A dividend of USD 0.066 per share was declared for holders of Chess Depositary Instruments (CDIs) trading on the
Australian Securities Exchange. Holders of CDIs receive an amount in AUD equivalent to USD 0.66 per US Common
Stock based on the exchange rate on the record date, and reflecting the 10:1 ratio between CDIs and NYSE shares.
