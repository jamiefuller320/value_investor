Notice of Meeting 
The full Notice of Meeting which sets out the Agenda (including details of all resolutions being put to the meeting), important voting information 
and an Explanatory Memorandum can be found at: https://www.perenti.com/investors/asx-announcements/  
The Company’s Annual Report is also available at: https://www.perenti.com/investors/reports-presentations/  
In planning for the Meeting, Perenti has focused on ensuring the safety of shareholders and its team members whilst seeking to equally maximise 
the opportunity for shareholder participation and, at this time, plans to hold the Meeting both online and in person (i.e. a hybrid meeting). 
In the event that it is necessary or appropriate for Perenti to make alternative arrangements for the Meeting, information will be lodged with the 
ASX at www.asx.com.au (ASX: PRN) and Perenti’s website at: https://perenti.com/ 
If you have any difficulties obtaining a copy of the full Notice of Meeting, please contact Perenti’s share registry, MUFG Corporate Markets, at: 
support@cm.mpms.mufg.com 
 
Attendance via online platform 
Shareholders are able to participate in the Meeting virtually via the online platform at https://meetings.openbriefing.com/PRN26  
We recommend logging into the online platform at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled start time for the Meeting using the instructions below: 
1. Enter https://meetings.openbriefing.com/PRN26 into a supported web browser on your computer or online device; 
2. Shareholders will need their Shareholder Reference Number (SRN) or Holder Identification Number (HIN) printed on their Proxy Form; and 
3. Proxyholders will need their proxy code which MUFG Corporate Markets will provide via email prior to the Meeting. 
 
Shareholders attending virtually will be able to ask questions and vote during the Meeting. 
Further information on how to participate virtually is set out in the full Notice of Meeting and the Virtual Meeting Online Guide, which are both 
available at: https://www.perenti.com/investors/asx-announcements/ 
 
Attendance in person 
The Meeting will also be held in person at The Parmelia Hilton, Swan Room, 14 Mill Street, Perth. 
 
Proxy Lodgements 
Shareholders who choose to lodge a proxy should follow the instructions on their personalised Proxy Form (enclosed), which must be received by 
Perenti’s share registry, MUFG Corporate Markets, by 10.30am (AWST) Wednesday 14 October 2026.
