## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Perenti is a Perth-headquartered diversified mining services group (contract underground mining via Barminco, open-pit services via AMS, drilling, and technology) with heavy gold exposure and a growing contract backlog. The quantitative screen rates the name **buy**, but the screening snapshot carries **zero populated metrics** (`data_quality_score` 0.0, `models_passed` 0, `timing_signal`: insufficient_data), so the signal is directionally positive but not yet evidence-backed in this pack. The value hook is a cyclical services compounder trading on undemanding multiples versus peers, returning capital via dividends and buybacks while net debt has fallen materially. The key debate is whether record operating earnings and major contract wins (Bellevue, Fourmile, Duketon) outweigh portfolio reshaping noise, heavy sustaining capex, and the episodic contract-loss risk exemplified by Khoemacau/Zone 5.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Perenti offers exposure to gold-mining activity without taking commodity price risk directly: revenue is largely contract-based, the order book has been rebuilt after the Zone 5 setback, and management is actively recycling capital (equipment sales, buybacks, dividends). Secondary commentary (Market Index, July 2025) cited a ~9.7x forward P/E versus larger peer Macmahon at ~6.8x, with management targeting a 30–40% underlying NPAT payout and analysts forecasting ~4% dividend yield over FY25–27. Business quality rests on scale in underground mining (Barminco, AUMS), geographic diversification across four continents, and commercial discipline—management exited Khoemacau when returns failed internal hurdles (ASX media release, 22 April 2025). Contract momentum supports the screen’s buy label qualitatively: Bellevue Gold (~A$850m, four years), Barrick Fourmile (~A$192–275m), Regis Duketon extension (~A$180m), and Ghana Iduapriem fleet sale/extension activity. Net debt has improved from A$422m (FY24) to A$227m (FY26 per Yahoo), leverage is below the threshold management cited for resuming dividends, and an on-market buyback of up to 84.5 million shares was launched alongside FY26 results (news, August 2026). The screen buy is plausible on cash generation, capital returns, and relative valuation, but this pack cannot tie it to passed quantitative models—metrics are absent.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical exposure:** Revenue and margins correlate with mining-customer capex and gold exploration activity; FY26 revenue declined despite “record” operating earnings headlines, illustrating mix and contract-timing volatility.

**Customer and contract concentration:** Large contracts (Bellevue, AngloGold, Barrick, Endeavour, Regis) dominate the narrative; loss or non-renewal of a major contract—as with Khoemacau/Zone 5 (~A$800m over five years)—can create multi-year EBITDA gaps.

**Capital intensity and FCF:** Sustaining capex near **A$350m** and episodic growth capex compress FCF (FY26 FCF **A$95m** vs **A$200m** in FY25). Yahoo quarterly cashflow is empty (`ttm_cashflow_suppressed`), limiting near-term cash-conversion analysis.

**Portfolio reshaping / earnings quality:** Discontinued operations (**–A$63m** in FY26), impairments (**A$54m**), and equipment/fleet sales (Beetle **A$71m** deal, August 2026; AngloGold Iduapriem fleet sale, July 2026) add accounting noise; reported EPS is not a clean run-rate metric.

**Working capital:** H1 FY25 late debtors (**A$42m**) show receivables timing risk in a labour-and-equipment-intensive model.

**Leverage and liquidity:** Net debt has improved but gross debt remains **A$571m**; interest expense **A$56m** in FY26. No covenant or going-concern language was available in extracted filing bodies—annual report text was not ingested.

**Governance / data quality:** The filings feed mixes **Perenti (ASX: PRN)** with **Princes Group (LSE: PRN)**; several indexed “annual” bodies are wrong-entity RNS releases. Verify all figures against the ASX Appendix 4E PDF before trading.

**Geographic and operational risk:** African operations (Ghana Iduapriem, prior Botswana exposure) carry sovereign, FX, and handover risks.
