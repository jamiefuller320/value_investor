Notice of Annual General Meeting Pressure Technologies PLC 25 February 2025 25 February 2025 Pressure Technologies plc ("Pressure Technologies" or "the Company") Notice of 2025 Annual General Meeting Pressure Technologies plc (AIM: PRES), the specialist engineering group, has today published the notice of its 2025 annual general meeting (the "AGM"), which is available to download from the Company's website under ' Investors; Reports, Documents and Presentations ' and will be posted to shareholders who have elected to receive a hard copy. The Company's AGM will take place on Thursday 20 March 2025 at 09:30 am at the offices of Singer Capital Markets, 1 Bartholomew Lane, London, EC2N 2AX. For further information, please contact: Pressure Technologies plc Chris Walters, Chief Executive Tel: 0333 015 0710 company.secretary@pressuretechnologies.co.uk Singer Capital Markets (Nomad and Broker) Rick Thompson / Asha Chotai Tel: 0207 496 3000 COMPANY DESCRIPTION www.pressuretechnologies.com Pressure Technologies plc is based in Sheffield and operates through its wholly owned subsidiary Chesterfield Special Cylinders ("CSC"). CSC is a market leading designer, manufacturer and supplier of safety-critical, high-pressure containment products and services to a global customer base in the defence, hydrogen energy, oil & gas and industrial markets.
