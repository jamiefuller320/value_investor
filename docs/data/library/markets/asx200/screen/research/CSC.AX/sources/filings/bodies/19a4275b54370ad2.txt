Item 4. 
Summary of Material Change 
On September 21, 2026, Capstone entered into a definitive agreement to sell its Cozamin 
copper-silver-zinc-lead mine (“Cozamin”), located in Zacatecas, Mexico, to Luca Mining 
Corp. (“Luca”) for total consideration of up to US$385 million (the “Transaction”). 
The total consideration payable by Luca to Capstone is comprised of US$275 million in 
upfront cash, subject to customary adjustments, US$15 million in Luca shares to be issued 
to Capstone at closing, US$35 million in deferred consideration to be received on the first 
anniversary of closing, and up to US$60 million in contingent cash consideration (the 
“Contingent Consideration”) payable to Capstone tied to annual average copper prices. 
Item 5. 
Full Description of Material Change 
5.1 Full Description of Material Change 
On September 21, 2026, Capstone entered into a definitive agreement to sell Cozamin, 
located in Zacatecas, Mexico, to Luca for total consideration of up to US$385 million. 
Under the terms of the Transaction, Capstone will receive total consideration of up to 
US$385 million, comprising: 
 
US$275 million in upfront cash, subject to customary closing adjustments, based 
on an October 31, 2026, lock-box date on a cash-free and debt-free basis and 
assuming a normalized level of working capital; 
 
US$15 million in Luca shares to be issued to Capstone at closing; 
 
US$35 million in deferred consideration, payable in cash and/or Luca shares at 
Luca’s option, to be received on the first anniversary of closing; and  
 
up to US$60 million in Contingent Consideration tied to annual average copper 
prices, payable to Capstone following the end of each of the 2027, 2028 and 2029 
calendar years, based on the average annual LME Copper Cash Settlement Price 
(“LME Cu Price”) for the applicable year. The annual contingent payment will be 
US$10 million if the LME Cu Price is between US$7.00/lb and US$7.75/lb, US$15
