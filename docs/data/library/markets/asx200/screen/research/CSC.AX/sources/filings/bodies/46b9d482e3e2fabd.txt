November 12, 2026 
Capstone Copper Provides Update on Operations 
Vancouver, British Columbia – Capstone Copper Corp. (“Capstone” or the “Company”) (TSX:CS) (ASX:CSC) 
provides an update on operations, including the planned restart of Pinto Valley and the risks of the ongoing national 
truckers strike in Chile.  
At Pinto Valley, plant operations were expected to resume in early November. However, during the commissioning 
of the primary crusher, it was identified that additional maintenance was required on a few of the major mechanical 
components. This has extended the scope and duration of the planned maintenance, as the specific components 
required are being manufactured off-site by the original equipment manufacturer. The primary crusher is now 
expected to be recommissioned in mid-December 2026. 
In the interim, the Company is redeploying temporary mobile crushing equipment. This is expected to enable a 
partial restart of the concentrator in mid-November, ahead of the primary crusher returning to service. During this 
interim period, the Company estimates a throughput capacity of up to 40 thousand tonnes per day. 
The Company expects the additional downtime at Pinto Valley to impact 2026 copper production by approximately 
5,000 tonnes over the remainder of the year, depending on the timing of the interim restart and the recommissioning 
of the primary crusher. As such, the Company is adjusting the bottom end of its 2026 consolidated copper production 
guidance1 to 195 thousand tonnes, from 200 thousand tonnes originally. 
A national strike has been initiated in Chile by a civic movement and a coalition of independent trucking associations 
in response to elevated fuel prices. This strike presents a risk to the Company’s 2026 production expectations 
associated with suspension of concentrate and cathode shipments, as well as delivery of key consumables such 
as fuel and sulphuric acid. At this time, production at both Mantoverde and Mantos Blancos remains unimpacted by 
the strike, attributable to preventative measures taken before the strike began and storage capacity available on-
site. The Company will continue to implement mitigation measures where possible to avoid impacts to production 
associated with the national trucker strike.  
About Capstone Copper Corp. 
Capstone Copper Corp. is an Americas-focused copper mining company headquartered in Vancouver, Canada. 
Capstone’s operating portfolio of assets includes the Pinto Valley copper mine located in Arizona, USA, the 
Cozamin copper-silver mine located in Zacatecas, Mexico, the Mantos Blancos copper-silver mine located in the 
Antofagasta region, Chile, and the Mantoverde copper-gold mine, located in the Atacama region, Chile. Capstone’s 
growth pipeline includes the fully permitted Santo Domingo copper-iron-gold project, located approximately 35 
kilometres northeast of Mantoverde in the Atacama region, Chile, as well as a portfolio of exploration properties in 
the Americas. 
Capstone Copper’s strategy is to unlock transformational copper production growth while executing on cost and 
operational improvements through innovation, optimization and safe and responsible production throughout our 
portfolio of assets. We focus on profitability and disciplined capital allocation to surface stakeholder value. We are 
committed to creating a positive impact in the lives of our people and local communities, while delivering compelling 
returns to investors by responsibly producing copper to meet the world’s growing needs.
