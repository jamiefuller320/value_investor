Results of 2024 Annual General Meeting Pressure Technologies PLC 21 March 2024 21 March 2024 Pressure Technologies plc ("Pressure Technologies" or "the Company") Results of 2024 Annual General Meeting Pressure Technologies plc (AIM: PRES), the specialist engineering group, announces the voting results of its 2024 annual general meeting (the "AGM") held today. Resolutions 1-12 were proposed by the Board and all were duly passed. Resolution 13 was proposed by the Board as a special resolution, requiring a 75% majority of votes cast to pass. This resolution received votes in favour equating to 66.34% of those cast and so was not passed. The Board notes this vote against Resolution 13 and will engage with shareholders to understand their views.
