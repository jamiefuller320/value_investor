Alkemy Shareholder Call Alkemy Capital Investments PLC 02 June 2025 2 June 2025 Alkemy Capital Investments Plc Alkemy Shareholder Call Alkemy Capital Investments Plc ("Alkemy") (LSE: ALK), (JV2:FRA) is pleased to announce that it will host an investor webinar at 12pm (BST) on Monday 2 June 2025 to discuss the recent announcement relating to the progress of the Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) study being completed by Alkemy's wholly owned subsidiary, Tees Valley Lithium Limited ("TVL") The webinar will feature an update from Alkemy Chairman Paul Atherley, and TVL CEO Vikki Jeckell who will provide an update on the Company's progress and future outlook. The event will also include a live Q&A session, offering shareholders and investors the opportunity to engage directly with the leadership team.
