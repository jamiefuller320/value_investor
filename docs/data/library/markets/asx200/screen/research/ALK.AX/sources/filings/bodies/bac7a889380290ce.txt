UPON THE PUBLICATION OF THIS ANNOUNCEMENT, THIS INSIDE INFORMATION IS NOW CONSIDERED TO BE IN THE PUBLIC DOMAIN.] 19 November 2026 Alkemy Capital Investments Plc Glencore Binding Offtake Expanded to Cover 100% of TVL production Alkemy Capital Investments plc ("Alkemy") (LSE: ALK) (JV2:FRA), the 100% owner of Tees Valley Lithium ("TVL"), announces that TVL has signed an amendment to its existing binding offtake agreement with a wholly-owned subsidiary of Glencore plc ("Glencore") for battery-grade lithium hydroxide from TVL's refinery in Teesside, UK. Under the amended agreement, Glencore is committed to take all battery-grade lithium hydroxide that TVL makes available to it, up to 25,000 tonnes per annum. 25,000 tonnes per annum represents the full planned capacity of Train 1. The initial term is eight years from the start of commercial production, with an option to extend by a further two years by mutual agreement. Pricing remains market-based, and the commercial terms are confidential. The original agreement, signed in January 2026, covered up to 10,000 tonnes per annum over five years. TVL retains discretion over the annual allocation of production between Glencore and strategic customers. This preserves TVL’s ability to develop direct, long-term offtake relationships while maintaining Glencore as a key commercial partner. Highlights Full capacity coverage: binding agreement provides a committed buyer for all volumes TVL elects to allocate to Glencore, up to 25,000 tonnes per annum (100% of Train 1’s planned annual capacity) Extended Term: eight years from the start of commercial production, extendable to ten. Commercial Flexibility: TVL retains control of production allocations and may supply strategic customers directly. Strengthened Commercial Position The expanded agreement builds on TVL's existing relationship with Glencore, one of the world’s largest commodity marketers. Every tonne of Train 1’s planned output now has a contracted buyer. TVL continues to develop additional direct, long-term offtake relationships with strategic customers in the European battery supply chain, complementing its established partnership with Glencore. Vikki Jeckell, CEO of TVL, commented: “Securing a binding offtake agreement with Glencore covering up to 100% of Train 1’s planned production capacity is a major commercial milestone for the UK’s flagship lithium refiner, Tees Valley Lithium. Glencore’s commitment to purchase up to 25,000 tonnes annually over an initial eight-year term provides a contracted route to market for our entire planned first train output, while preserving flexibility to supply strategic customers directly. Together with the UK Government’s DRIVE35 offer in principle and our established technology partnership with Veolia, this agreement marks further progress in addressing the commercial and delivery requirements of the project. Our focus remains on building strategically important lithium refining capacity in Teesside.” About Glencore: Glencore is one of the world’s largest global diversified natural resource companies and a major producer and marketer of more than 60 commodities that advance everyday life. Through a network of assets, customers and suppliers that spans the globe, we produce, process, recycle, source, market and distribute the commodities that support decarbonisation while meeting the energy needs of today. With over 150,000 employees and contractors and a strong footprint in over 30 countries in both established and emerging regions for natural resources, our marketing and industrial activities are supported by a global network of more than 50 offices. Glencore’s customers are industrial consumers, such as those in the automotive, steel, power generation, battery manufacturing and oil sectors. We also provide financing, logistics and other services to producers and consumers of commodities. Glencore is proud to be a member of the Voluntary Principles on Security and Human Rights and the International Council on Mining and Metals. We are an active participant in the Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative. We will support the global effort to achieve the goals of the Paris Agreement through our efforts to decarbonise our own operational footprint.
