Disclaimer* Alkemy Capital Investments PLC 05 February 2026 5 February 2026 Alkemy Capital Investments Plc Grant of Share Awards Alkemy Capital Investments plc ("Alkemy") (LSE: ALK) (FRA: JV2) announces that it has granted long term incentive share awards ("Share Awards") over an aggregate 685,000 ordinary shares to directors and senior managers of Alkemy and Tees Valley Lithium, which represents approximately 6.4% of the Company's existing issued share capital. For the purposes of the allocation, a share price of £3.59 per ordinary share was used, being the 30-day VWAP to the date of this announcement. The new Share Awards are subject to the satisfaction of certain performance conditions to be interpreted at the discretion of the Alkemy Board over a three year review period. Upon vesting, no consideration is payable. Subject to vesting and such performance conditions being met, the new Share Awards will be allocated to the participant as fully paid ordinary shares, subject to any regulatory restrictions.
