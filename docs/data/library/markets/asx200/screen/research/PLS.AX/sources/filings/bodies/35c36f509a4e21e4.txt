PLS approves P2000 FID to lift production to ~2Mtpa 
PLS Group Limited (ASX: PLS) (PLS or the Company) is pleased to announce that it has taken a positive final 
investment decision (FID) for the P2000 Project  at its 100% -owned Pilgangoora Operation in Western 
Australia.  
The P2000 Project will increase Pilgangoora spodumene concentrate production to approximately 2.0 million 
tonnes per annum1 (Mtpa) through construction of the new Jimmagunya processing plant, while improving the 
operation's long term unit cost position, with first production expected in the September Quarter calendar year 
2029 (CY29).  
The completed P2000 Feasibility Study (FS) has materially enhanced the development concept established in 
the June 2024 Pre-Feasibility Study2 (PFS). Improved metallurgical understanding and processing capability 
through the P680 and P1000 expansions has enabled a more efficient approach to mining and processing the 
Pilgangoora orebody. This allows more mineralised material to be recovered as ore, reducing waste movement 
and strip ratio, and enabling higher production at lower unit operating costs. 
The P2000 FS supports investment of approximately A$1.9 billion (B) in P2000 and delivers an incremental 
post-tax NPV of A$2.7B3 (8% real) and post-tax IRR of 29.5%, based on a long- term SC6 price assumption of 
US$1,620/t4.  
PLS has also today announced an update to the Pilgangoora Ore Reserve and Mineral Resource5 and capital 
expenditure guidance associated with enhancing infrastructure at Pilgangoora6.  
PLS has also completed a separate underground PFS (Underground PFS)  evaluating the potential future 
development of underground mining at Pilgangoora, providing further long-term growth optionality beyond the 
P2000 FID.

P2000 FEASIBILITY STUDY  
Overview 
PLS’ 100% -owned Pilgangoora Operation is located in Western Australia’s Pilbara region, approximately 
140km by road from Port Hedland, through which PLS exports its spodumene concentrate. Since first 
production in 2018, PLS has progressively expanded the operation through a staged development strategy. 
P2000 represents the next major step in this strategy, increasing Pilgangoora’s spodumene concentrate 
production to approximately 2.0Mtpa through construction of the new Jimmagunya processing facility.  
The project comprises a 9.0Mtpa dry plant, a 6.3Mtpa wet plant, a tailings filtration facility and associated 
earthworks, services and infrastructure. 
The approximately A$1.9B project capital estimate includes previously approved pre- FID expenditure and 
excludes the separately announced enhancing infrastructure investments and any future underground 
development. The enhancing infrastructure investments i mprove the efficiency and capability of the existing 
Pilgangoora Operation and provide benefits independently of the scale or timing of future production growth.  
P2000 PFS to FS – improved processing capability unlocks greater value  
Further metallurgical testwork completed since the PFS, together with experience from operating the ore 
sorting facility at the Pilgan plant, has demonstrated that materially greater quantities of contact ore can be 
processed while maintaining spodumene concentrate product quality. 
This increased processing flexibility changes the optimal approach to mining the Pilgangoora orebody . More 
mineralised material can be recovered as ore, reducing waste movement and strip ratio and enabling a more 
productive mining method. 
Approximately 67Mt of mineralised contact material 9 previously classified as waste is now included as ore in 
the updated Ore Reserve as a result of this improved processing capability.  
To capture this opportunity, the Jimmagunya plant has increased in scale relative to the PFS, with wet plant 
capacity increasing 26% from 5.0Mtpa to 6.3Mtpa, increasing concentrate production, enabling a reduction in 
operating asset base to the Jimmagunya and Pilgan plants while preserving optionality for PLS through 
retaining flexible processing capacity at Ngungaju and potential additional future underground ore supply.

Ore Reserves - greater extraction of the Pilgangoora orebody 
The P2000 production profile is underpinned by the 2026 Pilgangoora Ore Reserve 5 released concurrently 
with the P2000 FID.  
The Jimmagunya flowsheet is the next evolution in the Pilgangoora Operation and has been designed to 
accommodate materially greater quantities of contact ore than the existing processing plants. This enables 
mineralised material previously classified as waste to be economically processed and incorporated into the 
mine plan. 
Approximately 67Mt of contact ore9 is included in the 2026 Pilgangoora Ore Reserve as a result of this change. 
Existing contact ore stockpiles can also be processed earlier and at higher rates than contemplated under the 
previous life-of-mine plan.  
Further testwork has also demonstrated increased ore sorting efficiency and the ability to accommodate higher 
levels of waste within the ore feed. Accordingly, ore sorting has been incorporated as a modifying factor and 
the reporting reference point for the updated Ore Reserve has changed from “ before run-of-mine” processing 
(ROM) to “after crushing and ore sorting” at the coarse ore stockpile (COS), representing wet plant feed. 
Including the maiden Underground Ore Reserve (46.5Mt at 1.50% Li2O), the updated Pilgangoora Ore Reserve 
is 249.2Mt at the COS at 1.24% Li2O, representing a 23% increase in reported Ore Reserve tonnes and a 31% 
increase in contained lithia relative to the Ore Reserve reported in PLS’ FY26 Annual Report.  
The increased ability to process contact ore also enables a less selective and more productive approach to 
open-pit mining. The P2000 open- pit mine plan comprises approximately 194Mt of COS ore at a life- of-mine 
strip ratio of approximately 5.6x. 
The current open- pit Ore Reserve supports approximately 20 years of production based on the P2000 FS

Jimmagunya enables a larger, more efficient operation 
The new processing plant, named Jimmagunya (pronounced JIM-A-GUN-YA) by Nyamal elders and meaning 
"spring country", will be located adjacent to the existing Pilgan plant and becomes PLS' third major processing 
facility at Pilgangoora. T he plant has been designed to complement existing processing facilities and enable 
more efficient extraction of the broader Pilgangoora orebody. 
Under the expanded operating model, production will be concentrated at the larger co-located Jimmagunya 
and Pilgan processing facilities, which will account for the majority of Pilgangoora’s output. The Ngungaju 
processing facility will be idled and retained as flexible processing capacity and operated where commercially 
beneficial, taking account of feed availability, operating costs and market conditions.  
The processing facilities have complementary capabilities, providing flexibility to match ore characteristics with 
the most appropriate processing route.  Jimmagunya is designed to process higher levels of contact material 
and waste rock  through ore sorting and whole- of-ore flotation, while higher -grade coarse ore can be 
preferentially directed to the Pilgan processing plant, optimising recovery and value across the Pilgangoora 
Operation. 
Jimmagunya will comprise: 
• A 9.0Mtpa dry Plant, incorporating crushing, ore sorting and covered coarse ore stockpiling . 
• A 6.3Mtpa wet Plant, incorporating grinding, classification, magnetic separation, desliming, flotation, 
tantalum recovery and concentrate dewatering. 
Average lithia recovery through the Jimmagunya wet plant is expected to be approximately 70%, reflecting the 
characteristics of its feed. By economically processing more mineralised contact material, the revised 
configuration is expected to increase overall orebody extraction by approximately 17%. Further processing 
and mining optimisation opportunities have been identified, with PLS targeting longer-term wet plant recoveries 
aligned with the performance of the existing Pilgan plant. 
At steady state, P2000 is expected to increase Pilgangoora production to approximately 2.0Mtpa, with average 
production of approximately 1.97Mtpa of SC5.2 spodumene concentrate over the first 10 years 1. 
The expanded scale, greater orebody extraction and lower strip ratio are expected to deliver average FOB unit 
operating costs of approximately A$620/t7 (real) over the first 10 years at steady state, strengthening the 
operation's competitiveness through the cycle. PLS will continue to seek opportunities to further reduce FOB 
costs through operational cost efficiencies including the ongoing Cost Smart Future Ready Program.  
Under the P2000 operating model, Pilgangoora is also expected to produce ~900 klbs per annum of contained 
Ta2O5, as ~5% primary tantalite concentrate, on average over the first ten years as a by -product credit 1. 
Tantalum is widely recognised as a critical mineral and is becoming strategically important for its applications 
in electronics and defence.

Project infrastructure and approvals 
P2000 is supported by established Pilgangoora infrastructure, together with targeted investment required for 
the expanded operating scale. 
Water P2000 includes dry -stack tailings using belt filtration to recover process water before co-
disposal of filtered tailings within waste- rock landforms. This  system, combined with other 
water efficiency measures, has the potential to deliver up to a 40% water intensity reduction 
per tonne of product. Planned water exploration and management measures are expected to 
increase future water supply beyond 2030 to balance future demand. 
Port The P2000 FS assumes export through Lumsden Point at Port Hedland using a third- party 
owned storage and handling solution.  PLS continues to assess opportunities to further 
optimise long-term port solutions for the expanded operation. 
Power The P2000 FS  assumes additional onsite generation capacity across the interconnected 
Pilgan and Ngungaju power systems. PLS continues to assess opportunities to further 
optimise the long-term power solution for the expanded operation. 
Approvals PLS holds the core tenure and primary operating approvals required for construction and is 
progressing the remaining approvals and licence amendments required for the expanded life-
of-mine operating model.

The project incorporates a number of sustainability-related outcomes including increased recovery of

Lithium market outlook  
Lithium has been a strong growth market over the past decade, driven by the acceleration of electrification and 
the rapid growth of lithium -ion adoption. This structural growth is expected to continue as electrification 
expands across transport and energy systems. Benchmark Mineral Intelligence’s  base case forecasts global 
lithium demand to grow at approximately 8% per annum to 2040, reaching approximately 5.3Mt LCE — around 
three times the size of the market today. Electric vehicles are expected to remain the largest source of demand, 
while battery energy storage is emerging as an increasingly significant growth market, broadening the 
underlying demand base for lithium. 
Meeting this growth will require substantial new supply at a time when new lithium projects are becoming 
increasingly complex, capital intensive and slower to develop. Mine development timelines have extended to 
approximately 18 years 17 , increasing the strategic value of established operations capable of bringing 
significant new supply to market sooner.  
These supply challenges are also reflected in long-term price expectations.  Benchmark Mineral Intelligence 
has recently increased its long- term spodumene price forecast to approximately US$2, 580/t (SC6 FOB 
Australia)18, compared with the long- term price assumption of US $1,620/t (SC6 CIF China) 4 adopted in the 
P2000 FS. 
While the long-term outlook remains supportive, lithium markets are expected to remain cyclical, reinforcing 
the importance of low -cost, long-life and reliable supply through the cycle. Pilgangoora - the world’s largest 
independent hard-rock lithium operation is particularly well positioned in this environment.

About PLS 
PLS is a leading global producer of lithium materials, with a diversified portfolio of assets and strategic 
partnerships in the rapidly growing battery materials sector. PLS owns 100% of the world’s largest, 
independent hard-rock lithium operation, the Pilgangoora Operation in Australia, and the Colina Lithium Project
