Enhancing infrastructure to strengthen Pilgangoora Operation  
PLS Group Limited (ASX: PLS) ( PLS or the Company) is pleased to advise that the Company  intends to 
undertake an approximately A$600 – 650 million (M) investment in enhanc ing infrastructure at its long -life, 
100%-owned Pilgangoora Operation. The investment is expected to reduce operating costs, improve 
productivity and support the long-term operation of Pilgangoora. 
Since commencing production in 2018, the Pilgangoora Operation has evolved significantly. Progressive 
growth of the orebody has underpinned a long- life operation, while the Altura acquisition expanded the 
operating footprint and successive plant expansions materially increased production capacity. This 
combination of long mine life, increased scale and greater operating flexibility provides PLS the opportunity to 
invest in permanent infrastructure that can lower operating costs, improve productivity and further strengthen 
Pilgangoora’s competitive position over the long term. 
The investment comprises upgrades to Wodgina Road East, a new permanent village and a new mine services 
facility. These investments are expected to deliver benefits to the existing Pilgangoora Operation, and their 
concurrent execution provides the additional infrastructure capacity to support the P2000 1 expansion. 
HIGHLIGHTS  
• Approximately A$600–650M investment in enhanced infrastructure at Pilgangoora, phased over three 
financial years through to FY29, with the majority of expenditure expected in FY28. 
• The investments are expected to reduce operating costs and improve productivity in the existing 
Pilgangoora Operation, while providing additional infrastructure capacity to support the planned P2000 
expansion.  
• Wodgina Road East: upgrade and sealing of the ~24km main access road will enable higher-capacity 
RAV10 ultra-quad haulage, reducing daily concentrate haulage movements by ~35% and lowering 
transport and road maintenance costs. Supported by a co-funding model with the Western Australian 
government for the public section of the road.  
• Permanent Village: new 1,200-bed village will improve accommodation quality, supporting workforce 
wellbeing, attraction and retention. Progressive relocation and decommissioning of existing 
accommodation are expected to enable more efficient waste placement and haulage, improving mine 
sequencing and operational flexibility. 
• Mine Services Facility: new purpose-built facility will support the transition to larger ultra-class haulage 
equipment, improving equipment availability and maintenance productivity while reducing 
maintenance costs.

WODGINA ROAD EAST 
The Wodgina Road East project involves upgrading and sealing the approximately 24km main access road 
connecting the Pilgangoora Operation to the Great Northern Highway. The upgrade will enable the transition 
from RAV7 triple road trains to higher -capacity RAV10 ultra-quad configurations for spodumene concentrate 
haulage, reducing the number of daily concentrate haulage movements required by ~35%. 
The upgraded road is expected to lower unit transport costs through more efficient haulage, reduce 
maintenance requirements and lower water use for dust suppression. The upgrade is also expected to 
enhance road safety, strengthen climate resilience and support more efficient site logistics.  
The road upgrade includes: 
• Sealing and upgrading the road pavement. 
• Improving road alignment and grades to achieve RAV10 road classification.  
• Upgrading 13 engineered floodway crossings and integrating three rail crossings . 
• Constructing a new intersection with the Great Northern Highway, including a RAV10- compliant 2km 
acceleration lane. 
PLS is pursuing a co-funded model with the WA State government and Federal government for the public road 
components of the upgrade. The WA government has committed an initial A$11M towards the Great Northern 
Highway intersection upgrade, with potential further government contributions for the approximately 8km public 
road section subject to relevant budget processes. 
Work has commenced on the road upgrade, and the project is expected to be completed by the end of calendar 
year 2028 (CY28).  
PERMANENT VILLAGE 
PLS will build a new 1,200 -bed permanent village for the Pilgangoora Operation, including central facilities, 
utilities, communications and power. Designed to modern industry standards, the cyclone- rated facility will 
provide shelter-in-place capability and progressively replace ageing, dispersed accommodation. It is expected 
to improve accommodation quality and consistency, supporting workforce wellbeing, attraction and retention.  
Since operations commenced, resource growth and successive Ore Reserve upgrades have expanded the 
planned mining footprint and associated waste landform requirements. The original camp, Carlindi Village, 
was originally located well outside the mining footprint, but mining activities have progressively moved closer, 
constraining mine planning. 
Relocation and subsequent decommissioning of Carlindi  are expected to enable nearby waste landforms to 
be developed as designed and in a more efficient sequence, supporting shorter haul distances and greater 
operational flexibility.  
The cleared Carlindi site also provides an opportunity to accommodate future mining infrastructure, including 
facilities supporting potential underground operations, reducing the need to clear additional land for those 
facilities. 
Design and vendor engagement have commenced, with the new village expected to progressively enter 
operational use from late CY27. Transferring operational personnel to the new village would make existing 
accommodation available for P2000 construction personnel before its eventual decommissioning, reducing the 
requirement for additional temporary accommodation during peak construction. 
MINE SERVICES FACILITY 
PLS has progressively evolved its mining model at Pilgangoora, including the transition towards an owner -
operated mining fleet. The next stage will introduce larger ultra- class haulage equipment to improve mining 
efficiency, with P2000 increasing the scale over which these benefits can be realised.

The existing mine services infrastructure is not suited to the scale and dimensions of  the larger fleet. PLS will 
therefore build a new mine services facility to replace the existing facilities, including eight enclosed service 
bays, two mine technology bays, fuel and lubrication systems, parts storage and associated utilities.  
The new facility is expected to improve equipment availability and maintenance productivity, while reducing 
maintenance costs. It will also enable major lifecycle maintenance to be undertaken on site, with associated 
operational benefits.

About PLS 
PLS is a leading global producer of lithium materials, with a diversified portfolio of assets and strategic 
partnerships in the rapidly growing battery materials sector. PLS owns 100% of the world’s largest, 
independent hard-rock lithium operation, the Pilgangoora Operation in Australia, and the Colina Lithium Project 
in Brazil. PLS is also integrated into the lithium value chain through its joint venture with POSCO in South 
Korea, which manufactures battery-grade lithium hydroxide. With significant scale, high-quality assets, and a 
strong commitment to advancing the global energy transition, PLS has established enduring partnerships with 
leading international companies in the sector including POSCO and Ganfeng.
