# Amcor plc (AMC.AX) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-08-16T12:56:14.741873+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Amcor plc (AMC.AX) is a global flexible and rigid packaging group listed on the ASX as CHESS Depositary Interests over NYSE-listed AMCR. The quantitative screen rates the name a **buy** (4/22 models; composite 66%), driven chiefly by **cheapness, dividend yield/growth, and risk** metrics rather than classic quality or deep-value screens. The investment hook is a **~5.6% dividend yield** on a business scaling post-Berry Global, with management lifting the quarterly payout to **US$0.65** (A$0.92 per CDI) for the period ended 30 June 2026. The central debate is whether Berry integration synergies and earnings momentum justify **materially higher leverage** (debt/equity ~128%) and a balance sheet heavily loaded with goodwill, or whether reported earnings quality and cash conversion remain too thin for a durable income compounder.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value-oriented investor, Amcor offers an income-plus-re-rating setup rather than a net-net or Graham-style defensive compounder. The screen passes **High Dividend Yield**, **Dividend Growth**, **Composite Value**, and **Earnings Quality** — three of four metric families (cheapness, dividend, risk) — with full data quality (20/20 metrics). At **P/E 19.4**, **P/B 1.8**, and **5.6% yield**, the stock screens as reasonably priced for a large-cap consumer packaging franchise with global scale, sticky customer relationships, and recurring demand across food, beverage, healthcare, and personal care.

Business quality is mixed but improving on a pro forma basis. Amcor is integrating Berry Global — news flow cites **~32% EBITDA growth** and **~US$285m synergy capture** in FY26 — which expands rigid packaging exposure and cost base. Operating cash flow remains solid (screen TTM **US$1.39bn**; FCF TTM **US$1.50bn**), supporting the dividend raise signalled in the ASX distribution filing. ROE (**9.4%**) and margins fail higher-quality screens (Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, Quality Value), reflecting acquisition-driven intangibles, integration charges, and cyclical resin/input-cost pass-through dynamics rather than a wide-moat compounder profile.

The buy case rests on: (1) **above-market yield with a recent increase**, attractive to income investors on ASX; (2) **composite value** ranking (universe ~83rd percentile; sector-relative composite 62%); (3) **earnings quality** screen pass despite GAAP noise from restructuring/M&A; and (4) a **new, improving** screen signal (conviction 24%, one week at buy). This is a **yield + deleveraging + synergy** story, not a low-P/B deep value or Piotroski quality play (F-Score **5/9**; Financial Health fails on leverage).

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Leverage and integration:** Post-Berry, debt/equity (~**128%** per screen) and net debt ~**US$13.3bn** (Yahoo FY2025) dominate the risk profile. The stock fails Graham, Schloss, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, and Financial Health screens on leverage and ROE. Integration execution, synergy realisation, and deleveraging pace are unresolved.

**Dividend coverage:** The ASX filing confirms a higher quarterly payout (US$0.65), but Yahoo FY2025 shows **cash dividends exceeding FCF**. Sustainability depends on post-integration cash generation (screen TTM FCF is higher, but not filing-verified).

**Earnings quality:** Reported net income fell **~51%** from FY2023 to FY2025 (Yahoo) amid large restructuring/M&A charges. Piotroski F-Score **5/9** — fails on ROA improvement, share dilution, gross margin, and asset turnover trends.

**Balance sheet / accounting:** **Negative tangible book** and **~US$18.7bn** goodwill/intangibles create impairment sensitivity if volumes or margins disappoint. Share count rose sharply (Berry issuance); screen flags **share dilution** failure.

**Cyclical and competitive:** Flexible packaging is exposed to **resin/raw-material inflation**, customer pass-through timing, and volume cyclicality in consumer end-markets. Sustainability regulation (plastic reduction, recycled content mandates) is an ongoing structural headwind for conventional packaging — not detailed in available filing bodies.

**Governance / listing complexity:** ASX CDI over NYSE creates **currency, withholding tax (0% franking)**, and cross-border disclosure fragmentation. Primary ASX filing coverage in this pack is limited to a dividend notice.

**Research pack gaps:** Absence of annual/interim filing bodies prevents assessment of going-concern statements, debt covenants, litigation contingencies, or customer concentration disclosures.
