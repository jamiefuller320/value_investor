SEPTEMBER 2026 QUARTERLY PERFORMANCE UPDATE 
 
Fortescue Ltd (Fortescue, ASX: FMG) provides a preliminary production and financial update for 
the September 2026 quarter (Q1 FY27). Fortescue's September 2026 Quarterly Production Report 
will be released on 22 October 2026. 
 
• 
Total iron ore shipments were 46.8 million tonnes (Mt) in Q1 FY27, six per cent lower than Q1 
FY26, including 2.5Mt from Iron Bridge. 
• 
Hematite realised price was US$80/dry metric tonne for the quarter, representing 82 per cent 
of the average Platts 61% CFR Index. 
• 
Cash was US$3.2 billion and net debt was US$2.8 billion at 30 September 2026, compared 
with net debt of US$0.9 billion at 30 June 2026. This is after payment of the FY26 final dividend 
of US$1.0 billion and capital expenditure of US$0.9 billion during the quarter. 
 
Iron ore shipments were affected by maintenance during the quarter, including scheduled port 
outload shutdown activity. Stocks across the supply chain remained healthy at quarter-end. 
 
Net operating cash flow was impacted by higher working capital, including increased product 
inventory. Iron ore sales of 42.9Mt were below shipments of 46.8Mt, reflecting impacts from 
ongoing negotiations with China Mineral Resources Group (CMRG). 
 
Guidance for FY27 shipments, C1 unit cost and capital expenditure remains unchanged, subject 
to negotiations with CMRG.
