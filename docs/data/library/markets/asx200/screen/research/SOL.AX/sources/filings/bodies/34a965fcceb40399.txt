ASX Announcement 
FY26 Full Year Results Presentation Webcast and Key Dates 
Diversified investment house, Washington H. Soul Pattinson and Company Limited (“Soul Patts”) 
(ASX:SOL), advises that its financial results for the full year ended 31 July 2026 and final 
dividend1 will be announced on Thursday, 24 September 2026.  
Soul Patts CEO and Managing Director, Todd Barlow, and Chief Financial Officer, David Grbin, 
will deliver the presentation via an audio webcast commencing at 11.00am (AEST). 
Registration for the webcast is available via this link: 
https://ccmediaframe.com/?id=LfmRglOC 
Please ensure you are registered and dial in to the webcast ahead of the commencement 
time. A recording of the webcast will be available on Soul Patts’ website following the 
presentation. 
2026 Annual General Meeting  
The 2026 Annual General Meeting (AGM) will be held on Friday, 27 November 2026. The 
closing date for receipt of director nominations for the 2026 AGM is Thursday, 24 September 
2026.  
Information on how to participate in the 2026 AGM will be included in the Notice of Meeting to 
be released to shareholders, published on the ASX announcements platform and Soul Patts 
website on Monday, 26 October 2026.
