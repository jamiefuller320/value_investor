MONADELPHOUS CONTRACTS UPDATE 
 
Engineering company Monadelphous Group Limited (ASX: MND) (“Monadelphous” or “the Company”) 
today announced the award of a number of new contracts in the resources sector valued at 
approximately $150 million.  
 
The Company has secured a four-year contract to continue providing maintenance services and minor 
projects on Rio Tinto’s marine infrastructure at the Dampier and Cape Lambert Ports in Western 
Australia. 
 
Monadelphous has also been awarded a contract with BHP associated with the Ministers North Project 
in the Pilbara region of Western Australia. The work, which includes the establishment and maintenance 
of temporary non-process infrastructure facilities and associated services, is expected to be completed 
by mid-2028. 
 
Finally, the Company has secured a three-year contract (with a two-year extension option) to continue 
providing shutdown services across Whitehaven Coal’s mine sites in Queensland and New South Wales.

About Monadelphous 
 
With over 50 years of experience, Monadelphous Group Limited (ASX: MND) is a leading Australian 
engineering group providing construction, maintenance and industrial services to the resources, energy 
and infrastructure sectors. The Company has two operating divisions − Engineering Construction, 
providing large-scale multidisciplinary and vertically integrated project management and construction 
services, and Maintenance and Industrial Services, specialising in the planning, management and 
execution of mechanical and electrical maintenance services, shutdowns and sustaining capital works.  
 
Monadelphous is headquartered in Perth, Western Australia, with a major office in Brisbane, Queensland, 
and offices, projects, facilities and workshops across Australia and in China, Mongolia, Papua New Guinea, 
Vietnam and the Philippines. Please visit www.monadelphous.com.au for further information.
