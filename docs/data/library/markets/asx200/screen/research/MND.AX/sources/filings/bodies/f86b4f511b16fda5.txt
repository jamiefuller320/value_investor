MONADELPHOUS BUSINESS UPDATE 
 
Engineering company Monadelphous Group Limited (ASX: MND) (“Monadelphous” or “the Company”) 
today provided guidance for the Company’s financial results for the half year ending 31 December 2026. 
 
The Company has experienced strong operating conditions throughout the first four months of the 
2027 financial year. Activity levels have remained elevated across the business on the back of the 
substantial level of new contracts and contract extensions awarded over the last couple of years, with a 
further $830 million in new contracts secured since 1 July 2026. 
 
Demand for engineering construction services continues to be particularly strong, contributing a larger 
proportion of the Company's total revenue than in the first half of the previous financial year. Increased 
revenue from integrated construction projects and a full six-month contribution from Kerman 
Contracting are the primary drivers of the growth. 
 
The Company is currently forecasting revenue for the half year ending 31 December 2026 to be 
approximately $1.7 billion. Operating activity will moderate in the second half of the financial year with 
the completion of work currently under way. 
 
Continued strong operational performance and growth-driven economies of scale, together with a 
substantial contribution from a number of successful projects approaching completion during the 
period, are expected to support a strong earnings result for the six months to 31 December 2026. At 
this stage the Company is forecasting net profit after tax for the half year to be in the range of $80 
million to $85 million.

About Monadelphous 
 
With over 50 years of experience, Monadelphous Group Limited (ASX: MND) is a leading Australian 
engineering group providing construction, maintenance and industrial services to the resources, energy 
and infrastructure sectors. The Company has two operating divisions − Engineering Construction, 
providing large-scale multidisciplinary and vertically integrated project management and construction 
services, and Maintenance and Industrial Services, specialising in the planning, management and 
execution of mechanical and electrical maintenance services, shutdowns and sustaining capital works.  
 
Monadelphous is headquartered in Perth, Western Australia, with a major office in Brisbane, Queensland, 
and offices, projects, facilities and workshops across Australia and in China, Mongolia, Papua New Guinea, 
Vietnam and the Philippines. Please visit www.monadelphous.com.au for further information.
