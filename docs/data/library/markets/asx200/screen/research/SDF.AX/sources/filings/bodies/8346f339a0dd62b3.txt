Steadfast Group operates insurance broker and agency Networks in Australia, New Zealand, 
Singapore and the USA. The brokers and agencies in Steadfast’s Networks place around $25  billion 
in gross written premium annually. 
Steadfast provides a broad range of services and solutions to support the broker and agency 
businesses in its Networks, including market access, technology, risk solutions and operational 
support. Steadfast also acts as a long-term partner by offering its members equity solutions to 
support succession, perpetuation and acquisition growth. 
Steadfast has a majority shareholding in a portfolio of underwriting agencies providing specialist 
insurance products in niche market segments to the open market. 
Steadfast also owns an established Lloyd's broking operation, offering wholesale placement for 
brokers and agents around the world as well as direct insurance solutions.
