November 9, 2026
Life360 Reports Q3 2026 Results
Record third quarter global net Paying Circle additions of 188 thousand, reaching 3.4 million
Monthly Active User additions of 5.3 million, reaching 107.7 million 
Total revenue grew 40% year-over-year to $174.1 million 
SAN FRANCISCO, California. Life360, Inc. (Life360 or the Company) (NASDAQ: LIF, ASX: 360), the 
provider of the market-leading family connection and safety mobile application, today announced unaudited 
financial results for the third quarter (Q3'26) ended September 30, 2026. 
Life360 delivered strong quarterly results, with its highest-ever net Paying Circle additions for a third quarter, 
and international Monthly Active Users (MAU) crossing over the 50 million mark. 
“Life360 continues to demonstrate its invaluable place in everyday family life and translate that into 
meaningful value for our shareholders. Our impressive third quarter operating and financial results are a 
direct reflection of the trust families place in us to keep them connected and safe,” said Life360 Chief 
Executive Officer Lauren Antonoff. “We saw healthy MAU growth in Q3, with 5.3 million net new active 
members bringing us to 107.7 million MAU globally, and we added a third-quarter record of 188,000 net new 
Paying Circles. Our subscription engine continues to get even stronger, and early results from our U.S. price 
changes are encouraging. In advertising, we’re building the foundation for a much larger business, with 
growing proof points from leading brands providing positive signals for 2027. Meanwhile, as consumers 
adopt personal AI assistants, we’re making Life360’s family intelligence available to third-party AI agents to 
simplify everyday coordination through the tools families already use. Members decide whether to share 
their Circle’s information with an agent. We believe AI agents are a meaningful opportunity to increase the 
value we deliver.”
"As foreshadowed in our Q2’26 results, Q3’26 delivered revenue growth acceleration, along with moderating 
operating expense growth following a period of investment in the first half," said Chief Financial Officer 
Russell Burke. "Quarterly revenue grew 40% year-over-year to $174.1 million. Looking ahead to Q4’26, we 
expect continued revenue momentum underpinned by recent subscription price increases and the seasonal 
uplift in advertising, combined with cost efficiency and seasonally lower growth media spend. We expect 
strengthening operating leverage as we continue to balance growth investment with our commitment to 
margin expansion.
"We ended Q3’26 with $465.0 million in cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and short-term investments, 
and we generated operating cash flows of $27.2 million, up 3% year-over-year. Following the 
announcement of our share repurchase program in May 2026, we have repurchased shares of common 
stock for an aggregate purchase consideration of $44.4 million, leaving approximately $180.6 million 
available for future repurchases under the program. This is in line with our commitment to return value to 
our shareholders and reflects the same disciplined approach we outlined when the Board authorized the 
program. We plan to offset dilution measured by stock-based compensation expense on a rolling 12 month 
basis from the launch of the program. We will remain opportunistic and retain the flexibility to adjust the 
pace and timing to market conditions.”

Adjusted EBITDA
To supplement our consolidated financial statements prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP, we 
use certain non-GAAP financial measures, as described below, to facilitate analysis of our financial and 
business trends and for internal planning and forecasting purposes. For more information, see the 
“Supplementary and Non-GAAP Financial Information” section below.
Non-GAAP financial measures include adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and 
amortization (Adjusted EBITDA) and Adjusted EBITDA Margin. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income, 
excluding (i) gain on change in fair value of investments, (ii) provision for (benefit from) income taxes, (iii) 
depreciation and amortization, (iv) interest income, (v) other expense, net, (vi) acquisition-related 
transaction and integration costs, (vii) stock-based compensation, (viii) channel restructuring costs, (ix) 
workplace restructuring costs, and (x) warehouse relocation costs. These items are excluded from Adjusted 
EBITDA because they are non-cash in nature, because the amount and timing of these items are 
unpredictable, or because they are not driven by core results of operations and render comparisons with 
prior periods and competitors less meaningful.

About Life360
Life360, a family connection and safety company, keeps people close to the ones they love. The category-
leading mobile app and hardware tracking devices empower members to stay connected to the people, 
pets, and things they care about most, with a range of services, including location sharing, safe driver 
reports, and crash detection with emergency dispatch. As a remote-first company based in the San 
Francisco Bay Area, Life360 serves approximately 107.7 million monthly active users (MAU), as of 
September 30, 2026, across more than 180 countries. Life360 delivers peace of mind and enhances 
everyday family life in all the moments that matter, big and small. For more information, please visit 
life360.com.
