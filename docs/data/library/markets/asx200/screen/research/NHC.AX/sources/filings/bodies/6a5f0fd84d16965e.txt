Investor and Analyst Call Details 
New Hope Corporation Limited (ASX:NHC) (the Company) will release its Quarterly Activities Report for 
the quarter ending 31 October 2026 on Monday 16 November 2026.  
The Company’s Chief Executive Officer, Rob Bishop, and members of the Executive Leadership Team will 
host an investor and analyst call via webcast to discuss the Quarterly Activities Report and the Company’s 
performance during the quarter, and answer investor and analyst questions.  
The webcast will commence at 11:00am AEDT / 10:00am AEST.  
Registration for the webcast is available via the following link: 
https://ccmediaframe.com/?id=8O47mXWD 
Participants are encouraged to register well in advance to ensure their registrations are recorded in time for 
the start of the call. Please ensure you are registered and dial in to the webcast at least five minutes before 
the scheduled commencement time.  
A transcript of the call will be available following the call on the Company’s website at 
https://newhopegroup.com.au/results-and-reports/#quarterly-reports.
