Chief Financial Officer Monique Connolly has exercised 36,931 vested performance rights at an exercise price of A$0.04 with an expiry date of 17 October 2034 and 41,464 vested performance rights at an exercise price of $0.04 with an expiry date of 26 April 2035. The performance rights converted into 78,395 ordinary shares in Greatland. The conversion was satisfied using existing ordinary shares held in Greatland's employee share trust, with no new ordinary shares being issued as a result of the exercise. Ms Connolly retains ownership of the 78,398 ordinary shares resulting from the exercise and conversion of the performance rights.
