ASX RELEASE 
 
Annual General Meeting Webcast 
 
 
5 December 2018 
 
 
Incitec Pivot Limited ( ASX : IPL) wishes to advise that a webcast audio facility will be available 
for the 2018 Annual General Meeting, which is being held at 10.30 am (Melbourne time) on 
Thursday, 20 December 2018 at the Clarendon Auditorium, Level 2, Melbourne Exhibition 
Centre, 2 Clarendon Street, South Wharf. 
 
Details of the webcast are available via the Investor Relations home page of IPL’s website, 
www.incitecpivot.com.au. 
 
Please note the webcast will not be interactive.  Accordingly, shareholders who wish to vote at 
the meeting should attend in person or by proxy.  Proxy forms were sent to shareholders on 
19 November 2018 and additional forms may be obtained by contacting IPL's share registry, 
Link Market Services Limited on +61 1300 303 780. 
 
Full details regarding the 2018 Annual General Meeting are contained in the Notice of Annual 
General Meeting, which was sent to shareholders and lodged with the ASX on 19 November 
2018.
