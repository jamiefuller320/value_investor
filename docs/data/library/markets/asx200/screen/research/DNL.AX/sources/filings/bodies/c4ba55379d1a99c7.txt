Record explosives performance delivers strong FY26 results  
FY26 performance 
• 
Zero Harm: Total Recordable Injury Frequency Rate (TRIFR) for the rolling 12-month 
period ended 30 September 2026 was 1.01, up from 0.951 at 30 September 2025 
• 
Statutory Net Profit after Tax and IMIs2: $66m (FY25: $53m loss) / NPAT ex IMIs: 
$331m (FY25: $423m) 
• 
Statutory EBIT ex IMIs: $504m (FY25: $714m) / EBITDA ex IMIs: $778m (FY25: 
$1,012m) 
• 
Explosives underlying EBIT ex IMIs: $485m (FY25: $434m) / EBITDA ex IMIs: $759m 
(FY25: $702m) 
• 
Earnings Per Share ex IMIs: 18.8 cents per share (FY25: 22.8 cps) 
• 
Return on Invested Capital including goodwill (ROIC): 6.8% (FY25: 8.2%) 
• 
Final dividend: 5.0 cents per share (50% franked) representing a ~50% payout ratio 
• 
Growth markets: Strong performances from Canada and WA, with new contracts 
commencing in Africa and LATAM     
• 
Energetics: US$581m IMX project awarded to the Nitradyn JV in September 
• 
Fertilisers separation: concluded with the sale of Phosphate Hill in June 
• 
Transformation program: delivered $185m of EBIT to date and a 70% FY26 exit run rate3 
FY26 highlights  
Dyno Nobel (ASX:DNL) has delivered another year of strong underlying4 earnings growth 
in the explosives business, with EBIT of $485m up 12% on the prior corresponding period. 
Record performances were delivered from DNA and DNAP, while DNEL continued to 
build momentum across its expanding operations in key growth regions, despite 
economic challenges in Europe. The result highlights the resilience of the established 
explosives businesses and continued progress against the Company’s transformation 
program, which was supported by record joint venture income in FY26.  
The sale of Phosphate Hill in June marked the conclusion of the Fertilisers separation 
and achieved Dyno Nobel’s strategic objective of transforming into a pure play 
explosives leader. 
Significant progress was also made on the Company’s growth objectives with the 
Nitradyn JV awarded a US$581m IMX project to be built at Dyno Nobel’s Graham, 
Kentucky site. Combined with the TNT project announced in FY25, these projects total 
US$1bn of government funded energetics contracts awarded to date.

Commentary from Dyno Nobel’s CEO & Managing Director 
CEO & Managing Director, Mauro Neves, said: 
“FY26 has been an outstanding year for Dyno Nobel. We delivered on earnings guidance 
provided at this time last year, completed our separation from the Fertilisers business, 
and made significant progress on our energetics growth objectives. 
“Safety continues to be our top priority, and despite a rise in our total recordable injury 
frequency rate (TRIFR) in FY26, I am pleased to report the second half saw a material 
TRIFR improvement with zero process safety incidents, and no incidents classified as 
serious harm for the year. We will continue our focus on field leadership and proactive 
hazard identification, with focused efforts on high-risk, high-consequence activities, 
particularly in explosives and process safety. 
“Our transformation program has continued to deliver excellent results, driving record 
earnings performances from both DNA and DNAP, supported by favourable demand 
across our established markets and the continued success of Dyno Nobel's technology-
led solutions. Despite scheduled turnarounds impacting earnings in FY27, we expect to 
finish the year strongly with an exit run rate of 85% - 95% of the estimated $300m uplift 
from the program. 
“This continued transformation success provides a clear pathway to deliver our FY28 EBIT 
ambition of $600m, highlighting the resilience of our business through ongoing global 
volatility. With increasing confidence in our longer-term growth aspirations, we also

Underlying business performance5 
Dyno Nobel Asia Pacific (DNAP): EBIT of $281m, +10% YoY (FY25: $255m). DNAP delivered 
record EBIT despite wet weather impacts in the first half of the year and global market 
disruption stemming from the Middle East conflict. The metals segment was the primary 
driver of growth, following new customer wins and the successful renewal of contracts. 
Record production levels were achieved at the Helidon facility with the introduction of an 
automated assembly line, with production capacity expanded at Moranbah through the 
successful commissioning of a key debottlenecking project. The Malaysian business also 
delivered a strong result, exceeding expectations. 
Dyno Nobel Americas (DNA): EBIT of $224m, +19% YoY (FY25: $189m). The record DNA 
performance (EBIT +28% YoY in USD) was driven by successful transformation initiatives 
and favourable market conditions. The strong Metals performance across copper, gold 
and iron ore continued to be underpinned by medium-term structural demand drivers in 
metals markets. Quarry & construction growth was driven by a significant uplift in civil 
and technology related spending through federal infrastructure funding commitments, 
substantial investment in AI data centres and electrification initiatives. Continued 
material growth in joint venture income reflected the strength of the domestic market 
and the strategic value of DNA’s joint venture model. 
Dyno Nobel EMEA & LATAM (DNEL): EBIT of $18m, -40% YoY (FY25: $30m). The DNEL 
result reflected economic challenges in Europe, including disruption to key shipping 
routes due to the Middle East conflict, reduced quarry & construction spending in 
France and adverse weather conditions in Chile. DNEL made meaningful strategic

Fertilisers separation concluded 
Separation of the Fertilisers business concluded in FY26 with the sale of Phosphate Hill 
to Ryowa II GPS Pty Ltd (Ryowa), a Queensland based investment group, on 30 June 
2026. Ryowa is a wholly owned subsidiary of Mayfair Australia Corporation. 
Capital management initiatives 
Dyno Nobel ceased its buyback program in September 2026, maintaining balance 
sheet strength for high-returning opportunities across energetics and commercial 
explosives. A total of $574m worth of shares were bought back at an average price of 
$3.01 since the program commenced in July 2024.  
FY27 outlook 
FY27 underlying EBIT guidance for Dyno Nobel is expected to be ~$515m-$555m, with an
