Third Quarter 2026 Results
• Production: Alumina production increased 11 percent sequentially to 2.5 million metric tons primarily due to improved
stability at the Pinjarra, Australia refinery. In the Aluminum segment, production increased 2 percent sequentially to 649,000
metric tons primarily due to capacity restarts completed in the second quarter 2026 and the completion of the Alumar smelter
restart in the third quarter 2026.
• Shipments: In the Alumina segment, third-party shipments of alumina increased 2 percent sequentially primarily due to
higher production. In Aluminum, total shipments decreased 5 percent sequentially following elevated second quarter 2026
shipment volumes associated with first quarter inventory repositioning.
• Revenue: The Company generated third-party revenue of $3.7 billion, a 7 percent sequential decrease. Alumina segment
third-party revenue increased 7 percent, supported by higher average realized alumina prices and shipments. Aluminum
segment third-party revenue reflected lower shipments and average realized prices, resulting in a 9 percent sequential
decrease.
• Net income attributable to Alcoa Corporation was $483 million, or $1.81 per share. Sequentially, the results reflect
favorable mark-to-market changes on the Saudi Arabian Mining Company (Ma’aden) shares and energy contracts, improved
production costs, and higher alumina prices, partially offset by lower aluminum prices and shipments.
• Adjusted net income attributable to Alcoa Corporation was $485 million, or $1.82 per share, excluding the impact from net
special items of $2 million. Notable special items include mark-to-market gains on energy contracts of $32 million, a mark-to-
market gain on the Ma’aden shares of $28 million, demolition and remediation costs at closed locations of $25 million, and
external costs related to the AliGroup acquisition of $14 million.
• Adjusted EBITDA excluding special items was $789 million, as improved production costs in both the Aluminum and
Alumina segments and higher alumina prices were more than offset by lower aluminum prices and shipments, resulting in a
sequential decrease of $112 million.
• Cash: Cash provided from operations was $643 million. Cash provided from financing activities was $2.6 billion, reflecting
financing obtained for the AliGroup acquisition. Cash used for investing activities was $183 million, including capital
expenditures of $171 million. Free cash flow was $472 million. Alcoa ended the quarter with a cash balance of $4.4 billion.
• Working capital: For the third quarter, Receivables from customers of $1.3 billion, Inventories of $2.6 billion and Accounts
payable, trade of $1.9 billion comprised DWC working capital. Alcoa reported 50 days working capital, reflecting a sequential
increase of 4 days primarily on an increase in inventory days due to higher aluminum volumes. Days working capital was
consistent with the third quarter 2025.
Key Actions
• AliGroup acquisition:
o During the third quarter 2026, Alcoa continued to advance the AliGroup acquisition, progressing regulatory approvals as well
as transition and integration planning. On October 15, 2026, South32 shareholders approved the acquisition. The
transaction remains on track to close in the first half of 2027.
o On September 23, 2026, Alcoa completed offerings of $1.5 billion aggregate principal amount of 6.625 percent senior notes
due 2034 and $1.1 billion aggregate principal amount of 6.875 percent senior notes due 2036 (the Notes). The Company
intends to use the proceeds, together with cash on hand, to fund the cash consideration of approximately $3.1 billion for the
AliGroup acquisition as well as related fees and expenses. Upon completion of the debt offerings, the Company terminated
the remaining outstanding commitments for bridge financing.
• Massena East agreement: During the third quarter 2026, Alcoa entered into definitive agreements to sell portions of the
Massena East site. The transaction creates the potential for meaningful cash proceeds as approved data center capacity is
developed by the buyer. Closing is expected within the next six months. At closing, Alcoa expects to receive approximately
$50 million to $65 million, depending on the amount of approved data center capacity. Alcoa may receive an additional
approximately $60 million to $250 million for approved capacity expansions and capacity-based installment payments as
approved capacity is commissioned during the seven-year period following closing, with most of the proceeds expected to be
received during the first five years. Closing is subject to permitting and regulatory approvals, satisfaction of the conditions
required to begin construction, and other customary closing conditions.

2026 Outlook
The Company does not provide reconciliations of the forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures Adjusted EBITDA and
Adjusted Net Income, including transformation, intersegment eliminations and other corporate Adjusted EBITDA; operational tax
expense; and other expense; each excluding special items, to the most directly comparable forward-looking GAAP financial
measures because it is impractical to forecast certain special items, such as restructuring charges and mark-to-market contracts,
without unreasonable efforts due to the variability and complexity associated with predicting the occurrence and financial impact of

Alcoa reaffirms its 2026 Alumina segment production and shipment projection of 9.5 to 9.6 million metric tons and 11.5 to 11.6
million metric tons, respectively. The difference between production and shipments reflects trading volumes and externally
sourced alumina to fulfill customer contracts.
Alcoa reaffirms its 2026 Aluminum segment production and shipment projection of 2.4 to 2.6 million metric tons and 2.6 to 2.8
million metric tons, respectively.
For the fourth quarter 2026, the Company expects to maintain the strong operational performance delivered in the third quarter
2026. Higher production and shipments are expected in both segments, as improved refining system stability is maintained and
the smelting portfolio continues to deliver incremental output.
Within the fourth quarter 2026 Alumina Segment Adjusted EBITDA, the Company expects performance to be consistent
sequentially.
Within the fourth quarter 2026 Aluminum Segment Adjusted EBITDA, the Company expects higher raw material costs and
planned maintenance, intended to support continued operational stability, to result in unfavorable impacts of approximately $30
million sequentially. Alumina costs in the Aluminum segment are expected to be unfavorable by approximately $30 million
sequentially.
Other income is expected to increase by approximately $20 million in the fourth quarter 2026 due to higher interest earned on
cash held for the AliGroup acquisition.
Alcoa expects Interest expense to approximate $185 million for the year, an increase from the prior projection, as a result of the
Notes issuance.
Based on current alumina and aluminum market conditions, Alcoa expects fourth quarter 2026 operational tax expense to
approximate $65 million to $75 million, which may vary with market conditions and jurisdictional profitability.
