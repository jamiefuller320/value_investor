Subscription, Director’s Dealing and RPT Amazing AI PLC 23 June 2025 23 June 2025 Amazing AI plc (" AAI ", the " Company " or the " Group ") Subscription, Director's Dealing, Related Party Transaction and Issue of Equity Amazing AI plc (AQSE: AAI) - 23 June 2025: AAI, a global fintech group specialising in online consumer loans, announced that it has raised £122,849.44 via a subscription through the issue of 24,569,888 new ordinary shares of £0.005 each in the Company (" Subscription Shares ") at a price of £0.005 per share (" Subscription ") from existing shareholders. The funds raised will be used to fund the Company's business plan and for general working capital purposes. While the Subscription, along with the Amended Loan Facility announced on 16 June 2025, strengthens capital availability, the Company will continue to monitor its working capital position and is evaluating further financing options to support its growth strategy. Application has been made for the 24,569,888 Subscription Shares to be admitted to trading on AQSE Growth Market (" Admission ") and it is expected that Admission will take place on, or around, 27 June 2025. Following Admission of the Subscription Shares, the Company's enlarged issued share capital will comprise 113,178,559 ordinary shares. The Company does not hold any shares in treasury.
