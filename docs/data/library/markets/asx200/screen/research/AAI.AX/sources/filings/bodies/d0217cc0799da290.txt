No idea! No expectations and definitely no predictions, remember (prediction is futile because the future is unknown!)? What I am pretty darn sure of , though, is that we'll see the following technical factors occur around the time it does : Candles: Supply-side candles (i.e., black-bodied candles and or upward pointing shadows) = ⚠️ Price action : A lower peak, and or a lower trough (i.e., supply reinforcement and demand removal) = ⚠️ Short term trend ribbon : The XJO price will close below the short term trend ribbon (i.e., the short term trend ribbon is no longer acting as a zone of dynamic excess demand) = ⚠️ View: Until I see at least some of the above, I'll remain FRP ( F ull R isk P osition = My personal allowable capital allocation limit for my investments in Australian stocks is 100%).
