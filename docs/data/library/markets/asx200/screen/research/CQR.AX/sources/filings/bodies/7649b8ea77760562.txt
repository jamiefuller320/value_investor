Charter Hall Retail REIT (ASX: CQR) today announced the appointment of Ms Kate Melrose as an 
Independent Non-Executive Director of the Responsible Entity for CQR, Charter Hall Retail 
Management Limited. Ms Melrose's appointment takes effect today. 
Kate has 30 years' experience in ASX-listed and private entities, with a background spanning real 
estate asset classes in valuation, property economics, research, and transactions roles. Kate's 
executive career includes 14 years with Lendlease across development, marketing and strategy, and 
10 years as Executive General Manager, Project Sales, Stakeholder Engagement and Innovation at 
Ingenia Communities. 
Kate is a Graduate of the Australian Institute of Company Directors (GAICD), holds a Bachelor of 
Business (Land Economics), a Real Estate Licence (NSW), and is a member of the Urban 
Development Institute of Australia (UDIA) and a committee member of the Australian Property Institute 
(API). 
CQR also announces the retirement of Michael Gorman from the Board, effective at the conclusion of 
CQR’s annual unitholder meeting in October.  
CHRML Chairman, Roger Davis, said: “On behalf of the Board, I am pleased to welcome Kate as a 
Non-Executive Director. Her deep property experience across a range of sectors will be a valuable 
addition to the Board. I would also like to thank Michael Gorman for his significant contribution to CQR 
over the past 10 years, particularly during its transition beyond traditional shopping centres to its 
current market-leading convenience retail platform.” 
Announcement authorised by the CHRML Board
