Management team strengthened with 
Appointment of Chief Financial Officer & Chief 
Operating Officer 
 
Catalyst Metals Limited ( Catalyst or the Company) (ASX:CYL) is pleased to announce the 
appointment of Mr Ben MacKinnon and Rob Williamson as Chief Financial Officer  and Chief 
Operating Officer, respectively.  
Mr MacKinnon is a Chartered Accountant with over 2 5 years’ experience across mining , 
mining contracting services, audit, capital markets, acquisitions and financings. Most recently, 
Mr MacKinnon was Chief Financial Officer of copper and zinc producer Develop Global Limited 
from 2023 to 2026.  Prior to this Mr MacKinnon held the role of Chief Financial Officer for 
private drilling contractor DDH1 Drilling where he oversaw multiple acquisitions  and 
ultimately the listing of DDH1 on the ASX.  
Mr Williamson is a Mining Engineer with over 20 years of experience both internationally and 
domestically. Most recently he was Executive General Manager for Northern Star’s Yandal 
operations overseeing the Jundee and Thunderbox operating centres. Prior to this Rob worked 
with Saracen as General Manager for Carosue Dam. Rob’s years of experience have been spent 
across several other commodities and disciplines including various base metals, tunnelling and 
mining contracting roles.  
The Board also thanks Mr  Sylvain Guillaume who stepped into the role as interim CFO over 
the past 12 months, playing an integral role in the business during a period of uncertainty. Mr 
Guillaume will remain with Catalyst leading commercial projects.  
Managing Director & CEO, James Champion de Crespigny said: 
We are pleased to have Rob and Ben joining us before the end of the year. Both bring a new 
take on the business. Their operationally focused, contracting mindset we hope places the 
Company in good stead for the next phase of the business’ life.  
I’d like to take the time to mention Sylvain Guillaume – a key member of the team for the last 
three years and who has stepped into a difficult situation, as he often does, and steered the 
finance department over the last 12 months.  
It is through Ben, Rob and Anthony Buckingham, we will manage the key value drivers of the 
business – finance and capital management, operations and project management.  
The Board welcome Mr MacKinnon and Mr Williamson to the Company and looks forward to 
their contribution to the Company’s growth.  
 
This announcement has been approved for release by the Board of Directors of Catalyst
