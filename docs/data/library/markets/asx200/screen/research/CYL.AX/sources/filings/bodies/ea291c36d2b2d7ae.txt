ASX Announcement 
 
MULTIPLE ZONES OF ANOMALOUS BASE METALS 
INTERSECTED AT YELLOW MOUNTAIN 
 
 
 
Highlights 
• Initial Reverse Circulation (“RC”) drilling of the large eastern Induced 
Polarisation (“IP”) target at Yellow Mountain in New South Wales has 
intersected multiple zones of anomalous copper, lead and zinc mineralisation, 
confirming the presence of base metals within the broader target area. 
• Two RC holes (YMRC101 and YMRC102) tested portions of a substantial 750m-
long IP chargeability anomaly, situated east of the previously reported high-
grade polymetallic intercepts, including 113m @ 1.17% CuEq1.  
• Initial drilling intersected zones of low-grade copper, zinc, lead and silver, up to 
25m width in YMRC101 and 25m width in YMRC102, within the broader 750m-
long IP chargeability anomaly. 
• The eastern IP anomaly extends approximately 750m along strike, with the 
majority of the chargeability response remaining untested by drilling.  
• The potential for base metal sulphide mineralisation elsewhere within the 
anomaly remains highly prospective and is yet to be assessed. 
• Drilling at Yellow Mountain and Overflow was interrupted due to drilling 
contractor operational issues, resulting in the early cessation of the program. 
Alchemy is progressing arrangements with an alternative drilling contractor to 
complete the remaining ~13 planned holes, including priority targets at Yellow 
Mountain and Overflow. 
Alchemy Resources Limited (ASX: ALY) (“Alchemy” or “the Company”) is pleased to announce 
the receipt of initial assays from the RC drilling program at the Company’s 80% owned Yellow 
Mountain Project in New South Wales. 
 
Chief Executive Officer Mr James Wilson commented: “Initial drilling of the large 750-metre-
long eastern IP anomaly at Yellow Mountain has intersected zones of base-metal anomalism, 
providing valuable geological information to guide further exploration for higher-grade 
sulphide mineralisation along strike and at depth. Importantly, we have several compelling 
targets yet to be tested, including the western IP anomaly along strike from our previous high-
grade polymetallic intercepts of 113 metres at 1.17% CuEq, as well as extensions to known high-

YELLOW MOUNTAIN PROJECT 
The Yellow Mountain Project is located 200km southeast of Cobar in New South Wales (NSW) 
where the historic mine workings were reported from the mid-1800s. Accurate production 
records do not exist for the mine; however, the mine reportedly produced 2.74t of lead, 360kg 
of copper and 6.2kg of silver from an open pit2. A maiden RC drill program completed in early 
September 2025 returned significant intercepts including 113m @ 1.17% CuEq in YMRC004 and 
31m @ 1.54% CuEq in YMRC0051. IP surveys were completed in early 2026 and outlined 
numerous coincident chargeability anomalies over areas of known mineralisation, as well as 
areas that will be tested with the current program.   
Figure 2: Alchemy’s Yellow Mountain Project location 
RC DRILL PROGRAM  
Following the significant 2025 drilling results at Yellow Mountain, Alchemy identified two 
substantial IP targets exceeding 30 mV/V, known as T1 and T2. At this stage the initial drill holes 
on the T2 target have been completed. Availability issues with the drilling contractor have 
interrupted the drilling program, with the contractor completing two (2) of the fifteen (15) 
planned holes before drilling was suspended. Alchemy is sourcing a replacement contractor 
to complete the remaining holes, which is expected to occur in November 2026.  
Eastern IP Target (T2): Initial drilling of two holes into the extensive 750m-long eastern IP anomaly 
has successfully identified broad zones of copper, lead and zinc anomalism, providing 
encouraging evidence of base-metal mineralisation associated with this large geophysical 
target. These results support further exploration of this potentially separate mineralised system 
and importantly, the majority of the 750m strike extent remains untested. Together with the 
potential for depth extension, this provides significant scope to investigate higher-grade

Next Steps 
• Complete the remaining RC drill holes at Yellow Mountain and Overflow, including 
testing the high-priority western IP target (T1) and extensions to known high-grade gold 
mineralisation at Overflow. 
 
• Review and interpret geological and structural data from the drilling to better 
understand the controls on copper, base-metal and gold mineralisation across both 
projects.  
 
• Integrate drilling assays with geological logging and geophysical datasets to refine the 
interpretation of mineralised structures and identify potential higher-grade zones. 
 
• Evaluate the remaining strike and depth potential of the eastern IP anomaly (T2) at 
Yellow Mountain, incorporating results from the initial two drill holes. 
 
• Prioritise follow-up exploration and drilling targets based on the combined geological, 
geochemical and geophysical results, with a focus on identifying extensions to known 
mineralisation and new discoveries.

All data below was used to calculate CuEq calculations in Alchemy’s ASX Announcement 
dated 1 October 2025 “Yellow Mountain drilling delivers outstanding intercept of 113m @ 1.17% 
CuEq” including recoveries and commodity prices at the time.  
Copper Equivalent “CuEq” grade is estimated with the following formula: 
CuEqInsitu % = (1.000 * Cu %) + (1.1989 * Au g/t) + (0.0146 * Ag g/t) + (0.1899 * Pb %) + (0.2895 
* Zn %) 
 
CuEqRecovered % = (0.8000 * Cu %) + (0.9112 * Au g/t) + (0.0094 * Ag g/t) + (0.1501 * Pb %) + 
(0.1737 * Zn %) 
(*Troy Ounce = 31.1034768g) 
It is the Company's opinion that elements included in the metal equivalent calculation have 
a reasonable potential to be recovered (as evidenced in similar multi-commodity mines) and 
sold.
