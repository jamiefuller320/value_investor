Operational excellence underpins cash flow generation and unlocks interim dividend 
“Stanmore delivered another strong operational performance in the third quarter, with South Walker Creek achieving 
new quarterly records for both ROM and saleable production following our deliberate prioritisation of stripping and pit 
preparation earlier in the year. Most importantly, these outcomes were achieved with no serious accidents during the 
quarter. 
Seaborne supply tightness continued to support market conditions during the quarter, with disruptions across key 
Russian and Chinese production basins driving coal prices higher. This translated to an average sales price of US$163/t 
in the third quarter, up 6% quarter-on-quarter; 18% higher than the same period last year on a year-to-date basis. These 
improved market conditions, combined with strong operational performance, supported positive cash flow during the 
quarter. Together with the Eagle Downs stamp duty refund, this contributed to a US$53 million reduction in net debt. 
At the half year, the Board prudently deferred an interim dividend to preserve balance sheet flexibility, particularly given 
the second-half weighting of coal sales and uncertainty surrounding the then pending Moranbah South acquisition. 
With a strong third quarter now delivered, improved year-to-date cash generation, supportive market conditions and 
robust liquidity, we are pleased to report that the Board has resolved to declare a fully franked interim dividend of 2.2 
US cents per share. 
Looking ahead, we enter the fourth quarter with healthy ROM stocks and production tracking towards the top end of 
our reaffirmed full-year saleable production guidance. At the same time, we’re focused on completing the Moranbah 
South acquisition, a compelling opportunity to add a significant resource right next door to Eagle Downs and the Isaac 
Plains Complex.” 
Marcelo Matos, Chief Executive Officer & Executive Director

Operational Highlights 
Safety 
Stanmore continues to demonstrate strong safety performance, with no serious accidents recorded during the 
quarter. The rolling 12-month Serious Accident Frequency Rate (SAFR) remained steady at 0.53 as at 30 September 
2026, remaining well below the industry average2. This result reflects continued focus on managing critical risks and 
strengthening control effectiveness across operations. 
South Walker Creek 
Following a deliberate approach in the first half of 2026 to prioritise stripping and pit preparation, South Walker Creek 
delivered 2.8Mt Run of Mine (ROM) production and 1.9Mt saleable production in the third quarter, surpassing its 
previous quarterly ROM production record of 2.5Mt set in the December 2025 quarter. This record mining volume, 
together with strong CHPP performance also translated to a quarterly record for saleable production. With healthy 
ROM stocks heading into the fourth quarter, South Walker Creek is well-positioned to meet its saleable production 
Guidance for the full year, which remains unchanged. 
Poitrel 
Poitrel’s third-quarter performance reflected the planned CHPP shutdown, deferred from June and completed in July. 
Saleable production decreased quarter-on-quarter as CHPP feed was constrained during the shutdown, before 
processing rates returned to normal in August and September. ROM volumes were strong at 1.9Mt, supporting a 
build-up in ROM coal inventories at quarter end and positioning the operation well for the fourth quarter. Year-to-date 
saleable production of 3.6Mt remains on track towards the upper end of full-year Guidance. 
Isaac Plains Complex 
As previously announced, Stanmore assumed full control of mining operations at Isaac Downs during the quarter. 
The truck and excavator fleets delivered a strong performance, which together with an optimisation of the dragline 
dig methodology supported operational efficiencies, resulting in improved strip ratio, saleable production and total 
coal sales quarter-on-quarter. 
Development and Exploration Projects 
Exploration 
Exploration expenditure was approximately A$4.9 million in the third quarter, supporting resource definition and 
evaluation across the portfolio. The Isaac Downs Extension 3D seismic program and the 2D seismic programs at 
South Walker Creek and Bee Creek concluded during the quarter.

Projects 
During the quarter, Stanmore announced it has agreed to acquire 100% of the Moranbah South tenements from 
Exxaro Resources for US$105 million1. The tenements are located immediately adjacent to Eagle Downs and the 
Isaac Plains Complex and contain 724Mt of Measured and Indicated Coal Resources in the Goonyella Middle Seam, 
expected to be of premium hard coking coal quality.  
Completion is anticipated within the fourth quarter of 2026, conditional on Exxaro completing its acquisition of Anglo 
American's 50% interest in the tenements and on certain limited conditions precedent. The Eagle Downs studies 
continue to progress, and we intend to incorporate the Moranbah South deposit (upon completion of the transaction) 
into a broader study scope as a potential integrated joint development with Eagle Downs, among other options. 
The Isaac Downs Extension project continued to progress through the approvals process during the quarter. Detailed 
infrastructure design scopes were also issued for tender, positioning the project for early development once 
approvals are received. 
 
Corporate 
Stanmore concluded the quarter with total cash of US$231 million, which, after factoring in the balance of the new 
term loan of US$250 million, translates to net debt2 of US$19 million. This is an improvement of US$53 million over 
the quarter, with cash flows supported by improved market conditions and the second-half weighted sales profile. 
Key non-operational cash flow items included US$22 million of capital expenditures and an initial payment of US$5 
million towards the acquisition of Moranbah South. These outflows were partially offset by a refund of US$26 million 
received during the quarter, following Stanmore's successful objection to stamp duty paid in 2025 as part of the 
acquisition of the Eagle Downs project. 
The debt refinancing was completed during the quarter, with the new US$250 million term loan drawn and the 
maturity of the US$200 million revolving credit facility extended. Including available cash and undrawn debt capacity, 
Stanmore’s liquidity3 was US$501 million as at 30 September 2026, providing a strong platform to fund the remaining 
US$100 million completion payment for Moranbah South. 
In determining the fully franked dividend of 2.2 US cents per share (approximately US$20 million), the Board 
considered the Company’s year-to-date cash generation, balance sheet strength, liquidity and committed payment 
obligations, including the expected completion payment for Moranbah South. The Board is satisfied that the 
declaration of the interim dividend is consistent with Stanmore’s dividend policy and its disciplined approach to 
capital allocation.

Metallurgical Coal Markets 
Concurrent shortages of coal from key Russian and Chinese production basins became the primary driver for pricing 
during the quarter. For China, the impacts of safety inspections and curtailment of domestic production volumes in 
excess of permitted limits, in combination with a reduction in clearance rates of Mongolian landborne volumes drove 
a supply shortage and a rapid escalation in domestic pricing. As a result, Chinese imports of seaborne coking coal 
increased materially during the quarter with a high premium payable for any prompt cargo availability.   
Concurrently, Russian exports were materially disrupted from the Black Sea terminals due to escalation of vessel 
strikes. These volumes were not fully replaced with exports from Baltic or Far East terminals.  In addition, further 
economic and availability pressures from fuel and labour inputs meant Russian offered pricing for exports increased 
materially through the quarter. Amid this environment, delays and reduced availability were observed from key North 
American coal export terminals.   
The net effect drove FOB Australia seaborne premium coking coal pricing to a peak of US$285 per tonne in the 
quarter. Australian production in the quarter was strong, with favourable weather and a return to production from 
previously idled mines, however, producers appear to have capitalised on the very high demand from China and 
inventory availability in Australia is expected to be very limited.  
The escalation in coal and broader energy prices has placed pressure on steel margins, including within China. The 
direction of steel production post-holiday season in China, as well as any potential trade flow outcomes from the 
tariff discussions between China and the United States will be important factors in the fourth quarter. 
Notwithstanding that, India’s emergence from the monsoon season, together with the commissioning of new large-
scale Indian coke oven facilities, is expected to provide ongoing support for seaborne demand.

About Stanmore Resources Limited (ASX: SMR) 
Stanmore Resources Limited controls and operates the South Walker Creek, Poitrel and Isaac Plains Complex metallurgical coal mines as well as 
the undeveloped Isaac Downs Extension, Eagle Downs and Lancewood projects in Queensland’s Bowen Basin region. Stanmore Resources holds 
several additional high-quality prospective coal tenements located in Queensland’s Bowen and Surat basins. The Company is focused on the creation 
of shareholder value via the efficient operation of its mining assets and the identification of further development opportunities within the region.
