About Stanmore Resources Limited (ASX: SMR) 
Stanmore Resources Limited controls and operates the South Walker Creek, Poitrel and Isaac Plains Complex metallurgical coal mines as well as the 
undeveloped Isaac Downs Extension, Eagle Downs and Lancewood projects in Queensland’s Bowen Basin region. Stanmore Resources holds several 
additional high-quality prospective coal tenements located in Queensland’s Bowen and Surat Basins. The Company is focused on the creation of 
shareholder value via the efficient operation of its mining assets and the identification of further development opportunities within the region.  
More information about Stanmore can be found at stanmore.au.
