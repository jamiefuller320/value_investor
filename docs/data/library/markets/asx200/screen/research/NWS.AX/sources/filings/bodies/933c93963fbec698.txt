whole civilization will die tonight ... I

ably will.” He said last night was “one of
the most important moments in the
long and complex history of the World”.
The US and Iran were considering a
last-ditch appeal by Pakistan for a two-
week ceasefire to allow more time for
talks. Pakistan’s prime minister also
called for Iran to open the Strait of Hor-
muz “as a goodwill gesture”.

forces had “tools in our toolbox that we

the 25th Amendment, which allows the

A Message from Robert Thomson
News Corp reported another year of record revenue 
and proﬁtability on a continuing operations basis, with 
revenues rising 7% to $9 billion and Total Segment 
EBITDA increasing 15% to over $1 .6 billion. As a result, 
free cash ﬂow increased signiﬁcantly, rising 42% to  
$811 million, and our net income from continuing 
operations rose 15% to $743 million. Our enhanced  
cash position enabled us to aggressively return capital  
to shareholders, with the buyback accelerating to well 
over four times the prior year’s rate at $643 million. 
The robustness of our strategy has given us a ﬁrm 
foundation for future growth. Our sterling performance 
in ﬁscal 2026 was a product of sustained development of 
our core growth engines – Dow Jones, Digital Real Estate 
Services and Book Publishing. That positive trajectory,  
and the record proﬁts, reﬂected our clear digital strategy 
and our focus on recurring revenues.   
Fiscal 2026 was marked by a profound conﬂuence of 
technological, economic and political turbulence. Much 
of the world is being reshaped by artiﬁcial intelligence, 
but it is only as useful and trustworthy as the quality and 
integrity of its inputs. News Corp plays a crucial role in the 
emerging information ecosystem. Without our journalists, 
our authors, our data, our brands and our professional 
expertise, users would be drowning in a slimy sea  
of AI slop.
That is why we remain dedicated to cultivating 
partnerships with those who have shown integrity  
at a time of institutional infelicities. We have trusted 
content relationships with honorable companies who 
respect the importance of provenance. However,  
under our woo and sue approach, we are also taking 
aggressive action against those who pilfer and proﬁt from 
our work and shamelessly steal our content at scale. 
Delving into our ﬁscal 2026 performance, Dow Jones 
delivered impressive results, with revenues rising  
7% to nearly $2.5 billion and Segment EBITDA growing 
13% to $663 million. At an Investor Brieﬁng in March,  
the team outlined a path to $1 billion in Segment EBITDA, 
and it is fair to say we are well on the way to reaching 
that milestone.
Dow Jones’ B-2-B continued to ﬂourish, led by 
strength at Risk and Compliance and Dow Jones 
Energy, which beneﬁtted from expanded product 
portfolios and the promise of signiﬁcant growth 
ahead in their respective markets. 
The expansion of enterprise subscriptions 
continued, and the Dow Jones news business 
ﬁnished the year with over 6.7 million total 
subscriptions, an increase of 7%, while circulation 
revenues improved, digital direct subscription 
ARPU accelerated and digital advertising was 
buoyant in the fourth quarter.
