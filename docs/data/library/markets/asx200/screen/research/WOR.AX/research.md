## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Worley Limited screens as a buy on classic value metrics — P/B ~1.0, FCF yield ~9.2%, dividend yield ~4.7%, and moderate leverage (D/E ~47%) — against a globally diversified engineering platform exposed to energy transition, infrastructure, and sustainability capex. The quantitative case is anchored in asset-backed cheapness and cash generation, not high ROE (6.5%). The central debate is whether FY25 earnings recovery (Yahoo: net income A$409m, EPS A$0.77) and contract wins (APA, AEP, Anglo Asian) outweigh repeated FY26 guidance downgrades tied to Middle East project delays and FX headwinds. Primary filing coverage is a material gap: one HY26 interim announcement is indexed but not parsed; no annual report body is available. Until verified against full ASX releases, treat the screen signal as valuation-supported but earnings-execution uncertain.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
Worley fits a value investor’s checklist on paper. The screen passes all four metric families — cheapness, dividend, GARP, and risk — with nine individual models including Schloss Low P/B, FCF Yield, Magic Formula, and Acquirer's Multiple. At P/E 16.1 and P/B 1.01, the market prices the stock near tangible asset value despite a global backlog narrative in sustainability and energy infrastructure.

Business quality is mixed but improving off a low base. Yahoo annual data (fallback — no filing bodies available) shows a clear earnings inflection: net income recovered from A$37m in FY23 (distorted by A$290m unusual charges) to A$303m in FY24 and A$409m in FY25, with EBITDA rising from A$616m to A$748m. Free cash flow strengthened to A$665m in FY25, supporting the screen’s FCF yield signal and consistent dividends (~A$264m paid annually). The company’s strategic positioning — engineering services across oil & gas, chemicals, mining, power, and sustainability — offers optionality on multi-decade capex themes (energy transition, nuclear, data-centre/power infrastructure) without pure commodity price exposure.

The buy case for a value investor is therefore: (1) balance-sheet-adjacent valuation with net debt of A$1.23bn against A$962m cash (Yahoo FY25); (2) demonstrated FCF and dividend capacity; (3) contract momentum in Australia and the US; (4) potential mean reversion if FY26 headwinds prove transient. The screen’s low conviction score (48%, building) appropriately reflects that price has lagged fundamentals — news cites ~18% one-year decline — suggesting the market is sceptical of guidance credibility rather than dismissing the franchise outright.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Guidance credibility and earnings volatility.** Worley flagged a **A$30–40m EBITA hit** from Middle East conflict impacts (Motley Fool Australia, 21 Apr 2026; thebull.com.au, 20 Apr 2026), followed by a **fresh FY26 profit warning** citing Middle East delays and currency effects (AFR, 25 Jun 2026: “Worley faces investor trust crisis after fresh profit warning”; Motley Fool, 25 Jun 2026). Repeated downgrades are a governance-of-expectations red flag not captured by static value screens.

**Geopolitical and FX exposure.** A meaningful share of revenue is derived outside Australia; Middle East project delays and foreign-currency translation (stocksdownunder.com, 25 Jun 2026) create earnings volatility that trailing multiples understate.

**Low return on equity.** ROE of 6.5% (screen) remains below cost of equity for an asset-heavy services business, reflecting goodwill drag and margin pressure. Simply Wall St (3 Nov 2025) noted decelerating rates of return.

**Goodwill and intangible risk.** A$5.98bn of goodwill/intangibles against A$5.68bn equity means impairment would materially affect book value and potentially covenant headroom (covenant terms **not available** in filing bodies).

**Cyclical end-markets.** Engineering and construction services remain tied to customer capex cycles in energy, chemicals, and mining. Revenue fell in FY25 despite earnings growth.

**Debt uptick.** Total debt rose A$380m in FY25 (Yahoo) even as cash increased, suggesting active refinancing or working-capital funding; net debt improved modestly.

**Thin primary disclosure in this pack.** Absence of parsed ASX filing bodies prevents assessment of litigation provisions, contract disputes, or going-concern statements. **Do not infer comfort on these topics.**
