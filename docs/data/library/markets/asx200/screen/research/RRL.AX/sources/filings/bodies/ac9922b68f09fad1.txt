First Quarter Continues Operational Delivery to Plan 
 
Regis Resources Ltd (ASX: RRL, Regis or the Company) is pleased to announce the following preliminary, 
unaudited business update for the first quarter of FY27, ended 30 September 2026. 
 
• 
While significant rainfall impacted some activities in September, group gold production1 met expectations 
at 83.0koz. 
• 
Pre-tax cash and bullion of $146M was generated (including the $51M Vault break fee) in the quarter, 
before $53M in tax payments. 
• 
$1.28 billion cash and bullion2 on hand at 30 September 2026.
