Alison Barrass to join Contact Board 
 
Alison Barrass will join the Board of Contact Energy Limited ('Contact') as an independent 
director on 1 September 2026.  
 
Alison is a professional director and former CEO with a broad range of skills and experience 
having held executive roles in sales, marketing and operations in New Zealand and abroad. 
Alison is a director of Suncorp’s New Zealand Boards (Vero Insurance New Zealand Limited 
and Vero Liability Insurance Limited), Precinct Properties, and has recently been appointed 
to the Board of Summerset Group Holdings Limited. 
 
"We are delighted to welcome Alison to the Contact Board. Alison's strong customer 
perspective, significant governance experience, and strategic expertise will add value to the 
Board, and we look forward to her contribution to Contact," said Chair Rob McDonald.  
 
Alison will fill a casual vacancy on the Board from 1 September and will stand for election by 
shareholders at the Annual Shareholder Meeting on 16 September 2026.
