Notification of Extraordinary General Meeting 
Date 
 
In accordance with Listing Rule 3.13.1, Yancoal Australia Ltd (the Company) advises that, subject to approval 
by the Company’s Board at its meeting to be held on or about 23 October 2026,  an Extraordinary General 
Meeting (the EGM) of shareholders will be held on Thursday, 10 December 2026.  
 
It is proposed that a n item of business at the EGM will be the election of a Director. In accordance with ASX 
Listing Rule 3.13.1 and rule 8.1(i) (3) and (4)  of the Company’s constitution, the closing date for receipt of  
external nominations from persons wishing to be considered for election as a Director is Thursday, 22 October 
2026. Any nominations must be received at the Company’s registered office no later than 5.00pm (Sydney 
time) on Thursday, 22 October 2026. 
 
Further details of the meeting will be provided in the notice of meeting, which will be despatched to the 
shareholders of the Company in early November 2026.
