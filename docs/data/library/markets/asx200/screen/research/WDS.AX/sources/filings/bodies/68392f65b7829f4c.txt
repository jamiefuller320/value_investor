THIRD QUARTER REPORT FOR PERIOD ENDED 30 SEPTEMBER 2026
ASX: WDS | NYSE: WDS 
Wednesday, 21 October 2026
Strong performance from improved production and pricing 
Performance highlights 
•
Achieved $84/boe realised LNG price during Q3 2026, an increase of 27% compared to Q2 2026 reflecting 
price lags from Q2 2026, realised values from LNG portfolio optimisation undertaken in Q2 2026 and 
stronger gas hub market pricing. 
•
Delivered quarterly operating revenue of $4,281 million, up 2% on Q2 2026 from strong LNG performance 
offset by lower average realised pricing from the liquids portfolio.
•
Delivered quarterly production volumes of 46.2 MMboe (503 Mboe/d), up 12% from Q2 2026 reflecting 
higher production from Pluto LNG following turnaround and maintenance activities completed in Q2 2026.
•
Achieved operational reliability of greater than 99% at Sangomar, NWS Project LNG and Pluto LNG.
•
Completed NWS single train planned major turnaround campaign on schedule and budget. 
Project highlights
•
The Scarborough Energy Project was 99% complete and remains on budget and on track for first LNG 
cargo this year.
•
The Trion Project was 71% complete at quarter end and remains on budget, with floating production unit 
(FPU) fabrication advancing to 96% completion,  targeting first oil in 2028.
•
The Louisiana LNG Project remains on budget, targeting first LNG in 2029. It is 32% complete with Train 1 
41% complete.
Business and portfolio highlights
•
Entered into an agreement to sell Woodside’s 70% operated interest in the Calypso Project to bp.
•
Completed the strategic review of Beaumont New Ammonia (BNA) and will initiate a formal process to 
reduce Woodside’s investment and achieve value for shareholders.

Woodside CEO Liz Westcott said the strong third quarter performance reflected the company’s highly reliable 
operations and disciplined execution of major growth projects. 
“Amid ongoing volatility in global energy markets, we have remained a reliable supplier to our customers. 
Increased quarterly operating revenue reflected increased production and strong realised prices.
“We delivered quarterly production of 46.2 million barrels of oil equivalent, up 12% from the previous quarter 
following the successful completion of the Pluto LNG turnaround in the second quarter and the NWS 
turnaround in the third quarter. 
“We maintained outstanding reliability across our global portfolio, with reliability of 99.8% at Pluto LNG and 
99.3% at the NWS Project in Australia, and 99.2% at Sangomar in Senegal.
“We recorded three process safety events during the quarter. Safety remains our highest priority and we are 
focused on applying the lessons learned from these incidents and strengthening our controls in this vitally 
important area.  
“We continued executing our major growth projects to budget and schedule. The Scarborough Energy Project is 
now 99% complete, with gas from the Scarborough reservoir introduced into the FPU and exported into the 
trunkline during the quarter. We remain on track for first LNG cargo this year.
“The Trion Project is now 71% complete and targeting first oil in 2028. Fabrication of the FPU is well advanced, 
with integration and pre-commissioning activities underway.
“Louisiana LNG is targeting first LNG in 2029, with construction progressing across all three trains and the 
project 32% complete. 
“We continued to optimise our portfolio through the ongoing integration of the Gippsland Basin assets and 
divestment of our operated interest in the Calypso Project. Following completion of the Beaumont New 
Ammonia strategic review, we will now initiate a formal process to reduce our investment and achieve value 
for our shareholders. These actions demonstrate our disciplined approach to portfolio management and capital 
allocation, with a clear focus on investments that deliver long-term shareholder value.
“We remain committed to supplying Australia’s domestic gas market and are assessing our plans for four 
proposed development wells in the Bass Strait. Development will depend on technical assessments and the 
implications of the Federal Government’s domestic gas reservation scheme.”

Pluto LNG
•
Achieved quarterly LNG reliability of 99.8%.
•
Preparation for the subsea tie-in and commissioning of the XNA-03 infill well continued ahead of targeted 
start-up in Q4 2026.
North West Shelf Project
•
Achieved quarterly LNG reliability of 99.3%.
•
Completed single LNG train planned turnaround campaign in September 2026 on budget and schedule.
•
Hearings on three legal proceedings in the Federal Court of Australia associated with the NWS Project

Subject to the receipt of all regulatory approvals, the asset swap transaction continues to target

Beaumont New Ammonia
•
BNA suspended operations for approximately 30 days following a Tier 1 process safety event. There were 
no injuries to personnel or damage to equipment and no lasting impact to the environment. The facility 
was safely restarted following completion of an internal investigation and implementation of appropriate 
actions.
•
Production during the quarter was constrained to 69% of nameplate capacity due to third-party feedstock 
availability and the 30 day shut down. Third-party cargoes were sourced to satisfy customer commitments 
on similar financial terms.
•
Commencement of lower-carbon ammonia production remains targeted for 2027, subject to 
commissioning of Linde’s low-carbon hydrogen facilities and start-up of ExxonMobil’s carbon capture and 
storage (CCS) infrastructure. During the quarter, the Texas Railroad Commission voted to approve the 
permit for ExxonMobil’s Rose carbon storage project, moving it into the final stages of a multi-year 
regulatory process.
•
Completed the strategic review of BNA. In light of the evolving pace of lower-carbon ammonia market 
development, Woodside will initiate a formal process to reduce its investment in BNA investment and 
achieve value for shareholders.
Marketing
Revenue and trading 
•
Strong marketing performance reflected the realisation of value from LNG portfolio optimisation 
undertaken in Q2 2026, with revenue recognised as cargoes were delivered in Q3 2026. 
•
Average realised LNG prices improved quarter-on-quarter, reflecting the realisation of price lags from Q2 
2026 and stronger gas hub market pricing in the lead up to northern hemisphere winter months. 
•
Approximately 33% of LNG sold was linked to gas hub indices during the quarter.
•
The impact of crude supply disruptions softened during the quarter as export flows recovered and market 
balances improved, resulting in lower market prices for crude oil and weaker realised premiums for the 
liquids portfolio (oil, condensate and liquids traded).

Projects
Scarborough Energy Project
•
The Scarborough and Pluto Train 2 projects remain on budget and were 99% complete at the end of the 
quarter (excluding Pluto Train 1 modifications).
•
The FPU and subsea production system commissioning and start-up activities continued, including well 
clean-up and export of gas to pressurise the trunkline. Well clean-up results are in line with reservoir and 
well performance expectations. 
•
Completed all mechanical runs of Pluto Train 2 compressors and, subsequent to the period, Scarborough 
gas was introduced to the warm end (gas pre-treatment) of the LNG train. Activities in the cold end 
continue to progress, which will enable the commencement of gas liquefaction.
•
Following the Pluto Train 1 planned major maintenance campaign in Q2, modifications to Pluto Train 1 
which will allow processing of Scarborough gas continued. The final module arrived onsite with activities 
focused on module hook-up and integration.
•
First LNG cargo is on track for Q4 2026.

•

integration into the hull.
•
The project is targeting first oil in 2028.
Louisiana LNG
•
The Louisiana LNG Project remains on budget and was 32% complete at the end of the quarter.
•
LNG terminal construction advanced across marine, site works and LNG tanks, with LNG Tank 1 wall 
concrete reaching 50% completion.
•

installation progressed. 
•
Trains 2 and 3 were 27% and 22% complete respectively at the end of the quarter, with above-ground 
piping installation commenced for Train 2 and first concrete foundation poured for Train 3.
•
Structural steel supply challenges associated with ongoing disruptions in the Middle East continue to be 
mitigated through alternative logistics routes and fabrication sources.
•
Ongoing engagement with high-quality counterparties for equity participation and LNG offtake continues.
•
The project is targeting first LNG in 2029.

Hedging
•
As at 30 September 2026, approximately 87% of the 30 MMboe of 2026 oil-linked production previously 
hedged (at an average price of $74.23 per barrel) had been cash settled. No additional oil-linked corporate 
hedges were entered during the quarter and the 2027 hedge position of 10 MMboe remains unchanged.
•
Continued to utilise Henry Hub and TTF commodity swaps in connection with overall management of 
Corpus Christi LNG volumes.
•
For 2026 year to date, hedge settlements resulted in a net cash outflow of approximately $565 million, and 
a reduction to pre-tax profit of $519 million. Cash settlements on oil-linked hedges occur in advance of the 
related profit and loss impact, resulting in temporary differences. 
Embedded commodity derivative
•
In 2023, Woodside entered a revised long-term gas sale and purchase contract with Perdaman. A 
component of the selling price is linked to the price of urea, creating an embedded commodity derivative 
in the contract. The fair value of the embedded derivative is estimated using a Monte Carlo simulation 
model. 
•
As there is no long-term urea forward curve, TTF continues to be used as a proxy to simulate the value of 
the derivative over the life of the contract.
•
For 2026 year to date, an unrealised pre-tax loss of approximately $179 million is expected to be 
recognised through other expense.

As at 30 September 2026, Woodside had liquidity of approximately $7,800 million and net debt (including 
lease liabilities) of approximately $8,900 million.
