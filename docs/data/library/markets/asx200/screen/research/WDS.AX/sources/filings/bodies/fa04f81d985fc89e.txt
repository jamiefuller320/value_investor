CAPITAL MARKETS DAY 2026 
Woodside today outlined a clear plan to build on its strong foundations by sharpening operational and project 
execution and maximising value from its global LNG-led portfolio.  
 
Supported by a new capital framework and shareholder distribution policy, the company is targeting portfolio 
return on average capital employed of 12-15% by the mid-2030s. 
 
At its Capital Markets Day in Sydney, Woodside CEO and Managing Director Liz Westcott said the company 
is entering its next phase from a position of strength, giving Woodside greater capacity to invest in value-
accretive growth opportunities and return capital to shareholders.  
 
“Long-term demand for reliable energy remains compelling. Woodside’s multi-basin LNG position and 
deepwater oil capability, combined with sharper execution and capital discipline, position us to meet that 
demand and deliver value for shareholders. 
 
“Woodside has world-class assets, deep operating and project capabilities and enduring customer 
relationships. We have returned nearly US$13 billion to shareholders since 2022 while building a portfolio 
positioned to generate strong cash flows into the next decade. 
 
“Scarborough is 99% complete and on track for first LNG cargo this year, Trion is targeted for first oil in 2028 
and Louisiana LNG is targeted for first LNG in 2029. Together with our base business, these projects are 
expected to support sales volume growth of more than 30% to approximately 280 million barrels of oil 
equivalent in 2032, and generate more than US$40 billion of adjusted operating cash flow from 2027 to 
2032.1 
 
“Our priority now is to extract more value from our portfolio. We are targeting US$400 million of annual 
structural cost reductions from 2028, improving operational efficiency and productivity, and actively 
managing the portfolio to concentrate capital where it earns the highest returns.  
 
“We announced a new distribution policy of 20 – 40% of adjusted operating cash flows.  We will prioritise 
20% of adjusted operating cash flow as a base dividend, with up to another 20% available for additional 
dividends and/or buybacks.  We also announced our new capital framework under which growth must 
compete for capital against these additional distributions.
