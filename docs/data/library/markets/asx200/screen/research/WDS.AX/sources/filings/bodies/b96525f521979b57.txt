Telstra ( TLS ) (+3.6%) hit all-time highs after its dividend beat expectations and it upsized its buyback. Sonic Healthcare ( SHL ) (+9.8%) surged after beating expectations and re-affirming guidance. Hub24 ( HUB ) (+14.1% ) jumped on a strong earnings result. In Asian trade, spot gold (+0.1%) was little changed at around US$4,990/oz, but gold miners traded higher, led by Northern Star Resources ( NST ) (+1.2%) and Evolution Mining ( EVN ) (+2.4%) .
