Origin Energy ( ORG ) (-1.4%) and Coles Group ( COL ) (-0.5%) were notable decliners respectively. In commodities, Brent crude dropped 3.7% in Asian trade to US$96.53/bbl as easing geopolitical tensions reduced the risk premium embedded in energy markets. Gold surged in Asian trade, rising 3.1% to US$4,538/oz, while silver jumped 4.9% to US$73.00/oz as risk appetite improved and safe-haven flows stabilised. Lower oil equals lower inflation, equals lower market yields... equals good for non-yielding gold! Copper rose 0.84% in Asian trade to US$5.50/lb, reflecting improved sentiment toward global growth, while iron ore fell 2.2% in Singapore trade to US$105.40/t. Lithium prices continued their gains in China trade, with GFEX lithium carbonate futures rising 3.8% to CNY158,300/t, while rare earths edged higher with NdPr up 0.2% to CNY712,500/t.
