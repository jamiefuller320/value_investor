WOODSIDE AGREES GREATER SUNRISE SALE TO TIMOR GAP 
 
Woodside has entered into an agreement with TIMOR GAP to sell its participating interest in the Sunrise Joint 
Venture, including the titles containing the Greater Sunrise fields1, for total consideration of up to $200 million.2  
 
Completion of the transaction is subject to satisfaction of conditions precedent, including relevant government, 
regulatory and joint venture approvals, with completion targeted to occur in H1 2027.  
  
Chief Executive Officer Liz Westcott said the transaction reflects Woodside's disciplined approach to portfolio 
management and capital allocation.  
 
“Greater Sunrise has been an important part of Woodside’s portfolio for many years, and we recognise the 
project has the potential to deliver long-term economic and energy security benefits for Timor-Leste and the 
region. 
 
“While Sunrise remains a high-quality resource, the transaction further simplifies Woodside's portfolio and 
allows capital and management attention to be focused on opportunities that present the greatest shareholder 
value. 
 
“Woodside has worked closely with the Governments of Timor-Leste and Australia, TIMOR GAP and Osaka 
Gas to advance Greater Sunrise, including the proposed new fiscal, regulatory and legal framework required 
to support its future development. 
 
“Reaching alignment on the key principles underpinning a new Greater Sunrise Production Sharing Contract 
and associated fiscal framework in August provides an appropriate point for Woodside to sell its participating 
interest to TIMOR GAP. 
 
“We are committed to working constructively with TIMOR GAP, Osaka Gas and the Governments of Timor-
Leste and Australia to support an orderly transition and preserve the progress made towards the future 
development of Greater Sunrise.

Following completion of the transaction, Woodside will no longer hold a participating interest in the Sunrise 
Joint Venture.   
 
TIMOR GAP currently holds a 56.56% interest in the Sunrise Joint Venture and announced on 2 September 
2026 that it had entered into an agreement with Osaka Gas to acquire Osaka Gas’ 10% interest in the Sunrise 
Joint Venture subject to satisfaction of relevant conditions precedent.
