Disclaimer* Bridgepoint Group plc 27 July 2026 Bridgepoint Group plc (" Bridgepoint " or the " Company ") Admission of New Ordinary Shares and Total Voting Rights Further to Bridgepoint's announcement on 22 July 2026 in connection with the proposed issue of 3,657,040 ordinary shares of £0.00005 each (" New Ordinary Shares ") the Company announces that the New Ordinary Shares have today been admitted to trading on the London Stock Exchange's main market for listed securities. In conformity with the Financial Conduct Authority's Disclosure and Transparency Rules, Bridgepoint announces that its total issued share capital following the issue of the New Ordinary Shares comprises: · 881,728,587 ordinary shares of £0.00005 each with voting rights; and · 500 deferred shares of £81 each, 1 deferred share of £1, and 1 deferred share of £0.01, each with no voting rights. Bridgepoint holds no shares in treasury. T he total number of voting rights in the Company is therefore 881,728,587, and this figure may be used by shareholders at such time as the denominator for the calculation by which they will determine if they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, Bridgepoint under the Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules. Although not part of total issued share capital, for completeness Bridgepoint notes that there are also 102,239,292 operating partnership units in Bridgepoint OP LP that are exchangeable for 102,239,292 Bridgepoint ordinary shares of £0.00005 each.
