Disclaimer* Bridgepoint Group plc 14 September 2026 Bridgepoint Group plc (" Bridgepoint " or the " Company ") Issue of New Ordinary Shares and Total Voting Rights Further to Bridgepoint's announcement on 9 September 2026 in connection with the proposed issue of 10 million ordinary shares of £0.00005 each (" New Ordinary Shares ") the Company announces that the New Ordinary Shares have today been admitted to trading on the London Stock Exchange's main market for listed securities. In conformity with the Financial Conduct Authority's Disclosure and Transparency Rules, Bridgepoint announces that its total issued share capital following the issue of the New Ordinary Shares comprises: · 901,860,660 ordinary shares of £0.00005 each with voting rights; and · 500 deferred shares of £81 each, 1 deferred share of £1, and 1 deferred share of £0.01, each with no voting rights. Bridgepoint holds no shares in treasury. T he total number of voting rights in the Company is therefore 901,860,660, and this figure may be used by shareholders at such time as the denominator for the calculation by which they will determine if they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, Bridgepoint under the Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules. Although not part of total issued share capital, for completeness Bridgepoint notes that there are also 92,239,292 operating partnership units in Bridgepoint OP LP that are exchangeable for 92,239,292 Bridgepoint ordinary shares of £0.00005 each.
