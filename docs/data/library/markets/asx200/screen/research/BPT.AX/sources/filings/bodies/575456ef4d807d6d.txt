Comments from Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, Mr Brett Woods: 
“We delivered a strong start to FY27, with production up 9% to 5.4 MMboe and sales revenue increasing 41% 
to $565 million for the quarter, which is the highest quarterly sales revenue result ever realised by Beach. The 
result reflects disciplined execution across our operations and stronger performance from the Waitsia Gas 
Plant, which achieved a record average monthly production rate of 219 TJ/day in August. 
“Although we achieved this result whilst reaching 21 consecutive months without a recordable injury, the streak 
came to an end shortly after quarter-end with a contractor experiencing a minor hand injury. We remain 
committed to continuously strengthening our safety performance and protecting our people. 
“Disciplined execution of our marketing strategy continued to maximise portfolio value, delivering a 7% 
increase in the average realised gas price and crude oil sales price premiums of approximately US$19/bbl. Our 
strategy is focused on creating value through greater supply flexibility, lower transportation costs and a broader 
range of commodity-linked price structures. Our first JKM-linked domestic gas sales contract on the East Coast 
demonstrates how this approach can deliver better outcomes for both Beach and our customers. 
“Long-term gas security also depends on policy settings that encourage investment in supply. The proposed 
110% oversupply within the Domestic Gas Reservation Bill may drive lower prices in the short term but 
discourage new projects and increase the risk of longer-term shortages for manufacturers, households and the 
broader economy. The final framework must recognise genuine investment-led additional supply, including 
incremental production from existing fields, to strengthen gas security,” Mr Woods said.

Sales revenue 
Sales revenue increased 41% from the prior quarter to $565 million, driven by higher sales volumes, with 
incremental benefits from marketing and trading efforts continuing to realise strong oil premiums and gas 
prices.  
The average realised price across all products increased 7% from the prior quarter to $90 per boe. The average 
realised gas price rose 7% to $11.9 per GJ, while the average realised oil price decreased 4% to $166 per barrel. 
The realised oil price was supported by strong price premiums (~US$19 per barrel), a result that has improved 
with crude sales executed in Q2 FY27 (~US$28 per barrel). 
Two LNG cargoes were sold an average realised price of $15.2 per MMBtu generating revenue of $115 million.

Gas marketing and portfolio optimisation 
Since 1 July 2026, Beach has executed multiple medium-term domestic gas sales contracts totalling 14 PJ across 
FY28-30, including its first JKM-linked domestic gas sales contract on the East Coast of Australia. The contracts 
incorporate flexible supply arrangements and efficient transportation solutions, while diversifying Beach’s

Hedging 
As at 30 September 2026, Beach had no hedging in place. 
Otway price review process 
The price review process for the Otway Basin offshore Gas Sales Agreement, for the three-year period 
commencing 1 July 2026, is ongoing with the parties now in arbitration. Upon finalisation of the review process, 
the contract price outcome will be backdated to 1 July 2026.

completion criteria satisfied, transfer of the licence and completion of the transaction is now expected in H2

Class action settlement 
During the quarter Beach executed a deed of settlement to settle the shareholder class action proceeding 
commenced against it in the Supreme Court of Victoria in November 2021 for an amount of $43.5 million, 
inclusive of interest and costs. The deed is subject to approval by the Supreme Court of Victoria with a hearing 
expected to occur by end of CY2026. The full settlement payment will be met by Beach’s available insurance 
proceeds, with no impact on FY27 earnings or cashflow.

Perth Basin 
Production  
Quarterly gas production of 1,365 kboe was 3% above the prior quarter. The Waitsia Gas Plant achieved a 
record average monthly production rate of 219 TJ/day in August before entering a planned statutory shutdown 
on 4 September. The shutdown was optimally aligned with a North West Shelf processor outage.  
During the subsequent ramp-up, several air cooler plugs that were replaced during the shutdown failed their 
leak testing program and required rectification. This delayed the final start-up until 8 October. All planned 
works, including hot water valve replacement and a multitude of equipment modifications and/or upgrades 
were completed to address prior reliability concerns. Since returning to normal operations, the Waitsia Gas 
Plant has produced at an average rate of ~235 TJ/day.  
The joint venture reached a final investment decision to progress the Waitsia Inlet Compression project during 
the quarter. Long-lead items are well progressed, key contracts are being executed, and the compressor turbine 
package is due to arrive by end-CY2026. Site works are expected to commence in H2 FY27 with start-up 
expected in FY29. Capital expenditure of approximately $120 million (net to Beach) is expected, split broadly 
equally over FY27 and FY28, with FY27 expenditure included in group capex guidance.

domestic gas price, in FY27.  
Otway Basin 
Production  
Total gas and gas liquids production of 1.5 MMboe was 20% above the prior quarter, mainly due to strong 
operational performance, partly offset by a disruption to the inlet gas compressor in mid-July. The inlet 
compressor operation was fully restored by late August.  
A planned 28-day integrity shutdown of the Otway Gas Plant commenced shortly after quarter-end. Pleasingly, 
the statutory shutdown interval has been safely extended from four years to six years, supported by a strong 
focus on reliability and proactive maintenance. 
Nearshore campaign 
Beach and its joint venture partner are progressing the two-well nearshore Otway exploration campaign 
targeting the Enterprise East and Selje prospects, with a final investment decision expected in Q2 FY27 and 
drilling planned to commence in H1 FY28. The proposed campaign will utilise existing Beach-operated 
infrastructure and require total capital expenditure of approximately $175 – 200 million (gross). On a success 
basis, the project is targeting an all-in development cost of approximately $5/GJ, with first gas from FY29, 
subject to regulatory and environmental approvals. 
Deeper water campaign 
Beach is progressing a potential farm-out arrangement of its 100%-owned T/30P and T/50P permits in the 
deeper water Otway Basin, with a strong level of interest received from preliminary conversations. 
Cooper Basin Western Flank  
Production  
Total oil and gas production of 416 kboe was 15% above the prior quarter. Oil production of 311 kbbl was 21% 
above the prior quarter due to restoration of flood impacted oil wells and production from new well 
connections. At quarter-end, approximately 300 bopd remains offline due to inundation but eight of nine 
appraisal and development wells drilled in FY26 have been brought online and are cumulatively producing in-
line with initial expectations.  
Exploration, appraisal and development 
The Ventia 101 rig returned to Beach shortly after quarter-end, spudding the first of two oil development wells 
in early October. A nine-well exploration campaign is expected to follow (an increase from prior plans of eight) 
targeting expansion of existing play horizons and potentially unlocking new drilling opportunities. The drilling 
campaign is expected to conclude in Q3 FY27.

Cooper Basin JV 
Production 
Total oil and gas production of 1.4 MMboe was in-line with the prior quarter with continued strong and 
predictable operations.  
Exploration, appraisal and development 
Beach participated in 13 wells during the quarter, achieving an overall success rate of 85% from 10 gas 
development wells, one gas exploration well and two oil development wells. Upcoming activity will focus on 
Granite Wash appraisal, Big Lake Central development and Moomba North Patchawarra appraisal.  
Moomba Central Optimisation project 
During the quarter, Beach and joint venture partner Santos continued early-stage activities for the Moomba 
Central Optimisation project, including commencement of detailed design and locking in major long-lead 
procurement. Detailed engineering and ongoing procurement and sourcing will continue in Q2 FY27 and the 
project is targeting completion in H1 FY29. The Cooper Basin joint venture is targeting more than $400 million 
in capital and operating cost savings over the life of the Central Fields (net to Beach), and up to $3 per barrel 
reduction in Cooper Basin unit costs.  
During the quarter, brownfield work continued in earnest at both the Moomba and Port Bonython facilities. At 
quarter-end, the Moomba Simplification project is more than 50% complete.  
Moomba CCS  
Total captured and stored emissions of 274 ktCO2e were 23% below the prior quarter (Q4 FY26: 357 ktCO2e) 
due to an 18-day shutdown of the Moomba CCS facility in August to conduct scheduled maintenance activities. 
The total captured and stored emissions represented 77% of the gas stream available for storage during the 
period. The facility and injection wells continue to perform within expectation. 
Bass Basin 
Production  
Total gas and gas liquids production of 351 kboe was 55% above the prior quarter due to successful 
maintenance of the offshore compressor and better than anticipated asset reliability. The Lang Lang Gas Plant 
produced at an average daily rate of 16 TJ/day. Installation of a new compressor bundle assembly is expected 
in late FY27.  
Equinox rig campaign 
Beach finalised the Equinox rig campaign during the quarter with the Yolla 1 legacy well safely plugged and 
abandoned within budget. The Equinox rig was released from Beach on 15 August and mobilised to another 
consortium member.  
Taranaki Basin 
Production 
Total gas and gas liquids production of 324 kboe was 1% above the prior quarter due to continued high plant 
availability and strong customer nominations. The Kupe Gas Plant produced at an average daily rate of 31 
TJ/day (gross). A seven-day shutdown of the Kupe Gas Plant is planned for November 2026 during which time 
Beach will install a new inlet compressor bundle and conduct minor maintenance activities at the plant.
