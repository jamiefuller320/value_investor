ASX Announcement  
Cessation of Alternate Director 
 
Reference #044/26 Date 1 October 2026 
Beach Energy Limited (ASX: BPT, Beach) advises that Ms Margaret Hall has ceased as Alternate Director to Mr 
Ryan Stokes AO, effective 30 September 2026. 
Ms Hall was appointed to the Board in November 2021. She retired as a Director in July 2023 and was appointed 
Alternate Director to Mr Ryan Stokes AO at that time.  
Beach Chair, Mr Ryan Stokes AO, said: “I thank Margaret for her contribution to Beach and wish her well for 
the future.” 
 
Authorisation 
This announcement has been authorised for release by the Beach Energy Board of Directors.
