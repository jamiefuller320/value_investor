The General Meeting was convened to invite shareholders to consider resolutions in connection with the proposed acquisition by Bridgepoint of Kayne Anderson Real Estate (the " Transaction "), as described in the announcement released on 29 June 2026 and the circular (including the Notice of General Meeting) published on 11 September 2026. A poll was held on each of the resolutions and each was passed by the required majority. Resolution 1 was passed as an ordinary resolution, and resolution 2 was passed as a special resolution.
