Omega Oil & Gas Limited (ASX: OMA) (“Omega” or “the Company”) is pleased to announce 
that drilling operations have commenced at Canyon North- 1 (Figure 1), the third well in its 
2026/27 appraisal program, using H&P FlexRig® 648. 
Extending the Campaign North 
Canyon North-1 follows the Canyon-3 and Canyon-4 
wells in Omega’s 100% owned PCA 342. Canyon-4 
delivered Omega’s best Canyon well result to date, 
intersecting a 589m gross oil and gas-bearing Permian 
sequence with 197m of aggregate net pay, compared 
with 526m and 170m at Canyon-3 and 433m and 132m 
at Canyon- 1. Average log  porosity in the primary 
Canyon Sandstone target increased from 7.4% at 
Canyon-1 to 12.1% at Canyon-42. Average log porosity 
in the widespread Lower Kianga interval was 10.7% in 
Canyon-4 and 11% in Canyon-3. All log -derived 
porosity measurements will be calibrated to measured 
core porosities as results of ongoing core analysis are 
received in coming months.  
Building on the results from Canyon -3 and Canyon-4 
within the PCA focus area, Canyon North- 1 and 
Canyon North-2 shift the focus of the program to 
demonstrating scale. These wells are designed to 
extend evidence of the resource beyond Omega’s PCA 
areas and to characterise reservoir and fluid properties 
across the central and northern shaded  orange areas 
shown on Figure 1  – each covering area s of 
approximately 400 square kilometres. 
Canyon North-1 Well Objectives 
Canyon North-1 is located approximately 6km from, and 250m updip of, the Tasmania -1 well, 
drilled in 2012. Tasmania-1 penetrated the same Permian reservoirs targeted by the Canyon 
wells, and the Canyon Sandstone demonstrates continuous geology from the Canyon wells to 
Tasmania-1, correlating a further approximately 40km northwest to Fantome-1. 
The entire, consistent, regionally-extensive Permian sequence will be tested, including the 
Canyon Sandstone, Lower Kianga and other reservoir intervals. The primary objective of the 
well is to characterise the reservoir quality and hydrocarbon phase and to test the extension 
of the Permian reservoir system north of Omega’s PCA areas. Total measured depth is planned 
at approximately 4,000m. 
The well will be drilled to Total Depth  and logged with a comprehensive wireline suite. 
Conventional core will not be acquired in Canyon North-1, as the Canyon Sandstone was cored 
in the nearby Tasmania-1 well.

ABOUT OMEGA OIL AND GAS 
Omega Oil & Gas Limited (ASX: OMA) is a dynamic Australian exploration company with a 
highly experienced team focussed on unlocking the vast, deep oil and gas potential of 
Queensland’s Taroom Trough, an emerging producing province within the Bowen Basin. The 
Company’s breakthrough at the Canyon-1H well revealed substantial oil and gas flows. The 
successful execution of the drilling, fracture stimulation and testing program at Canyon-1H 
underscores Omega’s technical and operational expertise. 
Omega’s “play-opening” Canyon-1H well highlighted the presence of a large and prospective 
petroleum system, potentially capable of supporting decades of commercial production. 
Backed by prominent resource investors and driven by technical expertise, Omega is 
positioned to become a key contributor to Australia’s energy future.
