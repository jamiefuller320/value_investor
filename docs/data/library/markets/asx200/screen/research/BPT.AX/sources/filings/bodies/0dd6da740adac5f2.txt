Beach Energy Limited (ASX: BPT, Beach) is pleased to announce that it has entered into binding agreements 
with Elixir Energy Limited (ASX: EXR, Elixir) and its wholly-owned subsidiaries, to farm-in to its core acreage in 
the Taroom Trough, Queensland (Transaction), subject to conditions precedent, including regulatory and other 
approvals including pre-emptive rights for interests in ATP 2056 and ATP 2057. 
The Transaction builds on Beach’s existing Taroom Trough position, which includes a 25% non-operated 
interest in ATP 2081 and the recently awarded Block 10 (to be renamed ATP 2082), representing 1,888 km2 
gross acreage (472 km2 net to Beach). Completion of the Transaction will establish Beach as one of the largest 
acreage holders in the Taroom Trough. 
Strategically, the farm-in structure enables a low-cost entry, while also providing Beach with operational control 
of key Taroom Trough acreage; enabling significant influence over scope and prioritisation of exploration and 
appraisal activity while maintaining Beach’s disciplined approach to capital allocation.  
Beach’s deep technical and operational capabilities position the joint venture for success. The company will 
utilise its strengths to efficiently and effectively appraise the acreage within a supportive Queensland onshore 
jurisdiction, where the Government has clearly demonstrated support for the resource sector. Beach is pleased 
to be working with Elixir, who was one of the first operators to identify the play, and who share similar focus, 
cost discipline and priority in moving the project forward. 
Beach Energy Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, Brett Woods, said: “The new partnership with 
Elixir represents a compelling opportunity to secure a material, operated position in the Taroom Trough, an 
emerging resource play within an established oil and gas production province. It offers significant running 
room for future resource and reserves growth.

Transaction overview 
Under the Transaction, Beach will acquire half of Elixir’s core Taroom Trough acreage for the following 
consideration, comprising: 
• 
$25 million cash payment payable on Transaction completion, anticipated in H2 FY27;  
• 
Carry of Elixir’s share of the exploration and appraisal work program and costs (including GG&A) of $90 
million, over an approximate four-year farm-in period following completion, with Beach retaining full 
discretion to define the works program and determine how carry is deployed (with any unspent carry at 
the end of the farm-in period payable to Elixir in cash); 
• 
An Area of Mutual Interest (AMI) arrangement, enabling both parties to participate in additional acreage 
acquisitions withing a defined area of the Taroom Trough during the farm-in period, subject to conditions 
including existing pre-emptive rights 
The Transaction is expected to complete in H2 FY27 and is subject to pre-emptive rights for interests in 
ATP2056 and ATP2057, receipt of customary regulatory approvals and other conditions.  
Acreage overview 
The current acreage acquired spans 2,859 km2 (gross) across the western flank and central portions of the 
Taroom Trough, approximately 20-120 km southeast of the Wallumbilla Gas Hub in Queensland, Australia. It is 
well positioned near critical East Coast gas market infrastructure, with established supply lines and a capable 
services sector providing efficient routes to market. The acreage also benefits from a supportive Queensland 
Government committed to investment, stakeholder collaboration and efficient resource development, 
reinforcing its alignment with Beach’s disciplined investment criteria.  
The western flank includes substantial discovered gas resources in the Tinowon (Dunk), Overstone and Lorelle 
reservoirs, in part the subject of recent successful drilling and extended well testing in Shell’s ATP 645/PCA 305. 
Elixir previously reported an independently certified third-party view of 2C Contingent Resources of ~3.5 Tcfe 
net (prior to Beach farm-in), highlighting the potential within the acreage. Beach intends to make an initial 2C 
Contingent Resource booking as part of its FY27 reserves process. A summary of the acreage is outlined below.
