ASX ANNOUNCEMENT 2nd October 2026 
 
 
 Beach Energy Process Deed and 
Inbound Interest  
Elixir Energy Limited (ASX: EXR, Elixir or the Company) provides an update regarding the 
strategic transaction discussions referred to in the Company’s announcement dated 10 
September 2026. 
As previously announced, Elixir and Beach Energy Limited (Beach) entered into  a Process 
Deed on 21 August 2026, which provided for a period of exclusivity in connection with a 
potential strategic farm-in to Elixir’s Taroom Trough portfolio. That exclusivity period expired 
at close of business on 1 October 2026 prior to the parties reaching final agreement on the 
terms and conditions of the binding documentation regarding the proposed transaction.  
Whilst Elixir and Beach have made significant progress towards a binding transaction, with 
the exclusivity period having expired, Elixir is now able to engage with the other interested 
parties regarding potential strategic transactions involving the Company and/or its Taroom 
Trough portfolio. 
Unsolicited interest has been received by the Company from additional third parties during 
the exclusivity period which Elixir is now able to fully consider. 
Elixir remains in discussions with Beach regarding its proposed strategic farm -in, and Beach 
continues to be an active participant in the Company’s strategic discussions. The expiry of 
exclusivity does not terminate those discussions or preclude Elixir and  Beach from 
progressing a potential transaction. 
The Board intends to consider all available strategic alternatives with a view to maximising 
value for Elixir shareholders. 
There is no assurance that the ongoing discussions with Beach, or engagement with any other 
interested party, will result in a binding transaction. Elixir will keep shareholders informed of 
any material developments in accordance with its continuous disclo sure obligations under 
the ASX Listing Rules.
