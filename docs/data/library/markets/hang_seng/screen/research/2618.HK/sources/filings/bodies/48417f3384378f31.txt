FINANCIAL INFORMATION AND BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS

The board (the “ Board”) of directors (the “ Directors”) of JD Logistics, Inc. (the “ Company” or

requires, the Group. In addition, “ JD Group” refers to JD.com, Inc. (NASDAQ: JD; HKEX: 9618 
(HKD counter) and 89618 (RMB counter)), its subsidiaries, including JD Health International Inc. 
(HKEX: 6618 (HKD counter) and 86618 (RMB counter)) and JINGDONG Industrials, Inc. (HKEX: 
7618), and its consolidated affiliated entities, excluding the Group. Any discrepancies in percentage 
ratios are due to rounding.

BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS
• We remain committed to deeply cultivating the core track of our integrated supply chain. In 
the third quarter of 2026, revenue from external integrated supply chain customers reached 
RMB10.5 billion, representing a year-over-year increase of 17.3%. We achieved sound growth 
in both the number of external integrated supply chain customers and the average revenue 
per customer (the “ ARPC”). Leveraging our comprehensive fulfillment capabilities across all 
channels and multiple scenarios, we continued to deepen the value of our integrated supply 
chain. During the launch of the iPhone 18 Pro series in September 2026, powered by algorithm-
driven sales forecasting and inventory planning, we pre-deployed inventory to nationwide 
warehouses and densely deployed front warehouses, while collaborating with an extensive 
network of offline stores, achieving efficient synergy across multi-tier networks and deep 
inventory pre-positioning. After the launch, backed by our full-spectrum fulfillment network 
ranging from on-demand delivery to traditional B2C warehousing and distribution, we achieved 
“minute-level” delivery in core cities and extensive coverage across counties and districts 
nationwide, which efficiently and smoothly handled the surge in order demand and drove a 
substantial increase in fulfilled order volume. Currently, this new product launch solution 
has been applied to order peak scenarios for multiple leading consumer electronics brands, 
including new product launches and omni-channel promotions, continuously verifying the 
scalable replicability of our integrated supply chain capabilities.
• We firmly advanced our globalization strategy and systematically replicated and extended 
our mature integrated supply chain model in China into overseas markets. In the third quarter 
of 2026, our international business maintained rapid growth momentum. In Europe, we 
significantly enhanced the coverage capability of our self-operated network, providing efficient 
and reliable fulfillment support for Joybuy, JD.com’s online retail business in Europe. At the 
same time, we accelerated the automation upgrade of our overseas warehousing operations and 
deployed the LangzuTech Tote Handling, our proprietary automated warehousing solution, 
across multiple warehouses in the UK, Germany and other regions, improving storage 
utilization and operational efficiency while enhancing operational stability. Meanwhile, across 
regions including Europe, the Americas, the Asia-Pacific and the Middle East, we continued 
to deepen our cooperation with both Chinese companies going global and local overseas 
customers. For example, in markets such as Malaysia, aligning with the go-global trend of the 
home appliance industry, we provided differentiated services such as “integrated delivery and 
installation” to a number of leading home appliance brands, driving rapid growth in business 
scale. With the continued densification of our overseas warehousing and distribution network, 
the large-scale deployment of automated equipment and increasing business density, the 
operational efficiency and fulfillment certainty of our overseas business continue to strengthen.
3
• We have always regarded technology as the key to building our long-term barrier, and we 
have continued to promote the deep integration and scaled application of artificial intelligence 
(AI) and automation across all logistics processes, accelerating the transformation of our 
technological innovations into core capabilities that empower our operations internally 
and enable commercialization externally. In the third quarter of 2026, we introduced the 
latest lineup of our “MetaBrain+LangzuTech” robotic fleet. Our supply chain large model, 
“MetaBrain 3.0”, serves as the intelligent decision-making hub, providing real-time scheduling 
and command for 9 products and 11 self-developed robots deployed across all logistics 
processes, including warehousing, sorting and delivery. Among them, the LangzuTech 
Tote Handling, our proprietary automated warehousing solution, has scaled into complex 
environments such as cold storage warehouses. The newly launched LangzuTech Tote Handling 
(Low-Temperature Version) has operated continuously in the Chengdu cold-chain warehouse 
for over 100 days, successfully processing more than one million automated outbound orders. 
The LangzuTech Mini Van 6 Plus, our latest-generation autonomous delivery vehicle, built on 
a light-map end-to-end architecture, has achieved Level-4 (L4) autonomous driving in routine 
operations at hundreds of sites. The deep collaboration between software and hardware has 
driven continuous improvements in operational efficiency, fulfillment certainty and customer 
experience. Meanwhile, we accelerated the external commercialization of our self-developed 
technologies. During the quarter, the scale of external contracts secured for our self-developed 
automated equipment recorded solid growth. We established benchmark smart warehousing 
projects in industries such as automotive and semiconductors, and successfully expanded into 
overseas markets, continuously demonstrating the commercialization potential and industry-
enabling value of JD Logistics’ self-developed core technologies.
• As of September 30, 2026, our warehouse network covered nearly all counties and districts in 
China, consisting of over 1,800 self-operated warehouses and over 2,000 third-party warehouse 
owner-operated cloud warehouses under our Open Warehouse Platform. Our warehouse 
network has an aggregate gross floor area of more than 36 million square meters, including 
warehouse space managed through the Open Warehouse Platform.
• We remain committed to our mission to “drive superior efficiency and sustainability for global 
supply chain through technology”, achieving significant progress in our sustainability and 
environmental, social and governance (ESG) practices. Recently, S&P Global, a leading global 
rating agency, announced the results of its 2026 Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA). 
Our S&P Global ESG Score and CSA Score continued to rank No. 1 in the logistics industry in 
Chinese Mainland, placing us among the top 4% in the global Transportation and Infrastructure 
Industry, and reflecting our continued progress across key ESG areas.
• Driven by our unwavering commitment and unremitting efforts to create more jobs and make 
contribution to the society, JD Logistics’ total expenditure for human resources, including both 
our own employees and external personnel who work for JD Logistics, amounted to RMB119.1 
billion for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026.

Revenue increased by 16.1% to RMB64.0 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, 
from RMB55.1 billion for the same period of 2025.
Revenue from integrated supply chain customers increased to RMB30.2 billion for the three months 
ended September 30, 2026, from RMB30.1 billion for the same period of 2025. The increase in 
revenue from integrated supply chain customers was primarily driven by a 17.3% increase in 
revenue from external integrated supply chain customers to RMB10.5 billion for the three months 
ended September 30, 2026, from RMB8.9 billion for the same period of 2025, with a year-over-year 
increase in the number of external integrated supply chain customers and the ARPC. The number of 
our external integrated supply chain customers, who have contributed to our revenue for the periods 
presented, increased to 73,911 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from 66,809 for the 
same period of 2025. Meanwhile, our ARPC increased to RMB141,842 for the three months ended 
September 30, 2026, from RMB133,722 for the same period of 2025. The simultaneous increases 
in both the number of external integrated supply chain customers and the ARPC reflected strong 
customer endorsement, driven by our continuously enhanced integrated supply chain capabilities, 
deepening collaborations, and growing stickiness.

coupled with the growth in express delivery and other services.

Revenue from JD Group was RMB19.7 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, 
compared with RMB21.2 billion for the same period of 2025, primarily because starting from 
January 2026, we began providing on-demand delivery services directly to external customers on

2025, we provided on-demand delivery services to JD Group through our full-time riders, with the 
associated revenue included in revenue from JD Group. In late October 2025, we acquired the on-
demand delivery business from JD Group and integrated our full-time rider resources to establish 
comprehensive on-demand delivery capabilities. Accordingly, effective January 2026, revenue from 
on-demand delivery services directly provided to external customers was included in revenue from 
external customers, instead of revenue from internal integrated supply chain customers (i.e. JD 
Group).
Cost of revenue
Cost of revenue increased by 15.8% to RMB58.0 billion for the three months ended September 30, 
2026, from RMB50.1 billion for the same period of 2025, which was in line with the growth of our 
revenue during the corresponding period.
Employee benefit expenses for operational employees involved in warehouse management, sorting, 
picking, packaging, shipping, delivery and customer services remained relatively stable at RMB21.6 
billion and RMB21.8 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and for the same 
period of 2025, respectively.
Outsourcing cost, mainly including costs charged by third-party transportation companies, express 
delivery companies and other service providers for sorting, shipping, delivery and labor outsourcing 
services, increased by 43.9% to RMB24.4 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, 
from RMB17.0 billion for the same period of 2025, primarily driven by the acquisition of the on-
demand delivery business in the fourth quarter of 2025.
7
Rental cost, mainly including depreciation of right-of-use assets and leasing expenses for short-term 
leases, remained relatively stable at RMB3.4 billion and RMB3.2 billion for the three months ended 
September 30, 2026 and for the same period of 2025, respectively.
Vehicle cost, mainly including fuel cost and road and bridge tolls, remained relatively stable 
at RMB3.3 billion and RMB3.1 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and for 
the same period of 2025, respectively. To mitigate the challenges posed by rising fuel prices, 
we continuously improved vehicle load factors and turnaround efficiency through intelligent 
dispatching algorithms, while optimizing our transport capacity mix by increasing the proportion of 
new energy vehicles, thereby partially offsetting the impact of fuel cost increases.
Depreciation of property and equipment and amortization of other intangible assets increased by 
10.3% to RMB1.3 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from RMB1.2 billion for 
the same period of 2025, primarily driven by the increase in logistics equipment and vehicles.
Other cost of revenue, mainly including cost of packaging and other consumable materials, cost 
of installation and maintenance services, water and electricity charges, and aviation operating 
expenses, remained relatively stable at RMB4.0 billion and RMB3.8 billion for the three months 
ended September 30, 2026 and for the same period of 2025, respectively.
Gross profit and gross profit margin
As a result of the foregoing, our gross profit and gross profit margin for the three months ended 
September 30, 2026 were RMB6.0 billion and 9.3%, respectively, compared to gross profit and 
gross profit margin of RMB5.0 billion and 9.1% for the same period of 2025, respectively.
Selling and marketing expenses
Selling and marketing expenses increased by 11.6% to RMB1.8 billion for the three months ended 
September 30, 2026, from RMB1.6 billion for the same period of 2025, primarily driven by the 
expansion of selling and marketing team to promote our service offerings.
Research and development expenses
Research and development expenses increased by 15.6% to RMB1.2 billion for the three months 
ended September 30, 2026, from RMB1.1 billion for the same period of 2025, primarily driven by 
our continuing investment in technology and innovation.
8
General and administrative expenses
General and administrative expenses increased by 20.8% to RMB1.3 billion for the three months 
ended September 30, 2026, from RMB1.1 billion for the same period of 2025, primarily driven by 
the increase in share-based payment expenses.
Profit for the period
We recorded a net profit of RMB1.7 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, 
compared to a net profit of RMB2.0 billion for the same period of 2025.
Non-IFRS Measures
To supplement our consolidated results, which are presented in accordance with IFRS Accounting 
Standards (“ IFRSs”), we also use non-IFRS operating profit, non-IFRS profit and non-IFRS 
EBITDA as additional financial measures, which are not required by, or presented in accordance 
with IFRSs. We believe these non-IFRS measures facilitate comparisons of core operating 
performance from period to period and from company to company by eliminating potential impacts 
of items which our management considers non-indicative of our core operating performance.
We belie ve these non-IFRS measures provide useful information to investors and others in 
understanding and evaluating our results of operations in the same manner as they help our 
management. However, our presentation of non-IFRS measures may not be comparable to similarly 
titled measures presented by other companies. The use of non-IFRS measures has limitations as an 
analytical tool, and you should not consider them in isolation from, or as a substitute for an analysis 
of, our results of operations or financial condition as reported under IFRSs.
We define “non-IFRS operating profit” as operating profit excluding share-based payment expenses 
and amortization of intangible assets resulting from acquisitions. We define “non-IFRS profit” and 
“non-IFRS EBITDA” as profit for the period, adjusted by excluding certain reconciling items as 
detailed in the reconciliation tables below.

Liquidity and Free Cash Flow
Our cash resources include cash and cash equivalents, term deposits, wealth management products 
classified as financial assets at fair value through profit or loss, treasury investments at amortized 
cost included in “prepayments, other receivables and other assets”, and restricted cash. As of 
September 30, 2026, the aggregate amount of cash resources of the Group was RMB40.0 billion.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Group had free cash inflow of RMB2.3 
billion, compared to a free cash inflow of RMB0.6 billion for the same period of 2025. This was a 
result of net cash generated from operating activities of RMB5.6 billion, less payments for capital 
expenditures net of related proceeds from disposals of RMB1.2 billion and payments relating to 
leases of RMB2.1 billion.
