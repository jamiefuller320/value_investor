– 2 –
The board (the “ Board”) of directors (the “ Directors”) of WH Group Limited (the 
“Company”) is pleased to announce the unaudited consolidated results of the Company 
and its subsidiaries (the “ Group”) for the three months ended 31 March 2026 (the 
“Period”). This announcement is made by the Company on a voluntary basis to enhance 
the practice of good corporate governance and further promote the transparency and 
accountability of the Company.
INDUSTRY OVERVIEW
As a leading pork company with global operations, our business is impacted by the 
market dynamics of hog industry  in each market that we operate. Supply and demand 
drive the movements in prices for hogs and pork and consequently impact our costs and 
sales. During the Period, hog production volume in the People’s Republic of China 
(“China”) increased by 2.8% year on year. The increase in market supplies drove the 
average hog price in China down to RMB12.35 (equivalent to approximately US$1.78) 
per kilogram (“ kg”), which represented a decrease of 22.8% comparing to that of the 
three months ended 31 March 2025 (the “ Comparable Period ”). In the United States of 
America (the “ US”), the average hog price for the Period remained stable at US$1.44 per 
kg while the average pork cutout value for the Period slightly increased by 1.1% to 
US$2.11 as a result of robust demand. In Europe, as the outbreak of African Swine 
Fever triggered trade restrictions, the average carcass price of the member states of the 
European Union during the Period decreased by 17.7% to Euro 1.54 (equivalent to 
approximately US$1.80) per kg, representing an average hog price of approximately Euro 
1.16 or US$1.36 per kg.

Packaged Meats
During the Period, our sales volume of packaged meats was 781 thousand metric tons, 
which represented an increase of 9.4% from that of the Comparable Period. In China, 
sales volume increased by 15.0%, benefiting from the transformation of products and 
channels. In North America, sales volume increased by 3.5% due to higher seasonal 
demand driven by earlier Easter. In Europe, sales volume increased by 8.8% due to the 
completion of acquisition of Wolf, a German premium sausages producer, as well as 
organic growth of the existing business. Revenue of our packaged meats during the 
Period increased by 11.5% because of higher sales volume and average sales price. 
Operating profit from our packaged meats also increased by 15.4% from that of the 
Comparable Period. In China, operating profit increased by 25.6% primarily due to the 
increase in sales volume and savings from lower raw materials cost. Operating profit in 
North America increased by 4.5% as sales volume increased. The negative impact of 
higher production and distribution costs was offset by higher average sales price and 
lower administrative spending. In Europe, operating profit significantly increased by 
40.6% as we achieved volume growth and benefited from low raw material costs.
Pork
Sales volume of pork increased by 5.6% to 1,037 thousand metric tons in the Period. In 
China, North America and Europe, sales volumes increased by 14.9%, 0.6% and 0.3% 
respectively. It is our operating strategy to optimise processing volume according to the 
market dynamics in each region. Our pork revenue during the Period decreased by 2.7% 
from that of the Comparable Period. In China, revenue decreased by 2.2% as the 
decrease in pork prices offset the increase in sales volume. In North America, revenue 
decreased by 5.2% primarily due to lower hedging revenue. In Europe, however, revenue 
increased by 12.1% as the impact of strong local currencies against US dollars and 
increases in sales of value-added products outweighed the decrease in pork prices. 
Operating profit from pork decreased notably by 34.4% because of the losses recorded in 
China and Europe. In China, the operating loss was primarily due to the operational 
challenges faced by hog production. In Europe, the operating loss was the result of 
depressed hog prices and elevated costs which lowered the overall profitability of the 
vertically integrated pork production chain. On the other hand, operating profit in North 
America increased by 5.4% as the decrease in revenue was made up by the savings in 
costs and expenses.
– 5 –
OUTLOOK
The Company delivered a solid increase in sales volume, revenue and profit during the 
first quarter of 2026. Looking forward, we will continue to focus on our core packaged 
meats business by transforming our products and channels in China, maintaining strong 
resilience in the US as well as expanding scale and growing profit in Europe. Meanwhile, 
we will further strengthen our pork business by improving the hog production operation 
and enhancing the added value of processing. Having said that, the dynamic development 
of global economic and political situations may create uncertainties and challenges to our 
operation. We will adapt to the changing circumstances, leverage on our advantages, 
continuously adhere to our “industrialisation, diversification, globalisation, digitalisation” 
and “two-adjustments one-control” strategies, strive to achieve stable and positive 
operating results, as well as to provide returns to shareholders of the Company.
