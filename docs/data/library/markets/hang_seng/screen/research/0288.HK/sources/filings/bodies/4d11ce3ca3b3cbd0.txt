– 2 –
The board (the “ Board”) of directors (the “ Directors”) of WH Group Limited (the 
“Company”) is pleased to announce the unaudited consolidated results of the Company 
and its subsidiaries (the “ Group”) for the nine months ended 30 September 2026 (the 
“Period”). This announcement is made by the Company on a voluntary basis to enhance 
the practice of good corporate governance and further promote the transparency and 
accountability of the Company.
INDUSTRY OVERVIEW
As a leading pork company in the world, our business is closely associated with the 
market dynamics of the hog industry in each market that we operate. Supply and 
demand drive the movement in prices of hogs and pork and consequently impact our 
inputs (costs) and outputs (sales). During the Period, the average hog price in the 
People’s Republic of China (“ China”) was RMB11.25 (equivalent to approximately 
US$1.65) per kilogram (“ kg”), representing a decrease of 25.5% from that of the nine 
months ended 30 September 2025 (the “ Comparable Period ”) as a result of abundant 
supply of hogs. In the United States of America (the “ US”), the average hog price 
decreased by 6.3% to US$1.50 per kg during the Period while the average pork cutout 
value for the Period decreased by 7.0% to US$2.13 per kg due to insufficient demand. In 
Europe, as a result of subdued demand and regional oversupply of hogs , the average 
carcass price of the member states of the European Union during the Period decreased 
by 20.4% to Euro 1.60 (equivalent to approximately US$1.86) per kg, representing an 
average hog price of approximately Euro 1.21 or US$1.40 per kg.

– 4 –
Packaged Meats
During the Period, sales volume of packaged meats increased by 3.9%. In China, sales 
volume increased by 5.8% due to the growth of sales in emerging channels. In North 
America, sales volume decreased by 0.6% as consumption demand was weak . In Europe, 
sales volume increased by 11.0% due to the contribution of newly acquired business. 
Total revenue of packaged meats increased by 4.7% during the Period. In China, revenue 
increased by 10.6% driven by the increase in sales volume and favourable currency 
impact. In North America, revenue decreased by 0.6% in line with the sales volume. In 
Europe, revenue increased by 16.8% due to volume growth and improved product mix. 
Operating profit of packaged meats in the Period increased by 3.8%. In China, operating 
profit increased by 5.7% as sales volume increased and raw material costs decreased, but 
part of the benefit was offset by the increase in manufacturing costs and marketing 
expenses. In North America, although we benefited from lower raw material costs, 
operating profit decreased by 2.5% due to elevated supply chain costs and increased 
promotion efforts. In Europe, operating profit increased notably by 36.9% mainly due to 
the increase in sales volume and decrease in raw material costs.
Pork
During the Period, sales volume of pork increased by 3.0%. In China, sales volume 
increased by 14.9% due to expanded sales network. In North America and Europe, sales 
volume decreased by 3.3% and 7.6% respectively as we adjusted production level in 
response to market demand. In spite of the increase in the overall sales volume, the 
decline in pork prices across operating regions drove the decrease in pork revenue by 
7.1%. In the Period, pork operating profit decreased significantly by 71.4%. In North 
America, operating profit decreased by 52.4% as market spread between hog and pork 
prices substantially narrowed since the later half of the second quarter. In China, we 
recorded an operating loss of US$4 million as the historical low hog prices impacted the 
results of upstream production and international trade businesses. In Europe, the 
operating loss was US$16 million as the persistently low hog prices pressured the 
performance of our vertically integrated pork production chain.
Acquisition of Nathan’s Famous
In January 2026, the Group entered into an agreement to acquire all of the issued and 
outstanding shares of Nathan’s Famous, Inc. (“ Nathan’s Famous ”), which owns a highly 
recognised beef hot dog brand “Nathan’s Famous” in the US, at a price of US$102 per 
share in cash. In October 2026, the acquisition was completed.
– 5 –
Continuous Rationalisation of Hog Production in the US
In October 2026, the Group entered into agreements with VisionAg Hog Production, 
LLC (“ VisionAg”) to sell approximately 40 thousand sows and the associated commercial 
hogs in North Carolina, the US to VisionAg, and assign certain contract grower 
agreements to VisionAg. As a result of these agreements, we will produce approximately 
900 thousand fewer hogs annually in the US. With our continuous efforts to rationalise 
the hog production operation in the US, its annual internal supply of hogs is expected to 
reduce progressively from approximately 15 million in 2024 to a target of less than 10 
million.
OUTLOOK
The global pork market faces widespread price declines in 2026 driven by regional 
oversupply and sluggish consumer demand. Against this backdrop, the Group achieved 
growth in sales volume and maintained stable turnover in the first three quarters. 
However, our profit level was under pressure.
Looking forward, unfavourable market conditions including weak consumption, cost 
inflation, volatile commodity prices, severe market competition and policy uncertainties 
will continue to have an impact on our operation. To navigate through these challenges, 
we will leverage our advantages and adapt to the evolving environment. We will also 
escalate efforts in optimising product and channel mix, maintain pricing discipline and 
rigorously manage costs and expenses.
Packaged meats is our core business. We will focus on volume growth and profit 
enhancement to deliver persistently solid results. Pork is our major business, we will 
continue to rationalise our vertically integrated industrial chain, manage risks and 
volatility, improve operation efficiency and upgrade profitability.
Our financial strengths, which are characterised by healthy operating cashflows, ample 
liquidity and low leverage ratio, will always support us in staying resilient and seizing 
opportunities. By adhering to the strategies of “Industrialisation, Diversification, 
Globalisation, Digitalisation”, we will strive to achieve good and stable operating results, 
as well as to create long-term shareholder value.
