Key Elements APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting CATL China Mobile China Telecom China Unicom Lan Fo'an Liao Min Loan Prime Rate (LPR) Tesla Meta Netflix SpaceX Hang Seng Index Hong Kong stocks face a four-session trading week due to the holiday, with market focus concentrated on two main threads: the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Finance Ministers' Meeting being held in Hong Kong, and a string of heavyweight Chinese companies—CATL (3750.HK), China Mobile (0941.HK), China Telecom (0728.HK) and China Unicom (0762.HK)—reporting quarterly results. Analysts generally believe the Hang Seng Index will struggle to break out of its range-bound pattern in the near term until the direction of US tech earnings and Treasury yields becomes clearer. The APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting is scheduled for October 20–21 in Hong Kong. Chinese Minister of Finance Lan Fo'an will lead the Chinese delegation, attend and chair the meeting, with Vice Minister Liao Min speaking on behalf of China. This year's agenda covers fiscal policy supporting livelihood development, digital infrastructure financing, inclusive finance and exchanges of young financial talent. Market participants noted that the key things to watch are whether the two topics of digital infrastructure financing and inclusive finance can produce concrete cooperation arrangements, and how various economies assess global growth, fiscal policy and financial stability risks. Most analysts, however, view the finance ministers' meeting as a policy communication exercise with limited direct impact on Hong Kong stock trading. On corporate earnings, this week's reporting schedule is notably dense. CATL, Tianyue Advanced and Han's CNC will report on Tuesday, with China Mobile disclosing its first-three-quarter operating results the same day; China Telecom will release key operating data for the first three quarters on Thursday, and China Unicom will report its first-three-quarter financial and operating metrics on Friday. Market attention on the three telecom carriers centers on the growth momentum of cloud, computing power and government-enterprise businesses, which in recent quarters have become the main engines for carriers to offset declining revenue from traditional voice and data services. CATL's power battery and energy storage shipment data is seen as an important indicator for validating global demand for new-energy vehicles and the strength of the energy storage sector. The table below summarizes the week's key events for Hong Kong stocks: Date Event Oct 20 (Tue) China's 1-year and 5-year Loan Prime Rates (LPR) released; APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting opens in Hong Kong; CATL, Tianyue Advanced and Han's CNC report earnings; China Mobile discloses first-three-quarter results Oct 21 (Wed) Second day of APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting; South Korea releases export and import data for the first 20 days of October; Tesla, Meta and Lam Research report Oct 22 (Thu) Bank of Korea announces rate decision; European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde speaks; China Telecom releases first-three-quarter operating data Oct 23 (Fri) Japan releases September CPI; US October S&P Global manufacturing and services PMIs and University of Michigan consumer sentiment index released; China Unicom reports first-three-quarter financial and operating metrics Note: Hong Kong stocks trade for only four sessions this week. On China macro data, Tuesday's release of the October Loan Prime Rate (LPR) is the week's first focal point. The 1-year LPR stood at 3.0% and the 5-year-plus at 3.5% in September, unchanged for 16 consecutive months, and the market widely expects them to hold steady again, though post-meeting statements and subsequent policy signals will remain in focus. In addition, the 2026 Financial Street Forum annual meeting was held in Beijing's Financial Street from October 19–22; the 19th China-EU Investment, Trade and Technology Cooperation Fair will be held in Chengdu from October 21–23. Overseas markets are equally busy. The US earnings season is entering its dense phase, with Tesla (TSLA), Meta (META) and SpaceX set to report, and Netflix also on the reporting list. Tesla previously disclosed third-quarter vehicle deliveries of more than 486,000 units and energy storage deployments of 13.7GWh; this earnings report will further reveal the revenue, automotive business margin and cash flow performance corresponding to delivery growth, and analysts will press on the follow-up rollout of Robotaxi, Full Self-Driving (FSD) and new models during the conference call. SpaceX will release its second post-listing earnings report after the US market close on Tuesday, with the market expecting its adjusted earnings per share to potentially turn positive for the first time. On Wednesday, South Korea releases export and import data for the first 20 days of October, with semiconductor export performance providing clues on AI and memory demand. On impact analysis, analysts noted that although China's economic data has improved recently, it is still insufficient to single-handedly support Hong Kong stocks, and the key to the outlook remains the performance of US AI concept stocks and the direction of US Treasury yields. If US tech giants' earnings release positive capital expenditure signals, it would help lift sentiment in Hong Kong's AI and semiconductor supply chains; conversely, if Treasury yields rise again, funding-side pressure will curb room for valuation recovery. With the APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting unlikely to be a substantive positive, the Hang Seng Index is expected to trade in a 23,500–24,600 range in the near term. Investors should watch for divergence among individual stocks after CATL and the three telecom carriers report, as well as the marginal impact of the LPR outcome on property and domestic-demand sectors.
