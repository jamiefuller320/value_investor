So far this year, facing opportunities and challenges, China Unicom has adhered to "Preserve and 
Innovate, Steady and Far -reaching", focused on the four core arenas of "connectivity", "computing 
power", "service", and "security", accelerated its pace of innovative transformation, and promoted the 
achievement of high-quality development. 
 
In the first three quarters of 2026, the Company achieved operating revenue of RMB 294.6 billion, 
representing a year-on-year increase of 0.6%. Profit before income tax was RMB 16.2 billion . Profit 
attributable to equity shareholders of the Company was RMB 13.3 billion , down by 33.6% year-on-
year. 
 
Consolidating the operational foundation. The Company’s connectivity scale witnessed growth, 
with the total number of connections2 exceeding 1.3 billion . Within that,  mobile and broadband 
subscribers saw a net addition of 12.4 0 million, and IoT connections saw a net addition of over 66 
million. Network capacity and capabilities were upgraded, with the total number of 4G/5G base 
stations exceeding 4.80 million and 10G optical networks undergoing commercial trials in more than 
100 cities . The number of standard cabinets3 reached 1.2 0 million, and the total capacity of IDC 
resources reached 3,000 MW. Service scenarios were expanded, with over 10,000 5G factories built, 
and the number of paying users of Cloud-AI products surpassing 120 million. 
 
Unleashing growth momentum. Computing power demonstrated strong growth momentum, with 
computing power revenue1 growing at 12.2% year-on-year. Within that, IDC revenue grew by 10.4% 
year-on-year. The Company enhanced its  end-to-end capabilities across the generation, storage, 
transmission, cleansing, and application of tokens, act ing as an operator of high -value tokens  to 
accelerate the scale adoption of AI. Internationalisation accelerated, with international business 
revenue increasing by 14.5% year-on-year. Products were iterated swiftly, as the Company deepened 
the "drag-and-drop" model, opened up ready-to-use capability modules, and enhanced delivery quality 
and efficiency. The Company d eployed multiple formats, such as "set meals", " à la carte", and 
"buffets", to overcome homogeneous competition with differentiated products. 
 
Stimulating innovation vitality. The Company opened up its innovation system and further increased 
technological research investment, with R&D expenses increasing by 7.5% year -on-year. Centering 
on intelligent foundation, smart applications, and security protection,  it has  released nine major 
achievements of preservation and innovation4. The Company enhanced its security capability, opening 
up two mature platforms, namely "Unicom Mogong" and "Security Hub", in order to promote  "AI 
empowerment of security" and "security assurance of AI" . The number of major institutional and 
industry customers served by security business5 exceeded 480,000. 
 
Facing the valuable opportunity where artificial intelligence accelerates its transformation from a 
technology into an industr y growth driver, China Unicom places technological innovation at a core 
position of its overall development, achieving a transformation from a "traditional pipeline provider" 
into a " integrated digital and intelligent service provider", and striving to create greater value for 
shareholders, customers, and the society.
