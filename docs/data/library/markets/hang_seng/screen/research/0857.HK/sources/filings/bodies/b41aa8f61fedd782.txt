1.3  Differences between CAS and IFRS Accounting Standards 
The consolidated net profit for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was RMB171,977 million 
under IFRS Accounting Standards and RMB171,974 million under CAS, with a difference of RMB3 
million. The consolidated shareholders’ equity as of September 30, 2026 was RMB1,845,752 million 
under IFRS Accounting Standards and RMB1,845,973 million under CAS, with a difference of RMB221 
million. These differences under the different accounting standards were primarily due to the revaluation 
for assets other than fixed assets and  oil and gas properties in 1999. During the restructuring  of the 
Company in 1999, a valuation was carried out  for assets and liabilities injected by China National 
Petroleum Corporation (its Chinese name  was changed from 中國石油天然氣集團公司 to 中國石油
天然氣集團有限公司 on December 19, 2017, abbreviated as “CNPC” before and after the change of 
name). The valuation results on assets other than fixed assets and oil and gas properties  were not 
recognized in the financial statements prepared under IFRS Accounting Standards.

3. Business Review 
In the first three quarters of 2026, global economic growth has experienced a slowdown but remained 
resilient. The economy of China remained stable and moved towards new and better development, with 
the gross domestic product (GDP) growing by 4.6% as compared with the same period of last year. 
Affected by geopolitical conditions, international crude oil prices fluctuated widely, with the average price 
increasing significantly as compared with the same period of last year. The average futures price of North 
Sea Brent crude oil was US$88.86 per barrel, representing an increase of 27.1% as compared with 
US$69.91 per barrel in the same period of last year; the average futures price of U.S. West Texas 
Intermediate crude oil was US$83.76 per barrel, representing an in crease of 25.7% as compared with 
US$66.65 per barrel in the same period of last year. The substitution by new energy sources accelerated
7 
 
in China and the market demand for refined oil products decreased , while domestic natural gas 
consumption decreased slightly. 
 
The Group actively responded to changes in domestic and international macroeconomic s, 
geopolitical and energy and chemicals market conditions, including the turbulent geopolitical situation in 
the Middle East, wide fluctuations in international oil  prices, substitution by new energy sources and 
decrease in demand for refined oil products. The Group effectively organized production and operations, 
further increased efforts in oil and gas exploration and development, diversified its resource procurement, 
ensured the high -load operation of refining and chemical facilities, and safeguarded the security of the 
industrial chain and supply chain as well as stable market supply. The Group vigorously developed 
emerging businesses such as new energy and new materials, and continued to promote the transformation 
and upgrading of its businesses. The Group carried out in-depth precision marketing to ensure the smooth 
operation of the industrial chain and the realization of value. The production and operations of the Group 
were safe and stable, its operating results increased significantly, and its financial position remained 
healthy. In the first three quarters of 2026, the average realized crude oil price of the Group was US$77.65 
per barrel, representing an increase of 18.5% as compared with US$65.55 per barrel in the same period of 
last year; the average selling price of domestic natural gas (including liquefied natural gas (“LNG”), same 
as below) was US$9.40 per thousand cubic feet, representing an increase of 1 .7% as compared with the 
same period of last year after excluding the impact of exchange rates. Under IFRS Accounting Standards, 
the Group recorded revenue of RMB2,321,064 million, representing an increase of 7.0% as compared with 
RMB2,169,063 million in the same period of last year, and profit for the period attributable to owners of 
the Company of RMB155,776 million, representing an increase of 21.9% as compared with RMB127,833 
million in the same period of last year. 
 
In respect of the oil, gas and new energy business, the Group increased efforts in exploration and 
development and continued to promote the growth of oil and gas reserves and production domestically; in 
terms of the overseas business, the Group actively responded to changes in geopolitical conditions, strived 
to ensure the safe and stable operation of production, coord inated the operation of existing projects and 
the development of new projects, and continued to optimize its asset structure; and in terms of the new 
energy business , the Group  coordinated the acquisition of quotas and project construction, and 
strengthened its integrated development with the oil and gas business. In the first three quarters of 2026, 
the Group’s crude oil output was 698.6 million barrels, of which domestic crude oil output was 590.4 
million barrels and overseas crude oil output was 108.2 million barrels. The marketable natural gas output 
was 4,069.4 billion cubic feet, of which domestic marketable natural gas output was 3,947.2 billion cubic 
feet, representing an increase of 2.3% as compared with 3,858.2 billion cubic feet in the same period  of 
last year, and overseas marketable natural gas output was 122.2 billion cubic feet, representing an increase 
of 2.7% as compared with 119.0 billion cubic feet in the same period of last year. The oil and natural gas 
equivalent output was 1,376.8 million barrels, of which domestic oil and natural gas equivalent output was 
1,248.3 million barrels, representing an increase of 1.1% as compared with 1,234.3 million barrels in the 
same period of last year ; and overseas oil and natural gas equivalent output wa s 128.6 million barrels, 
representing a decrease of 10.0% as compared with 142.8 million barrels in the same period of last year, 
primarily due to the impact of the geopolitical situation and the decline in crude oil production in the 
Middle East. In the third quarter  of 2026, the Group’s oil and natural gas equivalent output was 456.2 
million barrels, representing an increase of 1.3% as compared with the second quarter of 2026, of which 
overseas oil and natural gas equivalent output was 45.3 million barrels, representing an increase of 24.2% 
as compared with the second quarter of 2026. In the first three quarters of 2026, energy output from the 
Group’s wind and photovoltaic power plants amounted to 7.84 billion kilowatt -hours, representing an 
increase of 35.5% as compared with 5.79 billion kilowatt -hours in the same period of last year. The 
geothermal heating  (cooling) covering more than  80 million square meters  (including areas under 
entrusted operation and maintenance ) was completed. The oil, gas and new energy segment recorded 
revenue of RMB666,494 million, representing an increase of 6.5% as compared with RMB625,847 million

increase of 19.7% as compared with RMB127,009 million in the same period of last year, primarily due 
to the increase in crude oil prices, the increase in natural gas production and the strong efforts of the Group 
to control oil and gas production costs.

compared with RMB826,056 million in the same per iod of last year, and a profit from operations of 
RMB23,132 million, representing an increase of RMB6,892 million as compared with RMB16,240
