SEHK:6618 Trailing 12-Month Earnings & Revenue History as at Aug 2026 JD Health’s Margin Story Is Hitting Its Marks The bullish pitch around JD Health International is that omnichannel scale and AI should steadily lift margins while services become a larger earnings engine. The latest half year results give that view some hard evidence. Non IFRS operating margin reached 8.5% with nine straight quarters of year on year gains, which fits a narrative of improving operating leverage rather than one off cost cuts. Service revenue of RMB 7.0b is rising as a share of the total and is backed by concrete milestones. The “Dr. Da Wei” AI doctor has grown its user base multiple times over and is already embedded in consultations and conversions, even if monetization is still early. Fulfillment expense as a ratio of revenue is flat at about 10%, which suggests JD Health is holding the line on its dependence on JD.com logistics while letting procurement and mix do more of the margin work. JD Health Bear Case Meets Regulatory And AI Reality The bearish view is that JD Health faces rising regulatory and AI compliance costs that could eventually squeeze margins and cash generation. The latest half year results do not fully support that yet, but they also do not clear the risks. Gross margin is 26.1% and non IFRS operating margin is 8.5%, with fulfillment at about 10% of revenue. That suggests regulatory scrutiny in nutrition and supplements is a drag on growth mix, not yet a visible hit to group level profitability. However, AI products such as “Dr. Da Wei” are still largely non commercial and management openly flags uncertainty around the timing of meaningful AI revenue. With medical devices growing slower than other segments and nutrition under regulatory pressure, the feared mix and policy headwinds are present, just partially masked for now by procurement gains and cost discipline.
