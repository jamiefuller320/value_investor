MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS 
1. Industry Overview 
 
In the third quarter of 2026, demand in China’s home appliance market remained under pressure, with industry 
retail sales reaching RMB 179.1 billion1, down 10% year over year. Affected by one- off factors including a high base 
in the same period of 2025 and the diminishing effect of national trade -in policies, compounded by a lower baseline 
demand and weak consumer sentiment, the overall industry experienced simultaneous declines in both volume and value 
alongside a downward shift in product mix. 
 
Overseas markets continued to diverge: weighed down by high interest rates and a sluggish housing market, overall 
U.S. appliance demand remained soft, with third-quarter core industry shipments declining approximately 4% year over 
year2; in Europe, retail sales value of refrigerators and washing machines remained largely flat year over year in the first 
three quarters, while sales volume achieved low single-digit growth3, as consumers favouring value-for-money energy 
efficient products with strong ESG attributes. 
 
Emerging markets including South Asia, Southeast Asia, and Latin America maintained rapid growth, benefiting 
from urbanization and demographic dividends. 
 
2. Financial Performance and Operational Initiatives 
 
(I) Revenue and Profit Performance 
 
In the first three quarters of 2026, the Company recorded revenue of RMB 221.819 billion and net profit attributable 
to shareholders of the listed company of RMB 14.999 billion, representing decreases of 5.23% and 13.67% compared 
to the same period in 2025, respectively. 
 
In the third quarter of 2026, the Company recorded revenue of RMB 69.704 billion and net profit attributable to 
shareholders of the listed company of RMB 4.682 billion, down 10.13% and 12.31% compared to the same period in 
2025, respectively. During the reporting period, foreign exchange fluctuations and one -off expenses weighed 
significantly on profit : ①Due to the appreciation of the Renminbi, foreign exchange losses recognized in financial 
expenses for the quarter amounted to RMB 502 million, partially offset by hedging gains of RMB 138 million, resulting 
in a net foreign exchange loss of RMB 364 million (foreign exchange gain of RMB 134 million in the same period of 
2025); fluctuations in foreign exchange gains and losses increased year-over-year losses by RMB 498 million. ② The 
Company proactively advanced global supply chain optimization and overseas regional platform integration, incurring 
one-off expenses of approximately RMB 500 million.  
 
(II) Operational Initiatives 
 
During the reporting period, the Company advanced organizational transformation and business model upgrading, 
building long-term competitiveness from the integration of the Smart HVAC Solutions and Smart Home Appliances 
businesses, channel transformation, supply chain optimization, and AI empowerment. The Smart HVAC Solutions 
business refined its collaborative framework, strengthening competitiveness ac ross R&D, procurement, and 
manufacturing; the Smart Home Appliances business initiated organizational integration to accelerate global supply 
chain deployment, improve operational efficiency, and unlock marketing synergy. The Company leveraged AI to drive 
transformation, comprehensively advancing intelligent upgrading across R&D, manufacturing, supply chain, marketing, 
and service to accelerate organizational restructuring and comprehensive efficiency improvements.

China Market 
 
During the reporting period, the Company drove product mix upgrades, deepened the transformation of its unified 
warehouse direct -to-consumer model, advanced GEO marketing transformation, and strengthened user reach, 
solidifying its industry leadership. AVC  data indicates the Company’s online retail value share reached 20.6%  from 
January to September, up 2.9 percentage points year over year; its offline retail value share reached 25.0%, up 1.7 
percentage points year over year. 
 
The Company remained committed to upgrading its product mix: the refrigeration business launched refrigerators 
equipped with industry- first AI cellular -level thawing technology, reinforcing its competitive edge in the high- end 
segment; the laundry business introduced an industry-first four-drum AI washing machine, precisely addressing multi-
zone washing needs and elevating the high -end fabric care experience; the air conditioning business accelerated the 
rollout of mid- to-high-end products to increase the market shares of floor-standing and large -capacity wall-mounted 
units. During the reporting period, according to GfK CMM data, Casarte achieved market share gains across most 
product categories. 
 
The Company deepened transformation of the unified warehouse direct-to-consumer model, with the onboarding 
rate of clients under model reaching 85% as of the end of the reporting period. Following the Company’s acquisition of 
RRS Logistics, integration synergies have gradually materialized; in the third quarter of 2026, average dail y orders 
delivered directly to consumers increased by 37% year over year. 
 
Overseas Markets 
 
The Company adhered to a brand-building strategy tailored to local market conditions, deepening global product 
platform integration and localized supply chain deployment. 
 
During the reporting period, in North America, pricing realization and strong growth in Air & Water and Small 
Domestic Appliances partially offset the impact of weaker demand on overall revenue. The business expanded its core 
appliance share through the first three quarters despite industry headwinds. On the product front, GE Appliances 
expanded its commercial HVAC portfolio with a new VRF4 platform and broadened its Café and GE Profile countertop-
appliance portfolios, including GE Profile Opal Mini nugget ice makers and refreshed Café coffee makers, toasters and 
countertop ovens. 
 
Core European markets achieved mid-to-high single-digit growth in local currency. Leveraging product innovation 
and global platform synergy, the Company gained market share in refrigerators and washing machines, with retail value 
shares in refrigeration and laundry increasing by 0.9 and 0.4 percentage points year over year, respectively, during the

accelerated the R&D and market deployment of R290 refrigerant air conditioners and heat pumps  to capture rapid 
growth in HVAC business.

III. Gross Profit Margin 
The gross profit margin for the first three quarters of 2026 reached 27.2%, remaining largely unchanged from the same 
period in 2025. In the China market, although the Company pursued an end- to-end cost optimization strategy and 
enhanced competitiveness through measures such as hedging against commodity price increases, opening up the supplier 
network, improving component commonality, launching platforms for best-selling products and raising the level of in-
house supply chain production, the gross profit margin declined year on year due to rising commodity prices, 
deterioration in the industry structure and intense end -market price competition. In overseas markets, despite higher 
tariffs, commodity prices and ocean freight costs, the Company responded proactively through its global supply chain 
layout and cost optimization initiatives, and benefited from a one-off tax refund, resulting in a year-on-year increase in 
gross profit margin. 
IV. Expense Ratios 
1. The selling expense ratio for the first three quarters of 2026 was 10.6%, remaining largely unchanged from the same 
period in 2025. In the domestic market, the Company enhanced the efficiency of marketing expenditure through the 
integration of large HVAC and large white goods businesses and marketing transformation, and continued to advance 
the centralized warehousing ToC  model, with the expense ratio remaining broadly flat. In overseas markets, the 
Company continued to advance the upgrade of its four-network system and the transformation of e-commerce channels; 
with the results of channel transformation becoming evident, the selling expense ratio improved year on year. 
2. The administrative expense ratio for the first three quarters of 2026 was 3.9%, representing an increase of 0.2 percentage 
points as compared with the same period in 2025. In particular, in the domestic market, the administrative expense ratio 
improved year on year through organizational efficiency enhancement; in overseas markets, the Company promoted the 
development of organizational operating capabilities in emerging markets, leading to increased investment in human 
resources and a year-on-year increase in the administrative expense ratio. 
3. The financial expense ratio for the first three quarters of 2026 was 0.7% ("+" as expense, "- " as income), representing 
an increase of 0.8 percentage points as compared with the same period in 2025, primarily attributable to increased 
exchange losses resulting from the appreciation of Renminbi. 
V. Working Capital 
1. Trade and bill receivable turnover days 
Trade and bill receivable turnover days were 47.5 days in the first three quarters of 2026, representing an increase of 
3.9 days as compared to the end of 2025, mainly attributable to the structural changes resulting from channel expansion 
in domestic market, as well as the adjustments to the trade and bill receivable factoring schedule in overseas markets. 
2. Inventory turnover days 
Inventory turnover days were 79.7 days in the first three quarters of 2026, representing an increase of 5.6 days as 
compared to the end of 2025, mainly attributable to advance stocking in response to rising commodity prices and major 
promotional events.

VII. Capital Expenditure 
The Company assesses the capital expenditure and investments of its business segments from time to time. Capital 
expenditure for the first three quarters of 2026 was RMB7.425 billion, of which RMB3.615 billion and RMB3.810 
billion were used in China and overseas, respectively, primarily for plant and equipment construction, digital 
transformation and ecosystem development. 
VIII. Gearing Ratio 
The Company's gearing ratio was 56.9% as at the end of the third quarter of 2026, representing a decrease of 0.5 
percentage points as compared to the end of 2025.
