Kuaishou Revenue Rises 12% as Kling AI Monetization Accelerates — TradingView News Search Products Community Markets Brokers More EN Get started News / GuruFocus / Kuaishou Revenue Rises 12% as Kling AI Monetization Accelerates Kuaishou Revenue Rises 12% as Kling AI Monetization Accelerates 1 min read 1024 Kuaishou Technology (KUASF) is beginning to show early signs of scaled AI monetization, with its latest quarterly results suggesting that growing demand for generative tools could be supporting both revenue and earnings expansion. Fourth-quarter revenue rose 12% to 39.6 billion yuan, broadly in line with expectations, while adjusted net income increased 16%, coming in ahead of analyst forecasts. The performance appears closely tied to rising adoption of its AI-driven offerings, particularly as usage expands across both professional creators and enterprise clients. A key driver in that narrative is Kling AI, the company's text-to-video platform, which continues to gain traction as monetization efforts accelerate. Revenue from Kling AI reached 340 million yuan in the quarter, up from more than 300 million yuan in the previous period, suggesting that commercial use cases could be scaling alongside user growth. Management indicated that demand from global clients is strengthening, and expects revenue from the product may more than double this year, reinforcing its position as a potential long-term growth engine within the broader platform. At the same time, management signaled that the path forward may require sustained investment. Research and development spending increased 19% last year, driven largely by higher AI-related investment and employee costs, while capital expenditure could reach around 26 billion yuan, reflecting an increase of about 11 billion yuan from 2025. Executives pointed to higher inference computing needs and upcoming model upgrades as key drivers of spending. While earnings growth could remain in the low- to mid-teen range, according to Bloomberg Intelligence, rising development and computing costs may continue to weigh on margins through 2026, suggesting investors may need to balance near-term profitability with longer-term AI-driven expansion.
