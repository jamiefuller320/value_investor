RESULTS
During the first nine months of 2026, the Group recorded revenue of RMB25,260 million and reported 
profit attributable to shareholders of the Company of RMB7,076 million, representing increases of 
27.0% and 101.5%, respectively, as compared with the same period last year. Excluding fair value 
changes on financial assets measured at FVTPL and employee share-based compensation expense, 
underlying profit attributable to shareholders of the Company amounted to RMB7,220 million, 
representing an increase of 134.5% as compared with the same period last year.
Basic earnings per share based on reported profit attributable to shareholders of the Company as 
shown in the financial statements amounted to RMB62.14 cents, representing an increase of 102.3% as 
compared with the same period last year.

In January 2026, the Group entered into a strategic collaboration and licence agreement with 
AstraZeneca (the “Agreement”) for the development of innovative long-acting peptide medicines, and 
received an upfront payment of US$1.2 billion (the “ Upfront Payment”) pursuant to the terms of the 
Agreement in May 2026. US$840 million (equivalent to approximately RMB5.74 billion) of the 
Upfront Payment was recognised as licence fee income during the first nine months of 2026, 
contributing significant revenue to the Group during the reporting period.
Excluding the impact of the licence fee income, sales revenue of the finished drugs business for the 
reporting period increased by 10.6% as compared with the same period last year,  among which the 
nervous system and digestion and metabolism therapeutic areas achieved significant growth.
The Group will continue to advance academic promotion, lower-tier market penetration and retail-
channel expansion, while increasing research and development (“ R&D”) investment, facilitating new 
product launches and expanding overseas markets through out-licensing and international cooperation 
to promote diversified revenue growth.
Bulk Products Business
During the first nine months of 2026, the bulk products business recorded sales revenue of RMB2,336 
million, representing a year-on-year decrease of 22.3%.
Vitamin C
During the first nine months of 2026, sales revenue of Vitamin C products amounted to RMB1,451 
million, representing a decrease of 18.9% as compared with the same period last year, primarily due to 
a decline in product selling prices. Meanwhile, the release of industry capacity resulted in increased 
supply and intensified competition, driving down overall market price. The Group will keep on 
consolidating product quality, enhancing customer services, steadily expanding overseas sales 
channels, and continuously increasing its market share.
– 4 –
Antibiotics
During the first nine months of 2026, sales revenue of antibiotics products amounted to RMB885 
million, representing a decrease of 27.3% as compared with the same period last year, mainly due to 
the intensified market competition and the effect of downstream products being selected in the 11th 
batch of the national volume-based procurement. During the reporting period, the prices of penicillin-
series products declined to a certain extent; both the sales volume and price of 7-ACA decreased; and 
the sales volume of penem-series products declined to a certain extent.
Functional Food and Other Businesses
During the first nine months of 2026, sales revenue of functional food and other businesses amounted 
to RMB1,276 million, representing a decrease of 11.1% as compared with the same period last year, 
mainly due to the decrease in both sales volume and prices of anhydrous glucose and caffeine during 
the period.
R&D
R&D expenses for the period increased by 10.9% to RMB4,643 million as compared with the same 
period last year, accounting for 30.2% of the revenue from the finished drugs business (excluding the 
impact of licence fee income).
Regulatory Updates
Since the beginning of 2026, the regulatory progress of the Group in the PRC is as follows: 7 
innovative drugs have obtained marketing approvals; applications for marketing approval of 10 drugs 
have been accepted; 5 drugs have been granted Breakthrough Therapy designations; 32 drugs have 
obtained clinical trial approvals. In addition, the Group received 12 clinical trial approvals and a Fast 
Track designation in overseas markets and received marketing approval for one self-developed drug in 
the European Union.

OUT-LICENSING
Sustained-Release Drug Delivery Technology Platform and AI-driven Peptide Drug Discovery 
Platform
In January 2026, the Group entered into a strategic collaboration and license agreement with 
AstraZeneca for the development of innovative long-acting peptide therapeutics. AstraZeneca obtained 
exclusive global rights to the Group’s portfolio of once-monthly injectable weight management 
products, (excluding mainland China, the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao

to US$13.8 billion in potential sales milestone payments, plus up to double-digit tiered royalties.
The Group received the US$1.2 billion upfront payment and a US$25 million development milestone 
payment in May and July 2026, respectively, indicating that the related R&D work is progressing 
steadily as planned.
siRNA Drug Discovery Platform
In July 2026, the Group entered into an agreement with AstraZeneca to jointly develop novel small 
nucleic acid drugs with the potential to treat renal diseases across multiple indications, leveraging the 
Group’s siRNA drug discovery platform and extrahepatic targeted delivery platform. AstraZeneca will 
have the option to obtain the exclusive rights for development, manufacturing and commercialisation 
globally or outside of China, as applicable. The Group will receive an upfront payment of US$30 
million and is eligible to receive up to US$540 million in potential development milestone payments 
and up to US$1.2 billion in potential sales milestone payments, plus single-digit percentage sales 
royalties.
The Group received the upfront payment of US$30 million in August 2026, and the collaboration is 
progressing smoothly according to plan.
ESTABLISHMENT OF A JOINT VENTURE
In August 2026, the Group and AstraZeneca entered into a joint venture contract to establish a joint 
venture for the construction of a new-generation biologics manufacturing facility in Shijiazhuang, 
Hebei, China (the “ Joint Venture”). The Joint Venture aims to combine the Group’s AI-driven Good 
Manufacturing Practice (GMP) system and pharmaceutical manufacturing construction and operational 
capabilities with AstraZeneca’s expertise in global quality standards and supply chain management, 
with a commitment to delivering high-quality medicines to patients worldwide. The Group and 
AstraZeneca will contribute capital at an equity ratio of 51% and 49%, respectively, and will jointly 
manage the construction and day-to-day operations of the Joint Venture by leveraging their respective 
expertise. The joint establishment of the Joint Venture by both parties will help integrate the Group’s 
advantages in building highly automated, intelligent, and lean manufacturing facilities and current 
Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) operations with AstraZeneca’s global quality system and supply 
management experience, thereby creating high-quality manufacturing capabilities that meet the 
demands of the international market.

Sale of goods
Revenue is recognised at a point in time when control of the goods has been transferred, being when the goods 
have been delivered to the customer’s specific location. Following the delivery, the customer bears the risks of 
obsolescence and loss in relation to the goods.
Licence fee income
(i) Revenue recognised at a point in time
The Group provides licence of its patented intellectual property or commercialisation rights to customers. 
Licence fee income is recognised at a point in time when the customer obtains control of the intellectual 
property. The consideration received comprises a fixed element (the upfront payment) and variable 
elements (including but not limited to milestone payments and sales-based royalties).
