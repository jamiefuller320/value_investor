CSPC Pharmaceutical Group’s Shenzhen‑listed subsidiary, CSPC Innovation Pharmaceutical, released unaudited interim results for the six months ended 30 June 2026 showing a sharp turnaround to profitability. Revenue surged 208.72% year on year to RMB 3.24 billion, while net profit attributable to shareholders jumped to RMB 1.26 billion from a small loss a year earlier. Excluding non‑recurring items, net profit reached RMB 1.23 billion, and operating cash flow swung to RMB 2.78 billion from a negative base, reflecting stronger core performance. Total assets rose 38.75% to RMB 8.71 billion and net assets climbed 47.58%, lifting the weighted average return on net assets to 38.20%, results that underscore CSPC Innovation’s rapidly improving financial strength but remain separate from the parent company’s consolidated figures, as the board cautioned investors. More about CSPC Pharmaceutical Group CSPC Pharmaceutical Group is a Hong Kong‑incorporated drug maker with operations spanning innovative and generic medicines, with a focus on the Chinese market. Its subsidiary, CSPC Innovation Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., is listed on the ChiNext Market of the Shenzhen Stock Exchange and concentrates on R&D‑driven pharmaceutical products under PRC accounting standards.
