II. PERFORMANCE OF THIRD QUARTERLY RESULTS
During the third quarter of 2026, the Group continued to deliver solid operating performance, 
recording total sales volume of 812,523 units, representing a YoY increase of 7%. Driven by 
substantial growth in export sales and a rising contribution from high-value products, the Group’s 
revenue for the period increased by 10% YoY, outpacing the growth in sales volume and indicating 
an improvement in product mix and average selling price.
Profit attributable to owners of the parent for the third quarter of 2026 amounted to RMB4.84 
billion (third quarter of 2025 (restated): RMB3.79 billion), representing a YoY increase of 28%.
To facilitate an assessment of the Group’s underlying operating performance, core profit attributable 
to owners of the parent, a non-HKFRS financial measure, for the third quarter of 2026 amounted 
to RMB5.21 billion (third quarter of 2025 (restated): RMB3.94 billion), representing a YoY 
increase of 32%.
For the nine months ended 30 September 2026, core profit attributable to owners of the parent 
amounted to RMB14.90 billion (nine months ended 30 September 2025 (restated): RMB10.56 
billion), representing a YoY increase of 41%, while profit attributable to owners of the parent 
amounted to RMB13.93 billion (nine months ended 30 September 2025 (restated): RMB13.05 
billion), representing a YoY increase of 7%. Core profit grew at a faster rate than profit attributable 
to owners of the parent, primarily because the Group recognised net foreign exchange losses 
during the period, as compared to net foreign exchange gains recognised in the corresponding 
period last year. The growth in core profit also exceeded the growth in revenue, reflecting the 
Group’s continued improvement in overall operating performance.

IV. BASIS OF PREPARATION AND CONSOLIDATION
The condensed consolidated financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis. 
As at 30 September 2026, the Group recorded net current liabilities of approximately RMB8,745 
million, compared with approximately RMB15,042 million as at 30 June 2026, representing an 
improvement of approximately RMB6,297 million, or 42% The improvement was primarily 
attributable to improved operating cash inflows and enhanced working capital management. 
Having considered the Group’s financial position, cash flow projections and available financing 
resources, the directors are of the view that the Group has adequate resources to continue in 
operational existence for the foreseeable future and that no material uncertainty exists that may 
cast significant doubt on the Group’s ability to continue as a going concern.
Notwithstanding its net current liabilities position, the Board believes that the Group has sufficient 
financial resources to meet its working capital requirements and financial obligations as and when 
they fall due. This assessment is based on the following factors:
1. The Group’s profitable business segments continue to generate stable operating cash inflows;
2. The Group maintains access to banking facilities and other financing arrangements through 
its established relationships with financial institutions; and
3. Management has implemented and continues to execute measures to enhance liquidity, 
including optimising its balance sheet structure, accelerating the collection of receivables 
and prudently managing capital expenditures.
Based on the foregoing, the directors are satisfied that the Group will have sufficient financial 
resources to meet its financial obligations as and when they fall due for at least twelve months 
from 30 September 2026. Accordingly, the condensed consolidated financial statements have 
been prepared on a going concern basis.

The above transactions (collectively, the “Transactions”) were completed in May 2026. Upon 
completion of the Transactions, the above entities became wholly-owned subsidiaries of the 
Company.
As the entities acquired were under the common control of Mr. Li Shu Fu, the controlling party 
of the Company, both before and after completion of the Transactions, the Transactions were 
accounted for as business combinations under common control. Accordingly, the principles of 
merger accounting for common control combinations set out in Accounting Guideline 5 (Revised) 
“Merger Accounting for Common Control Combinations” have been applied.
As a result, the condensed consolidated financial statements have been prepared as if the acquired 
entities had been consolidated with the Group from the date on which they first came under the 
common control of Mr. Li Shu Fu.
