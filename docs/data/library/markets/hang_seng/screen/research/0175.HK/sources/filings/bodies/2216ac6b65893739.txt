Key Elements Geely Automobile Holdings 00175.HK Full-Domain AI Intelligent Vehicles Geely Automobile Holdings Limited (00175.HK) released its 2026 interim results on August 17. First-half total revenue reached 173.6 billion yuan (approximately $25.8 billion), up 15% year-on-year and hitting an all-time high, marking six consecutive years of steady growth. Core net profit attributable to shareholders reached 9.68 billion yuan (approximately $1.4 billion), surging 46% year-on-year, significantly outpacing revenue growth. Core net margin came in at 5.6%, up 27% year-on-year. On the profitability front, gross margin improved to 17.9%, while revenue per vehicle rose 16% year-on-year to 112,000 yuan (approximately $16,650). Against a backdrop of overall pressure on the auto industry, Geely Auto leveraged its "One Geely" strategy, with its four major brands delivering coordinated momentum and achieving notable results in premiumization and globalization. The company said that in the second half of 2026, it will consolidate its domestic market position in China, accelerate overseas expansion, and use full-domain AI as its core engine to continue deepening high-quality development, accelerating its push toward becoming a "leader in full-domain AI intelligent vehicles." Core financial metrics improve across the board Looking at the interim report data, Geely Auto achieved breakthroughs on both revenue scale and profit quality. The 173.6 billion yuan revenue total not only set a company record, but the 15% year-on-year growth rate also indicates that the core business remains on a steady expansion trajectory. The profit side was even more impressive. Core net profit attributable to shareholders of 9.68 billion yuan, corresponding to a 46% year-on-year increase, far outpaced revenue growth, reflecting continued improvement in cost control, product mix optimization, and brand pricing power. Core net margin rose from approximately 4.4% in the same period last year to 5.6%, up 27% year-on-year, demonstrating a systematic improvement in profitability efficiency. "One Geely" strategy drives growth Behind the performance growth is the continued deepening of Geely Auto's "One Geely" strategy. By integrating resources across its Geely, Lynk & Co, Zeekr, and Livan brands, the strategy achieves synergies in R&D, procurement, manufacturing, and channel operations, reducing overall operating costs while enhancing each brand's competitiveness in its respective market segment. Premiumization is the key factor driving the increase in revenue per vehicle. The 16% year-on-year rise to 112,000 yuan signals that Geely Auto's product mix is steadily migrating toward the mid-to-high end. Sales contributions from premium new energy brands such as Zeekr, along with Lynk & Co's solid position in the internal combustion and hybrid markets, have jointly underpinned this trend. On globalization, the company explicitly stated it will accelerate overseas expansion in the second half. Despite a complex external environment—including the EU's additional tariffs on Chinese electric vehicles and rising trade barriers in certain emerging markets—Geely Auto continues to view overseas markets as a key source of incremental growth, indicating that its earlier investments in channel networks, localized operations, and brand recognition have entered a harvest phase. Full-domain AI becomes the core narrative for the next phase In terms of strategic positioning, Geely Auto's interim report explicitly sets forth "leader in full-domain AI intelligent vehicles" as its vision, an upgrade in positioning that merits attention. Unlike the industry's previous emphasis on individual capabilities such as "intelligent cockpit" and "intelligent driving," "full-domain AI" points to end-to-end intelligent transformation spanning R&D and design, manufacturing, supply chain management, user operations, and after-sales service. This means AI's role within Geely's system is evolving from a product-feature level to the foundational architecture of the company's core competitiveness. From an industry competitive landscape perspective, new energy vehicle penetration in China entered a high-level plateau phase by 2026, and the incremental dividend from electrification alone is diminishing. The next phase of competition will increasingly focus on intelligent capabilities and system efficiency. Geely Auto's adoption of full-domain AI as its core engine is essentially an early strategic move to position itself for the post-electrification era. For investors, the signal from this interim report is relatively clear: Geely Auto is expanding revenue scale while simultaneously improving profit quality. The rise in revenue per vehicle validates the effectiveness of its brand-upward strategy, while the strategic positioning around full-domain AI provides a new narrative framework for medium-to-long-term growth.
