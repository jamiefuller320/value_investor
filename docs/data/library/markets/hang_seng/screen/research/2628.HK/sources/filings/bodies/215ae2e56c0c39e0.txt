1. QUARTERLY BUSINESS OVERVIEW 1
In the first three quarters of 2026, the Company upheld the theme of high-quality development, focused on 
long-term value creation and operating performance enhancement, and actively engaged in the “Five Priorities” 
of finance. Adhering to the customer-centric approach, it continued to deepen asset-liability interaction and 
intensified cross-channel operating synergies. As a result, in the first three quarters, the Company’s business 
advanced steadily and market-leading position remained solidified with ongoing improvement, manifesting 
its development resilience and consolidating long-term competitive advantages.
Major Business Highlights:
• Gross investment yield 2 was 3.23%, remaining at the forefront of the industry.
• The value of new business led the industry and achieved a relatively rapid growth of 13.1% on the basis 
of the corresponding period of last year.
• Net profit adjusted for short-term fluctuations in open market equity exposures 3 totalled RMB94,220 
million, representing a relatively rapid increase of 15.8% year on year.
• Gross written premiums, first-year regular premiums and the scale of the sales force continued to rank 
first in the industry.
• Both the scale of first-year regular premiums with a payment duration of ten years or longer and their 
proportion in first-year regular premiums maintained industry-leading positions, continuously demonstrating 
the Company’s development resilience.
• Solvency adequacy remained adequate and robust, with the core and comprehensive solvency ratios 
reaching 156.29% and 197.56%, respectively.
• In the integrated risk rating for insurance companies, the Company has maintained a Class A rating for 
33 consecutive quarters.
• The Company’s MSCI ESG rating was upgraded to AAA, ranking among the global leaders in sustainable 
management.

The insurance business achieved steady growth, with the value of new business leading the industry.
In the first three quarters of 2026, the Company recorded gross written premiums of RMB676,144 million, 
representing a year-on-year increase of 1.0%. In particular, first-year regular premiums totalled RMB116,751 
million, continuing to rank first in the industry and reflecting a year-on-year increase of 6.2%. Both the 
scale of first-year regular premiums with a payment duration of ten years or longer and their proportion 
in first-year regular premiums maintained industry-leading positions. The renewal premiums amounted to 
RMB459,476 million, with a year-on-year increase of 1.7%. The Company further deepened its development 
strategy of diversified products and businesses, with premiums from new policies from life insurance 4, 
annuity insurance and health insurance accounting for 22.74%, 41.30% and 32.20%, respectively. Through 
scientific management of liability costs and ongoing efficiency improvements of intensive utilisation of 
resources, the guaranteed rates for both new business liabilities and in-force business liabilities declined. 
In the first three quarters of 2026, the value of new business achieved a relatively rapid growth of 13.1% 
on the basis of the corresponding period of last year. The surrender rate 5 was 0.66%, a decrease of 0.08 
percentage point year on year.
The Company continued to deepen sales system reforms, with its sales force advancing in both scale 
and quality.
Adhering to the overall principle of improving quality while stabilising quantity and aiming at “optimising new 
recruits and strengthening existing team”, the Company further enhanced the professionalisation, specialisation 
and rejuvenation of the sales force. As at the end of the Reporting Period, the number of its total sales force 
was 665,000, of which the number of agents of the individual agent channel was 616,000, continuing to 
maintain the largest sales force in the industry. The Company insisted on differentiated development and 
professionalisation of its sales force, and strengthened multi-dimensional recruitment of high-quality agents 
and phased agent development. As a result, the quality and efficiency of team building continued to improve, 
with both high-performance agents and high-quality new recruits increasing significantly year on year.

The Company was committed to its investment strategy, with its gross investment yield remaining at 
the forefront of the industry.
In the first three quarters of 2026, bond market interest rates continued to decline, while the stock market 
saw heightened volatility. Upholding the philosophy of long-term investment, value investment and prudent 
investment, the Company deepened asset-liability interaction, continuously optimised allocations across 
major asset classes in light of market conditions, maintained its strategic focus on cross-cycle investment, 
and consistently enhanced its capabilities to respond to market changes. For fixed-income investments, the 
Company strengthened allocation timing and active management, and further consolidated the allocation 
of core holdings, solidifying the foundation for investment returns. For equity investments, the Company 
deepened its diversified strategies and long-term deployment, and continuously optimised its portfolio 
structure, enhancing the resilience of investment returns. In the first three quarters of 2026, the Company’s 
gross investment income 6 reached RMB190,177 million, with a gross investment yield of 3.23%, remaining 
at the forefront of the industry.
The Company’s development resilience continued to manifest, and long-term competitive advantages 
were further consolidated.
As at the end of the Reporting Period, the Company’s total assets and investment assets reached RMB8,152,647 
million and RMB7,995,332 million, respectively, reflecting increases of 7.4% and 7.7% from the end of 
2025, respectively. Net assets amounted to RMB665,880 million, representing an increase of 9.4% from 
the end of 2025. Affected by factors including short-term fluctuations of the capital market during the 
Reporting Period, net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company in the first three quarters of 2026 
was RMB89,430 million. Net profit adjusted for short-term fluctuations in open market equity exposures 
totalled RMB94,220 million, representing a relatively rapid increase of 15.8% year on year. As at the end 
of the Reporting Period, the Company’s solvency adequacy remained adequate and robust, with the core 
and comprehensive solvency ratios reaching 156.29% and 197.56%, respectively, demonstrating its strong 
endogenous capital generation capability.
The Company will adhere to the customer-centric approach, earnestly implement the core high-quality 
development philosophy of “three consistencies”, “three enhancements” and “three breakthroughs”, continue 
to deepen asset-liability interaction, steadily advance the buildup of its healthcare and senior-care ecosystem, 
push forward digital-intelligent transformation and strengthen risk prevention and control, with an aim to 
drive its comprehensive strength to a new stage with new strides in reform and development, and strive to 
achieve a strong start to the “15th Five-Year Plan” period.
