Key Elements CNOOC CNOOC Limited 600938.SS Brazil's Buzios Oilfield Guyana's Stabroek Block Bohai Sea Guolian Minsheng Securities CNOOC Limited (600938.SS, hereinafter referred to as "CNOOC") released its 2025 annual results report on March 26. Despite challenges from declining international oil prices, the company achieved annual operating revenue of CNY 398.2 billion (approximately $57.6 billion) and net profit attributable to equity holders of CNY 122.1 billion (approximately $17.7 billion), driven by robust growth in oil and gas production and excellent cost management capabilities. Notably, the company's net oil and gas production reached 777 million barrels of oil equivalent (BOE), a year-on-year increase of 7%, setting a new historical record. According to the annual report data, the company's average realized oil price in 2025 was $66.47 per barrel, a decrease of approximately 13.4% year-on-year, largely in line with the trend of international oil prices. The decline in oil prices was the primary reason for the year-on-year decrease in the company's revenue and net profit. However, the company effectively offset some of the negative impacts through increased production and stringent cost control. The all-in cost for the full year was $27.9 per BOE, down 2.2% year-on-year, further solidifying its competitive cost advantage. Production Structure: Strong Domestic Growth, Steady Overseas Progress In terms of production composition, China's offshore areas remain the company's core production region, contributing 64.5% of net proved reserves and 69.1% of net production. In 2025, domestic oil and gas net production reached 537 million BOE, a year-on-year increase of 9.0%, with natural gas production showing particularly significant growth at 16.7%. For overseas operations, oil and gas net production was 240 million BOE, up 2.7% year-on-year. Projects in South America, such as the Buzios oilfield in Brazil and the Stabroek block in Guyana, have become important engines for overseas production growth. Significant Exploration and Development Achievements, Promising Future Growth In exploration and development, CNOOC achieved multiple breakthroughs in 2025. The company made 6 new discoveries and successfully appraised 28 oil and gas structures throughout the year, particularly achieving another hundred-million-ton oilfield discovery in the shallow layers of the slope zone in the Bohai Sea. Concurrently, 16 new projects commenced production successfully, including the Bozhong 26-6 oilfield, the Kenli 10-2 oilfield cluster, as well as overseas projects like Phase 7 of Brazil's Buzios and Guyana's Yellowtail. The commencement of these projects has laid a solid foundation for sustained future production growth. The company has set a production target for 2026 of 780 to 800 million BOE, representing an increase of 0.3% to 2.9% compared to 2025. In a subsequent research report, Guolian Minsheng Securities pointed out that the overseas Guyana Stabroek block is expected to have 8 phases in operation by 2030, and Brazil's Buzios oilfield is expected to have 11 phases in operation by 2027, indicating clear medium-to-long-term growth potential for the company's production. Focus on Shareholder Returns, Maintaining High Dividend Policy CNOOC continued its tradition of emphasizing shareholder returns. The board of directors proposed a final dividend for 2025 of HKD 0.55 per share (tax inclusive). Combined with the interim dividend of HKD 0.73 per share already paid, the total annual dividend amounts to HKD 1.28 per share. Calculated at the exchange rate on December 31, 2025, the total annual dividend payout ratio was approximately 45% of the net profit attributable to equity holders. In its commentary report, Guolian Minsheng Securities calculated that based on the closing price on March 26, 2026, the dividend yield for CNOOC's A-shares and H-shares is approximately 2.8% and 5.1%, respectively. The report believes the company possesses characteristics such as significant potential for reserve and production growth, strong cost control capabilities, and a high willingness to pay dividends. Against the backdrop of a high oil price environment, its high-dividend attribute is attractive to investors. Market View: A Low-Valuation Leader with Oil Price Sensitivity Regarding the company's future prospects, Guolian Minsheng Securities gave a positive assessment. The report noted that as China's State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission (SASAC) incorporates listed companies' value realization and market value management into relevant assessment systems, the undervalued valuations of central state-owned enterprises are expected to see a rational recovery. The institution forecasts CNOOC's net profit attributable to equity holders for 2026 to 2028 to reach CNY 163.56 billion (approximately $23.7 billion), CNY 157.7 billion (approximately $22.8 billion), and CNY 161.35 billion (approximately $23.3 billion), respectively, and maintains a "Buy" rating. The report also highlighted related risks, including potential underperformance in oil and gas exploration and construction, impacts of international geopolitical changes on crude oil prices, slower-than-expected macroeconomic growth, and risks associated with changes in energy policies. Overall, CNOOC demonstrated its operational resilience as a leading enterprise in China's oil and gas industry in 2025. In a volatile oil price market environment, the company has provided investors with relatively stable return expectations by continuously increasing production, strictly controlling costs, and adhering to a high dividend payout ratio. Going forward, the sequential commissioning of new domestic and international projects will be a key driver for the company's sustained growth.
