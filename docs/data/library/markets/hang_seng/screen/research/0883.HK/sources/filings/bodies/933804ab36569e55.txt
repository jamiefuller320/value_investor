III.  BUSINESS REVIEW
Other important information on the Company’s operation during the reporting period that needs to be brought to 
the attention of investors
✓ Applicable □ Not applicable
In the first three quarters of 2026, the global economy was impacted by both geopolitical shocks and emerging 
industry drivers, with overall growth moderately slowing and inflation trending upward. International oil prices rose 
rapidly and then fluctuated within a wide range at high levels, with the average price of Brent crude oil futures at 
US$88.86 per barrel, representing an increase of 27.1% year-on-year (“YoY”). The Company achieved steady growth 
in net oil and gas production, continued to strengthen the cost control, and reached a new record high in profitability.
In the first three quarters of 2026, the Company’s net oil and gas production reached 593.7 million BOE, with a 
growth of 2.7% YoY. Net production in China reached 408.5 million BOE, with an increase of 1.9% YoY, mainly 
benefiting from the production contribution of oil and gas fields such as Kenli 10-2; overseas net production was 
185.1 million BOE, with an increase of 4.3% YoY, mainly benefiting from the production contributions of projects 
in Guyana and Brazil. In the third quarter, the Company’s net oil and gas production reached 194.9 million BOE, 
with an increase of 0.6% YoY.
In the first three quarters of 2026, the Company made five new discoveries and successfully appraised 23 oil and 
gas bearing structures. In China, the successful appraisal of Bozhong 8-3 South and Huizhou 21-8 further expanded 
the reserve base, with the two fields having the potential to become large and medium-sized oil and gas fields; the 
successful appraisal of Caofeidian 22-3 demonstrated promising exploration prospects in the Paleogene lithologic 
play of the Chengbei lower uplift. Overseas, the Company orderly advanced seismic acquisition across its newly 
acquired exploration blocks in Iraq and Kazakhstan. In the third quarter, the Company made one new discovery and 
successfully appraised seven oil and gas bearing structures.
- 8 -
In terms of development and production, 9 new projects commenced production in the first three quarters of 2026. 
In the third quarter, 4 new projects including Bozhong 26-6 Oilfield Development Project (Phase Π) and Panyu 10-4 
Oilfield Development Project have been put into production, and other new projects progressed smoothly.
The oil and gas sales revenue of the Company reached approximately RMB305.35 billion for the first three quarters 
of 2026, representing an increase of 19.5% YoY, mainly due to higher oil prices. Net profit attributable to equity 
shareholders of the Company amounted to RMB119.12 billion, representing an increase of 16.8% YoY. In the first 
three quarters, the Company’s average realised oil price increased by 26.4% YoY to US$86.32 per barrel, and the 
average realised gas price was US$8.13 per thousand cubic feet, representing an increase of 3.4% YoY. All-in cost 
in the first three quarters was US$30.57 per BOE, continuing to maintain cost competitiveness.
In the first three quarters of 2026, the Company’s capital expenditures amounted to approximately RMB89.9 billion, 
increasing by 4.5% YoY as a result of increased workloads of adjustment wells. During the reporting period, the 
Company remained stable and controllable in safety, and environmental protection.
