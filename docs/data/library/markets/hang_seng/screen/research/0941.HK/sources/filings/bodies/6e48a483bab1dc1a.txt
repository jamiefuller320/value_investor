RESULTS FOR THE FIRST THREE QUARTERS OF 2026
The unaudited results and performance indicators of the Group for the first three 
quarters of 2026:
• Operating revenue was RMB780.3 billion, down by 1.8% year-on-year
• Profit attributable to equity shareholders was RMB106.2 billion, down by 7.9% year-
on-year
• Mobile customers totaled 1,014 million, with a net addition of 8.89 million
• Integrated broadband network customers totaled 342 million, with a net addition of 
13.05 million

– 3 –
In the first three  quarters of 2026, the Group continued to  advance its  “network-based full-
stack innovation” strategy, channeling its efforts into strengthening, optimizing and expanding 
communications services, computing services and AI services. It achieved generally stable 
operating results.
In the first three quarters of 2026, the Group’s operating revenue decreased by 1.8% year-on-
year to RM B780.3 billion. Of which,  revenue from principal businesses decreased  by 3.7% 
year-on-year to RMB657.7 billion. Revenue from other businesses increased by 9.9% year-on-
year to RMB122.6 billion.
Profit attributable to equity shareholders decreased by 7.9% year-on-year to RMB106.2 billion 
in the first three quarters. Margin of profit attributable to equity shareholders was 13.6%. 
EBITDA decreased by 6.7% year-on-year to RMB247.5 billion, with EBITDA as a percentage 
of principal business revenue standing at 37.6%.
The Group will continue to accelerate its transformation from a communications operator 
into a technology services enterprise. It will advance the integrated deployment of 
communications, computing and AI networks, and strengthen integrated innovation across 
communications, computing and AI services. It will also reinforce its strategic positioning 
in AI, accelerate expansion in the computing market, and consolidate the foundation of 
communications services. By making every effort to improve quality and efficiency and 
continuously enhancing its core competitiveness, the Group will build a leading technology 
services enterprise to high standards,  thereby creating greater value for its shareholders and 
customers.
