## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Hang Lung Properties Limited (0101.HK) screens as a buy on deep asset discount and cash-return metrics: P/B ~0.27, FCF yield ~9.7%, and dividend yield ~7.1%, with seven of 22 quantitative models passing across cheapness, dividend, GARP, and risk families. The central debate is whether the market is correctly pricing prolonged mainland retail weakness and higher financing costs, or over-discounting a stabilising asset base with improving free cash flow. Primary regulatory filings were not retrieved in this research pack, so conviction rests on secondary data and headline news rather than audited disclosure.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Hang Lung offers a classic asset-backed deep-value setup rather than an earnings-growth story. The quantitative screen passes Schloss Low P/B, FCF Yield, High Dividend Yield, Dividend Growth, Neff PEGY, Composite Value, and Earnings Quality, with composite score 68% (sector-relative 64%) and full data quality (20/20 metrics). At P/B 0.27 against shareholders' equity of roughly HK$134.7bn (Yahoo, FY2025), the market implies a substantial haircut to investment-property values that may exceed fundamental impairment if occupancy and rental reversion stabilise. FCF of HK$5.6bn in FY2025 (Yahoo) against a market cap implied by these metrics supports a ~9.7% FCF yield, suggesting the business can service debt and return capital even while reported net income compresses. Dividend yield of 7.1% remains screen-qualifying, though payouts have been cut from prior peaks. Strategically, management is executing its "V.3" mainland expansion (Westlake 66 Hangzhou opened 2026, Plaza 66 footprint expansion in Shanghai, Wuxi retail addition), which aligns with a long-duration value thesis: buy distressed asset value, wait for portfolio maturation and rental recovery. The screen fails quality and financial-health models (Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, Piotroski F-Score, Financial Health), which is consistent with depressed ROE (1.6%) and earnings volatility — acceptable trade-offs for a Schloss-style buyer, not for a quality compounder.
