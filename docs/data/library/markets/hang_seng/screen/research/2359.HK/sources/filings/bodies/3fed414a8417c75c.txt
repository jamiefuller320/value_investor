I. Company Profile
WuXi AppTec is a trusted partner and contributor to the pharmaceutical and life 
sciences industries, providing R&D and manufacturing services that help advance 
healthcare innovation. With operations across Asia, Europe, and North America, we 
offer integrated, end-to-end services through our unique CRDMO platform. We are 
privileged to work alongside partners across 30+ countries, supporting their efforts to 
bring breakthrough treatments to patients. Guided by our vision that every drug can be 
made and every disease can be treated, we are committed to advancing breakthroughs 
for patients — one collaboration at a time.

In the first three quarters of 2026, total revenue reached RMB45.54 billion, up 38.6% 
YoY. Revenue from continuing operations increased 46.3% YoY. Backlog for 
continuing operations reached RMB76.74 billion as of September 30, 2026, up 36.4% 
YoY.
(1) WuXi Chemistry
Revenue of WuXi Chemistry reached RMB39.36 billion, up 51.5% YoY, mainly 
driven by steady pipeline progression to late stages, and the sequential ramp-up 
of new capacity throughout last year. During the first three quarters of 2026:
— CRDMO business model drives continuous growth. We are accelerating 
proactive capacity planning, and initiated the new Changzhou site ahead of 
schedule to better meet growing customer demand.
— Small molecule drug discovery (“R ”, Research) continues to generate 
downstream opportunities. In the past 12 months, the Company successfully 
synthesized and delivered more than 450,000 new compounds. 314 
molecules were converted from R to D phase during the first three quarters 
of 2026.
— Small molecule development and manufacturing (“D” & “M”, Development 
and Manufacturing) service maintains strong momentum.
i. The small molecule CDMO pipeline continued to expand. Driven 
by ‘follow the molecule’ and ‘win the molecule’, small molecule 
CRDMO pipeline efficiently converts and captures high-quality 
molecules, delivering sustained growth. 1,089 new molecules were 
newly added in the first three quarters of 2026. As of September 30, 
2026, our pipeline reached 3,863 molecules, including 103 commercial 
projects, 94 in phase III, 402 in phase II and 3,264 in phase I and 
pre-clinical stages. Commercial and phase III projects increased by 23 
during the first three quarters of 2026.
ii. As our business model continuously attracts high-quality molecules, 
and the pipeline steadily advances to late stages, aligned with proactive 
capacity planning and strong execution, revenue of small molecule 
D&M rose 65.6% YoY to RMB23.58 billion.

quarters of 2026:

(2) WuXi Testing
Revenue of WuXi Testing increased 32.0% YoY to RMB3.91 billion. During the 
first three quarters of 2026:
— Revenue of drug safety evaluation services grew 40.6% YoY, maintaining 
its industry-leading position in the Asia-Pacific region.
— The Company is enabling customers’ global partnerships to accelerate 
innovation. New modality business continued its strong momentum, 
contributing more than 35% of WuXi Testing revenue, while maintaining its 
leading position in areas including nucleic acids, conjugates, multispecific 
antibodies and peptides.
— DMPK continues to enhance its science and service capabilities, and 
accelerate execution of “HI + AI” strategy through integration and

(OECD) GLP periodic inspections, respectively. In addition, Suzhou facility 
successfully completed its first South Korea Ministry of Food and Drug 
Safety (MFDS) and three consecutive Japan Pharmaceuticals and Medical

(3) WuXi Biology
Revenue of WuXi Biology reached RMB2.19 billion, up 12.4% YoY. We enable 
global customers through integrated in vitro & in vivo drug discovery capabilities, 
cross-regional collaboration and end-to-end solutions in emerging areas. During 
the first three quarters of 2026:
— WuXi Biology follows the science and strategically builds differentiated 
drug discovery capabilities in emerging areas, actively developing its global

model by continuously contributing more than 20% of the Company’s new 
customers.

use cases in pharmacology, pathology, and beyond. In response to the needs 
of AIDD/AI4S customers, we provide high-throughput, diverse, customized,

revenue, with rapid new customer growth in areas including nucleic acids, 
antibody conjugates and peptides.

management and operations, continue to enhance production and operational 
efficiency, and strengthen resilience to navigate complex and dynamic market 
conditions.

IV. Non-IFRS Measures
To supplement our condensed consolidated financial statements presented in 
accordance with IFRS, we use adjusted non-IFRS gross profit, adjusted non-IFRS net 
profit attributable to owners of the Company and adjusted free cash flow (collectively 
the “adjusted non-IFRS measures”) as additional financial measures.
The adjusted non-IFRS measures are not an alternative to (i) profit before income 
tax or profit for the period (as determined in accordance with IFRS) as a measure of 
our operating performance, (ii) cash flows from operating, investing and financing 
activities as a measure of our ability to meet our cash needs, or (iii) any other measures 
of performance or liquidity.
We believe that the adjusted non-IFRS measures are useful for understanding and 
assessing our core business performance and operating trends, and we believe that 
management and investors may benefit from referring to these measures in assessing 
our financial performance by eliminating the impact of certain unusual, non-recurring, 
non-cash and/or non-operating items that we do not consider indicative of the 
performance of our core business.
The management of the Company believes such adjusted non-IFRS measures are 
widely accepted and adopted in the industry in which the Company operates. However, 
you should not view adjusted results on a stand-alone basis or as a substitute for 
results under IFRS, or as being comparable to results reported or forecasted by other 
companies.

Description of differences in accounting standards:
The differences in accounting standards primarily arise from the different accounting 
treatments applied to changes in the carrying amount of long-term equity investments 
resulting from the passive dilution of the Company’s equity interest in an associate. 
In 2023, the Company’s equity interest in its associate, WuXi XDC Cayman Inc., was 
passively diluted following the completion of the associate’s public offering. In 2025, 
the Company’s equity interest in the same associate was further passively diluted as a 
result of the associate’s issuance of additional shares. Under CAS, when an investee’s 
equity changes other than net profit or loss, other comprehensive income, and profit 
distributions, the investor shall adjust the carrying amount of its long-term equity 
investment, with the corresponding amount recognized in capital reserves. Under 
IFRS, however, the impact arising from such passive dilution of a long-term equity 
investment in an associate is recognized in profit or loss for the current period.
During the first three quarters of 2025 and 2026, the Company disposed of part of its 
equity interest in the associate, resulting in a decrease in its ownership percentage. 
Under CAS, the related passive dilution impact previously recognized in capital reserves 
is reclassified to investment income based on the proportion disposed of. Accordingly, 
differences in accounting standards arose.
