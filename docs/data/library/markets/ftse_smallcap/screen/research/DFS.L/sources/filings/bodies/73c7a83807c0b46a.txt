BETA Close X DFS Furniture PLC announced on October 21, 2025, the grant of conditional share awards to a person discharging managerial responsibility under the Deferred Bonus Plan. Tim Stacey, an Executive Director, received 93,942 conditional shares with a nominal value of £0.10. These awards, structured as options with a nil exercise price, are equivalent to 25% of the 2025 short-term incentive award. The Deferred Awards will become exercisable around the second anniversary of the grant date and will accrue Dividend Equivalents between the award and vesting date.
