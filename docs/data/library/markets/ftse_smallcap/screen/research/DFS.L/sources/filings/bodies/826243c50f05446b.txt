The awards granted under the Plan are structured as options with a nil exercise price over a number of shares (the "Deferred Awards"). No consideration was paid for the grant of the Deferred Awards. The Deferred Awards will become exercisable on or around the second anniversary of the date of grant and under the rules of the Plan the award will accrue Dividend Equivalents between the award date and the vesting date.
