On 17 September 2026, the boards of DNO, Bidco and Capricorn made an announcement pursuant to Rule 2.7 of the Code setting out the terms of a revised cash offer by Bidco for the entire issued and to be issued share capital of Capricorn, to be implemented by way of a court-sanctioned scheme of arrangement under Part 26 of the Companies Act 2006 (the “ DNO Scheme ”). The DNO Scheme was conditional, amongst other things, on the Court Meeting and the General Meeting being held on or before the 22 nd day after 16 October 2026, being the originally expected date of such Meetings, or such later date as (i) may be agreed between Bidco and Capricorn or (ii) (in a competitive situation) may be specified by Bidco with the consent of the Panel, and in each case that (if so required) the Court may allow (the “ Mini Long Stop Date ”). The Mini Long Stop Date was therefore 7 November 2026. Since conditions 2.1(b) and 2.2(b), as set out in the scheme document in relation to the DNO Scheme published on 21 September 2026 (the “ Scheme Document ”), were not satisfied on or before 7 November 2026, Bidco has invoked the Mini Long Stop Date condition of the DNO Scheme and, as such, the DNO Scheme has lapsed, with effect from this announcement.
