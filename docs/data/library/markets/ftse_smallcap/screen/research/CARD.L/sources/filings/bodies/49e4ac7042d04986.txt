BETA Close X Card Factory plc announced the results of its Annual General Meeting, where all resolutions were voted on by shareholders. While the Annual Report and Accounts, dividend, auditor re-appointment, and share allotment authorities received strong support with over 99% of votes cast in favour, resolutions concerning the re-election of directors Paul Moody and Darcy Willson-Rymer received approximately 74% and 75% support respectively, and special resolutions to disapply pre-emption rights received approximately 74% and 70% support. The Board acknowledges these outcomes, particularly the less than 80% support for ordinary resolutions 3, 4, and 5, and the failure of special resolutions 13 and 14, indicating diverse shareholder views on board diversity, sustainability governance, and strategic priorities, and will engage further with shareholders on these matters.
