The Group manages assets on behalf of sophisticated institutional and wealth management clients worldwide across a diverse range of asset classes and strategies. CLIG provides a trading update for its quarter ended 30 September 2026 below. The numbers that follow are unaudited. Flows & Performance Group Funds under Management (FuM) at 30 September 2026 were $11.5 billion. The Group continues to benefit from a diversified product offering, strong long-term investment capabilities, and ongoing client engagement across both equity and fixed income strategies. Sentiment for our asset classes was muted for the quarter, however, investment performance remained strong over the long term. Net outflows were primarily driven by client rebalancing and portfolio allocation decisions across the City of London Investment Management (CLIM) business following periods of strong relative and absolute investment performance. These withdrawals were concentrated in Emerging Markets, International Equity and Other. Investment performance and client outcomes remain our main priorities. Over the past six months, we have enhanced collaboration across the Group and connected our investment and client-facing teams more closely. We have increased engagement with clients, consultants and industry stakeholders to better understand their evolving needs and where our expertise can add the most value. During the quarter, the Group recorded gross inflows of $87 million and outflows of $754 million, resulting in net outflows of $667 million. Market and investment performance reduced FuM by a further $127 million, reflecting volatile market conditions during the period. CLIM recorded gross inflows of $47 million during the quarter, led by International Equity and Emerging Markets, partially offsetting outflows of $650 million. As a result, CLIM FuM declined from $8.1 billion to $7.4 billion at quarter end. Karpus Investment Management (KIM) generated gross inflows of $40 million, supported by Taxable Fixed Income, Growth Balanced and Tax-Sensitive Fixed Income. Net outflows of $64 million were primarily attributable to client asset allocation decisions within Growth Balanced and Conservative Balanced strategies. Combined with modest negative market performance, KIM FuM declined from $4.3 billion to $4.1 billion. Despite volatile market conditions, the group believes that it retains a strong investment and financial position. Our success continues to be built on investment outcomes for clients, delivered by long-tenured investment teams applying disciplined, repeatable processes across market cycles. We are also focused on strengthening market recognition of our investment capabilities across KIM (fixed income) and CLIM (equities). To build on this, we have instituted sales and marketing initiatives, including expanded business development and consultant relations coverage, a more active thought-leadership programme, and refreshed marketing that communicates our investment insights more clearly. Together, these efforts are designed to increase awareness of our capabilities and create a more coordinated client experience, while preserving the distinct strengths and identities of each business. With a strong financial foundation, experienced investment teams and a clear strategic direction, we believe that we are well positioned to continue to deepen existing relationships, further broaden our reach with prospective clients, and support the long-term growth of the Group.
