PDMR Dealing City of London Investment Group PLC 29 October 2024 29 October 2024 CITY OF LONDON INVESTMENT GROUP PLC ("City of London", "CLIG", "the Group" or "the Company") Notification and public disclosure of transactions by persons discharging managerial responsibilities and persons closely associated with them. Deepranjan Agrawal, a person discharging managerial responsibilities ("PDMR") of City of London Investment Group PLC (LSE: CLIG), has been awarded 14,888 Restricted Share Awards (the "Award") comprising 14,888 ordinary shares of £0.01 each in the Company ("Ordinary Shares") from the Employee Benefit Trust (the "EBT") under the Group's Employee Incentive Plan (the "EIP"). Of these, 7,444 Ordinary Shares relate to an employee waived bonus ("Deferred Shares"), with the remaining 7,444 Ordinary Shares representing matching awards made by the Company ("Bonus Shares") under the terms of the Plan. The cost to Mr. Agrawal was £3.735 per Deferred Share and nil cost per Bonus Share. The Deferred Shares and Bonus Shares awarded under the EIP carry certain restrictions which will lift on vesting of the Award. For further details of the EIP scheme please refer to CLIG's latest annual report and accounts at https://clig.com/investor-reports/#results-centre-on . Following the grant of the Award, Mr. Agrawal's beneficial interest is 112,389 Ordinary Shares, representing approximately 0.2% of the Company's issued share capital.
