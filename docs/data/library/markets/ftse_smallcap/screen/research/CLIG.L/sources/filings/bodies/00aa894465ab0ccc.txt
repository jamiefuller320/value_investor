Holding(s) in Company City of London Investment Group PLC 13 June 2025 13 June 2025 CITY OF LONDON INVESTMENT GROUP PLC ("City of London", "the Company") Holding(s) in Company City of London (LSE: CLIG), a leading specialist asset management group offering a range of institutional and retail products investing primarily in closed-end funds, announces that the Company's Employee Benefit Trust purchased 400,000 ordinary shares of 1p each on 11 June 2025 at a price of £3.40 per share. Following these purchases, the Trust holds 1,750,055 ordinary shares in the Company representing 3.5% of the Company's issued capital, of which 854,550 (1.7% of the Company's issued capital) is held in custody for employees under the terms of the Employee Incentive Plan (EIP).
