## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
City of London Investment Group Plc (CLIG.L) is a UK-listed specialist asset manager focused on closed-end fund (CEF) investing. The quantitative screen rates the name a **buy** (11/22 models; composite 55%), anchored on moderate valuation (P/E 14.9, P/B 2.1), a 6.9% dividend yield, 14% ROE, minimal leverage (D/E ~3%), and strong quality markers (Piotroski 8/9, FCF yield pass). The investment hook is a capital-light, cash-generative fee business trading below many peers on earnings, with a shareholder-friendly payout policy. The key debate is whether recent AUM recovery (pre-close update: funds under management up 13.9% to $12.3bn, outflows slowing) can sustain earnings and a dividend that, on Yahoo data, has exceeded reported net income in the latest year. **Critical gap:** no UK RNS or statutory filing bodies were retrieved (`filings_index.json` is empty), so pension, covenant, and going-concern language could not be verified from primary sources.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, CLIG offers a rare combination of **cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk** families all passing on the screen—five of five metric families, with high data completeness (20/20). The business model is structurally attractive: fee revenue on externally managed CEF assets, limited capex (£0.1m in FY2025 per Yahoo), and recurring operating cash flow (£25.2m) that comfortably exceeds maintenance needs.

Screen highlights reinforce the case:
- **Graham Enterprising, FCF Yield, Acquirer’s Multiple:** earnings and cash generation at a reasonable price.
- **Quality Value, Economic Moat, Earnings Quality, Financial Health:** balance-sheet strength (cash £35.5m; equity £152.5m) and consistent profitability recovery in FY2025.
- **Dividend Growth, Magic Formula, Neff PEGY:** income plus modest growth at P/E 14.9—not deep value, but enterprising value with a yield near 7%.

Business quality is supported by niche CEF expertise (long track record; affiliated US entity CLIM holds significant CEF stakes, e.g. 11.1% of Morgan Stanley India Fund per July 2026 news). Management alignment is modestly positive: CEO Cooper Abbott purchased 5,000 shares at £3.979 (7 April 2026). The July 2026 pre-close statement points to improving operating momentum—AUM growth and slower redemptions—while management **maintained its dividend outlook**, which aligns with the screen’s dividend-family pass.

The screen signal is **new** (one week, conviction 35%, trend improving), so this is an early-stage buy rather than a long-established signal. Failed models (Graham Defensive, Deep Value, Buffett Quality, High Dividend Yield) suggest the name is not a net-net or ultra-quality compounder; it is better framed as a **yield-plus-recovery** play on AUM and fee income normalisation.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Filing verification gap (material):** With no RNS or statutory body text, going-concern assessments, pension obligations, lease covenants, related-party transactions, and contingent liabilities **cannot** be reviewed. The gap-fill map flags Companies House accounts and Investegate full-text re-pull as priority follow-ups.

**AUM and market cyclicality:** Revenue fell from FY2022 to FY2023–FY2024 as markets and flows weakened; recovery in FY2025 and the July 2026 AUM update may partly reflect market appreciation rather than durable net inflows. Fee income remains exposed to CEF discounts, global equity volatility, and EM/frontier sentiment (CLIG’s historical focus areas).

**Dividend sustainability:** Yahoo shows cash dividends at or above net income in recent years (106% of NI in FY2025). FCF covers the dividend, but without filings the board’s dividend policy, distributable reserves, and any capital requirements for seed investments remain unverified.

**Intangible-heavy balance sheet:** Goodwill (£90.1m) and other intangibles (£27.2m) dominate; tangible book is £35.2m. Impairment risk is unassessable without annual-report note disclosure.

**Leverage trend:** Screen leverage is low, but capital-lease obligations rose 47% year-on-year (Yahoo), contributing to the sole Piotroski miss—worth monitoring in the next accounts.

**Governance / concentration:** Specialist CEF manager with affiliated US operations; related-party and seed-capital policies not visible in this pack. No adverse regulatory actions appeared in the news manifest.

**Competitive:** CEF specialist niche is defensible but scale-limited; larger multi-asset managers and passive vehicles compete for institutional and retail allocations.

**Macro (colour only):** UK small-cap and global EM/frontier exposure add sentiment sensitivity; not used to override the screen signal.
