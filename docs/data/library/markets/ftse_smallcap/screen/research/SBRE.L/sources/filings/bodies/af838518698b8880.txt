Our continuing focus on "profit as the target and volume as an outcome" underpins our confidence in delivering a strong full-year result, which we expect will be slightly ahead of 2025. We have maintained pricing discipline, strong premium volumes and a robust solvency position despite continuing weak market conditions. We will continue to apply this discipline and focus as we move into 2027 rather than pursue volume for its own sake. Claims inflation continues to run slightly ahead of historical norms at 6% to 7%, but we do not see market premiums year-to-date keeping pace with this level of cost increase. Sabre is fully covering this through our own pricing, whilst delivering good premium volumes. Our Ambition 2030 plans continue to progress well as we test and roll-out our various initiatives. The combination of our short-term discipline and longer-term growth ambition leave us well positioned to deliver sustainable performance and earnings growth over time."
