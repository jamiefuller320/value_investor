BETA Close X Sabre Insurance Group PLC announced on April 7, 2026, that several directors and persons discharging managerial responsibilities (PDMRs) were granted conditional awards of ordinary shares under the 2017 Deferred Bonus Plan, reflecting bonuses earned for the financial year ending December 31, 2025. Specifically, the Chief Executive Officer received 154,138 shares, the Chief Risk and Governance Officer received 63,845 shares, the Claims Director received 120,238 shares, the Chief Financial Officer received 80,921 shares, and the Chief Actuary received 80,932 shares, all at a price of £1.553 per share. These awards will vest on the second anniversary of the grant date.
