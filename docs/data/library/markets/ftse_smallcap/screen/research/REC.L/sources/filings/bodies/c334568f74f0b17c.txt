Disclaimer* Record PLC 06 November 2026 6 November 2026 RECORD PLC INTERIM RESULTS FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED 30 SEPTEMBER 2026 Strengthening margins while investing for growth Record plc ("Record", "the Company" or "Group"), the specialist asset manager, today announces its unaudited results for the six months ended 30 September 2026 ("H1 FY27"). Financial headlines: · Revenue increased by 19% to £22.8 million (H1 FY26: £19.2 million). · Management fees increased by 6% to £18.6 million (H1 FY26: £17.5 million), with growth across all three product pillars. · Strong performance fees of £2.2 million (H1 FY26: £0.8 million). · AUM increased to a new high of $130.3 billion (FY26: $114.6 billion), including net inflows of $12.7 billion. · Operating margin increased to 32.3% (H1 FY26: 23.4%) in line with higher-margin growth and continued cost discipline. · Profit after tax attributable to Record plc shareholders increased by 49% to £5.6 million (H1 FY26: £3.7 million). · Basic EPS of 2.84 pence (H1 FY26: 1.93 pence). · Interim dividend declared at 1.00 pence per share (H1 FY26: 2.15 pence per share). Key developments: · Additional FX Alpha and Frontier mandate wins, alongside continued demand for Solutions for Asset Managers. · Further commitments secured and two deployments completed by the Record Infrastructure Equity Fund. · Albert Soleiman appointed as Group Chief Financial Officer, strengthening our leadership. · New dividend policy announced, targeting a 40-60% payout range of annual after tax profits. Commenting on the results, Jan Witte, Chief Executive Officer of Record plc, said: "This was a strong first half for the Group, both operationally and financially. We are executing against our strategy and positioning the business for sustainable, long-term growth and an improved quality of earnings. That progress is clear in our results, with record AUM, inflows across all three product pillars and a return to revenue and earnings growth. Our new dividend policy supports our capital allocation framework for the next phase of that strategy, striking the right balance between maintaining balance sheet strength, providing flexibility to invest in growth and delivering sustainable returns to shareholders. Performance across the Group continues to support our expectations for the current financial year." When I became CEO, it was important that our strategy was not a departure from Record's past, but a direct extension of it. Now beginning its third year of execution, our established currency management business continues to provide a resilient foundation and, alongside it, we are building a broader business focused on higher-margin products and long-term recurring revenues. That strategy is now visibly reflected in our results. Assets Under Management ("AUM") reached another new high during the period, supported by new business across all three of our product pillars: Risk Management, Absolute Return and Private Markets. Improving the quality of our earnings has been a key focus of our strategy, and this half shows clear progress. We won new FX Alpha and Frontier mandates, saw continued growth in Solutions for Asset Managers, and made further deployments in the Record Infrastructure Equity Fund. Each of these growth areas earns higher margins than our traditional mandates, and together they are steadily reshaping our revenue. The Group performed well this period, with profits considerably ahead of the same period last year. Performance fees made a strong contribution, but, even excluding performance fees, revenue still grew faster than our costs. This is the operating leverage we have been working towards. As the fragmented market environment continues to evolve, institutional investors are placing more emphasis on diversification, on actively managing their currency exposures and on finding sources of return that are uncorrelated with traditional assets. These are areas where Record's expertise is particularly relevant, and the new business we have won this half shows that clients increasingly look to us for solutions. To capture these opportunities, it is important that our approach to capital allocation supports our strategy. The dividend is a key part of that approach, which we remain committed to. As indicated in the FY26 full-year results announcement, the Board highlighted the need to review the dividend policy, in order to ensure that it is sustainable and aligned with the growth targets of the business. For the last two years, the Group has returned over 90% of earnings through dividends. Over the same period, we have invested significantly in broadening our product range and developing higher-margin solutions. In this context, the dividend level is not sustainable for the long-term, as a business needs to retain sufficient flexibility to be able to invest in new growth opportunities from a strong capital base. Following its review, the Board has introduced a new dividend policy, with a total ordinary payout range of between 40% and 60% of profit after tax attributable to equity holders. This strikes the right balance between maintaining balance sheet strength, having a sensible buffer above our regulatory capital, and retaining the flexibility to invest in the business, all whilst delivering a sustainable dividend to shareholders. We will provide more detail on our capital allocation policy in the full year results announcement. The Board has declared an interim dividend of 1.00 pence per share, and the new dividend policy will be implemented for the full year. The capital we retain will be put to work. Our priorities include broadening our FX Alpha and Solutions for Asset Managers offerings, building out our private credit and private equity strategies, expanding our EM and Frontier capabilities, and investing in the people and technology needed to scale our platform efficiently. As we continue on this trajectory, I am delighted to welcome Albert Soleiman, who joined the Record plc Board as Chief Financial Officer on 28 September 2026. Albert brings significant capital markets and financial services experience from his previous roles, and I look forward to working closely with him as we continue to grow. I would also like to thank Samantha Dunn for her work as Interim Chief Financial Officer during the transition period. The progress we have made in the first half of FY27 reinforces my confidence that we are on the right path. With record AUM, strong momentum across all three product pillars and a strengthened leadership team, Record is well positioned to create sustainable long-term value for our clients and shareholders. Performance across the Group continues to support our expectations for the current financial year.
