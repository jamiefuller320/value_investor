# Macfarlane Group PLC (MACF.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T10:31:36.659357+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Macfarlane Group PLC is a UK protective packaging distributor and manufacturer rated **buy** on the quantitative screen (4/22 models; composite 57%), chiefly on cheapness (P/B 0.9, FCF yield ~15.6%), dividend, GARP, and risk factors. The stock trades below book value with a ~5.3% yield and sustained free cash flow despite a sharp FY2025 earnings collapse. The central debate is whether FY2025’s profit downturn—driven by UK distribution pricing pressure, cost inflation, and disruption from a fatal accident at a recently acquired site—is cyclical/transitory or marks a structural step-down in returns. Primary filing body extracts are unavailable in the research pack; financial analysis relies on Yahoo fallback and news flow, limiting verification of going-concern language, pension disclosures, and interim trends.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, MACF.L offers a classic “cash-rich, earnings-poor” profile: the screen passes FCF Yield, Neff PEGY (0.04), Dividend Growth, and Earnings Quality, spanning all four factor families (cheapness, dividend, GARP, risk). At P/E 16.8 on depressed FY2025 earnings but P/B 0.9 and ROE 5.2%, the market appears to capitalise trough profits while anchoring on balance-sheet value and cash generation.

Business quality is mixed but not broken. Revenue grew to £300.8m in FY2025 (Yahoo fallback), the eleventh consecutive year of top-line expansion per management commentary, reflecting bolt-on M&A and market share gains in UK packaging distribution and manufacturing. Operating cash flow remained robust at £24.8m and free cash flow at £20.1m (Yahoo), supporting dividends (~£5.8m paid) and ongoing share buybacks (65.55–68p per share, July 2026). Net debt rose to £16.2m but remains modest relative to EBITDA (~£31.1m).

The buy case rests on mean reversion: if operating margins recover from FY2025’s compressed £12.5m operating profit (from £23.6m in FY2024), earnings power could re-rate the stock without requiring multiple expansion. Institutional ownership (~83%) and active capital return reinforce a shareholder-friendly governance overlay. The screen’s stable, building conviction (39%, three weeks at signal) aligns with a patient accumulation stance rather than aggressive deployment.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Earnings and cyclicality:** FY2025 profitability “tumbled” (Yahoo Finance, 2 Mar 2026) amid UK packaging distribution price competition and higher operating costs. Without filing bodies, margin bridge, segment detail, and management’s forward guidance cannot be verified.

**Operational and regulatory:** Management cited operational and financial disruption from a **fatal accident at a recently acquired business** (H2 earnings call highlights, 3 Mar 2026). Potential HSE investigation, remediation costs, reputational damage, and insurance/contingency exposures are unquantified in available sources.

**Balance sheet and goodwill:** Goodwill (£74.5m) plus intangibles (£30.4m) exceed tangible equity; sustained margin weakness raises impairment risk not captured by static screen metrics.

**Leverage and leases:** Total debt rose to £89.4m; capitalised leases £58.8m. Interest expense nearly doubled YoY. Net debt/EBITDA remains manageable (~0.5x) but direction is unfavourable.

**Pension:** Defined benefit obligation £6.0m persists; full triennial valuation and funding schedule unavailable without annual report body.

**Dividend sustainability:** Flat dividend against halved earnings; third-party commentary explicitly warns dividend investors (Simply Wall St, 11 May 2026). Payout supported by FCF today but vulnerable if cash conversion weakens.

**Governance / disclosure gap:** Five Companies House annual accounts are indexed but bodies were not extracted—going-concern statements, covenant terms, contingent liabilities, and related-party disclosures cannot be reviewed. This is a material research gap.

**Market sentiment:** One-year share price loss linked to shrinking earnings (Yahoo Finance, 28 Nov 2025); screen timing signal is “insufficient_data.”
