BETA Close X Forterra plc announced on June 11, 2026, that awards were granted under its Long Term Incentive Plan to the Chief Executive Officer and other persons discharging managerial responsibilities. A total of 288,331 ordinary shares were awarded as Restricted Share Awards and 576,662 as Performance Share Awards. These awards, with a nominal cost option exercise price of £0.01 per share, are based on a share price of £1.73 and are structured to vest on June 11, 2029, contingent upon the achievement of specific performance conditions related to adjusted EPS growth, total shareholder return, and carbon emissions intensity reduction.
