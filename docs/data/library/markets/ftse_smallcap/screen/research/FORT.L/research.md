## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Forterra plc is a UK building-products manufacturer (clay bricks, concrete blocks and related materials) whose shares screen as a **buy** on cheapness, cash generation, dividend and GARP factors. The quantitative pack flags a 14.2% FCF yield, 4.7% dividend yield, P/E of 16.9 and P/B of 1.2, with seven of 22 models passing across five factor families. Yahoo-sourced financials (primary filing bodies are unavailable in this pack) show FY2025 revenue recovering to £386m and free cash flow strengthening to £44.5m, though reported earnings remain well below the 2022 cyclical peak. The central debate is whether normalised cash returns and balance-sheet repair justify accumulation, or whether a structurally softer UK housing market, muted ROE (7.4%) and recent analyst fair-value downgrades limit upside.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical and demand risk:** Forterra’s revenue collapse from £455.5m (2022) to ~£344m (2023–24) underscores high sensitivity to UK residential construction and repair-maintenance-improvement demand. FY2025 recovery to £386m may partly reflect volume or price normalisation rather than a sustained up-cycle; news from May 2025 (“Forterra stock tumbles on revenue decline, rising costs”, Investing.com UK) flags market concern on margins and top-line pressure.

**Returns and earnings quality:** ROE of 7.4% (screen) and reported EPS of 8p remain far below the 27p earned in 2022. FY2025 included £7.2m of negative unusual items (Yahoo); without filing footnotes the nature of these charges (restructuring, asset impairments, energy hedging) is unverified.

**Leverage and liquidity:** Although net debt has fallen, FY2025 cash of £6.1m is thin relative to £81.7m gross debt and £78.6m inventory. Interest cover must be monitored if rates stay elevated or EBITDA softens. No covenant or going-concern disclosures are available in this pack.

**Governance / sentiment:** Multiple insiders increased holdings (Yahoo/Simply Wall St, Apr–Jun 2026), which is a positive signal, but concurrent analyst fair-value cuts (Yahoo Finance, 1 Jul 2026: estimate reduced from £2.41 to £1.99) suggest the sell-side is more cautious on growth.

**Regulatory / environmental:** As a heavy manufacturer (bricks, blocks), Forterra faces energy-cost volatility and UK decarbonisation policy risk; no ESG or regulatory filing language is present in sourced materials.

**Research-pack limitations:** Absence of RNS results text, zero interim filings, and no filing bodies mean pension deficit size, contingent liabilities, customer concentration and audit emphasis-of-matter paragraphs **cannot be assessed** — a material verify-before-trade gap.
