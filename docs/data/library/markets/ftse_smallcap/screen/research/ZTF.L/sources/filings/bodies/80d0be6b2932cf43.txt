Disclaimer* Zotefoams PLC 30 September 2025 Zotefoams plc (the 'Company') NOTIFICATION OF TRANSACTIONS OF DIRECTORS/PDMRs 30 September 2025 - The Company has been informed that its Group CFO and PDMR, Mr G C McGrath, acquired on 29 September 2025, 20,154 ordinary shares in the Company on the vesting of a nil-cost award granted to him in September 2020 under the Company's Long Term Incentive Plan (the 2020 LTIP Award ) and that Mr McGrath has (i) sold 10,376 of the vested shares at a price of £4.029 each to satisfy the tax liability which arose on the vesting; and (ii) has retained the remaining 11,655 shares. Vesting of the 2020 LTIP Award was subject to the satisfaction of performance targets over a performance period and to a 2-year holding period post the third anniversary of the grant (cumulatively a 5-year Restricted Period ). In accordance with the rules governing the 2020 LTIP Award, the volume of Company shares vesting under the 2020 LTIP Award also takes account of the dividends which would have been paid on them during the Restricted Period.
