BETA Close X Henry Boot PLC announced that all resolutions presented at its Annual General Meeting on May 21, 2026, were passed by shareholders. Notably, the resolution to receive the Directors' Report and Financial Statements received 99.79% of the vote, while the declaration of a final dividend was approved with 99.98% of votes cast. Reappointments of directors and auditors also saw strong support, with most director reappointments exceeding 96% approval and the reappointment of Ernst & Young LLP as auditors receiving 99.83% of the vote. The company had 134,511,827 ordinary shares in issue eligible to vote.
