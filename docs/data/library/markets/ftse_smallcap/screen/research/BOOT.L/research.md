# Henry Boot PLC (BOOT.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T19:32:41.279428+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Henry Boot is a UK land promotion, property development and housebuilding group trading at a deep discount to book (P/B ~0.5, P/E ~10.2, yield ~4.8%), with the quantitative screen passing seven of 22 models across cheapness, dividend and risk families. The value case rests on one of the country’s largest land banks (Hallam Land), a £1.2–1.4bn HBD development pipeline including Golden Valley and Duxford Av Tech, and active capital recycling — set against cyclical UK housing demand, rising net debt and a weak 22 July 2026 profit warning. The central debate is whether tangible NAV and high-IRR land sales are mispriced, or whether near-term earnings compression, negative free cash flow and management transition cap rerating until FY2026 results clarify trajectory.

---

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, BOOT screens as an asset-backed land and development play rather than a high-ROE compounder. The screen flags buy with composite score 66%, passing Graham Defensive, Schloss low P/B, earnings yield, low P/E plus high yield, dividend growth, composite value and financial health; current ratio ~4.85 supports the risk bucket. At roughly half of Yahoo-reported book value (~£423m equity at FY2025), the market prices scepticism into returns, not insolvency.

Business quality is defensible but not pristine. Henry Boot has operated since 1886 with family-influenced stewardship, a top-five UK land portfolio (potential for 100,000+ homes per RNS), and a strategic pivot toward high-quality land, prime development and premium homes — evidenced by the September 2025 disposal of loss-making Henry Boot Construction (£49.7m revenue, £2.7m operating loss in FY2024; £4.0m MBO consideration) and ongoing plot disposals at strong ungeared IRRs (9–66%). HBD’s forward-funded Golden Valley phase one (£95m GDV, 68% pre-let) and Arcadis letting at Manchester’s Island scheme (£48/sq ft headline rent) show execution capability on flagship projects.

The screen’s buy signal aligns with balance-sheet depth and income (dividends ~£10.5m paid in FY2025 per Yahoo) but not with earnings momentum: ROE is only 4.3% and revenue has contracted since FY2023. The thesis is that inventory (~£368m), investment property (~£95m) and planning gains on strategic sites offer asymmetric upside if UK land markets stabilise; the 4.8% yield provides carry while waiting. Screen conviction is moderate (38%) but persistent over five weeks.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical and earnings**
- 22 July 2026 profit warning (news only; no filing body) signals FY2026 PBT materially below expectations despite record 2025 plot volumes — timing and pricing of land monetisation remain uncertain.
- Multi-year revenue and operating profit downtrend (Yahoo); ROE ~4.3% suggests capital is not earning through the cycle at current valuations.
- UK housebuilding and land markets remain planning-, rate- and sentiment-sensitive; RNS commentary references domestic nerves and macro disruption (news, July 2026).

**Balance sheet and liquidity**
- Inventory-heavy balance sheet (£368m) creates mark-to-market and absorption risk if consents or buyer appetite slow.
- FY2025 negative free cash flow and net debt up ~£46m; cash £8.4m vs £116m total debt — thin liquidity cover despite strong current ratio (inventory-driven).
- HBC vendor loan note (£4m initial, five-year term) creates counterparty/recovery risk; initial carrying amount anticipated below £4m per disposal RNS.

**Strategic and execution**
- Construction exit reduces diversification and removes ~21% of headcount; near-term revenue base narrows.
- Golden Valley (£1bn scheme; phase one completion early 2028) and Duxford Av Tech (£162m GDV) are long-dated, capital-intensive and pre-letting dependent.
- CEO transition: Ed Hutchinson named CEO on July 2026 Golden Valley RNS; Tim Roberts held the role on September 2025 announcements — succession risk during a leverage uptick.

**Governance**
- Mixed insider signals: Emma Sibley purchase £15,400 (Kalkine Media, 24 July 2026) vs insider sale flagged (simplywall.st, 15 November 2025).
- Warrent Thompson appointed to lead Stonebridge Homes (Sharecast.com, 12 June 2026) amid broader leadership change.

**Pension and disclosure**
- Pension position improved in Yahoo data but cannot be verified from annual report narrative.
- **Material gap:** five annual Companies House filings indexed without bodies; zero interim filings; July 2026 trading update absent from index — prevents assessment of going-concern language, contingencies, litigation or banking covenants.

**Third-party scepticism**
- “Stay cautious with this lowly rated property stock” (Investors’ Chronicle, 23 September 2025).
