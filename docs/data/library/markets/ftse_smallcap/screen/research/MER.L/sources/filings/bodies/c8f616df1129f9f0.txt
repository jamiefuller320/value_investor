The company also received approval for proposed amendments to its Long Term Incentive Plan, the re-appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as auditor, and the authorisation for directors to fix auditor remuneration. Mears has extended its activities to provide broader housing solutions to solve the challenge posed by the lack of affordable housing and to provide accommodation and support for the most vulnerable.
