Disclaimer* Mears Group PLC 19 October 2026 Mears Group PLC ("Mears" or "the Group" or "the Company") Completion of Buyback programme Mears (LSE: MER), the leading provider of housing services to the public and regulated sectors in the UK, is pleased to announce that it has completed its share buyback programme, details of which were announced on 1 April 2026. Over the duration of the programme, the Group purchased and cancelled 4,928,004 Ordinary Shares of 1p each, at a total consideration of £20.0m and an average price of 405.8p, representing c.5.7% of the Group's issued share capital at the start of the buyback.
