Cash returns (dividends €2.7bn; buybacks €2.0bn in 2025 per Yahoo) underpin the dividend-family pass.

The screen’s “building” conviction (63%, two weeks at signal) is reasonable: fundamentals appear solid, but primary-source verification is blocked by the filing mismatch, and news flow on Vinci itself is thin relative to ticker noise. For a value investor, the hook is a compounder trading below many global infrastructure peers on earnings, with a covered yield and FCF that has outpaced net income growth—provided leverage and concession politics remain manageable.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Data integrity (primary red flag).** Indexed SEC filings describe Dollar General, not Vinci. Any going-concern, covenant, contingency or litigation language in those bodies is **not applicable** to Vinci and must not be imported into this memo.

**Leverage and interest burden (Yahoo).** Net debt ~€17.6bn and total debt ~€37.7bn at end-2025; interest expense has doubled since 2022. Further rate pressure or acquisition-led leverage could compress equity FCF available for dividends/buybacks.

**Balance-sheet structure.** Large intangible/concession assets (€60bn+) drive negative tangible equity; impairment or regulatory concession resets would hit book value and sentiment more than reported earnings.

**Cyclical and regulatory exposure (qualitative; not from Vinci filings in pack).** Construction margins are procyclical; motorway and airport concessions depend on traffic volumes, tariff frameworks and political consent (renationalisation debates in France and abroad remain an overhang for the sector). Yahoo shows rising “other operating expenses” (€9.5bn → €11.3bn, 2023–25), warranting scrutiny in primary accounts.

**Pension.** €1.17bn non-current pension/post-retirement liability (2025 Yahoo)—material but not dominant relative to EBITDA.

**Governance / management transition.** News reports CFO transition: Thierry Mirville succeeded Christian Labeyrie effective 1 June 2026—standard succession but worth monitoring for capital-allocation continuity.

**Screen limitations.** Strong risk-family pass may underweight concession political risk, M&A integration (historically active), and negative working-capital volatility. Macro markers (EUR/USD 1.14; Euro Stoxx 50 ~6,281) are neutral context only—not used to adjust the screen signal.
