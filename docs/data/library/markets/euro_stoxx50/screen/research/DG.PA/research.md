## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Data integrity (primary red flag).** No Vinci filings are indexed; orphaned body extracts describe Dollar General. Any going-concern, covenant, contingency or litigation language in those bodies is **not applicable** to Vinci and has not been imported here.

**Leverage and interest burden (Yahoo).** Net debt ~€17.6bn and total debt ~€37.7bn at end-2025; interest expense has doubled since 2022. Further rate pressure or acquisition-led leverage could compress FCF available for dividends and buybacks. 2024 net debt step-up warrants reconciliation against primary accounts.

**Balance-sheet structure.** Large intangible/concession assets (€60bn+) drive negative tangible equity; impairment or regulatory concession resets would hit book value and sentiment more than reported earnings.

**Cyclical and regulatory exposure (qualitative; not from Vinci filings in pack).** Construction margins are procyclical; motorway and airport concessions depend on traffic volumes, tariff frameworks and political consent (renationalisation debates in France and abroad remain a sector overhang). Yahoo shows rising "other operating expenses" (€9.5bn → €11.3bn, 2023–25), warranting scrutiny in primary accounts.

**Pension.** €1.17bn non-current pension/post-retirement liability (2025 Yahoo)—material but not dominant relative to EBITDA.

**Governance / management transition.** News reports CFO transition: Thierry Mirville succeeded Christian Labeyrie effective 1 June 2026—standard succession but worth monitoring for capital-allocation continuity.

**Screen limitations.** Strong risk-family pass may underweight concession political risk, M&A integration (historically active) and negative working-capital volatility. Macro markers (EUR/USD 1.14; Euro Stoxx 50 ~6,281) are neutral context only—not used to adjust the screen signal.
