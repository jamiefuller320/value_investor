BETA Close X TotalEnergies SE has announced the purchase of 1,767,146 of its own shares between May 25 and May 29, 2026, for a total amount of €134,999,815.60. The weighted average purchase price across these transactions was €76.39 per share, with purchases occurring on the XPAR and CEUX markets. This share repurchase activity is in accordance with prior shareholder authorizations.
