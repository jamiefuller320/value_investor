BETA Close X TotalEnergies has announced its dividend timetable for the fiscal year 2027, with ex-dividend dates set for September 30, 2027, December 31, 2027, March 31, 2028, and June 30, 2028, for the first interim, second interim, third interim, and final dividends respectively. Payment dates will follow shortly after, with Euronext payments on October 4, 2027, January 4, 2028, April 4, 2028, and July 4, 2028, and NYSE payments on October 21, 2027, January 21, 2028, April 24, 2028, and July 24, 2028. These dates are subject to the approval of the 2026 financial statements and the allocation of earnings by the Board of Directors and Shareholders' Meeting.
