BETA Close X TotalEnergies SE has announced transactions by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) in accordance with the UK Market Abuse Regulation. Bernard Pinatel, a Member of the Executive Committee, acquired 5.5658 units of TOTALENERGIES ACTIONNARIAT FRANCE « A » PART C at a price of 75.0625 EUR, totaling 417.78 EUR. Aurelien Hamelle, also a Member of the Executive Committee, acquired 123.5354 units of TOTALENERGIES ACTIONNARIAT FRANCE RELAIS 2026 at 100.4324 EUR, amounting to 12,406.96 EUR, and an additional 3.6308 units of the same instrument at 100.4324 EUR, totaling 364.65 EUR. These acquisitions, representing employee savings including profit-sharing and employer matching contributions, occurred outside a trading venue on May 29, 2025.
