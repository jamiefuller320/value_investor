Cost cuts meet an AI pivot The planned reductions affect Nokia sites in Espoo, Tampere and Oulu. For investors, the timing matters because Nokia's latest reported quarter already showed a sharp split between growth and restructuring pressure. The same report said Nokia expected EUR 800.00 million of restructuring charges for fiscal 2026, making execution and cost control important counterweights to the growth story.
