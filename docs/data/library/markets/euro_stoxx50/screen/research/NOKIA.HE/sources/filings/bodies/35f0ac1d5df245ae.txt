Local digital news provider The Paper reports the deal to buy the 50 percent equity stake from Nokia's former partner, China Huaxin Post & Telecommunications Technology, closed on 12 December. Nokia Shanghai Bell specialises in Chinese sales of telecoms and data centre equipment, software and services to operators and enterprises.
