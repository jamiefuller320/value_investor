## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Koninklijke Ahold Delhaize is a large defensive food retailer screening as a strong buy on cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP and risk metrics (P/E 13.3, P/B 2.0, yield 4.3%, ROE 15.0%). Eight of 22 models pass, spanning Graham Enterprising, FCF Yield, Neff PEGY, Dividend Growth, Magic Formula, Piotroski F-Score, Composite Value and Earnings Quality — an unusually broad pass set for a euro consumer-defensive name.

For a value investor, the screen signal aligns with observable business characteristics visible in Yahoo financials (see Financial Review): revenue has grown from €87.0bn (2022) to €92.4bn (2025); net income rebounded to €2.26bn in 2025 after a softer 2024 (€1.76bn); diluted EPS rose 32% to €2.50; and free cash flow has remained in a €3.6–4.4bn band across four years. Capital allocation supports the dividend and GARP passes: 2025 dividends of €1.07bn and share repurchases of €1.01bn, with diluted shares falling from 933m to 905m.

Balance-sheet metrics from Yahoo (net debt €2.9bn vs EBITDA €7.3bn in 2025) support the risk-family pass despite substantial capitalised lease obligations (~€12.0bn). The screen’s cheapness does not appear driven by distress: operating cash flow was €6.99bn in 2025 and the business continues to fund both growth capex (€2.56bn) and returns to shareholders.

The thesis is qualified, not rejected, by source gaps. ## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
Filing data integrity. The research pack contains no indexed Ahold Delhaize filings; existing body extracts belong to another issuer. Risk language from `filings/bodies/` (debt covenants, going concern, contingencies, legal proceedings) cannot be applied to Ahold Delhaize. This is the most material red flag in the pack itself.

Regulatory and legal (news-sourced). Ahold Delhaize USA agreed to pay a $40m settlement (June 2026) over allegations that supermarket pharmacy chains (Giant, Hannaford, Stop & Shop, Food Lion) submitted inflated prescription drug prices to federal healthcare programmes including Medicare Part D, Medicaid and TRICARE. This raises compliance and reputational risk in a non-core but material US business line; primary filing contingency disclosure was not available to assess reserves or ongoing exposure.

Competitive and cyclical. US grocery faces intensifying competition from Walmart, Kroger consolidation (e.g. Giant Eagle acquisition, July 2026), discounters Aldi/Lidl and e-commerce. Industry reports note White House pressure on major US grocers over beef pricing (July 2026), signalling potential margin and political scrutiny. European operations face similar discounter pressure and wage inflation.

Management transition. Group CEO Thierry Garnier (ex-Carrefour) took the helm in 2025; Claire Peters (ex-Amazon, Woolworths, Tesco) was nominated as Ahold Delhaize USA CEO effective September 2025, pending shareholder approval. Execution risk around US turnaround, omnichannel and private-label strategy is elevated during this dual transition.

Balance sheet structure (Yahoo). Capitalised lease obligations (~€12bn) and negative working capital increase effective leverage beyond headline net debt of €2.9bn. Pension obligations (~€972m combined) add to adjusted leverage not fully reflected in simple P/E or P/B screens.

Governance / sentiment. Bearish thematic coverage includes “3 Reasons to Avoid AD and 1 Stock to Buy Instead” (Yahoo Finance, 16 July 2026) and “3 Reasons AD is Risky and 1 Stock to Buy Instead” (28 January 2026), reflecting scepticism on growth and valuation assumptions.

Macro (colour only). EUR/USD at 1.14 and Euro Stoxx 50 at 6,281 (24 July 2026) provide no automatic veto; substantial US revenue exposure implies USD/EUR moves affect reported euro earnings.
