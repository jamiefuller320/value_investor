Continued growth in the third quarter of 2026 
 
Paris, October 12, 2026 
 
LVMH Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton, the world’s leading high -quality products group, recorded 
revenue of €57.1 billion in the first nine months of 2026. Organic revenue growth came to 2%. Backed 
by its diverse range of businesses, LVMH remained very solid in an uncertain geopolitical and economic 
environment, and maintained its innovative momentum.  
In the third quarter, organic revenue growth reflected the impact on business activity of a less buoyant 
environment in China, while the United States, Europe and Japan saw good growth.

The Wines & Spirits business group recorded organic revenue growth of 5% in the first nine months of 
2026. The champagne business saw further signs of the recovery observed since the beginning of the year, 
particularly in Europe. Moët & Chandon continued to enjoy a high- profile presence during its second 
season as the Official Champagne of Formula 1 Grand Prix races. Hennessy cognac saw good momentum 
in Asia, while the US market benefited from  the launch of the new VS range of ready-to-serve cocktails. 
Provence rosé wines continued to make good progress.  
 
The Fashion & Leather Goods  business group continued to benefit from the buoyant US market but 
was affected in the third quarter by the slowdown in demand in China. The creative renewal of our 
Maisons bore fruit . Louis Vuitton paid tribute to its iconic bags for the 130th anniversary of the 
Monogram, revisiting the original canvas used for the Maison’s first trunks with the new Monogram 
Emblème jacquard weave. Ready- to-wear collections designed by Nicolas Ghesquière saw good 
momentum, as did the Men’s collections designed by Pharrell Williams. The Maison continued to offer 
its customers a range of unique in-store experiences, exemplified by LV The Place Seoul and immersive 
new travel -inspired locations around the world. Christian Dior benefited from  the major success of

Jonathan Anderson’s designs. Demand was especially strong for its ready -to-wear and leather goods 
lines. After Osaka in May, a new House of Dior flagship store was opened in September, on Via Monte 
Napoleone in Milan. Loro Piana, which achieved an excellent performance, presented Solutions  For, its 
latest ready -to-wear collection of functional , creative  pieces, and opened a new center for knitwear 
excellence in Ghemme , Italy. At Celine, collections designed by Michael Rider were a major success, 
as was Fendi’s new Baguette bag, revisited by Maria Grazia Chiuri. Loewe, with Jack McCollough and 
Lazaro Hernandez, and Givenchy, with Sarah Burton, continued the creative renewal of their 
collections. Rimowa and Berluti confirmed their good momentum. On September  1, Marc Jacobs was 
sold to WHP Global.  
 
The Perfumes & Cosmetics business group, for which revenue remained stable on an organic basis in the 
first nine months of 2026, maintained its highly selective retail approach. LVMH’s historic brands 
performed well. Parfums Christian Dior benefited from the launches of J’adore Intense, Sauvage Extrait 
and eau de parfum versions of Dior Addict. In high perfumery, new signature scents were unveiled within 
La Collection Privée. Good momentum in makeup – driven by Forever and Backstage in particular – also 
contributed to the Maison’s performance. Guerlain saw substantial growth, driven by the launch of its 
L’Heure Bleue scent, the success of its L’Art & La Matière and Aqua Allegoria fragrance collections, as 
well as accelerating growth in its iconic Rouge G lipstick. Parfums Givenchy focused on the development 
of L’Interdit. Maison Francis Kurkdjian expanded its Oud collection and Acqua di Parma celebrated its 
110th anniversary. 
 
The Watches & Jewelry business group recorded organic revenue growth of 8% in the first nine months 
of 2026, driven by the very good performance in jewelry. Tiffany & Co. continued to successfully 
strengthen its iconic product lines  – HardWear, Knot and Sixteen Stone  in particular  – and to renovate 
its store network. Following its launch in New  York, the Blue Book 2026: Hidden Garden  high jewelry 
collection presented its fall expression in Venice, illustrating the Maison’s powerful creativity. Bvlgari 
unveiled its new Eclettica line of high jewelry and prestige watches, which generated record- breaking 
revenue. Its Kaleidos  exhibition stopped over in Shanghai, while a monumental installation unveiled in 
New York celebrated its iconic Serpenti design. Chaumet continued to develop its Bee de Chaumet  
collection. In watches, TAG Heuer enjoyed a high-profile presence at the Grand Prix races of Formula 1, 
in particular as Title Partner in Madrid.  
 
In Selective Retailing , organic revenue growth came to 6% in the first nine months of 2026. Sephora 
saw strong growth in its revenue, with further market share gains in several countries, consolidating its 
global leadership position. Its performance was driven by its solid brand portfolio, with sustained growth 
in its many historic brands and the ongoing ramp-up of exclusive launches, including Rhode and several 
C-Beauty brands. The retail network continued to expand, including new market entries in Belgium and 
Croatia. Le Bon Marché posted revenue growth, driven by its ever -unique slate of events. Most of DFS’ 
businesses were sold.  
 
OUTLOOK 
Despite an economic and geopolitical environment that remains uncertain, the Group remains confident 
and will maintain a strategy focused on continuously enhancing the desirability of its brands, drawing on 
the authenticity and quality of its products, its excellence in retail and its agile organization. LVMH will 
draw on its powerful brands and the talent of its teams to reinforce its global leadership position in luxury 
goods once again in 2026.
