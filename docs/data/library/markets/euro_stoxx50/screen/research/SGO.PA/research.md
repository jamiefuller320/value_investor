# Compagnie de Saint-Gobain S.A. (SGO.PA) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-07-25T18:39:43.037820+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Saint-Gobain is a global leader in light and sustainable construction trading at modest multiples (P/E 13.3, P/B 1.5, dividend yield 3.0%) against a stabilised earnings base and strong cash conversion. The quantitative screen rates the name a strong buy: eight of 22 models pass across all four metric families (cheapness, dividend, GARP, risk), with an 86% composite score. The central debate is whether portfolio rotation under the new “Lead & Grow” plan (2026–2030)—Nordic distribution divestiture, chemicals bolt-ons, margin-led growth—will compound value, or whether softer 2026 EBITDA margin guidance and cyclical construction headwinds cap upside. Primary filing coverage is partial: the indexed shareholder letter (No. 102, March 2026) corroborates 2025 results and strategy, but no annual report or interim release is in the library; Yahoo financials fill remaining gaps.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
Saint-Gobain fits a classic value screen. The group passes Graham Enterprising, FCF Yield, Neff PEGY, Dividend Growth, Magic Formula, Acquirer’s Multiple, Composite Value, and Financial Health—covering cheapness, income, growth-at-a-reasonable-price, and balance-sheet quality in one package. Key screen metrics: composite score 86% (sector-relative 78%), P/E 13.3, P/B 1.5, dividend yield 3.0%, ROE 11.7%, debt/equity 72%, and FCF yield supported by ~€3.1bn FCF per the screen (Yahoo: €3.45bn; shareholder letter: €3.8bn).

Business quality underwrites the screen. Per the shareholder letter, 2025 sales reached €46.5bn (+2.1% in local currencies), operating income €5.3bn (+3.8% LC) at an 11.4% margin, recurring net income €3.3bn, and free cash flow €3.8bn (58% conversion). The completed “Grow & Impact” plan (2021–2025) met all financial and strategic targets; “Lead & Grow” targets mid-single-digit average sales growth, EBITDA margin of 15–18%, ROCE >13%, and >20% sales rotation through 2030. Portfolio actions—Dahl Nordic sale to Kesko (~€1.2bn), Xypex Chemical acquisition, Cemix and FOSROC in construction chemicals (+15.9% LC)—shift exposure toward higher-margin solutions rather than pure volume. Shareholder returns are tangible: proposed dividend €2.30 (+4.5%) and €402m net buybacks in 2025.

For a value investor, the hook is a de-rated industrial compounder with resilient margins, geographic diversification (Asia/emerging +12.6% LC), and a valuation that does not require a construction boom. Conviction on the screen is building (46%, two weeks at signal, stable trend); timing data are insufficient for a tactical overlay.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
Cyclical exposure. Construction volumes remain tied to European and global building cycles. Sector commentary (Holcim labour-shortage warning, 25 June 2026) highlights uneven European recovery; EMEA sales were flat in LC in 2025 despite H2 improvement.

Margin guidance. The shareholder letter guides 2026 EBITDA margin at more than 15.0%, below the 15.5% delivered in 2025 (per 26 February 2026 news). H1 weather disruption adds near-term uncertainty—directly challenging the GARP thesis if earnings growth stalls.

Leverage and M&A. Net debt has risen ~€3bn over two years (Yahoo) while the group continues bolt-on acquisitions (Xypex, July 2026) alongside divestitures (Dahl Nordic). Heavy goodwill/intangibles increase impairment risk; Yahoo shows recurring special charges and impairments (€83m unusual items, €216m capital-asset impairment in 2025).

Pension and provisions. Non-current pension liabilities ~€1.5bn plus current pension items (€139m) and long-term provisions (~€975m) per Yahoo. Funding status and discount-rate sensitivity cannot be assessed without the universal registration document or annual report.

Governance and listing. London Stock Exchange delisting (15 February 2026 news) reduces secondary liquidity for UK holders. ~48% institutional ownership (21 July 2025, simplywall.st) concentrates influence. The shareholder letter notes ~493m shares and estimated ownership structure at 31 December 2025.

Filing gaps. No going-concern, contingency, or covenant language is available from indexed filing bodies. The letter references net debt/EBITDA target of 1.5x–2.0x but does not quote covenant headroom. Absence of annual and interim regulatory filings limits verification of litigation, environmental provisions, and debt terms.
