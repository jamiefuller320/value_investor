Sales totaled €11.6 billion in the third quarter, up by 2.5% like-for-like and by 1.2% over the 
nine months to end-September. All Regions delivered growth in the third quarter. 
 
Construction chemicals continued its strong momentum, outperforming with 6.3% like-for-like 
sales growth over the quarter (5.6% over the nine months to end -September); all Regions 
delivered strong growth. 
 
In a contrasted macroeconomic environment and uncertain geopolitical landscape, the Group 
continued to roll out its “Lead & Grow” strategy aimed at accelerating the deployment of its 
solutions on the residential, non-residential and infrastructure markets, country by country. 
Thanks to its value-added solutions and its disciplined execution, prices increased 3.2% in the 
third quarter (an increase of 1.5% over the nine months to end-September), in a more pronounced 
inflationary environment. 
 
The negative 2.1% structure impact in the third quarter (negative 1.1% impact over the nine 
months to end-September) mainly reflects the disposal of businesses in distribution (Nordics, Brazil 
and Belgium) and in Germany ( dry mortars, off -site construction and HKO). Acquisitions were 
focused on high-growth countries : Indocement (Indonesia), Morteros de Europa  (Dominican 
Republic), QSI Peru and PMIM (Vietnam). The exchange rate impact was a positive 1.4% in the 
quarter (negative 0.3% over the nine months to end-September), mainly due to the appreciation of 
currencies in the Americas against the euro.

Performance by Region (sales)
 
Europe, Middle East & Africa: sales growth 
 
Europe rose 2.5% like-for-like in the third quarter (up 1.9% over the nine months to end -
September), with a contrasted situation by country. 
 
- Northern Europe was up by 3.4% like-for-like, thanks to growth in its main countries apart 
from the UK, which continues to be impacted by the construction market downturn. Nordic 
countries reported further good growth, benefiting from improved mix with innovative solutions 
(technical flooring, high performance low-carbon plasterboards), increased exposure to non-
residential markets and multi-product specification capabilit ies. Switzerland and Eastern 
Europe continued to outperform thanks to their comprehensive and differentiated offering 
(Lanaé® and Isorigid® insulation) and to growth in industrial solutions . Poland and the Czech 
Republic delivered double-digit growth , driven by infrastructure and a broader range of 
solutions. Germany was up slightly, supported by the non -residential renovation market and 
the rollout of its infrastructure solutions, enjoying commercial success in technical mortars for 
energy infrastructure. The Region is active in over 200 data center projects, specifying an 
increasingly broad range of solutions, from civil engineering and foundation works to the 
building envelope, interior solutions, technical insulation , metal structures and fire -resistant 
glazing.  
 
- Southern Europe, Middle East & Africa rose by 1.6% like-for-like. France posted slight 
growth in a quarter characterized by caution: renovation was affected by the uncertainties 
weighing on consumer confidence, while new construction delivered further growth over the 
period. Although the summer heatwaves had a slightly negative short-term impact, the need 
to adapt to climate change represents a growth opportunity for the Group, thanks to its 
solutions that can reduce indoor temperatures by up to 10°C. Spain-Italy rose slightly, driven 
once again by interior solutions which continue to capture market share. The Middle East saw 
further double -digit growth led by construction chemicals, especially in the infrastructure 
market, with Turkey delivering a strong outperformance , including solutions delivered for the 
Izmit metro. In Southern Europe, the Group is deploying advanced AI-powered optimization 
systems in its plasterboard plants, initially at a pilot site at Quinto in Spain, with a potential gain 
of around 1.5 production days per line per year. 
 
Americas: slight growth in sales 
 
The Region posted a slight increase of 0.6% like-for-like in the third quarter, reflecting growth 
in North America and despite the contraction in Latin America. 
 
- North America saw 2.0% like-for-like growth thanks to an increase in prices and volumes 
driven by construction, while industrial solutions remained down; inflation is accelerating  
(particularly for transport). Roofing and plasterboard were up slightly, outperforming thanks to 
differentiated solutions, in a market affected by the absence of major weather events and by 
the decline in new residential construction. The outperformance in interior solutions  was 
supported by non -residential applications. The Group deliver ed another strong 
outperformance in construction chemicals, with high-single-digit growth and market share 
gains. CertainTeed Integrity Roof System ® solutions for climate resilience captured market 
share (up 10% over the nine-month period), and the number of roofing contractor companies 
accredited by CertainTeed continued to grow (up 10% over the nine-month period). The Group 
bolstered its technical support capabilities and the added value its solutions bring to customers, 
particularly in non-residential markets, with the inauguration during the quarter of a new fire 
testing facility at the Saint-Gobain Innovation Hub in Northborough (Massachusetts).
3 
- Latin America contracted by  3.0% like-for-like in volatile markets. In Brazil, a cautious 
attitude prevailed ahead of the presidential elections, but the “Lead & Grow” strategy is being 
rolled out swiftly, with a double-digit rise in the value of the specified project pipeline over the 
nine months to end-September – particularly in non-residential markets – thanks to inter-brand 
key account teams . The region saw further growth in construction chemicals , in Brazil – 
particularly in infrastructure  – in Mexico and in Central America thanks to the success of 
Cemix, as well as in Peru with the integration of QSI Peru. 
 
Asia-Pacific: strong sales growth driven by India  
 
The Region reported 8.0% like-for-like growth in the third quarter (after 7.0% in the first half ), 
thanks to strong momentum in India and South-East Asia.  
 
India captured further market share, with double-digit organic growth  driven by the strong 
reputation of the Saint-Gobain brand in the country, along with its comprehensive, innovative and 
sustainable offering. The Group was awarded new projects in non -residential and infrastructure, 
illustrating its construction chemicals leadership (for example, the Dhirubhai Ambani Green Energy 
Giga Complex). An AI-powered customer recommendation tool has been developed and deployed 
within sales teams with promising conversion rates. South-East Asia continued to grow, thanks to 
Vietnam, the Philippines, Indonesia and Malaysia , benefiting from the increasing adoption of 
sustainable construction solutions and a greater market share in data centers. The start-up of new 
lightweight board  production lines in Malaysia and Vietnam, along with the completion of the 
acquisition of a new plasterboard production line in Vietnam, will help extend this growth 
momentum. Australia reported further slight sales growth, driven by new construction. Saint -
Gobain recorded another outperformance in China, with modest growth in a market that remains 
down, with the start -up of Asia’s largest automated plasterboard production line in Guangdong 
province. 
2026 outlook
 
In a contrasted macroeconomic environment and uncertain geopol itical landscape, the 
Group expects like-for-like sales growth for the second half of 2026. 
 
Saint-Gobain expects an EBITDA margin of more than 15.0% in 2026.

Glossary: 
- Changes on an actual structure basis reflect changes in published indicators between two periods  
- Changes in local currencies reflect actual performance, applying exchange rates for the previous period to indicators for the period under review  
- Like-for-like changes (constant structure and exchange rates) reflect underlying performance excluding the impacts of :  
• changes in scope, by calculating indicators for the period under review based on the scope of consolidation of the previous period (structure impact) 
• changes in foreign exchange rates, by calculating indicators for the period under review and those for the previous period based on exchange rates 
for the previous period (exchange rate impact) 
- EBITDA: operating income plus operating depreciation & amortization and non -operating costs 
- EBITDA margin: EBITDA divided by sales
