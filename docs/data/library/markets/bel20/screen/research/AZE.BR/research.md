# Azelis Group NV (AZE.BR) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-09-04T07:35:34.775660+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Azelis Group NV is a Belgium-listed global distributor of specialty chemicals and food ingredients, positioned as an innovation-led consolidator in a fragmented market. The quantitative screen rates the name a buy (5/22 models; composite 57%, sector-relative 62%), chiefly on FCF yield (7.6%), Neff PEGY (0.14), earnings quality, financial health and Magic Formula metrics, with P/B at 1.0x. The valuation hook is cash generation against a goodwill-heavy balance sheet: the FY2024 ESEF integrated report cites €342m free cash flow and adjusted EBITA of €471m on €4.2bn revenue, while the market prices modest growth (2.9%) and a thin 1.9% dividend yield at ~26x earnings. The central debate is whether Azelis is a durable compounder whose M&A-led model and H2 2024/H1 2026 return to organic growth will restore earnings momentum, or a leveraged roll-up facing cyclical headwinds, post-PE overhang (EQT exit) and structurally low reported ROE (4.1%) that the quality screens reject.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Azelis offers a hybrid profile: not deep value (P/B 1.0x, failed Deep Value and Schloss), but a GARP/cash-yield case supported by demonstrable cash conversion and balance-sheet flexibility after a 2024 debt refinancing.

The screen passes three of five factor families — cheapness, GARP and risk — which fits a business that generates substantial free cash despite muted top-line growth. FY2024 filing KPIs show revenue broadly stable at €4,214m, adjusted EBITA €471m (conversion margin 45.7%) and free cash flow €342m; Yahoo annual data (fallback) shows operating cash flow of $422m and free cash flow of $397m in the latest year, consistent with the screen’s $224m TTM FCF figure once basis differences are allowed for. Current ratio of 1.95x and a Financial Health model pass suggest near-term liquidity is adequate despite net debt of c.$1.45bn (Yahoo, fallback).

Business quality is mixed but directionally supportive of the buy signal. Azelis is one of two global distributors focused on specialty chemicals, with 70+ formulation labs, a digital platform scaled across 100+ legal entities, and ~60 acquisitions since 2017 (eight in 2024). Management reported organic revenue growth returning in H2 2024 and, per yfinance coverage of the H1 2026 earnings call (31 July 2026), the first positive organic growth in over a year driven by Q2, with margins stable. That trajectory, if sustained, would address the screen’s Lynch PEG failure (growth 2.9% vs 5% floor) and low ROE.

The failed models — Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, Quality Value, High Dividend, Piotroski F-Score (6/9) — flag real constraints: tangible book value is negative (goodwill/intangibles ~$3.8bn per Yahoo), leverage is material (debt/equity 64.7%), and statutory net income fell to $111m in the latest Yahoo year from $181m prior year. The buy case therefore rests on cash yield and acquisition discipline at book value, not on classic quality-at-a-discount. Relative to Basic Materials peers in the screen universe (median buy names pass 7–13 models), Azelis is a lower-conviction quant pick whose edge is FCF, not ROE.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Leverage and acquisition** — Net debt ~$1.45bn (Yahoo) on equity ~$2.7bn; debt/equity 64.7% (screen). FY2024 filing cites successful refinancing, but continued M&A ($152m in 2025) and amortisation ($83m) sustain leverage. Goodwill/intangible-heavy balance sheet limits tangible downside protection; Schloss and Net-Net models fail accordingly.

**Cyclical / macro** — Filing risk framework highlights geopolitical volatility, inflation, financial uncertainty and EU chemical producer competitiveness pressures. Revenue was flat in FY2024; organic growth recovery is recent and not yet validated across a full year in audited filings.

**Regulatory / ESG** — Increasing chemical regulation and sustainability compliance are core operating risks; Scope 3 emissions of 5.4m tCO₂e (FY2024 filing) underscore downstream exposure. Climate transition is flagged as a top material risk in the ESEF sustainability linkage.

**Competitive** — Fragmented market (<15% share for top 10 distributors) supports consolidation thesis but also price competition; principal consolidation could alter mandat economics.

**Governance / key person** — 2024 leadership transition (CEO, Chairman) appears orderly. EQT fully exited its remaining stake (yfinance, 27 February 2026), removing a supportive holder and potentially increasing free float volatility. May 2026 AGM (yfinance, 11 April 2026) covers share-capital powers, buyback authority, founder rights and board composition — founder-rights structures warrant monitoring.

**Customer / supplier concentration** — Not quantified in available extracts; diversified end-markets (Life Sciences, Industrial Chemicals) cited in filing, but principal and regional concentration risk is noted qualitatively in ERM.

**Pension** — Immaterial at $13m non-current (Yahoo); not a primary red flag.

**Liquidity / covenants** — No covenant breach or going-concern language in available filing extract; refinancing cited as successful. Detailed covenant headroom not present in body extract — gap for verify-before-trade.

**Accounting** — Large goodwill ($2.5bn) creates impairment sensitivity if growth stalls. Special charges present in Yahoo income statements ($6.1m in 2025). Adjusted EBITA (€471m) vs reported operating income trend ($313m latest Yahoo year) implies a material adjusted-statutory gap — investors must reconcile non-GAAP metrics.
