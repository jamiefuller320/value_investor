NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED INTERIM FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (Unaudited) (Dollars in millions, except per share amounts) Note 1. Background and Basis of Presentation Background Garrett Motion Inc. (the “Company” or “Garrett”) is a technology leader delivering differentiated solutions for emission reduction and energy efficiency. We design, manufacture, and sell highly engineered turbocharging, air and fluid compression, and electric motor technologies to original equipment manufacturers (“OEMs”) and independent aftermarket distributors in the mobility and industrial fields. We have significant expertise in delivering highly engineered products at scale for internal combustion engines using gasoline, diesel, natural gas, and hydrogen, as well as for vehicles. Our products are key enablers for fuel economy, energy efficiency, thermal management, and compliance with greenhouse gas and other targets. Tariff Recoveries The Company may seek refunds of previously paid U.S. tariffs through applicable governmental refund programs and administrative claim processes. Refund claims are evaluated based on the specific facts and circumstances, including the status of legal and regulatory developments affecting the availability of such refunds. The Company recognizes tariff refund recoveries when it is probable that a refund will be realized and the amount of the recovery can be reasonably estimated. Tariff refund recoveries are recorded as a reduction of cost of goods sold. Amounts recognized but not yet received are recorded as receivables. Where a portion of the refunds is expected to be passed on to the Company’s customers to the extent the Company had previously collected such tariff reimbursements under separate agreements with its customers, a corresponding liability is recognized as a reduction of net sales. We define “Adjusted EBIT” as EBIT, plus the sum of (i) repositioning costs, (ii) foreign exchange (gain) loss on debt net of related hedging (gains) losses, (iii) discounting costs on factoring, (iv) gain on sale of equity investment, (v) acquisition and divestiture expenses, (vi) other income, and (vii) debt refinancing and redemption costs, if any. We believe that EBIT and Adjusted EBIT are important indicators of operating performance and provide useful information for investors and management because EBIT and Adjusted EBIT exclude the effects of income taxes, as well as the effects of financing activities by eliminating the effects of interest. Certain adjustment items, while periodically affecting our results, may also vary significantly from period to period and have disproportionate effect in a given period, which affects the comparability of our results.
