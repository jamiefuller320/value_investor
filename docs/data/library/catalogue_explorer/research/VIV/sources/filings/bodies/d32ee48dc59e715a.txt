Unipersonal (“ TEF Infra ”), corresponding to 24.99% of FiBrasil’s total share capital, through the execution of a Share Purchase Agreement (“ SPA ”), for the amount of R$458,719,774.51, paid in a single installment on the signing date (“ Transaction ”). The Transaction is part of the Company’s strategy to achieve greater operational synergies in its fiber network activities, supporting the full consolidation of FiBrasil within the Company.
