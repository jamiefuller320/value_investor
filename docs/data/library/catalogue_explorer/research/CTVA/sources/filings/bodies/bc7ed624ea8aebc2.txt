The following table summarizes the impacts of the company’s foreign currency hedging program on the company’s results of operations. The company routinely uses foreign currency exchange contracts to offset its net exposures, by currency, related to the foreign monetary assets and liabilities. The objective of this program is to maintain an approximately balanced position in foreign currencies in order to minimize, on an basis, the effects of exchange rate changes on net monetary asset positions.
