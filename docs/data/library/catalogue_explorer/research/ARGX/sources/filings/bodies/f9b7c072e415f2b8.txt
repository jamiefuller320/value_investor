Expand the global VYVGART opportunity and launch VYVGART SC as a syringe VYVGART® (IV: efgartigimod and SC: efgartigimod alfa and hyaluronidase-qvfc) is a targeted IgG fragment approved in three indications, including generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG) globally, primary immune thrombocytopenia (ITP) in Japan, and chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy (CIDP) in the U.S., Japan and China. The syringe (PFS) is now approved for use in the U.S. and EU. argenx is to sustain commercial growth through 2025, driven by global expansion, earlier treatment adoption, and the launch of the PFS to support growth momentum in both gMG and CIDP.
