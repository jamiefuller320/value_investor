Barak will take over as Chair from David Wells, who joined Wise in 2019 as a Director and has served as Chair since 2021. As part of his duties, Barak will serve as Chair of the Nominating and Corporate Governance committee. David will step down as Chair and as a Director in April 2027. Barak is a highly experienced technology executive and board director with nearly three decades of experience building and scaling global technology businesses. He spent 25 years at NICE, a technology company. There, he started as an engineer and later served as Chief Executive Officer from 2014 to 2024, driving its transformation into a leader serving thousands of enterprises worldwide. He brings both deep operational and experience, currently serving on the Boards of FactSet, CCC Intelligent Solutions, Similarweb and, previously, Payoneer. The Board welcomes Barak to Wise. Kristo Käärmann, and Chief Executive Officer, commented: “Barak brings a rare combination: years of experience building and scaling enterprise technology, and a track record of partnering with companies on their strategy. I've been impressed by what he built at NICE over 25 years - joining as an engineer and transforming it into a global cloud enterprise software leader. That operator mindset, combined with his board experience at public companies, makes him an exceptional partner and Chair as we continue to build ‘the’ network for the world’s money. “On behalf of the Board and all Wisers, I would like to thank David for all his contributions. When David joined us in 2019, we had 6 million customers. Today we serve nearly 19 million customers and some of the largest banks in the world. Under his chairmanship, we scaled our impact for people and businesses globally and became a company. I wish him every success in the future.” Barak Eilam, Chair Designate, said: "Wise is one of the most distinctive and important technology companies in global financial services. The team has built an extraordinary business by fundamentally improving how people and businesses move money across borders, combining a strong customer proposition with a and model. I see a significant opportunity ahead to build on that foundation, expand Wise’s impact globally, and create substantial value for customers and owners. I’m excited to work with the Board and leadership team as Wise enters its next phase of growth.” David Wells, outgoing Chair, commented: "Serving as Chair of Wise and partnering with Kristo and his leadership team through an important phase of Wise's growth and maturity has been a significant part of my work over the last 7 years. I am proud of the company's growth in an increasingly dynamic market and of the global Board we've built, whose skills are for the next phase of growth and evolution for Wise. Wise's vision to make money move without borders is more important than ever, and Barak is a strong leader to carry this forward.
