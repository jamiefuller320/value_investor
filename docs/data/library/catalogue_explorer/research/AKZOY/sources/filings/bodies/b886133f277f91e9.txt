(“AkzoNobel”) entered into a Merger Agreement (as amended by Amendment No. 1 to Merger Agreement, dated May 27, 2026, and Amendment No. 2 to Merger Agreement, dated July 23, 2026) with Axalta Coating Systems Ltd. (“Axalta”), pursuant to which, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth therein, AkzoNobel and Axalta have agreed to combine their respective businesses in a merger of equals (the “Merger”), with AkzoNobel (upon completion of the Merger, “MergeCo”) serving as the combined company’s listed parent. AkzoNobel today commenced a private offering of notes (the “Notes Offering”) by Akzo Nobel, Inc., a subsidiary of AkzoNobel (the “Notes Issuer”), the net proceeds of the Notes Offering are intended to be used for general corporate purposes and may also be used to refinance, from time to time, in whole or in part, (i) the term loans due December 20, 2029 issued and outstanding pursuant to the credit agreement, dated February 1, 2013, by and among Axalta Coating Systems Dutch Holding B.V., Axalta Coating Systems U.S. Holdings, Inc., Axalta Coating Systems U.S., Inc., Axalta, as borrowers, the lenders from time to time party thereto and Barclays Bank PLC, as administrative agent and collateral agent, as amended from time to time, and (ii) Axalta’s outstanding $500 million senior notes due 2027, (iii) Axalta’s outstanding $700 million senior notes due 2029 and/or (iv) Axalta’s outstanding $500 million senior notes due 2031, in each case following the completion of the Merger. AkzoNobel’s offering memorandum for the Notes Offering incorporates by reference unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information giving effect to the Merger and the related transactions, consisting of (i) the unaudited pro forma condensed combined balance sheet of MergeCo as of September 30, 2026, giving effect to the Merger and the related transactions as if they had been consummated on September 30, 2026, and the unaudited pro forma condensed combined statement of income of MergeCo for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, giving effect to the Merger and the related transactions as if they had been consummated on January 1 2025, in each case together with the notes thereto, and (ii) the unaudited pro forma condensed combined statement of income of MergeCo for the year ended December 31, 2025, giving effect to the Merger and the related transactions as if they had been consummated on January 1, 2025.
