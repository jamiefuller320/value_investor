For companies, if any, acquired in the immediately preceding fiscal year, each of these organic revenue growth measures reflects presentation of full year incremental revenue derived from such companies as if they were combined throughout the prior period. In addition, each of these organic revenue growth measures excludes prior period revenue associated with divested businesses, if any. The exclusion of the prior period revenue is to present the results of the divested businesses within the results of the combined company for the same period of time in both the prior and current periods.
