On May 29, 2026, Corporation (the “Company”) received a notice (the “Notice”) from The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“Nasdaq”) stating that because the Company had not yet filed its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended March 31, 2026 (the “Form 10-Q”), the Company is not in compliance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(c)(1). Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) requires listed companies to timely file all required periodic financial reports with the Securities and Exchange Commission. The Notice has no immediate effect on the listing of the Company’s common stock on The NASDAQ Capital Market. The Company continues to work diligently to file the Form 10-Q as promptly as practicable. If the Company is unable to file the Form 10-Q by July 28, 2026, the Company intends to submit to Nasdaq a plan to regain compliance with the Nasdaq Listing Rules. On June 3, 2026, the Company issued a press release announcing the late filing. The press release issued by the Company in connection with the announcement is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1.
