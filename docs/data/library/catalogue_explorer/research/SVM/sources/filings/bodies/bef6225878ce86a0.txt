("Silvercorp" or the "Company") (TSX: SVM) (NYSE American: SVM) reports production and sales figures for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026 ("Q2 Fiscal 2027"). Silvercorp expects to release its Q2 Fiscal 2027 unaudited interim financial results on Thursday, November 5, 2026, after market close. Q2 Fiscal 2027 Operational Highlights: • During the quarter, 213,320 tonnes of ore were processed at the Ying Mining District and produced 0.9 million ounces of silver, 1,371 ounces of gold, or 1.0 million ounces of silver equivalent, 7.6 million pounds of lead, and 0.7 million pounds of zinc, representing decreases of 41%, 34%, 42%, 41%, and 51% in silver, gold, silver equivalent, lead and zinc, respectively, over the same quarter last year ("Q2 Fiscal 2026"), in line with an expected 40-50% impact previously disclosed on June 29, 2026. Production resumed at the Ying Mining District and ended the quarter at a 90% operating rate; • Revenue of $72.1 million, representing a decrease of 13% over Q2 Fiscal 2026 due to lower production, was partially offset by an 82% higher average realized silver price of $61.83 per ounce and a 32% higher gold price of $3,955 per ounce; • Safety Compliance Progressing: Major safety improvement works at the Ying Mining District have been substantially completed, inspected by the government and approved to resume operations, which include tunnel enlargement and support, equipment upgrades, replacing all power cables with , cables, and expanding the "Six Major Safety Systems" to all tunnels. Approximately $13.4 million were incurred in Q2 Fiscal 2027 for these improvement works. The company is also working on permitting of environment, safety and land, to build several waste rock dumps at the Ying Mining district to accommodate material from extensive tunneling, as the aggregate plant cannot process and sell all the waste rock from the tunnels. Meanwhile the GC Mine remains under suspension pending a new mine development design approval and additional work completed; • Mill Expansion on Track: Construction of the No. 3 mill at the Ying Mining District is advancing as planned, with most of the drawings having been completed and delivered to the construction team, and the foundations for the machine shop, crushed ore silo, and main plant have been completed, welding for the elevated water tank is finished, and excavation for the tailings thickener is currently underway. Mine Development & Construction Updates: • Kuanping mine construction continued, with 1,507 metres ("m") of ramp development, 462 m of exploration tunneling and 4,759 m of drilling completed; • El Domo mine construction was impacted by persistent heavy rainfall throughout Q2 Fiscal 2027, which is typically dry season, but due to the effects of this year's Super El Niño. During the quarter, approximately 1,009,239 cubic metres of excavation and were completed, despite precipitation of 620 millimetres - 489% higher than the average. Not only were construction works suspended during 77 days of rain, but many finished engineering works were repeatedly damaged by the heavy rainfall and required repeated repairing. This has especially impacted the construction of the Tailing Storage Facility (TSF), as all surface water runs to the low point of the initial dam site. Significant efforts were put into the water management systems, such as building more water drainage and diversion systems, beyond the original engineering design. Civil works at the Process Plant are progressing well as this site is protected by the Northeast Water Channel. The foundations for the plant buildings and main equipment were completed, waiting for installation. The structural steel for the Process Plant has been shipped from the factory, and the first batch of major long equipment has also arrived in the Ecuador port. Construction of a Substation at the El Domo site and the external power line to connect to the power grid has also commenced, while 10 of 17 MW capacity are ready to be shipped to site as an independent power supply, as required by Ecuador regulations. has also been impacted by heavy rainfall and progressed across three working areas, with a fleet of ten 60-tonne haul trucks and twenty to thirty 30-tonne trucks from local community members; • Chaarat ZAAV project: A camp with 110 beds for our site management team and 290 beds for contractors with winter heating facilities has been constructed and is in use. Since mid May 2026, CRCC19 has advanced 1,880 m of a 15-metre wide access road and has reached the Tulkubash ore body, and pit has commenced. Site preparation, surface clearing and earthworks for the construction of the Heap Leach Pad are completed by CRCC19, with 0.93 million cubic metres of material being cut and removed under the supervision of independent QA/QC agents, SLR Engineering and . The site is now largely ready for drainage and liner installation and facility construction, scheduled for the Spring of 2027 after completion of Kyrgyz local adaptation and legalization of the 2021 Ausenco design and after selection of the qualified contractors. Preliminary design for the ADR and crushing plants by BGRIMM, assisted by a Kyrgyzstan engineering firm to comply with Kyrgyz standards, has been completed. The equipment tender bids for the ADR and crushing plants are in progress. GC Mine to Remain Suspended Until New Mine Development Design Approval The GC Mine processed 477 tonnes of stockpiled ore, and produced 0.01 million ounces of silver, 0.2 million pounds of lead and 0.1 million pounds of zinc. Mining activities have been suspended since late June 2026 as the company has not updated its 2014 technical design for mining operation to reflect changes in geology and tunneling advancement. Safety inspections by the government have been rigidly focused on any deviation of the actual development from the original mine design or any updated mine design. Accordingly, resumption of production at the GC Mine is pending the completion of an updated mine development design by a qualified Chinese engineering firm, and then a completed filing with the National Safety Production Administration Bureau and Department of Emergency Management of Guangdong Province. Once the filing is accepted by government authorities, the GC Mine will be able to construct production facilities, with the Phase 1 intended to upgrade the existing mining areas, and the Phase 2 to develop new, deeper levels below -300 m elevation. Silvercorp has engaged Changsha Institute of Mining Research Co., Ltd. to update the mine development plan design, including the safety facilities, which is expected to be completed by the end of December 2026 and then approved by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Emergency Management in March 2027. It may take 6-8 months to upgrade the Phase 1 development and to get the mine to resume production by November of 2027. As the GC Mine contributes less than 10% to total revenue, the Company does not expect the suspension to have a material impact on its overall operations or financial performance during this period. Most technical personnel at GC have been reassigned to operations at the Ying Mining District, the BYP project permitting process, and construction of the El Domo and Condor projects in Ecuador and the Chaarat project in Kyrgyzstan.
