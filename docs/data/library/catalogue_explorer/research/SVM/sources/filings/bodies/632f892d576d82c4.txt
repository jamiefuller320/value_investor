("Silvercorp" or the "Company") (TSX: SVM) (NYSE American: SVM) (the "Company") announces an update for its budget and construction schedule for the construction of the El Domo Project (the "Project"). The construction budget for the Project has been updated to $298.2 million, an increase of $14.6 million compared to the $283.6 million estimate dated February 4, 2026 (for reference, the 2021 Feasibility Study Budget was $248 million). The main items of increase or decrease are as follows: 1. Diesel fuel adjustment : Ecuador eliminated diesel subsidies , resulting in the diesel price increasing from under $3/gallon in December 2024 to $5.64/gallon in October 2026, an increase of $2.7/gallon. As a result, the fuel adjustment for Package #1 is $3.6 million and for Package #2 is $4.7 million, totaling $8.3 million for remaining construction. 2. Enhancement work for Water Management Related Infrastructure : Due to the heavy rainfall, additional work to upgrade the designs and enhance the construction for reinforcement results in $3.5 million, plus site cleaning costs of $1.2 million, for a $4.7 million budget addition. This is added to the Package #1 TSF budget. 3. Equipment and Facilities for Site Power Supply: $2.4 million increase mainly due to the additional demand for the power cables and power distribution facilities based on the detailed engineering design. 4. Construction on Processing Plant: $3.7 million increase according to actual bill of quantities in the Package #3 construction contract. 5. New Items: a. Two cofferdams and pipes beneath the TSF Starter Dam to drain the water from the impoundment area during the Construction Period, plus cemented roadside ditches are required to divert water from the construction site, at a cost close to $2 million. This is added to the Package #1 TSF budget. b. Two new Reactive Power Compensators (SVG) are estimated at $2 million plus other power supply related items of $1.3 million for a total of $3.3 million. 6. Savings of $9.8 million were mainly achieved through: (i) using rocks from the process plant site preparation for haul road construction instead of purchasing for $1.8 million; (ii) eliminating the West Water Channel for $1.5 million, as the explosives magazine site has effectively diverted surface water, (iii) reducing the permanent camp size for $0.7 million as the Construction Camp currently in use is satisfactory for use, and (iv) reducing of contingency for $2.7 million. Due to the extended rainfall experienced at site caused by Super El Nino, the El Domo completion date is expected to be delayed by four months. The Company is now forecasting that the El Domo Project is scheduled to be in production by November 1 st , 2027.
