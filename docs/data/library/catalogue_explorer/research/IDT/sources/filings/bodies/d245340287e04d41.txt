Conversely, Wednesday is typically the day of the week on which IDT’s cash balance is at its highest level, after IDT collects cash from digital processors and retailers for all of the remittances originated during the preceding weekend but before the new weekly cycle of prefunding disbursements for the upcoming weekend begins again. This weekly cycle constitutes a significant working capital use of the Company’s cash, and, as such, the day of the week on which the quarter ends can have significant impact on the cash balance reported at the balance sheet date.
