NOTES TO CONDENSED CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS We manage our exposure to fluctuations in interest rates on our debt by entering into interest rate swap agreements to convert a portion of our debt to a fixed rate. On June 30, 2025, we entered into a interest rate swap agreement (the "2025 Swap"). The 2025 Swap converts the component of the interest rate to 3.759 %. As of August 31, 2026, the 2025 Swap resulted in a total fixed rate of 5.509 %. The 2025 Swap has a notional amount of $ 290.0 million and a maturity date of June 30, 2027. The objective of the 2025 Swap is to eliminate the variability of cash flows in interest payments attributable to changes in benchmark SOFR interest rates. The hedged risk is the interest rate risk exposure to changes in interest payments, attributable to changes in benchmark SOFR interest rates over the interest rate swap term. The changes in cash flows of the interest rate swap are expected to exactly offset changes in cash flows of the debt. We designated the 2025 Swap as a cash flow hedge at inception. Cash settlements, in the form of cash payments or cash receipts, of the 2025 Swap are recognized in "Interest expense, net" in the consolidated statement of operations. At August 31, 2026, changes in fair value attributable to the effective portion of the 2025 Swap were included on the consolidated balance sheets in "Accumulated other comprehensive loss." For derivative instruments that qualify for hedge accounting treatment, the fair value is recognized on our consolidated balance sheets as derivative assets or liabilities with offsetting changes in fair value recognized in accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) until reclassified into earnings when the related interest expense is recognized. During the six months ended August 31, 2026, we reclassified $ 0.14 million before income tax, or $ 0.11 million net of tax, from accumulated other comprehensive income to earnings for the 2025 Swap. 10. Fair Value Measurements Recurring Fair Value Measurements Fair value is the price that would be received to sell an asset or paid to transfer a liability in an orderly transaction between market participants. In accordance with ASC 820, Fair Value Measurement ("ASC 820"), certain of our assets and liabilities, which are carried at fair value, are classified in one of the following three categories: • Level 1: Quoted market prices in active markets for identical assets or liabilities; • Level 2: Observable inputs, other than Level 1, or unobservable inputs corroborated by market data; or, • Level 3: Unobservable inputs that are not corroborated by market data and reflect the Company’s own assumptions. The carrying amount of our financial instruments (cash and cash equivalents, accounts receivable, accounts payable and accrued liabilities) approximates the fair value of these instruments based upon either their nature or their variable market rate of interest.
