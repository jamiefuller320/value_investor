.1 2 ea031230601ex99-1.htm PRESS RELEASE DATED NOVEMBER 16, 2026 Exhibit 99.1 Ticketplus Surpasses 2026 Expansion Target with Launch in Bolivia, Its 13th Active Market Second New Market This Month; with Bolivia, Ticketplus Now Operates in Nine of South America’s 12 Countries SANTIAGO, Chile, November 16, 2026 -- Ticketplus Ltd. (“Ticketplus” or the “Company”) (NYSE American: TP), a technology company providing infrastructure that powers live entertainment across Latin America, today announced the launch of its platform in Bolivia, marking the Company’s 13 th active market and its second new market this month, following the Company’s entry into Brazil on November 5. With the addition of Bolivia, Ticketplus has surpassed its previously announced 2026 target of at least 12 active markets. “We set a goal of entering at least one new market by , and in November alone, we delivered two,” said Yethro Dinamarca, Chairman of Ticketplus. “We remain focused on disciplined execution against the goals we set, and we intend to continue building Ticketplus with that same approach. Our entry into Brazil established our presence in the region’s largest market, while Bolivia further strengthens our footprint at the heart of South America. We believe each new market increases the value of our platform, creating greater opportunities for the local operators we serve today and those we expect to welcome in the future.” Highlights ● Exceeded 2026 expansion target ahead of schedule. Ticketplus’s footprint has expanded from 11 active markets as of September 30 to 13 active markets following its launches in Brazil and Bolivia, surpassing its target more than a month before . ● Growing scale. In the first nine months of 2026, the platform processed more than 62,400 events and Total Platform Sales (GMV) of US$457.8 million, up 131.5% . ● Expanded presence across South America. Ticketplus now operates in nine of South America’s 12 countries, reaching markets with a combined population of more than 400 million people, or over 90% of the continent’s approximately 435 million residents, according to 2024 World Bank data. ● A connected regional footprint. Bolivia borders Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay and Peru, all five of which are already Ticketplus markets. With Bolivia, the Company’s presence in South America is now contiguous, from Colombia to Argentina. ● Built for Bolivia. The platform supports QR code payments, the most widely used method for mobile payments in the country: according to the Central Bank of Bolivia’s July 2026 Financial Stability Report, 85 of every 100 payments made through mobile banking or mobile wallets in the first half of 2026 used QR codes. It also supports Bolivian invoicing requirements and regulations. Through the Company’s model, local event operators can leverage the Company’s technology and infrastructure while maintaining their own brands and customer relationships. ● A platform for events of every size. This launch introduces the Ticketplus platform to Bolivia for the first time, and it is available, through the Company’s model, to the entire market: from international tour promoters to local producers, venues and event organizers. Live entertainment in South America crosses borders, and with Bolivia, a promoter touring the Southern Cone and the Andes can now run show dates across nine South American countries on the same technology. At the same time, a Bolivian producer gains access to the same technology, with the same ticketing, payments, access control and data, under its own brand. About Ticketplus Ticketplus Ltd. (NYSE American: TP) is a technology company providing underlying infrastructure that powers live events across Latin America. Its proprietary, platform integrates ticketing, payments, access control and data analytics, enabling events of all sizes to operate on a unified technological foundation. The Company operates through a Full Operation model and a SaaS model in 13 markets: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, the Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Mexico, Paraguay, Peru, the United States and Uruguay. In the first nine months of 2026, the platform processed more than 62,400 events and Total Platform Sales (GMV) of US$457.8 million, up 131.5% . For more information, please visit the Ticketplus investor relations website at investors.ticketplus.com. Total Platform Sales (GMV) is an operating metric that represents the total value of all sales processed through the Company’s platform, including tickets and other products and services, before any deductions for fees, refunds, or commissions, and regardless of revenue recognition treatment.
