(“Ticketplus” or the “Company”) (NYSE American: TP), a technology company providing infrastructure that powers live entertainment across Latin America, today announced the official launch of its platform in Brazil, the region’s largest economy and most populous country. With the launch, Brazil becomes Ticketplus’s 12th active market, meeting the Company’s previously announced 2026 outlook of at least 12 active markets ahead of . Highlights ● 12 active markets, ahead of schedule. Brazil brings Ticketplus to the 12 active markets targeted in its 2026 outlook. ● Footprint across all six of Latin America’s largest economies. Ticketplus now operates in Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile and Peru. ● Built for Brazil. The platform runs in Portuguese, accepts cards and Pix through leading Brazilian payment providers, and is integrated with Brazilian invoicing and consumer protection requirements. ● model. Empowers Brazilian operators to run their businesses on Ticketplus technology, under their own brands. This launch marks the first time Ticketplus’s platform is available to Brazilian promoters, venues and ticketing operators. Serving as the underlying technology provider, Ticketplus provides solutions, from ticketing and payments to access control and data, while its partners keep full brand ownership and their direct relationships with their audiences. as the growth engine Brazil joins the model that is driving Ticketplus’s growth. During the first nine months of 2026, sales processed for partners more than tripled year over year and accounted for 57.7% of the platform’s Total Platform Sales (GMV). Each new market is added on top of the same technology already running across the region. Brazil, with a population of approximately 212 million, has more people than all of Ticketplus’s other South American markets combined, according to 2024 World Bank data. “When we reported our third quarter results, we said that expanding into new markets through our model was our priority for the rest of the year. Brazil is the first, and it is the largest market in the region,” said Chen, Chief Executive Officer of Ticketplus. “We adapted the platform to Brazil’s payments, invoicing, regulations and language, and it is now live. We believe Brazil represents one of the largest opportunities for our platform, and we are entering with the same technology that already powers events across the region.” About Ticketplus Ticketplus Ltd. (NYSE American: TP) is a technology company providing underlying infrastructure that powers live events across Latin America. Its proprietary, platform integrates ticketing, payments, access control and data analytics, enabling events of all sizes to run on a single technology foundation. The Company operates through a Full Operation model and a SaaS model in 12 markets: Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, the Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Mexico, Paraguay, Peru, the United States and Uruguay. In the first nine months of 2026, the platform processed more than 62,400 events and Total Platform Sales (GMV) of US$457.8 million, up 131.5% year over year.
