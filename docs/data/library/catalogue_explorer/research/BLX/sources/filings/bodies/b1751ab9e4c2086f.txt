Clawback Policy In 2023, the Bank adopted a clawback policy that complies with NYSE filed listing standards and SEC rules. Under the policy, if the Bank is required to prepare an accounting restatement due to material noncompliance with any financial reporting requirement under the U.S. federal securities laws, the Board shall, subject to certain exceptions, seek to recover “excess compensation” from each individual who is a Section 16 officer or was a Section 16 officer during the performance period for such compensation. For this purpose, excess compensation generally is the amount of compensation that is based on a financial performance measure that is in excess of the amount that otherwise would have been received had such compensation been determined based on restated amounts in the accounting restatement. The clawback policy applies to compensation for which the financial performance metric was attained during the period preceding the date of the accounting restatement.
