This ASU does not impact our consolidated financial position, results of operations or cash flows. The required disclosures are included in Note 17. Accounting Guidance and Disclosure Rules Issued But Not Yet Adopted In December 2023, the Financial Accounting Standards Board (“FASB”) issued ASU 2023-09, Income Taxes (Topic 740) , to enhance the transparency and decision usefulness of income tax disclosures, primarily related to the rate reconciliation and income taxes paid disclosures. This ASU does not impact our consolidated financial position, results of operations or cash flows. We are currently evaluating the impact of ASU 2024-03 on our financial statements.
