It designs, manufactures and markets systems and equipment to move, measure, control, dispense and spray fluid and coating materials. Management classifies the Company’s business into three reportable segments: Contractor, Industrial and Expansion Markets. Key strategies include developing and marketing new products, leveraging products and technologies into additional, growing markets, expanding distribution globally and completing strategic acquisitions that provide additional channels and technologies.
