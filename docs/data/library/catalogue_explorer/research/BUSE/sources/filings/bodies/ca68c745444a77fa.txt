Loans Held for Sale Loans held for sale are loans which Busey has the intent and ability to sell to investors and/or in the secondary mortgage market. A portfolio loan is reclassified to held for sale when management decides to sell it in the near term. As of September 30, 2026, loans held for sale included $ 3.0 million of mortgage loans and $ 65.1 million of commercial loans. Busey accounts for loans held for sale at LOCOM, which is determined on an individual loan basis. Loans held for sale are carried at amortized historical cost less loan and downward fair value adjustments, as may be applicable. Net unrealized losses, if any, are recognized through a valuation allowance by charges to income. When a portfolio loan is transferred to held for sale, any ACL previously recorded on the loan is reversed through the provision for credit losses, and the loan is transferred at the lower of amortized cost or fair value. Gain or loss on sale is recognized at the settlement date and is determined by the difference between the sales proceeds and the carrying amount, net of the value of any servicing asset retained. A pool of lender finance commercial loans acquired in the acquisition of CrossFirst was reclassified to held for sale during the three months ended September 30, 2026. The loans, which were included in the C&I and other commercial lending activity within the commercial loans portfolio segment, had an amortized cost basis of $ 65.4 million on the transfer date. As a result of the transfer, Busey recognized both: (1) a $ 0.7 million LOCOM adjustment (measured at the transfer date and updated as of September 30, 2026) as a reduction to other noninterest income, primarily attributable to the purchase premium assigned to these loans at acquisition; and (2) a $ 0.7 million release of allowance for credit losses as a reduction to provision expense.
