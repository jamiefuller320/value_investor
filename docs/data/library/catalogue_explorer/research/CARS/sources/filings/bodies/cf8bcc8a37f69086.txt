Cars.com Inc., d/b/a Cars Commerce Inc. (the "Company" or "Cars Commerce") is an technology company empowering the automotive industry. The Company simplifies everything about car buying and selling with powerful products, solutions and machine learning artificial intelligence technologies that span pretail, retail and activities – enabling more efficient and profitable retail operations. The Cars Commerce platform is organized around four brands: the flagship automotive marketplace and dealer reputation site Cars.com, digital retail technology and marketing services from Dealer Inspire, essential and appraisal technology from AccuTrade and exclusive media solutions from the Cars Commerce Media Network. UVs and Traffic are fundamental to our business. They are indicative of our consumer reach and the level of engagement consumers have with our platform. Traffic increases can result in increased impressions, clicks and other connections that we can ultimately monetize through our products and services. We define UVs in a given month as the number of distinct visitors that engage with our platform during that month. Visitors are identified when a user first visits an individual Cars.com property on an individual device/browser combination or installs one of our mobile apps on an individual device. If a visitor accesses more than one of our web properties or apps or uses more than one device or browser, each of those unique property/browser/app/device combinations counts toward the number of UVs.
