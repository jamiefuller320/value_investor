.1 2 q3productionresults.htm .1 Document PRESS RELEASE Aya Gold & Silver Reports Record Q3-2026 Production of 2.03 Million AgEq Ounces Montreal, Quebec, October 14, 2026 - Aya Gold & Silver Inc. (TSX: AYA; NASDAQ: AYA) (“Aya” or the “Company”) is pleased to announce record quarterly silver production at its Zgounder Silver Mine (“Zgounder”), as well as further advancement of its Boumadine pyrite reclaim operation in the Kingdom of Morocco for the period ended September 30, 2026. Q3-2026 Operational Summary • Record consolidated production of 2.03 million silver equivalent ounces ("Moz AgEq"), up 51% ("YoY") and up 21% ("QoQ"); including 0.54 Moz AgEq 1 from the Boumadine pyrite reclaim operation 2 up 186% QoQ. • Record Zgounder silver production of 1.50 Moz Ag, up 11% YoY. • Record processing rate of 4,096 tonnes per day (“tpd”), representing a 5% increase over Q2-2026. • Mining rate averaged 4,746 tpd , for the combined open pit and underground operations. • Silver recovery averaged 92.0% during the quarter. “ Q3 was another record quarter at Zgounder, with records in both silver production and mill throughput. This strong operational execution is a direct result of the optimization work our team has implemented at site," said Benoit La Salle, President & CEO. Reported silver production excludes 138,100 oz of silver cement which was not smelted as of . The mill processed a record of 376,863 tonnes ("t") of ore (4,096 tpd) in Q3-2026, up 23% YoY, at an average feed grade of 146 grams per tonne ("g/t") Ag. Mill availability was strong at 99.4% and metallurgical performance remained within expectations, with silver recovery of 92.0% for the quarter. Mill throughput continues to benefit from a crushing contractor who will remain on site until the planned crusher expansion is completed, expected early 2027. Mining operations achieved an average mining rate of 4,746 tpd at 143 g/t Ag, reflecting strong performance across both underground and operations. The mining rate reached 3,486 tpd with a strip ratio of 12, while the underground operations contributed 1,260 tpd. The construction of the second phase of the tailings storage facility ("TSF") was completed in early Q3-2026, as planned. The ore stockpile increased by 16% QoQ to 434,538 t, in line with the Company's objective of building a inventory to support the mine plan. Boumadine In late 2025, Aya began reclaiming and selling its historical pyrite stockpile at Boumadine. During Q3-2026, the operation produced 536,879 oz AgEq ( ratio of 68:1) 1 . Material reclaimed and crushed totaled 49,890 t at average grades of 151 g/t Ag and 2.61 g/t Au. Reclaiming operations are progressing as planned. Tonnes of pyrite reclaimed at Boumadine increased by 191% QoQ, reflecting a more normalized operating run rate, following disruptions in the first half of the year. The reclamation and sale of the historical pyrite stockpile at Boumadine, announced on November 19, 2025, is expected to be of limited duration, lasting approximately 20 to 24 months from that date, or until the stockpile is depleted. The overall Boumadine polymetallic project remains at the exploration and evaluation stage and is not in commercial production.
