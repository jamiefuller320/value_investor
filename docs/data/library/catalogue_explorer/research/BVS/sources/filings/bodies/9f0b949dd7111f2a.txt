The Company is a global medical device company focused on helping patients recover and live life to the fullest by relieving pain and addressing musculoskeletal challenges through a diverse portfolio of , innovative, and solutions. The Company is headquartered in Durham, North Carolina and has approximately 930 employees at March 29, 2025.
