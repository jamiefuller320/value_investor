.1 2 exhibit991q2f26newsrelease.htm EXHIBIT 99.1 Document Exhibit 99.1 The Scotts Company NEWS ScottsMiracle-Gro Reports Strong Second Quarter Results; Increase in Sales and Gross Margin Improvement Drive EPS Growth Net sales increased by 5% Gross margin rate improved by over 200 basis points Net leverage at 3.71x, down from prior year of 4.41x MARYSVILLE, Ohio, April 29, 2026 – The Scotts Company (NYSE: SMG), the leading marketer of branded consumer lawn and garden products in North America, today reported results for the second quarter ended March 28, 2026. “Our performance reflects progress on all our financial imperatives,” said Jim Hagedorn, chairman and CEO. “We continued our growth trajectory and delivered meaningful leverage ratio improvement, putting us in position for more shareholder friendly actions including the previously announced share repurchase program. At the same time, we are reinvesting in our consumer franchise with a focus on achieving our fiscal 2026 guidance that is foundational to our financial targets.” Mark Scheiwer, chief financial officer and chief accounting officer, added, “We delivered a strong second quarter, executing on net sales growth, gross margin expansion and other key financial priorities. We are driving profitability growth and improved free cash flow while making incremental investments in consumer activation and capital expenditures. Strong sales and POS momentum continued in April, further boosting our confidence in the outlook.” Fiscal 2026 Second Quarter Highlights • Net sales were $1.46 billion, an increase of 5% versus prior year. • GAAP and adjusted gross margin rate of 41.8% improved by 280 and 240 basis points over prior year, respectively. • GAAP net income from continuing operations of $4.46 per share and adjusted net income from continuing operations of $4.53 per share improved by 18 percent and 13 percent over prior year, respectively. • adjusted EBITDA of $437.4 million improved by 9 percent over prior year. • Net leverage of 3.71x improved 0.70x versus last year. Fiscal 2026 Outlook The fiscal 2026 guidance that has been reaffirmed by the Company includes: • U.S. Consumer net sales low growth • adjusted gross margin rate of at least 32% • adjusted net income per share from continuing operations of $4.15 to $4.35 • adjusted EBITDA mid growth • Free cash flow of $275 million, driving leverage ratio down to the high 3’s The Company will file a Form 8-K prior to the start of the conference call that will include financial results for the three and six months ended March 28, 2026.
