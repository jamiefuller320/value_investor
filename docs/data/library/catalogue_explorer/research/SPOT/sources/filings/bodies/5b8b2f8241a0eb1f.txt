Creators can generate recurring revenue from fans by offering exclusive content and experiences, with the flexibility to customize how they package and price their offerings. Announced that audiobooks on Spotify are going global, expanding from 22 markets to more than 180 by the end of 2026. With this expansion, listeners in new markets will gain access to more than 350,000 titles, including catalogs in Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Polish and more, spanning over 120 languages. Expanded the Spotify Partner Program to more than 35 new markets, giving eligible creators in Italy, Spain, Brazil, Mexico, Poland, Colombia and beyond the opportunity to earn revenue based on engagement. The expansion helps more creators around the world grow their audiences and find new ways to earn from their content on Spotify. Powering a more personalized listening experience with users in control Managed Accounts are now available to families in the U.S., U.K., Australia, France, Germany and the Netherlands, giving listeners 13 and under their own space to discover music and build their own listening experience, while parents and guardians stay in control of access. Launched Spotify integration with Muse to give listeners new ways to enjoy their favorite audio content through an AI agent conversation. By connecting Muse to Spotify, listeners can ask their agent to play music, save songs to their Library, build personalized playlists, find new books, podcasts and more. Taste Profile launched to Premium Subscribers in the U.S., giving users a new way to see how Spotify understands their unique taste across music, podcasts and audiobooks. Users can review and edit Spotify’s view of their taste, giving them more transparency and control over their experience while helping shape what gets recommended next on their Home feed. variance Our guidance incorporates the impact of Social Charges on compensation, the vast majority of which appear in Operating Expenses. The amount of Social Charges we accrue for and ultimately pay can be volatile, as they are tied to the value of our share price. Since we do not forecast stock price changes in our guidance, meaningful movements in our stock price over the course of a quarter can lead to meaningful changes in Social Charges. As an example, at the Q3 close, our stock price was $487.38. In Q3, a 10% increase or decrease in our stock price compared to the price would have an approximate +/- €20M impact on Social Charges.
