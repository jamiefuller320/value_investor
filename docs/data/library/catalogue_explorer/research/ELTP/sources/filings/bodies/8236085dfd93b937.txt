There are no significant segment expenses or other segment items that are separately provided to the CODM beyond research and development and general and administrative expenses. The CODM does not receive segment level information related to depreciation, amortization, capital expenditures, or other items, and therefore such items are excluded. The ANDA segment follows the same accounting policies used in the preparation of the Company’s consolidated financial statements.
