Reasons for and Benefits of the Transaction The Company is an investment holding company, and its subsidiaries are principally engaged in restaurant operations, delivery business, and the sale of condiment products and food ingredients in overseas markets outside Greater China. Yihai Group is principally engaged in the research and development, manufacturing, distribution, and sale of hot pot condiments, compound condiments, and convenient food products. Being the supplier of SUPER HI Customized Products and General Products for the Group, Yihai Group has established a , stable and mutually beneficial business relationship with the Group. The Directors consider that the continuation of such relationship is beneficial for a stable operation and business expansion of the Group and in the interests of the Company and the Shareholders as a whole. – 13 – LETTER FROM THE BOARD The Company believes that the Second Renewed Yihai Master Purchase Agreement does not constitute undue reliance on Yihai Group or the Controlling Shareholders on the following grounds: (1) and mutually beneficial relationship There has been a continued , stable and mutually beneficial business relationship between the Group and Yihai Group. Yihai Group has been the supplier of the SUPER HI Customized Products and General Products of the Group since 2012. The mass production capabilities of Yihai Group have ensured the stable supply of products that are price competitive, of high quality and compliant with stringent food safety standards to the Group, while the and stable business relationship enables Yihai Group to grow alongside the Group’s expansion. In addition, Yihai Group has every incentive to protect the proprietary and trade secrets due to the importance of the Group to its business, and the research teams of both Yihai Group and the Group have been working closely to continuously upgrade and explore hot pot soup flavoring formulae. To maintain a mutually beneficial relationship is in the best interest of the shareholders of both Yihai Group and the Group. (2) Importance of the Group to Yihai Group The purchase amount by the Group from Yihai Group for each of the two years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026 accounted for at least 20% of the total revenue of Yihai Group’s overseas business for the relevant period, being approximately US$16.6 million, US$19.1 million and US$10.3 million, respectively. Meanwhile, in 2024, 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026, the procurement from Yihai Group, which comprised SUPER HI Customized Products, represented approximately 65.27%, 67.71% and 67.65%, respectively, of the Group’s total soup base costs, and accordingly represented the majority of such costs, reflecting the stable and substantial demand that the Group provides to Yihai Group. Such percentages do not take into account food ingredients which are used primarily in menu dishes and, to a lesser extent, in soup bases, as those ingredients generally represent only a small portion of the Group’s total soup base costs. Given the leading market position of the Group in the sector in the overseas market and the contribution of the Group to the overseas business of Yihai Group, the Company considers that Yihai Group has an incentive to deliver products at reasonable prices. – 14 – LETTER FROM THE BOARD (3) Sourcing from alternative suppliers The formulae for the SUPER HI Customized Products are owned by the Group. The Group has been identifying alternative suppliers for the SUPER HI Customized Products in case the supply from Yihai Group cannot meet the requirements and demand of the Group. The Company may consider engaging these alternative local third party suppliers to provide the SUPER HI Customized Products if the local regulatory authorities do not allow the import of certain materials used in these products from Yihai. The Company may also take into account other factors, including delivery cost and delivery time, in determining whether to engage these alternative local suppliers. When the Company wishes to engage third party suppliers, the Company will enter into negotiations with Yihai Group in advance. In 2024, 2025 and up to the Latest Practicable Date, the Group had not engaged any local supplier without the prior consent of Yihai Group, and there was no dispute, claim or other legal proceeding between the Company and Yihai Group in relation to this matter. The Group has entered into agreements with certain Independent Third Party suppliers of comparable soup base products, who have been added into the “Qualified Supplier List” maintained for the purpose of its business, to procure specific types of soup base products which Yihai Group is currently unable to manufacture. Such alternative suppliers are able to supply similar soup base products of comparable quality and pricing terms as those offered by Yihai Group. As the Group owns the formulae, which are tailored to the local taste and different from those of Haidilao International Holding Ltd., and the raw materials for the SUPER HI Customized Products can be readily sourced, the Company believes that in the unlikely event that Yihai Group ceases or is unable to supply to the Group, the Group would be able to source from alternative suppliers the SUPER HI Customized Products that meet its requirements and demand within a reasonable period of time, and the business of the Group would not be adversely affected. The Company has also identified one or two alternative suppliers for each of the jurisdictions where the Group has operations, who could manufacture the SUPER HI Customized Products that meet the requirements of the Group and with comparable pricing terms. When selecting the alternative suppliers, the Group may consider those and reliable companies with proven business records, high brand recognition and good reputation. The Group will also request such suppliers to undertake to the Company under the relevant agreements that they will use best commercial efforts to (i) keep confidential the Condiments Formulae, and (ii) unless with the Company’s written consent, refrain from selling products that use these formulae to any other third parties. They will also be requested to provide indemnity to the Company if any loss is incurred as a result of their leakage of the Condiments Formulae. Considering the influence of its brand, the scale of its business and the agreements, it is reasonably expected that those third party suppliers have every incentive to protect the proprietary and trade secrets and will not recklessly breach the contractual obligations under the agreements so as to maintain a mutually beneficial relationship with the Group. The Company will take various factors, including the qualifications, the operation history as well as the brand and reputation of the suppliers, the manufacturing capabilities, the pricing terms, the turnaround time, the historical cooperation records, etc., into consideration to decide whether the Company will engage Yihai or other alternative suppliers. – 15 – LETTER FROM THE BOARD Under the Second Renewed Yihai Master Purchase Agreement, Yihai Group is the supplier of the General Products to the Group for display and sale to consumers in the restaurants of the Group and on the online platforms of the Group. (4) Fair and reasonable pricing terms Under the Second Renewed Yihai Master Purchase Agreement, the pricing of SUPER HI Customized Products and General Products is determined by fair and arm’s length negotiation with reference to historical transaction prices, the relevant cost structure (including raw material costs and selling and administrative expenses), Yihai Group’s net profit margin level in comparable transactions with Independent Third Parties, and market prices of comparable products in transactions with other Independent Third Parties. Any adjustment requires the mutual agreement of the parties, and the final transaction terms will, where comparable transactions are available, be no less favourable to the Group than terms available from Independent Third Parties. The Group will use objective comparison criteria including product specifications, quality and requirements, order volume, delivery terms, geographic market, payment terms and timing. Where no directly comparable supplier or product is available, the Group will use a documented method or another reasonable alternative method, such as a benchmarked price or price adjusted for documented cost changes, subject to review and approval.
