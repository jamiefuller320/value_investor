Explanatory Note On May 28, 2025, TAT Technologies Ltd. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing a proposed underwritten public offering of 4,150,000 of its ordinary shares. In connection with the offering, TAT and the Selling Shareholders intend to grant the underwriters an option to purchase up to an additional 242,298 and 380,202 ordinary shares, respectively, at the public offering price less the underwriting discount and commissions.
