Announces Direct Listing of Common Stock on Nasdaq Shares expected to begin trading on October 29, 2026, under the ticker symbol “GBC.” ATLANTA, Georgia, October 27, 2026 — Georgia Banking Company, Inc. (“GBC” or the “Company”), the holding company for Georgia Banking Company (the “Bank”), today announced that its common stock has been approved for direct listing on the Nasdaq Capital Market® (“Nasdaq”) and is expected to begin trading on Thursday, October 29, 2026, under the ticker symbol “GBC.” No new shares will be issued or sold by the Company in connection with the listing. The listing is being made in connection with an effective registration statement filed on Form S-1, as amended (the “Registration Statement”), with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”). The Registration Statement covers the potential resale from time to time of up to 7,074,016 shares of the Company's common stock by the selling shareholders named in the prospectus. The Company’s common stock has been registered under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), and GBC is subject to the reporting requirements of the Exchange Act and will file periodic reports with the SEC, including annual reports on Form 10-K and quarterly reports on Form 10-Q. “Listing on Nasdaq is a natural next step in the growth of Georgia Banking Company,” said Bartow Morgan, Jr., Chief Executive Officer of GBC and the Bank. “Over the past five years we have grown significantly, including through mergers with Primary Bancshares Corporation and Tandem Bancorp, and welcomed a strong group of institutional investors alongside our local shareholders. We believe the public listing will give our shareholders greater liquidity and transparency and may broaden our investor base as we continue to increase our visibility building the bank of choice for growing companies across Atlanta.” Performance Trust Capital Partners, LLC is acting as financial advisor to the Company in connection with the direct listing, and Troutman Pepper Locke LLP is serving as legal counsel to the Company. 1 The Company has filed the Registration Statement (including a prospectus) with the SEC for the offering to which this press release relates. About Georgia Banking Company, Inc. Georgia Banking Company is a financial institution headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and a wholly owned subsidiary of Georgia Banking Company, Inc. As of September 30, 2026, the bank had approximately $3.1 billion in total assets. Under the leadership of CEO Bartow Morgan, Jr., a Georgia banker, GBC has established itself as a trusted partner to businesses, professionals, and families across its markets.
