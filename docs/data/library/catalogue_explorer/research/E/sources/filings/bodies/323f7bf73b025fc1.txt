Eni S.p.A. /s/ Giulia Saba Name: Giulia Saba Title: Head of Corporate Secretary’s Staff Office Date: April 2, 2026 Eni: Board of Directors approves bond issue Rome, 2 April 2026 – Eni's Board of Directors, chaired by Giuseppe Zafarana, today approved the possible issuance of one or more bonds, to be placed with institutional investors according to market condition, with a value up to a maximum aggregate amount of 10 billion euro, or its equivalent in other currencies, to be issued in one or more tranches by 31 March 2028. The bonds, if issued, will enable to maintain Eni’s financial structure and will be used for Eni’s general corporate purposes. The bonds may be listed on one or more regulated markets.
