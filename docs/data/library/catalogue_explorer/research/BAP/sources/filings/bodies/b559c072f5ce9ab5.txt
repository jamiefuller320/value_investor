Update Key Rating Drivers IDRs Driven by Those of Main Subsidiary: Credicorp Ltd.’s Issuer Default Ratings (IDRs) are driven primarily by the IDRs of its main subsidiary, BCP, which boasts a strong business and financial profile. Credicorp’s IDR of ‘BBB’ with a Stable Rating Outlook is equalized to BCP’s rating, mainly due to Credicorp’s low double leverage and strong liquidity management. Low Double Leverage: Credicorp’s double leverage was low at 99.2% at YE24; this compares to the 2021-2024 average of 100.4%. The bank holding company has a long track record of significant dividend flows (particularly from BCP), which provide the majority of its liquidity. Strong Corporate Strategy: Credicorp is a holding company that maintains an integrated business platform comprising leading Peruvian and Bolivian banking, insurance, pension, and asset and wealth management companies, as well as entities in the microfinance (SME) sector in Peru and Colombia. Consistent Performance : Credicorp’s capital structure benefits from its subsidiaries’ sound performance, which allows it to maintain consistent dividend flows. Consolidated profitability at YE24 improved, benefiting from loan portfolio growth and asset quality improvement, a stronger net interest margin primarily due to an increase in deposits, and significant gains from trading. Senior Debt Ratings: The senior global debt rating is at the same level as Credicorp’s IDRs of ‘BBB’, as the likelihood of default on the notes is the same as that of Credicorp.
