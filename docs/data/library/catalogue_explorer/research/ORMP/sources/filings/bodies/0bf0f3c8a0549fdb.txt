Alpha DaRT™ Platform and Technology Alpha Tau’s Alpha DaRT platform is designed to deliver highly localized alpha radiation through intratumoral insertion of impregnated sources into solid tumors. When the radium decays, its daughters are released and disperse while emitting alpha particles aimed at destroying tumor cells while sparing surrounding healthy tissue. This approach potentially offers a novel treatment solution for patients with otherwise cancers where conventional external beam radiation may be limited. 47 Clinical Development Progress Alpha Tau is currently conducting an extensive clinical program with five concurrent trials in the United States, alongside additional trials in France, Italy, Israel, Japan, and planned studies in the UK. In October 2026, Alpha Tau also signed a memorandum of understanding with Samsung Medical Center in Seoul, South Korea, regarding a potential clinical trial of Alpha DaRT in pancreatic cancer patients, which would be subject to regulatory and ethics committee approvals. Alpha Tau’s U.S. clinical trials are summarized below: ● ReSTART Pivotal Trial (Recurrent SCC Treatment with Alpha DaRT Radiation Therapy): A pivotal study in patients with recurrent cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (cSCC), the second most common form of skin cancer. Alpha Tau has begun submitting modules of its Modular Approval (PMA) application to the FDA and has finished treating patients in its ReSTART pivotal trial, with data from the trial expected in late 2026 or early 2027. ● IMPACT Study (Intratumoral Pancreatic Alpha Combination Trial): A pilot study combining Alpha DaRT with chemotherapy in patients with newly diagnosed unresectable locally advanced or metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma. In a pooled analysis of its trials in Canada and Israel presented at DDW 2026, Alpha DaRT showed a 100% local disease control rate with a favorable safety profile, and a pooled analysis of three Phase I/II studies presented at the 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting reported median overall survival longer than historical benchmarks across all examined , with no deaths. The FDA approved an IDE supplement adding a second regimen alongside mFOLFIRINOX and expanding enrollment. Alpha Tau completed enrollment on August 31, 2026, with 48 patients at 10 clinical centers in the United States, Canada and Israel. Initial data are targeted for early 2027 and are intended to inform the design of a planned pivotal study. ● GBM Feasibility Study : A study in patients with recurrent glioblastoma multiforme (GBM), a highly aggressive malignant brain tumor. Alpha Tau treated the first patient in its REGAIN (Recurrent Glioblastoma Intratumoral Therapy) trial at Ohio State University’s Comprehensive Cancer Center. Interim results from the first three patients, treated between December 2025 and March 2026, demonstrated a 100% local disease control rate and a 67% complete response rate under Response Assessment in (RANO) criteria, with a single associated grade 3 serious adverse event that resolved and no local or distant recurrences as of the data .
