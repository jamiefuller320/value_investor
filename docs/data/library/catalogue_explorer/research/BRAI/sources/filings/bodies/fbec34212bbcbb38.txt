The Company has launched Force AI , a unified orchestration layer that facilitates customer operations on any leading large language model (LLM) without vendor — meaning clients are not tied to a single AI provider and can switch models as cost, performance or regulatory needs change. “Enterprises need AI that works across every channel, every agent, and every conversation, on the model they trust and in an environment they control,” said Natraj Balasubramanian, Braiin Founder and CEO. “RADIUS is the engine behind our CX business, and Force AI is how we scale it. With Force AI, our clients can bring OpenAI, Claude, Gemini or their own models into production contact center operations with minimal headaches and compliance already built in.” The expansion makes RADIUS a single AI command center for the full customer interaction lifecycle. It is built on Force AI, a unified AI orchestration layer that supports multiple and LLMs. Force AI powers three product lines: AI Engage , an agentic AI layer whose agents serve customers autonomously; AI Assist , a for human agents; and AI Insight , which analyzes every interaction for intelligence. Together they let enterprises build secure, scalable, and customer experiences. The Force AI launch combines three essential and competencies: ● A unified AI capability suite across channels: voice, chat, messaging, email, social and video ● An open integration architecture spanning OpenAI, Anthropic’s Claude, Google Gemini, Azure OpenAI and bespoke AI models ● Production deployments with enterprise and clients across India, Southeast Asia and other international markets According to Grand View Research , the global contact center software market is estimated at USD 56.9 billion in 2026 and is projected to reach USD 227.6 billion by 2033, growing at a CAGR of 21.9%. The RADIUS expansion positions Braiin to compete for a larger share of this market. RADIUS already operates at production scale, having handled more than 4.5 million customer interactions with 99.9% uptime in the twelve months ended March 31, 2026.
