Braiin’s financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June; FY2026 covers the twelve months from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. FY2026 was Braiin’s first year as a consolidated group. As reported in the Company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F and audited consolidated financial statements for FY2026 filed with the SEC on 12 November 2026, Braiin’s FY2026 revenue was US$25.9 million, reflecting the contribution of the VIS, Vega and Connect Simple businesses from their acquisition on 12 February 2026 to 30 June 2026 (approximately 4.5 months). On a pro forma basis, as presented in the Form 20-F, combined revenue of the acquired businesses was US$78.3 million and pro forma Adjusted EBITDA* was US$0.21 million. The cost rationalization initiatives currently underway are not captured in pro forma Adjusted EBITDA*. To illustrate the benefits of these initiatives, management separately prepared a scenario, which is not included in the Form 20-F, that incorporates the assumed effect of remuneration savings and other operating improvements; on that basis, illustrative pro forma Adjusted EBITDA* was US$5.47 million. Other highlights from the year, which included the Company’s listing on Nasdaq, were: ● Financial performance: reported FY2026 revenue of US$25.9 million and loss for the year of US$24.7 million (mainly on account of and cash listing charges, applied against a revenue base of approximately 4.5 months rather than a full year); combined pro forma revenue of the acquired businesses of US$78.3 million for FY2026, compared with US$73.4 million for FY2025; and illustrative pro forma Adjusted EBITDA* of US$5.47 million, an unaudited scenario based on estimated earnings and assumed remuneration savings and operating improvements. A full reconciliation from loss for the year, and important qualifications, are set out under “ Financial Measures” below. ● Balance sheet: cash of US$5.7 million and net assets of US$210.1 million at 30 June 2026. ● Workforce: the Group had 728 employees and 64 consultants and contractors (792 in total) at 30 June 2026; there was no significant change in the Group’s employee headcount in FY2026 compared with FY2025. ● Nasdaq listing: Braiin’s ordinary shares commenced trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker “BRAI” on 12 February 2026, the same day on which the Company completed the acquisitions of the VIS group, the Vega group and Connect Simple Pty Ltd, providing the Company with access to U.S. capital markets and a currency to execute its acquisition-led growth strategy. ● CX / Agentic AI: Braiin continued to build out its agentic AI customer experience capability, anchored by two proprietary assets. The first is its RADIUS CCaaS ( ) platform, built around Human+AI orchestration: AI agents handle routine, tasks, while human agents, supported by AI , manage escalations, interactions and complex workflows. The second is its patented Flamingo AI machine learning technology, which enables , bespoke AI solutions for clients. Together, these position the Company to execute its Human+AI CX strategy. ● PropTech / LivTech: Braiin built out its UK residential lif ecycle (“LivTech”) platform through a series of strategic partnerships: ○ an exclusive strategic partnership for 4.5 million homes in the UK, providing exclusive access to Tenancy Deposit Scheme (TDS) consumers and embedding distribution within tenancy onboarding; ○ a partnership with Switchcraft, a leading UK switching infrastructure provider, to embed utility, broadband and telecom switching into residential workflows (May 2026); and ○ the integration of ClearBank’s embedded banking infrastructure, adding a payments and settlement layer (June 2026). The Company also launched in New Zealand (May 2026). Subsequent to year end, Braiin partnered with Muval to extend its agentic AI relocation offering into the UK and US (July 2026). ● AgTech: Braiin’s AgTech business, which operates across India, Sri Lanka and Australia, continued to gain traction, supported by a binding contract base of approximately US$34.3 million in Sri Lanka. ● Intellectual property: Braiin’s IP portfolio is anchored by Flamingo AI (U.S. Patent No. 10,650,818) and the proprietary RADIUS platform. Subsequent to year end, the Company filed ten (10) U.S. Copyright Office applications covering AI methods and systems for UAV/drone applications. ● Acquisition strategy: Braiin signed a term sheet to acquire a 50.1% majority stake in Cumbria Capital Ltd, the parent company of Home.cc, a LivTech platform in the UK. Completion remains subject to due diligence, definitive agreements and applicable approvals. Braiin’s dedicated M&A and Strategy team has developed a disciplined, acquisition playbook built around five core levers: expanding product offerings, widening geographic coverage, deepening technology capabilities, diversifying across sectors, and creating value by driving AI transformation within traditional IT companies. Over the past seven months the team has evaluated more than 50 relevant acquisition opportunities with multiple active engagements, ranging from US$5 million to US$300 million in revenue. ● Future growth: Braiin’s future growth is anchored on four AI initiatives across its verticals: ○ Force AI : launched subsequent to year end, Force AI is the unified AI orchestration layer at the core of the RADIUS CCaaS platform. It powers autonomous AI agents (AI Engage), for human agents (AI Assist) and interaction intelligence (AI Insight) across voice, chat, messaging, email, social and video, and is designed to operate with any large or small language model. Braiin believes Force AI has the potential to transform how enterprises engage their customers and to drive a new phase of growth for Braiin in the global CX market; ○ ARIA: launched in July 2026, ARIA (Agentic Real Estate Intelligence and Automation) is an workforce designed for the real estate industry, executing workflows across property management, leasing, compliance and customer engagement with human review of key decisions. Braiin believes ARIA has the potential to change how the real estate industry operates and to open a new chapter of growth for Braiin’s PropTech business; ○ AgTech: deeper AI analytics, combining drone and satellite imagery, IoT sensor data and predictive models to give insight into crop health, irrigation, soil conditions, pest risk and yield, followed by the expansion of the AgTech business in India and Australia; and ○ Flamingo AI: leveraging Flamingo AI to build , bespoke AI solutions for clients that think like the client, act like the client, and operate seamlessly within the client’s existing processes, systems and guardrails. Flamingo AI is designed to make AI work the way each client needs it to, creating deeply embedded, highly personalised solutions that can drive stronger customer outcomes, increase customer stickiness and create competitive differentiation for Braiin. Looking ahead, these capabilities are expected to provide multiple engines for rapid and sustainable growth. Force AI, ARIA, AgTech analytics and Flamingo AI’s approach are designed to create a distinctive competitive edge for Braiin’s solutions, increasing the depth of client integration and the potential for recurring, relationships. Together with Braiin’s acquisition strategy and expanding global footprint, these platforms are expected to support sustainable growth and strengthen the Company’s position as a differentiated global applied AI platform. * Adjusted EBITDA, pro forma Adjusted EBITDA and illustrative pro forma Adjusted EBITDA are financial measures. Financial Measures: Adjusted EBITDA, Pro Forma Adjusted EBITDA and Illustrative Pro Forma Adjusted EBITDA Adjusted EBITDA, pro forma Adjusted EBITDA and illustrative pro forma Adjusted EBITDA are measures. They are not a substitute for loss for the year, the most directly comparable IFRS measure, and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. Management uses these measures to evaluate the operating performance of the Group and believes they assist investors in understanding the performance of the acquired businesses on a comparable basis. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as loss for the year before income tax, finance costs, finance income and depreciation and amortization, excluding payments, fair value losses on financial instruments, net foreign exchange gains and losses and listing and transaction costs. Pro forma Adjusted EBITDA is Adjusted EBITDA calculated as if the acquisitions completed on 12 February 2026 had occurred on 1 July 2025, on the basis presented in the Form 20-F; it does not reflect the amortization of any intangible assets that may be recognized on completion of the purchase price allocations. Illustrative pro forma Adjusted EBITDA is a separate scenario for the acquired businesses before Group corporate costs and implementation costs. It assumes the acquired businesses had been controlled throughout FY2026 and includes estimated earnings, the assumed effect of remuneration savings and other operating improvements. It does not represent earnings achieved during FY2026, has not been prepared in accordance with Article 11 of Regulation S-X and is not a forecast of future results. Pro forma revenue is based on the actual revenue of the acquired businesses for the twelve months from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
