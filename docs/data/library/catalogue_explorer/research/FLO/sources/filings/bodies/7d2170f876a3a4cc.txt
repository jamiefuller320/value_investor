PLANT CLOSURE COSTS AND IMPAIRMENT OF ASSETS — On February 12, 2025, the company announced the closure of its Bailey Street Bakery located in Atlanta, Georgia. The bakery produced bread and bun products and ceased production on April 16, 2025. This bakery closure is part of our strategy to optimize capacity within our supply chain. Closure costs included equipment asset impairment charges and equipment relocation costs of $ 6.1 million and severance costs of $ 1.3 million and were recognized in the first quarter of Fiscal 2025. In the second quarter of Fiscal 2025, the company classified the bakery as held for sale. INVESTMENT IN UNCONSOLIDATED AFFILIATE — In the third quarter of Fiscal 2026, the company recognized a loss of $ 1.5 million for its remaining investment in Base Culture, a Clearwater, company with one manufacturing facility, upon Base Culture selling all of its remaining operating assets to an unaffiliated entity . No proceeds were available for distribution to holders of its common stock. The investment had been accounted for at cost, less any impairment, as the company did not control, nor did it have the ability to significantly influence Base Culture. RECOVERY ON INFERIOR INGREDIENTS — In the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025, the company recognized $ 2.7 million of identifiable and measurable costs associated with product losses. These product losses resulted from inferior coconut sugar and cashew flour used in certain of Simple Mills' products due to tiny fragments of metal present in the ingredients and from the presence of gluten in certain of Canyon Bakehouse's products. During the second and third quarters of Fiscal 2026, the company received a partial reimbursement of $ 2.1 million associated with Simple Mills' loss and received $ 0.5 million for reimbursement of the Canyon Bakehouse loss in the third quarter of Fiscal 2026. These recoveries are included as a separate line item of the Condensed Consolidated Statements of Income. Additionally, we recognized a recovery of $ 0.6 million for indirect losses associated with the Simple Mills loss and this amount is included in the selling, distribution, and administrative expenses line item of the Condensed Consolidated Statements of Income. We continue to seek recovery of all losses through appropriate means. GOODWILL AND INTANGIBLE ASSETS Goodwill is tested for impairment annually and whenever events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount of a reporting unit may exceed its fair value. The company performs a quantitative impairment assessment by comparing the fair value of each reporting unit with its carrying amount. intangible assets are also tested for impairment annually and whenever events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount of an asset may exceed its fair value. During the third quarter of Fiscal 2026, the company experienced certain triggering events including, but not limited to, significant decreases in its sales and operating results, significant decline in its stock price, and a downgrade of its corporate credit rating. The company’s operating results were negatively impacted by volatility in global and U.S. economies, as a result of, among other things, the inflationary economic environment, increased competition in the fresh packaged bread category, and shifts in consumer preferences. As a result of these triggering events, the company performed a quantitative interim impairment assessment of goodwill and intangible assets for its two reporting units, legacy Flowers Foods and Simple Mills, during the third quarter ended October 10, 2026. This assessment was performed consistently with the valuation methodology utilized in its annual testing. The projected cash flows for the legacy Flowers Foods reporting unit were discounted at 9.5 % and its estimated fair value was determined to exceed its carrying value by approximately 13.8 %. Based on equal weighting of the income and market approaches, and holding other valuation assumptions constant, the discount rate for the legacy Flowers Foods reporting unit would have to exceed 12.3 % or forecasted EBITDA margin across all future periods would have to be reduced approximately 1.4 % of revenue (or 16 % lower) in order for the estimated fair value of the reporting unit to fall below its carrying value. The projected cash flows for the Simple Mills reporting unit were discounted at 8.5 % and its estimated fair value was determined to exceed its carrying value by approximately 7.7 %. Based on equal weighting of the income and market approaches, and holding other valuation assumptions constant, the discount rate for the Simple Mills reporting unit would have to exceed 9.2 % or forecasted EBITDA margin across all future periods would have to be reduced approximately 1.8 % of revenue (or 11 % lower) in order for the estimated fair value of the reporting unit to fall below its carrying value. 18 No impairment of goodwill or intangible assets was identified as a result of the interim impairment assessments performed in the third quarter ended October 10, 2026. At October 10, 2026, the total goodwill associated with the legacy Flowers Foods and Simple Mills reporting units was $ 679.9 million and $ 367.2 million, respectively. In management’s opinion, the goodwill balance of either reporting unit could be at risk of impairment in the near term if the reporting unit’s operations do not perform in line with management’s expectations or if there is a negative change in the financial outlook for that reporting unit or in other factors such as the discount rate or market multiples.
