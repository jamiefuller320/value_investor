Industry conditions generally improved from 2017 through 2019. The compound annual growth rate (“CAGR”) of containerized trade volumes from 2010 through 2024 was 3.0%, which rate is the outcome of, among other things, a period of negative growth in 2020 in response to the pandemic, a subsequent rebound in 2021, a period of further negative growth in 2022 and 2023 resulting from geopolitical tensions driving inflationary headwinds, followed by a rebound in 2024. Volumes are estimated to have increased in 2025 by approximately 5.0%.
