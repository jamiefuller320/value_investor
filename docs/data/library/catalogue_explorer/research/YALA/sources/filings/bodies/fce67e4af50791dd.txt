Financial Measures To supplement the financial measures prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States, or GAAP, this press release presents financial measures, namely operating income, net income, net margin and basic and diluted earnings per ordinary share, as supplemental measures to review and assess the Company’s operating performance. The presentation of the financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. We define operating income as operating income excluding compensation. We define net income as net income excluding compensation. We define net margin as net income as a percentage of revenues. We define net income attributable to Yalla Group Limited’s shareholders as net income attributable to Yalla Group Limited’s shareholders, excluding compensation. We define earnings per ordinary share as net income attributable to Yalla Group Limited’s shareholders, divided by the weighted average number of basic and diluted shares outstanding. By excluding the impact of compensation expenses, which are charges, the Company believes that the financial measures help identify underlying trends in its business and enhance the overall understanding of the Company’s past performance and future prospects. Investors can better understand the Company’s operating and financial performance, compare business trends among different reporting periods on a consistent basis and assess its core operating results, as they exclude compensation expenses, which are not expected to result in cash payments. The Company also believes that the financial measures allow for greater visibility with respect to key metrics used by the Company’s management in its financial and operational . The financial measure is not defined under U.S. GAAP and is not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. The financial measure has limitations as analytical tools. One of the key limitations of using the financial measures is that they do not reflect all items of income and expense that affect the Company’s operations. compensation has been and may continue to be incurred in the Company’s business and is not reflected in the presentation of financial measures. Further, the financial measure may differ from the information used by other companies, including peer companies, and therefore their comparability may be limited. The Company compensates for these limitations by providing the relevant disclosure of its financial measures in the reconciliations to the nearest U.S. GAAP performance measures, all of which should be considered when evaluating its performance. The Company encourages investors and others to review its financial information in its entirety and not rely on a single financial measure.
