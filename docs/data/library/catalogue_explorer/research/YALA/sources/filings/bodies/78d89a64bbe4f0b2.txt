The increase was primarily driven by our broadening user base and enhanced monetization capability. In the third quarter of 2025, revenues generated from chatting services were US$55.5 million, and revenues from games services were US$33.8 million. Costs and expenses Our total costs and expenses were US$55.9 million in the third quarter of 2025, a 1.0% decrease from US$56.4 million in the third quarter of 2024. Our cost of revenues was US$28.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, a 10.7% decrease from US$31.8 million in the third quarter of 2024, primarily due to lower commission fees paid to payment platforms as a result of diversified payment channels and lower compensation expenses recognized in the third quarter of 2025. Cost of revenues as a percentage of our total revenues decreased to 31.7% in the third quarter of 2025 from 35.8% in the third quarter of 2024. Our selling and marketing expenses were US$9.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, a 30.3% increase from US$7.4 million in the third quarter of 2024, primarily due to higher advertising and market promotion expenses attributable to our continued user acquisition efforts and expanding product portfolio. Selling and marketing expenses as a percentage of our total revenues increased to 10.7 % in the third quarter of 2025 from 8.3% in the third quarter of 2024. Our general and administrative expenses were US$9.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, a 9.0% decrease from US$10.1 million in the third quarter of 2024, primarily due to a decrease in incentive compensation and professional service fees. General and administrative expenses as a percentage of our total revenues decreased to 10.3% in the third quarter of 2025 from 11.4% in the third quarter of 2024. Our technology and product development expenses were US$8.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, a 21.4% increase from US$7.1 million in the third quarter of 2024, primarily due to an increase in salaries and benefits for our technology and product development staff, driven by an increase in the headcount to support the development of new businesses and expansion of our product portfolio. The presentation of the financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. We define operating income as operating income excluding compensation. We define net income as net income excluding compensation. We define net margin as net income as a percentage of revenues. By excluding the impact of compensation expenses, which are charges, the Company believes that the financial measures help identify underlying trends in its business and enhance the overall understanding of the Company’s past performance and future prospects. Investors can better understand the Company’s operating and financial performance, compare business trends among different reporting periods on a consistent basis and assess its core operating results, as they exclude compensation expenses, which are not expected to result in cash payments. The Company also believes that the financial measures allow for greater visibility with respect to key metrics used by the Company’s management in its financial and operational . The financial measure is not defined under U.S. GAAP and is not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. The financial measure has limitations as analytical tools. One of the key limitations of using the financial measures is that they do not reflect all items of income and expense that affect the Company’s operations. compensation has been and may continue to be incurred in the Company’s business and is not reflected in the presentation of financial measures. Further, the financial measure may differ from the information used by other companies, including peer companies, and therefore their comparability may be limited. The Company compensates for these limitations by providing the relevant disclosure of its financial measures in the reconciliations to the nearest U.S. GAAP performance measures, all of which should be considered when evaluating its performance. The Company encourages investors and others to review its financial information in its entirety and not rely on a single financial measure. About Yalla Group Limited Yalla Group Limited is the largest online social networking and gaming company, in terms of revenues in 2022. The Company operates two flagship mobile applications, Yalla, a group chat platform, and Yalla Ludo, a casual gaming application featuring online versions of board games, popular in MENA, with voice chat and localized Majlis functionality. Building on the success of Yalla and Yalla Ludo, the Company continues to add engaging new content, creating a , integrated ecosystem dedicated to fulfilling MENA users’ evolving online social networking and gaming needs. Through its holding subsidiary, Yalla Game Limited, the Company has expanded its capabilities in and games in the MENA region, leveraging its local expertise to bring innovative gaming content to its users. In addition, the growing Yalla ecosystem includes YallaChat, an IM product tailored for Arabic users, WeMuslim, a product that supports Arabic users in observing their customs, and casual games such as Yalla Baloot and 101 Okey Yalla, developed to sustain vibrant local gaming communities in MENA. Yalla is also actively exploring outside of MENA with Yalla Parchis, a Ludo game designed for the South American markets. Yalla’s mobile applications deliver a seamless experience that fosters a sense of loyalty and belonging, establishing highly devoted and engaged user communities through close attention to detail and localized appeal that profoundly resonates with users.
