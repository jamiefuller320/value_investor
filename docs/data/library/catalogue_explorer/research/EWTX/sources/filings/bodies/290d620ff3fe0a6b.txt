Notes to Condensed Financial Statements (Unaudited) NOTE 1 DESCRIPTION OF BUSINESS Organization and Description of Business Edgewise Therapeutics, Inc. (the Company) is a biopharmaceutical company focused on advancing therapies for people living with serious cardiovascular diseases. The Company’s pipeline includes , a novel, oral, selective, cardiac sarcomere modulator, in development for the treatment of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM), including both obstructive HCM (oHCM) and nonobstructive HCM (nHCM). The Company is also advancing , an oral cardiac sarcomere modulator in clinical development for the treatment of heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF). In addition, the Company is leveraging its precision medicine platform to advance additional research programs focused on genetically defined cardiovascular and cardiometabolic diseases. On May 31, 2026, the Company entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement (Sevasemten Purchase Agreement) with Servier Pharmaceuticals LLC and Les Laboratoires Servier (Servier) for the sale of the sevasemten muscular dystrophy program, including certain related assets, intellectual property, , select employees, contracts, regulatory filings and clinical data, and assumed certain related liabilities (Sevasemten Sale). The Sevasemten Sale closed on July 10, 2026. Following completion of the Sevasemten Sale, the Company is focused on advancing its cardiovascular pipeline and related research and development activities.
