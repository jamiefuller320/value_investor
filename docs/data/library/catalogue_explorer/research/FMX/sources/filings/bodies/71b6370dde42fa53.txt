Risk Factors Risks Related to Our Company FEMSA’s business depends on its relationship with The Company, and changes in this relationship may adversely affect FEMSA’s business, financial condition and results of operations. Substantially all of FEMSA, S.A.B. de C.V.’s (“ FEMSA”) sales are derived from sales of trademark beverages. FEMSA produces, markets, sells and distributes trademark beverages through standard bottler agreements in the territories where it operates, which we refer to as “ FEMSA’s territories.” FEMSA is required to purchase concentrate for all trademark beverages from affiliates of The Company (“TCCC”), which price is determined from time to time by TCCC in all such territories. FEMSA is also required to purchase sweeteners and other raw materials only from companies authorized by TCCC. Increases in the cost, disruption of supply or shortage of ingredients for concentrate could have an adverse effect on FEMSA’s business. See “Item 4. Information on the Company— FEMSA— FEMSA’s Territories.” In addition, under FEMSA’s bottler agreements, it is prohibited from bottling or distributing any other beverages without TCCC’s authorization or consent, and it may not transfer control of the bottler rights of any of its territories without prior consent from TCCC. TCCC makes significant contributions to FEMSA’s marketing expenses, although it is not required to contribute a particular amount. Accordingly, TCCC may discontinue or reduce such contributions at any time. FEMSA depends on TCCC to continue with its bottler agreements. FEMSA’s bottler agreements are automatically renewable for terms, subject to the right of either party to give prior notice that it does not wish to renew the applicable agreement. In addition, these agreements generally may be terminated in the case of material breach. See “Item 10. Additional Information—Material Contracts—Material Contracts Relating to FEMSA—Bottler Agreements.” Termination of any such bottler agreement would prevent FEMSA from selling trademark beverages in the affected territory.
