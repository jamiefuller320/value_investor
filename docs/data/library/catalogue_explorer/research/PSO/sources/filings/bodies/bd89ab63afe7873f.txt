Assessment & Qualifications Assessment & Qualifications revenue increased 4% for the period, with growth accelerating to 10% in Q3. Pearson Professional Assessments revenue growth accelerated in Q3, driven by new contract launches, including a global certification programme with Anthropic, highlighting growing demand for the trusted verification of AI skills. Revenue increased 7% for the period, reflecting contributions from both recently launched contracts and those launched in the prior year, partially offset by headwinds in PDRI, which were fully annualised in the first half of the year. In Q3, US Student Assessment secured a new long term statewide assessment award in Pennsylvania, effective from the 2027-2028 academic year assessment cycle. For the period, revenue declined 4%, driven by the previously disclosed loss of the New Jersey contract, partially offset by the biennial NAEP testing cycle and delivery phasing benefits that are expected to reverse in Q4. Clinical Assessment revenue grew 8% for the period, due to the continued traction of our products, including in international markets, as well as pricing and digital product growth. We continue to lead in the application of innovative technologies with the launch of an assistant for clinical and psychoeducational reports, enhancing practitioner productivity and supporting evaluation outcomes. UK & International Qualifications revenue increased 7% for the period driven by new contracts, volume and pricing. For Assessment & Qualifications, we continue to expect low to digit revenue growth for the full year driven by new contracts, products and pricing. Virtual Learning Virtual Learning revenue increased 17% for the period, with Q3 up 13%, driven by strong enrolment growth, funding upside and favourable mix. Enrolment growth for the 2026/27 academic year was 8% in the Fall semester, reflecting continued demand for virtual schooling and strong execution, underpinning our confidence for the academic year. We successfully opened five new schools for the 2026/27 academic year, bringing our total network to 46 schools across 32 states. This included our first school in North Carolina, a new state for Pearson, where early demand has been encouraging. Full year expectations for Virtual Learning remain unchanged, with stronger growth than 2025 driven by a full year of enrolment growth. Higher Education In Higher Education, revenue increased 2% for the period benefiting from growth of 20% in Inclusive Access. Revenue also increased 2% in Q3, with growth in US Higher Education revenue driven by a solid performance in core US Courseware, partially offset by declines in K12, reflecting delayed adoptions and funding pressures. International Higher Education grew in Q3 driven by the monetisation of our enterprise strategic partnerships alongside stable performance across mature and emerging markets as our turnaround plan progresses. We continue to advance our strategy to help learners become workforce ready in the age of AI, recently announcing new products designed to connect career exploration, skills development and credentials, helping learners progress from education into employment. The first offerings, Career Studio for School and Career Studio for Work, will bring together our content, assessments, certifications and support to address growing workforce readiness and skills needs. We continue to expect Higher Education to grow in 2026. Growth in our core US courseware business will be supported by continued product and platform innovation, pricing and Inclusive Access, partially offset by challenging conditions in the K12 market. English Language Learning English Language Learning revenue declined 4% for the period, as growth in Institutional was more than offset by challenging market conditions in PTE, driven by tight migration policies and geopolitical disruption. Our operational performance remains resilient, and we are confident in the attractiveness of the business, although market pressures are expected to persist. In Q3, revenue declined 5%, driven by lower PTE revenue partially offset by strong growth in Institutional, including Enterprise. PTE was further impacted by a strong prior year comparator following the test enhancements implemented last year. We continue to expand the footprint of our global Institutional business, launching new courseware to support Ministry of Education reforms in Italy, Poland and Spain, and announcing a partnership with Squirrel Ai that combines Pearson’s English content with its adaptive learning technology. We also launched Official PTE Academic AI Practice, giving test takers AI driven preparation tools and personalised feedback. We continue to expect Institutional to grow for the full year, driven by market share gains and pricing while PTE is expected to decline given the challenging market backdrop. Enterprise Learning & Skills Enterprise Learning & Skills revenue increased 7% for the period and 6% in Q3. Vocational Qualifications delivered another solid performance in Q3, driven by recently awarded contracts, including the extension of our vocational education programme with the Jordanian Ministry of Education and an agreement with the Uzbekistan Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation to roll out BTEC qualifications nationwide. Enterprise Solutions continued to grow, supported by the ongoing monetisation of strategic partnerships. Demand from technology services and software partners for skilling programmes remained strong, particularly for priority engineering roles. We are helping employees progress from foundational capability to proficiency in high value skills through integrated assessment, learning and certification. We continue to expect growth in 2026 to be driven by a solid performance in Vocational Qualifications and strategic account growth in Enterprise Solutions. 2026 guidance summary Underlying Revenue growth Group digit growth.
