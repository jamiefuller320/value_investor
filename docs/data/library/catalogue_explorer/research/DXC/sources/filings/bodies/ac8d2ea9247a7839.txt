DXC also issued PSU awards that are considered to have a market condition. Settlement of shares for these PSU awards will be made shortly subsequent to the end of the third fiscal year, subject to certain market conditions and continued employment through the last day of the third fiscal year. The fair value of RSUs and PSUs is based on the Company’s common stock closing price on the grant date. For PSUs with a condition, DXC uses a Monte Carlo simulation model to value the grants.
