ASU 2023-09 also includes certain other amendments intended to improve the effectiveness of income tax disclosures. ASU 2023-09 is effective for the Company's fiscal year beginning January 1, 2025 and allows the use of a prospective or retrospective approach. The Company plans to adopt the standard 8 on January 1, 2025 and has not yet determined the potential impact of its adoption of ASU 2023-09 on the Company's audited Consolidated Financial Statements.
