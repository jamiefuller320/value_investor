Business Trends Beginning in the second quarter of 2026, we have experienced headwinds to demand due to the rapid evolution of how AI is changing the way customers discover, evaluate and purchase software solutions. This shift has increased budget scrutiny from customers, including longer sales and approval cycles with larger buying committees among larger customers and greater price sensitivity and budget optimization among smaller customers. We have made deliberate changes to our product, pricing and approach, including free trials of our AI agents and pricing tied to the value customers receive, which we believe are the right decisions for our business. However, these changes have contributed to extended customer buying cycles and adversely affected our revenue growth in the near term. These demand headwinds intensified during the third quarter of 2026, and we expect these dynamics to continue to adversely affect our results of operations.
