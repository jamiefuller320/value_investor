Market Information Our common stock is traded on the NASDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol “MMSI.” As of February 21, 2025, the number of shares of our common stock outstanding was 58,834,568 held by approximately 90 shareholders of record, not including shareholders whose shares are held in securities position listings. We did not repurchase any shares during the years ended December 31, 2024, 2023 and 2022. Dividends We have never declared or paid cash dividends on shares of our common stock. We presently intend to retain any future earnings for use in our business and, therefore, do not anticipate paying any dividends on shares of our common stock in the foreseeable future.
