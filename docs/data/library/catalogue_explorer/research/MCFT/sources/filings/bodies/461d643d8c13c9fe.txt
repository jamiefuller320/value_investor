All material corporate costs are included in the Performance and Wake segment. The Company incurred acquisition-related costs of $ 15.2 million during fiscal 2026 related to the Marine Products Transaction. These costs are included within general and administrative expenses. The Company’s CODM is the Chief Executive Officer. The CODM uses Adjusted EBITDA, a measure, in the annual budget and forecasting process. Subsequent to the process, the CODM considers variances to assess the performance of and allocate resources to the Company’s segments based on Adjusted EBITDA. Adjusted EBITDA excludes depreciation and amortization, share based compensation, senior leadership transition and organizational realignment costs, and business development consulting costs.
