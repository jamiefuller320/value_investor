U.S. Eastern Time on November 24, 2026 Guangzhou, China, November 24, 2026 — Vipshop Holdings Limited (NYSE: VIPS), a leading retailer in China (“Vipshop” or the “Company”), today announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Third Quarter 2026 Highlights · Total net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB20.4 billion (US$3.0 billion), compared with RMB21.4 billion in the prior year period. · GMV 1 for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB42.2 billion, compared with RMB43.1 billion in the prior year period. Within that, GMV from Shan Shan Outlets for the third quarter of 2026 increased by 11.7% year over year. · Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB4.7 billion (US$697.8 million), compared with RMB4.9 billion in the prior year period. · Net income attributable to Vipshop’s shareholders for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB1.1 billion (US$157.2 million), compared with RMB1.2 billion in the prior year period . · net income attributable to Vipshop’s shareholders 2 for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB1.3 billion (US$187.9 million), compared with RMB1.5 billion in the prior year period . · The number of active customers 3 for the third quarter of 2026 was 38.3 million, compared with 40.1 million in the prior year period. · Total orders 4 for the third quarter of 2026 were 151.8 million, compared with 166.4 million in the prior year period. Mr. Eric Shen, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Vipshop, stated, “We navigated a complex retail environment in the third quarter, maintaining disciplined execution to protect the quality and resilience of our business. By refining our merchandising strategy around core supply to deliver exceptional value and relevance, we sustained high retention, healthy unit economics and solid growth among our core Super VIP customers. Moving forward, we will further differentiate our merchandise, deepen customer loyalty and drive operational efficiency to strengthen our foundation for growth.” Mr. Mark Wang, Chief Financial Officer of Vipshop, further commented, “We delivered results in line with expectations, maintaining stable and healthy core operating profitability amid a challenging demand landscape. We continue to balance disciplined investments for emerging opportunities with consistent capital returns, advancing our share repurchase program and declaring a special dividend of approximately US$337.9 million, as part of our shareholder return promises. For the periods presented in this press release, net income attributable to Vipshop’s shareholders is net income attributable to Vipshop’s shareholders excluding (i) compensation expenses, (ii) impairment loss of investments, (iii) investment gain and revaluation of investments excluding dividends, (iv) reconciling items on the share of equity method investments, and (v) tax effects on adjustments. net income attributable to Vipshop’s shareholders per diluted ADS is computed using net income attributable to Vipshop’s shareholders divided by weighted average number of diluted ADS outstanding for computing diluted earnings per ADS. income from operations is income from operations excluding compensation expenses. operating margin is income from operations as a percentage of total net revenues. net margin attributable to Vipshop’s shareholders is net income attributable to Vipshop’s shareholders as a percentage of total net revenues. Free cash flow is net cash from operating activities adding back the impact from internet financing activities and less capital expenditures, which include purchase and deposits of property and equipment and land use rights. Impact from internet financing activities added back or deducted from free cash flow contains changes in the balances of financial products, which are primarily consumer financing and supplier financing that the Company provides to customers and suppliers. The Company believes that separate analysis and exclusion of the impact of (i) compensation expenses, (ii) impairment loss of investments, (iii) investment gain and revaluation of investments excluding dividends, (iv) reconciling items on the share of equity method investments, and (v) tax effects on adjustments add clarity to the constituent parts of its performance. The Company reviews these financial measures together with GAAP financial measures to obtain a better understanding of its operating performance. It uses these financial measures for planning, forecasting, and measuring results against the forecast. The Company believes that financial measures are useful supplemental information for investors and analysts to assess its operating performance without the effect of (i) compensation expenses, (ii) impairment loss of investments, (iii) investment gain and revaluation of investments excluding dividends, (iv) reconciling items on the share of equity method investments, and (v) tax effects on adjustments.
