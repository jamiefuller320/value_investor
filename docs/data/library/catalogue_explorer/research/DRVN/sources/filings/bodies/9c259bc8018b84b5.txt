Adjusted EBITDA is a supplemental measure of the operating performance of the Company’s segments and may not be comparable to similar measures reported by other companies. Other segment items primarily include, but are not limited to, payroll and costs, costs of inventory and supplies, utilities, and rent expense as well as marketing costs associated with businesses within the reportable segments.
