Material Accounting Policies, Continued 2.1.2 New and amended standards or interpretations not yet adopted by the Group, Continued [Changes in the presentation and structure of income statement] Korean IFRS 1118 requires all income and expenses in the income statement to be classified into one of five categories: the operating category, investing category, financing category, income taxes category, and discontinued operations category. Under the standard, all income and expenses that are not classified as investing, financing, income tax, or discontinued operations categories are classified in the operating category. Accordingly, the operating category is a residual category, and operating profit or loss is defined as the total of income and expenses classified within that category. The classification of income and expenses into categories depends on the Group’s main business activities. The Group has preliminarily assessed that investing in particular types of assets or providing financing to customers does not constitute a main business activity. The application of Korean IFRS 1118 is expected to affect the presentation and disaggregation of income and expenses but is not expected to have an impact on the Group’s profit or loss for the period or total net assets. The Group will be required to present the newly defined subtotals of ‘operating profit or loss’, ‘profit or loss before financing and income taxes’ and ‘profit or loss’. The new operating profit or loss may differ from the operating profit currently presented by the Group. A reconciliation between operating profit or loss calculated in accordance with Korean IFRS 1001 and operating profit or loss as defined by Korean IFRS 1118 will be disclosed in the notes. Based on the information currently available, the Group has performed an overall preliminary assessment of the impact of applying Korean IFRS 1118 and has identified the following potential impacts. Income and expenses arising from investments accounted for using the equity method are expected to be classified in the investing category. Accordingly, the Group’s share of profit or loss of associates and joint ventures is expected to be classified and presented in the investing category. Dividend income from investments held by the Group is expected to be classified and presented in the investing category. Interest income arising from certain financial assets held by the Group (e.g. interest income arising from cash and cash equivalents) is expected to be classified and presented in the investing category. Interest expense is expected to be classified and presented in the financing category. Foreign exchange differences arising from foreign currency transactions and translation are expected to be classified in the same category as the income and expenses from the items that gave rise to the foreign exchange differences. Accordingly, the Group’s foreign exchange differences are expected to be classified in the operating, investing and financing categories. Gains and losses on financial instruments designated as hedging instruments are expected to be classified in the same category as the income and expenses affected by the risks that the financial instruments are used to manage. 18 SK hynix Inc. and Subsidiaries Notes to the Condensed Consolidated Interim Financial Statements September 30, 2026 and 2025 and December 31, 2025 (Unaudited) 2. [Introduction of disclosure of performance measures] performance measures (MPMs) are subtotals of income and expenses used in public communications outside the consolidated financial statements to communicate management’s view of an aspect of the financial performance of the Group as a whole to users of financial statements. The Group is required to disclose information relating to MPMs in a single note to the consolidated financial statements. The Group is establishing a process for determining relevant public communications when identifying MPMs. The Group plans to continue reviewing performance measures used in future public communications. If any MPMs are identified, the Group plans to disclose the reason for using each measure, the method of calculation, a reconciliation between each MPM and the most directly comparable subtotal specified by Korean IFRS 1118, and the income tax effect and the effect on interests of each reconciling item. [Principles of aggregation and disaggregation] Korean IFRS 1118 introduces enhanced principles for aggregating and disaggregating information in the consolidated financial statements according to the characteristics of the information. The standard also introduces guidance on labeling and describing items presented in the primary consolidated financial statements or disclosed in the notes. The Group plans to aggregate and disaggregate items based on similar or dissimilar characteristics to present useful structured summaries in the primary consolidated financial statements and disclose additional material information in the notes. The Group is currently reviewing items presented as ‘other’ and plans to use more informative labels or separately disaggregate material items, as appropriate. The Group currently uses ‘profit for the period’ as the starting point for cash flows from operating activities and, accordingly, certain reconciling items are expected to change.
