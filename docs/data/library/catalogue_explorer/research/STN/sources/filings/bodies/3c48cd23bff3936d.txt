Q3 2026 compared to Q3 2025 Stantec achieved strong third quarter adjusted net income of $212.1 million and adjusted earnings per share of $1.89, reflecting an increase of 23.5%, driven by net revenue growth and strong operational performance. Net revenue increased 9.4% or $159.5 million, to $1.9 billion, driven by organic growth of 5.3% and acquisition growth of 2.6%. Stantec achieved organic growth in all of its regions and businesses, most notably in Water with organic growth. Project margin increased 10.5% or $97.0 million, to $1,024.9 million, reflecting an improvement of 60 basis points to 55.0%. This was a result of net revenue growth, solid project execution and favorable project recoveries. Adjusted EBITDA increased 16.6% or $53.7 million, to $377.1 million. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 20.2%, an increase of 120 basis points compared to Q3 2025. The growth in margin was primarily due to solid project margin and lower administrative and marketing expenses as a percentage of net revenue, reflecting our focus on efficient management of operations and optimization of discretionary spending. Net income increased 19.7% or $29.6 million, to $179.6 million, and diluted EPS increased 21.2%, or $0.28, to $1.60, mainly due to net revenue growth, improvements in project margin, and, as a percentage of net revenue, a 60 basis point reduction in administrative and marketing expenses. Similarly, adjusted net income grew 21.8% or $38.0 million, to $212.1 million, equating to 11.4% of net revenue—an increase of 120 basis points compared to Q3 2025. Adjusted EPS increased 23.5% or $0.36, to $1.89. Contract backlog stands at $9.2 billion at September 30, 2026, achieving an 11.6% increase, driven by 9.9% organic growth. Organic growth was achieved in all of our regions and businesses, driven primarily by organic growth in Canada and Global and in Stantec's Buildings, Water and Energy & Resources businesses. Contract backlog represents approximately 13 months of work. Cash flows from operations increased $76.4 million to $392.3 million compared to Q3 2025, reflecting strong revenue growth and improvements from the timing of collections and payments within working capital. Days sales outstanding (DSO) was 76 days, an increase of three days compared to Q3 2025 and approximates its target of 75 days. Net debt to adjusted EBITDA (on a trailing basis) at September 30, 2026 was 1.2x and remained within Stantec's internal target range of 1.0x to 2.0x. Consistent with the Company's capital allocation strategy, common shares of 1,723,229 were repurchased under its normal course issuer bid (NCIB) during the quarter, for an aggregate price of $174.5 million.
