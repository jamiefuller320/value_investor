.1 2 exh_991.htm PRESS RELEASE EdgarFiling EXHIBIT 99.1 Stantec selected to help modernize San Diego’s critical water infrastructure for generations to come EDMONTON, Alberta and SAN DIEGO, Nov. 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TSX, Stantec, a global leader in sustainable design and engineering, has been selected by the City of San Diego for a 10-year, US$75 million capacity engineering and construction services contract that will help strengthen and modernize 11 dams serving more than 1.3 million residents across the region. Under the contract, Stantec will provide engineering, environmental, design, and construction support services for improvement projects across multiple dams. The award builds on a trusted relationship with the city and expands dam improvement services that began three years ago under a $7 million capacity contract. “Our decades of experience delivering dam engineering services give Stantec a deep understanding of the technical, operational, and community considerations involved in maintaining these critical assets,” said Clayton Bock, executive vice president, Energy and Resources at Stantec. “We’re proud to bring that experience and our multidisciplinary expertise to support the City of San Diego as it invests in safe, resilient water infrastructure that’s fundamental to strong communities, economic growth, and quality of life.” The City of San Diego's Public Utilities Department estimates that completing the full dam rehabilitation and modernization program could require approximately $3 billion in investment over the coming decades, reflecting both the scale of the effort and the commitment required to secure the region’s water future. Building on decades of dam expertise The new contract extends Stantec's longstanding relationship with the City of San Diego, and builds on years of collaboration supporting dam planning, engineering, geotechnical investigations, and technical evaluations in the region. Stantec has helped deliver some of North America's most transformative dam rehabilitation and modernization projects, including the San Vicente Dam Raise for the San Diego County Water Authority. The landmark project expanded regional water storage capacity and became North America’s tallest dam raise at the time of its completion, enhancing water reliability for generations of Southern Californians.
