The final results of the agenda items submitted to a vote of the shareholders are as follows: Agenda Item 1: Acknowledgement of the Annual Report and the Audit Reports and Approval of the Management Report, the Annual Consolidated Financial Statements of On Holding AG and the Annual Financial Statements of On Holding AG for 2024 On Holding shareholders acknowledged the Annual Report and the Audit Reports and approved the Management Report, the Annual Consolidated Financial Statements of On Holding AG and the Annual Financial Statements of On Holding AG for 2024. Agenda Item 2: Appropriation of 2024 Financial Results On Holding shareholders approved that the profit for the year 2024 in the amount of KCHF 63,241.6 increases the “profit carried forward” from the financial year 2023 of KCHF 36,207.4, resulting in a new balance of “profit carried forward” of KCHF 99,449.0. Agenda Item 3: Approval of the Report on Matters On Holding shareholders approved the Report on Matters of On Holding AG for 2024. Agenda Item 4: Discharge of the Members of the Board of Directors and of the Executive Officers On Holding shareholders approved that the members of the Board of Directors and of the Executive Officers be discharged from liability for the financial year 2024. Agenda Item 5: of Alex Pérez as Proposed Representative of the Holders of Class A Shares on the Board of Directors On Holding shareholders approved the of Alex Pérez as representative of the holders of Class A shares on the Board of Directors for a term of office of one year (until completion of the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting in 2026). Agenda Item 6: of the Members of the Board of Directors On Holding shareholders approved the of David Allemann, Amy Banse, Olivier Bernhard, Caspar Coppetti, Dennis Durkin, Laura Miele and Alex Pérez as members of the Board of Directors, each for a term of office of one year (until completion of the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting in 2026). Agenda Item 7: Election of Helena Helmersson as new Member of the Board of Director On Holding shareholders approved the election of Helena Helmersson as a new member of the Board of Directors for a term of office of one year (until completion of the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting in 2026). Agenda Item 8: of the of the Board of Directors Agenda Item 8.1: of David Allemann as of the Board of Directors On Holding shareholders approved the of David Allemann as of the Board of Directors for a term of office of one year (until completion of the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting in 2026). Agenda Item 8.2: of Caspar Coppetti as of the Board of Directors On Holding shareholders approved the of Caspar Coppetti as of the Board of Directors for a term of office of one year (until completion of the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting in 2026). Agenda Item 9: Elections of the Members of the Nomination and Compensation Committee On Holding shareholders approved the election of Amy Banse, Helena Helmersson and Alex Pérez as members of the Nomination and Compensation Committee, each for a term of office of one year (until completion of the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting in 2026). Agenda Item 10: of the Independent Proxy Representative On Holding shareholders approved the of Anwaltskanzlei Keller AG ( .206.696), Splügenstrasse 8, 8002 Zurich, Switzerland, as Independent Proxy Representative for a term of office of one year (until completion of the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting in 2026). Agenda Item 11: of Statutory Auditors On Holding shareholders approved the of PricewaterhouseCoopers AG ( .839.438), in Zurich, Switzerland, as statutory auditors of On Holding AG for a term of office of one year (until completion of the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting in 2026). Agenda Item 12: Compensation Report; Approval of the Compensation of the Board of Directors and the Executive Officers Agenda Item 12.1: Consultative Vote on the 2024 Compensation Report On Holding shareholders took note of and endorsed the 2024 Compensation Report in a consultative vote. Agenda Item 12.2: Approval of the Maximum Aggregate Compensation for the Members of the Board of Directors for the Period between the 2025 Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting and the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting to be held in 2026 On Holding shareholders approved the maximum aggregate compensation of CHF 2,000,000 for the members of the Board of Directors for the period between the 2025 Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting and the next Annual General Shareholders’ Meeting to be held in 2026. Agenda Item 12.3: Approval of the Maximum Aggregate Compensation for the Executive Officers for the Financial Year 2026 On Holding shareholders approved the maximum aggregate compensation of CHF 30,000,000 for the Executive Officers (including, where applicable, for their activities as executive members of the Board of Directors) for the financial year 2026. Agenda Item 13: Conversion of Class B Shares to Class A Shares / Amendment of Article 3, Article 3b and Article 3c of the Articles of Association On Holding shareholders approved the conversion of 10,520,820 Class B Shares into 1,052,082 Class A Shares, as well as the amendment of Article 3, Article 3b and Article 3c of the Articles of Association to reflect the updated numbers of registered Class A Shares and registered Class B Shares mentioned therein.
