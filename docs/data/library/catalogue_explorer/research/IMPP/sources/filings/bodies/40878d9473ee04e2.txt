(the “Subsidiaries”), each owning one tanker. On December 3, 2021, StealthGas Inc. distributed the 318,351 common shares and 795,878 8.75 % Series A Preferred Shares (with a liquidation preference of $ 25.00 per share) in Imperial to holders of StealthGas Inc.’s common stock on a pro rata basis (the “ ”).
