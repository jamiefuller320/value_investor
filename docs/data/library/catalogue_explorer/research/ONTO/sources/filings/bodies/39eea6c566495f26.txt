Acquisitions and Investments Investment in Rigaku On August 10, 2026, the Company completed the previously announced acquisition of 61,123,436 shares ( 27 % of the issued and outstanding shares) of common stock of Rigaku for $ 719.5 million from Atom Investment, L.P., an affiliate of The Carlyle Group (the “Rigaku Transaction”), funded with a portion of the net proceeds from the issuance and sale of the Company’s 0.00 % Convertible Senior Notes due 2031 (“2031 Notes”) and cash on hand. Rigaku’s common stock is listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange. In connection with the Rigaku Transaction, the Company received the right to nominate one director to Rigaku’s board of directors. The Company’s 27 % ownership interest results in a presumption of significant influence under ASC 323, Investments - Equity Method and Joint Ventures . At initial recognition, the Company irrevocably elected the fair value option under ASC 825, Financial Instruments , for its investment in Rigaku. The Company elected the fair value option because Rigaku’s shares are publicly traded and have a readily determinable fair value, and management believes fair value provides the most relevant and transparent measure of the economic value of the investment. The Company does not control Rigaku and does not consolidate Rigaku’s financial results. The investment in Rigaku is presented as “Investment in Rigaku,” a asset, on the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets. The investment is remeasured to fair value at each reporting date based on the quoted market price of Rigaku’s common stock on the Tokyo Stock Exchange, translated into U.S. dollars at the closing spot exchange rate. All changes in the U.S. dollar fair value of the investment, including amounts attributable to changes in Rigaku’s share price and changes in the Japanese yen to U.S. dollar exchange rate, are recognized in net income within “Unrealized gain (loss) on investment in Rigaku” under “Other income, net” in the Condensed Consolidated Statements of Operations. The components are not bifurcated, and no amounts related to this investment are recorded in other comprehensive income. Dividends declared by Rigaku, if any, are recognized as dividend income when the Company’s right to receive payment is established and do not reduce the carrying amount of the investment. Under the fair value option, the Company does not recognize its proportionate share of Rigaku’s net income or loss, amortization of acquisition-date basis difference, or equity method adjustments for Rigaku’s other comprehensive income items. Changes in the fair value of the investment are recognized currently in net income but are not taxable or deductible until realized. The resulting difference between the carrying amount of the investment and its tax basis is a temporary difference, for which the Company recognizes deferred taxes.
