C apital reduction and amendment of Bylaws . Dear Sirs, I am writing to you in my capacity as Head of Market Relations of Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A. (“ tgs ”), to report that the Annual General, Special and Class Shareholders’ Meeting held on this date approved: (1) To reduce tgs ’ capital due to cancellation of tgs ’ treasury shares for an amount of AR$ 41,734,225 representing 41,734,225 Class B shares of a nominal value of AR$ 1 each and entitled to 1 vote per share. As a result of said capital reduction, tgs ’ capital stock shall turn from AR$ 794,495,283 to AR$ 752,761,058, and (2) To amend sections 5 and 6 and eliminate section 13 of tgs ’ Bylaws in order to: - Establish that Class A shares shall represent 51% or more of the capital stock, while Class B shares shall represent the remaining capital stock. - Eliminate from tgs ’ Bylaws any reference to Class C shares as there no longer exist any Class C shares. - Include the possibility to convert Class A shares into Class B shares, as long as Class A shares continue to represent at least 51% of the capital stock after conversion.
