(OTCID Pink: “STLE”) (“Steele”), the bank holding company for Central Penn Bank & Trust, announced that on November 21, 2025, Steele’s Board of Directors declared a regular cash dividend. Dividend Declared On November 20, 2025, Steele’s Board of Directors declared a regular cash dividend of $0.75 per share for the December 31, 2025. The dividend is payable December 29, 2025, to shareholders of record as of December 15, 2025. dividends declared in 2025 total $1.49 per share compared to $1.45 per share for the same period in 2024. About Steele Bancorp, Inc. Steele Bancorp, Inc. is the bank holding company for Central Penn Bank & Trust, headquartered in Mifflinburg, Pennsylvania. Central Penn Bank & Trust is a commercial bank, serving customers from thirteen locations in Centre, Northumberland, Snyder, and Union counties in central Pennsylvania. The Bank has 172 employees and total assets of $1.25 billion as of September 30, 2025.
