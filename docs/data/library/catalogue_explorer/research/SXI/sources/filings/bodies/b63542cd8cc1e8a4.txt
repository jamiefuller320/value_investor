STANDEX INTERNATIONAL CORPORATION 2025 ANNUAL REPORT ON FORM 10-K/A EXPLANATORY NOTE This Amendment on Form 10-K/A (“Amendment No. 1”) amends the registrant’s Form 10-K for the year ended June 30, 2025, as filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on August 1, 2025 (the “Form 10-K”), solely for the purpose of correcting an inadvertent date error on the signature page in the previously filed 10-K Report.
