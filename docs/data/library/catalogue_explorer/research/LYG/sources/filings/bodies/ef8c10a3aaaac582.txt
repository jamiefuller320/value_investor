Form 40-F Index to Exhibits Item No. 1 Regulatory News Service Announcement, 22 October 2026 re : Board Change 22 October 2026 LLOYDS BANKING GROUP PLC: BOARD APPOINTMENT Lloyds Banking Group plc (the “Group”) is pleased to announce the appointment of Chris Rhodes as an independent director of the Group and a member of its Audit Committee and Board Risk Committee, in all cases with effect from 1 November 2026. Biographical details Chris has over 30 years of UK banking industry experience having held senior leadership roles across finance, treasury, operations, and product and proposition development and marketing. Chris started his career at Ernst & Whinney (a legacy firm of EY). He then joined Alliance & Leicester plc where he qualified as a chartered accountant and undertook a range of roles including Interim Chief Executive Officer, Group Finance Director, Managing Director of Retail Banking and Operations Director. He then joined Nationwide Building Society and held roles including Chief Financial Officer, Chief Products and Proposition Officer and Group Retail Director. Until earlier this year Chris was Chief Executive Officer of Virgin Money following its acquisition by Nationwide Building Society. Chris currently has no other external commitments. Sir Robin Budenberg, Chair, said: “We are delighted that Chris is joining our Board.
