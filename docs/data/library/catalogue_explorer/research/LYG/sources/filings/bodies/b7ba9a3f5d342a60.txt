Form 40-F Index to Exhibits Item No. 1 Regulatory News Service Announcement, 25 September 2026 re : COMPLETION OF £1.75BN SHARE BUYBACK PROGRAMME 25 September 2026 LLOYDS BANKING GROUP COMPLETES £1.75 BILLION SHARE BUYBACK PROGRAMME Following the purchase of ordinary shares on 24 September 2026, Lloyds Banking Group plc (the " Company ") announces that the Company's share buyback programme announced on 30 January 2026 was completed in accordance with its terms. The programme was managed by Goldman Sachs International.
