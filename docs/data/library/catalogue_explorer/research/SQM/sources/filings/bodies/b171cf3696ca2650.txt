The Company assesses the credit impairment of its receivables as of each reporting date. A financial asset has credit impairment when one or more events have a negative impact on the expected cash flows from it. Evidence of credit impairment for a debtor is as follows: - Significant financial hardship - Breach of contract due to default - Probability of going bankrupt The Company applies the simplified approach to measure expected credit losses using the lifetime expected loss on all trade receivables.
