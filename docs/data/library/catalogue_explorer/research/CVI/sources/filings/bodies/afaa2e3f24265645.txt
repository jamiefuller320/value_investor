AND SUBSIDIARIES NOTES TO THE CONDENSED CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS (unaudited) (1) Organization and Nature of Business Organization CVR Energy, Inc. (“CVR Energy ”, “CVR ”, “we ”, “us ”, “our ”, or the “Company”) is a diversified holding company primarily engaged in the petroleum refining and marketing industry (the “Petroleum Segment”) and the nitrogen fertilizer manufacturing industry through its interest in CVR Partners, LP, a publicly traded limited partnership (the “Nitrogen Fertilizer Segment” or “CVR Partners”). The Petroleum Segment refines and markets high value transportation fuels which consist of gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, and distillates, as well as activities related to crude oil gathering and logistics that support refinery operations. CVR Partners produces and markets nitrogen fertilizer products primarily in the form of ammonia and urea ammonium nitrate (“UAN”) for the farming industry. CVR’s common stock is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (“NYSE”) under the symbol “CVI”. As of September 30, 2026, Icahn Enterprises L.P. and its affiliates, including Mr. Carl C. Icahn (“IEP”), owned approximately 71 % of the Company’s outstanding common stock. CVR Partners, LP As of September 30, 2026, public common unitholders held approximately 60 % of CVR Partners’ outstanding common units; CVR Energy, through its subsidiaries, held approximately 37 % of CVR Partners’ outstanding common units and 100 % of CVR Partners’ general partner interests, while IEP held approximately 3 % of CVR Partners’ outstanding common units. The noncontrolling interest reflected on the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets of CVR is only impacted by the results of and distributions from CVR Partners. Pending Acquisition of Northern PADD II Refining Assets On November 2, 2026, the Company entered into an equity and asset purchase agreement (the “Purchase Agreement”) with certain indirect subsidiaries and affiliates of Marathon Petroleum Corporation (the “Sellers”) to acquire the refinery located in St. Paul Park, Minnesota (the “St. Paul Park Refinery”), the refinery located in Mandan, North Dakota (the “Mandan Refinery”), a 17 % interest in each of Minnesota Pipe Line Company, LLC and MPL Investments, Inc., and associated wholesale marketing and logistics assets (collectively, the “Target Assets”), and to assume certain liabilities related to the Target Assets, for aggregate cash consideration of approximately $ 4.2 billion, subject to customary closing adjustments, including net working capital and inventory adjustments (the “Acquisition”). The Acquisition is expected to be accounted for as a business combination under Accounting Standards Codification (“ASC”) 805, Business Combinations , with the Company as the accounting acquirer. The consummation of the Acquisition is subject to customary closing conditions, including the expiration or termination of the waiting period under the Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended, and certain other regulatory approvals. The Purchase Agreement may be terminated under specified circumstances, in certain of which the Company would be required to pay the Sellers a termination fee of 6 %, 4 % or 2 % of the aggregate base purchase price, depending on the circumstances of termination. Financing Transactions and Commercial Agreements In connection with the Purchase Agreement, the Company entered into a debt commitment letter with Barclays Bank PLC and certain other financial institutions providing for a commitment to provide (i) a senior secured 364 -day bridge loan facility in an aggregate principal amount of up to $ 2.5 billion, to the extent the net cash proceeds of permanent senior secured financing are less than $ 2.5 billion, and (ii) consents to an amendment to the CVR Energy ABL to provide $ 550 million of incremental commitments, increasing total commitments to $ 1.1 billion upon the closing of the Acquisition (or, alternatively, commitments for a new $ 1.1 billion senior secured revolving credit facility). The Company also entered into (a) a subscription agreement with a commercial counterparty (the “Commercial Partner”) pursuant to which the Commercial Partner committed to purchase $ 800 million of shares of the Company’s common stock equivalent convertible preferred stock in a private placement, conditioned upon and concurrent with the closing of the Acquisition, and (b) a subscription agreement with IEP, the Company’s controlling stockholder, pursuant to which IEP committed to provide, directly or indirectly, up to $ 500 million of equity financing at the same price per share as the commercial counterparty, which commitment terminates upon the consummation of an offering of common stock generating gross proceeds to the Company of at least $ 500 million, together with a stockholders and registration rights agreement with IEP. The EPA’s final rule, effective in June 2026, (a) established applicable blending volumes and percentage standards under the RFS for 2026 and 2027 for cellulosic biofuel, diesel, advanced biofuel, and total renewable fuel, reflecting the highest renewable fuel volume in the program’s history, including for diesel and advanced biofuel; (b) partially waived the 2025 cellulosic biofuel volume requirement and revised the associated percentage standard due to a shortfall in cellulosic biofuel production; and (c) promulgated several regulatory changes to the RFS program, including the removal of renewable electricity as a qualifying renewable fuel under the RFS program and makes minor revisions to the biogas provisions of the RFS program (the “2026/2027 RFS Final Rule”). In the 2026/2027 RFS Final Rule, the EPA confirmed its intention to add to annual blending obligations volumes that account for 70% of volumes actually exempted by the EPA for 2023 and 2024 compliance years and those projected to be exempted by the EPA for the 2025 compliance year, estimated by the EPA to total 2.89 billion RINs. In October 2026, the EPA published a proposed rule considering two approaches: splitting the 2025 exempted volumes evenly across 2026 and 2027 or adding the 2025 exempted volumes to 2027 only. Because the reallocation proposal would revise the existing standards, the EPA has triggered the automatic compliance deadline extension provision that was promulgated as part of the Set 2 rule. The 2026 compliance deadline (currently March 31, 2027) will be extended to whichever of the following dates is applicable, as follows: (a) if the EPA finalizes the proposed revision to the 2026 RFS standards, the next quarterly reporting deadline after the effective date of the revised 2026 standards; (b) if the EPA withdraws the proposed revision to the 2026 RFS standards, the next quarterly reporting deadline that is 60 days after the withdrawal is published in the Federal Register; and (c) if the EPA does not finalize or withdraw the proposed revision to the 2026 RFS standards within 12 months of the date the proposal is published in the Federal Register, the next quarterly reporting deadline after that 12-month mark. Public comment on this proposed rule ends on December 4, 2026. Company Overview CVR Energy is a diversified holding company primarily engaged in the petroleum refining and marketing industry (the “Petroleum Segment”) and the nitrogen fertilizer manufacturing industry through its interest in CVR Partners, LP, a publicly traded limited partnership (the “Nitrogen Fertilizer Segment” or “CVR Partners”). The Petroleum Segment is an “independent petroleum refiner”, in that it does not have crude oil exploration or production operations, and is a marketer of high value transportation fuels primarily in the form of gasoline and diesel fuels. CVR Partners produces and markets nitrogen fertilizers primarily in the form of urea ammonium nitrate (“UAN”) and ammonia. We operate under two reportable segments: petroleum and nitrogen fertilizer, which are referred to in this document as our “Petroleum Segment” and our “Nitrogen Fertilizer Segment”, respectively.
