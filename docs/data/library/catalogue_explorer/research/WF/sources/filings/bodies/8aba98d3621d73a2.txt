Dividend Information As a financial holding company, we distribute dividends to our shareholders using the dividends we receive from our subsidiaries. Our subsidiaries pay dividends to us within the limits of their distributable profits after deducting the necessary internal reserves required to maintain the adequate regulatory capital set by the financial authorities for each sector. As such, our dividend levels are determined based on a comprehensive analysis taking into consideration the current and future asset growth of our subsidiaries and us, as well as the surplus funds after maintaining the appropriate capital adequacy ratios, among others. In February 2023, we announced our “Shareholder Return Policy and Group Capital Management Plan Based on the Total Shareholder Return (“TSR”) Ratio (including both dividends and treasury share buyback/cancellations)” through our investor relations disclosures. Furthermore, in our business report for the fiscal year 2023, we disclosed the modifications we made to our existing shareholder return policy, including an adjustment to the Common Equity Tier 1 (“CET1”) ratio target from 12% to 13%, taking into consideration the strengthened capital regulations, our enhanced loss absorption capacity, and the overall competitive landscape. In July 2024, we became the first Korean bank holding company to announce a “Corporate Value Enhancement Plan,” in which we announced that we would strive to enhance corporate value by “enhancing shareholder return capabilities based on the CET1 ratio,” and presented a TSR target of 50%.
