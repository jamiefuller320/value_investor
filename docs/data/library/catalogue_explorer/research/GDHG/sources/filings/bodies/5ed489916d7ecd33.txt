(“Dacheng Culture”) and Fuzhou Yibang Amusement Park Co., Ltd. (“Fuzhou Yibang”). Pursuant to the Cooperation Agreement, the parties will jointly develop wedding photography and light show projects in Tongling city and Changde city, China (the “Projects”). Nanping Golden Heaven will coordinate and supervise the operation of the Projects and participate in sharing of project profits during the 6-month cooperation period. Dacheng Culture and Fuzhou Yibang will be responsible for professional photography and light show activities and providing relevant venues, respectively.
