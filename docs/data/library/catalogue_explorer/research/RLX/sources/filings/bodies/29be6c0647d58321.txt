By pairing these strengths with dedicated local field teams and an open, ecosystem philosophy, we are gaining sharper retail insights that will empower us to tailor products to local consumer preferences, scale our portfolio, and capture market share as shifting global regulations reshape the industry.” Mr. Chao Lu, Chief Financial Officer of RLX Technology, commented, “We delivered strong third quarter financial results, with the consolidation driving a surge in net revenues to RMB1.92 billion, up 69.7% year over year. Financial Measures The Company uses net income, income from operations and basic and diluted net income per ADS, each a financial measure, in evaluating its operating results and for financial and operational purposes. net income represents net income excluding compensation expenses, amortization and depreciation of assets arising from fair value in business acquisitions, and tax effects on adjustments. income from operations represents net income from operations excluding compensation expenses and amortization and depreciation of assets arising from fair value in business acquisitions. basic and diluted net income per ADS is computed using net income attributable to RLX Technology Inc. and the same number of ADSs used in the U.S. GAAP basic and diluted net income per ADS calculation. The Company presents these financial measures because they are used by the management to evaluate its operating performance and formulate business plans. The Company believes that they help identify underlying trends in its business that could otherwise be distorted by the effect of certain expenses that are included in net income. The Company also believes that the use of the measures facilitates investors’ assessment of its operating performance, as they could provide useful information about its operating results, enhance the overall understanding of its past performance and future prospects, and allow for greater visibility with respect to key metrics used by the management in its financial and operational decision making. The financial measures are not defined under U.S. GAAP and are not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. The financial measures have limitations as analytical tools. They should not be considered in isolation or construed as an alternative to net income, basic and diluted net income per ADS or any other measure of performance or as an indicator of its operating performance. Investors are encouraged to review its historical financial measures against the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measures. The financial measures here may not be comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies. Other companies may calculate similarly titled measures differently, limiting their usefulness as comparative measures to our data. The Company encourages investors and others to review its financial information in its entirety and not rely on any single financial measure.
