AND SUBSIDIARIES Notes to Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements For the Interim Periods Ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 Note 1 — Overview Par Pacific Holdings, Inc. and its wholly owned subsidiaries (“Par” or the “Company”) provide both renewable and conventional fuels to the western United States. During the third quarter of 2026, we began presenting Renewables as a separate reportable segment, reflecting our continued investment in and development of our renewable fuels operations. Please read “Note 19—Segment Information” for further information. We currently operate in four primary business segments: 1) Refining - We own and operate four refineries. Our refineries in Kapolei, Hawaii; Newcastle, Wyoming; Tacoma, Washington; and Billings, Montana, convert crude oil into gasoline, distillate, asphalt, and other products to serve the state of Hawaii and areas ranging from the state of Washington to the Dakotas and Wyoming. 2) Retail - We operate fuel retail outlets in Hawaii, Washington, and Idaho. We operate convenience stores and fuel retail sites under our “Hele” and “nomnom” brands, “76” branded fuel retail sites, and other sites operated by third parties that sell gasoline, diesel, and retail merchandise such as soft drinks, prepared foods, and other sundries. We also operate unattended cardlock stations. 3) Logistics - We operate an extensive logistics network spanning the Pacific, the Northwest, and the Rocky Mountain regions. This network includes a mooring in Hawaii, a unit rail loading terminal in Washington, and other terminals, pipelines, trucking operations, marine vessels, storage facilities, loading and truck racks, and rail facilities for the movement of petroleum, refined products, and ethanol in and among the Hawaiian islands, between the U.S. West Coast and Hawaii, and in areas ranging from the state of Washington to the Dakotas and Wyoming. 4) Renewables - In April 2026, Hawaii Renewables, LLC (“Hawaii Renewables”), our joint venture with Alohi Renewable Energy LLC (“Alohi”), an entity owned by Mitsubishi Corporation and ENEOS Corporation, commenced operations at the new renewable fuels facility with our Hawaii refinery (“Renewable Fuels Facility”). The Renewable Fuels Facility is designed to produce approximately 61 million gallons annually of renewable diesel, sustainable aviation fuel, renewable naphtha, and liquefied petroleum gases. On August 25, 2026, Laramie Energy, LLC (“Laramie Energy”) entered into a definitive agreement to sell substantially all of its oil and gas assets to a third party. Upon closing, we will exit our noncontrolling ownership investment in Laramie Energy. The transaction is expected to close by the end of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions. Please read “Note 4—Investment in Laramie Energy” for further information. As of September 30, 2026, we owned the following investments: • a 46 % equity investment in Laramie Energy; • a 65 % equity investment in Yellowstone Energy Limited Partnership (“YELP”); • a 40 % equity investment in Yellowstone Pipeline Company (“YPLC”); and • a 63.5 % ownership interest in Hawaii Renewables. Our Corporate and Other reportable segment primarily includes general and administrative costs.
