Form 20-F ☒ Form 40-F ☐ 9F Inc. Reports First Half 2026 Unaudited Financial Results BEIJING, CHINA — November 6, 2026— 9F Inc. (“9F” or “the Company”) (NASDAQ: JFU), a digital technology service provider aiming to empower institutional partners with advanced financial technologies as well as attract investors with investment opportunities, today announced its unaudited financial results for the six months ended June 30, 2026. First Half 2026 Financial Highlights ● Total net revenues were RMB78.7 million (US$11.6 million), representing a decrease of 48.3% from RMB152.1 million in the first half of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to the decrease in our sales income revenues because of declined order volume, and the decrease in our technical services revenues resulted from the business contraction of our institutional partners which were actively adapting to the challenging market environment marked by continued industry contraction and tighter regulatory oversight. ● Net income was RMB704.6 million (US$103.8 million), as compared to net income of RMB218.6 million in the first half of 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to unrealized gains from our investments in marketable securities as of June 30, 2026, which were made on a temporary basis for better management of our cash reserve. Unrealized gains from our investments are primarily derived from unrealized gains on wealth management products held by the Company, which are measured at market valuation and are subject to significant volatility. If the Company chooses to dispose of these assets at an unfavorable time, such gains could turn negative and result in losses. Over the past six months, the Company’s operations have faced pressures from regulatory developments and business contraction, among other factors, and there is a possibility that profits may continue to decline and turn into losses. It is the income from wealth management products that has enabled our overall financial performance to remain stable. ● Adjusted net income 1 was RMB153.9 million (US$22.7 million), as compared to an adjusted net income of RMB105.1 million in the first half of 2025. ● Cash and cash equivalents and term deposits were RMB1,129.6 million (US$166.5 million), representing a 157.7% increase compared with RMB438.4 million as of December 31, 2025. The increase was primarily due to the disposals of certain marketable securities we hold for temporary cash management purposes. ● Restricted cash was RMB282.5 million (US$41.6 million), representing a 10.5% decrease from RMB315.6 million as of December 31, 2025, of which RMB198.3 million was cash subject to temporary judicial freeze in connection with certain ongoing litigations, and RMB65.7 million was cash we received from investors under Internet Securities Services for Hong Kong and U.S. stock markets for their investment in securities. Use of Financial Measures The Company uses adjusted net income, a financial measure, in evaluating its operating results and as a supplemental measure to review and assess its financial and operational performance. The Company believes that adjusted net income provides useful information about its core operating results, enhances the overall understanding of its past performance and future prospects and allows for greater visibility with respect to key metrics used by the Company’s management in its financial and operational . The Company also believes that adjusted net income, which excludes the effect of compensation expenses, impairment loss of investments, unrealized gain of investment in marketable securities and tax effect of adjustments, helps identify underlying trends in its business and helps the Company’s management formulate business plans. Adjusted net income is not defined under U.S. GAAP and is not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. This financial measure has limitations as an analytical tool, and when assessing the Company’s operating performance, cash flows or liquidity, investors should not consider it in isolation, or as a substitute for net income, cash flows provided by operating activities or other consolidated statements of operation and cash flow data prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Other companies, including peer companies in the industry, may calculate this measure differently, which may reduce its usefulness as a comparative measure. The Company encourages investors and others to review its financial information in its entirety and not rely on a single financial measure. The Company compensates for these limitations by reconciling the financial measure to the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP financial measure, which should be considered when evaluating the Company’s performance. For more information on this financial measure, please see the table captioned “Unaudited Reconciliations of GAAP and results” set forth at the end of this press release. Recent Development Shareholder Agreement On April 23, 2018, Nine F Capital Limited, an entity controlled by the family trust of our chairman of the board, Mr. Lei Sun, acquired 13,585,200 of our ordinary shares from Nine Fortune Limited, an entity controlled by our director, Mr. Yifan Ren, for approximately US$96 million. The closing and registration of the transfer of all such shares was subsequently closed. We have been advised that the parties subsequently entered into an amendment agreement (the “Amendment Agreement”), pursuant to which, the consideration for the target shares was as follows: (i) for 2,160,000 ordinary shares, the purchase consideration was set at US$9.259259 per share, for an aggregate consideration of approximately US$20.0 million, and (ii) for the remaining ordinary shares, a purchase price conditioned on the occurrence of certain event without any guaranteed minimum portion was set. Such event did not subsequently happen and therefore no purchase consideration for such remaining shares was applied. As of the date of this announcement, Nine F Capital Limited has paid in full all consideration payable by it under the Amendment Agreement in the total amount of US$20 million, and has no further payment obligations thereunder. As previously disclosed, Nine F Capital Limited satisfied the consideration using borrowed funds in 2018, and in connection with our initial public offering in 2019, Nine F Capital Limited sold 2,150,000 ordinary shares, with the proceeds from such sale applied in full toward repayment of the corresponding US$20 million loan. Such proceeds were designated exclusively for repayment of the loan in connection with the payment of the purchase price, and were remitted in full to the lending bank through a payment channel. Upon such repayment, Nine F Capital Limited did not receive any additional or residual proceeds from the sale of its shares in our initial public offering. We did not receive, and will not receive, any proceeds from the aforementioned transactions, as they were arrangements among the relevant shareholders and did not result in any proceeds being paid to the Company.
