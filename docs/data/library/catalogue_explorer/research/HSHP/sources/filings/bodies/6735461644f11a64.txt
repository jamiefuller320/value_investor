(HSHP) – Commercial Update and Key Information Relating to the Cash Distribution for September 2026 Hamilton, Bermuda, October 7 , 2026 Commercial update: In September 2026, Himalaya Shipping Ltd. (“Himalaya,” or the “Company”) achieved average time charter equivalent (“TCE”) earnings of approximately US$65,800 per day, gross [1] , including average daily scrubber benefits of approximately US$2,300 per day. The Company’s eight vessels trading on time charters earned approximately US$71,100 per day, gross, including average daily scrubber benefits. The Company’s four vessels trading on fixed time charters earned approximately US$55,100 per day, gross, including average daily scrubber benefits. The Baltic 5TC 180 Capesize Index averaged US$49,209 during September 2026. Cash distribution: The Board has approved a cash distribution of US$0.28 per share for September 2026. The distribution will be made from the Company's Contributed Surplus account.
