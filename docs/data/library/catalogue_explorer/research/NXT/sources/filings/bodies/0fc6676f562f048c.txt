Description of business and organization of Nextpower Inc. Nextpower Inc. and its subsidiaries (“Nextpower”, “we”, the “Company”) is a leading global provider of solar and energy technology solutions for power plants. Founded in 2013 by our Chief Executive Officer, Dan Shugar, Nextpower pioneered and remains the global market leader in solar tracking systems. Nextpower now delivers an integrated suite of structural, electrical, and digital solutions across the full lifecycle of solar power plants, from design and construction through operations and maintenance. Nextpower’s integrated solutions are designed to streamline project execution, increase energy yield and reliability, and enhance customer return on investment. Nextpower has operations in the United States, Brazil, Argentina, Peru, Mexico, Spain and other locations in Europe, India, Australia, the Middle East and Africa. 2. Product warranty Nextpower offers an assurance type warranty for its products against defects in design, materials and workmanship for a period ranging from two to ten years , depending on the component. For these assurance type warranties, a provision for estimated future costs related to warranty expense is recorded when they are probable and reasonably estimable, which is typically when products are delivered. The estimated warranty liability is based on the Company’s warranty model which relies on historical warranty claim information and assumptions based on the nature, frequency and average cost of claims for each product line by project. When little or no experience exists, the estimate is based on comparable product lines and/or estimated potential failure rates. These estimates are based on data from Nextpower specific projects. Estimates related to the outstanding warranty liability are on an ongoing basis using information and revisions are made as necessary.
