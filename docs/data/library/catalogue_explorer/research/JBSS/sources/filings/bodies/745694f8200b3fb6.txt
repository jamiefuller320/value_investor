Retail prices across snack nuts and trail mix have generally risen due to increased commodity costs and evolving global trade agreements. These higher prices, paired with general economic uncertainty, are causing consumers to purchase fewer snack products. As a result, sales volumes for snack nuts, trail mix and mainstream bars are declining for the Company and the industry overall. Many consumers are shifting to private brands, more affordable nuts or bars or choosing snacks outside these categories altogether. Consumers are also shifting to more retailers, such as mass merchandising retailers and club stores, not all of which we distribute or sell to. We see emerging health and wellness trends, use of drugs and other consumer health priorities impacting consumers' purchasing behavior. In response, we are focusing on products in our portfolio that we believe address shifting consumer priorities, as well as our strengths by leveraging our expertise in the snack nut, trail mix and bars categories; developing additional products; improving efficiency; innovating in product and packaging; and carefully managing trade spending and pricing to support our products.
