Additionally, commodity prices exhibit mean reversion, whereby asset prices tend to move back toward their average over time. A valuation approach capable of capturing the and mean reversion feature is therefore required. The Monte Carlo Simulation is a commonly used approach to model securities with payoffs and mean reversion features. Monte Carlo Simulation uses random sampling techniques based on continuous time Stochastic processes to generate a large number of possible, but random, asset price paths.
