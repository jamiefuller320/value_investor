We are on track to initiate an adaptive platform trial, which is scheduled to start with a randomized placebo controlled evaluating single agent COM701 therapy in Q2 2025. This development path is supported by a strong clinical and biological rationale and has the potential to open the door to advance COM701 as a single agent and as a backbone to future drug combinations.” Dr. continued, “We are also encouraged by the promising rilvegostomig data presented by our partner, AstraZeneca, in 2024. About Compugen Compugen is a therapeutic discovery and development company utilizing its broadly applicable predictive computational discovery platform (Unigen TM ) to identify new drug targets and biological pathways for developing cancer immunotherapies. Compugen has two proprietary product candidates in Phase 1 development: COM701, a potential antibody and COM902, a potential antibody targeting TIGIT for the treatment of solid tumors. Rilvegostomig, a /TIGIT bispecific antibody where the TIGIT component is derived from Compugen’s clinical stage antibody, COM902, is in Phase 3 development by AstraZeneca through a license agreement for the development of bispecific and multispecific antibodies. (previously COM503), a potential , high affinity binding protein antibody, which is in Phase 1 development is licensed to Gilead. In addition, the Company’s therapeutic pipeline of programs consists of research programs aiming to address various mechanisms to enhance immunity. Compugen is headquartered in Israel, with offices in San Francisco, CA. Compugen’s shares are listed on Nasdaq and the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol CGEN.
