REVENUE PERFORMANCE OBLIGATIONS We recognize revenue when control of promised goods or services (“performance obligations”) is transferred to customers in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to receive in exchange for those goods or services (“transaction price”). Unsatisfied performance obligations as of September 30, 2026 primarily consist of advances on stumpage contracts, unearned license revenue, unearned carbon capture and storage revenue, hunting and other access rights on our timberlands, payments received for shipments where control of goods has not transferred, member related activities at an owned country club and certain obligations for real estate sales. Of these performance obligations, $ 42.7 million is expected to be recognized within the next twelve months , with the remaining $ 12.3 million expected to be recognized thereafter as we satisfy our performance obligations. We generally collect payment within one year of satisfying a performance obligation; therefore, we have elected the practical expedient not to adjust revenue for a financing component.
