Item 1.02 Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement. Effective as of April 30, 2026, , LLC (“ ”), a majority owned joint venture subsidiary of Strattec Security Corporation (the “Company”), entered into an Amended and Restated Credit Agreement with BMO Bank N.A. (the “New JV Credit Agreement”) that replaced ’s existing credit agreement, dated June 28, 2012, with BMO Bank N.A. (formerly BMO Harris Bank N.A.), as amended (the “Prior JV Credit Agreement”). The Company was a guarantor under the Prior JV Credit Agreement, and, as a result, previously disclosed the Prior JV Credit Agreement as a material definitive agreement. The Company is not a borrower, guarantor or other credit support party under the New JV Credit Agreement.
