Exchange rates were 1.3516, 1.3930, and 1.4403 CAD per USD on September 30, 2025, December 31, 2024, and September 30, 2024, respectively. UTMD manages its foreign currency risk without separate hedging transactions by invoicing customers in the local currency where costs of production were incurred, by converting currencies as transactions occur and by optimizing global account structures through liquidity management accounts.
