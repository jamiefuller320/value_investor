.1 2 .htm .1 .1 Agora, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results SANTA CLARA, Calif., November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Agora, Inc. (NASDAQ: API) (the “Company”), a pioneer and leader in conversational AI and engagement technology, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. “We are pleased to report our third consecutive quarter of accelerating revenue growth, driven by strength across both our engagement and conversational AI businesses, and our eighth consecutive quarter of GAAP profitability,” said Tony Zhao, Founder, Chairman, and CEO of Agora, Inc. “Adoption of our conversational AI products is expanding rapidly across industries and use cases, and we are now serving more than one million agent calls per day. We also continue to push the boundaries of technology. At our Intelligent Engagement Conference last month, we announced the Agora Conversational Agent System, built on our global network. The system can perceive, recognize intent, reason, and respond in a continuous, manner, and includes capabilities such as memory, evaluation, and . As we continue to enhance its performance, we believe the system will enable us to address a broader range of use cases and workflows, while further differentiating us from the competition.” Third Quarter 2026 Highlights • Total revenues for the quarter were $42.1 million, an increase of 19.0% from $35.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. • Active Customers as of September 30, 2026 were 4,024, compared to 3,944 as of September 30, 2025. • Net Retention Rate for the quarter was 110%, compared to 97% in the third quarter of 2025. • Net income for the quarter was $3.0 million, compared to $2.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. • Total cash, cash equivalents, bank deposits and financial products issued by banks as of September 30, 2026 was $363.0 million. • Net cash provided by operating activities for the quarter was $2.2 million, compared to $0.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results Revenues Total revenues were $42.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 19.0% from $35.4 million in the same period last year, primarily due to the continued expansion of our engagement service in sectors such as live shopping and social live streaming and rapid adoption of our conversational AI products. Cost of Revenues Cost of revenues was $15.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 27.9% from $12.0 million in the same period last year, primarily due to increases in bandwidth and server costs and costs related to conversational AI products. Gross Profit and Gross Margin Gross profit was $26.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 14.4% from $23.3 million in the same period last year. Gross margin was 63.4% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 66.0% in the same period last year, mainly due to product mix changes, including conversational AI products remaining at a stage. Operating Expenses Operating expenses were $28.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 11.9% from $25.3 million in the same period last year. • Research and development expenses were $15.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 14.6% from $13.8 million in the same period last year, primarily due to increased investment in conversational AI products. • Sales and marketing expenses were $6.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 3.2% from $6.5 million in the same period last year, primarily due to an increase in advertising expenses. • General and administrative expenses were $5.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 15.9% from $5.0 million in the same period last year, primarily due to an increase in compensation from $0.3 million in the third quarter of 2025 to $0.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, and an increase in allowance for current expected credit losses, mainly as a result of business expansion. Loss from Operations Loss from operations was $1.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.6 million in the same period last year. Interest Income Interest income was $3.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 6.9% from $3.9 million in the same period last year, primarily due to the decrease in the average principal amount. Investment Income (Loss) Investment income was $1.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to investment loss of $0.3 million in the same period last year, primarily due to fair value changes in equity investments. Net Income per American Depositary Share Attributable to Ordinary Shareholders Basic and diluted net income per American Depositary Share (“ADS”) attributable to ordinary shareholders were $0.04 and $0.03, respectively, in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $0.03 and $0.03, respectively, in the same period last year. 1 Share Repurchase Program During the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company repurchased approximately 1.8 million of its Class A ordinary shares (equivalent to approximately 0.5 million ADSs) for approximately US$1.9 million. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had repurchased approximately 180.3 million of its Class A ordinary shares (equivalent to approximately 45.1 million ADSs) for approximately US$161.8 million under the current share repurchase program. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had 333.4 million ordinary shares (equivalent to approximately 83.4 million ADSs) outstanding, compared to 449.8 million ordinary shares (equivalent to approximately 112.4 million ADSs) as of the inception of the program. The current share repurchase program will expire at the end of February 2027. Financial Outlook Based on currently available information, the Company expects total revenues for the fourth quarter of 2026 to be between $44.0 million and $45.0 million, representing growth of 15.3% to 17.9%. This outlook reflects the Company's current and preliminary views on the market and operational conditions, which are subject to change. Operating Metrics The Company also uses other operating metrics included in this press release and defined below to assess the performance of its business. Active Customers An active customer at the end of any period is defined as an organization or individual developer from which the Company generated more than $100 of revenue during the preceding 12 months, excluding customers from Easemob. Customers are counted based on unique customer account identifiers. Generally, one software application uses the same customer account identifier throughout its life cycle while one account may be used for multiple applications. Net Retention Rate Net Retention Rate is calculated by comparing the quarterly revenue from paying customers, excluding revenue from certain products, in the quarter four quarters prior to the most recent quarter to the quarterly revenue from the same set of customers in the most recent quarter. The Company believes Net Retention Rate facilitates operating performance comparisons on a basis.
