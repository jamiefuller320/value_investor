We conduct our operations in Chinese mainland through (i) our Chinese mainland subsidiaries and (ii) the VIEs with which we have maintained contractual arrangements and their subsidiaries in Chinese mainland. PRC laws and regulations impose certain restrictions or prohibitions on foreign ownership of companies that engage in certain telecommunication services, internet program services and certain other businesses. Accordingly, we operate these businesses in Chinese mainland through the VIEs and their subsidiaries, and rely on contractual arrangements among our Chinese mainland subsidiaries, the VIEs and their nominee shareholders to control the business operations of the VIEs.
