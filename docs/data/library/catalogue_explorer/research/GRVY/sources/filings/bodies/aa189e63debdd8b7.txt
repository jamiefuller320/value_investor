(NasdaqGM: GRVY) (“Gravity” or “Company”), a developer and publisher of online and mobile games based in South Korea, today announced its unaudited financial results for the fourth quarter ended December 31, 2025, prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board and business updates. PRELIMINARY UNAUDITED FOURTH QUARTER 2025 FINANCIAL RESULTS Tentative consolidated revenue for the fourth quarter of 2025 is KRW 113 billion, and tentative consolidated operating profit is KRW 12 billion. The preliminary fourth quarter of 2025 result is mainly attributed by decreased revenues from Ragnarok M: Classic launched in Southeast Asia on February 14, 2025 and in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau on April 16, 2025. Tentative consolidated revenue for the fourth quarter of 2025 represented an 18.6% decrease in QoQ and a 12.9% decrease in YoY. Unaudited preliminary consolidated revenue for 2025 is KRW 560 billion, and the operating profit is KRW 77 billion. The preliminary 2025 figures are unaudited and subject to revision. Final result for the fourth quarter and year ended December 31, 2025 will be provided by our annual report for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 on Form 20-F. Liquidity The balance of cash and cash equivalents and financial instruments was KRW 618,633 million as of December 31, 2025.
