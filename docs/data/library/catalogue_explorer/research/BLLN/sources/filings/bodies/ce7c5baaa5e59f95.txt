On November 7, 2025, the Company completed its IPO of an aggregate of 5,233,765 shares of Class A Common Stock (which includes the exercise in full of the underwriters’ option to purchase an additional 682,665 shares of Class A Common Stock) at a price to the public of $60.00 per share.
