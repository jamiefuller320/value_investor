Organizational changes included the immediate discontinuation of the Vaux Freight Movement System, as the Company focuses on the Vaux Autonomy™ product offering. MANAGEMENT’S DISCUSSIO N AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS ​ General ​ ArcBest Corporation™ (together with its subsidiaries, the “Company,” “ArcBest ® ,” “we,” “us,” and “our”) is a multibillion-dollar integrated logistics company that leverages connected technology, flexible capacity, and a full suite of shipping and logistics solutions across multiple modes of transportation to meet customers’ supply chain needs. Our operations are conducted through two reportable operating segments: , which consists of ABF Freight System, Inc. and certain other subsidiaries (“ABF Freight”), and , the Company’s logistics operations. References to the Company, including “we,” “us,” and “our,” in this Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, are primarily to the Company and its subsidiaries on a consolidated basis. ​ Restructuring Plan In July 2026, the Company announced a restructuring plan to realign our operating structure, reduce costs and simplify brand architecture, create a more seamless customer experience, and position the Company for growth and profitability. Effective August 1, 2026, the Company unified the MoLo ® and Panther ® brands and certain other subsidiaries under the ArcBest ® brand. The organizational changes included the immediate discontinuation of the Vaux Freight Movement System, as the Company focuses on the Vaux Autonomy™ product offering. During the second quarter of 2026, the Company recorded asset impairment charges of $25.7 million to write off the remaining carrying value of the Panther trade name and $50.8 million related to the discontinuation of the Vaux Freight Movement System. ​ The restructuring plan eliminates approximately 2% of the Company’s total positions through workforce reductions and the elimination of certain open positions and includes the closure of ten ABF Freight service centers, collectively representing approximately 1% of our network doors. The service center consolidations constituted a change of operations under our collective bargaining agreement (the “2023 ABF NMFA”) with the International Brotherhood of Teamsters (the “IBT”). The joint Change of Operations Committee approved the consolidations pursuant to the 2023 ABF NMFA, and the closures are expected to be completed during the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company evaluated the remaining restructuring actions for impairment and does not currently expect additional material impairment charges related to the restructuring plan.
