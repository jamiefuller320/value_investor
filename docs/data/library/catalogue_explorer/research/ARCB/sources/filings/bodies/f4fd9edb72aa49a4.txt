MANAGEMENT’S DISCUSSIO N AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS ​ General ​ ArcBest Corporation™ (together with its subsidiaries, the “Company,” “ArcBest ® ,” “we,” “us,” and “our”) is a multibillion-dollar integrated logistics company that leverages technology and a full suite of shipping and logistics solutions across multiple modes of transportation to meet customers’ supply chain needs. Our operations are conducted through two reportable operating segments: , which consists of ABF Freight System, Inc. and certain other subsidiaries (“ABF Freight”), and , which includes MoLo Solutions, LLC (“MoLo”), Panther Premium Logistics ® , and certain other subsidiaries. References to the Company, including “we,” “us,” and “our,” in this Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, are primarily to the Company and its subsidiaries on a consolidated basis. ​ Restructuring Plan In July 2026, the Company announced a restructuring plan designed to realign our operating structure, reduce costs and simplify brand architecture through a series of organizational changes designed to create a more seamless customer experience and position the Company for growth and profitability. Effective August 1, 2026, the MoLo ® Panther ® brands and certain other subsidiaries will operate under the ArcBest ® brand. The series of organizational changes included the discontinuation of Vaux Freight Movement System, as the Company focuses Vaux operations on the Vaux Smart Autonomy product offering.
