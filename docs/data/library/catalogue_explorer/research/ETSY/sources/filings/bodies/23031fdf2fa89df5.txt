Notes to Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements Note 1—Basis of Presentation and Summary of Significant Accounting Policies Description of Business Etsy operates a online marketplace that connects millions of creative entrepreneurs with buyers around the world. The Etsy marketplace is the global destination for unique, creative goods from independent sellers, connecting artisans with thoughtful consumers seeking items that reflect their tastes and values. The Company generates revenue primarily from marketplace activities, including transaction fees (inclusive of offsite advertising), payments processing fees, and listing fees, as well as from optional seller services, which include advertising and shipping labels. Basis of Consolidation The condensed consolidated financial statements include the accounts of Etsy and its subsidiaries. All intercompany balances and transactions have been eliminated in consolidation. On July 30, 2026 (the “Closing Date”), Etsy completed the sale of Depop Limited (“Depop”), its fashion resale marketplace, and on June 2, 2025, Etsy completed the sale of Reverb Holdings, Inc. (“Reverb”), its musical instrument marketplace. The financial results of Depop and Reverb have been included in Etsy’s condensed consolidated financial statements through July 30, 2026 and until June 2, 2025, respectively. Assets Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations The Company classifies a business as held for sale when management commits to a plan to sell it, the sale is probable within one year, and the business is actively marketed at a price that is reasonable in relation to its fair value. Upon such classification, the Company measures the business at the lower of its carrying value or fair value less costs to sell, and ceases prospective depreciation and amortization. If the disposal represents a strategic shift that has, or will have, a major effect on operations and financial results, the Company presents the business as a discontinued operation and retrospectively reclassifies prior period amounts. As previously disclosed, on February 15, 2026, the Company executed a Sale and Purchase Agreement, as supplemented on May 21, 2026 and as amended on July 12, 2026 (collectively, the "Purchase Agreement"), to sell all of the outstanding equity interests of Depop to eBay Inc. ("eBay"), a Delaware corporation. The Company entered into the Purchase Agreement in an effort to focus on driving growth in the core Etsy marketplace. The Company determined in the first quarter of 2026 that the assets of the Depop subsidiary met the criteria for classification as held for sale. The Company analyzed the quantitative and qualitative factors relevant to the divestiture of Depop and determined the disposal represents a strategic shift that has a major effect on its operations and financial results. As such, the assets and liabilities, results of operations, and cash flows of Depop are presented as discontinued operations in the Consolidated Balance Sheets, Consolidated Statements of Operations, and Condensed Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, respectively, for all periods presented through the Closing Date. In addition, divestiture-related expenses incurred as part of the Depop divestiture have been included in discontinued operations. Unless otherwise noted, all amounts and disclosures included in the Notes to Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements reflect only the Company’s continuing operations and exclude the results of the Depop marketplace.
