The Company continues to see strong growth from the Veth product line, along with the initial impact of expanding military demand at the Katsa operation in Finland, improved oil and gas demand and generally strong market conditions across the global enterprise. Global sales of marine and propulsion products improved 14.4% from the prior year, largely on the strength of the Veth product demand. Shipments of transmission products decreased (14.7%) with reduced shipments of ARFF transmissions as we transition assembly to our Lufkin facility. The North American region, saw a significant decrease in revenue ($3.1 million or 11.8%) as the current tariff structure continues to hamper shipments into the region. Sales into Europe increased 31.4%, or $9.8 million, on growing demand for the Veth and Katsa product lines. The Asia Pacific region increased 26.2%, or $3.8 million, on stronger commercial marine demand and improved shipments of oilfield transmissions into China. With this improvement, Asia Pacific represented 20.4% of fiscal first quarter revenue compared to 17.9% in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Currency translation had an unfavorable impact on fiscal 2027 sales compared to the prior year totaling $0.7 million, primarily due to the weakening of the euro against the U.S. dollar. 23 Sales for our manufacturing segment increased 8.4%, or $6.2 million, versus the same period last year. The Company’s Veth propulsion operation in the Netherlands experienced a 17.5% increase in sales compared to fiscal 2026, continuing a strong growth trend. The primary driver for this increase remains growing demand through market and geographic penetration for the Company’s innovative propulsion solutions around the globe, along with improving supply chain and operational performance. The Company’s Katsa operation in Finland experienced an increase of 31.9%, with acceleration in demand from European military applications. The Company’s domestic manufacturing operation had essentially flat revenue compared to the prior year first quarter, as improved oil and gas demand was offset by reduced ARFF shipments. The Company’s Italian manufacturing operations reported a 6.7% decrease in sales from fiscal 2026 reflecting a difficult European market for its marine and propulsion products. The Company’s Belgian manufacturing operation was essentially flat with the prior year first quarter, with shipments into the US still hampered by the existing tariff structure. The Company’s Swiss manufacturing operation, which supplies customized propellers for the global mega yacht and patrol boat markets, experienced a 15.0% decrease in sales compared to the first quarter of fiscal 2026, primarily due shipment timing on some larger propulsion projects. Sales for our distribution segment were up 23.3%, or $5.1 million, compared to fiscal 2026. The Company’s Asian distribution operations in Singapore, China and Japan experienced a 32.5% increase in sales on improved demand for energy related products in China and strong commercial marine activity in the region. The Company’s North American distribution operation reported a 23.8% increase compared to the prior year on a in tariff delayed demand. The Company’s European distribution operation was up 15.1% from the prior year, driven by improving European demand. The Company’s distribution operation in Australia and New Zealand, which provides boat accessories, propulsion and marine transmission systems for the pleasure craft market, saw a 14.7% increase in revenue on improving demand for pleasure craft products in the region. Gross profit for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 improved $2.5 million, or 10.9%, to $25.4 million on a sales increase of $8.7 million. Gross profit as a percentage of sales remained level with the prior year first quarter at 28.7%. Margin for the quarter was favorably impacted by the additional sales volume (approximately $3.5 million). This positive movement was partially offset by a less favorable mix of products ($1.0 million), along with the incremental impact of tariff dilution. Marketing, engineering, and administrative (ME&A) expenses of $20.8 million were up just $0.1 million, or 0.7%, in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior fiscal year. As a percentage of sales, ME&A expenses decreased to 23.5% of sales versus 25.9% of sales in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The small increase in ME&A spending in fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year first quarter was primarily driven by an inflationary impact to wages and benefits nearly offset by active and aggressive spending controls. Interest expense of $0.4 million for the fiscal 2027 first quarter was $0.4 million lower than fiscal 2026 due to a reduced average balance and a lower average interest rate. In the fiscal 2027 first quarter, other expense, net, of $0.2 million decreased by $0.6 million from the prior fiscal year first quarter other expense, net, of $0.9 million. On June 30, 2026, Twin Disc, Incorporated (the “Company”) entered into a Credit Agreement (the “Credit Agreement”) among the Company, as Borrower; Kobelt Manufacturing Co. Ltd. ("Kobelt"), as Guarantor; Bank of Montreal, as a Lender, Administrative Agent, Swing Line Lender, and L/C Issuer; and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. ("Chase"), as a Lender. The Credit Agreement refinanced and replaced the credit agreement dated February 14, 2025 among the Company, Kobelt, and Bank of Montreal. Capitalized terms that are not otherwise defined herein are defined in the Credit Agreement. Pursuant to the Credit Agreement, Bank of Montreal and Chase (the “Lenders”) made Term Loans to the Company in an aggregate principal amount of $30.0 million. The maturity date of the Term Loans is June 30, 2031, and the Company is required to make principal installments on the Term Loans of at least $0.4 million per quarter (increasing to $0.6 million per quarter for the quarter ending on or about September 30, 2028, and $0.8 million per quarter for the quarter ending on or about September 30, 2030). 25 The Credit Agreement also allows the Company to enter into Revolving Loans with the Lenders from time to time prior to June 30, 2031 (the “Revolving Credit Termination Date”) in amounts not to exceed $60.0 million (the “Revolving Credit Commitment”). The Revolving Credit Commitment includes a $5.0 million sublimit for Swing Loans and a $4.0 million sublimit for Letters of Credit that may be requested by the Company from time to time until the Revolving Credit Termination Date. Each Swing Loan or Letter of Credit provided pursuant to the terms of the Credit Agreement shall be a Revolving Loan provided under the Revolving Credit Commitment. Interest rates under the Credit Agreement are based on the secured overnight financing rate (“SOFR”), the euro interbank offered rate (the “EURIBO Rate”), the Canadian Overnight Repo Rate Average (the “CORRA”), or a Base Rate based on the highest of the prime rate, federal funds rate, or Term SOFR. Loans under the Credit Agreement are designated as either as SOFR Loans, which accrue interest at a Term SOFR plus an Applicable Margin; Base Rate Loans, which accrue interest at the Base Rate plus an Applicable Margin; Eurodollar Loans, which accrue interest at the EURIBO Rate plus an Applicable Margin; Term CORRA Loans, which accrue interest at an Adjusted Term CORRA plus an Applicable Margin; Daily Compounded CORRA Loans, which accrue interest at a Daily Compounded CORRA plus an Applicable Margin; or Canadian Prime Rate Loans, which accrue interest at the Canadian Prime Rate plus an Applicable Margin. The Applicable Margin for Loans is between 1.50% and 3.00%, and the Applicable Margin for the Unused Revolving Credit Commitment is between 0.15% and 0.30% (each depending on the Company’s Total Funded Debt to EBITDA ratio). The Term Loan has been designated as a SOFR Loan. Borrowings under the Credit Agreement are secured by substantially all of the Company’s and Kobelt’s personal property, including accounts receivable, inventory, machinery and equipment, and intellectual property. The Company has also pledged 65% of its equity interests in certain foreign subsidiaries. To effect these security interests, the Company entered into an Amended and Restated Security Agreement, Amended and Restated IP Security Agreement, Amended and Restated Pledge Agreement, and Amended and Restated Perfection Certificate with the Administrative Agent, and the Company has entered into an Amended Restated Agreement as to Liens and Encumbrances and an Amended and Restated Negative Pledge Agreement with the Administrative Agent with regard to the Company’s real property. Upon the occurrence of an Event of Default, the Administrative Agent may take the following actions upon written notice to the Company: (1) terminate the remaining Commitments and all obligations of the Lenders under the Credit Agreement; (2) declare the principal and accrued interest of all Loans outstanding under the Credit Agreement to be immediately due and payable; and (3) demand the Company to immediately Cash Collateralize the outstanding L/C Obligations in an amount equal to 105% of the aggregate L/C Obligations. If such Event of Default is due to the Company’s bankruptcy, the actions and obligations listed above shall occur without notice to the Company. The Company remains in compliance with its liquidity and other covenants under the Credit Agreement. Management believes that available cash, the Credit Agreement, the unsecured lines of credit, cash generated from future operations, and potential access to debt markets will be adequate to fund the Company's cash and capital requirements for the foreseeable future.
