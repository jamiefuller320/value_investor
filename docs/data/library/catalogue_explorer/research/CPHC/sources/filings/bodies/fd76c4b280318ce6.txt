Overview: Canterbury Park Holding Corporation (the “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) conducts wagering operations and hosts “unbanked” card games at its Canterbury Park Racetrack and Casino facility (the “Racetrack”) in Shakopee, Minnesota, which is approximately 20 miles southwest of downtown Minneapolis. The Racetrack is the only facility in the State of Minnesota that offers live thoroughbred and quarter horse racing. The Company’s wagering operations include both wagering on thoroughbred and quarter horse races during live meets at the Racetrack each year from May through September, and wagering on races held at racetracks that are televised simultaneously at the Racetrack (“simulcasting”). Unbanked card games, in which patrons compete against each other, are hosted in the Casino at the Racetrack. The Casino typically operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week. The Casino offers both poker and table games at up to 80 tables. The Company also derives revenues from related services and activities, such as concessions, parking, advertising signage, publication sales, and from other entertainment events and activities held at the Racetrack. The Company uses the analysis to determine if expected future tax increment revenue will exceed the Company's development costs on infrastructure improvements. As a result of our analysis as well as initial payments received in 2025 and additional payments received in 2026 from the City of Shakopee, as of September 30, 2026, management believes the TIF receivable will be fully collectible and no allowance related to this receivable is necessary.
