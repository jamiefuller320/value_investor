First Quarter 2025 Financial Highlights: ● Total revenues for the first quarter were $16.6 million , compared to $14.1 million in the first quarter of 2024, or 17.5% growth . ● GAAP operating income for the period was $1.5 million, or 9% of revenue , compared to an operating loss of $0.3 million for the first quarter of 2024. ● operating income for the period was $3.1 million , or 19% of revenue , compared to operating income of $1.7 million, or 12% of revenue, for the first quarter of 2024. ● GAAP net income for the first quarter was $2.4 million , or $0.15 per diluted share , compared to GAAP net income of $0.8 million, or $0.05 per diluted share, for the first quarter of 2024. ● net income for the period was $4.1 million , or $0.25 per diluted share , compared to net income of $2.8 million, or $0.18 per diluted share, for the first quarter of 2024. ● $4.4 million of positive cash flow. As of March 31, 2025, the company had cash and cash equivalents, bank deposits of $99.1 million , and no debt, ending the first quarter with its highest ever cash levels. ● Raising 2025 revenue growth outlook from 12%-15% to 15%-18% , with a midpoint of $71.1 million – representing a 16.5% increase compared to 2024. Management Comments: “The exceptional financial performance in the first quarter of 2025 builds on our results from 2024. We are on track to deliver a sixth consecutive year of growth, driven by our outstanding team. This quarter we achieved record company revenue once again, and thanks to our operational excellence and disciplined expense management, we increased operating margins by more than 6.5% ,” said Benny Eppstein, Chief Executive Officer of RADCOM. “Our record results and continued momentum underscore the strength of our technology and position us well to navigate the market ahead. We are also expanding strategic partnerships that open new sales opportunities. Given the successful quarter, we are confident in raising our 2025 revenue growth outlook from 12%-15% to 15%-18% , with a midpoint of $71.1 million - representing a 16.5% increase compared to 2024.” 1 Recent Developments ● RADCOM announced recently the renewal and expansion of a , eight figures contract with a customer in North America underscoring the continued confidence in its solution. ● The company also announced partnerships in the first quarter with Nvidia and ServiceNow to further advance insights and enhance automated business processes. ● Last week ServiceNow unveiled the inclusion of RADCOM among the first vendors, and the first assurance vendor, to offer its AI Agent Fabric integration for seamless workflows. The solution delivers new levels of and communication and collaboration. ● The emergence of artificial intelligent agents (agentic AI) is driving the of intelligent automation, enabling the orchestration of multiple AI agents to autonomously manage complex, workflows across customer care, service management and service assurance.
