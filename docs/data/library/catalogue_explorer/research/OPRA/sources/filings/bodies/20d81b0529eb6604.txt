.1 2 .htm .1 .1 Exhibit 99.1 Opera Declares Upcoming Cash Dividend of $0.40 per Share Under Its Recurring Dividend Program OSLO, Norway, December 12, 2024 – Opera Limited (NASDAQ: OPRA) (“ Opera ”), one of the world’s major browser developers and a leading internet consumer brand, today announced that its Board of Directors has declared its next cash dividend. Opera’s upcoming dividend under its recurring dividend program will be $0.40 per share or American Depositary Share (“ ADS ”), each ADS representing one ordinary share. Payment will be made to shareholders of record as of the close of business on January 6, 2025 (the “ Record Date ”). Dividends to be paid to the holders of ADSs through the depositary bank, The Bank of New York Mellon, will be subject to the terms of the deposit agreement. Opera expects the depositary bank will distribute dividends on or about January 13, 2025. The aggregate amount of the dividend is approximately $35.4 million, based on a total of 88.5 million shares outstanding as of today.
