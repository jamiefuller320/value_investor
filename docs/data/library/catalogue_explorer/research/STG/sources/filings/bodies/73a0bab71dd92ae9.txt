Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operational Snapshots · Net revenues were RMB331.6 million (US$49.5 million), compared to RMB523.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. · Gross billings ( ) were RMB239.0 million (US$35.6 million), compared to RMB349.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. · Gross profit was RMB273.4 million (US$40.8 million), compared to RMB462.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. · Net income was RMB35.2 million (US$5.2 million), compared to RMB125.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. · Net income margin 1 was 10.6%, compared to 24.0% in the third quarter of 2025. · New student enrollments 2 were 84,879, compared to 137,493 in the third quarter of 2025. · As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s deferred revenue balance was RMB382.5 million (US$57.1 million), compared to RMB585.3 million as of December 31, 2025. 1 Net income margin is defined as net income as a percentage of net revenues. 2 New student enrollments for a given period refer to the total number of orders placed by students that newly enroll in at least one course during that period, including those students that enroll and then terminate their enrollment with us, excluding orders of our courses, such as “mini courses” and “RMB1 courses”, which we offer in the form of recorded videos or short live streaming, to strengthen our competitiveness and improve customer experience. 1 Mr. Tongbo Liu, Chief Executive Officer of Sunlands, commented, “During the third quarter of 2026, we remained focused on our core business, refining our course offerings and teaching services to better address evolving learner needs. As demand for lifelong learning among and senior learners continues to diversify, we are placing greater emphasis on course quality, personalized learning experiences, and learner engagement. Looking ahead, we will continue to build on our experience in adult education, further integrate technology into teaching and services, and strengthen our core business to support sustainable development.” Mr. Hangyu Li, Finance Director of Sunlands, added, “In the third quarter of 2026, net revenues were RMB331.6 million and net income was RMB35.2 million, representing net income margin of 10.6%. Against a lower revenue base, we reduced operating expenses by 25.3% and remained profitable for the 22nd consecutive quarter. We remain focused on aligning costs and resource allocation with our operating needs, preserving liquidity, and allocating capital prudently to support our core business and development.” 2 Financial Results for the Third Quarter of 2026 Net Revenues In the third quarter of 2026, net revenues decreased by 36.6% to RMB331.6 million (US$49.5 million) from RMB523.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to the decline in gross billings. Cost of Revenues Cost of revenues decreased by 3.6% to RMB58.1 million (US$8.7 million) in the third quarter of 2026 from RMB60.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was mainly due to declined service fees paid to educational institutions. Gross Profit Gross profit decreased by 40.9% to RMB273.4 million (US$40.8 million) in the third quarter of 2026 from RMB462.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Operating Expenses In the third quarter of 2026, operating expenses were RMB242.2 million (US$36.1 million), representing a 25.3% decrease from RMB324.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Sales and marketing expenses decreased by 26.4% to RMB206.0 million (US$ 30.7 million) in the third quarter of 2026 from RMB279.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was mainly due to the decreases of compensation for sales personnel and the spending on branding and marketing activities focused on interest courses offerings. General and administrative expenses decreased by 18.7% to RMB29.2 million (US$4.4 million) in the third quarter of 2026 from RMB36.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Product development expenses decreased by 18.9% to RMB7.0 million (US$1.0 million) in the third quarter of 2026 from RMB8.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was mainly due to declined compensation expenses related to the Company’s product development personnel. 3 Net Income Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB35.2 million (US$5.2 million), as compared to RMB125.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Basic and Diluted Net Income Per Share Basic and diluted net income per share was RMB5.53 (US$0.83) in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to RMB18.64 in the third quarter of 2025. Cash, Cash Equivalents, Restricted Cash and Investments As of September 30, 2026, the Company had RMB502.0 million (US$74.9 million) of cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash and RMB281.4 million (US$42.0 million) of investments, as compared to RMB576.8 million of cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash and RMB235.9 million of investments as of December 31, 2025. Deferred Revenue As of September 30, 2026, the Company had a deferred revenue balance of RMB382.5 million (US$57.1 million), as compared to RMB585.3 million as of December 31, 2025. Share Repurchase On May 29, 2026, the Company’s board of directors authorized a share repurchase program, under which the Company may repurchase up to US$50.0 million of Class A ordinary shares in the form of ADSs over the next 36 months. As of November 17, 2026, the Company had repurchased an aggregate of 716,479 ADSs for approximately US$2.4 million under the share repurchase program. 4 Financial Results for the First Nine Months of 2026 Net Revenues In the first nine months of 2026, net revenues decreased by 23.9% to RMB1,178.6 million (US$175.8 million) from RMB1,549.7 million in the first nine months of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to the decline in gross billings. Cost of Revenues Cost of revenues decreased by 13.6% to RMB174.9 million (US$26.1 million) in the first nine months of 2026 from RMB202.3 million in the first nine months of 2025. The decrease was mainly due to declined cost of revenues from sales of goods such as learning materials and books and decreased service fees paid to educational institutions. Gross Profit Gross profit decreased by 25.5% to RMB1,003.7 million (US$149.7 million) in the first nine months of 2026 from RMB1,347.4 million in the first nine months of 2025. Operating Expenses In the first nine months of 2026, operating expenses were RMB792.2 million (US$118.2 million), representing a 21.4% decrease from RMB1,008.1 million in the first nine months of 2025. Sales and marketing expenses decreased by 23.4% to RMB676.2 million (US$100.9 million) in the first nine months of 2026 from RMB882.7 million in the first nine months of 2025. The decrease was mainly due to the decreases of compensation for sales personnel and the spending on branding and marketing activities focused on interest courses offerings. General and administrative expenses decreased by 6.4% to RMB96.9 million (US$14.5 million) in the first nine months of 2026 from RMB103.6 million in the first nine months of 2025. Product development expenses decreased by 12.5% to RMB19.1 million (US$2.9 million) in the first nine months of 2026 from RMB21.9 million in the first nine months of 2025. 5 Net Income Net income for the first nine months of 2026 was RMB195.5 million (US$29.2 million), compared with RMB327.3 million in the first nine months of 2025. Basic and Diluted Net Income Per Share Basic and diluted net income per share was RMB29.79 (US$4.44) in the first nine months of 2026, compared with RMB48.50 in the first nine months of 2025. We define gross billings for a specific period as the total amount of cash received for the sale of course packages, net of the total amount of refunds paid in such period. Our management uses gross billings as a performance measurement because we generally bill our students for the entire course tuition at the time of sale of our course packages and recognize revenue proportionally over a period. EBITDA is defined as net income excluding depreciation and amortization, interest expense, interest income, and income tax expenses. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income excluding depreciation and amortization, interest expense, interest income, income tax expenses and impairment loss on assets. We believe that gross billings, EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA provide valuable insight into the sales of our course packages and the performance of our business. These financial measures should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, their most directly comparable financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP.
