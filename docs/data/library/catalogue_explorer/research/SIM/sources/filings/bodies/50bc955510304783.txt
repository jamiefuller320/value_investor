About Grupo Simec Grupo Simec is a diversified manufacturer, processor and distributor of SBQ steel and structural steel products with production and commercial operations in Mexico and Brazil, and limited commercial operations in the United States. Grupo Simec is also an important producer of structural and light structural steel products in Mexico in terms of sales volume. Grupo Simec’s SBQ products are used across a broad range of highly engineered applications, including axles, hubs and crankshafts for automobiles and light trucks, machine tools and equipment.
