Consideration and approval of the Financial Statements of the Company corresponding to the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Independent Report of the External Auditors E&Y (Pistrelli, Henry Martin y Asociados S.A., member firm of Ernst & Young Global) , and the Notes corresponding to the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. 2.
