By: /s/ Juan David Bastidas Name: Juan David Bastidas Title: Chief Legal Counsel Date: March 7, 2025 Item 1 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE ARCOS DORADOS TO HOST ITS 2025 ANNUAL GENERAL SHAREHOLDERS’ MEETING Montevideo, Uruguay, March 7, 2025 – Arcos Dorados Holdings Inc. (NYSE: ARCO) (“Arcos Dorados” or the “Company”), Latin America’s largest restaurant chain and the world’s largest independent McDonald’s franchisee, today announced that on March 7, 2025, its Board of Directors set the date for the Company's Annual Shareholders’ Meeting (“AGM”). The AGM will be held on April 25, 2025, in Willemstad, Curaçao, at 4:00 p.m. (local time), for all shareholders of record as of March 31, 2025.
