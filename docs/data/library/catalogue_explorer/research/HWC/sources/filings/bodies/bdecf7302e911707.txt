In conjunction with the adoption of ASU 2025-08, “Financial Instruments – Credit Losses (Topic 326): Purchased Loans,” (“ASU 2025-08”) as of January 1, 2026, the Company updated its accounting policy for acquired loans as described below. Acquired Loans Acquired loans are initially evaluated to determine if they qualify as purchased credit deteriorated (PCD) loans. Loans classified as PCD include those individual loans or groups of loans with similar risk characteristics that, as of the date of acquisition, are assessed as having experienced a deterioration in credit quality since origination. The assessment of what is credit deterioration since origination considers information including, but not limited to, financial assets that are delinquent, are on nonaccrual and/or are otherwise adversely risk rated as of the acquisition date. Loans that do not meet the criteria to be classified as PCD are then evaluated to determine whether they qualify as purchased seasoned loans (PSL). Loans are PSL if they are acquired at least 90 days after origination and the Company was not involved in the origination of the loan, or if they were acquired in a business combination. Excluded from the scope of PSL are credit card loans, debt securities, contract assets, trade receivables and leases. For loans classified as PCD and PSL, the Company bifurcates the fair value discount or premium between credit and noncredit components. The credit portion of the fair value discount or premium is added to the initial amortized cost basis of the loans with a corresponding increase to the allowance for credit losses at the date of acquisition. Any noncredit discount or premium resulting from acquired loans classified as PCD and PSL is allocated to each individual loan. All other acquired loans not classified as PCD or PSL and leases are recorded at the estimated fair value at acquisition, with an estimated allowance for credit loss recorded as a provision for credit losses through earnings in the period in which the acquisition has occurred. The noncredit discount or premium for loans classified as PCD and PSL and full discount for all other loans and leases are accreted to interest income using the interest method based on the effective interest rate at the acquisition date. Allowance for Credit Losses The calculation of the allowance for credit losses is performed using two primary approaches: a collective approach using a loss rate analysis for pools of loans that have similar risk characteristics, and a specific reserve analysis for credits individually evaluated. The allowance for credit losses for collectively evaluated portfolios is developed using multiple Moody’s macroeconomic forecasts applied in internally developed credit models for a reasonable and supportable period. Investments in Low Income Housing Tax Credit Entities The Company invests in certain affordable housing project limited partnerships that are qualified housing tax credit developments. These investments are considered variable interest entities for which the Company is not the primary beneficiary and, therefore, are not consolidated. These partnerships generate tax credits that are earned over a 10-year period, beginning with the year the rental activity begins. The Company has elected to use the practical expedient method of amortization, which approximates the proportional amortization method, whereby the investment cost is amortized in proportion to the allocated tax credits over the 10 year tax credit period. Additionally, the Company recognizes deferred taxes on the basis difference of the tax equity investment to reflect the financial impact of other tax benefits (e.g., tax operating losses) not included in the practical expedient amortization. The tax credits, when realized, are reflected in the consolidated statements of income as a reduction of income tax expense. The Company’s investments in affordable housing limited partnerships totaled $ 37.5 million at both September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025, with a balance net of accumulated amortization included in the other assets line item on our Consolidated Balance Sheets totaling $ 19.0 million and $ 21.8 mi llion, respectively, for those same periods. The net impact of the housing tax credit program was not material to our Consolidated Statements of Income or Cash Flows for the three or nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025. 6.
