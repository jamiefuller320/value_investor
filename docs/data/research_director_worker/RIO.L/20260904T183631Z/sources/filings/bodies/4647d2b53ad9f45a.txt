BETA Close X Rio Tinto has announced the currency exchange rates for its 2025 final dividend of 254.00 US cents per share, payable on April 16, 2026. Shareholders electing to receive their dividend in British pence will receive 191.770479 pence, while those opting for Australian dollars will receive 367.078546 Australian cents, and New Zealand dollar recipients will get 445.184471 New Zealand cents. These rates were determined based on the exchange rates applicable on April 7, 2026.
