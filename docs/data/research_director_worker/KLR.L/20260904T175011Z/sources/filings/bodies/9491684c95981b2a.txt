BETA Close X Keller Group plc announced on March 19, 2026, that its Executive Directors and Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) received Long Term Incentive Plan (LTIP) awards on March 18, 2026, including 10,386 Deferred Bonus Awards and 126,047 Performance Share Awards. The Deferred Bonus Awards are subject to a two-year deferral period, while the Performance Share Awards, valued based on an average closing price of 2,103 pence, have performance conditions tied to cumulative EPS, relative TSR versus the FTSE 250, and operating profit margin over a three-year period ending December 31, 2028, with vesting expected in March 2029. Disclaimer* 19 March 2026 Keller Group plc Director/PDMR Shareholding Keller Group plc (the "Company") announces that on 18 March 2026 it was notified of the following transactions in the Company's Ordinary shares of 10 pence each (“Shares”) undertaken by Executive Directors and other Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities ("PDMRs"): Long Term Incentive Plan (“LTIP”) grant On 18 March 2026 the Company granted the following awards under the LTIP: Deferred Bonus Awards totalling 10,386 Shares; and Performance Share Awards totalling 126,047 Shares. The grant to the Executive Directors and PDMRs listed in the tables below was approved by the Remuneration Committee under the terms of the LTIP. LTIP Deferred Bonus Awards – in respect of the year ended 31 December 2025 Executive Directors are subject to a deferral of 25% of any annual bonus payment into Shares for a period of two years. Other PDMRs are subject to a deferral into Shares of any annual bonus payment in excess of 100% of salary, also for a period of two years. The Deferred Bonus Awards retain eligibility for dividend equivalents from the date of grant until the vesting date. Malus and clawback apply.
