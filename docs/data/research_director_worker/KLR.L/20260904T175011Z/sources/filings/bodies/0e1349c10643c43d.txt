BETA Close X Keller Group plc announced that all resolutions were passed at its Annual General Meeting on May 20, 2026, with strong support for most proposals. Notably, the Annual Report and Accounts, the final dividend of 52.1p per Ordinary Share, and the re-appointment of Ernst & Young LLP as auditors all received 100% of votes cast in favour. The Directors' remuneration report was approved with 96.56% in favour, and the election of James Wroath as a Director passed with 99.94% of votes.
