For a value investor the hook is still cash and a conservative covenant balance sheet — if, and only if, company-definition sterling FCF covers the rebased dividend plus the live £100 million buyback after plant capex, leases and H1 working-capital absorption, and if North American infrastructure and data-centre work is not a peak-cycle print. H1 filings show that operating engine: North America was £984.4 million of revenue and £93.8 million of underlying operating profit (9.5% margin; about 61% of group revenue and about 80% of group underlying operating profit), with more data-centre projects in the half than in full FY2025. EME revenue fell 5.2% at constant currency; APAC margins compressed. That is a concentrated upcycle, not a diversified compounder, and cyclical_exposure_detected is false.

It is not a strong buy on this pack. Statutory profit after tax was almost flat in FY2025. Diluted underlying EPS rose 6% in the year and 22.4% in H1, with buybacks cited as an EPS tailwind. Yahoo ordinary shares fell from 71.4 million to 69.2 million and treasury shares rose from 1.69 million to 3.85 million. Neutral is the right stance: the H1 operating print is real; the cheapness and quality-compounder framing is not.
