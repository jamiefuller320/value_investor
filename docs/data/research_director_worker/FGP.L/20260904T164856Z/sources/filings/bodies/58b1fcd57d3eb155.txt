BETA Close X FirstGroup plc has announced the grant of conditional share awards to its Chief Executive Officer, Graham Sutherland, and Chief Financial Officer, Ryan Mangold, under its Executive Annual Bonus Plan and Long-Term Incentive Plan on 19 June 2026. Graham Sutherland received a total of 837,672 ordinary shares, comprising 175,710 under the EABP and 661,962 under the LTIP. Ryan Mangold was awarded a total of 631,411 ordinary shares, with 145,404 under the EABP and 486,007 under the LTIP. These awards were granted at nil cost and are subject to vesting conditions and, for the LTIP, an additional holding period.
