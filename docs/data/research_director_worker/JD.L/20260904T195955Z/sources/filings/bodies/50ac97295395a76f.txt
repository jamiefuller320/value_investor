Across The Markets: BHP, JD Sports Fashion, Oxford Nanopore 2026-08-17 08:06:34 (2 days ago) Related Companies: JD Sports Fashion (JD.) , BHP Group Limited NPV (DI) (BHP) , Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT) London equities made a cautious start to the week as investors balanced a relatively firm FTSE backdrop against a busy earnings calendar and ongoing questions over the global growth outlook. Attention is increasingly turning towards company-specific catalysts, with traders looking ahead to a number of significant updates due over the coming days. BHP was among the stocks drawing interest after investors positioned themselves ahead of the mining giant's full-year results. Recent operational updates have pointed to strong iron ore production and resilient performance across key assets, although softer copper market expectations could temper enthusiasm. Traders will be watching closely for guidance on costs, demand trends and capital allocation. JD Sports Fashion also remained firmly on investors' radars ahead of this week's scheduled trading statement. The retailer has faced a challenging consumer environment in several markets, but market participants are looking for signs that trading conditions improved through the summer and that management can offer a more constructive view of second-half performance. Meanwhile, Oxford Nanopore Technologies attracted attention ahead of its interim results. The life sciences specialist continues to be seen as a key growth story within the UK technology sector, and investors will be keen to assess progress in commercial adoption, revenue growth and the outlook for profitability. Across the wider market, sentiment remained broadly constructive after the FTSE 100 traded near record levels in recent sessions, although stock selection continued to dominate trading activity. Recent leadership from defensive names such as Diageo and Admiral, alongside strength in Vodafone, suggests investors remain selective rather than aggressively risk-on. With a relatively light macroeconomic calendar and no major central bank events immediately ahead, company news is likely to remain the principal driver of share price performance this week. Earnings updates from BHP, JD Sports and Oxford Nanopore could therefore provide some of the market's most notable moves in the sessions ahead.
