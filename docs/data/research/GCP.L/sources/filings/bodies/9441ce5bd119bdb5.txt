At 30 September 2026: The net asset value was, as previously announced, 99.39 pence per ordinary share; The Company was exposed to a diversified and partially inflation protected portfolio of 45 investments with an unaudited valuation of £739.6 million; and The portfolio had a weight-adjusted average annualised yield of 8.1%, principal outstanding of £802.4 million and an average life of 10 years. Capital allocation At 30 September 2026, following repayment in the quarter, the Company’s revolving credit facility was fully undrawn (30 June 2026: £24.0 million). The Company bought back 57,280,346 ordinary shares in the quarter. On 17 August 2026, the Company announced that a borrower to which the Company had extended a loan secured against a portfolio of operational renewable energy projects in the UK (the "Loan") repaid in full the c. £31.5 million outstanding under the Loan. This was materially in line with the valuation, and was one of the Company's lower returning investments, relative to the Company's average annualised portfolio interest rate. The Company continues to progress the pipeline of disposals including the sale of supported social housing assets, solar and onshore wind assets.
