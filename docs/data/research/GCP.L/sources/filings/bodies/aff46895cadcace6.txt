Net asset value and dividend declaration GCP Infrastructure Investments Ltd 26 October 2026 GCP Infrastructure Investments Limited ("GCP Infra" or the "Company") LEI: 213800W64MNATSIV5Z47 Net asset value and dividend declaration 26 October 2026 Net Asset Value GCP Infra announces that at close of business on 30 September 2026, the unaudited net asset value ("NAV") per ordinary share of the Company was 99.39 pence (30 June 2026: 98.60 pence), an increase of 0.79 pence per ordinary share. The NAV takes into account cash, other assets, accrued liabilities and expenses and leverage of the Company attributable to the ordinary share class. Updates to forecast electricity prices, driven by higher futures prices in the short-term and medium-term forecasts from the Company's third-party power price consultant, resulted in a net increase of 0.97 pence per ordinary share, including the impact of hedging arrangements. Actual generation across the renewable energy portfolio increased NAV per ordinary share by 0.12 pence, and the valuation effect of unwinding discount rates, project specific updates across the whole portfolio, and other movements led to a net decrease of 1.49 pence per ordinary share. Project-specific updates included a negative valuation adjustment to the gas peaking portfolio, reflecting feedback from a recent market sounding exercise, and an update to the long-term forecast for the 500kW anaerobic digestion plant. At the prevailing discount at which the Company's ordinary shares trade to the Company's net asset value per ordinary share, such excess cash balance will be used to continue the Company's share buyback programme. At 30 September 2026, the Company's revolving credit facility ("RCF") remains fully undrawn. Further supporting the capital allocation policy, the Company bought back 57,280,346 ordinary shares in the quarter, contributing a 1.19 pence per ordinary share increase to NAV at 30 September 2026. The Company continues to progress the pipeline of disposals including the sale of supported social housing assets, solar and onshore wind assets. Dividend GCP Infra is pleased to announce a dividend of 1.75 pence per ordinary share for the period from 1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026. This is in line with the Company's annual dividend target of 7.00 pence per ordinary share. The dividend will be paid on 4 December 2026 to holders of ordinary shares recorded on the register as at the close of business on 6 November 2026.
