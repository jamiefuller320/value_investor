BETA Close X Travis Perkins PLC announced that all resolutions proposed at its Annual General Meeting on May 21, 2026, were passed by shareholders, with resolutions 1 to 14 passing as ordinary and 15 to 17 as special resolutions. The company noted significant votes against resolutions 14 and 15, concerning the allotment of shares and securities free from pre-emption rights, and will engage with shareholders to understand the reasons for this opposition. The final dividend of 7.5 pence per ordinary share was approved, and Deloitte LLP was reappointed as auditor. The total number of ordinary shares eligible for voting was 212,509,334.
