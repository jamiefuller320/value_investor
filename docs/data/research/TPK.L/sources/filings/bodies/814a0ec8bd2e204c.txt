UK construction output and volumes remain subdued, most notably in new build residential, as macroeconomic uncertainty persists. The Merchanting businesses continue to actively manage their transaction mix, prioritising higher margin trade and protecting the Group against credit risk in the current environment. This approach is helping to mitigate the impact to profitability, despite the volume headwinds. In addition, the Group is annualising the deeper promotional activity undertaken in Q3 last year. Against the inflationary backdrop of this year, the Merchanting businesses are also focused on achieving an effective pass through of input inflation. Toolstation UK delivered solid revenue growth in the quarter as it continues to expand in line with expectations and looks ahead to further store openings in 2027. Toolstation Benelux performance remains challenging and the Group continues to explore opportunities arising from its current strategic review to address this. The Group continues to implement measures that are enhancing its operational performance, free cash flow generation and robust financial position. Gavin Slark, Chief Executive Officer, commented: “In challenging market conditions we remain focused on what we can control. Our ongoing cost discipline and trading approach are protecting profitability whilst we continue to identify and implement further operational efficiencies.
