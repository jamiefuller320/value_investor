1. Basis of preparation

Computacenter plc (the “Company”) is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in
England and Wales. The Company's ordinary shares are traded on the London Stock Exchange.

The Company Interim Accounts (the “Accounts”) for the period ended 28 February 2026 have
been properly prepared to satisfy the requirements of sections 836 to 838 of the Companies Act
2006 (the “Act”) and provide a true and fair view of the Company's assets, liabilities, financial
position, and profit for the period ended 28 February 2026.

These Accounts contain information about Computacenter plc solely as an individual company.
In preparing the Accounts, the accounting policies adopted are consistent with those set out in
pages 220 to 221 of the Computacenter pic Annual Report and Accounts 2024 (”2024 Annual
Report”).

The financial information presented does not constitute statutory accounts within the meaning of
- section 434(3) of the Companies Act 2006 (the “Act”) and does not contain all of the information
required for full annual financial statements. Statutory accounts for the year ended 31 December
2024 were published within the 2024 Annual Report, a copy of which was delivered to the
Registrar of Companies for England and Wales, and should be read in conjunction with these
Accounts.

The Accounts are prepared in pound sterling (£) and all values are rounded to the nearest hundred
thousand, except when otherwise indicated. The Directors have elected not to present
comparative figures for the period, nor the note disclosures that would normally form part of a
complete set of annual financial statements.

2. Impairment loss on investments

The carrying values of investments are reviewed annually or when events or changes in
circumstances indicate that the carrying value may not be recoverable. The Company assesses if
such indicators exist at the end of each reporting period by considering external and internal
factors, including whether the carrying amount of an investment exceeds the investee’s net assets
or if a dividend exceeds the total comprehensive income of the investee. The Company also
evaluates its investments annually for any indicators of impairment reversal.

During the period, trading performance of CComputacenter France’ SAS (CC France), a wholly
owned subsidiary, was weaker than previously expected and future forecasts have been revised
downwards, including less favourable assumptions in respect of profitability and working capital.
As a consequence, an impairment loss of £121.lm has therefore been recognised during the period
and the Company's investment in CC France is now fully impaired. The impairment loss is
included within the Company's profit for the period of £142.2m.

3. Dividends received

Dividends received are recognised as income in the Profit and Loss Account when the right to
receive payment is established.

5. Other reserves

Share premium
The share premium account is used to record the aggregate amount or value of premiums paid
when the Company's shares are issued/redeemed at a premium.

Capital redemption reserve
The capital redemption reserve is used to maintain the Company's capital following the purchase
and cancellation of its own shares.

Own shares held

Shares in the Company held by the Company are classified within shareholders’ equity as own
shares held and are recognised at cost. Consideration received for the sale of such shares is also
recognised in equity, with any difference between the proceeds from sale and the original cost
being taken to reserves. No gain or loss is recognised in the Profit and Loss Account on the
purchase, sale, issue or cancellation of equity shares.

6. Retained Earnings

The Company's profits reported within these Accounts include cash-settled dividends from
subsidiaries of £260.8m, which were received subsequent to the conclusion of the Company‘s
financial year ended 31 December 2025.

As at 28 February 2026, the Company had retained earnings of £633.1m, which included a
cumulative credit to equity of £100.8m relating to share-based payments. Following an
assessment of the profits available for distribution that occurred during the period, and as a
matter of prudence, the Company has excluded this balance from its distributable reserves.

After further deducting the £258.3m reserve for own shares held, the Company's distributable
reserves are approximately £274.0m.

7. Dividend distribution

Equity dividend distributions to the Company's shareholders are recognised in the Company's
financial statements in the period in which the dividends are approved by the Company’s
shareholders.
