Directorate Change Computacenter PLC 09 January 2025 Computacenter plc Incorporated in England Registration number: 03110569 LEI: 549300XSXUZ1I19DB105 ISIN: GB00BV9FP302 Computacenter plc Directorate Change Computacenter plc ("Computacenter" or the "Company"), a leading independent technology and services provider, is pleased to announce the appointment of Simon McNamara as an independent Non-Executive Director of its Board of Directors (the "Board"), with immediate effect. Simon most recently served for ten years as Chief Administration Officer at Natwest Group, where he led the transformation of its technology and services proposition, and oversaw more than 30,000 employees across the UK, EMEA and India. Prior to joining Natwest Group, Simon was Chief Information Officer of Standard Chartered Bank Consumer Bank, based in Singapore, where he was responsible for developing and implementing the Group Technology and Operations strategy. Simon has also held a number of other senior Information Technology and Operations positions in the global financial services industry at Westpac Banking Corporation, Deutsche Bank, BNP Paribas, and Midland Bank. He was also a founding partner in a successful software start-up company, CATS INC, in Silicon Valley. Pauline Campbell, Chair of the Board, commented: "We are very pleased to welcome Simon to Computacenter. He brings over 35 years of experience as a technology and operations executive, with a significant amount of that time spent holding senior leadership roles. His deep understanding of technology and of digital transformation, and his view on how these can enable and support the strategy of large corporate organisations, will be of great value to the Board".
