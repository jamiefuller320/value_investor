The event will set out how Computacenter’s customer focus, scale and capabilities support its growth and long-term value creation. The event will be led by Mike Norris, Chief Executive Officer, and Keith Mortimer, Chief Financial Officer, with presentations from senior leaders covering: Customer growth: How Computacenter wins, retains and grows relationships with large corporate and public sector customers by helping them maximise the value of their technology investments. Scale and capabilities: How Computacenter combines its people, operating expertise and infrastructure across Technology Sourcing and Services to meet complex customer requirements at scale. North America growth: How Computacenter can further leverage its proven operating model and investments to deliver against the significant growth opportunities across hyperscale, enterprise and government customers. UK & European Data Center growth: How Computacenter designs, sources, integrates and deploys complex data center infrastructure, including AI infrastructure, and how these capabilities position the Group to capture growth opportunities across the UK and Europe. Strength of the financial model: How customer growth, scale benefits and operating efficiency translate into profit and cash generation, supported by disciplined working capital management and capital allocation. The event will also cover the drivers supporting the Group’s existing ambitions to grow ahead of the market, improve operating efficiency and generate further strong cash flows. Attendees will also tour the Hatfield Integration Center and Rack Integration Lab, including its liquid-cooling capabilities. The Group last updated the market on current trading on 26 October 2026. No further update will be provided at the event.
