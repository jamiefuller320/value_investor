Our affordable, inclusive proposition has continued to ensure we are well-positioned in an environment where consumers are closely watching what they spend. "Our Canadian business has made good progress. A strong H2 performance reflects the investments we've made in existing and new centres in the territory, and we have made a material step forward in our pipeline for new centres. "As we move into FY27, we have a solid platform to build on. Our cash-generative model enables us to continue to invest in new centre openings and customer-facing upgrades.
