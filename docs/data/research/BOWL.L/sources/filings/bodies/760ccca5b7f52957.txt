BETA Close X Hollywood Bowl Group plc announced that on February 5, 2026, several Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibility (PDMRs) exercised nil-cost options granted under the Long-Term Incentive Plan and subsequently sold shares to cover tax liabilities and dealing costs. Specifically, Stephen Burns exercised 300,353 options and sold 141,871 shares, Melanie Dickinson exercised 77,694 options and sold 36,700 shares, Laurence Keen exercised 196,841 options and sold 92,981 shares, Mat Hart exercised 97,118 options and sold 45,875 shares, and Darryl Lewis exercised 64,905 options and sold 30,659 shares. These transactions occurred on the London Stock Exchange at a price of £2.6274 per share, with a total of 736,901 new ordinary shares issued and expected to be admitted to trading on February 11, 2026.
