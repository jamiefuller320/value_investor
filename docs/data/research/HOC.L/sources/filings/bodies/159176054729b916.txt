The transition to the new mining contractor was completed during the quarter, with the operation reaching planned mining capacity and material movement increasing to nameplate levels towards the end of the period. This progress was complemented by improved operating conditions, more productive pushback activities and better haulage roads and access routes. Ongoing pushback development is expected to provide continued access to higher-grade areas during the fourth quarter, building on the progressive improvement in grades achieved in September. With respect to the plant, the thickener has stabilised following commissioning and is performing above expectations, reducing filtration cycle times to planned levels and restoring continuity in tailings disposal. This has also resolved the water management constraints reported in H1. Plant reliability and throughput rates improved steadily during the quarter, with metallurgical recoveries returning to planned levels, supported by operational and maintenance initiatives outlined in the Q2 statement.
