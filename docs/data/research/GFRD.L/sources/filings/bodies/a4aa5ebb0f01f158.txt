Disclaimer* Galliford Try Holdings PLC 07 October 2026 Galliford Try appointed to £1.6 billion University of Oxford framework Galliford Try, a leading UK construction group, announces that it has been appointed to the Oxford Inspire framework for construction services on behalf of the University of Oxford. The business has been selected for Lot 2 of the framework, which is worth up to £1.6 billion over eight years, for projects in excess of £15 million in value, which will include large-scale, new build and refurbishment schemes. Bill Hocking, Chief Executive for Galliford Try, commented: "Our business has a strong track record in delivering high-quality facilities to all parts of the education sector. We are delighted to be appointed to the Oxford Inspire framework and we look forward to delivering for staff and students across the University's estate."
