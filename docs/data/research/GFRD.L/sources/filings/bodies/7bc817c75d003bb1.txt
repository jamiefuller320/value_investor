Disclaimer* Galliford Try Holdings PLC 22 April 2026 22 April 2026 GALLIFORD TRY HOLDINGS PLC Completion of Share Buyback Programme Galliford Try Holdings plc ("Galliford Try"), the UK construction group, announces that it has completed its third share buyback programme, announced on 17 September 2025. Galliford Try confirms that, under this share buyback programme, a total of 1,957,703 ordinary shares of 50 pence each ("Ordinary Shares") were repurchased, and subsequently cancelled, at an average price of approximately £5.11 per Ordinary Share, and a total cost of £10 million.
