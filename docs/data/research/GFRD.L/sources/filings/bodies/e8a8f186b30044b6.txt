Disclaimer* Galliford Try Holdings PLC 20 October 2026 Galliford Try appointed to £5.0 billion Pagabo Major Works framework Galliford Try, a leading UK construction group, announces that it has been appointed to the £5.0 billion Pagabo National Framework for Major Works. The business has been awarded places on Lot 4 (projects valued from £30 million to £60 million) across the South West, South East, and London regions and Lot 5 (in excess of £60 million) for Scotland. The framework will run for a four-year term and covers public sector buildings and facilities for local authorities and other public bodies across the UK. Bill Hocking, Chief Executive for Galliford Try, commented: "Having been recently appointed to Pagabo's Infrastructure framework, we are delighted to be working with them once again on this important public sector framework. We look forward to collaborating together to deliver high-quality facilities for communities across the country."
