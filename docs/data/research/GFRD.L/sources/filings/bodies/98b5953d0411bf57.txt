Disclaimer* Galliford Try Holdings PLC 05 October 2026 Galliford Try appointed to £3.0 billion Constructing West Midlands Framework Galliford Try, a leading UK construction group, announces that it has been appointed to the latest iteration of the £3.0 billion Constructing West Midlands Framework. The framework covers public sector buildings for local authorities across sectors including education, housing, health, and leisure. Galliford Try has been selected in the Major Works lot for projects worth in excess of £10 million. Bill Hocking, Chief Executive for Galliford Try, commented: "We are delighted to be working once again with our partners at Constructing West Midlands to deliver much-needed public infrastructure for communities across the region."
