Development During the period, the Group undertook new planting of over 430 hectares and replanted 1,785 hectares, in line with its five-year target to replant around 10,000 hectares to sustain long-term productivity and yield improvement. Construction of the Group's ninth mill at KAP Estate in Kalimantan is on track, with commissioning targeted for early January 2027. As at 30 September 2026, building and structural works were 98% complete, with all machinery fully fabricated and delivered to site by early October 2026. At JJU Estate, the rehabilitation of identified planted areas is underway. Development and new planting will proceed in phases, subject to the completion of the relevant approvals. Based on current progress, the development is expected to take approximately three to five years to complete. Weather Conditions and Fire Prevention The prevailing El Niño conditions and prolonged dry weather may affect FFB yields in 2027. To date, there have been no fires arising from the dry conditions that have materially affected the Group ' s operations. While haze conditions have raised health concerns, field operations continue with appropriate precautions in place, including the provision of protective masks and medical care to workers where required. The Group also continues to engage with local communities, providing training and promoting awareness of fire prevention and firefighting practices to help mitigate fire risks. Should FFB yields be impacted, CPO supply and demand dynamics would likely see some support for CPO pricing. Dividend The Board is pleased to declare an interim dividend of 3.73 cents per share in respect of the six months ended 30 June 2026 (2025 interim dividend: 3.73 cents per share, adjusted for the 10-for-1 share split ). The interim dividend will be paid on 11 December 2026 to those shareholders on the register on 6 November 2026. While the dividend is declared in US Dollars, shareholders can elect to receive their dividends in Pounds Sterling. In the absence of any specific instruction up to the date of closing of the register on 6 November 2026, shareholders with registered addresses in the UK will be deemed to have elected to receive their dividends in Pounds Sterling and those with registered addresses outside the UK will be deemed to have elected to receive their dividends in US Dollars. For shareholders opting to receive their dividends in Pounds Sterling, the final dividend of 3.73 cents per share will be converted at the exchange rate prevailing on 6 November 2026 (the record date). Outlook CPO prices are expected to remain supported through the final quarter of 2026, underpinned by food demand, firm energy markets and Indonesia's B50 biodiesel rollout, which is expected to strengthen domestic CPO consumption. Following strong production in 9m 2026, the Group expects continued production recovery and the contribution from Pinago to support full-year performance. The Group continues to pursue selective brownfield opportunities that meet its disciplined investment and sustainability criteria. Supported by favourable long-term CPO fundamentals, the Board remains confident in delivering results in line with market expectations for the full year, 2026.
