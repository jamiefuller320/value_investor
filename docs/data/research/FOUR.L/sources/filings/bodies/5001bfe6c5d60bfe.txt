BETA Close X TotalEnergies has secured four Production Sharing Contracts (PSC) for exploration blocks LB-6, LB-11, LB-17, and LB-29 offshore Liberia, following the 2024 Direct Negotiation Licensing Round. These blocks, situated in the south of the Liberia Basin, cover a total area of approximately 12,700 square kilometers. As part of the work program, TotalEnergies is committed to acquiring one firm 3D seismic survey. The company aims to diversify its exploration portfolio and leverage its deepwater operations expertise in these areas, which hold potential for large-scale, cost-effective, and low-emission developments. Disclaimer* TotalEnergies (Paris:TTE) (LSE:TTE) (NYSE:TTE) has signed four Production Sharing Contracts (PSC) for the LB-6, LB-11, LB-17 and LB-29 Exploration blocks offshore Liberia, which were awarded following the 2024 Direct Negotiation Licensing Round organized by the Liberia Petroleum Regulatory Agency. The blocks LB-6, LB-11, LB-17 and LB-29, covering an area of approximately 12,700 square kilometers, are located in the south of the Liberia Basin. The work program includes acquiring one firm 3D seismic survey. “TotalEnergies is enthusiastic to be part of the resumption of exploration activities in offshore Liberia,” said Kevin McLachlan, Senior Vice-President Exploration at TotalEnergies . “Entering these blocks aligns with our strategy of diversifying our Exploration portfolio in high-potential new oil-prone basins. These areas hold significant potential for prospects that have the potential for large-scale discoveries that lead to cost-effective, low-emission developments, leveraging the Company’s proven expertise in deepwater operations.” About TotalEnergies TotalEnergies is a global integrated energy company that produces and markets energies: oil and biofuels, natural gas, biogas and low-carbon hydrogen, renewables and electricity. Our more than 100,000 employees are committed to providing as many people as possible with energy that is more reliable, more affordable and more sustainable.
