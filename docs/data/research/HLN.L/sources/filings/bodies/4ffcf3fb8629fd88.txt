Dividend Consistent with our policy to pay approximately one third of the prior year full year dividend as the interim dividend the Board has declared a H1 2026 interim dividend of 2.4 pence per ordinary share, up 9% on the interim dividend in 2025. This interim dividend is expected to be paid on 17 September 2026 to holders of ordinary shares and US American Depositary Shares (ADS) on the register as of 14 August 2026 (the record date). The date is expected to be 13 August 2026 for ordinary shareholders and 14 August 2026 for holders of ADS. For ordinary shareholders wishing to participate in the Dividend Reinvestment Programme (DRIP), the election deadline for the DRIP is 28 August 2026. The DRIP is provided by Equiniti Financial www.shareview.co.uk . Subject to market conditions and Board approval, Haleon expects to grow its ordinary dividend at least in line with adjusted earnings.
