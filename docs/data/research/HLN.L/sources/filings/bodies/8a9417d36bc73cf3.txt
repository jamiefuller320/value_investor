Distribution Restrictions - United Kingdom" in the full announcement linked below. 11 August 2026: Haleon plc (the "Company" or "Haleon") (LSE/NYSE: HLN) today announces that its wholly owned subsidiary, Haleon US Capital LLC (the " Offeror ") is offering to buy back any and all of its outstanding $1,999,350,000 3.375% Fixed Rate Senior Notes due March 2027. Bondholders will receive a price equal to the Total Consideration based on the Reference Yield and the Fixed Spread plus Accrued Interest (each as defined in the full announcement) (the " Tender Offer "). This Tender Offer is in line with Haleon's previously stated capital allocation priorities. Together, with the planned issue of new bonds, it is expected to help the Company to proactively manage and optimise its debt portfolio.
