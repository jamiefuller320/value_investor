Trading performance strengthened as the quarter progressed, with sales down c.3% year-on-year in the first six weeks during a period of exceptionally warm weather, before returning to growth of c.4% in the final seven weeks. Despite the challenging trading backdrop, we believe we continued to outperform an overall soft market. Most of our categories during the quarter saw a similar trading pattern; however Made to Measure delivered double digit growth, supported by excellent execution from our specialist store colleagues. Cook and Dine also performed well, particularly in the second half of the quarter, reflecting the success of recent range reviews and product refreshes. Gross margin increased by 10bps year-on-year in the first quarter, benefiting from an FX tailwind. Strategic and operational update We are making good progress with Winning Hearts & Homes, our three-year growth plan to build a bigger, better and bolder Dunelm. Our strategy is focused on three growth engines to: Become the homewares specialist Building on the encouraging results from work undertaken on specific product ranges, we now have further trials live in plain-dye bedding, focused on range rationalisation, and in Egyptian Cotton towels, focused on improving value perception. We have also launched our autumn brand marketing campaign, supporting our ambition to build deeper customer connection and strengthen Dunelm’s broad appeal. Deliver seamless omnichannel experiences Our new store expansion plans are progressing well, with two openings expected in the first half of our financial year. We have completed five store renewals, including one since the quarter end, with encouraging sales uplifts seen in early weeks of trading. We are also carrying out a larger refit of our Doncaster store. This will reflect the latest proposition and build in learnings and improvements from St. Albans, which is delivering strong sales growth following its refit earlier this year. Transform our capabilities As previously disclosed, we have begun implementing our organisational design and cost removal plans. These savings create capacity to invest in future growth opportunities, including establishing a dedicated change team and strengthening the use of data and analytics across the business. Operationally, we continue to make good progress in simplifying processes and improving productivity. Following successful early trials, RFID is now live in a small number of stores, with coverage extended across the majority of key categories in those locations, supporting improved product availability and store efficiency. In our contact centre, we have also deployed AI-enabled tools to efficiently enhance the customer experience. Summary and outlook Whilst the consumer backdrop remains uncertain, we are encouraged by the improvement in trading through the latter part of the quarter as we enter our peak trading period. We are also pleased with the progress being made against our strategic priorities, including further range trials, an improving and expanding store estate, and initiatives to transform the way we work. Our differentiated and trusted proposition, disciplined execution and the progress we are making through our Winning Hearts & Homes strategy reinforce our confidence in Dunelm’s future prospects, and in our ability to deliver sustainable long-term growth. At this early stage in the year, our expectations for FY27 adjusted PBT 5 remain unchanged and broadly in line with FY26. Serving millions of customers each year, Dunelm offers a specialist homewares proposition built on value, quality, choice and style. Its extensive range of predominantly own-brand products spans furniture, bedding, curtains, blinds, kitchenware, dining, lighting, outdoor living, decoration and DIY, alongside specialist services including Made-to-Measure window treatments. Founded in 1979 as a market stall in Leicester, Dunelm has grown into a FTSE-listed business with 204 stores across the UK and Ireland. Customers can shop seamlessly through its omnichannel offer, which includes stores, home delivery, Click & Collect and the Dunelm App. Today, Dunelm's ambition is to build a deeper connection with customers through its long-term growth strategy, Winning Hearts & Homes, building on its strengths as a homewares specialist while creating seamless customer experiences and investing in the capabilities needed to support sustainable long-term growth. The business is headquartered in Leicester and employs approximately 12,000 colleagues. It has been listed on the London Stock Exchange since 2006 (DNLM.L).
