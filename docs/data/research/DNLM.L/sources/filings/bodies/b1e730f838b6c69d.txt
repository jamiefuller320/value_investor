BETA Close X Dunelm Group plc announced that all resolutions presented at its Annual General Meeting on November 19, 2025, were duly passed by shareholders. The company's accounts, dividend, director re-elections, auditor appointments, and various share allotment and purchase authorities received strong support, with most resolutions passing with over 96% of votes in favour. Notably, Resolution 20, concerning a Rule 9 waiver, passed with 96.96% of votes for, representing 51.61% of the voting capital. The company has 201,544,221 total voting rights.
