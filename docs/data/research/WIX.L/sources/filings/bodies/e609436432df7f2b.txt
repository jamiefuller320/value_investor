Customer growth continues to drive strong overall volume increases, with mild price inflation starting to come through after a sustained period of deflation. Further market outperformance has led to continued market share growth, with notable strength in timber, flooring, roofing and garden. On a two-year basis, Retail LFL increased by 11.5%. Within Retail, TradePro continued to perform well, with sales up 8% year-on-year, as local trade professionals continue to choose Wickes to save them time and money. Active TradePro members 7 increased by 8% year-on-year to 684,000. DIY sales were also in mid-single-digit growth, supported by strong demand across core home improvement categories. In Design & Installation, delivered sales 8 were broadly flat. We continue to see healthy revenue and volume growth in Lifestyle Kitchens and Bespoke Bathrooms, however our Bespoke Kitchen ranges are seeing customers being more considered for larger purchases. Accordingly we have seen the overall number of D&I projects remain flat, but ordered sales 9 by value for the quarter were lower than last year. Our property strategy is progressing well, with three stores refitted or refreshed in the quarter and two new stores opened, in Saffron Walden and Colne. For the full year 2026 we will open 5 new stores and refit or refresh 12 stores. We have acquired the freehold of our Perry Barr store for £6.3m 10 ; the site was at risk of re-development and we have now secured the future of this profitable store. Our value-led and differentiated business model leaves us well-positioned to continue outperforming the market. With our strong sales performance and with a good productivity plan in place, we are on track to meet consensus expectations 11 which on average show a growth in adjusted PBT of c. 10% for 2026. We expect to report Q4 trading in late January 2027. David Wood, Chief Executive of Wickes, commented: “We’ve delivered a strong third quarter, with growth accelerating across Retail as more customers choose Wickes for their home improvement projects. Our continued market share gains demonstrate the strength of our value-led proposition and our ability to outperform in a competitive market. In response to gradual structural shifts in its markets over recent years, Wickes has a balanced business focusing on three key customer journeys - TradePro, DIY (together reported as Retail) and our project-based Design & Installation division. Wickes operates from its network of 231 stores, which support nationwide fulfilment from convenient locations throughout the United Kingdom, and through its digital channels including its website, TradePro mobile app for trade members, and Wickes DIY app. These digital channels allow customers to research and order an extended range of Wickes products and services, arrange virtual and in-person design consultations, and organise convenient Home Delivery or Click-and-Collect.
