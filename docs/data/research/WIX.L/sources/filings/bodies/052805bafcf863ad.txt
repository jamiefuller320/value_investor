BETA Close X Wickes Group plc reported a strong trading update for the six months ending 27 December 2025, with Group revenue increasing by 6.3% year-on-year, driven by volume-led growth across all business areas. Retail revenue grew by 6.2%, with TradePro sales up 8% and DIY sales in mid-single digits, while Design & Installation revenue increased by 6.9%.
