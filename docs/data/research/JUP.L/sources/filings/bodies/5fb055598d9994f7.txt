RNS Number : 8608O Fenikso Limited 31 July 2026 Fenikso Limited Receipt of funds for repayment of US$51.9m loan 31 st July 2026: Fenikso Limited (AQSE: FNK) (the " Company "), the Cayman Islands enterprise company, announces the receipt of funds under the Lekoil & Gas Investments ("LOGI") Loan entered in to on 31 December 2022 as part of the Fenikso restructuring. Fenikso Limited received US$486,612 as partial repayment of the loan of US$51,919,467 . The remaining amount under the loan is US$30,933,481. The amount received equates to 8.65% of the most recent payment from crude oil sales received by LOGI. The next payment is scheduled for September 2026. The Directors of Fenikso accept responsibility for this announcement.
