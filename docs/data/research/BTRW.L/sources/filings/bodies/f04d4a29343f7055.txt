Disclaimer* Barratt Redrow PLC 06 October 2026 BARRATT REDROW PLC Notification and public disclosure of a transaction by a person discharging managerial responsibilities This notification is made in accordance with the requirements of the UK Market Abuse Regulation. Following Dean Banks’ appointment as Chief Executive Officer of Barratt Redrow plc (the “Company”), the Company announces that, on 5 October 2026, it granted him a conditional award over 820,995 ordinary shares of 10 pence each in the Company (the “Recruitment Award”). The Recruitment Award replaces awards forfeited by Dean Banks on leaving his previous employer and has a value of £2.526m. The Recruitment Award has been granted under the Barratt Redrow plc Performance Share Plan 2023 (the “Plan”) in five tranches, with the vesting dates aligned with those of the forfeited awards. Details of the Recruitment Award are set out below: No. of shares subject to the Award Vesting Date Tranche 1 506,200 1 April 2027 Tranche 2 172,252 1 April 2028 Tranche 3 97,480 1 April 2029 Tranche 4 31,625 1 April 2030 Tranche 5 13,438 1 April 2031 The price used to calculate the number of shares over which the Recruitment Award was granted is 307.68p, being the average of the closing prices, as derived from the London Stock Exchange's Daily Official List, for each of the dealing days in the period of three months ending with the day before the award date (excluding any dealing days that fall within any period when dealing restrictions apply). The Recruitment Award is subject to continued employment. Dividend equivalents will accrue on the Recruitment Award from the date of grant until the relevant vesting date. The Recruitment Award is subject to the malus and clawback provisions contained in the Plan.
