Trading Update The Group’s Wash.ME operations continued to expand and grow throughout FY 2026. During the year the Group installed 1,224 net new laundry machines, a c.16% year-on-year increase in the number of machines in operation as of 31 October 2026. The Group also agreed a milestone strategic partnership with ASDA, with an overall ambition to target up to 700 Wash.ME laundry machines across ASDA sites in the UK. Wash.ME is expected to deliver a c.14% increase in revenue in FY 2026 compared with FY 2025 (up c.+12% at constant currency 1 ), achieved despite softer consumer demand for laundry drying services due to a sustained period of abnormally hot and dry summer weather across Europe. Trading across the majority of the Group’s photobooth operations remained robust against a backdrop of softer consumer demand as a result of travel uncertainty and the conflict in the Middle East. Demand levels for the Group’s photobooths in Japan and Germany remained challenging, primarily due to the impact of regulatory changes to official photo ID in these countries. As a result, Photo.ME revenue in FY 2026 is expected to be down approximately 4% compared with FY 2025 (down c.4% at constant currency 1 ). The Board expects total revenue for FY 2026 to be not less than approximately £309 million and adjusted profit before tax 2 to be not less than approximately £66 million.
