BETA Close X Pets at Home Group Plc announced that all resolutions presented at its Annual General Meeting on July 9, 2026, were passed by shareholders. Key resolutions included the approval of the directors' report and accounts for the year ended March 26, 2026, with 99.95% of votes in favour, and the directors' remuneration report, which received 85.06% approval. The company also secured strong support for its Directors' Remuneration Policy (96.39% for) and the Performance Share Plan (98.19% for).
