Disclaimer* Pets At Home Group Plc 23 January 2026 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE, 23 January 2026 PETS AT HOME GROUP PLC Confirmation of Board Changes Further to the announcement on 29 September 2025, Pets at Home Group Plc ("Company") is pleased to confirm that Sarah Pollard will join the Company on 23 March 2026 as Chief Financial Officer ("CFO") Designate.
