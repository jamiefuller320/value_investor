BETA Close X Tesco PLC has announced that several Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) have received ordinary shares upon release from the Long-Term Incentive Plan 2021, with the share price on release being 466.60 pence. Notably, Ken Murphy received 1,291,639 shares, Imran Nawaz 627,003 shares, and Natasha Adams 401,930 shares, with some of these net shares subject to a holding period until July 2028. Additionally, Stewart Gilliland purchased 1,120 shares at £4.7055 each, while several PDMRs, including Natasha Adams, Guus Dekkers, and Andrew Yaxley, sold a total of 1,024,726 shares at 467.06 pence each. Geoff Byrne also exercised options for 8,517 shares at £1.82 each.
