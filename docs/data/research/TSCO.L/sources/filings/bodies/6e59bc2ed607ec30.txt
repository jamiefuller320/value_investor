BETA Close X Tesco PLC reported a strong first quarter for 2026/27, with group sales reaching £16,826 million and like-for-like sales increasing by 1.0%, driven by a 1.8% rise in the UK and ROI combined. Customer satisfaction in the UK saw a significant improvement, with Net Promoter Score up six points year-on-year, supported by a 2.6% increase in food sales and a 9% growth in Finest products. While Booker experienced a 3.2% decline in like-for-like sales, partly due to a contract exit, Central Europe saw a 0.8% increase. The company reaffirmed its full-year adjusted operating profit guidance of £3.0 billion to £3.3 billion and expects to complete its £750 million share buyback program by April 2027, having already repurchased £341 million worth of shares.
