BETA Close X Tesco PLC has announced that on June 5, 2026, the Trustees of the Share Incentive Plan purchased ordinary shares on behalf of several Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs). Ken Murphy, Guus Dekkers, and Ashwin Prasad each acquired 31, 31, and 30 shares respectively, while Kay Majid purchased 24 shares, Andrew Yaxley bought 30 shares, and Jonny McQuarrie acquired 6 shares.
