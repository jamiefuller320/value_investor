BETA Close X Tesco PLC announced on June 23, 2026, that executive directors and members of the executive committee were granted awards under the Long-Term Incentive Plan 2021 on June 22, 2026. These awards, valued at 446.6 pence per share, are conditional shares that will vest on June 22, 2029, subject to performance targets. Ken Murphy received 947,377 shares, Imran Nawaz received 554,187 shares, and other executives received varying amounts, totaling over 3.5 million shares granted. Awards to Ken Murphy and Imran Nawaz will have an additional two-year post-vesting holding period.
