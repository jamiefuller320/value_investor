BETA Close X Tesco PLC announced that all resolutions proposed at its Annual General Meeting on June 18, 2026, were passed by shareholders. Notably, the report and accounts received 100% approval, the Directors' remuneration report passed with 95.18% in favour, and the final dividend declaration was approved by 99.96% of votes. Re-elections of directors generally saw strong support, with most receiving over 99% of votes cast, although Resolution 7 concerning Thierry Garnier was withdrawn. Special resolutions also passed with varying degrees of support, including the authority to allot shares (93.13% for) and disapply pre-emption rights for acquisitions (89.14% for).
