BETA Close X Drax Group plc has announced transactions by persons discharging managerial responsibilities following the vesting of awards under its Deferred Share Plan and Long-Term Incentive Plan. On April 1, 2026, CEO Will Gardiner vested 74,066 DSP shares and 114,421 LTIP shares, selling 34,934 and 53,967 respectively at 886.12 pence per share to cover tax liabilities, retaining 39,132 DSP shares and 60,454 LTIP shares.
