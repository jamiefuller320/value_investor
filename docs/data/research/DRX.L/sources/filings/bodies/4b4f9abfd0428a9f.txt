BETA Close X Drax Group plc announced the results of its Annual General Meeting held on April 30, 2026, where all 22 resolutions were passed by poll. Key resolutions included the adoption of the Annual Report and Accounts with 100% approval, the approval of the Annual Report on Remuneration with 97.81% in favour, and the approval of the Directors' Remuneration Policy with 93.18% in favour. The final dividend of 17.4 pence per share was approved by 99.22% of votes. Resolutions concerning the re-appointment of auditors and authority to purchase own shares also received strong support, with 99.99% and 97.95% respectively.
