As separately announced by the Company today, the Company is also conducting a non-pre-emptive placing of New Ordinary Shares (the " Placing Shares ") to institutional investors (the " Placing ") by way of an accelerated bookbuilding process (the " Bookbuild "). For the avoidance of doubt, the Retail Offer is not part of the Placing. The issue price of the Retail Offer Shares will be equal to the placing price for the Placing Shares (the " Placing Price ") and will be determined at the close of the Bookbuild (the " Issue Price "). The Retail Offer is conditional on the New Ordinary Shares to be issued pursuant to the Retail Offer and the Placing being admitted to trading on the main market for listed securities of London Stock Exchange plc (" Admission "). Admission is expected to take place at 8:00 a.m. on 22 September 2026. The Retail Offer will not be completed without the Placing also being completed. Peter Kelly and other persons acting in concert with him (within the meaning of the UK City Code on Takeovers and Mergers) are prohibited from subscribing for any Retail Offer Shares as part of the Retail Offer. Reason for the Retail Offer The Company values its retail investor base and believes that it is in the best interests of shareholders as well as wider stakeholders, to provide retail and other interested investors the opportunity to participate in the Retail Offer alongside the Placing in line with the Pre-Emption Group guidelines. The Company intends to use the net proceeds raised pursuant to the Placing and the Retail Offer to part-fund the acquisition of General Datatech, L.P. (" GDT "), a leading multi-vendor IT solutions provider for an enterprise value of approximately $1,050 million (£785 million [1] ) (" Acquisition "). In the event that the Acquisition does not complete, the board of directors of the Company (the " Board ") would consider, in light of circumstances at the time, the appropriate use of the net proceeds raised pursuant to the Placing and the Retail Offer, including the extent to which they should be retained for general purposes or used in relation to other capital investments or acquisition opportunities as well as the appropriate extent to which the funds would be returned to the Company's shareholders. Details of the Acquisition can be found in the separate announcement made by the Company today.
