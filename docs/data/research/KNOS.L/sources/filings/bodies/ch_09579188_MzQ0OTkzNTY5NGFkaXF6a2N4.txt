General information and basis of preparation
Basis of preparation

Kainos Group plc ("the Company") is a public limited company incorporated in the United
Kingdom under the Companies Act 2006 (registration number 9579188). The address of the
registered office is 2! Farringdon Road, 2" Floor, London, ECIM 3HA.

These Interim Company Financial Statements have been prepared under sections 836 and 838
of the Companies Act 2006 for the purposes of confirming that the Company has sufficient
distributable reserves to support if required the full value of the £30.0 million share buyback
programme approved on 8" November 2024.

These financial statements contain information about Kainos Group ple as an individual
company and do not contain consolidated financial information for the Group. These accounts
are abridged and unaudited but are otherwise prepared on a consistent basis and following the
same accounting policies as the annual accounts for the period to 31 March 2024.

These interim accounts do not constitute statutory accounts within the meaning of sections
434(3) of the Companies Act 2006. The statutory accounts for the year ended 31 March 2024
have been filed with the registrar of companies and can be found on the Group’s website. The
auditor's report on those accounts was unqualified, did not contain an emphasis of matter
paragraph and did not contain any statement under Section 498(2) or (3) of the Companies Act
2006.

The Directors have chosen not to present comparative figures for the period and the note
disclosures that would normally form part of a set of complete financial statements.
