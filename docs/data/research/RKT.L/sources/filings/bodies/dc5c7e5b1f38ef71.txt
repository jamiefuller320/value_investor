Disclaimer* Reckitt Benckiser Group PLC 24 July 2026 24 July 2026 Divestment of Russia Hygiene business Reckitt Benckiser Group plc ("Reckitt") (LSE: RKT) has agreed to divest its Russian Hygiene business to Arnest Management LLC ("Arnest"). Russia Hygiene represented c.1% of Core Reckitt's Net Revenue in the year ended 31 st December 2025. Subject to the satisfaction of customary conditions, including relevant UK regulatory approvals, the transaction is expected to complete during the second half of 2026. Reckitt retains ownership of the Russia Health business which continues to supply consumer health products.
