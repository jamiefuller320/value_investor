Disclaimer* Reckitt Benckiser Group PLC 14 May 2026 14 May 2026 RECKITT 'RECKITT FOCUS ON' INVESTOR SEMINAR Today we will host the next event in our series of 'Reckitt Focus On' investor seminars, which provide incremental insights into our business, strategy, and key growth drivers. Today's event, Reckitt Focus On: Digital Science, will bring together our digital and R&D teams to show how we are combining proprietary data, predictive science and generative AI technology to innovate with speed, scale and superiority, creating repeatable functionality that unlocks productivity and category growth. The presentation will be led by Angela Naef PhD, Chief R&D Officer. She will be joined by Shannon Eisenhardt, CFO, Nigel Richardson, Chief Information and Digitisation Officer, and Bastien Parizot, SVP GBS & AI Enterprise. No new material financial information or trading information will be disclosed. The event will begin at 15:00 BST and will be live-streamed. Registration details and other information can be found at: Reckitt Focus On: Digital Science | Reckitt.com A replay of the event will be available on the website afterwards.
