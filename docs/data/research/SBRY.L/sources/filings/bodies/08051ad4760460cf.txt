BETA Close X Sainsbury's reported a strong first quarter for the 16 weeks ending June 20, 2026, with total retail sales excluding fuel increasing by 2.7% to £9,153 million, driven by a 3.1% rise in Sainsbury's sales to £8,041 million, though General Merchandise and Clothing saw a 3.7% decline. Grocery sales grew by 3.6% to £7,603 million, while Argos sales experienced a slight decrease of 0.5% to £1,114 million. The company maintained its competitive value position, leading to volume growth and market share gains, and expects to deliver total underlying operating profit between £975 million and £1,075 million for the full year.
