Disclaimer* Kingfisher PLC 04 November 2026 KINGFISHER PLC Block Admission Application Kingfisher plc (‘Kingfisher’) announces that an application has been made to the London Stock Exchange (the ‘LSE’) for the block admission of a total of 3,000,000 ordinary shares of 15 5/7 pence each (ISIN: GB0033195214) (‘Shares’) to be admitted to trading on the LSE's Main Market. It is expected that admission will be effective at 8.00 am on 5 November 2026. The Shares will be issued in connection with the Kingfisher ShareSave Plan. When issued, these Shares will rank pari passu in all respects with the existing ordinary shares of Kingfisher. The new block admission application is in addition to Kingfisher’s existing block admission which has 1,626,665 shares remaining unallotted as at 3 November 2026.
