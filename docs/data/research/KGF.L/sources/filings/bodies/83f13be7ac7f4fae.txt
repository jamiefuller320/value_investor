BETA Close X Kingfisher PLC announced on April 24, 2026, that on April 23, 2026, several Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibility (PDMRs) were granted awards under the Kingfisher Performance Share Plan and its French Free Share Sub Plan. These awards, valued at nil cost per share and calculated based on an average share price of 300.2 pence, are subject to continued employment and, where applicable, performance conditions. The total number of shares awarded across all PDMRs amounts to 3,740,000, with specific grants detailed for individuals including Thierry Garnier, Bhavesh Mistry, Graham Bell, Chris Blatchford, Sapna Dutta, Pascal Gil, John Mewett, Nils Swolkień, and Laurent Vittoz. The measures and targets that will apply to the awards subject to performance conditions are set out in the Company's 2025/26 Annual Report and Accounts, which is available at www.kingfisher.com .
