About Kingfisher plc Kingfisher plc (Kingfisher) is an international home improvement company with over 1,800 stores, supported by a team of c.70,000 colleagues. We operate in seven countries across Europe under retail banners including B&Q, Screwfix, Castorama, Brico Dépôt, and Koçtaş. We offer home improvement products and services to consumers and trade professionals who shop in our stores and via our e-commerce channels.
