On 25 September 2026, Thierry exercised 1,552,662 nil-cost options over Ordinary Shares of 15 5/7p in the Company, granted to him under the Kingfisher Alignment Share and Transformation Plan (KASTIP) and the Kingfisher Performance Share Plan (KPSP).
