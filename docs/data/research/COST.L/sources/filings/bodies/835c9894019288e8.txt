Disclaimer* Costain Group PLC 30 April 2026 Costain Group PLC (the Company) 30 April 2026 DIRECTOR/PDMR/PCA DEALING The Company announces that Fiona MacAulay, Non-Executive Director, has purchased 4,169 Ordinary Shares in the Company at £1.79 per share on 29 April 2026. Her resulting shareholding in the Company is 10,516 shares representing 0.003% of the Company's issued share capital. Further to the previous Director/PCA Dealing announcement on 22 April 2026, Alex Vaughan, Chief Executive Officer, has decided to retain the balance of his shares following the exercise of his awards under the Long Term Incentive Plan (LTIP) and Share Deferral Plan (SDP), which vested on 8 April 2026 and 9 April 2026 respectively. Following the sale of sufficient shares to cover the income tax and NI liability arising on exercise (and detailed in the previous announcement), the remaining 415,015 shares were transferred to his spouse on 29 April 2026. Following the transaction, Alex's resulting shareholding in the Company is 985,752 shares, representing 0.4% of the Company's issued share capital. Together with his qualifying LTIP and SDP options, the total value of Alex's beneficial shareholding is 764% of salary (Executive Directors shareholding guideline is 200% of salary).
