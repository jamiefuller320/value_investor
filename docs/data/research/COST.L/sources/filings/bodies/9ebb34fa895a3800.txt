Disclaimer* Costain Group PLC 23 November 2026 Costain Group PLC (the Company) 23 November 2026 Block Listing Application The Company announces that an application has been made to the London Stock Exchange for a block listing of 3,675,609 ordinary shares of 1p each in the Company (the "Shares"), to be admitted to trading on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange. The Shares will be issued pursuant to the exercise of share options under the Company’s Employee Sharesave Plan and will, upon issue, rank pari passu in all respects with the Company’s existing issued Ordinary Shares. Admission of these Shares is expected to become effective on Friday 27 November 2026.
