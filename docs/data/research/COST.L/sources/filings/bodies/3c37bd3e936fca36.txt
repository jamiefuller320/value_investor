BETA Close X Costain Group PLC announced that Chief Executive Officer Alex Vaughan and Chief Financial Officer Helen Willis exercised share awards on April 17, 2026, under the 2023 Share Deferral Plan and 2014 Long Term Incentive Plan. Following the exercise, Mr. Vaughan now beneficially holds 985,752 shares with a value of £4,242,602, representing 764% of his salary, while Ms. Willis beneficially holds 344,645 shares valued at £2,585,606, or 555% of her salary. A portion of the exercised shares were sold to cover tax and National Insurance liabilities, with Mr. Vaughan retaining his remaining shares and Ms. Willis transferring hers to her spouse.
