BETA Close X Serco Group PLC announced the results of its Annual General Meeting, confirming that all resolutions were passed by a poll. Key resolutions included the approval of the Annual Report and Accounts with 758,363,622 votes for, the Directors' Remuneration Report with 756,552,651 votes for, and the final dividend of 3.05 pence per share with 758,732,424 votes for. Director re-elections and auditor reappointment also received strong support, with most resolutions passing with over 97% of votes cast.
