4 REASONS FOR THE OFFER AND ESTIMATED NET AMOUNT OF PROCEEDS  
Reasons for the offer: The Issuer intends to use an amount equal to the net 
proceeds of the Notes to finance and/or refinance new or 
existing Eligible Green Assets in the Renewable Energy 
category as described in the Issuer's Sustainable and 
Transition Financing Framework (as defined in the 
Prospectus dated 5 December 2025, as supplemented on 
3 August 2026). 
 
To the extent that Eligible Green Assets in this category 
are unavailable, including where they unexpectedly 
mature or pending full allocation, the Issuer intends to 
hold and/or invest any surplus proceeds at its discretion 
in cash or short term liquid investments until additional 
Eligible Green Assets are available. The Issuer plans to 
allocate an amount equal to 80 per cent. of the net 
proceeds to refinancing existing Eligible Green Assets, 
with an amount equal to the remaining 20 per cent. to be 
allocated to financing new Eligible Green Assets over the 
next 12 months.  
The Framework is available on the Issuer’s website 
alongside a second party opinion regarding alignment of 
the Framework with the International Capital Market 
Association 2025 Green Bond Principles, although 
neither the Framework nor the second party opinion is 
incorporated in, nor do they form part of, these Final 
Terms or the Prospectus.
