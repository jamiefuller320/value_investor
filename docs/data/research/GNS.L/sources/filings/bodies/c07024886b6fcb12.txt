BETA Close X Genus PLC announced that all resolutions presented at its Annual General Meeting on November 19, 2025, were passed by poll, with resolutions 1 to 16 approved as ordinary resolutions and resolutions 17 to 20 as special resolutions. Notably, the final dividend of 21.7 pence per ordinary share received 100% in favour, and the re-appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as auditor also achieved 99.99% approval. The total number of ordinary shares eligible to vote was 66,537,765, with 70.37% of the issued share capital represented by validly cast votes.
