ABOUT
PAN AFRICAN

THE AFRICAN-FOCUSED GOLD PRODUCER

Pan African is an African-focused mid-tier gold producer, dual primary listed on the AIM of the LSE (ticker: PAF) and the main board of the
JSE (ticker: PAN) as well as the A2X Market (A2X). Our shares trade on the OTCOX Best Market (OTCQX) in the United States of America
(USA) through a Level 1 American Depository Receipt (ADA) programme (ticker: PAFRY), sponsored by the Bank of New York Mellon, and
ordinary shares (ticker: PAFRF).

OUR STRATEGY
To safely optimise the value of our mineral deposits, utilising our combined knowledge base and Mineral Resources in an
entrepreneurial manner to generate compelling returns and to ensure the long-term sustainability of our business.
