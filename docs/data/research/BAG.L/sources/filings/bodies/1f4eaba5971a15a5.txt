Barr PLC announced that its Chief Executive Officer, Euan Sutherland, Chief Finance & Operating Officer, Stuart Lorimer, and Chief Legal & Sustainability Officer, Julie Barr, purchased ordinary shares on November 10, 2025, on the London Stock Exchange.
