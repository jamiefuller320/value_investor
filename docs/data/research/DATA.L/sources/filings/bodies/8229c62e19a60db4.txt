The full version of the SSE Announcement is as follows: AIR CHINA LIMITED ANNOUNCEMENT ON KEY OPERATING DATA OF SEPTEMBER 2026 In September 2026, Air China Limited (the " Company ") and its subsidiaries (the " Group ") recorded a year-on-year increase in both combined passenger capacity (measured by available seat kilometers (the " ASK ")) and passenger traffic (measured by revenue passenger kilometers (the " RPK ")). Passenger capacity recorded a year-on-year increase of 3.5%, and passenger traffic recorded a year-on-year increase of 6.3%. Among which, for domestic routes, passenger capacity decreased by 0.4% year on year, and passenger traffic increased by 2.7% year on year. For international routes, passenger capacity increased by 14.2% year on year, and passenger traffic increased by 16.2% year on year. For regional routes, passenger capacity decreased by 11.3% year on year, and passenger traffic decreased by 4.2% year on year. The passenger load factor was 85.4%, representing an increase of 2.3 percentage points as compared to the same period last year. Among which, the passenger load factor for domestic routes increased by 2.7 percentage points year on year, the passenger load factor for international routes increased by 1.4 percentage points year on year, and the passenger load factor for regional routes increased by 5.9 percentage points year on year. In terms of cargo operations, cargo capacity (measured by available freight tonne kilometers (the " AFTK ")) increased by 6.3% year on year. Cargo and mail traffic (measured by revenue freight tonne kilometers (the " RFTK ")) increased by 10.5% year on year. The cargo load factor was 42.5%, representing a year-on-year increase of 1.6 percentage points. With effect from 4 September 2026, the Company launched the new route Beijing Capital-Bucharest-Zagreb with three flights per week. In September 2026, the Group introduced one A320 series aircraft, and retired one B737 series aircraft and two A320 series aircraft. At the end of September 2026, the Group operated a fleet of 974 aircraft, including 436 self-owned aircraft, 258 aircraft under finance lease and 280 aircraft under operating lease.
