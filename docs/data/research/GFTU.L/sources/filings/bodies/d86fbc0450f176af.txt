BETA Close X Grafton Group plc announced that all ordinary and special resolutions were passed at its Annual General Meeting on 15 May 2026. Key resolutions included the approval of the financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025, the declaration of a final dividend of 27.0 pence per Ordinary Share, and the re-election of directors, with most receiving over 99% of votes in favour. The continuation of PricewaterhouseCoopers as auditors was also approved with 99.37% of votes for, and the company received strong support for authorising directors to allot securities and for market purchases of its own shares.
