STRATEGIC REPORT

Introduction

This strategic report has been prepared in accordance with the requirements of Section 414 of the Companies
Act 2006. Its purpose is to inform shareholders and help them to assess how the directors have performed their
duty to promote the success of Grafton Group (UK) ple (“the Company”).

Principal activities
“The Company operates as an investment holding company.

Review of business for the year and state of affairs at 31 December 2019

The statement of profit and loss and other comprehensive income for the year ended 31 December 2019 and
the balance sheet at 31 December 2019 are set out on pages 9 and 10, respectively. The profit after tax for the
year amounted to Stg£92,108,005 (2018: Stg£93,866,032). Dividend income increased by 55% in 2019, which
partially offset the additional one-off costs incurred in 2019.

Principal risks and uncertainties

The principal risk and uncertainty affecting the Company surrounds the collectability of intercompany
receivables and the carrying value of investments in subsidiaries. The directors have assessed that the
investments in subsidiaries are recoverable and believe in relation to the amounts due from Group companies
that any expected credit loss would not be material.

Key performance indicators

The Company is an investment holding and finance company. Given the straightforward nature of the Company,
the directors are of the opinion that analysis of KPI’s is not necessary for an understanding of the development,
performance or position of the Company.

Financial risk management objectives and policies

The Company's activities expose it to a number of financial risks including cash flow risk and liquidity risk. The
Company adopts a prudent approach to liquidity management and to mitigate against cash flow and liquidity
risk continuously monitors forecasted and actual cash flows and maintains sufficient cash reserves to meet its
obligations.

Employment of disabled persons
All applications for employment from disabled persons are given full and fair consideration, due regard being
given to the aptitude and ability of the individual and the requirements of the position concerned.

Disabled persons are treated on equal terms with other employees as regards training, career development and
promotion. In the event of an existing employee becoming disabled, every effort is made to ensure continuity of
employment, and that appropriate training is given where necessary.

Section 172 statement for the reporting year ended 31 December 2019

As the Company is an investment holding and finance company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Grafton Group
ple ("Grafton”; “the Group”) the key board decisions made during the year were made in line with the strategic
goals and objectives of both the Company and the Group.

The Company is fully aligned with the approach taken by the Group in respect of the interests of the Group's
employees, suppliers, customers, the impact of the Group’s operations on the community and the environment,
the need to maintain high standards of business and conduct and the likely consequences of any decision in
the long term, details of which are set out in detail in the 2019 Annual Report of Grafton Group plc. As a wholly-
owned subsidiary, the Directors do not consider the factor set out in section 172(1)(f) (the need to act fairly
between the members of the company) relevant to the proper discharge of their duty under section 172.

Results and dividends

The statement of profit and loss and other comprehensive income for the year ended 31 December 2019 and
the balance sheet at 31 December 2019 are set out on pages 9 and 10, respectively. The profit after tax for the

A second interim dividend for 2018 of 12.00p was paid on 5 April 2019 amounting to £28,531,906 (2018: a

was paid on 11 October 2019 amounting to.£15,455,016 on the ‘C’ ordinary shares of Grafton Group (UK) pic
(2018: an interim dividend for 2018 of 6.0p was paid on 28 September 2018).

Going Concern

The Board of Directors have formed a judgment at the time of approving the financial statements that there is
a reasonable expectation that the Company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for
the foreseeable future.

At 31 December 2019 the Company had net current liabilities of £210.7 million including a net liability due to
Group companies of £329.8 million. The company is also dependent on dividend income from its operating
subsidiaries to meet its interest obligations and expenses. The ultimate parent company has given an
undertaking to use its best endeavours to provide financial support to the Company for at least one year from
the date of approval of these financial statements to enable the Company to meet its liabilities as they fall due.

The Board has taken account of the impact on the overall UK business of the COVID-19 related restrictions on
trading from March onwards in its going concern assessment. The ultimate impact of COVID-19 on the operating
subsidiaries is dependent on future developments which are highly uncertain and cannot be predicted, including
the timing and extent of recovery, potential further escalations of the virus and its impact on government
restrictions and economic activity. The Group put in place measures to protect liquidity and manage costs during
the period of initial restrictions and reduced activity and the Board is satisfied that additional measures could be
initiated, if required again.

The Board has considered the overall liquidity within the UK business and reasonably believe that these
uncertainties would not have a material impact on its ability to continue as a going concern as of the date of
approval of these financial statements.

The Board is satisfied that the going concern basis is appropriate in the preparation of these financial
statements.

Future developments
The directors consider both the results for the year and the Company's prospects to be satisfactory. It is the
directors’ intention to continue to develop the present activities of the Company.

Post balance sheet events

Dividend: A second interim dividend of 12.5p was due to be paid on 6 April 2020 amounting to £29.7m. A
decision was taken to suspend payment of this second interim dividend due to the COVID 19 pandemic. There
were no other material events since the year end which require disclosure in these accounts.

COVID-19 pandemic: The outbreak of Covid-19 in the United Kingdom, where the Company's operating
subsidiaries trade, resulted in the closure of the majority of distribution branches and manufacturing plants on
24 March 2020 on the basis of guidance provided by the UK Government.

The UK distribution business traded at significantly reduced levels principally from the provision of materials for
emergency supplies and essential projects. The easing of trading restrictions permitted the majority of locations
that were closed in March to either fully or partially reopen through the month of May and in June the distribution
branches and manufacturing plants operated fully, subject to government health and safety guidelines on social
distancing.

The businesses traded well in the period up to closure. The overall level of trading during the period since
reopening, while encouraging, was influenced by a range of factors including pent-up demand and may not be
indicative of.ongoing activity levels. The Group remains focused on appropriately managing its cost base as
restrictions ease and trading returns to a more sustainable level. The Directors are satisfied about the underlying
resilience of the operating subsidiaries.

In April 2020 the Company received approval from the Bank of England to issue commercial paper under the
Covid Corporate Finance Facility. The Company has not utilised this facility.

Political and charitable contributions
The Company made no political contributions during the year (2018; £Nil). Charitable donations in 2019
amounted to £2,561 (2018: £26,800).
