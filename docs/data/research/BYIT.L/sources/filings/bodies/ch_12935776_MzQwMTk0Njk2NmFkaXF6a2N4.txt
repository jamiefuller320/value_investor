Notes to the interim accounts

1. Accounting policies

1.1 General information

The interim accounts of Bytes Technology Group plc, (“the Company”) for the six months ended 31 August 2023 have
been prepared as justification of distribution in accordance with the requirements of sections 836 and 838 of the
Companies Act 2006 and were authorised for issue in accordance with a resolution of the directors on 24 October 2023.

The Company is a public limited company, incorporated and domiciled in the UK. Its registered address is Bytes House,
Randalls Way, Leatherhead, Surrey, KT22 7TW.

The interim accounts were approved by the Board of Directors on 24 October 2023. The financial information contained
in this report does not constitute statutory accounts within the meaning of section 434 of the Companies Act 2006. The
interim accounts should be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 28 February
2023, which were prepared in accordance with Financial Reporting Standard 101 (“FRS 101”) in conformity with the
requirements of the Companies Act 2006. The annual financial statements for the year ended 28 February 2023 were
approved by the Board of Directors on 22 May 2023 and have been delivered to the registrar. The auditor’s report on
those financial statements was unqualified, did not contain an emphasis of matter paragraph and did not contain any
statement under section 498(2) or (3) of the Companies Act 2006.

The Company is a holding company and the ultimate parent company of a group which is one of the UK’s leading
providers of IT software offerings and solutions, with a focus on cloud and security products. The Company has a primary
listing on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange (LSE) and a secondary listing on the Johannesburg Stock
Exchange (JSE).

1.2 Basis of preparation

The Company’s interim accounts comprise the interim statement of profit or loss, interim statement of financial
position, interim statement of changes in equity and a summary of significant accounting policies and the notes thereto.
These interim accounts contain information about the Company as an individual company and do not contain
consolidated financial information for the Group. These accounts are abridged and unaudited but are otherwise
prepared on a consistent basis and follow the same accounting policies as the annual financial statements for the year
ended 28 February 2023.

All amounts disclosed in the Company’s interim accounts and notes have been rounded off to the nearest thousand,
unless otherwise stated.

1.3 Going concern

The Company is a holding company and reliant upon the support of its subsidiary undertakings to operate as a going
concern. The directors have considered the period up to 28 February 2025 for their assessment of going concern,
together with several key dependencies such as the Company’s exposure to liquidity and cash flow risk.

The Company has £654.1 million of net assets at the statement of financial position date. Included within the net assets
are net current assets of £11.9 million. The Company has cash and cash equivalents of £6.8 million as at 31 August 2023
and the Company also has access to up to a further £30 million via a revolving credit facility. Accordingly, the directors
conclude it to be appropriate that the financial statements be prepared on a going concern basis.

1.4 Critical accounting estimates and judgements
The preparation of the interim accounts requires the use of accounting estimates which, by definition, will seldom equal
the actual results. Management also needs to exercise judgement in applying the Company’s accounting policies.

The key accounting estimates and judgements reported in the financial statements for the year ended 28 February 2023
are still relevant. There are no new significant accounting estimates or judgements arising in the six-month period to 31
August 2023, nor to the date of publication of this interim report.

7. Borrowings

On 17 May 2023 the Group entered into a new three-year committed Revolving Credit Facility (RCF) for £30 million,
including an optional one-year extension to 17 May 2027, and a non-committed £20 million accordion to increase the
availability of funding should it be required for future activity. The new facility replaced the previous RCF which was
entered into in December 2020. The new facility has incurred an arrangement fee of £0.1 million, being 0.4% of the new
funds available. The Group has so far not drawn down any amount on this facility and to the extent that there is no
evidence that it is probable that some or all of the facility will be drawn down, the fee has been capitalised as a
prepayment and amortised over the period of the facility.
