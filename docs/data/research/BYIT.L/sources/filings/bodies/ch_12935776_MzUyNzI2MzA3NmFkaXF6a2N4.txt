Notes to the interim accounts

1. Accounting policies

1.1General information

The interim accounts of Bytes Technology Group plc, (“the Company”) for the three months ended 31 May 2026 have
been prepared as justification of distribution in accordance with the requirements of sections 836 and 838 of the
Companies Act 2006 and were authorised for issue in accordance with a resolution of the directors on 16 June 2026.

The Company is a public limited company, incorporated and domiciled in the UK. Its registered address is Bytes House,
Randalls Way, Leatherhead, Surrey, KT22 7TW.

The interim accounts were approved by the Board of Directors on 12 June 2026. The financial information contained in
this report does not constitute statutory accounts within the meaning of section 434 of the Companies Act 2006. The
interim accounts should be read in conjunction with the annual financial statements for the year ended 28 February
2026, which were prepared in accordance with Financial Reporting Standard 101 (“FRS 101”) in conformity with the
requirements of the Companies Act 2006. The annual financial statements for the year ended 28 February 2026 were
approved by the Board of Directors on 11 May 2026 and have been delivered to the registrar. The auditor's report on
those financial statements was unqualified, did not contain an emphasis of matter paragraph and did not contain any
statement under section 498(2) or (3) of the Companies Act 2006.

The Company is a holding company and the ultimate parent company of a group (“the Group”) which is one of the UK's
leading value-added IT resellers focused on subscription software, security, IT services, Al, cloud-based solutions and
hybrid infrastructure. The Company has a primary listing on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange (LSE) and a
secondary listing on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE).

1.2 Basis of preparation

The Company's interim accounts comprise the interim statement of profit or loss, interim statement of financial
position, interim statement of changes in equity and a summary of significant accounting policies and the notes thereto.
These interim accounts contain information about the Company as an individual company and do not contain
consolidated financial information for the Group. These accounts are abridged and unaudited but are otherwise
prepared on a consistent basis and follow the same accounting policies as the annual financial statements for the year
ended 28 February 2026.

All amounts disclosed in the Company's interim accounts and notes have been rounded off to the nearest thousand
pounds, unless otherwise stated.

1.3 Going concern

The Company is a holding company and reliant upon the support of its subsidiary undertakings to operate as a going
concern. The directors have considered the period up to 31 August 2027 for their assessment of going concern, together
with several key dependencies such as the Company’s exposure to liquidity and cash flow risk.

The Company has £680.6 million of net assets at the statement of financial position date. Included within the net assets
are net current assets of £38.5 million. The Company has cash and cash equivalents of £62.9 million as at 31 May 2026
and also has access to up to a further £30 million via a revolving credit facility. Accordingly, the directors conclude it to
be appropriate that the financial statements be prepared on a going concern basis.

1.4 Critical accounting estimates and judgements
The preparation of the interim accounts requires the use of accounting estimates which, by definition, will seldom equal
the actual results. Management also needs to exercise judgement in applying the Company’s accounting policies.

The key accounting estimates and judgements reported in the financial statements for the year ended 28 February 2026
are still relevant. There are no new significant accounting estimates or judgements arising in the three-month period to
31 May 2026, nor to the date of publication of this interim report.

7. Borrowings

In May 2026 the Group entered into a three-year extension of the £30 million Revolving Credit Facility (RCF), the facility
includes a one-year extension to 17 May 2030, and a non-committed £45 million accordion to increase the availability
of funding should it be required for future activity. The arrangement fee has been capitalised as a prepayment and will
be amortised over the three-year extension period. The financial covenants applicable to the there-year extension are
unchanged.

8. Share repurchase programme

On 12 May 2026 the Company announced a Share Repurchase Programme under which it has entered into an agreement
with its brokers, Numis Securities Limited and Peel Hunt LLP, to purchase the Company's shares for an aggregate value
of up to £25.0 million. The brokers will manage the purchase on a discretionary basis, purchasing shares within pre-set
parameters on the open market and making their trading decisions independently of the company. The Company
intends to cancel all shares purchased under the Programme.

At 31 May 2026 the Company had repurchased 1,045,907 shares for a total consideration of £3.68 million plus costs of
£27,700. The purchase has been treated as a distribution though retained earnings. Of this amount £2.26 million (plus
costs of £17,000) was paid at the period end with the balance of £1.42 million (plus costs of £10,700) accrued and paid
on 1 June 2026.

At the reporting date 652,337 of these shares had been cancelled and the remainder were cancelled on 3 June 2026.

A provision of £21.3 million has also been recognised through retained earnings for estimated further purchases after
the end of the reporting period
