Disclaimer* IG Group Holdings plc 08 September 2026 LEI No: 2138003A5Q1M7ANOUD76 08 September 2026 Notice of Underdog investor seminar IG Group Holdings plc ("IG") today announces that it will host a virtual seminar for institutional investors and analysts on Thursday 8 October 2026, from 2.00pm to 3.15pm (BST). The seminar will focus on Underdog, the US daily fantasy sports and prediction markets business that IG agreed to acquire on 30 July 2026, subject to regulatory approvals. The seminar provides an opportunity to hear directly from Underdog co-founder and Chief Executive Officer Jeremy Levine on the business's product offering, operations and strategy. Jeremy will be joined by IG Chief Executive Officer Breon Corcoran and IG Chief Financial Officer Clifford Abrahams, with a Q&A to follow. To register for the webcast or conference call, please use the links below: IG Group | Webcast | Shareholder Seminar: Underdog IG Group | Conference call | Shareholder Seminar: Underdog Presentation materials will be made available on IG's investor relations website following the event, at https://www.iggroup.com/investor-relations . As previously announced, IG will host a Strategy Update on Thursday 22 October 2026 at UBS, 5 Broadgate, London EC2M 2QS, with the option to join in person or watch via livestream. The Strategy Update will set out IG's strategy, capital allocation framework and guidance for the enlarged group, with registration details to follow. The 8 October seminar provides a dedicated opportunity to explore Underdog in greater detail ahead of that event. Separately, Jeremy Levine will participate in a fireside chat at the Bank of America Gaming and Lodging Conference in New York on Wednesday 9 September 2026 at 3.40pm Eastern Time. The session will be webcast by Bank of America, with a replay available on IG's investor relations website afterwards.
