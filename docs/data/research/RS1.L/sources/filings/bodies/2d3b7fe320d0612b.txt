BUSINESS REVIEW We made strong strategic and operational progress in the first half, delivering 7% like-for-like revenue growth as market conditions improved across all regions. PMI [1] indicators remained in expansionary territory throughout the period, and we saw accelerating momentum in EMEA and Asia Pacific and continued strong growth in the Americas. Industrial demand was stronger than anticipated, particularly in automation, energy infrastructure and data centre-related applications, which continue to benefit from structural growth trends, while demand remained healthy more broadly across all industry verticals. We are now entering the third year of our multi-year strategy with continued focus on strengthening our technology foundations, driving operational excellence, enhancing our customer understanding and experience and improving our digital tools and commercial capabilities to strengthen customer acquisition, increase share of wallet and improve sales effectiveness. These initiatives are now beginning to deliver with rolling 12 months average order value increasing by 10% [2] to £293, and revenue from high-potential accounts grew 11% on a like-for-like basis. eProcurement revenue increased 8%, alongside growth of 6% in services and solutions and 5% in RS PRO. During the period, we further connected our Customer Data Master (CDM), Customer Data Platform (CDP) and Customer Relationship Manager (CRM) capabilities, enabling integrated customer insights to be translated real-time into sales, marketing and digital activities. Alongside these customer-facing initiatives, we also continued to progress the next phase of our technology transformation, advancing our Digital Commerce, Warehouse Management and Process & Operational Excellence programmes. Together, these initiatives are creating a more agile, connected and scalable technology platform, that will ultimately enable us to increase automation and materially improve productivity and operational efficiency across the Group. This progress supported both revenue growth and improved financial performance ahead of expectations, with gross margin increasing 0.3 percentage points to 43.4%, or 0.6 percentage points on a like-for-like basis, benefiting from pricing inflation and price initiatives. As a result, adjusted operating profit was £142 million, up 15% with an adjusted operating margin of 9.2% up 0.7 percentage points on a like-for-like basis. Cash generation remained strong, with adjusted operating cash flow conversion of 96%, well above our >80% target despite inventory investment to support growth. Net debt increased from 2025/26 by £40 million to £369 million, largely as a result of our new distribution centres increasing our lease liabilities by £35 million. Net debt to EBITDA remained well within our target range at 1.1x. Customers Focus on higher value customers through harnessing data, effective strategic engagement, and optimising cost to serve. Our CDM platform has been live and driving quality improvements from the start of this year, providing a more consistent, micro segmented and unified view of customers across the Group. Combined with our existing CDP and CRM capabilities, it will strengthen the foundation of our connected customer intelligence capability, helping us better understand customer needs and behaviours, identify opportunities and engage customers more effectively across sales, marketing and digital channels. During the first half, we expanded the use of our CDP and CRM capabilities, launching 12 CDP powered campaigns and customer journeys across EMEA. These CDP powered initiatives, combined with the real-time integration of our CRM, enabled immediate creation and activation of leads and opportunities. Building on these foundations, we launched our first supplier sponsored campaign combining customer segmentation, personalised digital content and targeted communications, initially identifying more than 40,000 relevant customer profiles to target. As we continue to standardise data, connect platforms, scale adoption and expand customer use cases, these capabilities are supporting more personalised customer experiences and better-informed commercial decision making. CRM is embedded across all our EMEA markets and already used in the US and Canada. Further expansion is planned in Mexico and ultimately Asia Pacific. As an integral part of how our sales teams operate, it supports greater consistency, improved opportunity management, stronger selling discipline and better targeting of resources, enabling a more effective, agile and scalable sales approach. We are also increasingly embedding AI across our customer, sales and digital platforms to help turn data into insight and action more quickly. From identifying customer opportunities and enabling more personalised engagement, to improving seller productivity and enhancing digital search and product discovery, AI is becoming an important layer of this capability, supported by our unique and trusted customer data foundation created through our CDM, CDP and CRM investments. Experience Strengthen and tailor our customer experience to provide a digitally enabled, seamless omni channel service relevant for our customers’ needs. Our focus is on delivering First Choice outcomes for our target customers. During the period, we improved product discovery, purchasing journeys and site performance while continuing to invest in our next generation digital commerce platform. AI capabilities are integral to our digital channels, helping customers find products more quickly and efficiently through AI enabled search, while further developments are underway in conversational search, product recommendations and product discoverability. To make it easier for customers to find the products they need, we launched search engine optimisation content hubs across four major markets, enhanced manufacturer part number search and expanded our presence across both traditional search and emerging AI enabled channels. As customer buying behaviours evolve and AI assisted product research becomes more prevalent, these capabilities will help ensure our products remain visible, relevant and accessible. These initiatives are already driving stronger engagement, with search add-to-cart conversion increasing by 12.6%. Looking ahead, we remain focused on the deployment and optimisation of our next-generation digital commerce platform. Building on the initial launch in the Americas, we have continued to optimise the platform. In parallel, a materially enhanced version with improved architecture is now in final testing for an initial launch in Switzerland next year, ahead of its broader rollout beginning in 2027/28. It provides a more modern and scalable foundation for our digital experience, enabling faster innovation, greater personalisation and enhanced customer engagement over the coming years. Product and suppliers Deliver a seamless, mutually value creative supplier experience with appropriate and data driven breadth, depth and range curation. We've now fully deployed our enhanced Product Management System, giving us greater agility in managing our portfolio, enhancing product information and localisation, and improving both customer search-ability and inventory management. This has also supported the continued expansion of RS PRO, one of our key strategic growth drivers. During the half, RS PRO surpassed 100,000 products and launched a new AI enabled platform to accelerate product onboarding and improve efficiency. RS PRO revenue grew 16% on a like-for-like basis in Asia Pacific and 30% in the Americas, while EMEA delivered growth of 4% from a significantly larger base. RS PRO now contributes to 14% of Group revenue and 20% of EMEA revenue. We also continued to strengthen our pricing capabilities through greater pricing system flexibility, targeted pricing initiatives, improved discount discipline and increased use of AI enabled pricing tools. These capabilities are helping us to improve commercial execution, make better pricing decisions, differentiate propositions, and respond more effectively to inventory availability and supplier cost movements. Services and solutions Deliver valued, scalable solutions to build greater strategic engagement and drive product pull-through. eProcurement revenue increased by 8% on a like-for-like basis, including growth of 9% in EMEA. We also progressed the rollout of our upgraded Purchasing Manager solution in EMEA, enhancing functionality and supporting greater customer engagement with other regions to follow. In Mexico, we successfully tested and deployed a new SaaS based eProcurement implementation model. This simplifies deployment, improves scalability and should support greater customer adoption, creating an opportunity to expand eProcurement penetration over time. In RS Integrated Supply (RSIS), we continued to improve operational efficiency and execution while focusing our offer on attractive industrial verticals that value our outsourcing model and technology differentiation. We enhanced our proprietary MRO procurement platform through data cleansing and standardisation, global customer identifiers, improved multi-site inventory visibility and curated customer marketplaces. Operational excellence Deliver efficient physical, digital and process infrastructure, improved operating leverage and marginal drop-through. We continued to invest in the foundations required to support future growth and improve sustainability and efficiency. During the half, we made good progress upgrading our distribution infrastructure. In Italy and Ireland, we advanced the transition to new facilities, both of which are expected to go live in the second half and provide additional capacity to support growth in these attractive local markets and more broadly across EMEA. In Italy, we are also deploying our next-generation Robotic Warehouse Automation System, which will serve as the blueprint for automation of our regional distribution centres going forward. We have also upgraded the Warehouse Management System and technology environment in Nuneaton and remain on track for go-live in the second half. We also progressed our Process & Operational Excellence programme, which is driving process harmonisation in advance of our redesign and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) upgrade commencing in 2027/28 and are now moving into the detailed design phase. Our deployment approach will be phased over three years, market-by-market, with larger markets transitioning later in the programme. The sequencing of Digital Commerce, Warehouse Management and ERP deployments will be tailored to the needs of each market, supporting an efficient rollout and reducing transformation risk. This phased approach allows us to simplify and automate processes, reduce technology and maintenance costs, and improve supply chain and back-office efficiency, creating a lower cost and more scalable operating platform that supports future growth and stronger operating leverage. People Create an inclusive and engaging environment where everyone is proud and excited to come to work and can perform at their best, develop and thrive. Building the organisational capability to deliver change at pace remains a critical part of our enhancement journey. We have continued to strengthen our change management and programme management capabilities across the Group. We have also commenced rolling out ‘RS Shift’, our standard framework for change delivery, providing a consistent set of tools and processes. In programme management, we have established standardised processes and commenced capability-building initiatives to strengthen execution. These initiatives are helping create a more agile and resilient organisation. We have also continued to invest in leadership and future capability, expanding our Leadership Advantage programme to over 100 senior leaders and progressing Skills@RS to ensure we have the capabilities required for the future. Importantly, employee engagement remains strong, with our latest engagement score increasing four points year on year to 77, reflecting the positive progress we're making as we continue to evolve the organisation. Driving sustainable growth and stronger value creation ESG remains an important strategic priority for RS and is increasingly a point of differentiation with customers, suppliers and other stakeholders. Our approach is embedded within our strategy and supports the long term resilience and growth of the business. We continued to make good progress against our sustainability commitments, and our new Italy distribution centre is a good example of how operational excellence and sustainability go hand in hand. Designed to high environmental standards, the facility combines automation, increased capacity and energy efficient technologies, supporting improved customer service, lower operating costs and reduced environmental impact. We also expanded our Better World product range to around 36,000 products, giving our customers a greater choice of sustainable products they can trust, and launched our £5 million Better World Fund, reinforcing our commitment to supporting local communities and developing the next generation of engineers by 2030. Growth was focused in subregions outside of Australia/New Zealand, led by our largest categories of Automation & Control, Cables & Connectors, and Facilities & Maintenance, alongside particularly strong performance in Semis & Passives. Strategic sales, digital and marketing initiatives supported growth across customer segments, with Standard customer revenue increasing 14% and high-potential Corporate customer revenue increasing 22% on a like-for-like basis. RS PRO revenue increased 16% on a like-for-like basis, supported by targeted go-to-market initiatives, enhanced brand awareness, greater customer engagement and focused inventory management. Greater China and South East Asia delivered like-for-like growth of 32% and 20% respectively. In South East Asia, growth was driven by successful execution of the region's strategy to expand relationships with higher value Corporate customers, supported by strong digital performance. In Greater China, revenue growth was driven primarily by strength in electronics and continued demand associated with data centre investment. Japan and Korea, representing 9% of regional revenue, delivered 20% like-for-like revenue growth, supported by strong digital performance and the effectiveness of our website and digital marketing initiatives. Australia and New Zealand, representing 34% of regional revenue, delivered 1% like-for-like revenue growth, primarily reflecting softer market conditions in Australia, where PMIs remain below expansionary levels. Gross margin declined by 40 basis points on a like-for-like basis, reflecting a higher contribution from the faster growing Greater China business.
