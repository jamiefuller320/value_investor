At the Company's Annual General Meeting on 15 April 2026, shareholders approved the Directors' recommendation to declare and pay a final dividend in respect of the year ended 31 December 2025 of 6.8 cents per share. The conversion rate has been determined by taking the average US dollar/Sterling exchange rate between 14-16 April 2026, resulting in a final exchange rate to be applied of US$1.3552 to £1.
