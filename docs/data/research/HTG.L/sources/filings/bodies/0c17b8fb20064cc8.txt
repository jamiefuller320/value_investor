Disclaimer* Hunting PLC 16 October 2026 For immediate release 16 October 2026 Hunting PLC ("Hunting" or the "Company") Payment of 2026 Interim Dividend in Sterling Hunting PLC (LSE: HTG), the global precision engineering group, today announces the foreign exchange conversion rate to Sterling for the Interim Dividend to be paid to shareholders on 30 October 2026. As announced in the Company's 2026 Interim Results on 21 August 2026, the Directors declared an interim dividend of 7.0 cents per share. The conversion rate has been determined by taking the average US dollar/Sterling exchange rate between 13-15 October 2026, resulting in a final exchange rate to be applied of US$1.3253 to £1. The final Sterling value of the dividend to be paid to shareholders will therefore be: 5.2818 pence per Ordinary share.
