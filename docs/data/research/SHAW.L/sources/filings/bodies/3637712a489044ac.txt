Independent Shareholder Vote - Independent Director Elections (Resolutions 4, 6, 7 and 11-13) Resolutions 4, 6, 7 and 11 to 13 related to the election and re-election of Independent Non-Executive Directors. Under the UK Listing Rules (LR 6.4.6R), because PSC Marlin Holdco Limited and Marlinbass Limited each control more than 30 per cent. of the voting rights of the Company, the re-election of an Independent Director must be approved by a majority of both: (i) the shareholders of the Company; and (ii) the independent shareholders of the Company (that is, the shareholders of the Company entitled to vote on the election of Independent Directors who are not PSC Marlin Holdco Limited or Marlinbass Limited).
