Disclaimer* Endeavour reports tragic loss of life at Ity mine ENDEAVOUR REPORTS TRAGIC LOSS OF LIFE AT ITY MINE London, 9 October 2026 – Endeavour Mining plc (LSE:EDV, TSX:EDV, OTCQX:EDVMF) (“Endeavour” or the “Company”) regrets to report that an employee at its Ity mine, in Côte d’Ivoire, was found unresponsive in the processing plant and subsequently passed away on 7 October, despite medical efforts to resuscitate him at the mine site clinic. Endeavour has launched a comprehensive investigation and will report on the findings of this investigation within its Q3-2026 results. Mining and processing activities at the Ity mine continue uninterrupted. The health, safety and welfare of our colleagues remain our top priority, and we extend our sincere sympathies and support to our employee’s family, colleagues and friends.
