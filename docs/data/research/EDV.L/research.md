# Endeavour Mining plc (EDV.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-08-13T07:40:51.125825+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Endeavour Mining is a FTSE-listed West African gold producer whose investment case rests on record cash generation at elevated gold prices, a net-cash balance sheet, and a visible path to organic growth via Assafou and Sabodala-Massawa underground expansion. The quantitative screen rates the name a buy on cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk families (P/E 16.1, yield 3.5%, ROE 31.3%), though an FCF basis overlay adjusts the tactical signal to hold given filing FCF of $1.13bn versus screen TTM $1.76bn. The central debate is whether current margins and shareholder returns are sustainable through a production-weighted H2 2026, rising host-country royalties/taxes, and jurisdictional risk across Burkina Faso, Côte d’Ivoire, and Senegal—not whether the asset base is low quality.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value-oriented investor, EDV combines a cyclically leveraged but currently exceptional cash profile with governance-aligned capital return. The screen passes 11 of 22 models across five factor families with full data coverage (20/20 metrics), including Neff PEGY (PEGY ≈ 0.05), Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, Dividend Growth, Piotroski F-Score, and Financial Health—consistent with a producer generating substantial free cash at today’s gold price rather than a distressed deep-value trap.

Business quality is supported by filings: H1-2026 adjusted EBITDA of $1,611m (+38% YoY), record H1 free cash flow of $761m, net cash of $254m at 30 June 2026 (leverage 0.09× LTM adjusted EBITDA), and shareholder returns of $301m in H1 (minimum dividend programme plus buybacks). The 2026–2028 returns framework commits a minimum $1.0bn in dividends (subject to gold ≥$3,000/oz and leverage below 0.50×), with management signalling supplemental returns at prevailing prices. Organic optionality is material: Assafou DFS (320koz pa at $1,026/oz AISC in years 1–8; $5.1bn after-tax NPV at $4,000/oz gold) targets FID by end-2026, supporting a stated 1.5Moz group target by 2030.

The screen’s buy signal is credible on earnings quality and payout capacity, but conviction is tempered by the FCF reconciliation gap flagged in the screen itself and by H1-2026 production running 13% below H1-2025 (564koz vs 647koz), with Mana expected below guidance. Value investors should treat EDV as a cash-rich, returning cyclical rather than a re-rated growth compounder.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Jurisdictional and regulatory:** Operations concentrated in Burkina Faso, Côte d’Ivoire, and Senegal expose the group to mining-code changes, royalty escalation (Côte d’Ivoire royalties rose from 6% to 8%, retroactive from Q1-2025 per Q1/H1 RNS), withholding taxes on cash upstreaming ($150–170m guided WH tax for 2026), and potential permit/convention delays (Assafou mining convention negotiations; Ivorian mining code under review may alter fiscal terms pre-convention). RNS forward-looking language explicitly cites expropriation, licence non-renewal, and political instability.

**Operational and cyclical:** H1-2026 AISC of $1,871/oz (+46% YoY) reflects lower volumes and price-linked royalties; Mana is guided below production range and above cost range following Siou depletion and Aviera geotechnical pause. Production remains wet-season and mine-sequence dependent. Gold price reversal would compress margins faster than cost guidance suggests (royalty sensitivity $6–10/oz per $100/oz gold move, per H1 RNS).

**Safety:** Fatal contractor incidents at Mana (March 2026) and Lafigué (May 2026) are disclosed in RNS; TRIFR 0.72 TTM but fatalities remain a reputational and operational risk.

**Governance and litigation:** The FY2024 annual report body documents former CEO termination with ~$27.8m total clawback/forfeiture and a Canadian securities class action (Non-Executive Director Alison Baker appointed as defendant; additional fees in 2026). Current CEO Ian Cockerill held 228% of required shareholding vs 450% guideline at year-end 2025.

**Capital allocation:** Assafou upfront capex rose to $1,061m in DFS (+45% vs PFS); growth and strategic exploration investments (Altair, Koulou, East Star) compete with returns. Sustaining capex guidance increased $50m to $280m for FY-2026.

**Accounting / FCF:** Screen flags material FCF basis divergence (filing $1,131m vs TTM $1,764m vs company-adjusted $613m). Dividend-yield-dependent models pass on adjusted basis; verify cash conversion and sustaining vs growth capex classification before sizing yield trades.

No explicit going-concern qualification was found in available filing extracts.
