BETA Close X Rio Tinto has announced dealings by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibility (PDMRs) and Key Management Personnel (KMPs) under its UK Share Plan (UKSP). On April 30, 2026, three PDMRs/KMPs were awarded free Rio Tinto plc shares at a price of £72.25 per share. Peter Cunningham and Katie Jackson each received 49 shares, while Simon Trott was awarded 17 shares. These notifications are made to both the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) and the London Stock Exchange (LSE) as part of the company's dual listed company structure. UK Share Plan (UKSP) The UKSP is an HMRC approved Share Incentive Plan under which qualifying UK employees are able to purchase, on a quarterly basis, Rio Tinto plc shares. Rio Tinto plc shares are purchased out of monthly deductions from salary, and participants are allocated an equivalent number of shares free of charge (matching shares). Qualifying UK employees are also awarded Free Shares once a year. On 30 April 2026, the following PDMR / KMP were awarded Free Shares under the UKSP.
