BETA Close X Rio Tinto has announced the currency exchange rates for its 2026 interim dividend of 211.00 US cents per share, which will be paid on 24 September 2026. Shareholders electing to receive the dividend in pounds sterling will receive 156.661841 British pence, while those opting for Australian dollars will receive 296.410761 Australian cents, and New Zealand dollar recipients will get 366.478506 New Zealand cents. These rates are based on the exchange rates applicable on 15 September 2026.
