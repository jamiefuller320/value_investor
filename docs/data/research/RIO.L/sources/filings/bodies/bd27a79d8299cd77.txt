BETA Close X Rio Tinto has announced dealings in its securities by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibility (PDMRs) and Key Management Personnel (KMPs) on April 16, 2026. These transactions involved the acquisition of Rio Tinto plc shares through dividend reinvestment plans across various share schemes, including the Share Plan Account, UK Share Plan, and Global Employee Share Plan. Notably, Matthew Holcz acquired 15.28078 shares at £73.78339 each, Katie Jackson acquired 205.76502 shares at the same price, and Peter Cunningham acquired 125 shares at £73.78339.
