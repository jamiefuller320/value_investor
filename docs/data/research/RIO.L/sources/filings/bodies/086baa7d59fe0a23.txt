BETA Close X Rio Tinto plc has announced the award of free shares under its UK Share Plan to key management personnel. Chief Financial Officer Peter Cunningham, Chief Executive of Copper Katie Jackson, and Chief Executive Simon Trott each received awards of ordinary shares. Specifically, Peter Cunningham and Katie Jackson were awarded 49 ordinary shares each, while Simon Trott received 17 ordinary shares. All awards were made on April 30, 2026, at a price of £72.25 per share, and were conducted on the XLON market.
