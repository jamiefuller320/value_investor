BETA Close X Rio Tinto plc has announced shareholding updates for key management personnel, including Chief Financial Officer Peter Cunningham and Chief Executive of Copper Katie Jackson. Peter Cunningham's transactions involved the vesting of matching shares and shares in lieu of dividend under the Global Employee Share Plan, totaling 14.728855 shares, and the sale of 6.186108 shares to cover tax at £73.115 per share. Katie Jackson acquired and was awarded a total of 20.200666 ordinary shares at £73.52461 per share under the Global Employee Share Plan and the UK Share Plan. Jérôme Pécresse, Chief Executive of Aluminium, also acquired and was awarded 19.709316 ordinary shares at £73.52461 per share. These transactions occurred on April 17, 2026, and April 17, 2025, outside of a trading venue and on XLON respectively.
