BETA Close X Rio Tinto plc has announced the admission of 54,705 Ordinary Shares of 10p each to trading on the London Stock Exchange's Main Market, effective March 9, 2026. These shares are being issued to satisfy awards under the company's Global Employee Share Plan and are fully fungible with existing ordinary shares. Following this admission, the total number of ordinary shares in issue will be 1,256,023,286.
