BETA Close X Rio Tinto plc has announced that Matthew Holcz, Chief Executive of Iron Ore, has had 3,772 ordinary shares vest under the Management Share Award, with an additional 343 shares vesting in lieu of dividend, both outside a trading venue on February 20, 2026. Additionally, 2,013 ordinary shares were sold at AUD 162.4091 on the same date on XLON to cover tax liabilities related to the Management Share Award.
