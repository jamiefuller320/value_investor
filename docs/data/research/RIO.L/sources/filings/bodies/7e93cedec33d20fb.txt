BETA Close X Rio Tinto has announced the dates for its 2026 Annual General Meetings, with the closing date for nominations for director candidates, other than those recommended by the board, set for March 2, 2026. The Annual General Meetings for both Rio Tinto Limited and Rio Tinto plc will take place on May 6, 2026. Further details regarding the meeting arrangements are available on the company's website.
