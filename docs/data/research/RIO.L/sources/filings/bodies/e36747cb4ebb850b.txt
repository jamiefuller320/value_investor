BETA Close X Rio Tinto reported a significant step-change in performance for the first half of 2026, with underlying EBITDA increasing 28% to $14.8 billion and free cash flow rising 75% to $3.8 billion, driven by favorable commodity prices and a 3% increase in copper equivalent production. The company declared an interim ordinary dividend of $3.4 billion, a 43% increase, supported by strong cash flow and a robust balance sheet. Productivity benefits of $870 million have been realized, with a target of $1.8 billion annualised run-rate by year-end, contributing to a 43% rise in underlying earnings to $6.9 billion and a 17% underlying return on capital employed. Copper, Aluminium, and Lithium segments collectively contributed over 50% of underlying EBITDA, with significant growth in copper EBITDA by 84% to $5.7 billion.
