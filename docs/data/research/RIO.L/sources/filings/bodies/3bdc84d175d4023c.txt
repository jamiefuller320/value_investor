BETA Close X Rio Tinto plc has announced that Peter Cunningham, Chief Financial Officer, Katie Jackson, Chief Executive of Copper, Jérôme Pécresse, Chief Executive of Aluminium & Lithium, and Simon Trott, Chief Executive, have received awards under the company's Equity Incentive Plan 2018. Specifically, Mr. Cunningham was granted a Bonus Deferral Award of 6,885 ordinary shares and a Performance Share Award of 83,518 ordinary shares. Ms. Jackson received a Bonus Deferral Award of 5,535 ordinary shares and a Performance Share Award of 73,223 ordinary shares. Mr. Pécresse was awarded a Bonus Deferral Award of 8,651 ordinary shares and a Performance Share Award of 82,859 ordinary shares. Mr. Trott received a Bonus Deferral Award of 10,484 ordinary shares and a Performance Share Award of 146,011 ordinary shares. All awards were granted on March 19, 2026, outside of a trading venue, and are initial notifications.
