2 Basis of preparation

These interim financial statements have been prepared to provide the Directors with the financial position of the Company as
at 30 September 2026 for the purposes of ensuring sufficient distributable reserves are available in accordance with sections
836 and 838 of the Companies Act 2006.

These interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with FRS 101 “Reduced Disclosure Framework" and
the Companies Act 2006. FRS 101 applies the recognition and measurement requirements of UK-adopted international
accounting standards, with amendments where necessary in order to comply with the Companies Act 2006, together with
reduced disclosure requirements. The financial statements are presented in pounds sterling, which is the Company's
functional currency, and are rounded to the nearest thousand pounds.

The Company's financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 were prepared in accordance with FRS 102 "The
Financial Reporting Standard applicable in the UK and Republic of Ireland". The Company has adopted FRS 101 with effect
from 1 January 2026. The transition from FRS 102 to FRS 101 has not resulted in any change to the amounts recognised in
the comparative period or equity at 1 January 2025, 1 January 2026 or 30 September 2026, and no transition reconciliation is
therefore presented. Separately, the comparative information has been restated to correct two errors, relating to the
accounting for the Employee Benefit Trust and to an over-distribution of reserves by a subsidiary undertaking, as set out on
pages 9-11.

The Company is a qualifying entity for the purposes of FRS 101. As the parent of the group, it prepares publicly available
consolidated financial statements in accordance with UK-adopted international accounting standards, in which the Company
is included.

These interim financial statements are unaudited and do not constitute statutory financial statements within the meaning of
section 434 of the Companies Act 2006. They do not include all of the information and disclosures required for a complete set
of financial statements and should be read in conjunction with the Company's financial statements for the year ended 31
December 2025.

Statutory financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 have been delivered to the Registrar of Companies. The
auditor's report on those financial statements was not qualified, did not include a reference to any matters to which the auditor
drew attention by way of emphasis without qualifying the report, and did not contain a statement under section 498(2) or (3) of
the Companies Act 2006. To date, no statutory financial statements have been filed for any period ending later than 31
December 2025.

Employee Benefit Trust

The Company sponsors the Trustpilot Employee Benefit Trust (the “EBT’), a trust established by trust deed for the benefit of
employees and former employees of the Company and its subsidiaries. The Company has the power to appoint and remove
the trustee, funds the EBT's acquisition of ordinary shares in the Company by way of unsecured interest-free loan, and is
indemnified by the trust deed in respect of the trustee's liabilities. The EBT holds shares on an unallocated basis for the
purpose of satisfying awards granted under the Company's employee share plans. The trustee has waived all rights to
dividends on shares held by the EBT and does not exercise voting rights in respect of unallocated shares unless requested by

the Company.

The Directors have concluded that the EBT acts as the Company's agent and that the substance of the arrangement is that
the Company retains the risks and rewards attached to the shares held. The assets and liabilities of the EBT are therefore
accounted for as those of the Company. Ordinary shares of the Company held by the EBT are presented as a deduction from
equity at cost within own shares. No-gain or loss is recognised in profit or loss on the purchase, sale, trarisfer or cancellation
of those shares. Amounts advanced by the, Company to the EBT, and the corresponding obligation of the EBT, are
eliminated. The determination that the EBT acts as the Company's agent is a significant judgement made by the Directors in
applying the Company's accounting policies.

Employee Benefit Trust (continued)

In the financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 the assets and liabilities of the Employee
Benefit Trust were not accounted for as those of the Company: amounts advanced to the EBT were presented
as a receivable and shares held by the EBT were not recognised in equity. This was an error, and the correction
recognises ordinary shares acquired by the EBT in December 2025 at a cost of £1,406 thousand as a deduction
from equity, together with £1,128 thousand of consideration unpaid at 31 December 2025 and settled on 2
January 2026, and derecognises the £278 thousand advanced to the EBT. The correction has no effect on profit
or loss for the year ended 31 December 2025.

Over-distribution of reserves by a subsidiary undertaking

In December 2025 Trustpilot Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, declared an interim dividend of
£13,000 thousand in favour of the Company. It was subsequently been determined that as at the date of
declaration Trustpilot Ltd did not have sufficient distributable profits to make a distribution of that amount, and
£1,820 thousand of the dividend was made otherwise than in accordance with the Companies Act 2006,
meaning no dividend income could be recognised for this amount, The amount concerned is held by the
Company for the account of Trustpilot Ltd.

In the financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 the whole of the dividend was recognised as
dividend income on the basis of the Company's understanding at that time. In light of the above, a correction
reverses £1,820 thousand from dividend income and recognises a corresponding liability within amounts owed
to group undertakings.

The effect of the corréctions on each line item affected is set out on page 11. Neither correction has any effect
on equity at 1 January 2025, the EBT having been established in December 2025 and the dividend having been
declared in December 2025. Accordingly, no balance sheet as at 1 January 20285 is presented.
