1 Company information

Trustpilot Group pic (the "Company”) is a public company limited by shares,
incorporated on 8 February 2021, domiciled in the United Kingdom and registered in
England & Wales with company number 13184807, and having its registered office at Sth
Floor, The Minster Building, 21 Mincing Lane, London EC3R 7AG, United Kingdom.

2 Basis of preparation

These interim financial statements have been prepared to provide the Directors with
the financial position of the Company as at 27 August 2026 for the purposes of ensuring
sufficient distributable reserves are available in accordance with sections 836 and

838 of the Companies Act 2006.

These interim financial statements have been prepared in accordance with FRS 101
“Reduced Disclosure Framework" and the Companies Act 2006. FRS 101 applies the
recognition and measurement requirements of UK-adopted international accounting
standards, with amendments where necessary in order to comply with the Companies Act
2006, together with reduced disclosure requirements. The financial statements are
presented in pounds sterling, which is the Company's functional currency, and are
rounded to the nearest thousand pounds.

The Company's financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 were prepared
in accordance with FRS 102 "The Financial Reporting Standard applicable in the UK and
Republic of Ireland". The Company has adopted FRS 101 with effect from 1 January 2026.
The transition from FRS 102 to FRS 101 has not resulted in any change to the amounts
recognised in the comparative period or equity at 1 January 2025, 1 January 2026 or

27 August 2026, and no transition reconciliation is therefore presented. Separately,

the comparative information has been restated to correct an error in the accounting for

the Employee Benefit Trust, as set out on pages 9-10.

The Company is a qualifying entity for the purposes of FRS 101 and prepares publicly
available consolidated financial statements in accordance with UK-adopted
international accounting standards, in which the Company is included.

These interim financial statements are unaudited and do not constitute statutory
financial statements within the meaning of section 434 of the Companies Act 2006. They
do not include all of the information and disclosures required for a complete set of
financial statements and should be read in conjunction with the Company's financial
statements for the year ended 31 December 2025.

Statutory financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 have been delivered
to the Registrar of Companies. The auditor's report on those financial statements was
not qualified, did not include a reference to any matters to which the auditor drew
attention by way of emphasis without qualifying the report, and did not contain a
statement under section 498(2) or (3) of the Companies Act 2006. To date, no statutory
financial statements have been filed for any period ending later than 31 December

2025.

Employee Benefit Trust

The Company sponsors the Trustpilot Employee Benefit Trust (the "EBT"), a trust

established by trust deed for the benefit of employees and former employees of the

Company and its subsidiaries. The Company has the power to appoint and remove the trustee,
funds the EBT's acquisition of ordinary shares in the Company by way of unsecured
interest-free loan, and is indemnified by the trust deed in respect of the trustee's liabilities.

The EBT holds shares on an unallocated basis for the purpose of satisfying awards granted
under the Company's employee share plans. The trustee has waived all rights to dividends

on shares held by the EBT and does not exercise voting rights in respect of unallocated
shares unless requested by the Company.

The Directors have concluded that the EBT acts as the Company's agent and that the
substance of the arrangement is that the Company retains the risks and rewards
attaching to the shares held. The assets and liabilities of the EBT are therefore

accounted for as those of the Company. Ordinary shares of the Company held by the EBT
are presented as a deduction from equity at cost within own shares. No gain or loss is
recognised in profit or loss on the purchase, sale, transfer or cancellation of those

shares. Amounts advanced by the Company to the EBT, and the corresponding obligation
of the EBT, are eliminated. The determination that the EBT acts as the Company's agent
is a significant judgement made by the Directors in applying the Company's accounting
policies.
