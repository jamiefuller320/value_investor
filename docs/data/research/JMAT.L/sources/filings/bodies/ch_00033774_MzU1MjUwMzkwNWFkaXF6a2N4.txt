Purpose of preparation

These unaudited special purpose interim financial statements have been prepared under section 838 of the Companies Act 2006
(“the Act") for the purpose of confirming that Johnson Matthey plc (“the Company’) has sufficient distributable reserves to support
a share buyback to commence in September 2026 and conclude by 31% March 2027.

The accounts are not the annual statutory accounts required under the Act and do not include all the disclosures which would
have been required in such accounts. In the opinion of the Board any such disclosures omitted are not material for determining
whether the proposed distribution would contravene the relevant requirements of the Act.

Basis of preparation

Johnson Matthey pic (the "Company") is a public limited company incorporated under the Companies Act 2006 and domiciled in England
in the United Kingdom. The address of the registered office is HQ Building, Orchard Road, Royston, Hertfordshire, SG8 SHE, United
Kingdom.

These unaudited special purpose interim financial statements have been prepared for the period 1* April 2026 to 31% July 2026 in
accordance with Financial Reporting Standard 101 Reduced Disclosure Framework ("FRS 101") and under section 838 of the
Companies Act 2006, applying the accounting policies adopted in the Company's most recent annual financial statements.

These unaudited special purpose interim financial statements have been prepared for the specific purpose of determining distributable
reserves prior to the commencement of a share buyback. They do not constitute statutory financial statements within the meaning of
section 434 of the Companies Act 2006 and should be read in conjunction with the Company's most recent annual financial statements.

These unaudited special purpose interim financial statements are prepared on the historical cost basis except for certain assets and
liabilities which are measured at fair value. These unaudited special purpose interim financial statements have been prepared on a going
concern basis in accordance with the Companies Act 2006.

The Company does not include a cash flow statement in its annual financial statements, and therefore no cash flow statement is
presented in these unaudited special purpose interim financial statements. The preparation of the consolidated financial statements is
not required for the purposes of determining the Company's profits available for distribution.

Material accounting policies
The accounting policies have been applied consistently to all periods presented in these unaudited special purpose financial statements.
The accounting policies applied in preparing these unaudited special purpose interim financial statements are consistent with those
applied in the preparation of the Company's most recent annual financial statements for the year ended 31% March 2026.

Sources of estimation uncertainty and judgements made in applying accounting policies

The preparation of unaudited special purpose interim financial statements requires management to make judgements, estimates and
assumptions that affect the application of accounting policies and the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, income and expenses.

Actual results may differ from these estimates.

The sources of estimation uncertainty and judgements applied in the preparation of these unaudited special purpose interim financial
statements are consistent with those described in the Company's most recent annual financial statements.
