Disclaimer* Victrex PLC 16 November 2026 16 November 2026 Victrex plc ("Victrex", the "Company" or the "Group") Appointment of Chief Financial Officer Victrex announces the appointment of Adam Butcher as Chief Financial Officer ("CFO") and Executive Director, effective 15 February 2027, following a comprehensive search process. Adam brings senior finance experience across FTSE 100 and S&P 500 businesses, spanning financial planning and analysis, M&A, restructuring and business transformation. Most recently, Adam served as Vice President Finance, Catalyst Technologies at Honeywell Technologies, where he led the integration of the acquired Johnson Matthey Catalyst Technologies business, focusing on cost efficiencies, commercial repositioning and organisational integration. His previous experience includes roles at Rolls-Royce, General Electric, Baker Hughes and Wood Group, where he supported restructuring and business simplification. The appointment follows a comprehensive internal and external selection process and supports Victrex’s focus on delivering sustainable growth and long-term shareholder value. Chris Gilbert will continue as Interim CFO until Adam joins and will support an orderly handover. Dr James Routh, Chief Executive Officer, said: “I’m pleased Adam is joining Victrex, which completes the refresh of the executive leadership team. As outlined in our recent Capital Markets Event, we are progressing at pace with our strategic objectives, and we are excited about the opportunity to drive significant improvements in performance over the medium term.” There are no matters requiring disclosure under UKLR 6.4.8R in relation to Adam Butcher’s appointment. Notes: Adam’s remuneration arrangements have been set in accordance with the Directors’ Remuneration Policy. On appointment, his annual salary will be £400,000. Full details will be disclosed in the Victrex 2027 Annual Report.
