Disclaimer* Victrex PLC 19 November 2026 19 November 2026 Victrex plc ("Victrex", the "Company" or the "Group") Notice of Full Year Results Victrex plc, the innovative world leader in high-performance polymer solutions, will announce its full year results for the twelve months ended 30 September 2026 on Tuesday 1 December 2026. Analyst and investor presentation James Routh, Chief Executive Officer, and Chris Gilbert, Interim Chief Financial Officer, will present the results to analysts and investors at 9.00am (UK time) on Tuesday 1 December 2026 at the offices of Investec Bank plc, 30 Gresham Street, London EC2V 7QP. A dial-in facility will also be available. To register, please visit https://brrmedia.news/VCT_FY26 . A copy of the presentation will be available on the Group’s website at www.victrexplc.com under Investors / Results & Presentations. Retail investor presentation Management will also present the results via Investor Meet Company at 8.30am (UK time) on Wednesday 2 December 2026. The presentation is open to all existing and potential shareholders, with questions welcome in advance or during the live presentation. Investors can register for free via https://www.investormeetcompany.com/victrex-plc/register-investor . About Victrex: Victrex is an innovative world leader in high-performance polymer solutions, focused on the end markets of Aerospace and Defence, Automotive, Electronics, Energy & Industrial and Medical. Every day, millions of people use products and applications which contain our performance materials - from smartphones, aircraft and vehicles to energy production and medical devices. With over 45 years' experience, we develop world leading solutions in PEEK based polymers which shape future performance for our customers and our markets, driving value for our shareholders. Find out more at www.victrexplc.com.
