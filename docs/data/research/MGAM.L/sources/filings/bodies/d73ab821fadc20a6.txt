BETA Close X Morgan Advanced Materials plc announced on March 31, 2026, that several Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs), including the CFO, Group HR Director, CEO, and Presidents of various divisions, were awarded ordinary shares under the company's Long-Term Incentive Plan (LTIP) and Deferred Bonus Plan. These awards, made on March 26, 2026, at a price of GBP 1.929 per share, are conditional and subject to performance conditions over a three-year period for the LTIP awards, with the Deferred Bonus Plan awards vesting on March 26, 2029. The total number of shares awarded across these transactions amounts to 1,468,800.
