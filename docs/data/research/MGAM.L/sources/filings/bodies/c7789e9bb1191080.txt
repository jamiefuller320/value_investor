BETA Close X Morgan Advanced Materials plc announced that several Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs), including Maria Antoniou, Paul Boulton, John Righini, Damien Caby, and Desislava Jung, have participated in transactions involving the company's ordinary shares. These transactions involved the vesting of 15% of their 2023 Long-Term Incentive Plan awards, with a portion of the vested shares sold at GBP 1.847 per share to cover tax liabilities. Specifically, Maria Antoniou acquired 16,950 shares and sold 7,996, retaining 8,954; Paul Boulton acquired 6,491 shares and sold 3,070, retaining 3,421; John Righini sold 13,731 shares; Damien Caby acquired 13,731 shares and sold 6,599, retaining 7,132; and Desislava Jung sold 6,241 shares. All transactions occurred on March 23, 2026, on the London Stock Exchange.
