BETA Close X Morgan Advanced Materials plc announced that all resolutions presented at its 2026 Annual General Meeting on May 7, 2026, were passed by shareholders. The total number of ordinary shares with voting rights was 276,676,409, with 83.24% to 83.51% of the issued share capital voted on resolutions. Key resolutions, including the approval of audited accounts, directors' remuneration report, final dividend, and the re-election and election of directors, received overwhelming support, with votes for ranging from 90.91% to 100% of votes cast. However, resolutions concerning the general disapplication of pre-emption rights and the empowerment of directors to allot shares saw a notable percentage of votes against, at 8.23% and 8.45% respectively, with the disapplication of pre-emption rights for acquisitions receiving the highest opposition at 18.73%.
