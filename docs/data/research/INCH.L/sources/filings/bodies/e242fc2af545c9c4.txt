Disclaimer* Inchcape PLC 19 November 2026 19 November 2026 “In the Driving Seat” investor webinar – Our Growth Drivers Inchcape plc ("Inchcape" or the "Group"), the leading independent global automotive distributor, is today hosting an investor webinar, "In the Driving Seat", from 2.00pm – 4.15pm GMT. The webinar will focus on the Group's growth drivers, with the agenda and presenters as follows: Introduction and Overview - Duncan Tait, Chief Executive Scaling Through Distribution Contracts - Glafkos Persianis, CEO Europe & Africa Accelerating Growth Through M&A - Phil Jenkins, Chief M&A Officer Derco Deep Dive - Mike Bowers, CEO Americas Optimising Our Business - Romeo Lacerda, Chief Commercial Officer Delivering Profitable Growth - Adrian Lewis, Chief Financial Officer Q&A Session No new material information will be disclosed. Duncan Tait, Group Chief Executive, commented: “We have transformed Inchcape into the leading global automotive distributor, with a diversified and scaled portfolio of markets and OEM partners, a strong balance sheet and a highly cash-generative business model. “Today's event will demonstrate the opportunities we see in continuing to scale and optimise the business, in line with our Accelerate+ strategy. Our recent distribution contract wins provide an increasingly visible source of future growth, our disciplined approach to M&A continues to generate attractive returns, and we see substantial opportunity to create further value through the optimisation of our business. “Together, supported by our disciplined approach to capital allocation, these growth drivers underpin our confidence in our medium-term ambition to deliver more than 10% EPS CAGR to the end of 2030.” Materials and webcast To register for the webcast of the event please follow this link: https://brrmedia.news/INCH_WBNR_26 Presentation materials will be available at 1.00pm GMT today and a replay of the call will be available following the event on the Group’s website: www.inchcape.com . Inchcape works with our mobility company partners in smaller, more complex and harder-to-reach markets, which tend to be higher growth with low motorisation rates. By combining our in-market expertise with our unique technology and advanced data analytics, we create innovative customer experiences that deliver outstanding performance for our partners – building stronger automotive brands and creating sustainable growth. Our distribution platform connects the products of mobility company partners with customers, and our responsibilities span product planning and pricing, import and logistics, brand and marketing to operating digital sales, managing physical sales and aftermarket service channels. Our ambition is to deliver for our partners, our customers and our people – so they can realise their ambitions in the new world of mobility.
