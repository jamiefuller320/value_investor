"Australia remains a challenge - our key brand has underperformed, with deteriorating competitiveness in a rapidly-evolving market. We are taking further decisive action there, alongside our existing restructuring activity. We are confident these actions will improve our business in Australia over time, but we expect the current challenges in that market to continue impacting our performance there. "As a consequence, we now expect a lower Group operating margin for FY 2026 of c.5.5%, with adjusted EPS growth of c.10%, underpinned by continued momentum in the Americas and Europe & Africa. Our performance for this year and over the medium term will continue to be driven by our diversified OEM and market portfolio, distribution contract awards in recent years, strong cash generation and disciplined capital allocation, through share buybacks and value-accretive acquisitions. These factors support our continued confidence in our medium term ambition of delivering >10% EPS CAGR to the end of 2030. Inchcape works with our mobility company partners in smaller, more complex and harder-to-reach markets, which tend to be higher growth with low motorisation rates. By combining our in-market expertise with our unique technology and advanced data analytics, we create innovative customer experiences that deliver outstanding performance for our partners - building stronger automotive brands and creating sustainable growth. Our distribution platform connects the products of mobility company partners with customers, and our responsibilities span product planning and pricing, import and logistics, brand and marketing to operating digital sales, managing physical sales and aftermarket service channels. Our ambition is to deliver for our partners, our customers and our people - so they can realise their ambitions in the new world of mobility.
