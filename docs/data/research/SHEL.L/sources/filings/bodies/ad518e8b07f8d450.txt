PORTFOLIO DEVELOPMENTS Integrated Gas In July 2026, we reached an agreement to sell our 35% non-operated interest in Cyprus Offshore Block 12 containing the Aphrodite gas field, for up to $720 million, subject to customary adjustments and milestone-linked contingent payments. The transaction is expected to complete in early 2027, subject to regulatory approval and closing conditions. In September 2026, we completed the previously announced agreement to acquire ARC Resources Ltd. (“ARC”), following receipt of all required shareholder, court and regulatory approvals. The transaction adds approximately 370 kboe/d immediately across liquids and gas. In accordance with the terms of the agreement, ARC’s shareholders received CAD 8.20 in cash and 0.40247 ordinary shares of Shell plc for each ARC share, resulting in an equity value of USD 14.0 billion.1 In September 2026, Shell took a final investment decision on LNG Canada Phase 2. The project will increase LNG Canada’s production capacity from 14 million tonnes per annum (mtpa) to 28 mtpa. Shell has a 40% interest in LNG Canada and will receive nearly 6 mtpa of additional LNG from the expansion. Commercial operations are expected to begin in the early 2030s. Upstream In September 2026, we completed the previously announced sale of our 50% non-operated working interest in the Na Kika platform and associated fields in the Gulf of America, together with our 100%-owned Coulomb tieback, for cash proceeds of approximately $840 million reflecting adjustments between the effective date of July 1, 2025, and closing.
