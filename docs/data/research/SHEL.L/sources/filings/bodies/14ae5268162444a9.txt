BETA Close X Shell plc has announced the purchase of shares for cancellation as part of its ongoing share buy-back programme. On September 9, 2026, the company acquired 895,000 shares on the LSE at a volume-weighted average price of £35.2051, and 500,000 shares on XAMS at a volume-weighted average price of €41.1095. These transactions are being executed by Goldman Sachs International, which will make independent trading decisions within pre-set parameters until October 23, 2026, in accordance with UK and EU Market Abuse Regulations.
