BETA Close X Shell plc has announced conditional awards of performance shares under the Shell Share Plan 2023 to two Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs). Wael Sawan, Chief Executive Officer, received an award of 82,987 ordinary shares valued at £2,698,737.24, and Sinead Gorman, Chief Financial Officer, received an award of 10,964 ordinary shares valued at £356,549.28. These awards, made on May 20, 2026, are subject to performance conditions determined by the Remuneration Committee.
