BETA Close X Shell plc has announced the purchase of shares for cancellation as part of its ongoing share buy-back program. On August 13, 2026, the company acquired a total of 1,612,481 shares across various trading venues. Purchases on the LSE, Chi-X, and BATS involved a volume-weighted average price of £32.76 per share, with the highest price paid at £33.12 and the lowest at £32.535. On XAMS, CBOE DXE, and TQEX, the volume-weighted average price was €38.48 per euro, with prices ranging from €38.205 to €38.875. These transactions are being executed by Goldman Sachs International independently of the company.
