BETA Close X Vesuvius plc announced that all resolutions presented at its 2026 Annual General Meeting were passed by shareholders. The receipt of the Annual Report and Accounts received 100% of votes cast in favour, while the declaration of a final dividend was approved by 99.33% of votes cast. Directors' remuneration policies and reports also saw strong support, with 97.73% and 98.98% of votes in favour respectively. The re-election of directors and the reappointment and remuneration of the auditor were also overwhelmingly approved, with most resolutions securing over 98% of votes cast. Special resolutions concerning authority to allot shares and disapply pre-emption rights also passed with significant majorities, ranging from 97.52% to 98.25% in favour. The total number of voting rights in the company is 248,171,717.
