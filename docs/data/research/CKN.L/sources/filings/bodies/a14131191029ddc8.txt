BETA Close X Clarkson PLC announced on April 21, 2026, that its Executive Directors, Andi Case and Jeff Woyda, received deferred and performance share awards under the company's Long Term Incentive Plan. Mr. Case was granted 16,290 restricted shares and 17,288 nil-cost options, while Mr. Woyda received 4,213 restricted shares and 11,001 nil-cost options. Additionally, both directors acquired shares upon the vesting of prior awards, selling portions to cover tax liabilities, with Mr. Case retaining 7,190 shares and Mr. Woyda retaining 1,845 shares from a previous award, and Mr. Woyda retaining 2,432 shares from an exercised performance award. These transactions impact their respective shareholdings in the company.
