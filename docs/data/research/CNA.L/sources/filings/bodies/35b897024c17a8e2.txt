BETA Close X Centrica PLC has published its report on payments to governments for the year ended 31 December 2025, detailing total payments of £342,254,000. The majority of these payments, amounting to £341,492,000, were made to the United Kingdom, primarily consisting of £337,304,000 in taxes and £4,188,000 in fees.
