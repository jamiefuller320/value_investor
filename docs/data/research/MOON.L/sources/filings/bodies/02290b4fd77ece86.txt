J.P. Morgan Securities plc, which undertook the first instalment of the Buyback Programme on the Group’s behalf (as announced on 7 May 2026), repurchased 12,728,036 shares for an aggregate consideration of c.£32.5 million, including stamp duty and expenses. The Group has announced its intention to conduct up to £65 million of share buybacks in FY27, including the H1 FY27 Buyback Programme. This is in addition to the 1,996,871 shares purchased by the Moonpig Group plc Employee Benefit Trust for aggregate consideration of c.£4.3 million, excluding stamp duty and expenses, announced on 6 May 2026.
