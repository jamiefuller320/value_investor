Disclaimer* Balfour Beatty PLC 17 August 2026 TRANSACTIONS IN OWN SHARES Monday 17 August 2026 Balfour Beatty plc (the "Group" or "Balfour Beatty"), the international infrastructure group, announces that, in accordance with the terms of its share buyback programme announced on 5 January 2026, and increased on 11 March 2026 (the "Share Buyback"), the Group purchased 347,100 of its ordinary shares of 50 pence each through Merrill Lynch International between 10 August 2026 and 14 August 2026 . The Group will hold the repurchased shares in treasury. Following the purchase of these shares, the remaining number of ordinary shares in issue will be 476,925,685 (excluding treasury shares). The Group has, under the Share Buyback, repurchased 15,922,899 ordinary shares at a volume weighted average price of 809.201 pence, which are to be held in treasury with no voting rights attached. The shares repurchased under the Share Buyback are the only shares held in Treasury by the Group. Accordingly, the total number of voting rights in the Company is 476,925,685 . This figure may be used by shareholders as the denominator for the calculation by which they will determine whether they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, Balfour Beatty plc under the FCA's Disclosure and Transparency Rules.
