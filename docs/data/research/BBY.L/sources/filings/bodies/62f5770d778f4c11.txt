BETA Close X Balfour Beatty plc announced that conditional awards made under its Deferred Bonus Plan vested on March 31, 2026, including dividend equivalents. Chief Financial Officer Philip Harrison received a total of 101,517 ordinary shares, of which 47,785 shares were sold at £7.47 each to cover tax liabilities and expenses, leaving him with 53,732 retained shares. These transactions, involving the vesting and subsequent sale of shares, occurred outside a trading venue and on the London Stock Exchange respectively.
