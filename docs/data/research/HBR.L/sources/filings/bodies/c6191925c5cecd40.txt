The PSA Awards were subject to a three ‑ year relative TSR performance condition measured against the FTSE 100 and a sector peer group assessed over the period 1 January 2023 - 31 December 2025. Net shares released to the Executives, as shown below, are subject to a further two ‑ year post ‑ vesting holding period. The DBS Awards were granted in March 2023 in respect of 50 per cent of bonus amounts earned for the financial year ending 31 December 2022. In accordance with the Company's 2021 Remuneration Policy, these awards were subject to a three-year deferral period which has now ended. The tax and National Insurance liabilities arising on the release of the PSA and DBS Awards were settled by withholding a sufficient number of shares to cover those liabilities.
