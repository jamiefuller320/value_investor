Disclaimer* Harbour Energy PLC 10 July 2026 Harbour Energy plc ("Harbour" or the "Company") Completion of Waldorf acquisition 10 July 2026 Harbour Energy announces that it has completed the acquisition of substantially all the subsidiaries of Waldorf Energy Partners Ltd and Waldorf Production Ltd (Waldorf) in the UK for $163 million 1 .
