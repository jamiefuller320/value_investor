BETA Close X Premier Foods plc announced on June 1, 2026, that its executive directors and members of the Executive Leadership Team received nil-cost options for deferred bonus awards and long-term incentive plan awards on May 29, 2026. Specifically, the Chief Executive Officer, Alex Whitehouse, was granted 112,249 shares under the Deferred Bonus Plan and 627,308 shares under the Long Term Incentive Plan, while the Chief Financial Officer, Duncan Leggett, received 70,861 shares under the Deferred Bonus Plan and 314,919 shares under the Long Term Incentive Plan. Other executive team members also received substantial LTIP awards, with vesting contingent on continued employment and performance conditions related to total shareholder return and adjusted earnings per share. On 29 May 2026 (the "Grant Date"), executive directors of the Company were granted deferred bonus awards, over shares of 10p each in the Company, under the Premier Foods Deferred Bonus Plan (the "DBP") as part of their annual bonus award for the financial year ended 28 March 2026. Name Title Number of shares subject to option Alex Whitehouse Chief Executive Officer 112,249 Duncan Leggett Chief Financial Officer 70,861 Each deferred bonus award is structured as a nil cost option to acquire shares for nil consideration. They will normally vest and become exercisable under the DBP on the third anniversary of the Grant Date and remain exercisable until the tenth anniversary of the Grant Date. No consideration was paid for the grant of the deferred bonus awards and no consideration shall be payable when a deferred bonus award is exercised. 2026 Long Term Incentive Plan Award On 29 May 2026 (the "Grant Date"), the executive directors and members of the Executive Leadership Team of the Company (each a PDMR) were granted awards over ordinary shares of 10p each in the Company, under the Premier Foods Long Term Incentive Plan (the "LTIP"), as set out below. Name Title Number of shares subject to option Alex Whitehouse Chief Executive Officer 627,308 Duncan Leggett Chief Financial Officer 314,919 Jo Cullen Chief Information Officer 124,449 Catherine Thompson (née Lloyd) Chief Marketing Officer 137,524 Ruth McGowan Chief Strategy and Transformation Officer 122,789 Steve Morton Chief Supply Chain Officer 122,789 Matt Roberts Chief Customer Officer 137,524 Simon Rose General Counsel & Company Secretary 136,117 David Wilkinson HR and Communications Director 142,514 Each award is structured as a nil cost option to acquire shares for nil consideration. They will normally vest and become exercisable under the LTIP on the third anniversary of the Grant Date and remain exercisable until the day before the tenth anniversary of the Grant Date, subject to the participant continuing to hold an office or employment within the Group and the achievement of two independent performance conditions. The first condition comprises of a relative total shareholder return based condition that will apply to 50% of the total number of shares held under an award and the second is an adjusted earnings per share based target that will apply to 50% of the total number of shares held under an award. No consideration was paid for the grant of the awards and no consideration shall be payable when an award is exercised. Details of the 2026 LTIP award are set out in the Company's FY25/26 Annual Report, which will be published on 2 June 2026 and available to view on the Group's website (www.premierfoods.co.uk).
