BETA Close X Premier Foods plc announced that on December 15, 2025, persons discharging managerial responsibilities (PDMRs) were granted options under the 2025 Sharesave Plan to purchase ordinary shares at an option price of 140.2p per share. Each of the eight named PDMRs, including the CEO and CFO, received options for 2,850 shares, exercisable between February 1, 2029, and July 31, 2029. This notification is in accordance with Market Abuse Regulation requirements. Disclaimer* Premier Foods plc 16 December 2025 Premier Foods plc (the "Company" ) 16 December 2025 Notification of transactions by persons discharging managerial responsibilities ("PDMRs") and persons closely associated ("PCAs"). In common with all eligible employees, PDMRs were invited to participate in the Premier Foods plc Sharesave Plan 2025 (the "Plan"). The following options over ordinary shares of 10p each in the Company ("Shares"), were granted by the Company on 15 December 2025, at an option price of 140.2p per Share. As with all other eligible employees, when the Plan matures in February 2029, PDMRs will have the option to purchase the Shares from their accumulated monthly savings amounts, under the Plan.
