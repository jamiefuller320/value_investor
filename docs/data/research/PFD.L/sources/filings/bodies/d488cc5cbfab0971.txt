Director/PDMR Shareholding Premier Foods plc 02 June 2025 Premier Foods plc (the " Company ") Notification of transactions by persons discharging managerial responsibilities ("PDMRs") and persons closely associated ("PCAs") The Company announces that the following transactions, in ordinary shares of 10p each in the Company ("Shares"), were carried out on 30 May 2025. Alex Whitehouse, Chief Executive Office, sold 9,595 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 9,488 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share. He then transferred 9,595 Shares to Alison Hunt, PCA to Alex Whitehouse , for nil consideration. Alison Hunt, PCA to Alex Whitehouse, sold 9,595 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 9,487 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share. Duncan Leggett, Chief Financial Officer, sold 6,103 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 6,613 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share. Richard Martin, Chief Customer Officer, sold 6,103 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 6,051 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share. He then transferred 9,078 Shares to Joanne Martin, PCA to Richard Martin , for nil consideration. Joanne Martin, PCA to Richard Martin, sold 9,593 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 9,487 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share. Simon Rose, General Counsel & Company Secretary, sold 7,475 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 7,475 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share. He then transferred 262,427 Shares to Philippa Rose, PCA to Simon Rose , for nil consideration. Philippa Rose, PCA to Simon Rose, sold 11,104 Shares, at a price of 207.5p per Share, and purchased 11,104 Shares, at a price of 207.75p per Share. Paul Thompson, Chief Supply Chain Officer, sold 6,103 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 5,822 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share. David Wilkinson, HR & Communications Director, sold 6,103 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 6,348 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share. He then transferred 9,594 Shares to Jane Wilkinson, PCA to David Wilkinson , for nil consideration. Jane Wilkinson, PCA to David Wilkinson, sold 9,594 Shares, at a price of 209p per Share, and purchased 9,486 Shares, at a price of 209.25p per Share.
