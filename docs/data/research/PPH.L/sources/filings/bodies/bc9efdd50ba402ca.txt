The executive leadership team also proactively seeks out investor input through investor roadshows conducted by the Chief Financial Officer & Executive Director and the Co-Chief Executive Officer. These are exercises in gathering feedback from investors that are relayed to the Board and can input into Board discussions. Having conducted engagement specifically on governance, the Board understands that the votes cast against Resolution 12 by the independent shareholders were motivated by factors relating to remuneration targets, and their ability to review executive performance against targets agreed at the beginning of the financial year. It was felt by independent shareholders that the most appropriate way to address this point was to direct it towards Mr Keen, in his role as Chairman of the Remuneration Committee. In order to ensure independent shareholder interests are fully represented at Board level as set out in the Corporate Governance Code, the Company is seeking to expand the Board and is currently undergoing a recruitment process to appoint a new independent Non-Executive Director. In respect of this appointment, the Company specifically required a duly engaged independent recruitment agency to have due regard to diversity targets in presenting candidates for interview. The Company will shortly provide further updates on the outcome of this process, which is well underway.
