BETA Close X Ninety One plc has announced the award of forfeitable shares to two executive directors as part of its Executive Incentive Plan for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. Hendrik du Toit received an award of 289,551 forfeitable shares for nil consideration, and Kim McFarland was awarded 231,728 forfeitable shares for nil consideration. These awards vest in full after three years and are subject to post-vesting retention periods of 12 and 24 months for 50% of each award respectively. The transactions took place in London on June 24, 2026.
