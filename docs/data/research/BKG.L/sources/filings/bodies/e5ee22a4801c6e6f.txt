BETA Close X The Berkeley Group Holdings plc announced the results of its Annual General Meeting held on 11 September 2026, with all resolutions passing with significant shareholder support. Notably, the accounts for the year ended 30 April 2026 received 99.95% of votes in favour, and the Annual Report on Remuneration was approved by 98.59%. Directors were re-elected with strong majorities, including R Perrins with 95.35% of votes for. The re-appointment of KPMG LLP as auditor passed with 96.64% of votes in favour.
