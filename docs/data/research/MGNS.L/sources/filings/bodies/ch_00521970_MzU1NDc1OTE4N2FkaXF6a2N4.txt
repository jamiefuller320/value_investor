Material accounting policy information

Basis of preparation

Morgan Sindall Group plc (the ‘Company’) is a public limited company, domiciled and incorporated in the United
Kingdom. Its registration number is 00521970, and its registered address is Kent House, 14-17 Market Place,
London, W1W BAI.

These interim financial statements as at and for the financial period ended 28 October 2026 comprise the financial
results of Morgan Sindall Group Pic (the “Company’) as an individual company, excluding the financial results of its
subsidiaries. These interim financial statements are unaudited and have been prepared for the purpose of Sections
836 and 838 of the Companies Act 2006. They are abridged but are otherwise prepared on a consistent basis with
the annual report and accounts for the year ended December 2025.

The interim financial statements include an income statement, financial position as at 28 October 2026 and a
statement of changes in equity for the period started 1 January 2026 and ended 28 October 2026. These interim
financial statements and accompanying notes do not constitute Statutory accounts as defined by section 434 of
the Companies Act 2006. They do not include all the information required for full annual financial statements and
should be read in conjunction with the financial statements published as at and for the financial year ended 31
December 2025. There have been no material changes to the Company's accounting policies to those that were
published in the Company's financial statements for the financial year ended 31 December 2025.

A copy of the Statutory accounts for the financlal year ended 31 December 2025 was published in Morgan Sindall
Group Pic's Group Annual Report and Accounts and delivered to the Registrar of Companies in England and Wales.
The auditors reported on those accounts; their report was unqualified, did not draw attention to any matters by
way of emphasis and did not contain a statement under section 498 (2) or (3) of the Companies Act 2006.

Basls of accounting

The Morgan Sindall Employee Benefit Trust (‘the Trust’) is considered an extension of the Company on the basis
that the Trust was specifically created with the sole purpose of fulfilling the share schemes of the Company and
thus the assets and liabilities of the Trust plans are included on the Company balance sheet and shares held by
the Trust in the Company are presented as a deduction from equity.
