BETA Close X Genuit Group plc announced on April 14, 2026, that it granted nil-cost options under its Long Term Incentive Plan to Executive Directors and Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibility (PDMRs) on April 10, 2026. The Chief Executive Officer, Joe Vorih, received options for 430,744 shares, and the Chief Financial Officer, Tim Pullen, received options for 299,248 shares. Other PDMRs, including Edel Conway, Steve Currier, Martin Gisbourne, Lee Mellor, and Emma Versluys, were granted options totaling 55,865, 75,151, 57,791, 61,532, and 46,757 shares respectively. These awards are subject to performance conditions assessed over the period January 1, 2026, to December 31, 2028, and include a five-year holding period post-vesting.
