Lion Finance Group PLC publishes its Annual Report 2025 & Sustainability Report 2025  
 
Lion Finance Group PLC (the  "Company") has published its Annual Report and Accounts for the financial year 
ended 31 December 2025 (the "Annual Report 2025"). 
 
The Annual Report 202 5 is available on the Company's website at  https://lionfinancegroup.uk/annual-reports/latest-
annual-report/.  In accordance with UK Listing Rules 6.4.1 R and 6.4.3R, it will be submitted to the National Storage 
Mechanism and will be available for inspection shortly at https://data.fca.org.uk/#/nsm/nationalstoragemechanism.  
 
In accordance with Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rule 6.3.5R(1A), the regulated information required to be 
communicated in unedited full text is included in the Company's Annual Report which will shortly be available for 
inspection on the National Storage Mechanism. 
 
The Company’s Annual General Meeting (AGM) is scheduled for 22 May 2026 . The Notice of the AGM will be 
dispatched to shareholders on or around 15 April 2026. 
 
For our stakeholders seeking further insight into our ESG strategy and progress, our comprehensive 2025 
Sustainability Report is also available as a separate publication on the Company’s website.
