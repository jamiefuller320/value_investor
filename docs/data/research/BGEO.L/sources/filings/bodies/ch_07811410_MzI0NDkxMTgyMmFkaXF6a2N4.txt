Strategic Report

For the period ended 31 December 2018

The Directors present their strategic report for the period 31 December 2018.
Review of the Business

BGEO Group Limited, formerly known as BGEO Group PLC, (the “Company”) was incorporated under laws of
England and Wales. The Company is domiciled in the United Kingdom.

On July 3, 2017 the Company, announced its intention to separate its investment business from its banking
business (the “Demerger”). On 29 May 2018 the Demerger became effective, as a result of which the Company was
delisted from the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market effective 21 May 2018 and re-registered as a private
company. Bank of Georgia Group PLC became 100% shareholder of the Company. The principal activity of the
Company is to act as an intermediate holding company.

As a result of thé Demerger, the Company contributed the entire issued share capital of JSC Georgia Capital, the
investment business, in exchange for an interest-bearing loan with a fair value at the moment of contribution of
GEL 1,441,552 thousands to its shareholder, Bank of Georgia Group PLC.

Principal risks and uncertainties

exposure will fluctuate because of changes in foreign exchange rates.
