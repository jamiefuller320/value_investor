The Directors present their strategic report for the period 31 December 2023.
Review of the Business.

BGEO Group Limited, formerly known as BGEO Group PLC, (the “Company”) was incorporated under laws of
England and Wales. The Company is domiciled in the United Kingdom.

‘The principal activity of the Company is to act as an intermediate holding company.

Key Performance Indicators

Companies of the Bank of Georgia Group comprise banking and associated businesses, with the core entity being
JSC Bank of Georgia, a digital banking leader in Georgia. The Company’s key performance indicators, that give an
understanding of the development, performance and position of the business, are aligned with those of the
Group. The development, performance and position of the various business are discussed on pages 1 to 183 of Bank
of Georgia Group PLCs Annual Report and Accounts 2023 (the “Group Report”) and the key performance indicators
through which the Group's performance is measured are set out on pages 21-24 of the Group Report.
