Target and scale with
category leaders

We are deliberate about the customers we pursue.
Volex focuses on category leaders: businesses that

hold a number one, two or three position in their
respective markets, invest at the leading edge of their
industries and drive long term structural demand, By
aligning to their design cycles, embedding within their
manufacturing processes and becoming integral to how
they build their products, Volex creates durable, deeply
embedded relationships that are difficult for competiters
toreplicate.

This approach is exemplified by building up our EV
& Electrification business from a standing start to

more than $180 million in revenue in under a decade.

By committing early to single, highly demanding
category leader, Volex established the credentials that
subsequently opened doors with the majority of EV
manufacturers in North America and Europe. That same
Ccustomer-first logic underpins our positions in Off
Highway, Medical, Complex Industrial Technology and,
Consumer Electricals,

Targeting category leaders provides Volex with
exceptional revenue visibility, on major complex
programmes that can run for many years, while the
structural growth of our customers’ markets underpins
consistent organic outperformance. As complexity
within those markets increases, the criticality of our role
compounds accordingly.

Move up the complexity curve
in every market

Volex thrives where nanufacturing demands are most.
exacting. Across every end-market, our strategy is to
pursue increasingly complex applications; solutions that
require deep engineering expertise, stringent quality
standards and close collaboration with customers’
development teams. Complexity creates barriers to
substitution, supports premium pricing and underpins
our margin progression.

Our Complex industrial Technology business illustrates
this well. Having established a position supplying
high-speed copper data interconnect cables for data
centres, we developed products across each successive
generation, from 200 Gigabit-per-second through to 800
Gigabit-per-second, with capabilities now at 16 Terabit-
pper-second; each step represents a more technically
demanding product and higher-value contribution,

In Consumer Electricals, relationships that began with
power cords are increasingly encompassing internal
appliance harnesses worth five to ten times more per
Unit, reflecting growing customer confidence in our
ability to handle greater integration complexity.

Moving up the complexity curve also generates
transferable capability across the Group. Expertise
builtin one marker, such as the traceability and testing
disciplines developed in Medical manufacturing, can be
applied to adjacent sectors, improving the quality and
economics of new programmes and accelerating our
path to higher margins.

market positions that strengthen the organic growth
flywheel, rather than simply adding revenue. Every
transaction is capability-led, targeting businesses in
complex manufacturing segments where Volex already
has depth and where the acquired capability can be
deployed across the Group to generate incremental
value with existing customers

Our Off-Highway business illustrates this model.
Following entry into the European market through an
acquisition in Turkiye, we built strong positions with
construction and agricultural equipment manufacturers.
We are now replicating that capability in North America,
where initial customer wins have been secured, while
also developing complementary low-cost manufacturing
in Indonesia. The operational platform, customer
relationships and process standards established in
Europe provide the blueprint for each subsequent
geography.

contracts where the cable is the most technically critical
component. Acquisitions in Turkiye and Mexico have
similarly provided harnessing capabilities now being
offered to the Group's established category-leading
customers across multiple geographies.

Move to the Main Market
The Board has concluded that the time is
right for Volex to move from AIM to the Main
Market of the London Stock Exchange. The
Group has the scale and the governance
that the Main Market requires. Admission is,
‘expected to broaden the investor base and
improve access to index inclusion, The work
to prepare for admission is well advanced
and proceeding according to plan.

In support of the transition, the Company
launched a share buyback programme

in April 2026. The buyback is designed to
provide an orderly route for shareholders
‘who are unable to continue to hold the stock
under their existing mandates as the listing
cchanges, and to support liquidity through
the transition, The programme is being
conducted within the framework approved
by shareholders at the Annual General
Meeting.
