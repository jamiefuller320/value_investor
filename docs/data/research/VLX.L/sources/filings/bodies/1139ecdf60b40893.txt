LEI: 213800HBLQNH5FXXGE63 24 July 2026 Volex plc (" Volex " or the " Company ") Admission to trading on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange and AIM Cancellation Further to the announcement on 20 July 2026, Volex plc (LSE: VLX), the specialist integrated manufacturer of critical power and data transmission products, is pleased to announce that its ordinary shares (the "Ordinary Shares") will be admitted to the Equity Shares (Commercial Companies) Category of the Official List of the FCA and to trading on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange at 8:00 a.m. today. Simultaneously, the Ordinary Shares will be cancelled from trading on AIM. Volex is not offering any new Ordinary Shares nor any other securities in connection with Admission. Following Admission, the Company's Ordinary Shares will continue to be registered with their existing ISIN of GB0009390070 and the TIDM will remain VLX. On Admission, the Company's issued ordinary share capital will be 184,377,640 Ordinary Shares. There are no Ordinary Shares held in treasury, and therefore the total voting rights in the Company is 184,377,640. This figure may be used by shareholders in the Company as the denominator for the calculations by which they will determine if they are required to notify their interest in, or a change in their interest in, the share capital of the Company under the FCA's Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules. The Company's shareholders should consult their own professional advisers regarding the consequences of Admission on their personal tax position. Peel Hunt LLP is acting as Sponsor exclusively to the Company and no one else in connection with Admission. Nat Rothschild, Volex's Chief Executive Officer, commented: "Our move to the Main Market is a significant milestone for Volex, reflecting the growth in scale and performance we have delivered in recent years. AIM has been a valuable platform for us, supporting Volex through a period of real transformation and sustained growth. This move aligns our listing with the size and ambition of the business today, and we believe it will broaden our appeal to a wider group of UK and international investors as we continue to execute our strategy and deliver on our targets.
