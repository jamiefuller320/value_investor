Disclaimer* Volex PLC 22 October 2026 22 October 2026 Volex plc ("Volex", the "Company", or the "Group") Completion of £20m Share Buyback Programme Volex plc (LSE: VLX), the specialist integrated manufacturer of critical power and data transmission products, announces that, following the purchase of ordinary shares of 25 pence each in the Company (“Ordinary Shares”) on 20 October 2026, its £20 million share buyback programme, announced on 7 April 2026 and amended on 14 July 2026 (the “Programme”), has been completed in accordance with its terms. Under the Programme, the Company repurchased a total of 3,683,164 Ordinary Shares through Peel Hunt LLP at an average price of approximately 543.01 pence per Ordinary Share, for an aggregate consideration of £20 million (excluding dealing and associated costs). All Ordinary Shares repurchased under the Programme have been, or will be, cancelled. Following settlement and cancellation of the final purchases under the Programme, the Company’s issued share capital will comprise 181,892,509 Ordinary Shares, each carrying one voting right. No Ordinary Shares will be held in treasury. The total number of voting rights in the Company will therefore be 181,892,509. This figure may be used by shareholders as the denominator for the calculations by which they can determine if they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, the Company under the Financial Conduct Authority’s Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules.
