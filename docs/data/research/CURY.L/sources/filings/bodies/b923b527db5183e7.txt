BETA Close X Currys PLC announced on 30 July 2026 that Bruce Marsh, Group Chief Financial Officer, was granted a nil cost share award under the Deferred Share Bonus Plan. This award comprises 131,930 ordinary shares, representing 25 percent of his 2025/26 annual performance bonus entitlement, with a reference value of £221,643.
