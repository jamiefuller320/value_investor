Alex Baldock and Bruce Marsh exercised nil cost options over ordinary shares of 0.1 pence in the Company in respect of the 2023 LTIP Award (the 'Award') which was granted on the 28 July 2023 under the Currys plc Long Term Incentive Plan 2016. The Award vested on 28 July 2026, in accordance with the achievement of the applicable performance conditions . Further details of the achievement of the performance conditions are disclosed in the 2025/26 Directors' Remuneration Report, which will be available at www.currysplc.com from 30 July 2026. Alex Baldock exercised 3,700,765 options, made up of 3,642,742 options and 58,023 accrued dividend equivalents, and sold 1,742,877 to cover the tax liability arising on the transaction. The remaining 1,957,888 shares were retained. Bruce Marsh exercised 1,990,018 options, made up of 1,958,817 options and 31,201 accrued dividend equivalents, and sold 937,215 to cover the tax liability arising on the transaction. The remaining 1,052,803 shares were retained. Both exercises have been satisfied using market purchase shares and the retained shares are subject to a 2-year post vest holding requirement.
