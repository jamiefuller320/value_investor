BETA Close X Vistry Group PLC has announced that several individuals discharging managerial responsibilities, including Greg Fitzgerald, Timothy Lawlor, Stephen Teagle, Clare Bates, Michael Stirrop, and Michael Woolliscroft, have had conditional share awards vest on March 20, 2026, under the Deferred Bonus Plan. Following the vesting, a portion of these shares were sold at £3.5694 per share to cover income tax and National Insurance liabilities. Specifically, Greg Fitzgerald sold 34,642 shares, Timothy Lawlor sold 5,292 shares, Stephen Teagle sold 4,679 shares, Clare Bates sold 3,027 shares, Michael Stirrop sold 4,475 shares, and Michael Woolliscroft sold 5,761 shares. The vesting of shares occurred outside a trading venue, while the sales took place on the London Stock Exchange.
