BETA Close X Vistry Group PLC announced on April 14, 2026, that awards were granted under its Long Term Incentive Plan 2020, including performance share awards to key executives such as Adam Daniels (CEO) with 275,897 nil cost options and Timothy Lawlor (CFO) with 209,636 nil cost options.
