BETA Close X The Rank Group Plc has amended a prior announcement regarding the net vested shares received by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibility (PDMRs) under its Long Term Incentive Plan. Following the vesting of conditional share awards on 29 September 2025, shares were sold to cover tax and other liabilities. The amended net vested shares, subject to a two-year holding period, are now reported as 18,671 for John O'Reilly, 9,597 for Richard Harris, 1,416 for Andy Crump, 4,479 for Hazel Boyle, and 2,676 for Enric Monton. These transactions involved the acquisition of shares at nil cost and subsequent sales at 139.7257 pence per share.
