BETA Close X Rank Group Plc announced the vesting of shares from the 2022 Performance LTIP on September 29, 2025, with shares priced at 139.7257p. CEO John O'Reilly received 35,342 conditional shares, cash settled 16,611 for taxes, sold 16,671 and vested with a net of 18,731 shares. CFO Richard Harris received 18,168 conditional shares, cash settled 8,539 for taxes, sold 8,571 and vested with a net of 9,629 shares. Andy Crump received 2,719 conditional shares, cash settled 1,278 for taxes, sold 1,303 and vested with a net of 1,441 shares. Hazel Boyle received 8,499 conditional shares, cash settled 3,995 for taxes, sold 4,020 and vested with a net of 4,504 shares. Enric Monton received 4,912 conditional shares, cash settled 2,210 for taxes, sold 2,236 and vested with a net of 2,702 shares. The 2022 LTIP is the last award with a two-year holding period for all participants.
