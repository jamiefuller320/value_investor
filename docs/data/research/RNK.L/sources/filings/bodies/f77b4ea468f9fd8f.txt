BETA Close X The Rank Group Plc announced that on December 15, 2025, its Chief Executive Officer, John O'Reilly, received an award of 327,597 ordinary shares valued at £353,412, and its Chief Financial Officer, Richard Harris, received an award of 164,488 ordinary shares valued at £177,450, under the 2020 Deferred Bonus Plan.
