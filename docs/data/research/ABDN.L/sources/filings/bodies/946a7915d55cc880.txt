All figures in this announcement are unaudited and subject to revision. interactive investor (ii): Strong growth continues - Continued growth in customer numbers, with total customers of 533k up 8% year-on-year. Strong growth in SIPPs with 131k customers, up 34% year-on-year. - In a seasonally quieter quarter, Q3 net inflows were 32% higher at £2.5bn (Q3 2025: £1.9bn). Adviser: Improved service and strengthened leadership, net flows remain challenging - The return to net inflows is taking longer than anticipated, with net outflows in Q3 of £0.7bn unchanged relative to Q2. Within this, we saw gross inflows into Wrap of £1.4bn and continued net inflows into MPS. - AUMA of £84.5bn up 5% (31 December 2025: £80.4bn) reflecting positive markets. - Good traction in Aberdeen SIPP following its launch in December 2025, with AUM now over £1.4bn. - Completed insourcing of service teams in July, giving us more control of the end-to-end customer experience. - Adviser CEO Richard Denning has strengthened his leadership team to support a more targeted take-to-market strategy, with recent hires including a new Chief Distribution Officer and Chief Client Experience Officer. Investments: Net inflows in fixed income, real assets and alternatives - Institutional and Retail Wealth (I&RW) net inflows excluding liquidity of £1.0bn (Q3 2025: £0.2bn) included net inflows of £0.9bn, £1.1bn and £0.8bn in fixed income, real assets and alternatives respectively. - Encouraging trends in strategic focus areas, with real asset growth including positive inflows relating to our partnership with Border to Coast and rail infrastructure projects, and flows into commodity and precious metal ETFs within alternatives. - Equities net outflows of £1.9bn (Q3 2025: £1.6bn net outflow) remained a headwind in Q3, although we have seen positive momentum and net inflows across our GEM income product range. - Insurance Partners outflows of £2.5bn (Q3 2025: £1.1bn) largely reflect Standard Life's heritage business in run-off. - Total AUM was up at £393.2bn (31 December 2025: £390.4bn) with the year-to-date increase driven by positive markets and the acquisitions of Herald Investment Trust (£0.8bn) and the MFS closed end funds (£1.3bn). Outlook - In October we reduced our stake in Standard Life plc from c.10.3% to c.5.2%, generating c.£430m. We intend to deploy these proceeds in accordance with our disciplined capital allocation policy, which is focused on maintaining a strong balance sheet, investing in the business to support sustainable profitable growth, reducing and optimising debt over time, and delivering sustainable returns to shareholders. - Consistent with this policy, and as indicated at our HY 2026 results, we have notified relevant bondholders that we will redeem £210m of Additional Tier 1 (AT1) debt at its first call date in December 2026. - We are confident in the delivery of the Group's FY 2026 targets of adjusted operating profit of at least £300m and net capital generation of c.£300m. 1.
