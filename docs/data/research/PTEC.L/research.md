# Playtech plc (PTEC.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-09-02T08:33:53.609140+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Playtech plc has repositioned as a predominantly B2B gambling-technology supplier following the €2.3bn Snaitech disposal and ~€1.8bn special dividend, with FY25 continuing revenue of €763.6m and adjusted EBITDA of €197.0m. The quantitative screen flags a buy on cheapness (P/B ~0.97), balance-sheet quality (net cash €28.5m; D/E ~24%) and FCF-yield models, though reported continuing operations remain loss-making and filing FCF (€29.5m) sits well below the company’s €70–100m medium-term target. Trading updates through July 2026 guide FY26 adjusted EBITDA to at least €270m, driven by US/LatAm momentum and Caliente/Hard Rock Digital economics. The central debate is whether equity-accounted investment income and Americas growth can sustain earnings and cash conversion against UK tax increases, LatAm regulatory churn, and unresolved Evolution litigation tied to the 2021 Black Cube report.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The value case rests on a post-Snaitech “pure-play” B2B platform trading near book after a sharp de-rating in October 2025, with operational inflection now visible in trading updates rather than headline FY25 revenue decline.

**Quantitative screen alignment.** Six of 22 models pass (Schloss Low P/B, Deep Value, FCF Yield, Economic Moat, Composite Value, Financial Health), spanning cheapness, quality and risk families, with composite score 0.70 versus sector-relative 0.53. P/B 0.97 and D/E 24% fit classic deep-value and financial-health criteria. The screen’s adjusted signal is Hold owing to FCF basis divergence (filing −€52.9m vs company-adjusted €70m vs screen TTM €511m), which warrants manual verification but does not invalidate the asset-backed entry point.

**Business quality.** Playtech supplies platform (PAM+), content and Live casino infrastructure to regulated operators globally. FY25 US revenue rose ~100%; SaaS revenue grew 48% to €118.1m. The Caliente Interactive restructure (30.8% equity stake; €54.5m share of associate income in FY25) and Hard Rock Digital partnership embed high-margin, recurring economics alongside B2B licence fees. Management targets adjusted EBITDA of €250–300m and FCF of €70–100m medium term; July 2026 guidance of ≥€270m FY26 adjusted EBITDA implies meaningful progress.

**Capital allocation.** Net cash of €28.5m (from net debt €142.8m), €225m undrawn RCF, €300m 2028 bond outstanding, and ~8.3% share repurchase in H2 2025 signal balance-sheet discipline. Special dividend return crystallised Snaitech value; ongoing buybacks fund employee schemes rather than shrink float.

**Why buy for a value investor.** Asset cover (P/B <1), de-levered balance sheet, and accelerating Americas earnings offer a rerating path if FCF normalises toward management’s €70–100m band and litigation/regulatory noise subsides. The screen’s cheapness signal is supported by filing evidence; quality is mixed on reported GAAP metrics but stronger on adjusted, segment-level performance.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Regulatory.** UK Remote Gaming Duty rises to 40% (April 2026) and General Betting Duty to 25% on remote sports (April 2027); Sun Bingo prepayment fully impaired on this basis. Colombia faces VAT/tax uncertainty (16% emergency consumption tax on player GGR from March 2026). Brazil GGR tax phased from 12% to 15% by 2028. FY25 UK revenue fell 6%; management flags sector headwinds persisting into 2026.

**Litigation and governance.** Evolution AB identified Playtech Software Limited as commissioning party behind a 2021 Black Cube report (21 October 2025); Evolution sought to add Playtech to New Jersey proceedings (April 2026). Playtech disputes allegations and stands by the report; as at FY25 approval, no claim served on Playtech plc or subsidiaries. Reputational and legal tail risk remains unresolved.

**Customer concentration and contract risk.** Caliente restructure shifts economics from fee revenue to equity income; Hard Rock Digital became a largest customer with H2 2026 revenue expected at a “lower but more sustainable level” after PMR product peak. DraftKings, FanDuel and other US operators are growth drivers but create partner dependency.

**Accounting and earnings quality.** Large gap between adjusted (€197.0m EBITDA) and reported (−€5.7m EBITDA) continuing results; €87.6m Playtech incentive arrangements; fair-value losses on derivatives €26.9m. Effective adjusted tax rate 37.9% vs UK 25% headline. Screen ROE −10.6% reflects reported continuing losses, not adjusted earnings.

**Competitive.** Evolution is the dominant Live casino competitor; public dispute underscores intense rivalry. SaaS and platform markets remain fragmented but scale advantages matter.

**Liquidity and leverage.** Net cash position is modest (€28.5m) relative to €298.6m bond and ongoing Live studio investment (capex €41.9m + capitalised development €44.5m in FY25). RCF provides headroom; 2028 bond maturity is manageable but not trivial.

**Other.** HAPPYBET wind-down (2026); IGS held for sale; FX sensitivity (€12.9m FX loss in FY25 finance costs). No pension red flags identified in available filing extracts.
