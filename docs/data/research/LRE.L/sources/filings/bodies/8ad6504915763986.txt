Disclaimer* LANCASHIRE HOLDINGS LIMITED 4 November 2026 Hamilton, Bermuda Lancashire Holdings Limited (“Lancashire” or “the Group”) today announces its trading statement for the nine months ended 30 September 2026. Trading statement highlights • On track to deliver a high-teens RoE in 2026, and the Group is well positioned for 2027 • Strong underwriting and financial performance in the year to date • Active capital management: special dividend of 75 cents per share ($182 million) reflects strong operational performance and a robust capital position • Focus on managing the cycle. A diversified portfolio and balance sheet strength provide flexibility Alex Maloney, Group Chief Executive Officer, commented: “Lancashire delivered another strong underwriting performance in the first nine months of the year. While pricing has moderated in some parts of the market, we benefit from a diversified portfolio and rates continued to remain adequate across much of our business. Gross premiums written were broadly stable year-on-year at $1,806 million. The loss environment in the third quarter was also relatively benign, particularly for natural catastrophes, and our exposure to the conflict in the Middle East remains manageable and not material to the Group. Our conservatively-positioned investment portfolio continues to deliver an attractive level of investment income, although this was offset by the mark-to-market impact of higher government bond yields in the third quarter. Given the relatively short duration of the portfolio, the prevailing environment of higher yields should benefit investment income in future periods. The Group’s healthy performance for the year to date means that we remain on track to meet our 2026 full year guidance of a high-teens RoE. The positive operational performance of the Group, and our strong capital position, means we are able to declare a special dividend of 75 cents per common share, resulting in an aggregate distribution of approximately $182 million. Managing the cycle is central to how Lancashire has always operated and our increased diversification across products and geographies gives us additional flexibility. As pricing conditions vary across markets, the breadth of our portfolio enables us to remain disciplined and selective, directing appetite and capital to areas where returns remain most attractive. Where there are opportunities to build momentum, we will take them and we have continued to add talent to our bench strength and invest in new products that are complementary to our existing portfolio. We head into 2027 from a position of strength. NOTE REGARDING RPI METHODOLOGY: THE RENEWAL PRICE INDEX (“RPI”) IS AN INTERNAL METHODOLOGY THAT MANAGEMENT USES TO TRACK TRENDS IN PREMIUM RATES OF A PORTFOLIO OF INSURANCE AND REINSURANCE CONTRACTS. THE RPI WRITTEN IN THE RESPECTIVE SEGMENTS IS CALCULATED ON A PER CONTRACT BASIS AND REFLECTS MANAGEMENT’S ASSESSMENT OF RELATIVE CHANGES IN PRICE, TERMS, CONDITIONS AND LIMITS AND IS WEIGHTED BY PREMIUM VOLUME. THE RPI DOES NOT INCLUDE NEW BUSINESS, TO OFFER A CONSISTENT BASIS FOR ANALYSIS. THE CALCULATION INVOLVES A DEGREE OF JUDGEMENT IN RELATION TO COMPARABILITY OF CONTRACTS AND THE ASSESSMENT NOTED ABOVE. TO ENHANCE THE RPI METHODOLOGY, MANAGEMENT MAY REVISE THE METHODOLOGY AND ASSUMPTIONS UNDERLYING THE RPI, SO THE TRENDS IN PREMIUM RATES REFLECTED IN THE RPI MAY NOT BE COMPARABLE OVER TIME. CONSIDERATION IS ONLY GIVEN TO RENEWALS OF A COMPARABLE NATURE SO IT DOES NOT REFLECT EVERY CONTRACT IN THE PORTFOLIO OF CONTRACTS. THE FUTURE PROFITABILITY OF THE PORTFOLIO OF CONTRACTS WITHIN THE RPI IS DEPENDENT UPON MANY FACTORS BESIDES THE TRENDS IN PREMIUM RATES.
