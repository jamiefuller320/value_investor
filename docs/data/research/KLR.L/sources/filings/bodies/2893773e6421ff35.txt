BETA Close X Keller Group plc announced that on March 23, 2026, Executive Directors and Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities (PDMRs) acquired shares following the vesting of Long Term Incentive Plan awards. David Burke acquired a net of 6,757 shares from a deferred bonus award and 42,769 shares from a performance award, while Peter Wyton acquired 42,769 shares from a performance award, and Deepak Raj acquired 8,113 shares from a conditional award.
