Keller has continued to trade well through the period, and the Group remains on track to deliver significant year-on-year revenue and profit growth for the full year, in line with market expectations 1 that were materially upgraded following the strong performance in H1 2026. Key highlights Operational performance : The Group expects to deliver record volumes in 2026, driven by strong demand and excellent operational execution. Performance continues to be supported by infrastructure and commercial projects in North America, mining-related activity in Austral, our Australian civils business, and strong demand across the Nordics. These strengths have more than offset continued weakness in the US residential market, deferred environmental remediation work at Moretrench and weakness in the UK business. Order book: The order book remains at a record level of approximately £2.5 billion. It is well diversified by market and geography and is underpinned by the multi-year I-40 project, providing increased revenue visibility over the next three to four years. Cash generation and balance sheet: The Group expects continued strong free cash flow generation in 2026, reflecting the typical weighting towards the second half. Year-end net debt/cash is expected to be broadly neutral, subject to normal working capital movements, with leverage remaining well below the Group’s target range of 0.5x to 1.5x net debt to EBITDA. Capital allocation: The £100m share buyback programme is well progressed, with approximately £80 million of shares repurchased as at 31 October 2026. The Group remains committed to its disciplined capital allocation framework, prioritising investment in the business and dividends with significant optionality to allocate surplus capital to value accretive bolt-on acquisitions and additional shareholder returns. Keller continues to evaluate selective acquisition opportunities to accelerate growth in line with the Group’s strategy. Medium-term growth ambitions: At its recent Capital Markets Day, Keller provided a comprehensive update on the business and its sustainable growth strategy, together with new medium-term financial targets including: Organic revenue CAGR: 5–7% Group operating profit margin: c.7% ROCE: >30% Total free cash flow 2 (2026 to 2030): c.£800m Progressive dividend policy with cover of 2.5x to 3.5x Commenting on the trading update, James Wroath, Chief Executive Officer, said: “2026 is on track to be another record year for Keller - the fourth in a row - reflecting sustained excellent operational performance, the strength of our diversified portfolio and supportive end markets. “With a c.£2.5bn order book, healthy free cash flow generation and a strong balance sheet, we are set to enter 2027 with good visibility and momentum.
