BETA Close X BP PLC has released its first quarter 2026 trading statement, anticipating a broadly flat upstream production compared to the prior quarter, with gas and low carbon energy slightly higher and oil production and operations slightly lower. The company expects an impact of $0.1 to $0.2 billion on oil production and operations realizations due to price lags, while customers and products segment results will be influenced by seasonally lower volumes and margins, offset by stronger midstream performance and exceptional oil trading results. Capital expenditure is projected to be around $3.5 billion, similar to the previous quarter, but net debt is expected to increase to between $25 to $27 billion, primarily driven by a significant working capital build of $4 to $7 billion due to market volatility.
