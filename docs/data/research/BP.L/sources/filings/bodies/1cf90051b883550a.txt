BETA Close X BP PLC has released a second quarter 2026 trading statement indicating expected upstream production of 2,170 to 2,220 million barrels of oil equivalent per day, down from 2,339 million barrels of oil equivalent per day in the first quarter, due to seasonal maintenance and Middle East disruptions. The company anticipates positive impacts on realizations for both the Gas & Low Carbon Energy segment ($0.5 to $0.7 billion) and the Oil Production & Operations segment ($1.8 to $2.1 billion), though exploration write-offs are projected at approximately $(0.5) billion. The Customers & Products segment is expected to benefit from seasonally higher volumes and improved refining margins of $1.2 to $1.4 billion, despite lower refining throughput of 1,445 to 1,475 thousand barrels per day. Net debt is forecast to decrease to $22 to $23 billion from $25.3 billion, partly due to a $2.9 billion redemption of hybrid bonds.
