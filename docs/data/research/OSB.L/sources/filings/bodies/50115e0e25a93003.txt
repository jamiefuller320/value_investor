Tender offer results: projected incremental effect on certain financial metrics through to 2029 OSB GROUP PLC (the Group) has today announced the results of the tender offer for its MREL-eligible £300m Senior Holdco 2028 9.5% (the 2028 securities) and £400m Senior Holdco 2030 8.875% securities (the 2030 securities and together, the Senior Holdco securities). From 1 January 2026, the Bank of England’s preferred resolution strategy for the Group changed to Transfer. As a result, the Group’s MREL requirement is equal to its minimum capital requirements, defined as the sum of Pillar 1 and Pillar 2A, and these Senior Holdco securities are no longer required to meet its current MREL requirement. The tender offer is expected to settle on 16 November 2026. The Group received valid tenders totalling £634,434,000 in principal amount, exceeding the originally announced £500m cap. The Group has exercised its discretion to purchase all such validly tendered Senior Holdco securities without scaling and as such, will purchase £279,585,000 of the 2028 securities at 103.369% of principal amount of such 2028 securities, and £354,849,000 of the 2030 securities at 106.083% of principal amount of such 2030 securities. Following settlement, £20,415,000 of the 2028 securities and £45,151,000 of the 2030 securities will remain outstanding. Impact on 2026 The tender offer, once settled, is expected to reduce the Group’s profit before tax for 2026 by c.£42m through a one-off charge arising from the partial derecognition of the repurchased Senior Holdco securities. This one-off charge will comprise a c.£33m charge upon repurchase of the Senior Holdco securities, including transaction costs, and a further charge relating to hedge accounting adjustments which as at 31 October 2026 amounted to c.£9m. On a pro-forma basis, including accounting adjustments as at 31 October 2026 and the effect of taxation, it is estimated that the day-one impact of the tender offer would have reduced the Group’s first half 2026 CET1 ratio 1 and RoTE ratio 1 by c.20bps and c.1.5%, respectively. The 2026 one-off charge from partial derecognition of the repurchased Senior Holdco securities will not form part of net interest income (NII) and therefore will not impact net interest margin (NIM). Following completion and not including the one-off charge referred to above, the tender offer is expected to deliver a total income benefit through the reduction in coupon payments, net of hedging, on the Senior Holdco securities. Assuming the repurchased securities are replaced with central bank funding at a cost of SONIA c.+20bps, total income in 2026 is expected to increase by c.£4m and NIM 1 by c.1bp. Expected benefit in future years On the same replacement funding assumption, the transaction is expected to provide a total income benefit of c.£28m in 2027. This assumes, for illustrative purposes, that the 2028 securities are redeemed at their first call date in September 2027 2 . The benefit from reduced coupon payments, net of hedging, will continue during 2028 and into 2029, assuming, for illustrative purposes only, that the 2030 securities are redeemed at their first call date in January 2029 4 , supporting earnings and capital generation over that period.
