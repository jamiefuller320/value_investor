Notes to the Interim Financial Statements
For the period from 1 January 2020 to 18 November 2020

1. Basis of preparation

The unaudited interim financial statements of OSB GROUP PLC (‘the Company’) have been prepared in
accordance with section 838 of the companies Act 2006 for the purpose of determining the distributable
reserves of the Company in connection with redeeming the redeemable preference shares. The interim
financial statements do not constitute the statutory financial statements of the Company with the meaning
of section 434 of the Companies Act 2006.

These interim financial statements are abridged and unaudited. These financial statements have been
prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (‘IFRS’) except for certain
exemptions for interim accounts taken as permitted under section 838 of the Companies Act 2006 for
matters that are not material for determining whether a distribution world contravene Part 23 of the
Companies Act 2006.

These interim financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.

2. Accounting policies

The principal accounting policies applied in the preparation of these unaudited interim financial statements
are set out below:

Revenue
Revenue is recognised on an accruals basis as services are provided or on the performance of a significant
act.

Taxation
Taxation is recognised in profit or loss consistently with the recognition of items it relates to as is the
expected tax charge or credit on the taxable income or loss in the period.
