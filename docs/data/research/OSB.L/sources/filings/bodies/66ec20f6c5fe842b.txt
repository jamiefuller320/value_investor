The Offers were announced on 5 November 2026 and were made on the terms and subject to the conditions contained in the tender offer memorandum dated 5 November 2026 (the Tender Offer Memorandum ) prepared by the Offeror in connection with the Offers. Capitalised terms used but not otherwise defined in this announcement shall have the meanings given to them in the Tender Offer Memorandum. Expiration Deadline The Expiration Deadline for the Offers was 4.00 p.m. (London time) on 12 November 2026.
