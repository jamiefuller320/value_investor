OSB GROUP PLC (the Offeror ) announces today the final results of its separate invitations to holders of its outstanding: (i) £300,000,000 Fixed Rate Resetting Notes due 7 September 2028 (ISIN: XS2642668821) (the 2028 Notes ); and (ii) £400,000,000 Fixed Rate Resetting Notes due 16 January 2030 (ISIN: XS2747270986) (the 2030 Notes and, together with the 2028 Notes, the Notes and each a Series ) to tender their Notes for purchase by the Offeror for cash (each such invitation, an Offer , and together, the Offers ). The Offers were announced on 5 November 2026 and were made on the terms and subject to the conditions contained in the tender offer memorandum dated 5 November 2026 (the Tender Offer Memorandum ) prepared by the Offeror in connection with the Offers. Capitalised terms used but not otherwise defined in this announcement shall have the meanings given to them in the Tender Offer Memorandum. Expiration Deadline The Expiration Deadline for the Offers was 4.00 p.m. (London time) on 12 November 2026. As at the Expiration Deadline, the Offeror had received valid tenders for purchase pursuant to the Offers of (i) £279,585,000 in aggregate principal amount of the 2028 Notes; and (ii) £354,849,000 in aggregate principal amount of the 2030 Notes. Final Acceptance Amount The Offeror confirms that the Final Acceptance Amount is £634,434,000, to be allocated between the 2028 Notes and the 2030 Notes as set out below. Results of the Offers The Offeror will accept, (i) the 2028 Notes validly tendered pursuant to the relevant Offer in full, with no pro-rata scaling; and (ii) the 2030 Notes validly tendered pursuant to the relevant Offer in full, with no pro-rata scaling. Pricing for the Offers took place at around 11.00 a.m. (London time) (the Pricing Time ) today. The Offeror determined that the Purchase Price it will pay for the Notes validly tendered and accepted for purchase pursuant to each Offer will be: (i) in respect of the 2028 Notes, 103.369 per cent. of the principal amount of such 2028 Notes; and (ii) in respect of the 2030 Notes, 106.083 per cent. of the principal amount of such 2030 Notes, in each case based on a Settlement Date of 16 November 2026, and together with an Accrued Interest Payment.
