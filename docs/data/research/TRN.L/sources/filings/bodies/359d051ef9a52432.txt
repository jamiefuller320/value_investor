BETA Close X Trainline plc announced that all resolutions proposed at its Annual General Meeting on June 18, 2026, were passed by shareholders. Key resolutions included the approval of audited accounts for the year ended February 28, 2026, with 98.41% of votes in favour, and the Directors' Remuneration Report, which received 95.35% approval. Amendments to discretionary share plan rules were approved by 99.05% of votes, and all directors proposed for re-election received substantial support, with resolutions to re-elect directors receiving at least 96.46% of votes in favour.
