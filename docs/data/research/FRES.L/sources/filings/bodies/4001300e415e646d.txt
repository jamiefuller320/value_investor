Disclaimer* Fresnillo PLC 21 October 2026 Fresnillo plc 21 Upper Brook Street London W1K 7PY United Kingdom www.fresnilloplc.com 21 October 2026 THIRD QUARTER PRODUCTION REPORT FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED 30 SEPTEMBER 2026 Octavio Alvídrez, Chief Executive Officer, said: “Production of gold and silver was in line with expectations in the third quarter and we remain on track to meet our full- year guidance. Our core assets continue to deliver reliably, highlighted by strong gold performance at Fresnillo, while lower production volumes overall reflect lower ore grades at Juanicipio, Saucito, and San Julián, along with reduced volume of ore processed at Herradura and Ciénega. Our focus for the final quarter remains on maintaining operational discipline, continuous efficiency improvements, and safety across all our mines. We are well positioned to achieve our full-year targets while advancing our strong long-term growth pipeline." HIGHLIGHTS Silver Quarterly attributable silver production of 10.3 moz, decreased 5.5% vs. 2Q26, mainly due to the lower ore grade at Juanicipio and San Julián Veins, and a decrease in the volume of ore processed and lower ore grade at Ciénega. Quarterly attributable silver production down 11.6% vs. 3Q25, primarily due to the lower ore grade at Juanicipio, Saucito and San Julián Veins, and the end of the contribution from the Silverstream. Year-to-date attributable silver production of 32.4 moz, decreased 11.5% vs. YTD25, mainly due to the end of the contribution from the Silverstream, the lower ore grade at the underground mines, and to a lesser extent, the lower recovery rate at Ciénega, and a lower volume of ore processed at Fresnillo and Ciénega. Gold Quarterly attributable gold production of 135.1 koz, down 12.8% vs. 2Q26, mainly driven by the decreased volume of ore processed, and lower ore grade at Herradura, and, to a lesser extent, the decreased contribution from Noche Buena, lower ore grade at San Julián Veins and a decrease in the volume of ore processed at Ciénega. Quarterly attributable gold production decreased 10.7% vs. 3Q25, primarily due to the decrease in volume of ore processed and lower ore grade at Herradura, the lower contribution from Noche Buena, and the lower ore grade at Saucito, San Julián Veins, and Ciénega. This was mitigated by the higher ore grade and improved recovery rate at Fresnillo. Year-to-date attributable gold production of 425.9 koz, decreased 8.4% vs. YTD25, mainly due to the decrease in volume of ore processed and lower ore grade at Herradura, the lower ore grade at San Julián Veins and Saucito, and the lower contribution from Noche Buena. This was mitigated by the higher ore grades at Fresnillo and Juanicipio. By-Products Quarterly attributable by-product lead and zinc production decreased 5.9% and 10.0% vs. 2Q26, respectively, mainly driven by the lower ore grades at Fresnillo and Juanicipio. Quarterly attributable by-product lead production increased 6.9% vs. 3Q25, due to the higher ore grade at Fresnillo, partly offset by the lower ore grade at Saucito and Juanicipio. ABOUT FRESNILLO PLC Fresnillo plc is the world's largest primary silver producer and Mexico's largest gold producer, listed on the London and Mexican Stock Exchanges under the symbol FRES. Fresnillo plc has eight operating mines, all of them in Mexico - Fresnillo, Saucito, Juanicipio, Ciénega, Herradura, Soledad-Dipolos 1 , Noche Buena and San Julián Veins and five advanced exploration projects - Orisyvo, Rodeo, Guanajuato, Tajitos and Novador as well as a number of other long term exploration prospects. Fresnillo plc has mining concessions and exploration projects in Mexico, Peru, Chile and Canada. Fresnillo plc's goal is to maintain the Group's position as the world's largest primary silver company and Mexico's largest gold producer. 1 Operations at Soledad-Dipolos are currently suspended.
