1. COMPANY INFORMATION AND BASIS OF PREPARATION

AO World Pic (the “Company”) is a public company limited by shares, incorporated, domiciled and registered in England
in the UK. The registered number is 05525751 and the registered address is from 5A The Parklands, Lostock, Bolton,
BL6 4SD.

Company as at 22 July 2024 for the purposes of ensuring sufficient distributable reserves are available in accordance
with sections 836 and 838 of the Companies Act 2006. These interim financial statements are abridged and unaudited
but have been prepared using the accounting policies consistent with the Companies Act 2006 and in accordance with
the United Kingdom Accounting Standards, including Financial Reporting Standard 101.

These interim financial statements are not the Company’s statutory financial statements. Statutory financial
statements for the year ended 31 March 2023 are available at www.ao-world.com/investor-centre/annual-report-

in due course; the auditors report on those accounts were not qualified, did not include a reference to any matters
which the auditors drew attention by the way of emphasis without qualifying the report and did not contain statements
under section 498 (2) or (3) of the Companies Act 2006.
