Going concern basis

The forecast cashflows for the 12-month period to November 2027, used to support the assessment of going concern, incorporate a latest estimate of 
the ongoing impact of current market conditions on the Group (including the impact of the current Middle East conflict) and include a number of

variety of downsides that the Group might experience, such as a sustained economic recession and an inability for the Group to execute the

Based on the forecast cashflows, throughout the next 12-month period to November 2027, the Group does not anticipate needing to draw on its available 
facilities and has adequate headroom to meet the covenant requirements.

statements. For this reason, the directors consider it appropriate for the Group to adopt the going concern basis in preparing its interim financial
