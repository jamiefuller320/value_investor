Disclaimer* Marks and Spencer Group PLC 20 May 2026 MARKS AND SPENCER GROUP PLC Directorate change Marks and Spencer Group plc ("M&S") announces that Cheryl Potter is standing down from the Board with effect from 7 July 2026. She will therefore not be seeking re-election at the forthcoming AGM. Cheryl has served as a Non-Executive Director at M&S since 1 March 2023. Archie Norman commented, "On behalf of the Board, I would like to thank Cheryl for her significant contribution to Marks & Spencer. During her time as a Non-Executive Director, Cheryl has been a strong advocate for our transformation priorities and brought challenge and insight to Board discussions. We wish her every success in the future." Cheryl Potter said, "It has been a privilege to serve as a Non-Executive Director at Marks & Spencer. I am proud to have supported the Board and management team as the business progressed its transformation, including strengthening digital capability and driving operational improvements. I would like to thank my fellow Directors and colleagues, and I wish everyone at M&S continued success."
