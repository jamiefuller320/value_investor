BETA Close X ITV plc announced that all resolutions were passed at its Annual General Meeting on May 7, 2026, with significant shareholder support. Re-elections and appointments to the board, including Dawn Allen and Helen Ashton, received strong backing, generally above 95%. Resolutions concerning the authority to allot shares and the disapplication of pre-emption rights saw lower, though still majority, support, with 87.73% and 89.31% respectively for the allotment authority and 95.83% and 89.31% for pre-emption rights.
