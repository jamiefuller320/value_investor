Statutory timetable Date Action 5pm on 27 January 2027 Deadline for CMA and Ofcom to report back to the Secretary of State 4 November 2026 Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport issued a public interest intervention notice 23 July 2026 to 6 August 2026 Invitation to comment Phase 1 Public interest intervention notice (PIIN) 4 November 2026: an intervention notice has been given pursuant to section 42 of the Enterprise Act 2002 (the Act) in relation to the proposed acquisition of ITV M&E Holdings Limited by Sky UK Limited. On 15 October 2026, the Secretary of State wrote to Sky UK Limited and ITV M&E Holding Limited to inform them that the Secretary of State was minded to issue a PIIN on the basis that one or more public interest considerations may be relevant to a consideration of the relevant merger situation, as set out in the Act. Having considered the representations provided in response to her ‘minded to’ letter, the Secretary of State announced on 4 November 2026, her decision to issue a PIIN based on the following public interest considerations under section 58 of the Act: the need for free expression of opinion in news media (section 58(2A)(b)) the need for, to the extent that it is reasonable and practicable, a sufficient plurality of views in news media in each market for news media in the United Kingdom or a part of the United Kingdom (section 58(2B)) the need, in relation to every different audience in the United Kingdom or in a particular area or locality of the United Kingdom, for there to be a sufficient plurality of persons with control of the media enterprises serving each audience (section 58(2C)(a)) The PIIN also identifies the below public interest consideration which is not currently specified in the Act. For this consideration, the Secretary of State will bring forward legislation to amend section 58 of the Act to extend the application of the public interest consideration in section 58(2C)(b) relating to the availability of a wide range of broadcasting to on-demand programmes, as follows: the need for the availability throughout the United Kingdom of a wide range of (a) broadcasting and (b) on-demand programmes; which (in each case, taken as a whole) is of high quality and calculated to appeal to a wide variety of tastes and interests This PIIN triggers the requirement for the CMA to report to the Secretary of State on jurisdictional and competition matters, and for Ofcom to report on the media public interest considerations in section 58 of the Act set out above. Both the CMA and Ofcom are required to report back to the Secretary of State by 5pm on 27 January 2027.
