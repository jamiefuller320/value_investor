BETA Close X Halfords Group PLC announced the results of its 2025 Annual General Meeting, where all resolutions were passed on September 3, 2025. The total number of shares on the register as of September 1, 2025, was 218,928,736. Shareholders voted to receive the audited financial statements with 145,331,944 votes for and 1,708 against. A final dividend was declared with 146,009,957 votes for and 34,236 against. The Directors’ Remuneration Report was approved with 145,134,686 votes for and 908,178 against. Henry Birch was elected as a director with 145,615,355 votes for and 408,500 against. Shareholders also authorized the company to make political donations, with 128,412,235 votes for and 17,632,150 votes against.
