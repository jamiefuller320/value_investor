BETA Close X Halfords Group plc announced that several Directors/PDMRs were granted ordinary shares on December 4, 2025, at a price of £1.406 per share. Henry Birch, Chief Executive Officer, received 924,608 shares under the Performance Share Plan. Johanna Hartley, Chief Financial Officer, was granted 596,096 shares under the Performance Share Plan. Karen Bellairs, Chief Customer Officer, received a combined grant of 291,583 shares under the Performance Share Plan and Restricted Share Plan. Jessica Frame, Managing Director of Retail, was granted 216,216 shares under the Performance and Restricted Share Plans. Sarah Haywood, Chief Information Officer, received 199,145 shares under the Performance and Restricted Share Plans. Paul O’Hara, Chief People and Property Officer, was granted 170,696 shares under the Performance and Restricted Share Plans. Tim O’Gorman, General Counsel and Company Secretary, received 48,064 shares under the Performance and Restricted Share Plans. Adam Pay, Director of Garages and Mobile, was granted 170,696 shares under the Performance and Restricted Share Plans. Chris McShane, MD Business to Business and CEO and President of Avayler, received 150,781 shares under the Performance and Restricted Share Plans. These transactions were conducted on the London Stock Exchange's Main Market.
