BETA Close X On September 12, 2025, several PDMRs of Halfords Group plc purchased ordinary 1p shares at £1.368 per share on the London Stock Exchange. Johanna Hartley, CFO, acquired 46,144 shares through dividend reinvestment under the Performance Share Plan, bringing her total to 1,134,563 shares, and 4,427 shares under the Deferred Bonus Plan, resulting in a total of 108,863 shares. Karen Bellairs, Chief Customer Officer, purchased 6,936 shares via the Deferred Bonus Plan, holding 170,550 shares in total, and 43,254 shares under the Performance Share Plan, holding 1,063,510 shares in total. Paul O’Hara, Chief People and Property Officer, acquired 8,497 shares, holding 208,955 shares in total. Tim O’Gorman, General Counsel and Company Secretary, purchased 2,921 shares, holding 71,833 shares in total. Chris McShane, MD Business to Business and CEO and President of Avayler, acquired 6,877 shares, holding 169,088 shares in total. Adam Gerrard, Chief Information and Data Officer, purchased 5,273 shares, holding 129,651 shares in total.
