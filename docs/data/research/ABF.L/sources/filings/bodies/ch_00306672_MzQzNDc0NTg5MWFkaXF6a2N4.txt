Notes to the financial statements

Basis of preparation
These interim financial statements have been prepared for the purposes of sections 836 and 838 of the Companies Act 2006 and contain
information about ABF Investments plc as an individual company and do not contain consolidated financial information. These
financial statements are abridged and unaudited but are otherwise prepared on a consistent basis and following the same accounting
policies as the financial statements for the 52 weeks ended 16 September 2023. These interim accounts do not constitute statutory
accounts within the meaning of section 434(3) of the Companies Act 2006. Statutory financial statements for the 52 weeks ended 16
September 2023 were published in ABF Investment ple’s Annual Report and Financial Statements and delivered to the Registrar of
Companies in England and Wales. The auditor’s report on those financial statements was unqualified, did not include a reference to any
matters to which the auditor drew attention by way of emphasis without qualifying the report, and did not contain a statement under
sections 498(2) or (3) of the Companies Act 2006. No statutory accounts have been delivered to the Registrar in respect of the period
covered by these interim financial statements.
