# AEP Plantations Plc (AEP.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-09-02T10:52:53.586151+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
AEP Plantations is a London-listed Indonesian and Malaysian palm-oil producer whose quantitative profile — P/E 11.4, P/B 1.8, ~4.9% yield, net cash balance sheet and Piotroski F-Score 8/9 — fits a classic value-and-income screen. FY2025 was a strong year (revenue $465.2m, profit attributable to shareholders $90.9m), but H1 2026 results released today show flatter earnings quality: reported profit before tax rose 5.1% to $65.8m only with a $9.1m bargain-purchase gain on the Pinago acquisition, while underlying PBT was ~9% lower on replanting-related volume pressure. The investment case rests on asset-backed cash generation, active capital return (dividends and buybacks), and bolt-on M&A; the key debate is whether commodity prices, Indonesian export governance reforms and estate rejuvenation allow earnings to re-accelerate after the post-Pinago cash drawdown.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen flags AEP as a strong buy across cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP and risk families — a rare breadth pass (19/22 models). That aligns with the business: vertically integrated plantations and mills in Indonesia/Malaysia, minimal leverage (debt/equity 0.09; current ratio 8.7), and filing-aligned FY2025 free cash flow of $83.6m covering dividends ~2.4×. FY2025 Companies House annual accounts show revenue up 25% to $465.2m and EPS up 35% to 23.14 cents (post 10-for-1 split basis; 231.42 cents pre-split in the annual report), with net assets of $584.9m and cash net of short-term borrowings of $231.8m at year-end.

Management is deploying that strength deliberately: the $158.3m Pinago acquisition (May 2026) adds 14,300 ha of mature plantation; a £8m buyback launched July 2026; total FY2025/H1 2026 shareholder returns of $25.9m in H1 alone; and a proposed IPO of the Kalimantan subsidiary (PT AEP Nusantara) to fund regional growth. For a value investor, the hook is tangible book (~149.7 cents/share asset value pre-split equivalent in the 2025 annual report) plus earnings power when CPO prices hold near $853/mt ex-mill (H1 2026 average) and replanting headwinds fade in H2, as management guides.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Commodity cyclicality:** Earnings remain CPO/PK price-sensitive. H1 average CPO ex-mill was $853/mt (-1.2% YoY); outlook cites El Niño and inventory risks. Gross profit flat on higher revenue in H1 signals margin vulnerability if prices soften.

**Operational / replanting drag:** A 10,000 ha replanting programme to 2030 suppresses near-term FFB yields (North Sumatra/Riau delays noted in AGM update and interim). H1 own FFB internal source fell 8.1% excluding Pinago contribution.

**Indonesian regulatory:** May–September 2026 announcements on PT Danantara Sumberdaya Indonesia (DSI) export governance created market volatility (AEP shares fell sharply in May 2026 on policy headlines). Management states limited direct impact as CPO is sold domestically to refineries, but the framework is evolving (full implementation targeted end-2026). IPO of PT AEP Nusantara has slipped from Q4 2026 (AGM update) to 2027 (H1 report).

**Acquisition / accounting:** $9.1m bargain-purchase gain inflates H1 PBT; provisional Pinago PPA may be revised under IFRS 3. Post-deal cash fell ~53%; leverage remains low but liquidity buffer is thinner until operations catch up.

**Customer concentration:** H1 2026 revenue from four Indonesian customers was $89.9m (35.9% of group revenue); two customers each exceeded 10% (interim note 5). Mitigant: weekly tender process cited.

**Pension:** Retirement benefit net liability $8.2m at 30 June 2026 (interim balance sheet); remeasurement gain $152k in H1.

**FX translation:** Large negative exchange reserves ($413.5m cumulative) and $32.1m H1 translation loss affect reported equity though not necessarily cash.

**Governance / complexity:** Multi-jurisdiction structure (UK holdco, Indonesian operating subs, proposed partial IPO) adds transparency and minority-interest complexity. Unqualified FY2025 audit opinion per interim cross-reference.
