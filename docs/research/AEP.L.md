# AEP Plantations Plc (AEP.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-08-30T06:58:39.067309+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
AEP Plantations Plc is a London-listed upstream palm-oil producer with ~56,000 ha of mature plantings and seven mills across Indonesia and Malaysia, reporting in US dollars. FY2025 delivered a step-change in profitability — revenue rose 25% to $465.2m and profit attributable to shareholders grew 35% to $90.9m — on higher CPO/palm-kernel prices and volumes, leaving net cash of $231.8m and minimal borrowings. The quantitative screen flags strong value/quality (P/E ~12, yield ~5%, Piotroski 8/9, FCF dividend cover ~2.4×), though timing is neutral with the share price extended above its 200-day moving average. The central debate is whether commodity-driven earnings and capital returns (81¢ total dividend, active buybacks) outweigh replanting-related volume drag, Indonesia regulatory change, and execution risk around the Pinago acquisition and proposed Indonesian subsidiary IPO.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, AEP combines **cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk** families in one screen — five of five families passed, with Graham Defensive/Enterprising, FCF Yield, Buffett Quality, Magic Formula, and Piotroski F-Score (8/9) all clearing. That is unusual for a commodity name and reflects a balance sheet that screens more like a cash-rich compounder than a leveraged cyclical: current ratio ~8.7×, debt/equity ~0.09, and filing-aligned FCF of ~$83.6m against dividends paid of ~$34.6m in FY2025.

Business quality is supported by long-life plantation assets (weighted average palm age ~14 years), integrated milling, biogas/BioCNG monetisation, and a multi-year replanting programme aimed at sustaining yields. Management has shifted from consolidation (minority buy-ins, non-core disposals in 2023) to growth — Pinago Group acquisition, eighth/ninth mill construction, brownfield pipeline — while returning capital via rising dividends (81¢ in FY2025 vs 51¢ in FY2024) and an enlarged buyback programme. FTSE 250 promotion (Sep 2025) and the ten-for-one share split (Jun 2026) improve liquidity, addressing a historical small-cap discount.

The screen fails only deep-value/net-net criteria (P/B ~1.9, no NCAV), which is appropriate for an asset-backed plantation group trading near, not below, book. At ~12× earnings and ~5% yield with mid-teens ROE and strong cash conversion, the setup fits a **quality-at-a-reasonable-price dividend compounder** rather than a distressed deep-value play — consistent with a Strong Buy signal, subject to commodity and Indonesia-specific risks below.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Commodity cyclicality:** FY2023 demonstrated earnings sensitivity — revenue fell ~17% and EPS nearly halved when CPO prices normalised after 2022 peaks. FY2025 benefited from favourable prices; H1 2026 trading shows flat-to-lower ex-mill CPO ($859/mt). Management cites El Niño supply disruption and B50 biodiesel demand as supports, but this remains a forecast, not a locked-in outcome.

**Replanting volume drag:** Active replanting (~641 ha in first five months of 2026; ~4,800 ha since 2024) suppresses near-term FFB yields, particularly in Bengkulu and North Sumatra. This is structurally necessary but creates a multi-year headwind to own-crop production.

**Indonesia regulatory and policy:** The Jun 2026 trading update flags uncertainty around Indonesia’s commodity export centralisation under PT Danantara (DSI), though AEP sells CPO domestically and expects minimal direct impact. Historically, the FY2023 chairman’s statement referenced EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) concerns and RSPO certification costs (up to ~$18m) — compliance and certification remain ongoing execution items.

**Concentration and geography:** Operations are overwhelmingly Indonesia-focused (~82,000 ha of ~88,000 ha total landbank per FY2025 filing). FX translation (large negative exchange reserves on the balance sheet) and local policy shifts are material.

**M&A and IPO execution:** Pinago integration and the proposed Q4 2026 IPO of PT AEP Nusantara Plantations introduce timing, valuation, and regulatory approval risk. The KAP mill build (~65% structural completion at May 2026) adds capex and commissioning risk.

**Governance / technical compliance:** FY2023 annual report disclosed a Companies Act technical compliance issue around dividend/buyback distributable profits requiring interim accounts — resolved via ratification, but worth monitoring for UK parent-level distributions.

**Pension:** ~$8m non-current pension liability (Yahoo/filing-aligned balance sheet) is modest relative to cash but present.

**Liquidity / ticker confusion:** News coverage includes mis-attributed headlines for American Electric Power (AEP US ticker), reducing signal quality from automated news feeds.

No going-concern language was found in available filing extracts; the balance sheet appears robust. Covenant risk is low given minimal borrowings.
