# FirstGroup plc (FGP.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-08-10T20:46:05.878339+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
FirstGroup is a UK-focused bus and rail operator in a deliberate pivot: exit of North American assets, re-entry into London bus, open-access rail expansion, and progressive capital returns. The quantitative screen flags strong value (P/E ~9.2, earnings yield ~11%, dividend ~3.9%, Piotroski 8/9), supported by adjusted earnings growth and a balance sheet the company describes as strong on an adjusted-net-debt basis. The valuation hook is a cash-generative, post-restructuring UK transport platform trading below typical industrial multiples, with management guiding modest adjusted EPS growth and ongoing buybacks. The central debate is whether adjusted earnings and buyback-driven per-share growth can offset (i) UK rail nationalisation shrinking fee income, (ii) bus policy headwinds (£3 fare cap, franchising, weaker commercial volumes), and (iii) elevated lease-adjusted leverage and materially divergent free-cash-flow definitions — gaps the screen itself flags.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen’s Strong Buy rests on five passing factor families — cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk — with full data quality (20/20 metrics). Headline multiples (P/E 9.2, P/B 1.4, yield 3.9%, ROE 16.7%) and passes on Earnings Yield, Magic Formula, Acquirer’s Multiple, Dreman Contrarian, and Piotroski F-Score (8/9) align with a classic value-plus-quality profile. Business quality, on filing evidence, has improved: FY 2025 adjusted revenue rose 7% to £1.4bn and adjusted EPS rose 16% to 19.4p (Companies House annual accounts, FY to 29 March 2025), driven by First Bus yield/efficiency and First Rail open access and Rail Services growth.

Management’s UK-focused strategy — diversify away from pure DfT TOC fees toward First Bus London, Business & Coach, open-access rail, and Rail Services — gives a plausible path to at least maintain adjusted EPS through industry transition. Capital allocation is shareholder-friendly: progressive dividends (~3× adjusted EPS cover), completed £50m buyback (H1 FY 2026), and a further £100m programme launched June 2026 (RNS headline; body not extracted). For a value investor, the screen correctly identifies a de-rated, cash-returning operator with improving adjusted fundamentals; conviction is tempered by statutory/adjusted divergence, FCF definition noise, and structural policy risk not fully captured in backward-looking ratios.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Regulatory / structural**
- **Rail nationalisation:** Government policy to bring National Rail Contracts into public ownership on a rolling basis (SWR transferred May 2025; further transfers scheduled through 2026). Management anticipates lower FY 2026 First Rail adjusted revenue/profit vs FY 2025, partially offset by ~£125m cash inflow from TOC wind-down over three years from October 2025 (fees paid a year in arrears).
- **Bus franchising and fare policy:** Transition from £2 to £3 fare cap pressured commercial volumes (−7% H1); regional franchising (LCR, West Yorkshire, South Yorkshire, Wales, West Midlands) reshapes competitive dynamics — opportunity for FirstGroup as an experienced franchise operator, but margin and capital-allocation risk on bids.
- **Open-access regulatory risk:** ORR rejected Hull Trains London–Sheffield application (H1 report); further path/access approvals uncertain for Cardiff–York, Rochdale–London, and extensions.

**Financial / balance sheet**
- **Lease-heavy structure:** Reported net debt ~£992m at H1 (IFRS 16 leases £1,017m). Screen fails Financial Health (high debt/equity ~161%, current ratio ~0.73). Adjusted net debt is management’s preferred metric but understates economic leverage from rolling stock and bus fleet leases.
- **FCF divergence:** Screen flags FCF basis overlay; company-adjusted FCF (£113.5m) is far below Yahoo/filing-aligned £362.6m. FCF Yield model fails — do not rely on a single FCF figure for valuation.
- **Accelerated capex:** FY 2026 net bus capex guided ~£180m (electrification); H1 FCF negative. Medium-term bus capex ~£100m p.a. unless further government co-funding.

**Operational**
- Cost inflation, employer NIC (~£15–16m annualised group impact), industrial action (minor H1 bus impact), and integration risk on First Bus London (purchase price allocation still provisional at H1).
- **Pension:** Deficit sensitivity material (e.g. +1 year life expectancy adds ~£37m to deficit per H1 sensitivities); ~£65m escrow tied to 2030 valuations.

**Governance / filings**
- FY 2026 full-year filing body absent from research pack — verify adjusted full-year figures before sizing.
- Going concern language is standard positive (H1); no covenant breach disclosed in available extracts. Medium-term target: adjusted net debt/adjusted EBITDA below 2.0×.
