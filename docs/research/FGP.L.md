# FirstGroup plc (FGP.L) — Research memo

_Version 2 · Updated 2026-08-10T18:03:32.122068+00:00 · Mode: gap_fill_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
FirstGroup is a UK-focused public transport operator (First Bus, First Rail open access and DfT-contracted rail, Rail Services) that has repositioned after exiting North America. The quantitative screen rates it a **strong buy** (11/22 models; composite 92%), driven by cheapness (P/E 9.2, earnings yield ~11%), high reported FCF yield, dividend growth, and Piotroski F-Score 8/9. Filings show a credible earnings trajectory: FY 2025 adjusted EPS rose 16% to 19.4p on £1.37bn adjusted revenue; H1 FY 2026 adjusted EPS was 9.9p (+16%) with management guiding modest full-year EPS growth. The valuation hook is a cash-generative, buyback-active name (new £100m programme announced June 2026) trading below typical quality multiples, with open-access rail and bus M&A as growth offsets to DfT TOC nationalisation. The key debate is whether screen-level FCF and low P/E fully reflect elevated leverage (D/E ~161%, current ratio 0.73), statutory/adjusted earnings divergence, and structural bus volume pressure (£3 fare cap, NI costs) plus rail contract roll-off.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen’s strong-buy signal aligns with a business that has materially improved since the North American disposals: FY 2025 adjusted operating profit reached £222.8m (+9%) and free cash flow £113.5m (Annual Report 2025 / FY 2025 results presentation), with adjusted net debt of £86.9m and progressive dividends (6.5p total). Capital returns are active—£92m buybacks in FY 2025, £50m completed in H1 FY 2026, plus a £100m programme launched June 2026 (RNS headline; body not extracted).

Quality markers supporting the screen include: (1) **five model families passed**—cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, risk; (2) **Magic Formula, Acquirer’s Multiple, Dreman, Composite Value, Earnings Quality** all passing; (3) **dividend growth** with ~3.9% yield; (4) **ROE 16.7%** though below moat thresholds. Management is deliberately diversifying: First Bus London (~£150m H1 revenue), Business & Coach, open-access rail expansion (c.£500m Hitachi fleet order; capacity set to more than double), London Overground contract (mobilisation from May 2026), and bolt-on coach acquisitions.

For a value investor, the case is a **transition play at a discount**: earnings and cash conversion have improved, the balance sheet target is <2.0x adjusted net debt/EBITDA, and ~£125m TOC transition cash is expected over three years as National Rail Contracts transfer to public ownership—partially replacing fee income with capital return. The screen’s FCF-basis overlay (filing $362.6m vs company-adjusted £113.5m) warrants caution but does not invalidate the cheapness signal if adjusted metrics and bus/rail pipeline deliver as guided.

FCF shortfall vs prior year is **capex/M&A timing**, not a data error. Adjusted leverage is **serviceable** with undrawn RCF and no going-concern qualification. Rail nationalisation proceeds (~£90m cash over three years); bus volume pressure persists (−3% regional volumes).

**Still open:** covenant maintenance levels (debt note); Mistral disposal (~£46m FY 2027 adjusting gain, −0.6p ongoing EPS); London Overground and open-access execution vs £400m three-year cash guide; screen D/E 161% and current ratio 0.73 remain **misleading quality signals** without IFRS 16 adjustment.
