# FirstGroup plc (FGP.L) — Research memo

_Version 2 · Updated 2026-08-23T07:13:18.438721+00:00 · Mode: gap_fill_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
FirstGroup is a UK-focused bus and rail operator that has completed a multi-year pivot from North American disposal to a cleaner, UK-centric earnings base built on First Bus growth, open-access rail, and DfT management-fee contracts. The quantitative screen flags Strong Buy on cheapness (P/E ~9.0, earnings yield ~11%), dividend yield (~4.0%), and quality markers (Piotroski F-Score 8/9, ROE 16.7%), with five factor families passing. FY 2026 adjusted EPS rose to 20.3p (from 19.4p) despite a marginally lower adjusted operating profit, supported by buybacks and portfolio diversification including First Bus London. The valuation hook is a cash-generative business trading below 10x earnings with a progressive dividend and active capital return, but the key debate is whether accelerated bus electrification capex, rising adjusted net debt, and the structural transition of DfT rail contracts to public ownership leave reported free cash flow and earnings quality durable enough to justify the screen’s strong signal — especially given flagged FCF and earnings-basis divergences.

---

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, FirstGroup offers a rare combination in UK transport: visible adjusted earnings growth, shareholder returns, and a balance sheet that management targets below 2.0x adjusted net debt to adjusted EBITDA, without the legacy North American overhang. The screen’s Strong Buy rating is anchored in earnings yield, low P/E plus yield, Magic Formula, Acquirer’s Multiple, Dreman Contrarian, and Composite Value passes — a broad cheapness and quality cluster rather than a single metric. Business quality has improved materially: FY 2025 adjusted revenue was £1,370.0m with adjusted operating profit of £222.8m and adjusted EPS of 19.4p (FY 2025 results presentation); FY 2026 advanced to adjusted revenue of £1,718.7m, adjusted EPS of 20.3p, and total dividend of 7.2p (Companies House annual accounts, filed August 2026). Management is executing a disciplined capital allocation framework — progressive dividends (~3x adjusted EPS cover, tightening toward 2.5x), buybacks (£50m completed H1 2026; £100m programme launched June 2026), and bolt-on M&A — while repositioning earnings toward franchised bus, open-access rail, and Rail Services. The Piotroski score of 8/9 and dividend growth screen pass support the quality overlay, even as Graham-style and FCF-yield screens fail on leverage, liquidity, and FCF definition mismatch. Net: the screen correctly identifies a statistically cheap, improving UK transport compounder; deep research confirms the directional case but flags transition risk and accounting/FCF complexity that the screen partially adjusts for.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Evidenced (filings)**

- **Accounting / governance:** Multiple non-GAAP definitions (adjusted revenue, adjusted earnings ex-IFRS 16 on DfT TOCs, company FCF). FY 2026 had **zero adjusting items**, yet adjusted EPS rose while adjusted earnings fell — buyback-driven optics. Prior-year levy reclassification (£167.6m revenue/expense, nil P&L impact) shows comparability care needed.
- **Leverage (headline):** Screen D/E **161%** is **IFRS 16-inclusive** (Yahoo); not indicative of distress on management’s **£137.7m adjusted net debt**, but lease stack **£850m** is structurally real.
- **Liquidity:** Current ratio **0.73** (Yahoo); **£295m** undrawn RCF and going-concern confirmation to September 2027 (FY 2026 results RNS).
- **FCF / capex:** Company FCF **£73.8m** is cyclically depressed by bus electrification; not recurring at Yahoo-implied levels. Dividend coverage **~1.9×** on company basis vs screen **9.3×** on Yahoo basis.
- **Regulatory:** DfT TOC renationalisation (GWR December 2026; WCP ~FY 2027 end); bus franchising; fare-cap policy.
- **Cyclical:** Regional bus volumes **−3%** FY 2026; commercial **−6%**; open-access yield pressure.
- **Pension:** Net surplus **£19.9m** at FY 2026; **~£65m** in escrow pending 2030 valuations.

**Still open (alternate source needed)**

- **Covenant risk:** RCF/facility covenants and headroom — **Annual Report borrowing note** or credit agreement summary.
- **Lease maturity concentration:** Maturity profile beyond totals — **IFRS 16 note / results presentation lease table**.
- **Forward FCF:** Mistral sale (July 2026 RNS headline) — full disposal RNS body for pro-forma FCF and adjusting-item treatment.
