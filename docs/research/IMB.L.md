# Imperial Brands PLC (IMB.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-08-10T16:33:36.596732+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Imperial Brands is a global tobacco and nicotine challenger trading at a material discount to its cash-return profile: the quantitative screen flags Strong Buy on cheapness (FCF yield ~15.5%), dividend yield ~6%, and quality metrics (ROE 37%), with neutral technical timing (RSI 38, below 200-day MA). The investment case rests on sustained combustibles cash generation in five priority markets (~70% of adjusted tobacco operating profit), accelerating NGP scale-up, and a capital-allocation framework targeting progressive dividends plus large buybacks (£1.45bn programme currently active). The central debate is whether mid-single-digit adjusted profit growth and double-digit NGP revenue growth can offset structural volume decline, regulatory tightening, and a leveraged balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA target 2.0–2.5×) — particularly after reported earnings fell sharply in FY2025. Filings confirm strategic progress but interim disclosure is absent from the source pack, limiting near-term verification.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen’s Strong Buy signal aligns with a business that has rebuilt operational credibility under its consumer-led challenger strategy. Five annual Companies House accounts (FY2021–FY2025) document a consistent narrative: focus on US, Germany, UK, Spain and Australia; combustibles pricing and share stabilisation; and NGP net revenue growth (13.7% in FY2025 per the FY2025 annual report extract).

**Why the screen fires:**

| Factor | Evidence |
|--------|----------|
| **Cheapness** | FCF Yield, Acquirer’s Multiple, Composite Value models pass; FCF yield 15.5%, P/E 12.5 |
| **Quality / cash** | Buffett Quality, Earnings Quality pass; FY2024 filing cites £2.4bn free cash flow and 90–100% adjusted operating cash conversion |
| **Dividend** | High Dividend Yield (~6%), Dividend Growth pass; progressive policy with DPS rising from 141.17p (FY2022) to 153.42p (FY2024) |
| **GARP** | Neff PEGY 0.02 passes despite headline earnings growth of −38.2% (screen input) |
| **Risk family** | Magic Formula, Dreman Contrarian pass — market scepticism may create contrarian entry |

**Business quality:** Imperial is the smallest of the major international tobacco groups, which management argues supports agility in NGP (vapour, heated, modern oral). FY2024 Chair’s statement reports double-digit NGP net revenue growth with reduced NGP losses, and mid-single-digit adjusted operating profit growth — meeting stated strategic targets. Capital returns are substantial: FY2024 filing commits £1.25bn buyback for FY25 and £2.0bn total capital returns; news flow confirms a £1.45bn repurchase plan through 2026.

For a value investor, the hook is not growth at any price but **high, visible cash returns** (dividend + buyback ≈ 8–10%+ of market cap annually) from a stabilising combustibles base, funded by low-capex, cash-generative operations — provided leverage and regulatory risks remain manageable.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Regulatory and industry**

- FY2024 Chair’s statement highlights **prohibition-leaning policy** (Australia cited: ~30% illicit tobacco, restrictive NGP rules, excise revenue loss) — direct filing language on unintended consequences of over-restrictive regulation.
- Structural **volume decline** in combustibles industry-wide; Imperial’s model depends on pricing/mix and priority-market share stability (+5 bps aggregate share in five markets per FY2024 filing).
- **NGP competition** from larger peers; Imperial is investing to build scale but NGP was loss-making (losses reducing per FY2024 filing).

**Financial structure**

- Screen failures on **Financial Health**, **Graham Defensive/Enterprising**, **Deep Value**: high debt/equity, P/B 5.2, current ratio <1, P/B not below deep-value thresholds.
- **Piotroski F-Score 6/9** — fails (leverage not declining, ROA/turnover not improving).
- Net debt ticked up in 2025 (Yahoo); covenant detail not in body extracts — **verify debt maturity profile before sizing position**.

**Governance / management**

- News (Jun 2026): *“Imperial Brands Insider Lowered Holding By 59% During Last Year”* (simplywall.st) — not explained in filings pack; worth monitoring.
- Transformation programme risk acknowledged in FY2024 Chair’s statement: *“risks associated with a change programme of this ambition and scale.”*

**Pension and contingencies**

- Pension obligations ~**$801m** non-current (Yahoo); defined benefit pension **$314m** asset. Full contingency and litigation disclosure not captured in OCR filing extracts — **gap flagged**.

**Earnings quality**

- Large adjusted vs reported EPS gap (FY2022: 265.2p vs 165.9p). FY2025 net income decline (Yahoo) warrants scrutiny of one-offs vs structural margin pressure.

**Data gaps**

- No interim filings; no going-concern or covenant language retrieved from body extracts; principal risks section referenced in filing contents but not fully extracted in OCR bodies.
