# Smith & Nephew plc (SN.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-08-19T10:39:28.585891+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Smith & Nephew is a global medtech business (Orthopaedics, Sports Medicine & ENT, Advanced Wound Management) that the quantitative screen rates as a **Strong Buy**, driven by cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP and risk factors at P/E 19.7, P/B 2.4 and 2.6% yield. The investment case rests on a recovering earnings and cash-flow profile, disciplined capital return ($1bn+ of buybacks in 2025–26), and a product-led growth narrative under the new RISE strategy — offset by persistent US knee weakness, skin-substitute reimbursement headwinds and a valuation that fails deeper quality/moat screens (ROE 11.9%, Lynch PEG 3.5). The central debate is whether innovation-led mix shift (Sports Medicine now the largest division) and margin leverage can deliver ~6% revenue and ~$1.3bn trading profit in 2026, or whether orthopaedic share loss and policy risk cap re-rating. Primary annual and interim filing bodies are **absent** from the source pack; analysis leans on the Q1 2026 trading update and Yahoo annual statements, with gaps flagged throughout.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen’s Strong Buy rating is well supported on **valuation and cash economics**, less so on **franchise quality at Buffett/moat thresholds**.

**Why the screen fires:** Eight of 22 models pass across five factor families — Graham Enterprising, FCF Yield, Neff PEGY, Dividend Growth, Piotroski F-Score (7/9), Composite Value, Earnings Quality and Financial Health. At P/E 19.7 and P/B 2.36 the stock sits below typical medtech premium multiples whilst offering 2.6% dividend yield with net FCF/dividend coverage of ~2.6x (screen; filing-aligned FCF $852m for FY2025). Debt/equity of 73% is within the screen’s risk tolerance; current ratio 2.57 and declining leverage score positively on Piotroski. Statutory EPS rebounded sharply in FY2025 (basic EPS $0.721 vs $0.472 in 2024; Yahoo fallback), and FCF doubled to $852m from $606m — the kind of inflection value screens reward.

**Business quality tie-in:** Smith & Nephew is a diversified, innovation-weighted portfolio (management cites >50% of Q1 growth from products launched within five years). The Q1 2026 trading update (primary filing) shows underlying revenue growth of 3.1% (4.7% on adjusted daily sales), with Sports Medicine & ENT up 6.7% underlying and now larger than Orthopaedic Reconstruction — a structural mix improvement. Management reaffirmed FY2026 guidance for ~6% underlying revenue growth, ~$1.3bn trading profit and ~$800m FCF, and announced a further $500m buyback — signalling balance-sheet confidence and shareholder-friendly capital allocation (following a completed $500m programme in 2025 per the same filing).

**Where the screen is less convincing:** Failures on Graham Defensive, Deep Value, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat and Lynch PEG (3.46) flag that this is **enterprising value**, not deep value or wide-moat compounder territory. ROE at 11.9% sits below the 12–18% thresholds on several quality models. Timing is neutral (RSI ~39, price ~11% below 200-day MA) — attractive for accumulation but not a momentum catalyst.

For a value investor, the hook is a **cash-generative, self-help story** (RISE strategy, buybacks, margin leverage) at a reasonable multiple, with activist oversight (Cevian ~14% per news) as a potential governance catalyst — not a classic net-net or deep-discount asset.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Operational / competitive:** US knee implants remain a structural weak point (-10.3% Q1 underlying per filing), with deliberate market-share trade-offs ahead of LANDMARK launch — recovery is guided for H2 2026 but unproven. Q2 news (not in filing bodies) suggests broader US orthopaedic softness. Sports Medicine strength masks orthopaedic reconstruction share pressure versus Stryker, Zimmer and others.

**Regulatory / reimbursement:** Skin-substitute reimbursement reset ($20–40m guided impact in 2026); China Volume-Based Procurement expected in H2 2026 for Sports Medicine/ENT — filing flags delayed VBP but not eliminated. US FDA clearance activity is positive (product RNS) but does not remove payer/policy risk.

**Macro / tariffs:** Management guides ~$60m tariff impact in 2026 (Q1 trading update forward-looking statements). No covenant or going-concern language appears in available filing bodies; the forward-looking caution lists conflicts, reimbursement, litigation, supply chain and FX among key risks — standard but relevant for a globally exposed medtech.

**Governance / key person:** Orthopaedics President Craig Gaffin departing (May 2026 filing); Nathan Folkert appointed — execution risk during US knee turnaround. Activist Cevian Capital holds ~14% (news, Jul–Aug 2026) — potential catalyst but also pressure for sharper capital allocation or structural change. CEO share vesting and tax-related sales (Aug 2026 news) are routine but worth monitoring.

**Financial structure:** Leverage (D/E 73%) fails moat/Buffett screens; net debt ~$2.6bn limits deep-value classification. Goodwill/intangibles ~38% of total assets — acquisition and impairment risk. Restructuring charges persisted ($63m FY2025 unusual items, Yahoo).

**Filing / disclosure gap:** Absence of annual report and interim accounts in the source pack prevents verification of going-concern statements, pension triennial disclosures, litigation contingencies and covenant detail — a material research limitation before sizing positions.

**Earnings quality:** Strong FY2025 statutory EPS growth partly reflects lower restructuring drag; adjusted vs statutory divergence warrants scrutiny in full accounts (not available here).
