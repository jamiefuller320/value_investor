## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Smith & Nephew is a global medtech business (Orthopaedics, Sports Medicine & ENT, Advanced Wound Management) whose shares screen as a **strong buy** on a blend of reasonable valuation (P/E ~20, P/B ~2.4), improving cash generation, dividend support, and solid financial-health metrics (Piotroski F-Score 7/9). The investment case rests on a post-transformation earnings and FCF inflection, a product-led growth pipeline under the new RISE strategy, and aggressive capital return ($500m buyback announced in May 2026 on top of a completed $500m programme in 2025). The central debate is whether operational headwinds—US knee share loss, skin-substitute reimbursement reset, tariffs, and a soft Q2 2026—are transitional, or whether orthopaedic competitiveness and margin recovery will lag peers despite activist pressure (Cevian ~14%). Primary filing coverage in this pack is thin (no annual or interim accounts indexed); deeper verification before sizing is warranted.

Price sits ~9% below the 200-day moving average with neutral timing (RSI ~44)—consistent with accumulating into weakness rather than chasing momentum.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Operational / cyclical**
- **US Orthopaedics:** Four consecutive quarters of US hip outperformance, but US knees **-10.3%** in Q1; Q2 news suggests continued orthopaedic softness. LANDMARK knee and cementless variants are critical H2 2026 catalysts—execution risk is high.
- **Reimbursement:** Advanced Wound Bioactives hit by US skin-substitute reimbursement reset ($20–40m guided impact); volumes, pricing, and billing efficiency under pressure.
- **China VBP:** Volume-Based Procurement delayed for Sports Medicine/ENT but expected H2 2026—potential pricing/volume shock.
- **Tariffs:** ~$60m guided headwind in 2026.

**Competitive / strategic**
- Orthopaedics leadership transition: Nathan Folkert replaces Craig Gaffin (May 2026)—positive hire profile but integration risk during a product cycle inflection.
- Integrity Orthopaedics acquisition marginally dilutive to 2026 trading profit; integration and synergy realisation uncertain.
- Robotics competition intense; CORI XT early-stage—hip indication still under development per product RNS.

**Financial / governance**
- **Filing thinness:** Absence of indexed annual/interim accounts limits verification of covenants, going-concern language, pension triennial outcomes, and contingent liabilities. Forward-looking RNS cautions cite litigation, product recalls, M&A integration failure, FX, cybersecurity, and regulatory/reimbursement actions—standard medtech language but relevant given current reimbursement and tariff exposures.
- **Quality screens failed:** ROE below top-tier quality thresholds; EV/EBIT ~14.6 fails Acquirer’s Multiple; PEG ~3.5 fails Lynch—growth priced in despite near-term miss risk.
- **Intangibles:** Goodwill ~$3.1bn—impairment risk if orthopaedic turnaround stalls.
- **Activist overhang:** Cevian at ~14% may drive strategic or capital-structure pressure—could be positive or disruptive.

**Pension:** Non-current pension/post-retirement liabilities ~$84m (Yahoo 2025); defined benefit asset $64m—requires annual-report disclosure not available here.
