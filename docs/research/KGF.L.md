# Kingfisher plc (KGF.L) — Research memo

_Version 1 · Updated 2026-09-02T10:20:19.499919+00:00 · Mode: initial_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Kingfisher is a European home-improvement retailer (B&Q, Screwfix, Castorama, Brico Dépôt) trading below book (P/B ~0.9) with improving adjusted earnings, strong UK momentum, and a capital-return programme (£300m buyback completed; a further £300m underway). FY 25/26 (to 31 Jan 2026) delivered adjusted PBT of £560m (+6%) and free cash flow of £512m, with net leverage at 1.4x. The hold rating fits: cheapness and cash-return screens pass, but statutory ROE remains weak (~3.9% on the screen), P/E is ~22.5x on reported earnings, and continental Europe—especially France—continues to drag returns and absorb impairments. The central debate is whether trade/digital transformation and UK share gains can offset cyclical DIY weakness, French restructuring delays, and CEO succession risk after Thierry Garnier’s May 2026 resignation.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Kingfisher is a **hold**, not an accumulate: the balance sheet offers a tangible floor, but earnings power is still rebuilding and geographically uneven.

**What the screen captures well.** The quantitative pass set spans cheapness (Schloss Low P/B, Deep Value), FCF yield, dividend growth, Lynch/Neff GARP, Piotroski F-Score, earnings quality, and financial health—five of four required families. P/B ~0.87 and D/E ~38% suggest asset backing and manageable reported leverage; dividend yield ~3.9% with flat 12.40p total dividend (FY filing) supports income-oriented models. Data quality is full (20/20 metrics).

**What limits conviction.** ROE ~3.9% fails Graham Enterprising (ROE ≤8%); yield is not top-quartile. Statutory earnings remain well below the 2023 peak (£245m net income vs £471m in Yahoo FY2023), reflecting £182m adjusting items in FY 25/26 (Castorama France goodwill impairment, store impairments, Romania disposal loss, Turkey JV write-down). ROCE of 8.2% (filing) barely clears WACC of 8.4%—capital is not clearly compounding. The screen flags an **FCF basis mismatch** (filing £512m vs screen TTM £972m vs company-adjusted £450m); treat headline FCF yield with caution until definitions are reconciled.

**Business quality overlay.** Strategically, Kingfisher is executing: UK LFL +3.3%, trade penetration 30%, e-commerce 21%, marketplace GMV +58%. Q1 26/27 (to 30 Apr 2026) was softer on underlying LFL (-0.7%) but management reiterated full-year adjusted PBT £565–625m and FCF £450–510m. Asset support is real—the October 2025 property valuation was £2.8bn vs £2.2bn book—but continental retail margins (France retail profit margin 2.5%) and cyclical housing/DIY exposure cap the quality score. Net: **cheap enough to hold, not cheap enough on normalized earnings quality to push aggressively**.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Cyclical and market.** Home improvement is tied to housing turnover, mortgage rates, and weather. Q1 commentary cites low single-digit market declines across geographies; B&Q seasonal weakness from a late spring. French market down ~3% in FY 25/26 and ~10% since 2024—delaying Castorama’s path to 5–7% retail margin target.

**Competitive.** Screwfix gains share, but UK DIY is contested (Homebase closure transference may fade). Marketplace scaling adds platform competition; Castorama/Brico face entrenched local rivals.

**Geographic and accounting.** £182m adjusting items and recurring restructuring in France/Poland. Turkey JV fully impaired; Romania exited at a loss. Goodwill on Castorama France remains a sensitivity if the French market stays weak.

**Governance / key person.** CEO Thierry Garnier resigned 6 May 2026 with up to 12 months’ notice; successor search underway—execution risk during transition on “Powered by Kingfisher” strategy.

**Leverage and pensions.** Reported D/E is moderate, but **£2.2bn+ lease liabilities** inflate economic leverage; net debt/EBITDA 1.4x is acceptable but not spare. Pension largely in surplus in UK, but overseas deficit and actuarial valuation (2025 ongoing) warrant monitoring.

**Market sentiment.** “Kingfisher takes the perch as most shorted UK stock of 2025” (Investment Week, 21 Jan 2026)—suggests scepticism on recovery durability.

**Liquidity / covenants.** Filing confirms covenant compliance and >£1.1bn liquidity; no going-concern qualification in available extracts (full going-concern wording sits in Annual Report published 2 Apr 2026, body not in corpus beyond publication notice).

**FCF reliability.** Screen FCF mismatch and company-adjusted £450m figure imply dividend/buyback coverage should be stress-tested on filing FCF (£512m) less guided FY 26/27 range (£450–510m), not Yahoo TTM.
