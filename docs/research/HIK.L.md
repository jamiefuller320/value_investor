# Hikma Pharmaceuticals PLC (HIK.L) — Research memo

_Version 3 · Updated 2026-08-19T11:31:20.261064+00:00 · Mode: gap_fill_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Hikma Pharmaceuticals is a FTSE 100 multinational generic and branded medicines group with three divisions — Injectables, Branded (MENA) and Hikma Rx (US oral/specialty) — reporting in US dollars and generating roughly 58% of H1 2026 revenue from the United States. The quantitative screen rates the name a **Strong Buy** on cheapness (P/E 12.7, P/B 1.9), a 3.8% dividend yield, 15.4% ROE and broad model passes across quality, dividend, GARP and risk families, albeit with screen overlays on healthcare pricing pressure, earnings-basis divergence and FCF inconsistency. The valuation hook is a depressed multiple after a February 2026 guidance reset and share-price fall, partially reversed by a solid H1 2026 profit beat and a $250 million buyback. The central debate is whether Branded and Hikma Rx momentum, capital return and US manufacturing scale can offset Injectables margin compression, elevated capex and statutory earnings that are flattish once legal settlements are stripped out.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Hikma offers a rare combination of **sector-defensive cash generation**, **investment-grade balance sheet** (BBB/stable at S&P and Fitch) and **multiple valuation anchors** that align with the screen’s Strong Buy signal.

**Quantitative alignment.** Ten of 22 models pass, spanning Graham Enterprising, Neff PEGY, Quality Value, Buffett Quality, Dividend Growth, Magic Formula, Acquirer's Multiple, Dreman Contrarian, Composite Value and Financial Health. Key metrics — P/E 12.7, P/B 1.92, yield 3.8%, debt/equity 77%, current ratio 1.8 — sit comfortably within value thresholds. The screen passes five factor families (cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, risk) with full data quality (20/20 metrics). Failures are instructive rather than disqualifying: FCF Yield, Lynch PEG and Economic Moat fail on FCF conversion, negative statutory earnings growth and sub-moat ROE (15.4% vs 18% hurdle), consistent with a cyclical/generic pricing backdrop rather than a broken balance sheet.

**Business quality.** Primary filings show a structurally improving mix: Branded delivered 15% H1 2026 revenue growth and 32.5% core operating margin; Hikma Rx core operating margin expanded to 20.6% in H1 2026 from 17.3% in FY2025. The Group is the largest pharma company by sales in MENA and a top-three US injectables supplier by volume. FY2025 core operating profit of $741 million (+3%) on revenue of $3,349 million (+6% core, constant currency) demonstrates underlying earnings power despite Injectables headwinds.

**Capital allocation.** Management increased the total dividend 5% to 84 cents for FY2025 and launched a $250 million buyback in February 2026; by 5 August 2026, $227 million had been executed. ROIC was 16.0% at FY2025 (filing). These actions support the screen’s dividend and quality passes while the share price trades below broker re-rating levels post-H1.

**Why Strong Buy, not deep value.** The name is not a net-net or deep P/B play (P/B 1.9; Graham Defensive and Schloss fail). It is a **quality-at-a-reasonable-price** generic pharma compounder with a geographic and segment hedge — Branded growth funding Injectables reinvestment — that the screen correctly identifies at a sector-relative composite of 91%.
