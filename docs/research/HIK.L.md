# Hikma Pharmaceuticals PLC (HIK.L) — Research memo

_Version 3 · Updated 2026-09-06T07:39:55.508201+00:00 · Mode: gap_fill_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Hikma is a FTSE 100 generics and specialty pharmaceutical group spanning Injectables, Branded MENA, and US Hikma Rx, reporting in USD. The quantitative screen rates it **Strong Buy** (11/22 models, 81% composite, all five factor families passed) on a ~12× P/E, 1.8× P/B, ~4% yield and 15% ROE — cheap versus a diversified, FDA-inspected manufacturing footprint. FY2025 filings show revenue and core profit growth, but statutory free cash flow fell sharply to **$119m** (operating cash **$436m** less elevated capex and **$186m** legal settlements), creating a central debate on cash conversion and dividend durability. H1 2026 interim results were stronger (core operating profit **+9%**), and management has reiterated FY2026 guidance, but Injectables margins remain under pressure and net debt has risen. The value case rests on earnings power and capital return; the bear case on low reported growth, FCF definition divergence, and segment/regulatory risk.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The screen’s **Strong Buy** signal is well supported on classical value metrics: P/E 12.3, P/B 1.85, dividend yield ~4%, debt/equity 77%, current ratio 1.8, and passes across Graham Enterprising, Earnings Yield, Quality Value, Buffett Quality, Dividend Growth, Magic Formula, Acquirer’s Multiple, Dreman Contrarian, Composite Value and Financial Health. Conviction is **73%**, persistent for 13 weeks with stable trend — unusually consistent for healthcare.

Business quality aligns with several passes. FY2025 core revenue grew **6%** to **$3,349m**; core operating profit **+3%** to **$741m** (22.1% margin); Branded grew core operating profit **+19%**. The group operates **13 US FDA-inspected plants**, launched 84 products in 2025, and received **BBB** upgrades from S&P and Fitch. Return on average invested capital was **16.0%**. Capital return is active: FY2025 total dividend **84 cents** (+5%), a **$250m** buyback (completed second tranche Aug 2026 per news), and H1 2026 interim dividend **38 cents** (+6%).

Where the screen and business diverge explains failed models and the overlay caution. Statutory EPS growth is only **~1.7%**, failing Lynch PEG (PEG ~7.0) and Economic Moat screens. FCF Yield fails (3.6% vs 5% threshold): filing-aligned FCF **$119m** vs prior-year **$329m**, with statutory OCF-minus-capex dividend cover **0.64×** vs management-adjusted **1.31×** — a flagged definition divergence. Piotroski F-Score is **6/9** (leverage not declining; gross margin and asset turnover not improving). For a value investor, Hikma offers a **quality compounder trading at a cyclical/generic discount** after a difficult 2023 impairment year and Feb 2026 guidance reset, with H1 2026 suggesting operational recovery — but not a deep-value net-net or high-FCF-yield compounder without further verification of normalised cash generation.
