# Hikma Pharmaceuticals PLC (HIK.L) — Research memo

_Version 3 · Updated 2026-08-10T18:14:01.547961+00:00 · Mode: gap_fill_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Hikma is a diversified multinational generics and branded-pharmaceuticals group with roughly 60% of revenue in North America, a leading MENA Branded franchise, and investment-grade balance sheet (BBB/stable, S&P and Fitch). The quantitative screen flags a strong buy on cheapness (P/E 12.6, P/B 1.9), quality (ROE 15.4%), dividend (3.8% yield), and financial health, with 10 of 22 models passing at an 80% composite score. The valuation hook is a FTSE 100-quality cash-generative pharma name trading below mid-teens earnings after a February 2026 de-rating, now recovering on a solid H1 2026 beat and active $250m buyback. The key debate is whether Branded and Hikma Rx momentum can offset Injectables margin erosion, weak reported free-cash conversion, and rising leverage (net debt/core EBITDA 1.9x at H1 2026), or whether withdrawn medium-term guidance signals a structurally lower earnings trajectory.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Hikma offers a rare combination of screen-passing valuation, visible capital return, and business quality across three distinct segments. The screen passes five metric families — cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk — with Graham Enterprising, Buffett Quality, Dividend Growth, Magic Formula, and Financial Health among the ten passing models. Debt/equity of 77% and a current ratio of 1.8 sit within conservative thresholds, while ROE of 15.4% and a 3.8% dividend yield support the quality and income case.

Business quality is anchored in segment diversification. Branded (MENA) delivered 10% revenue growth and 19% core operating profit growth in FY2025, accelerating to 15% revenue and 23% core profit growth in H1 2026. Hikma Rx generated over $1bn in core revenue with improving margins (core operating margin 17.3% in FY2025, 20.6% in H1 2026). Injectables remains the US hospital injectables leader by volume but is in a deliberate reinvestment phase, with core margin guided down to 27–28% for 2026 from 31.0% in FY2025.

Capital allocation reinforces the case: total dividend rose 5% to 84 cents in FY2025, interim dividend up 6% to 38 cents in H1 2026, and $227m of the $250m buyback was executed by 5 August 2026. ROIC of 16.0% (FY2025 filing) and trailing core EBITDA of $887m (H1 2026 filing) support returns above the cost of capital despite statutory earnings noise from legal settlements.

The screen’s adjusted Buy (from Strong Buy) reflects two overlays worth heeding: statutory EPS growth is slightly negative (-4.7%) while adjusted EPS growth is positive (+1.7%), and FCF basis divergence (filing-aligned FCF $119m vs screen TTM $14.4m) causes FCF Yield and Earnings Quality models to fail. These do not invalidate the value case but argue for staged accumulation rather than full conviction at screen weight.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Evidenced — pricing, regulatory, pipeline (partially closed):**
- Principal risks explicitly cover pricing/reimbursement, regulatory policy, and IP (`5f26e81d036b3596.txt`).
- Structural US generic pricing pressure: Hikma Rx "modest"/"usual" price erosion (`5f26e81d036b3596.txt`, `ir_0e40d9707e30c3b7.txt`); FY2026 sodium oxybate competition expected with partial mix mitigation.
- Product-specific cliff proxies (not patent-dated): US competition on testosterone and calcitonin; authorised generic sodium oxybate still contributing but flagged for future competition (`ir_0e40d9707e30c3b7.txt`, `ir_a70365d580129295.txt`).
- US import tariffs on generics monitored; company cites majority US-manufactured sales as mitigation (`5f26e81d036b3596.txt`); China-to-US tariff impact ~$3m in FY2025 (`ir_0e40d9707e30c3b7.txt`).
- Pipeline activity credible (118 Injectables products FY2025; 212/131/58 programmes per H1 presentation) but Bedford revenue deferred to 2028; withdrawn medium-term margin guidance leaves replacement timing uncertain.

**Still open — payer and patent quantification:**
- Medicaid, NHS, and FDA Orange Book expiry schedules absent from all 38 body extracts. **Next source:** FDA Orange Book + NHS Drug Tariff crosswalk against disclosed top products (US wholesalers each 11% of H1 revenue).

**Evidenced — other (unchanged from prior pass):**
