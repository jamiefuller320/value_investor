## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Hikma Pharmaceuticals PLC (HIK.L) is a diversified generic and specialty pharmaceutical group spanning US injectables, US retail (Hikma Rx), and MENA branded generics, reporting in US dollars. The quantitative screen rates the name a strong buy (10/22 models; composite 81%; P/E 12.5, P/B 1.9, yield 3.9%, ROE 15.4%), supported by cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP and risk families. Filings show FY2025 core revenue of $3,349m (+6%) and core operating profit of $741m (+3%), with H1 2026 core operating profit up 9% and full-year 2026 guidance reiterated. The valuation hook is a FTSE 100-quality franchise trading below historical multiples after a February 2026 guidance reset, partially recovered on a strong H1 beat and a $250m buyback. The central debate is whether Branded and Hikma Rx momentum can offset Injectables margin erosion, elevated capex and weak reported free cash flow, and whether withdrawn medium-term guidance signals a structural reset rather than a cyclical trough.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Hikma offers a rare combination: a globally diversified, investment-grade (BBB/stable, S&P and Fitch) pharmaceutical platform with visible cash returns (84 cents total dividend in FY2025, +5%; $250m 2026 buyback) at modest absolute and sector-relative multiples. The screen passes Graham Enterprising, Neff PEGY, Quality Value, Buffett Quality, Dividend Growth, Magic Formula, Acquirer's Multiple, Dreman Contrarian, Composite Value and Financial Health — a breadth consistent with a quality compounder mispriced on near-term noise rather than balance-sheet distress.

Business quality is underwritten by segment leadership: third-largest US generic injectables supplier by volume and largest MENA pharma company by sales (FY2025 results release). US manufacturing (Ohio, New Jersey) provides a structural advantage amid tariff discussions. Branded delivered 10% core revenue growth and 19% core operating profit growth in FY2025; H1 2026 Branded revenue rose 15% with a 32.5% core margin. Hikma Rx margins improved to 17.3% in FY2025 and 20.6% in H1 2026 on mix and contract manufacturing.

The screen's adjusted signal (strong buy downgraded to buy) flags three reconciliations that a value investor should embrace rather than ignore: (1) healthcare price-erosion overlay — Hikma Rx faces "modest price erosion" per H1 2026 interim; (2) earnings-basis overlay — statutory EPS growth is flat to negative while core EPS grew 2% in FY2025; (3) FCF overlay — filing-aligned FCF of $119m in FY2025 versus screen TTM of $14.4m, with FCF yield failing at 3.5% versus a 5% threshold. These are not disqualifiers for a quality-value entry but define the diligence burden: the margin of safety lies in core earnings power and asset base, not in near-term cash conversion.

Governance has stabilised after disruption: Said Darwazah returned as CEO (December 2025), Victoria Hull became Chair, and a CFO search is under way (Acting CFO: Areb Kurdi). Capital allocation is shareholder-friendly — dividend growth, buyback, and continued R&D (5% of revenue in H1 2026, +18% spend). Net debt/core EBITDA of 1.6x at year-end 2025 and 1.9x at H1 2026 remains manageable within investment-grade headroom.
