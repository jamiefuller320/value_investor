_Version 3 · Updated 2026-09-02T11:07:08.807714+00:00 · Mode: gap_fill_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Hikma Pharmaceuticals PLC (HIK.L) is a FTSE 100 generics and specialty pharmaceutical group with a three-segment model: US-focused Injectables and Hikma Rx, plus fast-growing Branded MENA. The quantitative screen rates the name a strong buy (11/22 models; composite 81%), driven by cheapness (P/E 12.4, P/B 1.9), a 3.9% yield, solid ROE (15.4%), and investment-grade leverage (net debt/core EBITDA 1.6x at FY2025). Filings show earnings recovery after a difficult 2023: FY2025 core operating profit reached $741m on revenue of $3,349m, and H1 2026 delivered a 9% core operating profit beat with guidance reiterated. The valuation hook is a quality generic franchise trading below peers after a February 2026 guidance reset and management shuffle, partially offset by a $250m buyback. The key debate is whether Branded and Hikma Rx momentum can outweigh Injectables margin compression, heavy capex, and weak reported free cash flow ($119m in FY2025 vs $185m dividends) — a divergence the screen itself flags.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, Hikma offers a rare combination: a profitable, diversified generic platform at a mid-cycle multiple, with tangible capital return (5% dividend growth; $250m buyback largely executed by August 2026) and improving broker sentiment after H1 results. The screen passes five factor families — cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk — including Graham Enterprising, Quality Value, Buffett Quality, Dividend Growth, and Financial Health. Business quality is supported by filing disclosures: largest pharma company by sales in MENA, third-largest US injectables supplier by volume, BBB/stable ratings from S&P and Fitch, and ROIC of 16.0% in FY2025. Statutory EPS growth (182 cents in 2025 vs 162 cents in 2024) and core EPS stability (228 cents vs 224 cents) underpin the earnings-yield case. The screen fails FCF Yield, Lynch PEG, and Economic Moat models for identifiable reasons — low headline growth (~1.7%), thin FCF conversion, and sub-moat ROE/margins — which a value investor can accept if Injectables stabilises and capex normalises. H1 2026 progress (Branded revenue +15%, core group operating profit +9%) suggests the FY2026 core operating profit range of $720m–$770m is achievable, supporting the screen’s strong-buy signal while warranting scrutiny of cash conversion rather than outright rejection.
