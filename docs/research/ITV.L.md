_Version 3 · Updated 2026-08-30T07:44:04.951145+00:00 · Mode: gap_fill_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
ITV plc screens as a **strong buy** on quantitative cheapness (P/E ~11.8, P/B ~1.5, ~7% yield) with broad model pass rates (15/22), but the investment case is now dominated by the proposed **sale of Media & Entertainment (M&E) to Sky** for up to **£1.6bn**, targeting a **~£950m net cash return** to shareholders and a residual **ITV Studios–centric** listed entity. Filings show a business that has pivoted successfully toward studios and digital (two-thirds of revenue), yet **2025 free cash flow fell sharply** to **£187m** (FY RNS) from **£325m** in 2024, and statutory earnings were distorted by the prior-year BritBox gain. The central debate is whether headline valuation and the Sky proceeds compensate for **UK advertising cyclicality**, **regulatory delay/risk** on the deal (CMA, possible Phase 2, completion potentially **H2 2027**), and **uncertain post-transaction earnings power** for a standalone Studios business.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
The quantitative screen flags ITV as a classic **value + income** candidate: low P/E, sub-3x P/B, high dividend yield, moderate leverage (debt/equity ~52%), and passes across cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk families (composite score ~91%). Business quality is **mixed but improving strategically**: FY2025 RNS shows **group adjusted EBITA down only 1%** to **£534m** despite **TAR down 5%**, with **ITV Studios external revenue +10%** and **digital revenue +10%** to **£614m**; cost savings of **£63m** and ITVX investment recouped ahead of plan support the “More Than TV” transformation narrative.

For a value investor, the **valuation hook** is twofold: (1) earnings and cash flow from a still-integrated broadcaster–studios group trading below market multiples, and (2) **embedded optionality/catalyst** from the Sky transaction—**£950m net cash return** (ex contingent consideration), **£100m buyback** already announced (Jul 2026 interim), and a simplified **global content IP** story (95,000+ hour library, Zoo 55 digital distribution). Dividend discipline (**5.0p** ordinary, ~**£190m** annually) and **£1.3bn+ liquidity** (FY2025) reinforce the income angle.

However, screen failures on **Graham Defensive, net-net, Buffett Quality, Economic Moat, and Piotroski F-Score (3/9)** are consistent with filing evidence: **ROE ~13.5%** (below moat thresholds), **thin broadcast margins**, **working-capital-heavy** production economics, and **FCF basis divergence** (screen notes filing **£148m**, company-adjusted **£325m**, TTM **£212m**). The screen’s FCF overlay already adjusts the headline signal toward **buy**, not unqualified strong buy—deep research **partially confirms** cheapness but **does not fully validate** cash-backed quality at face value.
