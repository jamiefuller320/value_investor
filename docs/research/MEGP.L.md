# ME Group International plc (MEGP.L) — Research memo

_Version 3 · Updated 2026-08-23T07:22:59.184560+00:00 · Mode: gap_fill_

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
ME Group International plc (MEGP.L) operates a global estate of ~49,000 self-service vending units—chiefly photobooths (Photo.ME) and unattended laundry (Wash.ME)—across 16 countries, with long-dated site contracts in high-footfall locations. The quantitative screen rates the name a strong buy (15/22 models; composite 83%; P/E 8.6×, yield 6.9%, ROE 28.2%, debt/equity 28%), reflecting cheapness, quality cash generation, and dividend support against a business mixing mature photobooth cash flows with fast-growing laundry.

The valuation hook is a market pricing ME Group as a declining photobooth operator, while filings show laundry now contributing 46% of FY2025 EBITDA and strategic contracts (ASDA, SNCF, RATP) expanding the installed base. FY2025 delivered record profit before tax of £78.2m on £315.4m revenue, with cash generated from operations of £115.5m and net cash of £26.5m.

The central debate is whether near-term cyclical and regulatory pressure on photobooths—and a revised FY2026 PBT guidance of £69–74m—are transitory, or whether they reveal structural limits to the “value plus growth” narrative. Accounting restatements (cash-in-transit), a delayed FY2025 audit, and an interim dividend cut add governance friction that the screen does not fully capture.

## INVESTMENT THESIS
For a value investor, ME Group presents a rare combination: statistically cheap on earnings and yield, operationally profitable (EBITDA margin 38.2% in FY2025), and self-funded for growth. The screen passes five factor families—cheapness, quality, dividend, GARP, and risk—with high data quality (20/20 metrics). Filing-aligned free cash flow of £25.2m (FY2025 operating cash flow £90.8m after capex of £65.6m) supports an earnings-yield and FCF-yield case that is not purely accounting-based.

Business quality rests on durable competitive positioning: dominant photobooth share, high barriers to entry, no customer above 2% of turnover, and multi-year contracts with site owners. Wash.ME is the growth engine—1,145 net laundry units added in FY2025, EBITDA up 18.1% to £55.5m—and is reshaping the mix toward higher-return capital deployment. Management is returning capital (FY2025 dividend £32.6m; £18m buyback launched March 2026) while maintaining net cash.

The screen’s strong-buy signal aligns with FY2025 filing fundamentals. The adjusted “buy” overlay (interim EPS decline; net FCF/dividend coverage 0.84×) is directionally correct: the equity remains attractive on trailing numbers, but H1 FY2026 confirms that earnings momentum has slowed and capital returns are competing with heavy reinvestment. For a patient value holder, the asymmetry lies in buying a cash-generative franchise at single-digit earnings multiples while laundry scale and new partnerships (ASDA targeting up to 700 UK machines) compound; the screen’s cheapness and quality passes are well grounded in primary filings, not Yahoo proxies.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
**Evidenced (post 61-body refetch):**

- **Cyclical / consumer (Photo.ME):** Trading update (`ir_8a1ddede4c8b6528.txt`) attributes April photobooth weakness to official photo ID demand and travel uncertainty; interim (`ir_3f2f55026f8c6fe6.txt`) states recovery is partial and not expected to reach prior levels while travel uncertainty persists. Annual risk table (`ir_a006e8bd9b660a9c.txt`) explicitly links recession to discretionary spend, mitigated by needs-driven ID positioning — confirming hybrid exposure.
- **Regulatory (Photo.ME):** German and Japanese ID rule changes remain structural headwinds; next-gen biometric rollout H2 FY2026 per interim filing.
- **Dividend / liquidity:** FY net FCF/dividend 0.84×; H1 cash coverage ~0.33× on dividends paid. Net cash £7.5m at April 2026 after capex, dividends, tax, and buybacks. Interim dividend cut is management’s visible response; simultaneous £18m buyback (`1d660d1c237711ac.txt`) increases payout competition with reinvestment.
- **Governance / process:** FY25 audit delay and listing suspension (`32f4d3a0361630fd.txt`); cash-in-transit and dividend-timing restatements in interim accounts; strategic review terminated with no bid (`4430d9a965d6d46e.txt`); Montefiore secondary placing January 2026 (`71001742a9008234.txt`).
- **Competitive / contract:** UK photobooth contract roll-off; offset partly by ASDA/SNCF/RATP renewals. No customer >10% of revenue per interim Note 3.

**Still open:**

- **Photobooth revenue durability quantification:** Filings describe mix shift and cyclical April but do not split fun vs ID revenue. **Next source:** H1 FY2026 presentation product bridge.
- **FY2026 full-year dividend path:** H1 cut may be prudent rebalancing or a leading indicator of payout reduction. **Next source:** H2 trading update or FY2026 results dividend statement.
- **H1 covenant headroom:** Annual report cites comfortable historical compliance; no H1 update in bodies inspected.
